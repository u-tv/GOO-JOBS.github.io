Client Account Executive

🏢 Jobgether 📍 US Only 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Client Account Executive based in the United States. As a Client Account Executive, you will drive revenue growth and new customer acquisition by developing and executing strategic sales plans across an assigned territory. You will manage the full sales cycle, from prospecting and opportunity qualification to negotiation and contract closure, focusing on large enterprise customers. Combining software, professional services, and support solutions, you will help organizations address complex data challenges and achieve measurable business outcomes. You will build trusted relationships with senior industry executives, customers, and strategic partners while identifying opportunities to expand existing accounts. Working closely with Marketing, Presales, and Consulting teams, you will develop a sustainable sales pipeline and deliver against ambitious revenue targets. This is a fully remote opportunity offering autonomy, professional growth, and the chance to contribute to a collaborative, innovation-driven environment. Accountabilities: Develop and execute a comprehensive territory sales strategy focused on acquiring new enterprise customers, expanding existing accounts, and consistently achieving revenue objectives. Manage complex, consultative sales cycles while positioning integrated software, services, and support solutions that address customers' business and data management needs. Identify, prospect, develop, and close new business opportunities while maintaining strong customer satisfaction and building referenceable client relationships. Sell integrated software, professional services, and support solutions to drive customer success and long-term value. Manage assigned territories strategically by targeting key accounts and collaborating with selected partners to generate additional pipeline opportunities. Lead complex, enterprise-wide sales cycles and effectively communicate the value proposition to senior decision-makers. Collaborate with Marketing to develop territory strategies and target account plans, building a healthy rolling four-quarter pipeline. Coordinate team-selling activities with Presales and Consulting Services to deliver comprehensive customer solutions. Identify opportunities to acquire new customers and expand business within existing accounts in competitive markets. Build and maintain relationships with senior industry executives, enterprise customers, strategic partners, and system integrators. Develop in-depth knowledge of products, customer accounts, competitors, and industry trends, particularly across Data Migration, Master Data Management, Data Quality, and Data Governance. Understand customer requirements, industry-specific challenges, business priorities, operational constraints, and evolving market conditions. Leverage executive support and partner leadership engagement to advance sales strategies and resolve field-level challenges. Manage sales activities against quarterly and annual targets, consistently working to achieve and exceed assigned quotas. Maintain accurate pipeline records, forecast revenue, and track sales activities using Salesforce. Participate in individual and team meetings, training sessions, and industry conferences. Travel as required to support customer relationships and business development activities. Requirements: You are a highly motivated and results-oriented sales professional with a proven ability to acquire enterprise customers, manage complex sales cycles, and consistently exceed revenue targets. You combine strong commercial judgment with consultative selling expertise, excellent relationship-building skills, and the discipline needed to manage a strategic territory independently. You are also a collaborative team player who embraces continuous learning, accountability, innovation, and shared success. Ideally 7+ years of proven direct and/or indirect sales experience, preferably selling software and professional services to large enterprise customers. Experience with the Sandler sales methodology or comparable structured sales frameworks is preferred. Demonstrated expertise in territory and pipeline management, including prospecting, opportunity development, deal orchestration, and closing complex sales cycles. Strong background in value-based and solutions-oriented selling. Established relationships with industry decision-makers, enterprise customers, strategic partners, SAP partners, and system integrators. Proven track record of consistently exceeding sales objectives, with achievements such as President's Club or Sales Representative of the Year recognition preferred. Highly driven, ambitious, and motivated by achieving challenging business objectives. Strong organizational and time-management skills, with the ability to prioritize activities, manage resources effectively, and qualify opportunities strategically. Ability to learn quickly, understand complex solutions, and establish credibility with customers and stakeholders. Strong attention to detail when negotiating commercial agreements, contracts, and terms. Bachelor's degree in Business, Technology, or a related discipline is an advantage. Strong work ethic and a proactive, hands-on approach to responsibilities. Collaborative mindset, entrepreneurial spirit, and commitment to team success. Excellent written, verbal, presentation, and interpersonal communication skills. Alignment with core professional values: Think Big: Demonstrate ambition, courage, and a forward-thinking mindset while pursuing innovation and creating meaningful customer impact. Be Curious: Embrace continuous learning, explore new ideas, understand market developments, and challenge conventional approaches. Take Action: Take ownership, demonstrate initiative, make informed decisions, and turn opportunities into measurable results. Stronger Together: Build trust, respect diverse perspectives, collaborate openly, and contribute to an inclusive and supportive team environment. Benefits: Competitive annual base salary ranging from $125,000 to $175,000, depending on qualifications, experience, skills, and other relevant factors. Eligibility for additional variable compensation, bonuses, and commissions. Medical, dental, and vision insurance. 401(k) retirement plan. Paid time off. Fully remote working arrangement within the United States. Supportive and autonomous working environment that emphasizes trust and individual ownership rather than micromanagement. Access to learning resources and opportunities for continuous professional development. Opportunities to contribute to a rapidly growing organization and help shape its future direction. Collaborative team culture with strong peer support and shared accountability. Recognition of individual and collective achievements. Open organizational culture where employees are encouraged to share ideas, contribute perspectives, and have their work valued. Inclusive workplace committed to equal opportunities, diversity, and professional development.
