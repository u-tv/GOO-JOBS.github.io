Enterprise Automation Engineer

🏢 datadog 📍 New York, New York, USA 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Enterprise Automation Engineering drives efficiency across Datadog through systems automation and integrations, AI agents and MCP development. The team is increasingly embedded in cross-functional initiatives across People, Finance, GTM , Legal, Recruiting, and Technical Solutions — translating ambiguous business problems into scoped, buildable solutions and owning delivery end-to-end. As a Senior Automation Engineer, you design and build the technical solutions that power Datadog’s internal systems. You work closely with BSAs and business stakeholders to take well-scoped requirements and ship production-grade integrations, automations, agents, and internal apps. You will also actively shape how AI and agentic tooling gets embedded into Datadog’s internal workflows. At Datadog, we place value in our office culture - the relationships that it builds, the creativity it brings to the table, and the collaboration of being together. We operate as a hybrid workplace to ensure our employees can create a work-life harmony that best fits them. What You’ll Do: Build and ship: Architect, build, and maintain production-grade integrations, automations, agents, and internal apps across Datadog’s core business systems — Workday, Greenhouse, NetSuite, Salesforce, and more Lead technical design: Define data flows, error handling, scalability requirements, and architectural decisions for multi-system workflows Own the AI/agent layer: Design and develop agentic workflows, MCP servers, and LLM-integrated solutions that extend what the team can deliver Set platform standards: Define and enforce what production-ready means for internal systems — governance, observability, reliability, and maintainability; help the organization move from vibe-coded prototypes to scalable platforms Advise on build vs. buy: Evaluate tools and platforms and make recommendations on when to build custom vs. configure existing Ensure compliance: Collaborate with Security, Privacy, and Compliance to ensure solutions adhere to Datadog’s standards — GDPR , SOX , SOC 2, FedRAMP Who You Are: Demonstrated experience building with AI/agentic tools — not just evaluating them; you’ve shipped something with an LLM, an agent framework, or an MCP and can speak to the tradeoffs Strong judgment on build vs. buy, custom vs. configuration Able to communicate technical decisions clearly to non-technical stakeholders — you can explain why a design choice matters without losing the room Experience working autonomously on a small, high-ownership team at a high-growth company Senior Business Systems Engineer 5+ years administrating and integrating business systems (e.g., NetSuite, Zendesk, Salesforce or Workday) or in a similar role 4+ years leading medium to large sized cross-functional projects across Finance, HR, and Sales 3+ years designing and building multi-system integrations using middleware (e.g., Workato, Boomi, Mulesoft, Zapier) In-depth knowledge of SQL and object-oriented programming paradigms (e.g., Python, Java) to efficiently manipulate large volumes of data Datadog values people from all walks of life. We understand not everyone will meet all the above qualifications on day one. That's okay. If you’re passionate about technology and want to grow your skills, we encourage you to apply.
