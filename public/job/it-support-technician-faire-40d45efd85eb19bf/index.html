IT Support Technician

🏢 faire 📍 Kitchener-Waterloo, ON 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Faire Faire is a technology wholesale platform built on the belief that the future is local. Independent retailers around the globe collectively represent a multi-hundred-billion-dollar wholesale market that has historically been fragmented and offline. At Faire, we're using the power of tech, data, and machine learning to connect this thriving community of entrepreneurs across the globe. Picture your favorite boutique in town — we help them discover the best products from around the world to sell in their stores. With the right tools and insights, we believe that we can level the playing field so businesses can grow and local communities can thrive. We’re looking for smart, resourceful and passionate people to join us as we power the shop local movement. If you believe in community, come join ours. About this role: Faire is looking for an on-site IT Support Technician to join our IT team in Waterloo. Most of your day will be spent helping employees by working through the ticket queue, helping unblock employees when they run into technical issues, and supporting internal events — all with clear communication, solid troubleshooting, and reliable follow-through. Beyond the daily support work, we’re looking for someone who can take on small projects and unclear tasks without needing step-by-step direction. You should be comfortable identifying improvements, finding ways to reduce repetitive work, and handling one-off technical issues. This role includes both regular in-person support and opportunities to improve how our team works. This role requires full-time presence in our Kitchener-Waterloo office five days per week. What you’ll do: Daily Support Work in the Jira ticket queue throughout the day, handling tickets with strong communication and ownership Support employees with MacOS, SaaS tools, account access, and general troubleshooting Troubleshoot issues with devices, the network, Zoom rooms, etc., partnering with IT Engineering to implement solutions Communicate clearly with employees through Slack, Zoom, Jira, and in person Event Support Support internal events such as Makers Faire, including planning, setup, equipment testing, and day-of assistance Make sure event spaces, A/V setups, and demo stations are working properly Project & Improvement Work Take on ambiguous tasks and work independently to deliver high-quality results Look for ways to simplify, improve, or automate repetitive support tasks Update and create documentation in Notion to make processes clearer for the team Help solve unexpected or unusual technical problems and become someone the team can rely on in those moments Qualifications: 2+ years of experience in an IT Support, Help Desk, or deskside role Experience supporting MacOS, SaaS applications, and basic A/V setups Strong troubleshooting skills and the ability to solve issues independently Comfortable using tools such as Jira, Slack, Zoom, Notion, and FleetDM Clear written and verbal communication Organized and able to manage multiple tasks at once Comfortable working in the office and handling hands-on support needs Self-starter who can take initiative and run with tasks Interest in automation or improving workflows Experience assisting with company events Curiosity about new tools and ways to make IT support more efficient Must be able to travel internationally, 1 to 2 times per year.
