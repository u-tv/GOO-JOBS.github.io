Executive Engagement Marketing Manager, Americas

🏢 stripe 📍 Remote, US 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies - from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups - use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team What you’ll do We are seeking an organized, detail-oriented, and highly collaborative Executive Engagement Marketing Manager for the Americas to lead the operational execution of executive engagement and hospitality programs across the region. This role will translate program strategy into excellent execution by building the systems, communications, tools, tracking, and cross-functional coordination needed to deliver high-touch experiences for senior external audiences. You will work closely with Sales to identify and engage executives from targeted accounts, enable sellers with clear outreach materials and follow-up workflows, and ensure each program has the right operational foundation before, during, and after the event. The successful candidate is comfortable independently managing multiple complex workstreams, navigating ambiguity, and proactively improving how programs operate at scale.
