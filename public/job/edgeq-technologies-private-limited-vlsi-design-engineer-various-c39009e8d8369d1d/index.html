EdgeQ Technologies Private Limited: VLSI Design Engineer

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: United States VLSI Design Engineer Job Title: VLSI Design Engineer Location: United States (Remote) Work Mode: 100% Remote Experience: 2–8 Years Salary Range: $95,000 – $165,000 per year Employment Type: Full-Time Job Summary We are seeking a talented and experienced VLSI Design Engineer to join our semiconductor engineering team in the United States. The ideal candidate will be responsible for designing, developing, verifying, and optimizing digital integrated circuits (ICs), ASICs, and SoC architectures. The candidate should have strong knowledge of RTL design, Verilog/SystemVerilog, digital logic design, and semiconductor design methodologies. This position offers a fully remote work opportunity for qualified candidates across the United States. Key Responsibilities Design and develop digital circuits and RTL modules for ASIC and SoC platforms. Write, review, and optimize RTL code using Verilog and SystemVerilog. Perform RTL design, simulation, synthesis, and functional verification. Work on ASIC design and implementation workflows. Collaborate with physical design, verification, and semiconductor engineering teams. Analyze timing, power, and area (PPA) requirements. Debug design issues and resolve functional and performance-related problems. Perform logic synthesis and support timing closure activities. Review design specifications and prepare technical documentation. Participate in design reviews and continuous improvement initiatives. Required Skills & Qualifications 2–8 years of experience in VLSI Design or Semiconductor Engineering. Strong programming knowledge of Verilog and SystemVerilog. Experience with RTL design and digital logic design. Understanding of ASIC and SoC design methodologies. Knowledge of synthesis, simulation, and verification processes. Familiarity with timing analysis and optimization. Understanding of CMOS technology and semiconductor fundamentals. Experience with EDA tools such as Synopsys, Cadence, or Siemens EDA. Strong analytical, debugging, and problem-solving skills. Preferred Qualifications Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, VLSI, or a related field. Experience with FPGA prototyping and ASIC implementation. Knowledge of UVM-based verification methodologies. Familiarity with Static Timing Analysis (STA). Understanding of low-power design techniques. Experience with Python, Tcl, or Perl scripting. Knowledge of physical design, floorplanning, placement, and routing. Salary & Benefits Annual Salary: $95,000 – $165,000. Competitive compensation based on skills and experience. 100% remote work opportunity. Flexible working arrangements. Health, dental, and vision insurance. Paid time off and holidays. Retirement savings benefits, where applicable. Professional development and career growth opportunities. Work Location United States – Fully Remote (Candidates must be authorized to work in the United States). How to Apply Interested candidates can submit their updated resume along with contact details and relevant VLSI Design Engineering experience. Equal Opportunity Employer: We welcome qualified candidates from diverse professional backgrounds and encourage applications from eligible candidates across the United States. To apply: https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/edgeq-technologies-private-limited-vlsi-design-engineer
