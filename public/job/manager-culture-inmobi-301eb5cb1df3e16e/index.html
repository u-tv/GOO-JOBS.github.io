About the Role This is a strategic leadership role at the intersection of organizational behavior, psychology, people experience design, and transformation. You’ll diagnose how the organization actually functions, design the rituals and experiences that shape culture, and lead a team executing both strategy and craft. This is not program management. This is strategic thinking and deep organizational diagnosis.
