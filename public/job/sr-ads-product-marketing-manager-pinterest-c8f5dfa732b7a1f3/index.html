What you’ll do: Develop and execute end-to-end go-to-market strategies for Measure and Optimize product units, including audience definition, positioning, messaging, launch planning, promotion, enablement, and post-launch adoption. Translate complex technical product capabilities, measurement methodologies, and performance insights into clear, differentiated customer value propositions and narratives for advertisers, agencies, and internal teams. Partner with Product, Data Science, and other cross-functional teams to synthesize customer, market, competitive, and product-performance insights that inform product strategy, prioritization, and commercialization. Turn data science and analytics insights into credible, market-ready stories that demonstrate the value of specific platform capabilities, with appropriate proof points, methodology, and caveats. Create launch and enablement materials, including positioning frameworks, messaging guides, sales decks, one-pagers, FAQs, product pages, release communications, and training content. Lead cross-functional launch working groups; establish clear owners, milestones, dependencies, and escalation paths to ensure launch readiness and high-quality execution. Partner with commercial teams to identify adoption barriers, monitor launch and adoption metrics and use feedback and performance data to improve messaging, enablement and future go-to-market programs.
