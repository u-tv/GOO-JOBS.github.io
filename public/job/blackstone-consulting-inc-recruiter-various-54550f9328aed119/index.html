Headquarters: Los Angeles, California, United States URL: https://myjobs.adp.com/bci/cx/job-listing Talent Acquisition Recruiter (Temp to Hire) Compensation: $32-$35/hr Location: California (Remote) Blackstone Consulting, Inc is seeking a Talent Acquisition Recruiter to join their team. This role will be supporting recruitment efforts for our National Healthcare client throughout California. Job Description: The contract Talent Acquisition Recruiter has overall responsibility for the delivery of Talent & Acquisition transactional processes and reporting, as well as leading the progression of the function and the broadening of the scope of services provided. Future state of the HR Service Center may include multi-region focus (e.g.,East, West, Southwest, Southeast, North and South), expanded services beyond basic transactional support; as well as extending reach into supporting adjacent areas such as Global Mobility, and Staffing/Recruiting process standardization, centralization and all employee relations and HR support. This role will ensure smooth integration with other company functions including; due diligence, integration planning, execution and transition, employee relations, HR strategy projects, employee communications and may include the following responsibilities.
