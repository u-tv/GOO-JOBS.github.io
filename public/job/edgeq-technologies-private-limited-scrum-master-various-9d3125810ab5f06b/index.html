Job Description

Headquarters: United States Scrum Master Job Title: Scrum Master Location: New York, United States Work Mode: Full-Time | Remote / Fully Remote Experience: 1–10 Years Salary Range: $75,000 – $145,000 per year (Depending on Experience and Skills) Employment Type: Full-Time Work Arrangement: 100% Remote Opportunity Available Job Summary We are seeking an experienced and motivated Scrum Master to join our growing team in the United States. The ideal candidate will facilitate Agile development processes, lead Scrum ceremonies, remove project obstacles, and ensure successful delivery of projects while supporting cross-functional teams. Candidates based in New York and other eligible US locations are encouraged to apply. Fully remote opportunities are available. Key Responsibilities Facilitate daily Scrum meetings, sprint planning, sprint reviews, and retrospectives. Coach and guide development teams on Agile and Scrum methodologies. Remove obstacles and resolve issues affecting team productivity. Work closely with Product Owners to manage and prioritize product backlogs. Monitor sprint progress and ensure timely project delivery. Track team performance using Agile metrics such as velocity and burndown charts. Promote continuous improvement and effective team collaboration. Coordinate with stakeholders, developers, QA engineers, and management. Identify project risks and implement appropriate mitigation strategies. Ensure adherence to Agile principles and Scrum best practices. Required Skills & Qualifications 1–10 years of experience in Scrum Master or Agile project management roles. Strong knowledge of Agile methodologies and Scrum frameworks. Experience facilitating Scrum ceremonies and sprint management. Familiarity with Jira, Confluence, Azure DevOps, or similar tools. Excellent communication, leadership, and problem-solving skills. Ability to manage multiple teams and project priorities. Strong stakeholder management and conflict-resolution abilities. Scrum Master certification (CSM, PSM I/II) is preferred. Preferred Qualifications Experience working with distributed and remote teams. Knowledge of software development life cycles (SDLC). Experience with Agile reporting and performance metrics. Previous experience in IT or software development environments. Salary & Benefits Annual Salary: $75,000 – $145,000. Competitive compensation based on experience. 100% remote work opportunities. Flexible working arrangements. Professional development and certification support. Health insurance and other applicable employee benefits. Career growth opportunities. Work Location New York, United States (Fully Remote Available). How to Apply Interested candidates can submit their updated resume along with their contact details and relevant Scrum Master experience. Equal Opportunity Employer: We welcome qualified candidates from diverse professional backgrounds and encourage applications from eligible candidates across the United States.
