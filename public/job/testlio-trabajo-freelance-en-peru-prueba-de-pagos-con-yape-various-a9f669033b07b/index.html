Testlio: Trabajo Freelance en Perú | Prueba de Pagos con Yape

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Peru URL: https://testlio.com We're Testlio, a global software testing company that works with businesses around the world to improve the quality of their digital products, including apps, websites, and online payment experiences. We're currently looking for people in Peru who use Yape to help us check that online payments work correctly for local customers. All of our freelancer projects are 100% remote. Requirements: Currently living in Peru. Have an active Yape account. Conversational English to provide feedback Availability for approximately 30-60 minutes. What does the testing involve? You'll make a real purchase from a well-known online store using Yape, just like you normally would. The goal is to check that the payment process works correctly from start to finish. You'll be asked to: Follow simple instructions from our team. Complete a purchase using Yape on your phone or computer. Record your screen during the process and upload the recording through a form. This is a paid freelance opportunity ($12/hr) No previous software testing experience is required. If your profile is a match, a member of our team will reach out with more details and next steps. Please note that all official emails from our team come from an @testlio domain email address. #LI-REMOTE To apply: https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/testlio-trabajo-freelance-en-peru-prueba-de-pagos-con-yape
