MongoDB Technical Services exists to support our customers’ development, deployment and ongoing operations of mission-critical application data infrastructure, ensuring that they get the most out of their investment in MongoDB. We’re looking to speak with candidates based in New York City or Palo Alto for a hybrid working mode. This role reports to our EVP of Technical Services.
