Overview of the Role: As Manager, Ad Operations, you’ll play a critical role within InMobi’s North America Go-to-Market (GTM) organization, serving as the primary operational bridge between our regional Sales teams and Global Revenue Services (GRS) at our headquarters. Often referred to as “the Machine,” GRS is both the analytical core and the operational connective tissue of the company, linking systems, teams, and workflows to ensure flawless execution across our global exchange business. In this role, you’ll be the in-region triage and operational partner for InMobi Exchange, supporting Sales, Client Development, Agency Development, Programmatic Trading teams, DSP Partner Managers, and GRS teams across time zones. You’ll own media planning, real-time campaign and deal operations, and rapid issue resolution, while delivering proactive, data-driven insights that ensure seamless execution and exceptional performance across PMP and Brand IO activities. This is a highly collaborative, demand-facing operations role, built for someone who thrives in a dynamic environment, brings deep programmatic expertise, and balances consultative strength with operational rigor. Location : This position is based onsite in our New York City office.
