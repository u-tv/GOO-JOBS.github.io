Kyndryl: VRO Lead

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Dallas, Texas, United States URL: https://kyndryl.com Who We Are At Kyndryl, we design, build, manage and modernize the mission-critical technology systems that the world depends on every day. So why work at Kyndryl? We are always moving forward – always pushing ourselves to go further in our efforts to build a more equitable, inclusive world for our employees, our customers and our communities. The Role In the Value Realization Office at Kyndryl, you will play a critical role in ensuring that our customers achieve and maximize the tangible business outcomes promised in their digital transformation journeys. You will act as the strategic driver behind establishing, measuring, and protecting value across complex IT outsourcing, enterprise resource planning (ERP), and large-scale infrastructure engagements post-contract signature. In this role, you will define and execute the VRO operating model from the ground up, establishing governance cadences, RACI frameworks, and executive escalation paths tailored to each engagement. You will partner closely with client finance and operations leaders to rigorously construct and validate operational baselines, translating overarching deal cases into transparent, actionable benefits registers equipped with defined KPIs, milestones, and auditable proof points. Your financial acumen and analytical rigor will be central to defining robust measurement models for hard cost savings, risk and cost avoidance, productivity uplift, and service quality enhancements. By designing automated scorecards and dashboards, tracking realized gains, and diagnosing root causes behind value leakage or process drift, you will keep critical customer engagements on course. Furthermore, you will lead high-impact value reviews with C-suite stakeholders and Kyndryl account executives, turning proven realized value into a catalyst for account renewals, expansion, and long-term client advocacy. Key Responsibilities VRO Governance & Operating Model: Establish and lead the VRO governance framework across major IT outsourcing and transformation accounts, including meeting cadences, RACI matrices, roles, and escalation workflows. Baseline Validation & Benefits Architecture: Partner directly with client finance and operational teams to construct baseline metrics; convert complex deal value propositions into operational benefits registers with designated owners, timing, and verifiable evidence standards. Value Modeling & Auditable Tracking: Define rigorous measurement methodologies for hard savings, cost avoidance, operational productivity, and service quality; maintain financial models (TCO, ROI, payback periods) and establish auditable evidence trails for formal sign-offs. Executive Reviews & Leakage Mitigation: Facilitate monthly and quarterly value realization reviews with C-level client executives and Kyndryl leadership; identify root causes of value leakage (e.g., scope creep, process drift, delayed adoption) and enforce recovery action plans. Analytics & Tooling: Build dynamic value dashboards and scorecards using Power BI or equivalent platforms to deliver single-pane visibility into target versus realized gains. Cross-Functional Synergy & Practice Growth: Collaborate with delivery and Organizational Change Management (OCM) leads to tie workforce adoption metrics to benefit realization; coach VRO analysts and package frameworks into standardized practice IP. Your Future at Kyndryl Kyndryl’s mission to modernize enterprise IT and drive business transformation means that within the Value Realization Office, you will be positioned at the vital intersection of technology, finance, and client strategy. You will lead high-profile transformation initiatives, mentor and direct VRO analysts across concurrent engagements, and actively contribute to building reusable assets, benchmark repositories, and measurement standards that scale across our global practice. Who You Are You are good at what you do and possess the proven consulting and financial depth to prove it. You bring a growth mindset, navigating complex transformation headwinds with clarity and decisive problem-solving. You are relentlessly customer-focused—committed to ensuring our clients see verifiable return on investment and financial transparency. Finally, you are empathetic, collaborative, and borderless, partnering seamlessly across organizational change management (OCM), delivery, and executive teams to build trust and achieve shared success. Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Business, Finance, Engineering, or a related field. MBA preferred. 12+ years in consulting, transformation, or managed services, with at least 5 years leading benefits realization, value management, or financial tracking on large engagements. Proven experience building baselines, KPI frameworks, and benefits registers that have held up under client finance scrutiny. Strong financial modeling skills covering TCO, ROI, and payback, with working knowledge of IT outsourcing commercial structures. Track record presenting value results to client executives and managing difficult conversations about shortfalls. Lean Six Sigma Green or Black Belt preferred.
