The Commercial Legal Team at Pinterest negotiates Pinterest’s inbound and supplier agreements. We are looking for a Senior Commercial Counsel with a strong background in negotiating complex SaaS, AI and cloud infrastructure agreements to join our team. The position will manage workloads for and relationships with several of our cross functional partners, including Engineering, Security, Privacy, Finance, Procurement and IT. This role will play an important part in supporting Pinterest’s cloud strategy across multiple platforms and helping the business efficiently scale its technology and infrastructure partnerships. What you’ll do: Lead, coach and develop a team of legal professionals supporting a high volume of complex commercial and technology transactions. Manage hiring, onboarding, performance, development and resourcing for your team. Set team priorities and operating rhythms to optimize productivity, quality, stakeholder support and career development. Act as an escalation point for complex, contentious and unforeseen issues, and make sound decisions in ambiguous or time-sensitive situations. Guide negotiation strategy and legal risk assessment for SaaS, AI, cloud, infrastructure, data processing, professional services and other supplier agreements. Partner closely with Engineering, Infrastructure, Security, Privacy, Finance, Procurement and IT stakeholders to support strategic business initiatives and critical transactions. Improve how the work gets done by building scalable playbooks, templates, workflows, guidance and decision frameworks. Use metrics, milestones and feedback loops to drive accountability, improve execution and strengthen stakeholder experience. Build team and organizational capability through coaching, succession planning, exposure and stretch opportunities. Stay current with cloud contracting trends, emerging licensing and consumption models, and industry best practices to help Pinterest make informed, pragmatic decisions in a rapidly evolving space. What we’re looking for: JD with minimum 10 years of experience in a legal environment, with significant experience negotiating complex SaaS, cloud services, technology infrastructure and commercial transactions. Minimum 2 years of in-house experience at a technology company preferred. Strong people management experience, including coaching, performance management, development and workforce planning. Deep experience negotiating cloud compute, SaaS, hosting, data processing, professional services and other technology supplier agreements, with comfort advising on data privacy, data security, AI and general commercial risk. Strong judgment and comfort making risk-based decisions in ambiguous, fast-moving environments. Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills, including the ability to present clear, business-oriented solutions.
