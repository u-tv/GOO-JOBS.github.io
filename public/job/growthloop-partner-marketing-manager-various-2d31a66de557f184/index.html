GrowthLoop: Partner Marketing Manager

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: URL: http://www.growthloop.com Partner Marketing Manager Remote, North America As our Partner Marketing Manager, you’ll own marketing to, with, and through our partners, building on an established partner strategy. You’ll work closely with our partnerships and marketing teams to bring joint solutions to market, build relationships across our ecosystem, and help partner sellers understand where GrowthLoop fits and when to bring us into a customer conversation. This is a hands-on role: you’ll shape the plan and drive the details that deliver results. What You'll Do Own partner event marketing from planning through follow-up. Turn conferences and joint events into opportunities to reach the right buyers, deepen partner relationships, and create sales conversations. Lead co-branded product launches and customer announcements, coordinating the joint story, partner quotes and approvals, content, and promotion. Build marketing relationships with priority technology, cloud, and advisory partners. Develop joint campaigns that support shared sales priorities and give partners a reason to introduce GrowthLoop to their customers. Help sellers at our strategic partners understand, trust, and recommend GrowthLoop through relevant content, enablement, and opportunities to engage with our team. Bring key partner marketing opportunities into GrowthLoop’s broader quarterly marketing plan. Coordinate with partnerships, sales, marketing, and partner teams to deliver joint initiatives with clear owners, timelines, and goals. Proactively involve strategic partners in our major marketing announcements by securing endorsements and/or social media amplification and coordinating with content and product marketing. Work with content and product marketing to create partner-targeted content and sales materials, including solution one-pagers, decks, and joint customer stories. Collaborate with content marketing on co-branded content, such as drafting guest blog posts and lead generation assets (eBooks, whitepapers). Keep external partner listings and the partnerships sections of our website up to date. Run partner campaigns in HubSpot, including audience lists, campaign setup, lead capture, routing, and reporting, so engagement turns into timely sales follow-up. Work with sales to track partner engagement, event follow-up, and sales opportunities generated or supported by partner programs. Use the results to improve programs and decide where to focus next. Regularly analyze and report on partner engagement and results of partner marketing initiatives. What You Bring 4–6 years of relevant partner marketing experience in enterprise B2B. Experience running partner campaigns, joint launches, and event marketing, including lead capture and sales follow-up. You can explain what you owned and how you measured the results. Strong relationship-building skills. You can earn a partner’s trust, find a shared opportunity, and get commitments, content, and approvals across the finish line. Clear writing and messaging skills, with the ability to turn a joint solution into a story buyers and partner sellers understand. You’re comfortable creating content, collateral, and presentations. Strong project management and follow-through. You can coordinate multiple teams, manage competing deadlines, and keep work moving when priorities change. You’ve run partner marketing programs that led to customer introductions or sales conversations, and can explain what worked and how you measured it. Hands-on experience with HubSpot or a similar marketing automation platform. You’re comfortable setting up campaigns, managing lists and workflows, and tracking results yourself. About GrowthLoop GrowthLoop built the first composable CDP, helping marketers leverage live, first-party data from their cloud warehouse to launch highly targeted campaigns. That composable foundation gave way to the Compound Marketing Engine, an evolution of the composable CDP that drives compound growth by using agentic AI to accelerate the marketing cycle. Now, we’re building what comes next: an agentic system that uses causality data to find the actions that actually improve customer outcomes and gets smarter with every campaign cycle. Founder-led, we’re partnering with the world’s most innovative brands to close the loop between people, data, and AI. Enterprise marketing teams at Google, Costco, Ford, and Whirlpool (to name a few) choose GrowthLoop to launch smarter, more personalized marketing programs that compound growth with every campaign. About You You’re a builder who enjoys turning a shared idea into something real, whether it’s a partner event, a joint launch, or a campaign that opens doors. You take ownership from planning through follow-up, bring clarity when priorities shift, and care about the details that make the work successful. You build trust by listening, communicating directly and kindly, and doing what you say you’ll do. You’re curious about our partners, our customers, and how to do the work better, including using new tools thoughtfully. You pitch in where needed, make the people around you better, and measure success by what the team and our partners achieve together. What We Offer The on-target earnings range for this role is USD $125,000–$135,000. Total compensation includes equity. Final base salary decisions will be based on a variety of non-discriminatory factors unique to each candidate, such as the individual’s skill set, location, depth of experience, and qualifications.
