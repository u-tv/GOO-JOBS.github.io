Headquarters: Remote - U.S.A. Our Values If this sounds like you, you’ll fit right in. Who You Are You have a knack for process innovation and project management and are a true specialist in Benefits. When collaborating with stakeholders to define requirements related to our 401(k) MEP and Benefits Billing, you should serve as an expert who can build solutions from scratch and think creatively to enhance team productivity and processes. You possess an ability to perform complex analysis, develop findings, and improve project operations critical to Financial Benefits. As the Financial Benefits Project Manager, you will be tasked with developing processes and strategies to ensure that larger projects function and operate as intended. Your responsibilities will encompass onboarding, overseeing, and managing the new and existing projects related to both the 401(k) MEP and additional benefits billing, including Medical, Dental and Vision, commuter, voluntary benefits and more. Your role will require engagement with stakeholders from various sectors, including external auditors and partners, fiduciaries and internal teams, including the Retirement teams, the Benefits Billing team, Product and Engineering, Internal Audit, and Finance. The Financial Benefits Project Manager will help shape both the MEP Retirement benefit and Benefits Billing at Justworks by managing projects related to customer risk, contributions, compliance, payroll information, reconciliation and process automation. This position will impact the operating procedures of the team by understanding customer and policy data and recommending process changes to ensure customer and PEO compliance. Your Success Profile What You Will Work On Create and manage new and existing projects related to the 401(k) MEP Benefit and Benefits Billing including but not limited to: Quarterly and annual audits Documentation management End-of-Year Project Nondiscrimination Testing Ongoing process and reconciliation automations New benefit billing onboarding and integration Ensure the success of analytical aspects of major projects such as the annual audit, nondiscrimination testing, and end-of-year processes. Work with the Financial Benefits teams to evaluate current processes and implement solutions to manual procedures. Work with teams and other PMs to ensure process documentation continuity across Benefits. Collaborate with stakeholders from government agencies, employer groups, and individuals to gather insights into industry challenges, requirements, and desired outcomes. Develop and implement strategies to optimize the efficiency and effectiveness of the 401(k) MEP and Benefits Billing to identify areas for improvement. Prioritize updates based on user feedback, regulatory changes, and technological advancements. Ensure compliance with relevant regulations and guidelines governing 401(k) and MEPs. Keep up to date with current employment security laws, policies, and procedures. Communicate across various departments to make recommendations on processes to improve the operational efficiency of the Operations team and the performance of the plan. Oversee all Financial Benefits related customer communications. Identify errors in operations by analyzing, collecting, and reporting risk performance and Financial Benefits data. Perform other related responsibilities as needed based on department and/or organizational needs.
