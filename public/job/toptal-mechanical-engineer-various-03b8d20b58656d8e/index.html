Toptal: Mechanical Engineer

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: We're looking for a highly experienced Mechanical Engineer who can hit the ground running on a fast-paced, short-term remote engagement. This is a hands-on design role for someone who thrives under tight timelines and can move from onboarding to meaningful output within days, not weeks. You'll be working deep in Siemens NX on plastics-focused design challenges, contributing directly to parts that are headed toward injection molding production. What You'll Do Deliver mechanical design work in Siemens NX with a focus on plastic components for injection molding Design and refine TPU overmolded parts Manage heat insert placement and integration into plastic housings Apply functional dimensioning and geometric tolerancing to ensure manufacturability Perform tolerance stack-up analysis to validate fit and function across assemblies Support IP-rated enclosure design and button/user interface component design What You Bring Strong, hands-on experience with Siemens NX Proven track record designing injection-molded plastic parts Experience with TPU overmolding Practical knowledge of heat insert management and integration Solid grasp of functional dimensioning and tolerance stack-up analysis Experience with IP-rated design Experience with button/user interface design Ability to start immediately and ramp up quickly with minimal onboarding time To apply: https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/toptal-mechanical-engineer
