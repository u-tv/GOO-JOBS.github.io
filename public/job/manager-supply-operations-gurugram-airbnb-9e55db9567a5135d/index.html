Manager - Supply Operations, Gurugram

🏢 airbnb 📍 Gurgaon, India 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Airbnb was born in 2007 when two hosts welcomed three guests to their San Francisco home, and has since grown to over 5 million hosts who have welcomed over 2 billion guest arrivals in almost every country across the globe. Every day, hosts offer unique stays and experiences that make it possible for guests to connect with communities in a more authentic way. The Community you will join The Airbnb ACC Enterprise Ops team is a dynamic business unit dedicated to delivering safe, quality stays and exceptional experiences for travelers worldwide. It helps grow supply and increase supply effectiveness, utilization, & impact using people-powered operations. The Difference You Will Make The Manager, Supply Growth Operations leads multiple teams that find, engage and grow our Host community, and sets the direction for how that work gets done. This role blends strategy, people leadership, workflow design and stakeholder partnership. It owns both the operational outcomes and the business relationship. The manager frames ambiguous, cross-functional problems and solves them with data and sound judgment. They help decide where AI, vendors and in-house teams each fit, and build the leaders who carry the work forward. What You’ll Do: Strategy & Business Ownership Define and refine a multi-quarter strategy and roadmap for supply growth operations, tied to business goals and targets. Prioritise segments, channels and campaigns, and decide where to invest. Own pipeline volume, conversion, host quality, engagement results, cost and risk. Act as the senior escalation point for complex or high-impact decisions across the function. Problem Solving & Decision Making Frame ambiguous, cross-functional problems such as lead convertibility,campaign effectiveness, and the trade-off between volume and quality. Form hypotheses, analyse data, and choose the appropriate operations approach, with clear trade-offs and risks. Run root cause analyses on recurring issues and drive corrective action acrossteams. Workflow Design, AI & Automation Collaborate with Engineering, Program and Product teams on tool choice, process steps, iteration and ops metrics. Identify high-volume, rules-based work suited to AI. Own the logic and human-in-the-loop escalation design. Run supervised pilots with quality checks before scaling. Team Leadership & Development Lead, coach and develop managers, supervisors and senior experts, setting clear goals and holding a high performance bar. Build succession plans and career paths, and keep a healthy span of control. Foster a culture of innovation, attention to detail and host-centric thinking. Planning, Workforce & Vendor Management Make hybrid workforce decisions across AI, vendors and in-house teams. Use in-house teams to build capability and vendors to deliver scale. Plan headcount, capacity and the level mix using volume and performance data. Data, Reporting & Performance Management Own the function's KPI dashboard, including conversion, host quality, engagement, cycle time and cost. Report execution insights and trends to leadership, highlighting risks and opportunities. Contribute to quarterly and annual planning with data-backed forecasts. Cross-Functional Collaboration & Representation Negotiate scope and priorities with program management, product, sales, engineering and regional leaders, using business context rather than team output alone. Many of these meetings will be in US time zones, so flexibility is imperative. Collaborate with Learning & Development and Enablement to close skill gaps. Represent the function in broader forums, and build feedback loops from operations into product, policy and process. Your Expertise: Minimum 12+ years of overall operations experience, with at least 7 years leading managers or large teams. Proven track record of landing operating-model or process change at scale, with measurable results. Strong business acumen, shown by shaping priorities and mandates from a commercial context. Strong analytical and structured problem-solving skills, with hands-on fluency with AI and automation. Strong stakeholder management and influencing skills, with experience across global, cross-functional organizations. Demonstrated ability to make sound decisions in ambiguous, high-stakes situations. Strong coaching capability, with experience developing leaders and managing performance. Ability to thrive in a fast-paced environment with shifting priorities. Experience in lead generation, sales operations, marketplace growth or community and engagement programs is highly desirable. Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
