Oportun: Senior Product Manager (R14206)

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote - India Who We Are Oportun (Nasdaq: OPRT) is a mission-driven financial services company that puts its members' financial goals within reach. With intelligent borrowing, savings, and budgeting capabilities, Oportun empowers members with the confidence to build a better financial future. Since inception, Oportun has provided more than $22.7 billion in responsible and affordable credit, saved its members more than $2.5 billion in interest and fees, and helped its members set aside an average of more than $1,800 annually. Location Note We’re excited to consider talent currently based in India, as well as candidates who are open to relocating to India for this opportunity. Please note that, as this is a "Remote–India" position, the role must be performed from within India. The Opportunity Oportun is seeking a talented, customer-focused, and analytical Senior Product Manager to join the Servicing & Payments team. You will help create a trusted post-disbursement experience that strengthens members' confidence in Oportun, supports their financial health, and encourages continued engagement and repeat member behavior. You will define product strategy, set measurable objectives and key results, create outcome-oriented roadmaps, and partner with engineering, design, data, operations, risk, compliance, and other teams to deliver reliable, scalable solutions. You will help make repayment easy, intelligent, successful, and accurately reflected for lending members. At Oportun, we move fast and we hold each other to a high bar. If you care about doing your best work while actually moving the needle for real people, you'll fit right in. What You'll Do Your day-to-day You'll define and execute product strategy and roadmaps that improve repayment outcomes, member trust, and repeat engagement. You'll own initiatives end to end - from discovery and requirements through delivery, measurement, and iteration. You'll create simple, transparent, and reliable servicing and payment experiences across self-service and assisted channels. You'll use member insights, data, and experimentation to improve payer rate, payment success, DQ prevention, and lending-member NPS. You'll lead cross-functional delivery with engineering, design, data, operations, risk, compliance, legal, and finance. You'll communicate product strategy, trade-offs, progress, and measurable business impact to senior stakeholders. You'll bring ideas for how we can do things better - proactively, not just when asked. You'll look for ways to use AI tools thoughtfully - to work smarter, reduce manual work, and make better decisions faster. Who You Are The right fit looks like this You're customer-focused and analytical, with a track record of navigating complex problems and turning member needs and data into practical product decisions. You're collaborative, communicate clearly, and build consensus across a wide range of cross-functional partners. You're comfortable navigating complexity and don't wait for perfect information to move forward. You communicate clearly, give direct feedback, and expect the same in return. What You Bring Skills & experience You'll have 5+ years of product management experience in high-growth environments. You've demonstrated experience partnering with business executives and cross-functional teams across compliance, risk, operations, fraud, technology, business intelligence, finance, training, legal, and cybersecurity. You bring strong analytical problem-solving skills, including experience with data-informed decision making, testing and experimentation, metric definition, and data analysis. You've demonstrated a track record of shipping and iterating on impactful products while building consensus across a broad group of cross-functional partners. You bring strong product execution skills and the ability to help teams push through obstacles and blockers to deliver value for members. You communicate clearly and concisely and have strong documentation skills. Nice to Have Experience launching products within complex legal and regulatory frameworks, including financial services. Experience with SQL. Experience working with a multinational workforce. You've used AI tools (ChatGPT, Copilot, or similar) to improve your own productivity. Our AI Mindset We build with AI. We lead with judgment. AI isn't a feature here. It's how we work. We're excited about what AI makes possible, and we're looking for people who share that curiosity. You don't need to be an expert. You need to be open to experimenting , willing to learn, and able to apply new tools in ways that actually move the needle. In this role, you'll be encouraged to explore AI tools, find practical use cases for your function, and bring ideas for working smarter. We believe the best outcomes come when human judgment and AI capability work together.
