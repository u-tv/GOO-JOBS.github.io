About the Role We're hiring a Sales Specialist to support AllCare.ai's growth by developing referral relationships with healthcare providers, hospice agencies, senior living communities, and skilled nursing facilities throughout Southern California. This is a relationship-driven healthcare sales role that requires strong communication skills, confidence in conducting outbound outreach, and the ability to navigate sensitive healthcare conversations with empathy and professionalism. Your primary goal is to expand our hospice referral network, establish new partnerships, and drive patient referrals and admissions through consistent outreach and relationship development.
