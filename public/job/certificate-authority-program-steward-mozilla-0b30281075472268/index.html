Job Description

What you’ll do: Manage Mozilla’s root store: Make root store management your primary responsibility, through public review processes, and policy and audit evaluations and using certificate analysis tools, the Common CA Database (CCADB), to process root inclusions, trust changes, and removals and maintain accurate trust and revocation data. Evaluate CA compliance and investigate incidents: Review certificates, CA policies, audit reports, and incident responses. Identify gaps, ask precise follow-up questions, assess corrective actions, and support informed trust decisions. Develop security policy and standards: Contribute to Mozilla’s Root Store Policy, program guidance, and CCADB and CA/Browser Forum requirements through technical analysis, clear drafting, and constructive public discussion. Coordinate work and build program expertise: Collaborate with CAs, Mozilla engineers, auditors, and other root store operators to resolve issues and advance projects. Work closely with the CA Program Manager, document processes and decisions, and take on increasing responsibility. Improve tools and automate CA program tasks: Maintain and enhance supporting tools and workflows. Use AI coding assistants and software tools to streamline certificate analysis, compliance review, data reconciliation, reporting, and documentation, with appropriate validation and human review. What you’ll bring: Understanding of the Web PKI and internet security: Working knowledge of digital certificates, public key cryptography, TLS, certificate chains, and revocation, including how technical requirements and CA practices establish trust. Awareness of emerging threats and developments, including post-quantum cryptography, and an interest in evaluating their implications for browser trust and CA operations. Practical software engineering and automation skills: Experience developing, maintaining, testing, and debugging tools or services using existing codebases, APIs, structured data, Git-based workflows, and cloud applications. CA compliance and audit evaluation: Ability to critically evaluate Certificate Policies and Certification Practice Statements (CP/CPS), independent audit reports, and incident analyses against applicable requirements. Familiarity with industry requirements and WebTrust and ETSI audit frameworks and the ability to identify gaps, assess supporting evidence, and formulate precise follow-up questions. Analytical judgment and program management: Ability to investigate technical evidence, interpret specifications and policy requirements, and reach evidence-based conclusions. Prioritize competing requests, coordinate stakeholders, and follow complex issues through to resolution. Clear communication and effective collaboration: Excellent written communication and facilitation skills, including the ability to explain technical decisions, write precise documentation and constructive questions, guide public discussions, and navigate disagreement professionally. Work effectively with distributed teams and external stakeholders, seek feedback, and share knowledge while developing broader technical and program expertise.
