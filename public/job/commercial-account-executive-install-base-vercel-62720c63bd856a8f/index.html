Commercial Account Executive, Install Base

🏢 vercel 📍 Hybrid - San Francisco, New York City, Austin 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Vercel: Vercel is the agentic infrastructure company. We free people and agents to ship what’s next. For more than a decade, Vercel has shaped how the web is built. As the team behind Next.js, v0, and AI SDK, we create products that help builders move from idea to production with speed, security, and exceptional developer experience. Now, software is entering a new era, and the next generation of products will not just be used by people. They will be built, extended, and operated by agents. We are building the platform for that future, trusted by companies like OpenAI, PayPal, Ramp, Supreme, and millions of developers worldwide . Whether you’re building our products, supporting our customers, growing our community, or shaping our story, you’ll help define what comes next. About the Role: As an Account Executive on the Commercial Install Base team, you will own and grow a portfolio of existing Vercel customers. You will lead the full commercial relationship across your accounts, including account strategy, pipeline generation, expansion, renewal, and retention. You will partner with developers, engineering leaders, product teams, executives, and procurement stakeholders to understand what customers are building and connect Vercel to meaningful technical and business outcomes. This is a strategic hunting role within our existing customer base. You will use product adoption, usage signals, company priorities, and thoughtful account research to develop hypotheses, create new conversations, and uncover expansion opportunities. You will also quarterback the broader Vercel team, bringing together Solutions Engineering, Developer Success, Product, and other partners to remove barriers and drive customer outcomes. Success requires commercial judgment, technical curiosity, disciplined execution, and the ability to create momentum when the path forward is not obvious. What You Will Do: Own the commercial strategy, retention, and growth of a portfolio of existing Vercel customers. Build account plans that identify customer priorities, key stakeholders, current workloads, risks, whitespace, and expansion opportunities. Generate pipeline through proactive prospecting, multi-threading, product usage signals, and thoughtful account research. Build trusted relationships with developers, engineering leaders, executives, procurement partners, and other key stakeholders. Run end-to-end renewal and expansion cycles, including discovery, qualification, technical evaluation, negotiation, and close. Identify customer risk early and develop clear plans to protect recurring revenue. Connect Vercel’s platform to measurable outcomes such as faster product delivery, improved performance, stronger reliability, increased conversion, and greater developer productivity. Partner with Solutions Architects to understand customer architecture, uncover new workloads, and guide technical evaluations. Mobilize Developer Success, Product, Marketing, and executive resources at the right moments to advance customer outcomes. Maintain accurate forecasts, opportunity plans, renewal timelines, and customer context in Vercel’s systems. Use AI, automation, and repeatable workflows to improve account research, prioritization, and execution. Share successful plays, customer insights, and market feedback to help the team improve. About You: We are looking for a commercially driven Account Executive who combines strong sales fundamentals with technical curiosity and a commitment to customer outcomes. In practice, this looks like someone who: Has a history of achieving revenue targets through pipeline generation, opportunity management, negotiation, and closing. Has experience owning and growing existing customer relationships, including renewals and expansion. Proactively creates pipeline rather than relying primarily on inbound demand. Can develop a point of view on an account and turn thoughtful hypotheses into customer conversations. Builds credibility with technical, executive, and commercial stakeholders. Learns complex products quickly and is curious about how modern web and AI applications are built and scaled. Can translate technical capabilities into clear business value. Is comfortable navigating ambiguity, complex organizations, and multi-stakeholder sales cycles. Demonstrates strong account planning, forecasting, and opportunity management discipline. Uses data, customer signals, and judgment to prioritize time effectively. Is coachable, collaborative, proactive, and team-first. Is resilient and energized by complex commercial problems. Bonus If You: Have experience growing existing customers in SaaS, cloud, infrastructure, developer tools, or a product-led growth company. Have sold to developers, engineering leaders, product teams, or other technical buyers. Have led expansions across multiple workloads, teams, or business units. Have worked with customers navigating cloud modernization, AI adoption, or application development. Have experience in a high-growth environment where go-to-market motions were still evolving. Use tools such as Salesforce, Gong, Outreach, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, or ZoomInfo. Use AI, automation, or self-built workflows to improve your effectiveness. Aspire to coach others, own increasingly complex accounts, or grow into leadership. Benefits: Competitive compensation package, including equity. Inclusive Healthcare Package. Learn and Grow - we provide mentorship and send you to events that help you build your network and skills. Flexible Time Off.
