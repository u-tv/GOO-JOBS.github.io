Robinhood’s International Expansion team builds and scales customer-facing experiences for international markets across iOS, Android, web, and backend. The team is product-agnostic and helps bring Robinhood’s Brokerage, Money, and Crypto products to new markets, partnering closely on onboarding flows, QA, launch readiness, and region-specific execution. As an iOS Engineer on the International Expansion team, you would build and improve iOS experiences that help bring Robinhood’s products to customers in international markets. You would contribute to launches across Brokerage, Money, and Crypto, helping deliver high-quality mobile experiences for new regions while working as part of a cross-functional team spanning mobile, web, and backend engineering. This role is based in our Toronto, ON office, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week. At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.
