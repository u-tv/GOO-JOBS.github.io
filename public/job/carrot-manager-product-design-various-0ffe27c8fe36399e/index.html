Carrot: Manager, Product Design

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote About Carrot: Carrot is the leading global fertility and family care platform, built on intelligent care orchestration: the right clinical guidance, at the right moment, in the context of each member’s life. More than a thousand multinational employers, health plans, and health systems trust Carrot to support millions of members across 195 countries – from pre-pregnancy through menopause and major life moments in between. Carrot's comprehensive clinical program delivers industry-leading cost savings for plan sponsors and award-winning experiences and improved outcomes for millions of people worldwide. Carrot is widely regarded as a defining force in healthcare innovation as a recipient of several top-tier awards, including Fast Company's 'Most Innovative Companies' and CNBC's '100 Barrier Breaking Startups'. The company is regularly cited by leading global outlets — including The Economist, Bloomberg, The Wall Street Journal, NPR, ABC News, and Harvard Business Review — as a leading voice on digital health, the future of work, and family health. Learn more at get-carrot.com . The Opportunity Carrot’s User Experience team is seeking a Manager, Product Design to lead a team of senior product designers building our member-facing products. You’ll lead project work, raise the craft bar through direct feedback, and help our design function evolve as AI tools reshape the boundaries between design, product management, and engineering. You’ll partner with Content Design, Product Management, and Engineering leaders to deliver products that scale our impact to members worldwide. This is a true player-coach role: roughly half your time goes to hands-on design work, including design system and foundational direction, and half to leading and coaching the team. If you love sharpening experienced designers’ work, designing for a diverse set of member needs across all paths to parenthood, and shaping how a design team works in an AI-enabled future, this role is for you. What You’ll Own In this role, you will: Lead design initiatives from strategy to launch, ensuring work meets project goals, timelines, and business priorities. Give clear, actionable craft feedback through critiques and design reviews, holding a high bar for quality, consistency, and accessibility. Contribute hands-on design, prototypes, or direction on complex, high-visibility initiatives, including design system and foundational work. Coach designers to plan and run their own research, from study design through synthesis, and ensure qualitative and quantitative evidence shapes design decisions. Partner with Product and Engineering leaders on goals, scope, and delivery, and communicate the team’s results and design rationale to senior stakeholders. Drive adoption of AI tools that help designers work faster, more consistently, and with greater capacity, and evaluate what works. Help redefine how design works with product and engineering as role boundaries shift, and recommend process changes to the product development lifecycle. Hold regular 1:1s and coach designers on craft, influence, and career growth, tied to Carrot’s leveling framework. Own performance management, including goal-setting, reviews, and calibration input, and address gaps directly and fairly. Build an inclusive, psychologically safe culture where designers feel ownership of their work. What Success Looks Like In your first 12–18 months, you will have: Delivered design work on high-visibility initiatives that met project goals, timelines, and business priorities. Established a critique and design review rhythm that has visibly raised the quality, consistency, and accessibility of what the team ships. Introduced AI tools and practices that improved the team’s speed, consistency, or capacity, and helped clarify how design works with product and engineering as roles shift. Built a research practice in which designers plan and run their own studies, and evidence shapes design decisions. Coached each designer with clear goals, regular feedback, and a development path tied to Carrot’s leveling framework, and built a team culture where designers feel ownership of their work. About You You likely have: A Bachelor’s degree 10+ years in product design or UX/UI, including 2+ years managing designers. A portfolio of shipped, user-centered, member- or consumer-facing digital products, with evidence of leading a team’s output. A track record of coaching experienced designers, giving direct craft feedback, and handling performance conversations. Hands-on experience using AI tools to prototype and ship design work, and helping a team adapt its practices as roles evolve. Experience evolving a design system in collaboration with engineers, ideally across multiple products. Strong knowledge of interaction design, visual design, WCAG accessibility, and responsive and mobile design. Experience planning and running user research, from interviews and usability testing to quantitative analysis, and coaching designers to do the same. Excellent communication skills, including influencing senior stakeholders and articulating design rationale. Comfort with ambiguity and change in a fast-paced agile environment, with clear trade-off decisions. Nice to Have ⭐ While not required, we would be especially excited if you bring: Experience designing configuration-driven products, where rules and settings (such as eligibility or plan rules) determine what each person sees. Familiarity with the healthcare industry, insurance, finance, and compliance, alongside sensitivity to the challenges of fertility health and family forming around the world. Familiarity with front-end development principles (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) to collaborate effectively with engineers. Experience managing distributed or global teams across time zones. Demonstrated track record with strategic headcount allocation, resource budgeting, and external partner management. Why This Role Matters Carrot’s members come to us at some of the most personal and consequential moments of their lives, across every path to parenthood and lifelong fertility healthcare. The quality of our design shapes whether they feel understood, supported, and able to get the care they need. At the same time, the way design teams work is changing quickly. You’ll help define how a senior team adopts AI tools, how design works alongside product and engineering as role boundaries shift, and what great craft looks like in that environment, while staying close to the work yourself. Interview Process We believe in a thoughtful and transparent interview process designed to create alignment for both candidates and our team. For this role, candidates can expect: Recruiter Screen — 30 minutes Hiring Manager Interview — 30 minutes Portfolio Review Interview— 60 minutes Executive Leadership Conversation — 30 minutes Throughout the process, we will share additional context about the team, business priorities, customer landscape, and what success looks like in this role. Why Candidates Join Carrot Carrot offers the opportunity to do meaningful work in service of a deeply human mission. Highlights include: A mission-driven company focused on expanding access to fertility and family-forming care globally. A high-growth environment where leaders are empowered to shape strategy and drive impact. Competitive compensation, equity, and comprehensive benefits. Flexible Time Off and a culture that actively encourages employees to recharge. Comprehensive parental leave and family-forming benefits. A collaborative, inclusive, and values-driven culture. The opportunity to work alongside passionate, thoughtful teammates committed to improving healthcare outcomes worldwide. Compensation Carrot offers a holistic Total Rewards package designed to support our employees in all aspects of their life inside and outside of work, including health and wellness benefits, retirement savings plans, short- and long-term incentives, parental leave, family-forming assistance, and a competitive compensation package. Compensation for this role is structured as base salary plus equity, giving you ownership in Carrot’s long-term growth. This role does not include an annual bonus or variable compensation. Base Salary Range: $162,000- $190,000 Geo-Tier Adjustment: Up to $200,000 for highly experienced candidates within 50 miles of San Francisco, NYC, or Seattle.
