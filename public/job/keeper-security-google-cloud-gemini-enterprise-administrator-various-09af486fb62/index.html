Keeper Security: Google Cloud & Gemini Enterprise Administrator

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote, US Description Keeper Security is hiring a Senior Google Cloud & Gemini Enterprise Administrator to join our IT organization and manage Keeper’s Google Cloud, Google Workspace and Gemini Enterprise environments. This role offers 100% remote work. Keeper’s cybersecurity software is trusted by millions of people and thousands of organizations globally. Keeper is published in 23 languages and sold in over 150 countries. Join one of the fastest-growing cybersecurity companies and help securely scale the cloud and AI platforms used across Keeper’s global workforce. About Keeper Keeper Security is one of the fastest-growing cybersecurity software companies that protects thousands of organizations and millions of people in over 150 countries. Keeper is a pioneer of zero-knowledge and zero-trust security built for any IT environment. Its core offering, KeeperPAM®, is an AI-enabled, cloud-native platform that protects all users, devices and infrastructure from cyber attacks. Recognized for its innovation in the Gartner Magic Quadrant for Privileged Access Management (PAM), Keeper secures passwords and passkeys, infrastructure secrets, remote connections and endpoints with role-based enforcement policies, least privilege and just-in-time access. Learn why Keeper is trusted by leading organizations to defend against modern adversaries at KeeperSecurity.com. About the Job The Google Cloud & Gemini Enterprise Administrator will report to the Director of IT and serve as a key technical resource for Keeper’s Google Cloud and enterprise AI environments. You will administer Google Cloud projects, identity and access controls, Gemini Enterprise licensing and configuration, enterprise data integrations and related Google Workspace services. You will work closely with IT, Information Security, Compliance and business stakeholders to ensure Google and Gemini services are secure, scalable and configured according to enterprise governance requirements. This role requires strong hands-on Google administration experience along with an understanding of identity, security, SaaS administration and emerging enterprise AI technologies.
