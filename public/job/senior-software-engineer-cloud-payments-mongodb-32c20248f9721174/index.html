Senior Software Engineer, Cloud Payments

🏢 mongodb 📍 Toronto; Vancouver 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

MongoDB’s mission is to empower innovators to create, transform and disrupt industries by unleashing the power of software and data. Cloud Payments helps make that possible by ensuring that usage of MongoDB Atlas and our growing suite of products becomes accurate, reliable revenue every day. Our systems support the financial foundation of MongoDB’s cloud business, processing large volumes of usage and billing data where correctness, resilience and auditability matter. About the Cloud Payments team Cloud Payments is part of the Cloud Billing organization. As MongoDB’s cloud business grows, billing and payments become increasingly mission-critical. Our team: Ingests and aggregates detailed metering and usage data from internal services and major cloud providers, including AWS, Azure and GCP Calculates and generates daily customer bills for millions of customers worldwide Integrates with payment processors and internal financial systems that support Finance, Sales, Marketing and Accounting Improves customer-facing billing and payment experiences, as well as the reliability and scalability of backend systems We care deeply about code quality, correctness and observability. We work closely with technical teams and business stakeholders to solve problems that have direct customer and business impact. The role We’re looking for a Senior Software Engineer to provide technical leadership while designing, building and operating the services that power Cloud Payments. You’ll take on complex, ambiguous problems, shape solutions across service boundaries and help the team make sound engineering decisions for systems that process high-volume, financially significant data. You’ll work primarily in the JVM ecosystem, including Java and contribute across the software lifecycle: from clarifying the problem and setting technical direction through implementation, rollout and ongoing operation. You’ll raise the quality and effectiveness of the team through design feedback, mentorship and thoughtful collaboration, without needing formal people-management responsibility. This role can be based out of one of our Canadian offices, or remotely within Canada. What you’ll do Lead the technical design and delivery of substantial backend capabilities, from problem definition and requirements through launch, operation and iteration Break down ambiguous business and customer needs into pragmatic, well-scoped technical plans; identify risks, dependencies and meaningful tradeoffs early Design and evolve reliable, scalable services and data flows that ingest, process, reconcile and report large volumes of usage and billing data Set a high bar for correctness, resilience, security, auditability, testability and operability in systems with financial impact Guide architecture and implementation across services or teams, building alignment with engineering partners and stakeholders in Product, Finance, Revenue and other organizations Make systems easier and safer to operate by improving observability, incident response, automation and operational practices Raise engineering quality through design and code reviews, clear technical documentation and constructive feedback Mentor and support other engineers, sharing context and helping teammates grow their technical judgment and ownership Participate in the on-call rotation and contribute to a sustainable approach to reliability What we’re looking for Significant experience designing, building and operating production backend systems in a compiled OOM language, ideally Java or another JVM language - 6+ years of relevant experience Demonstrated ownership of complex projects, including making design decisions, managing delivery risks and following through on launches Strong understanding of distributed systems and the practical challenges of availability, consistency, scalability and failure recovery Experience designing for data correctness, including patterns such as idempotency, replay safety, reconciliation and auditability Ability to reason about service boundaries, APIs, data models and system evolution over time Experience with databases, data modeling and access patterns in production Strong operational bias: you use observability and production feedback to improve systems and you are willing to not only participate in on-call, but improve operational stance of the team Clear communication and a track record of influencing technical decisions across teams and with non-technical stakeholders A collaborative approach to technical leadership, including mentoring others and improving the team’s engineering practices Self-motivation to solve customer and business problems, not just deliver isolated tickets Nice to have Experience with billing, payments, or other financial systems where correctness, auditability and compliance are important Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS, GCP or Azure and communication protocols such as gRPC Familiarity with data-intensive applications, data pipelines, or data lakes Exposure to I/O frameworks such as Reactor Experience with containerization and orchestration, such as Docker and Kubernetes Prior experience developing with or operating MongoDB We don’t expect you to check every box. If you’re excited about the problem space and believe you could make a strong contribution, we’d love to hear from you.
