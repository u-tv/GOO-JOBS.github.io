Pinterest is looking for a Senior Corporate Communications Manager to help shape how our business, strategy and growth are understood by key external audiences. This person will play a central role in telling Pinterest’s corporate story across business, financial and advertiser audiences, while helping protect and strengthen the company’s reputation during high-stakes moments. This person will be a trusted thought partner and hands-on operator who can translate complex business priorities into clear, compelling narratives and lead execution across proactive and reactive communications programs. What you’ll do: Develop and execute communications strategies that advance Pinterest’s corporate reputation and support key business priorities across corporate and financial storytelling. This includes the development and execution communications related to financial reporting, earnings, 8Ks, M&A, and other business critical events. Partner closely with Finance, Investor Relations, and Legal teams to shape narratives tied to company performance, growth priorities and strategic milestones. Draft and edit high-impact communications materials, including press releases, messaging frameworks, Q&As, briefing documents, talking points, and executive materials Build and maintain strong relationships with business, financial and trade media, and identify opportunities to proactively shape coverage and broader market understanding. Counsel cross-functional, senior stakeholders and executive team on communications risks, opportunities and timing, balancing multiple priorities with sound judgment and speed. Support issues management and crisis communications related to corporate and financial, helping the team respond quickly and thoughtfully in high-stakes situations. Translate complex product, business and financial developments into clear, differentiated stories that break through with external audiences. Use AI-enabled tools and workflows to improve research, monitoring, briefing development and communications execution, while applying strong judgment and maintaining accountability for final outputs. What we’re looking for: 10+ years of experience in corporate communications, public relations or a related field. Strong media instincts and a track record of building relationships with business, financial and trade press. Excellent writing and editing skills, with experience producing polished, high-quality materials for executives and external audiences. Experience using AI tools to improve the speed, quality and insight of communications work, paired with strong judgment and accountability for accuracy. Bachelor’s degree in a relevant field such as Communications, Public Relations, Journalism or equivalent experience. Preferred qualifications: Experience working in-house at a global, publicly traded technology or platform company. Experience supporting communications across multiple business lines simultaneously. Experience with advertising, monetization or broader B2B platform storytelling. Familiarity with policy, trust & safety or regulatory communications in a complex corporate environment.
