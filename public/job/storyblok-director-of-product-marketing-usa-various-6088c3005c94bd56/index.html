Storyblok: Director of Product Marketing - USA

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote Storyblok is a headless CMS that enables marketers and developers to create with joy and publish with confidence. It empowers you to deliver structured and consistent content everywhere: websites, apps, AI search, and beyond. Legendary brands like Virgin Media O2, Oatly, and TomTom use Storyblok to make a bigger, faster market impact. WHAT IS IN IT FOR YOU You will be joining a growing company where you can contribute to many “firsts”. Plus these benefits: Monthly remote work stipend (home internet costs, electricity). Home office equipment package right at the start (laptop, keyboard, monitor…) Home office equipment upgrade (furniture, ear plugs …) or membership to a local co-working space after your onboarding Sick leave benefit, parental leave and 25 days of annual leave plus your local national holidays Personal development fund for courses, books, conferences, and material The annual international team-building trip, quarterly and monthly online get-togethers As a fully remote company, with work-life balance at its core, you’ll enjoy flexible schedules An international team that loves to have fun at work and works hard together to accomplish shared goals JOB SUMMARY As the Director of Product Marketing at Storyblok, We're looking for a Product Marketing Director to shape how we position, launch, and grow our product in the market- particularly in the US, UK and EMEA. This is a senior, hands-on role for someone who can translate complex technical capability into compelling, credible narratives — and who's comfortable in the room with engineers as much as with executives. You'll own the connective tissue between Product, Engineering, Sales, and Marketing at a stage where positioning decisions have outsized impact on growth. You will guide a talented team, focusing on sales enablement, website conversion, feature adoption, and Storyblok’s market positioning with industry analysts and customer review platforms. A key responsibility is to elevate our product marketing function to influence revenue, customer acquisition, and market leadership. Your mission is to enable Storyblok to win in the market by clearly articulating our value, differentiation, and future direction. What you’ll bring: You're genuinely comfortable operating in technical territory, not just translating it secondhand. You can read API documentation, understand headless CMS architecture, and hold a credible, detailed conversation with engineers and technical evaluators about how the product actually works — its components, integration points, and trade-offs versus alternative approaches. This fluency shows up in the quality of your messaging: you write positioning that developers trust because it's accurate, not just persuasive. You've likely worked hands-on with developers before, whether as a technical product marketer, solutions engineer, technical product manager, or in an engineering-adjacent role, and you know how to earn credibility with a technical audience rather than assume it. ESSENTIAL JOB FUNCTIONS Product Positioning & Messaging Own and evolve our core messaging and differentiated value proposition across all audiences (developers, marketers, partners, enterprise buyers), setting the strategic framework the wider team executes against Direct ongoing customer, market, and competitive research programs to ensure messaging stays relevant, differentiated, and current Set the standard for translating product capabilities and roadmap into solution-oriented, benefits-led messaging that resonates across the buyer journey Ensure messaging consistency and governance across all channels — website, campaigns, sales assets, analyst briefings, and customer communications Go-To-Market Strategy & Execution Own GTM strategy for major product and feature launches, driving alignment across Product, Growth, Sales, Customer Success, and Content leadership Define phased rollout strategies that drive awareness, adoption, and revenue impact across key customer segments Set the standard for launch toolkits — messaging frameworks, FAQs, competitive positioning, product one-pagers, internal training — and build the team capability to produce them at scale Establish the measurement framework for launch performance and adoption, using it to continuously improve release execution Sales Enablement & Sales Plays Build a structured sales playbook program aligned to product capabilities, customer pain points, and vertical use cases. Equip revenue teams with high-impact content (battlecards, ROI calculators, solution briefs, demo scripts, objection handling). Partner directly with Sales Leadership on rep onboarding, conversion effectiveness, and keeping messaging aligned to evolving sales motions Use sales performance data, win/loss interviews, and customer feedback to identify and prioritize enablement investments Analyst & Review Site Strategy Define and lead the analyst relations strategy to secure meaningful participation in key analyst evaluations (e.g., Gartner Magic Quadrant, Forrester Wave, IDC MarketScape). Orchestrate analyst briefing cycles, submission processes, and relationships that enhance Storyblok’s visibility and influence. Own our presence and growth strategy on review platforms (G2, Gartner Peer Insights), including review acquisition and profile optimization Establish ongoing monitoring and response mechanisms for public reviews and rankings Website & CRO Performance Partner with Growth Marketing and Web leadership to ensure product positioning is clearly and consistently represented site-wide Set priorities for CRO initiatives that improve engagement, conversion, and self-serve funnel performance Define messaging strategy and success metrics for key product pages, feature highlights, and trial flows Direct ongoing testing and optimization of messaging, CTAs, layouts, and journey flows based on data Lead, mentor, and inspire the Product Marketing team to deliver high-impact outcomes aligned with company goals. Set team structure, priorities, and ways of working in partnership with marketing leadership Coach the team on messaging craft, technical fluency, and cross-functional collaboration Foster a culture of ownership, curiosity, experimentation, and continuous learning. Develop individual growth plans, conduct regular performance reviews, and ensure team members have access to relevant coaching and training opportunities. Ensure strong collaboration across Product, Growth, Brand, and Sales teams to drive alignment and accountability. EDUCATION AND EXPERIENCE 8+ years of experience in Product Marketing, with at least 2+ years in a leadership role. Proven experience in a fast-growing B2B SaaS environment. Strong understanding of PLG motions, SaaS business models, and the product lifecycle. Demonstrated success in GTM execution, analyst engagement, and pricing strategy. Exceptional communication skills and executive presence. Skilled in cross-functional collaboration and stakeholder influence. Familiarity with tools like Salesforce, Marketing Automation, G2, and PMM platforms (e.g., Klue) a plus. Demonstrates AI literacy, with the ability to leverage AI tools to enhance productivity, decision-making, and quality of work while applying sound judgment and critical thinking.
