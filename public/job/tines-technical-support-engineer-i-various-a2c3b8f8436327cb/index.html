Tines: Technical Support Engineer I

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: United States - East (Remote) Tines is the complete environment for enterprise AI, trusted by organizations like Reddit, SAP, Mars, Fin, and Headspace. Tines gives all teams a safe, scalable way to turn their expertise into software, whatever their technical background. Since its founding in 2018, Tines has enabled over 600 enterprise customers to run their most important work, where reliability and security aren't optional. From IT and business operations to security and beyond, Tines empowers teams to build apps, agents and automations to solve complex problems and bring their ideas to life. Founded by Eoin Hinchy and Thomas Kinsella and co-headquartered in Dublin and Boston, Tines is truly agnostic and helps organizations bring their entire technology stack – any model, any tool, any system, any infrastructure – together. At Tines, we're driven by our values of Simplicity, Speed, and Soundness. We're committed to delivering exceptional customer experiences while fostering a company culture that nurtures individual curiosity, growth, and integrity. We’re excited about what’s next, and we’re looking for others to join us on our journey. A Technical Support Engineer provides technical support and consultation to customers and colleagues. They are essential in troubleshooting and diagnosing technical issues, providing timely resolutions, and ensuring customer satisfaction. This position focuses on responding to, owning, and resolving users' technical queries. What you will be doing Handling technical inquiries received across multiple channels (Slack, in-app chat, and email), maintaining high standards for responsiveness and ticket hygiene Troubleshoot customer inquiries, including but not limited to API integrations, systems issues, network and security-related impediments, 3rd party integrations, and multi-product workflows Resolve product or service problems by exploring the client's request, determining the cause of the problem, and selecting and explaining the best solution to solve the problem Interpreting API documents and creating Tines workflows Configuring and implementing authentication types like OAuth and JWT Documenting the steps needed to connect to new tools or document any new workflows Building, troubleshooting, documenting, and modifying workflows on remote sessions with customers Building strong relationships with other team members within Support and the wider company, acting as the voice of the customer, and maintaining continuity with the team's priorities and open issues despite working outside standard team hours for part of the week Contribute to Customer Support resources and deliver meaningful product feedback as the voice of the customer to leadership and relevant internal teams Proactively identifying improvements to the product, bugs, and otherwise determining high-impact opportunities to enhance the customer experience Writing clear, structured handover notes at the start and end of each shift so incoming and outgoing regional teams have full context with no gaps Making independent judgment calls on prioritization, troubleshooting approach, and escalation while working solo during unsupervised weekend hours, including recognizing when an issue requires paging an on-call engineer or escalating outside normal hours What you will bring to the role 2+ years of technical support experience within a SaaS environment, ideally in automation or a related field Practical experience constructing, testing, and debugging REST and GraphQL API requests, using developer documentation and SDK examples to understand and troubleshoot integrations Exposure to implementing or troubleshooting AI actions and agentic workflows, including LLM APIs, prompt structures, and protocols such as MCP Experience troubleshooting in a web-based environment, including HTTP, SSL, and basic networking Excellent communication, analytical, and troubleshooting skills, including clear written communication for handover documentation given the reliance on asynchronous handoffs with other regional shifts Ability to communicate complex technical concepts to non-technical and technical stakeholders alike Experience handling inbound technical customer requests in various form channels (chat, email) A proven track record of customer empathy, urgency, and technical competency Self-motivated and goal-oriented with excellent organizational and time management skills and a consistent quality bar whether working alongside the team or solo Familiarity with at least one modern programming language, such as Python, TypeScript, or Ruby Familiarity with query languages such as SQL, and GraphQL Demonstrated ability to work autonomously, make sound decisions without direct supervision, and exercise good judgment on when to escalate versus solve independently Comfort with ambiguity and self-directed problem-solving, including researching solutions independently using documentation, runbooks, and prior tickets Resilience and composure when handling difficult technical or customer issues without immediate backup or a second opinion Ability to context-switch effectively between collaborative, team-based work (weekdays) and independent, self-directed work (weekends) without losing continuity Desirable Experience An understanding of computer networking principles (Firewalls, Network Protocols, Subnetting) Experience supporting customers using AI and experience with leveraging AI to increase professional effectiveness Tines certification Familiarity with Docker containers, Postgres, Redis/Valkey, load balancers, and cloud orchestration platforms (AWS, Azure, GKE,
