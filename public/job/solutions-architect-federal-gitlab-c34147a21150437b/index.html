Solutions Architect - Public Sector The role A trusted advisor to U.S. government customers, helping them understand how GitLab’s DevSecOps platform addresses business and mission outcomes. Owns the technical evaluation and validation stages of the sales cycle and provides technical guidance from first conversation through successful deployment. What you’ll do - Engage government customers, onsite and remotely, with tailored demos, technical presentations and hands-on workshops throughout the pre-sales process. - Manage the technical aspects of the sales cycle, including high quality presentations, custom demos, proofs of concept, technical deep-dive sessions and workshops, RFI and RFP responses, and differentiating GitLab from alternatives. - Be the directly responsible individual (DRI) for technical evaluations and success plans, including POC/POV ownership, tender and audit support and technical input into proposals, capturing success criteria and handoff details for a smooth path to deployment and adoption. - Partner with the sales team to shape and execute account strategies that drive GitLab adoption within government environments. - Build deep relationships with technical and business stakeholders so they become advocates for GitLab within their organizations. - Run Value Stream Assessments to identify opportunities for customers to improve their software delivery, with support from a Senior SA. - Take part in conferences and trade shows, and contribute to case studies, white papers and media articles for government customers and partners. - Deliver world-class customer service during pre-sales, implementation and post-sales activities. - Channel customer feedback internally to influence roadmap and messaging, and keep building your expertise in GitLab and related technologies. What you’ll bring - Consumption experience: Independently owning a straightforward consumption-led technical motion with the AE in government environments, from usage-led discovery and technical POV through an adoption hypothesis and clear next steps toward production. - Technical experience: Hands-on experience with Git, CI/CD pipelines and containers, and working knowledge of on-premises, self-managed and restricted-network environments alongside cloud, including installation, configuration, upgrades and integration with existing enterprise tooling. - Experience supporting federal customers through technical evaluations, solution design and the path to authorization. - Experience with technical pre-sales, or as a professional in the field of information technology. - Experience with cloud computing platforms and related technologies in regulated or security conscious environments.Familiarity with federal security and compliance frameworks, such as FedRAMP, DoD Impact Levels, NIST 800-53 and the Risk Management Framework, DISA STIGs and FIPS 140, and how they shape deployment and tooling decisions. - The ability to connect technical solutions to measurable business or mission outcomes and to advise senior stakeholders on trade-offs and options. - Strong, confident communication and relationship building skills, including leading customer meetings, demos and value-focused conversations. - TS/SCI security clearance is a plus, but not a requirement. - Location in the Washington, DC metro area. - Openness to learning, seeking feedback and building new skills. - Ability to travel at least 25%. About the team The Public Sector Solutions Architecture team sits within GitLab Sales and works with U.S. federal and other government customers and partners to show how GitLab’s DevSecOps platform supports mission outcomes in regulated, security conscious environments. We work alongside the Public Sector sales team through every stage of the cycle, from first conversation and RFI or RFP responses to deployment and adoption. GitLab sells in a consumption-based model, so our work does not end at the proof of value. We stay close after the technical win to help agencies move from evaluation to production use and growth.
