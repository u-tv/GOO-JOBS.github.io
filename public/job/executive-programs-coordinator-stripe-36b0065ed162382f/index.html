Executive Programs Coordinator

🏢 stripe 📍 Remote in the US 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies - from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups - use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team What you’ll do We’re looking for a highly detail-oriented and obsessively organized contractor to join our Executive Programs team as a Project Manager. In this role, you’ll play a key part in developing and executing our executive events, including Stripe’s Customer Advisory Board (CAB), and our partnership with the World Economic Forum including the Annual Meeting in Davos. You’ll manage the planning of program sessions for our strategic users, oversee calendars for kick-off meetings and dry runs, and coordinate timelines across cross-functional stakeholder teams. Your clear and polished communication style and strong project management skills will help us create world-class experiences for our external and internal executive stakeholders and audiences. Responsibilities Project management: Manage project timelines in Google spreadsheets across multiple stakeholders for session development, content reviews, and general event execution tasks, ensuring project timelines stay on track, and the right stakeholders are engaged Create and manage calendar holds and comms with cross-functional teams, including Stripe executives, Product, Product Marketing, Executive Marketing, Sales, and Customer Success, to deliver clarity and ensure timely project delivery with input and alignment throughout Update timeline changes across all tracking and ensure downstream impacts are captured and communicated, keeping all stakeholders informed and focused on revised goals Proactively manage updates across briefs, project trackers, and program summaries, ensuring meticulous coordination of details and accuracy across all tools Optimize project management processes and tools, identifying areas for improvement and proactively implementing solutions to enhance efficiency Stakeholder management: Partner closely with Administrative Business Partners to coordinate calendars for Stripe executives’ participation in programs and internal reviews Provide clear, timely communication with cross-functional team members to align on project milestones, track progress, and achieve outcomes Build upon learnings by optimizing communications to standardize the delivery of information across calendars, emails, and briefing documents for an executive audience Event asset design management: Work with the Program Manager to oversee the development of design briefs and trackers, effectively collaborating with design partners and agencies throughout the design process Ensure all materials and presentations are prepared on time, polished, and aligned with Stripe’s messaging and branding Assist the Program Manager in creating briefs in Google Slides and Google Docs for participating Stripe executives, maintaining a keen attention to detail and a commitment to Stripe’s core principles of craft and beauty, including thorough quality assurance of all written content Who you are We're looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement. Minimum requirements Meticulous attention to detail, with a demonstrated ability to juggle multiple priorities while meeting deadlines and maintaining high-quality work Proven background in event and project management within a corporate environment, preferably in tech or product-driven organizations (previous experience with executive audiences a plus) Background in event management or an executive support role Strong understanding of cross-functional collaboration, with a proven ability to quickly establish rapport and communicate effectively with diverse teams Intermediate proficiency in Google Suite (e.g., Google Calendar, Google Slides, Spreadsheets) for design of internal briefs, project management timelines, and to use generally across all stages of planning and execution (experience with AI tools a plus) Exceptional verbal and written communication skills, with experience communicating to an executive audience and a commitment to precision and accuracy in all written materials
