About this team and role: At Mozilla, our Release Engineering team (RelEng) plays a crucial role in ensuring the seamless and reliable delivery of our world-class software products. As a key part of our engineering infrastructure, RelEng is responsible for the continuous deployment of our products, ensuring they reach our users efficiently and without interruption. For instance, we can reliably ship a security fix to > 200M users across different platforms and > 100 locales in under 24 hours. RelEng has an expertise in configuring and optimizing our build and deployment pipeline. In order to provide a faster and more reliable experience for Firefox developers, RelEng is looking for an engineer who can help optimize and maintain the pipeline. If you are passionate about release automation, the Release Engineering team at Mozilla is the place for you. Join us and be a part of a team that is making a significant impact on the web and beyond. What you’ll do: Write software that scales - tens of thousands of tasks are run to build, test, and release Firefox every single day Collaborate with senior+ engineers to improve the developers’ experience, then implement the improvements. Work with a geographically-distributed development team between North America and Europe Collaborate with others by requesting and providing code reviews Respond to critical service outages (during normal working hours - no on call rotation is required) What you’ll bring: You have a Bachelor's degree in computer science (or related programs) or equivalent work experience You have a minimum of 2 years of experience working in an object-oriented programming (OOP) language (preferably Python) You are passionate about CI/CD pipelines You have some previous experience on using cloud resources (e.g. on AWS, GCP, or Azure) You feel comfortable developing in Windows, macOS, and Linux environments, experience with shell scripting You have worked on a team distributed across time zones You enjoy getting familiar and improving existing code bases and tools Commitment to our values: Welcoming differences Being relationship-minded Practicing responsible participation Having grit What you’ll get: Generous performance-based bonus plans to all eligible employees - we share in our success as one team Rich medical, dental, and vision coverage Generous retirement contributions with 100% immediate vesting (regardless of whether you contribute) Quarterly all-company wellness days where everyone takes a pause together Country specific holidays plus a day off for your birthday One-time home office stipend Annual professional development budget Quarterly well-being stipend Considerable paid parental leave Employee referral bonus program Other benefits (life/AD&D, disability, EAP, etc. - varies by country) About Mozilla Mozilla exists to build the Internet as a public resource accessible to all because we believe that open and free is better than closed and controlled.
