Airbnb was born in 2007 when two hosts welcomed three guests to their San Francisco home, and has since grown to over 5 million hosts who have welcomed over 2 billion guest arrivals in almost every country across the globe. Every day, hosts offer unique stays and experiences that make it possible for guests to connect with communities in a more authentic way. The Community You Will Join: The world is changing how it works; not just in where we do it, but in how fast and how well we can do it. Airbnb has already shown that we can move with speed and intention. We navigated a pandemic, rebuilt our company from the ground up, had a successful IPO, and shipped product upgrades – all while working remotely. That experience didn't just change where we work. It changed what we believe is possible. Now, a new shift is underway. AI is fundamentally changing the leverage available to every team – compressing timelines, breaking down traditional job description silos, raising the ceiling on quality, and enabling small, focused groups to accomplish what once required much larger ones. At Airbnb, we are not treating AI as a future consideration. We are moving fast to embed it into how we operate today – using it to multiply the impact of every employee, every system, and decisions we make every day. The companies and teams that embrace this shift with urgency and discipline will define what's possible in the years ahead. We intend to be one of them. Collaboration Products The Collaboration Products team owns the enterprise systems that Airbnb's workforce relies on to plan, build, and deliver — including our Atlassian ecosystem, AI-powered tooling integrations, and custom-built engineering platforms. Our mission goes beyond keeping the lights on. We are moving fast to modernize our stack, retire technical debt, and integrate AI capabilities that directly multiply the productivity and quality of the teams we serve. We build with urgency, operate with rigor, and treat every system we touch as an opportunity to raise the bar on what BizTech delivers to Airbnb. The Difference You Will Make: The Senior Systems Engineer is a hands-on technical leader who moves fast, builds to last, and uses AI as a force multiplier — not a future initiative. In this role, you will own the configuration, migration, and continuous improvement of Airbnb's Atlassian platforms, including leading the active deprecation of Jira Data Center and full transition to Jira Cloud. But your impact goes beyond administration. You will identify where AI and automation can compress delivery cycles, reduce toil, and improve the quality and reliability of the systems our workforce depends on — and then build those improvements, quickly. You bring the platform depth to architect scalable solutions and the judgment to know when to move fast and when to be deliberate. You are the kind of engineer who raises the velocity and quality of everyone around you.
