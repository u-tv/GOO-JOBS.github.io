Senior Product Manager - Quote to Cash

🏢 mongodb 📍 Palo Alto 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About the Role MongoDB is seeking a strategic and execution-oriented Senior Product Manager to own the strategy, roadmap, and delivery of Revenue and Order to Cash (OTC) capabilities that enable accurate, scalable, and efficient revenue operations. This role will work closely with the Group Product Manager, Quote to Cash (QTC), owning the OTC and revenue processes within the broader QTC organization, while partnering across Finance, Revenue Operations, Sales, Enterprise Architecture, Engineering, and Program Management to improve the end-to-end order-to-cash lifecycle — order management through billing, collections, revenue recognition, and close — and help the business scale with reliable, increasingly automated revenue systems. You will own product discovery, prioritization, requirements, delivery partnership, and launch readiness across the OTC and revenue lifecycle — including order management, subscription and contract operations, billing, accounts receivable, collections, renewals, revenue recognition, month-end and quarter-end close, and ERP integrations. The central focus of this role is revenue accuracy and timeliness: reducing manual adjustments, strengthening SOX and audit controls, accelerating time to invoice and cash, and building a foundation for continuous process improvement across the revenue lifecycle. What You'll Do Define and maintain the Revenue/OTC product vision, strategy, roadmap, and release priorities in alignment with company objectives, with revenue accuracy and close efficiency as the north star Partner closely with the Group Product Manager, QTC to align the OTC and revenue roadmap with the broader Quote to Cash strategy, while owning the order-to-cash and revenue processes as your dedicated area of focus Build deep expertise in NetSuite and the surrounding revenue systems landscape, including billing, accounts receivable, revenue recognition, month-end and quarter-end close, order management, contract and subscription data, ERP integrations, and related Finance and GTM systems Elicit, analyze, and document business and functional requirements across the OTC lifecycle; translate them into clear epics, user stories, acceptance criteria, and an actionable product backlog Lead prioritization across competing Finance, Revenue Operations, and Sales needs, balancing near-term business value with long-term scalability and architectural health of revenue systems Partner with Stakeholders, Lead PdM, Engineering, Architecture, TPM/Program Management, and implementation partners to evaluate and integrate strategic first- and third-party solutions for OTC and revenue processes Define success metrics and KPIs for Revenue/OTC initiatives — such as billing accuracy, days sales outstanding (DSO), manual journal entry volume, and close cycle time — using data and stakeholder feedback to validate assumptions and measure post-launch outcomes Own and improve end-to-end revenue workflows across order capture, billing, accounts receivable, collections, renewals, revenue recognition, month-end/quarter-end close, and reporting, including support for subscription and consumption-based business models Provide product oversight for revenue controls, data definitions, business logic, integrations, and the overall consistency of the user experience across OTC systems Coordinate integrated, cross-functional testing and partner with technology and business leads on release strategy, change management, training, and adoption for revenue system changes Identify opportunities to improve revenue accuracy, accelerate time to invoice and cash, reduce billing leakage and manual journal entries, strengthen auditability and SOX compliance, simplify close processes, and maximize the return on investment of Revenue/OTC products Communicate product decisions, tradeoffs, risks, dependencies, and progress clearly to stakeholders and executive audiences, with particular fluency in revenue and close-related metrics What We're Looking For Required Experience and Skills 8+ years of relevant product management experience, with a proven track record of delivering enterprise business systems and influencing stakeholders at all levels Strong experience with Order to Cash (OTC), Quote to Cash, or adjacent revenue and finance processes, with a
