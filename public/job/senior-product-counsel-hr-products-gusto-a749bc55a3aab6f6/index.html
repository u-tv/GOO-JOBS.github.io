Senior Product Counsel - HR Products

🏢 gusto 📍 Denver, CO - Hybrid; New York, NY - Hybrid; San Francisco, CA - Hybrid 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Gusto At Gusto, we're on a mission to grow the small business economy. We handle the hard stuff — payroll, health insurance, 401(k)s, and HR — so owners can focus on their craft and their customers. With teams in Denver, San Francisco, and New York, we support more than 500,000 small businesses nationwide and are building a workplace that reflects the people we serve. All full-time employees receive competitive base pay, benefits, and equity (RSUs) — because everyone who helps build Gusto should share in its success. Offer amounts are determined by role, level, and location. Learn more about our Total Rewards philosophy . AI is a fundamental part of how work gets done at Gusto. We expect all team members to actively engage with AI tools relevant to their role and grow their fluency as the technology evolves. AI experience requirements vary by role and will be assessed during the interview process. About the Role: We are seeking a senior, strategic attorney who will apply deep employment law expertise and sound judgment to help shape and scale tech-forward HR products and services for small businesses. This is a unique opportunity to serve as a senior legal partner across Gusto’s HR offerings, which span HR software platforms, fractional HR services, and our Professional Employer Organization (PEO), one of Gusto’s most strategic business lines. Depending on business needs, you may support any or all of these offerings. In this role, you will move fluidly between high-level strategic counsel and hands-on work across HR, payroll, wage and hour, and co-employment issues. You will be a trusted advisor to business, product, and operations leadership, helping the business make informed decisions by giving concise, risk-adjusted advice on tradeoffs, clearly expressed rationale, and separating what must be solved now from what can wait. Success in this role requires an ownership mindset, sound judgment, appropriate risk calibration, and excellent written and verbal communication skills. It also requires curiosity, collaboration, flexibility, and a good sense of humor. Attorneys are expected to use AI tools to accelerate research and processes, synthesize insights, and refine positioning and content with speed and precision, while maintaining accuracy and consistency with legal best practices. About the Team: Gusto’s Legal & Compliance team partners with product and engineering teams to enable innovation, facilitate responsible company growth, accelerate the Gusto roadmap, and provide compliant products to our customers through solution-focused counsel on legal and business risks. We are passionate about using groundbreaking technology and excellent service to elevate the experience of small business owners and their employees. Each team member takes real ownership of their work and makes a meaningful impact every day. Here’s what you’ll do day-to-day: Serve as a senior legal partner to business leadership, product, and operations teams across Gusto’s HR software and services offerings, giving strategic counsel and hands-on support. Lead complex, cross-functional Legal & Compliance efforts, from product and service launches and compliance frameworks to big-picture risk mitigation strategies and Go-to-Market readiness. Give precise, risk-adjusted advice that frames tradeoffs and recommended priorities so the business can make informed decisions. Build scalable frameworks, guidance, resources, and training that help the business move quickly and confidently. Manage escalations and sensitive issues, aligning stakeholders across Legal & Compliance, Product, Finance, Operations, and senior leadership. Improve compliance processes and work product across offerings as the business scales. Here’s what we're looking for: 10+ years of legal experience, including significant in-house experience counseling product, operations, or other cross-functional business teams on employment law issues. Experience with supporting HR technology and/or services teams is a plus, but not required, and we welcome candidates with demonstrated experience ramping quickly in complex, regulated areas and across different business models. Deep employment law expertise is required, including focused payroll and wage and hour experience, such as advising on wage and hour requirements, claims, or class action litigation. Strong preference for product counseling experience. Demonstrated ability to give concise, clear advice on complex tradeoffs, with sound judgment about acceptable risk and what to prioritize now versus later. Proven ability to influence senior stakeholders and build consensus as a creative partner whose advice is grounded in business goals, legal context, and comfort with ambiguity. Ability to operate at both the strategic and tactical level, with strong prioritization and the ability to drive projects to completion across multiple offerings at once. Experience using AI tools to deliver efficiency in your individual work and as essential components in the processes you develop and improve. JD degree and membership in a State bar with a current license to practice law. Our cash compensation range for this role is $200,000/yr to $245,000/yr in Denver, and $221,000/yr to $272,000/yr in San Francisco & New York.
