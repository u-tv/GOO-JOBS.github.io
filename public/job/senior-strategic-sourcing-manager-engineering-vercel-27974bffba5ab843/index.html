Senior Strategic Sourcing Manager - Engineering

🏢 vercel 📍 Hybrid - San Francisco 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Vercel: Vercel is the agentic infrastructure company, freeing people and agents to ship what's next. For more than a decade we've helped builders move from idea to production with speed, security, and exceptional developer experience. Now we're scaling our products for both agents and people to ship and run software, built in the open and trusted by OpenAI, PayPal, Ramp, Supreme, and millions of developers worldwide. About the Role: As a Senior Strategic Sourcing Manager, Engineering, you’ll own commercial outcomes for the largest supplier relationships behind Vercel’s Product and Engineering orgs. Vercel’s revenue and spend are scaling fast, and sourcing has to scale with them. Your main focus is declining unit costs, paired with deal structures that grow with Vercel. You’ll also build the AI-powered sourcing workflows that shape how this function runs as deal volume climbs. This is a hybrid role based in San Francisco, in the office on Monday, Tuesday, and Friday. What You Will Do: Own sourcing strategy and execution for a portfolio of technology categories, primarily cloud infrastructure and software, including RFPs and direct sourcing to keep a competitive bench in mission-critical categories Lead negotiations from deal structure through terms, SLAs, and executive alignment, and present each deal to the executive team for CEO approval Design tiered, committed-volume, and usage-based pricing that reflects how Vercel consumes vendor services Run vendor management for key suppliers: QBRs, scorecards, SLA enforcement, escalations, and renewals, moving off auto-renewals where they don’t serve Vercel Build AI-powered sourcing tools and playbooks with Claude and v0 for redlining, negotiation prep, benchmarking, and vendor reporting Keep close relationship with the Product team to understand what they are working on and how it may impact our buy vs build decisions going forward. About You: 8+ years of experience in technology sourcing or procurement Owned negotiations for major deals with cloud infrastructure providers or hyperscalers, observability platforms, and major software providers Built rate and unit-cost analyses, TCO models, and pricing models that exposed the real economics of a vendor proposal Run vendor management programs: SLAs, scorecards, QBRs, and escalations Negotiated directly with executives, both internally and across the table Rebuilt your own daily workflows around AI tools Bonus If You: Sourced AI/inference, data services, or technical services for an engineering-heavy organization Built or used AI-powered procurement tooling for negotiation prep, redlining, or benchmarking Sourced at a high-growth company Compensation & Benefits: Competitive compensation package, including equity. Inclusive Healthcare Package. Learn and Grow - we provide mentorship and send you to events that help you build your network and skills. Flexible Time Off.
