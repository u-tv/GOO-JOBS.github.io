CircleCI: Director, Engineering

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote UK, Remote Canada, Remote US CircleCI is the platform that powers continuous integration and delivery for the world's best engineering teams — helping them build, test, and ship software quickly, safely, and confidently, including the new generation of AI-assisted development. Do you want to lead the teams that set that example for customers? The Director, Engineering is a strategic leader who directs operational units across the engineering organization. You will own the technical strategy and multi-quarter roadmap for your engineering organization (typically 4-5 teams including engineering managers), collaborating on organizational engineering strategy and partnering across functions to deliver measurable business impact. This is a broad, high-impact seat — you'll partner closely with the VP of Engineering and peer leaders across the org to shape structure, roadmap, and operating standards for a group that's growing and evolving fast. This role balances technical depth with strategic thinking, people development with execution, and short-term delivery with long-term sustainability. You will work with Product, Go-to-Market teams, customers, and executive leadership to drive CircleCI's mission forward. Strategic Planning Your Strategic Responsibilities: Lead and grow a multi-team engineering organization, with ownership of its structure, headcount, and provide input for product strategy Partner with the VP of Engineering and cross-functional stakeholders on technical strategy and multi-quarter roadmap for your organization, ensuring alignment with company objectives. Raise the bar on engineering practices, operating standards, and delivery quality across the group Build and develop a strong management layer — hiring, coaching, and growing engineering managers and leads Define operating metrics and team goals; craft strategies to scale infrastructure and meet business objectives Cultivate a culture of excellence that closes the gap between business/roadmap commitments and what gets delivered Lead distributed engineering teams to deliver highly available, highly secure experiences at scale Bring real AI competency to the role — not just personal use of AI tools, but building the systems and practices that let your teams get genuine leverage from AI Represent your organization's roadmap, tradeoffs, and constraints clearly to Product and other cross-functional partners Your Decision-Making Authority: You have full authority to: Hire, promote, and performance-manage all engineers and managers within your organization Make final decisions on administrative and operational matters within your domain Approve technical architecture decisions that impact your organization alongside highly experienced senior engineers Prioritize and sequence engineering work; allocate budget for team tools, training, and resources Represent engineering in cross-functional forums and make commitments on behalf of your organization What We're Looking For This is a key role with significant impact on CircleCI's engineering organization and business outcomes. 5+ years in engineering leadership, including direct management of managers Ability to translate business objectives into technical strategy and enable teams to deliver reliable, resilient, and secure features at scale A proven track record of building strong, aligned teams across varied technical surfaces Thorough understanding of developer tools market, practices, competition, and trends Genuine fluency with AI-assisted engineering workflows and what it takes to operationalize them at a team level Expert manager with ability to mentor and develop individual contributors and managers; build high-performing teams through recruiting, coaching, and creating growth opportunities Drive frameworks for people development, performance, inclusion, and belonging across your organization Data-driven problem solver who handles risk and uncertainty; About CircleCI CircleCI is the world’s largest continuous integration/continuous delivery (CI/CD) platform, and the hub where code moves from idea to delivery. As one of the most-used DevOps tools - processing more than 3 million jobs a day - CircleCI has unique access to data on how the most effective engineering teams work, and the tools to help software companies successfully leverage the power of AI into their commercial applications. Companies like Hinge, HuggingFace, and Samsung use us to improve engineering team productivity, release better products, and get to market faster. Founded in 2011 and headquartered in downtown San Francisco with a global, remote workforce, CircleCI is venture-backed by Base10, Greenspring Associates, Eleven Prime, IVP, Sapphire Ventures, Top Tier Capital Partners, Baseline Ventures, Threshold Ventures, Scale Venture Partners, Owl Rock Capital, Next Equity Partners, Heavybit, and Harrison Metal Capital. We use technology to support our recruiting team so they can spend more time on what matters most: getting to know you. Where we may use AI Job descriptions and messaging : AI may help draft job descriptions and role-specific messaging so they are clear and accurate. Application review : For some roles, we use an AI tool to review applications, including resumes and application responses, against criteria our hiring team sets for the role. The tool generates a fit score and summary to help recruiters organize and prioritize applications. Interview notes : With advance notice, an AI note-taking tool may join interviews to record, transcribe, and summarize the conversation. This lets interviewers focus on talking with you. You will be told before each interview, and you may opt out at any stage by letting your recruiter know. If you opt out, the tool will not join, and your candidacy is not affected. Outreach : AI may help draft candidate outreach and scheduling messages, which our team reviews before sending. Our commitment AI does not make hiring decisions at CircleCI. Our recruiters and hiring teams review candidates and make every decision to advance or reject an application. No candidate is rejected based solely on an AI score. We have tools and processes in place to test for bias and to review how these tools perform, and we adjust our approach as AI technology and the laws that govern it change.
