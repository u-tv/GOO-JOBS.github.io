Evolve Peptides: Operations Support Specialist

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Casper, Wyoming, USA URL: https://evolvepeptides.com/ Evolve Peptides is a US-based e-commerce company supplying research peptides to researchers and laboratories across the United States. Every batch is third-party tested, orders ship same day from our fulfillment center in Florida, and the whole operation runs on a small, fast-moving remote team. We are hiring an Operations Support Specialist to keep orders, inventory, customer chats and automations running smoothly every day. You will work directly with the founders and our operations team. The basics Pay: $12 USD per hour Hours: Full-time, 40 hours per week, Monday to Friday, US Eastern business hours (9-5) Engagement: Independent contractor, long-term Location: Fully remote. We are only considering candidates based in South Africa, Latin America, or Eastern Europe.
