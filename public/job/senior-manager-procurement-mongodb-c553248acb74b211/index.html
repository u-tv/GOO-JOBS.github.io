Senior Manager, Procurement

🏢 mongodb 📍 United States 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

MongoDB is looking for a Senior Manager, Procurement (Software) to support our growing company's needs. This role requires a strategic thinker, but also someone who is prepared to “roll up their sleeves.” It also requires interaction across the organization, globally. The right candidate will be a builder with strong analytical, negotiation and communication skills. This position will work in the Procurement Team managing procurement analyst and supporting the company in creating and implementing a category strategy for global Software. This role is based in the New York office and will be reporting to the Head of Procurement & Travel who is also based in New York. By joining the MongoDB Procurement team you will be gaining an enormous amount of experience while accelerating your career. This role will be based remotely in the United States. Key Responsibilities: Serve as the main point of contact for global Software contracts, leading category strategic initiatives and managing a team of procurement analysts Collaborate cross-functionally across the organization with stakeholders and the global Legal team to ensure vendor compliance and requirements are met Develop and execute a Software Category Management strategy and roadmap aligned with company growth and technology goals Own and manage a pipeline of software renewals, sourcing initiatives, and supplier engagements, ensuring proper planning and execution across the organization Build a deep understanding of software spend, market trends, licensing models, and supplier landscapes to unlock cost savings and value optimization Establish trusted partnerships with key stakeholders in Finance, Legal, IT, Security, and business teams to guide procurement decision-making Partner with global teams across regions and time zones, including regular collaboration during Pacific Time business hours Drive supplier negotiations, vendor performance management, and sourcing activities to secure optimal commercial outcomes Ensure all deals within your scope comply fully with the company purchasing policy So, what does success look like? The Senior Manager will be successful in this role when they can start executing the following strategic tasks and responsibilities after their first quarter with the company: Deliver superior and
