Keeper Security: Creative Localization Specialist

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote, US Description Keeper is hiring a detail-oriented Creative Localization Specialist to manage the localization of global creative assets across multiple languages and design platforms. This is a 100% remote position from select locations, with an opportunity to work a hybrid schedule for candidates based in the Chicago, IL metro area. Keeper’s cybersecurity software is trusted by millions of people and thousands of organizations globally. Keeper is published in 23 languages and is sold in over 150 countries. Join one of the fastest-growing cybersecurity companies and help deliver high-quality creative experiences to a global audience. About Keeper Keeper Security is one of the fastest-growing cybersecurity software companies that protects thousands of organizations and millions of people in over 150 countries. Keeper is a pioneer of zero-knowledge and zero-trust security built for any IT environment. Its core offering, KeeperPAM®, is an AI-enabled, cloud-native platform that protects all users, devices and infrastructure from cyber attacks. Recognized for its innovation in the Gartner Magic Quadrant for Privileged Access Management (PAM), Keeper secures passwords and passkeys, infrastructure secrets, remote connections and endpoints with role-based enforcement policies, least privilege and just-in-time access. Learn why Keeper is trusted by leading organizations to defend against modern adversaries at KeeperSecurity.com . About the Role As a Creative Localization Specialist, you will adapt existing creative assets into multiple languages and formats while ensuring accuracy, consistency, and adherence to Keeper’s brand and design standards. Reporting to the Director of Localization, you will work closely with the design team and Head of Product Design to manage localization workflows, resolve design challenges, and deliver polished assets across print, digital, and motion formats. Responsibilities Localize creative assets in Adobe Creative Suite (primarily, but not limited to InDesign and AfterEffects), Figma, and motion graphics files for 16+ target languages Manage design localization workflows by submitting translation requests through Smartling and applying completed translations to Figma and other design files Adapt layouts, spacing, formatting, and visual elements to accommodate language-specific text expansion and contraction Maintain consistency in terminology, brand guidelines, formatting, and design standards across localized assets Conduct quality assurance reviews to confirm accuracy, formatting, visual integrity, and overall design quality Support the localization of digital, print, presentation, video, and other creative materials Manage localization workflows, timelines, file handoffs, and deliverables across multiple languages and file types Collaborate with the Director of Localization, translation team, designers, and other stakeholders to identify and resolve localization challenges Maintain organized asset libraries, file naming conventions, version control, and final deliverables Track project status and communicate updates, risks, and outstanding needs to relevant stakeholders Support continuous improvements to localization processes, templates, and production workflows Participate in special projects for the
