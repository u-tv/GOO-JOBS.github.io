Headquarters: Phoenix, Arizona, United States URL: https://www.unitedhealthgroup.com At UnitedHealthcare, we’re simplifying the health care experience, creating healthier communities and removing barriers to quality care. At Surest, we are passionate about improving the health of our members! The Clinical Advocate - Behavioral Health Specialist is responsible for supporting members in navigating their Surest benefits to support improved health and well-being. In this role, the Clinical Advocate - Behavioral Health Specialist will focus on members and their families with mental health challenges and substance use disorders. Through telephonic coaching, resources, and referrals to applicable programs, the Clinical Advocate - Behavioral Health Specialist will work with members and their families and providers to establish a personal plan to improve their health. This position reports to the Clinical Operations Manager and has no direct reports. If you reside within PST time zone, you’ll enjoy the flexibility to work remotely * as you take on some tough challenges. *** Candidates must be willing to work 8:00 am – 5:00 PM PST *** Primary Responsibilities: Telephonically engage with Surest members to evaluate needs and provide the information or resources they need Complete Behavioral health assessments Help members understand options for treatment and associated benefits. Guide them to available resources when applicable. Engage in decision support and decision coaching conversations Assist members with finding providers, navigating the Surest app, or other tools Use active listening skills to assess members’ current status and level of activation. Tailor conversation to meet member where they are. Offer services and support beyond their immediate request based on their underlying needs Develop health goals with the member, if applicable and facilitate connecting them to resources that help them achieve their goals Provide expert consultation to other Clinical Advocates on the team or other business units Identify and execute process improvement opportunities Support cross-functional projects Participate in experiments for innovation Enable ad-hoc client requests, once approved You’ll be rewarded and recognized for your performance in an environment that will challenge you and give you clear direction on what it takes to succeed in your role as well as provide development for other roles you may be interested in. Required Qualifications: Masters degree in Social Work Fully Independent licensed practitioner (i.e. LCSW, LMFT, LPCC, LICSW or LISW) 3+ years of clinical experience including experience providing psychoeducation Outcomes oriented Basic computer proficiency (i.e. MS Word, Outlook) Reside in the PST time zone to support 8:00 am - 5:00 pm member base Preferred Qualifications: CADC, LADC, or other licensure related to chemical dependency Experience in substance use disorder, mental health, autism, gender affirming care, or pediatric behavioral health Proven ability to work in an ambiguous, fast-paced environment Proven comfortable questioning status quo and proposing ideas for improvement *All employees working remotely will be required to adhere to UnitedHealth Group’s Telecommuter Policy Pay is based on several factors including but not limited to local labor markets, education, work experience, certifications, etc. In addition to your salary, we offer benefits such as, a comprehensive benefits package, incentive and recognition programs, equity stock purchase and 401k contribution (all benefits are subject to eligibility requirements).
