podcasthub : Podcast Distribution & Growth Manager

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: UAE,DUBAI URL: http://podcasthub.ae We're looking for the person who takes a finished episode and makes it **reach and grow an audience**. You'll own how our shows perform on YouTube, Spotify and Apple — the titles, thumbnails, chapters, publishing, and the short-form that pulls new viewers in. We handle production in Dubai; you own distribution and growth. We're hiring in the US/UK because that's where the deepest podcast-growth talent lives. **What you'll own** *Publishing & optimisation* - Upload and schedule every episode across YouTube, Spotify, Apple and the clip platforms - Craft titles, thumbnails, descriptions, chapters/timestamps and tags — optimised per platform - Handle Spotify video setup, the Apple feed, YouTube "Set as podcast", captions and transcripts - Run a per-episode quality checklist before anything ships *Growth* - Own YouTube & Spotify growth strategy — read the data and double down on what works - Build the short-form plan (Shorts, Reels, TikTok) to turn viewers into subscribers - Track views, retention, subscribers and CTR, and feed insights back to the team - Stay ahead of platform changes and podcast trends **What we're looking for** - A proven track record growing a podcast or channel on YouTube + Spotify — **show us the numbers** - Deep knowledge of YouTube SEO, thumbnails, Spotify/Apple publishing and Shorts strategy - Hands-on with YouTube Studio, Spotify for Creators and analytics tools - Based in the **US or UK**, working remotely, with overlap hours for Dubai - Organised, data-driven, and someone who closes loops **Bonus** - Experience with video podcasts specifically - Editing/thumbnail literacy (enough to brief and review) - Familiarity with the clip / content-reward model To apply: https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/podcasthub-podcast-distribution-growth-manager
