Senior Atlassian Cloud Administrator

🏢 dropbox 📍 Remote - Poland 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Role Description As a Senior Atlassian Cloud Engineer at Dropbox, you will design, build, and evolve the platforms that help our employees collaborate and get support. With a focus on Jira Service Management (JSM), you will own solutions that simplify service delivery, automate manual processes, and improve the employee experience. You will lead the design and implementation of service workflows, portals, integrations, asset management, and AI-powered support experiences. Your work will also strengthen the security, reliability, and governance of our broader Atlassian ecosystem, including Jira Cloud and Confluence. Partnering with Engineering, Corporate IT, Security, and other stakeholders, you will translate complex needs into scalable, maintainable solutions. You will take projects from discovery through delivery and ongoing operations, making technical decisions that balance immediate needs with long-term platform health. Our Engineering Career Framework is viewable by anyone outside the company and describes what’s expected for our engineers at each of our career levels. Check out our blog post on this topic and more here . Responsibilities Own the design, implementation, and ongoing improvement of Jira Service Management capabilities that make employee support simpler, faster, and more effective. Build scalable workflows, service portals, queues, service-level agreements (SLAs), and automation across incident, request, problem, and change management. Develop secure, maintainable integrations between Atlassian Cloud and enterprise systems using APIs, webhooks, and scripting. Improve employee self-service through Confluence knowledge bases, Rovo, and AI-powered support experiences. Develop JSM Assets and configuration management capabilities that improve data quality, asset visibility, and service delivery. Strengthen governance, access controls, security, and operational reliability across Jira Cloud, JSM, and Confluence while reducing configuration complexity and technical debt. Partner with stakeholders across teams to define requirements, evaluate technical tradeoffs, and deliver projects from discovery through rollout and ongoing support. Create reusable configuration patterns, documentation, and runbooks, and use operational metrics and employee feedback to guide improvements. On-call work may be necessary occasionally to help address bugs, outages, or other operational issues, with the goal of maintaining a stable and high-quality experience for our customers. Requirements 9+ years of relevant experience in enterprise Atlassian platform administration, engineering, or equivalent technical work. Deep expertise in Jira Cloud and Jira Service Management, including configuration, permissions, governance, and troubleshooting. Demonstrated experience designing and implementing JSM workflows, service portals, queues, SLAs, and automation. Experience building and maintaining integrations using REST APIs, webhooks, and scripting. Strong understanding of SaaS security, access management, and operational reliability. Proven ability to solve ambiguous technical problems, make sound design decisions, and independently own complex projects through delivery. Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to document technical solutions and collaborate with technical and non-technical stakeholders. Preferred Qualifications Experience leading enterprise JSM implementations or service management transformation initiatives. Experience with JSM Assets and configuration management databases (CMDBs). Familiarity with ServiceNow or similar enterprise IT service management platforms. Experience with Rovo, AI agents, or AI-powered service workflows. Experience with Python, Groovy, JavaScript, or ScriptRunner. Experience integrating Atlassian products with Slack, identity providers, endpoint management tools, or monitoring platforms. Experience building Confluence knowledge bases and integrating them into employee self-service experiences. Durable Skills AI fluency means using these tools to amplify human judgment, not replace it.
