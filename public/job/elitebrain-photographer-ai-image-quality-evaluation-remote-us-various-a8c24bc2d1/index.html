ELITEBRAIN: Photographer – AI Image Quality Evaluation (Remote, U.S.)

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Pembroke Pines, Florida, United States URL: https://www.elitebrain.ai Company Description Our client is seeking photography professionals to support an initiative focused on evaluating and improving visual data quality for next-generation artificial intelligence systems. The project combines human creative expertise with detailed image assessment to help improve AI-generated visual content. Job Description We are seeking Photographers for a remote, contract opportunity supporting the evaluation and improvement of visual data used to train next-generation AI systems. You will apply your photography expertise to compare original photographs with AI-generated images, identify visual inconsistencies, and provide detailed written feedback. No prior experience in AI is required. Responsibilities: • Upload your own original photographic content through a secure digital platform. • Compare original photographs with AI-generated counterparts, identifying differences, visual flaws, and artifacts. • Evaluate lighting, composition, perspective, texture, and overall image quality. • Document findings using accurate technical vocabulary and clear, concise written English. • Participate in iterative review cycles and adjust feedback as project guidelines evolve. • Collaborate with the project team to clarify requirements and share observations. • Maintain current profile information to support project tracking and communication. Location: Remote within the United States. Engagement: Independent contractor. Compensation: USD $21–$65 per hour. Qualifications • Demonstrated background in photography, digital imaging, photo editing, graphic design, or a related creative discipline. • Strong understanding of lighting, composition, perspective, texture, and image quality. • Exceptional attention to detail and a meticulous, methodical approach to image comparison. • Strong written English skills, including accurate spelling, grammar, and technical descriptions. • Ability to provide clear, concise feedback and adapt to evolving project guidelines. • Ability to upload original photographic content that you own. • Effective communication and collaboration skills. • Must be based in the United States. Preferred: • Experience in image evaluation, image annotation, or quality assurance for digital or AI-related projects. No prior experience in artificial intelligence is required. Additional Information • Work arrangement: Remote. Candidates must be based in the United States. • Engagement type: Independent contractor. • Compensation: USD $21–$65 per hour. • Project focus: Evaluation of original photographs and AI-generated images to support the improvement of AI systems. • Original content: Participants will upload their own original photographs through a secure digital platform. • Prior AI experience is not required.
