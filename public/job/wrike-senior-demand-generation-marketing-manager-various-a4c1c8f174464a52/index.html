Wrike: Senior Demand Generation Marketing Manager

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: US - Remote Wrike is the most powerful work management platform. Built for teams and organizations looking to collaborate, create, and exceed every day, Wrike brings everyone and all work into a single place to remove complexity, increase productivity, and free people up to focus on their most purposeful work. Wrike is our people, not a place. As a distributed team, we own our growth, stay globally connected, and rely on the product we build to deliver impactful work alongside brilliant minds. You'll have real ownership over meaningful work, a global team that has your back, and the flexibility to do your best work your way. If that sounds like you, we'd love to hear from you. Our vision: A world where everyone is free to focus on their most purposeful work, together. About the Role: You'll own your outcomes, from the strategies you run to the conversations that shape what we build next. We're looking for a results-oriented Demand Marketing Manager with proven B2B SaaS experience to lead one of Wrike's flagship global integrated campaigns. These campaigns bring together product, industry and buyer-journey strategy into a coordinated program that generates pipeline, accelerates deals and drives expansion across our customer base. This is a hybrid role: part campaign leader, part hands-on practitioner. You'll own the campaign strategy, plan, budget and results. You'll also directly execute sponsored media and email/nurture programs. At the same time, you'll orchestrate the work of partner teams, including digital, ABM, field and events, and webinars, and make sure it all stays aligned with campaign goals and messaging. You'll partner closely with product marketing, customer marketing, content, digital, field and regional marketing, ABM, sales development, and marketing operations. The ideal candidate has managed complex, multi-region campaigns, thrives in a metrics-driven environment, and uses Wrike to run their own work. Your Impact: Campaign Strategy & Planning Own the integrated campaign brief, strategy and roadmap, aligned to company priorities, product launches, target industries and personas, and buyer journeys. Define the approach for both new-customer acquisition (Land) and existing-customer growth (Expand), including audiences, messaging and channel mix for each. Translate positioning from product marketing into campaign themes and use-case-led messaging that resonates with enterprise buying committees. Manage the campaign budget, allocating investment across channels and adjusting based on performance. Execution (Hands-On) Plan, launch and optimize sponsored media programs, including content syndication (including regional and local-language placements), sponsored webinars and virtual events, podcast sponsorships, and industry publication newsletter or dedicated email placements. Select and manage third-party media and event vendors, negotiate placements within budget, and track delivery and results. Build and manage email, nurture and lifecycle programs for prospects and existing customers, including customer newsletters and workflow- or role-based nurture streams. Brief creative and content teams, and manage production and launch of the assets each tactic needs. Monitor performance, run tests and optimize against targets for actions, MQLs, meetings and pipeline. Program Oversight & Cross-Functional Leadership Oversee the programs run by partner teams, including paid digital (managed by the digital team), ABM (1:1 and 1:few), events (field, executive and customer events), and Wrike-hosted webinars and "deminars", and keep them aligned to campaign strategy, messaging, timelines and goals. Integrate those programs with sponsored media and nurture into one coherent buyer journey, including follow-up and sales handoff. Work with sales and sales development to ensure campaign assets, plays and follow-up cadences support target accounts and pipeline goals. Balance global strategy with regional relevance, including localization, regional events and partner activity, across EMEA, the Americas and APJ. Workflow & Operations Run campaign planning in Wrike, including briefs, templates, dependencies, workback schedules and creative requests, to keep a large cross-functional program visible and on track. Work with marketing operations to build, QA and track programs across tools such as Marketo, Salesforce, 6sense and Tableau. Identify risks, resolve conflicts and escalate when needed to keep the program on schedule. Measurement & Optimization Define campaign KPIs across target-account coverage, engagement, intent, actions/MQLs, meetings, pipeline and revenue, including both sourced and influenced contribution. Establish baselines and measure impact on target accounts, then use the data to recommend and drive optimizations. Report regularly to senior leadership through dashboards, presentations and Wrike updates. Your Qualifications: 5–7 years of experience in B2B SaaS demand generation or integrated campaign roles, including leading large, multi-channel or multi-region programs. Hands-on experience executing sponsored media, content syndication and email/nurture programs. Experience leading both acquisition and expansion campaigns, with exposure to ABM. Demonstrated ability to align many workstreams and stakeholders across digital, events, webinars, ABM and sales outreach, including programs you don't directly own. Experience owning or managing a program budget and working with third-party vendors. Hands-on experience with work management tools (preferably Wrike) for campaign planning and collaboration. Familiarity with marketing automation, CRM and ABM tools such as Marketo, Salesforce, Tableau, 6sense or Sendoso. Strong project management skills, attention to detail and a data-driven mindset. Comfort working across time zones with global teams. Preferred Qualifications Experience marketing to complex enterprise or industry-specific buyers, such as manufacturing. Ownership of campaign workstreams in Wrike, including templates, dependencies and reporting. Experience coordinating localized campaigns, regional events and partner co-marketing. Understanding of enterprise SaaS buyer journeys and content strategies that support multi-stakeholder decisions. Our Work Style: A high-impact role on a high-performing demand generation team. A collaborative team culture that values initiative and innovation. Opportunity to make a direct contribution to revenue growth and brand visibility. Competitive salary, full benefits, and flexible working environment.
