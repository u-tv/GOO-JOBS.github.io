Celonis: Client Value Partner (CVP) - Oil & Gas Industry

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote, US, Texas Celonis is the trusted platform to industrialize Enterprise AI. At our core is the Celonis Context Model — which combines process data, business knowledge, and intelligence into a living digital twin of the enterprise that AI can actually understand, turning AI's operational blind spots into operational clarity. World's leading companies trust Celonis and its global ecosystem of partners to make their AI agents, people, systems, and processes work together, achieving transformational outcomes. We believe there's a massive opportunity to unlock global productivity and sustainability by giving AI the context to understand how every business process really works. Join our mission to make processes work for people, companies, and the planet. The Opportunity We are seeking an established consulting leader—a current Big Four Director, Managing Director, or Partner-track Senior Manager—to spearhead our highest-stakes Oil & Gas engagements. In this executive-level role, you will serve as the principal strategic architect for our most valuable Upstream, Midstream, Downstream, and LNG accounts globally. Leveraging the Celonis Process Intelligence platform, you will bridge the gap between macro energy transition strategies and modern cloud/AI execution models, moving clients from slide decks to realized operational value. The Impact You’ll Make C-Suite Advisory & Value Architecture Peer-to-Peer Advisory: Partner directly with Oil & Gas C-suite executives (CEOs, COOs, CFOs, Chief Sustainability Officers) to align macro pressures—capital discipline, decarbonization, yield optimization, and supply chain volatility—with execution-level transformation roadmaps. Map Enterprise Digital Twins: Oversee the deployment of the Celonis Process Intelligence Graph to build systemic, end-to-end operational models of complex oilfield, pipeline, or refining enterprises. Orchestrate $100M+ Business Cases: Direct the formulation of board-ready Celonis Value Assessments, delivering the economic justification, working capital improvements, and OPEX returns required to greenlight massive cloud, IoT, and Edge AI investments. Transformation Leadership & Ecosystem Governance Steer Enterprise Transformations: Serve as the executive sponsor for high-visibility, Celonis-driven operating model overhauls for Fortune 500 O&G clients, guiding them from diagnostic discovery to closed-loop automation. Architect Celonis Centers of Excellence (CoEs): Design and establish corporate-wide CoEs, defining the target operating models, governance structures, and enablement frameworks needed to embed data-driven decision-making into client cultures. Untangle Systemic Friction: Lead cross-functional teams to resolve complex operational leakages where siloed O&G stacks (SAP S/4HANA, Oracle, IBM Maximo, Hydrocarbon Accounting systems) collide—specifically optimizing mega-project capital procurement, asset turnaround management, and joint venture accounting. Commercial Growth & Scaled Innovation De-Risk High-Value Sales Cycles: Convert complex regulatory, environmental, and safety mandates (EPA, OSHA, BSEE, API standards) into automated, visual compliance audits within the Celonis platform, transforming abstract risk management into compelling business cases that accelerate C-suite sign-off. Commercialize Next-Gen "Process + AI" Use Cases: Direct the integration of industrial telemetry (refinery IoT, pipeline SCADA, digital oilfield data) into Celonis, translating complex data structures into high-margin, executive-ready offerings like predictive asset integrity and real-time carbon intensity tracking. Productize Oil & Gas Value Plays: Codify repeatable client successes into scalable Celonis apps and action templates, establishing a library of industry-specific intellectual property that drives rapid time-to-value, net-new ARR, and account expansion. The Profile We Are Looking For Consulting Pedigree: 12+ years of progressive experience within a Big Four or elite management consulting firm, currently operating at the Director, Managing Director, or Partner level. O&G Vertical Expertise: Deep, authoritative knowledge of the Oil & Gas sector (Upstream exploration/production, Midstream logistics, Downstream refining/petrochemicals, or LNG), with a track record of leading multi-million-dollar transformation programs. SaaS & Process Intelligence Vision: Strong conceptual understanding of Process Mining paradigms. Experience with Celonis is highly advantageous, but a proven ability to leverage enterprise software to reshape business processes is required. Architectural Fluency: Comprehensive understanding of the modern enterprise stack (SAP, Oracle, Maximo) and emerging data/AI paradigms (Azure AI, LLMs). Note: You do not need to write code or SQL queries, but you must possess the technical fluency to direct data scientists, engineers, and solution architects. Commercial Acumen: Proven capability in client origination, relationship management, and driving account growth through value-based selling. Education: Bachelor’s degree in Engineering (Petroleum, Chemical, or Industrial preferred), Computer Science, or Information Systems. An MBA from a top-tier business school is highly preferred. Visa sponsorship is not offered for this role. What Celonis can offer you: Pioneer Innovation: Work with the global leader in Process Mining and the Process Intelligence Graph to shape the future of AI-driven business operations. Ownership from Day 1: Every full-time "Celonaut" is an owner, receiving Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) and merit-based refresh grants. Unrivaled Family Support: Benefit from our inclusive parental leave policy—24 weeks of fully paid leave for primary carers and 12 weeks for supporting carers, available from your first day of employment. Work-Life Integration: Enjoy Unlimited PTO (in applicable regions) and generous PTO globally, as well as a flexible hybrid work model that balances remote focus with vibrant office collaboration. Continuous Growth: Elevate your skills through our 70-20-10 learning framework, mentorship programs, and access to a dedicated learning platform. Holistic Well-being: Prioritize your health with subsidized Wellhub memberships, mental health counseling, and dedicated "Wellness Weeks" that prioritize work/life balance. Drive Sustainability: Participate in annual Impact Days, where you receive paid time off to volunteer for community and environmental causes with your local office, or virtually. Global Inclusion & Belonging: Find community through our Inclusion Think Tank and participate in our annual Inclusion Days, ensuring every voice is heard and valued. Value-Driven Impact: Join a mission-led organization where our core values—Live for Customer Value, The Best Team Wins, We Own It, and Earth Is Our Future—drive every decision.
