Credit Policy & Governance Associate

🏢 brex 📍 Seattle, Washington, United States 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Why join us Brex is the intelligent finance platform that enables companies to spend smarter and move faster in more than 200 markets. By combining global corporate cards and banking with intuitive spend management, bill pay, and travel software, Brex enables founders and finance teams to accelerate operations, gain real-time visibility, and control spend effortlessly. Brex’s AI-native automation and world-class service eliminate manual expense and accounting tasks for customers so they can focus on what matters most. Tens of thousands of the world's best companies run on Brex, including DoorDash, Coinbase, Robinhood, Zoom, Plaid, Reddit, and SeatGeek. Working at Brex allows you to push your limits, challenge the status quo, and collaborate with some of the brightest minds in the industry. We’re committed to building a diverse team and inclusive culture and believe your potential should only be limited by how big you can dream. We make this a reality by empowering you with the tools, resources, and support you need to grow your career. Operations at Brex Operations is the backbone of Brex’s mission to power businesses through our Intelligent Finance Platform. We own credit, fraud, money movement, and payments, protecting our customers and our company. From product operations that ensure flawless launches to scalable systems that drive innovation and precision, we operate at the intersection of product, design, engineering, and customer success. If you want to work at the heart of the business, Operations is where you belong. What you'll do As an Associate, you will own credit policy and governance projects from analysis through recommendation and cross-functional implementation. You will be trusted to take a complex, loosely defined problem, decide how to approach it, and drive it to a result with light guidance from the team lead. Your center of gravity is governance and policy. You will operate and improve Brex's credit governance framework: keeping policies, procedures, and exception monitoring current and well documented, and owning the recurring reporting that goes to second-line and enterprise risk functions, including Capital One. You will also manage policy and compliance requests from our bank partners (Fifth Third and Emigrant) and help keep those relationships in good standing through our acquisition transition. Alongside that core, you will lead pieces of the team's risk appetite and provisioning work and the governance of our highest-balance, highest-exposure accounts. This is an internal-facing analytical and governance role: you will not be underwriting individual customers or working directly with Sales. It is a strong opportunity for someone who wants to grow toward owning a broader area of credit policy and governance in a newly regulated environment. Where you'll work This role will be based in our Seattle office. We are a hybrid environment that combines the energy and connections of being in the office with the benefits and flexibility of working from home. We currently require a minimum of three coordinated days in the office per week, Monday, Wednesday and Thursday. As a perk, we also have up to four weeks per year of fully remote work! Responsibilities Own the credit policy, procedure, and exception monitoring cadence, and the regular reporting to second-line risk functions, with minimal oversight. Draft and maintain credit policy documentation: write clear, well-supported recommendations, update policies based on performance data, and document changes ahead of implementation. Drive policy changes through implementation: define tooling requirements with Engineering, assess operational impact and develop SOPs with Underwriting, and build buy-in before rollout. Develop and document the team's risk appetite statement, including the analysis behind loss rate targets, concentration limits, and product-level exposure caps. Lead governance and risk detection for the portfolio's highest-risk, highest-exposure accounts, and contribute to the provisioning methodology for these accounts. Build and maintain Hex dashboards that surface credit portfolio metrics and policy performance, and use them to identify issues and propose action. Track and report on key credit metrics such as approval rates, loss rates, utilization, and limit adequacy across segments and policy cohorts, and translate findings into recommendations. Prepare portfolio reporting, policy review materials, and model governance documentation for second-line and enterprise risk functions. Serve as a day-to-day point of contact for bank partner policy requirements and associated compliance reporting. Contribute to credit frameworks for new embedded partnerships and customer segments, including the analysis and monitoring infrastructure needed to evaluate new channels as they launch. Proactively build cross-functional relationships with Risk, Compliance, Data, Product, and Engineering to unblock work, and communicate findings and trade-offs at different levels, from analysts to senior leadership. Requirements Bachelor's degree in a quantitative or analytical field such as economics, statistics, mathematics, finance, or a related discipline. 2 to 3 years of experience in credit risk, policy, audit, strategy, or a related analytical function. Strong SQL skills: you can independently pull, clean, and analyze large datasets to answer open-ended business questions. Demonstrated ability to solve complex, multi-variable problems and exercise judgment on method and solution, not just execute a defined analysis. Ability to understand what a credit policy is trying to accomplish and to design how you would measure whether it is working. Track record of delivering projects independently, managing stakeholder expectations, and explaining resource needs in terms of business impact. Experience working cross-functionally to drive a project to implementation, including partnering with Engineers and operational teams. Strong attention to detail and analytical rigor, with a habit of checking your own work before sharing it. Clear written and verbal communication, including the ability to summarize a complex analysis for a non-technical audience. Collaborative working style; this role sits at the intersection of several teams and requires someone who builds trust quickly and follows through. Bonus Points Direct experience with bank partner, regulatory, or second-line reporting and compliance requests. Familiarity with governance frameworks, policy documentation, exception monitoring, or audit remediation. Familiarity with credit risk concepts: how lenders think about approval rates, loss rates, and limit-setting while balancing customer experience. Prior experience in a credit, risk, or strategy adjacent function at Brex or a similar fintech. Experience with Hex or similar BI/analytics tools. Experience developing agentic workflows.
