Outmarket AI: Head of Growth

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote US Head of Growth Outmarket AI · Remote (US) · Full-time · Reports to the SVP of Marketing About Outmarket Insurance still runs on manual work. Most policies are sold through agents and brokers, and the people who know the work best are buried in the documents: checking policies line by line, comparing quotes, building proposals, on nights and weekends. We think that's backwards. The best people in insurance should spend their time advising clients. Outmarket, the AI platform built for insurance, makes that possible. Account teams drop in their documents and get back finished work to review, with every finding linked to the page it came from. Outmarket connects to the systems they already use. The judgment stays with the person; the groundwork doesn't. It's working. More than 10,000 people at over 300 agencies use Outmarket, including 30 of the top 100 U.S. insurance brokers. Most of those agencies found us through other customers. We raised a $34.5M Series B led by SignalFire four months after announcing our Series A because customers were pulling us forward faster than we planned. Why this role Demand that already exists. Customers recommend us to each other. Your job is to turn that word of mouth into an engine we can plan around. Ground floor. You'll join marketing while the team is being built, alongside a new Head of Product Marketing and Head of Content. You'll have room to shape the function instead of inheriting a playbook. A direct line. You'll work with the SVP of Marketing, the CRO, the founders and customers, and you'll see your decisions show up in pipeline within weeks. The role You own marketing's pipeline number. That means qualified meetings with the right brokers and the sales-accepted opportunities and dollars that follow, not leads. You'll run the programs (named-account campaigns, event follow-through, webinars, paid and nurture), own the programs budget, and work closely with the CRO and the account executives. There are two motions. Independent agencies buy through an outbound and events engine. Large brokers buy through a named-account program aimed at a buying committee: principal, producers, operations and often the person who runs their agency management system. You'll run both. A growth marketing associate reports to you from day one. The SVP of Marketing runs demand gen until you arrive and hands it to you in your first month. What you'll do Own the marketing-sourced pipeline number and report it weekly with sales: meetings, sales-accepted opportunities, pipeline dollars and cost per opportunity. Run the named-account program for our top 10 broker targets: choose accounts with sales, run buying-committee plays, and coordinate outbound, paid and event touches. Plan and run the campaign calendar, including renewal-season campaigns, customer webinars and one experiment a month. Own paid media on LinkedIn and search, favoring short customer videos over vendor claims. Cut whatever doesn't produce meetings. Turn events into pipeline with our Director of Industry Events & Programs: pre-show targeting, meeting booking and post-show follow-up. Build nurture and follow-up so no event lead or demo request goes stale, and own lead routing and the marketing tools you run on. Get the customer stories and video made by the Head of Content in front of the right buyers. What we're looking for 5+ years in B2B demand generation or growth marketing You've run named-account or ABM programs aimed at a buying committee and can show what they produced in opportunities, not MQLs. You've worked with a field sales team in an events-heavy motion and know how to turn a conference into meetings. You're hands-on with HubSpot or Salesforce, LinkedIn Ads and outbound tools, and you can build a campaign yourself before you hand it off. You've managed at least one person or a contractor team and can set priorities without hand-holding. You have startup experience at Series A to C, and you built a demand gen function How we work High agency: you find the work instead of waiting for it. Ownership: you carry a number and a function, not a task list. Startup pace: we ship weekly instead of planning quarterly. You don't need to know insurance. You'll spend your first month in agencies and on calls with producers. Curiosity is the requirement, and becoming fluent is part of the job.
