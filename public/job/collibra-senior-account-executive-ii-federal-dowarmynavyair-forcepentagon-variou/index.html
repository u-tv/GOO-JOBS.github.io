Collibra: Senior Account Executive II, Federal DOW/Army/Navy/Air Force/Pentagon

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote, USA Joining Collibra’s Public Sector team Make an impact at Collibra Public Sector by driving growth in US federal DOW agencies. This role will be responsible for selling Collibra's value and vision to prospects and existing customers, offering a complete solution that includes software, support and services. As part of our federal sales team, you will manage some of Collibra’s largest accounts. You will use your knowledge and skills in enterprise sales to establish trusted and credible relationships, build Collibra’s brand and awareness around our solutions, and serve as a data intelligence advisor throughout the customer journey. You will be actively involved in the entire sales cycle creating initial demand, progressing the opportunity through the technical and financial decision process, and ensuring adoption and expansion after the sale. Collibra Public Sector Account Executive II, Federal at Collibra are responsible for 5+ years in a technical customer facing role, ideally supporting Enterprise Class customers in an SaaS environment. Experience in Technical Account Management, Support or similar type of Premium or Signature Service organization dealing with Enterprise Software Application Support. Experience troubleshooting web-based applications. Experience with Java, REST API and SSO and a working knowledge of kubernetes, with VM, SQL, SSL, SSO, and LDAP a plus. Experience and a strong interest in developing deep customer relationships while facilitating discussions about enterprise IT architecture. You have Consistently exceeded assigned sales quotas. Experience in the data storage or data management software industry. 7 + years experience calling on federal defense accounts in a field sales capacity. Prior relationships with Departments of Defense/War, Army, Fourth Estate. Originated and closed complex sales with multiple technical and business stakeholders. Managed consultative sales processes, with value-based impacts or outcomes. A bachelor’s degree or equivalent related working experience. US citizenship (required) and active security clearance (preferred). Demonstrated proficiency in leveraging AI tools (e.g., Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT, Copilot) to solve real-world business challenges, drive measurable outcomes, or streamline workflows. Because this role supports the US government, it is required that this candidate be a US citizen who resides on US soil. You are Known for your integrity, and commitment to the customer. Detail and results oriented – you work backwards from a goal using documented plans. Composed, resourceful, and comfortable in high-growth high-expectation environments. Adaptive, accountable, and execution-oriented. A concise communicator, persuasive negotiator, collaborative seller. Proud of your work and aim for excellence. Flexible to travel as required. Measures of success Within your first month, you will have completed onboarding, connected with your team members as well as with your functional peers. Within your third month, you will be building a pipeline of business in your assigned territory. Within your sixth month, you will have solid foundation of prospective clients who you will be close to closing.
