Training Lead, Autonomous Vehicle Technician Operations

🏢 lyft 📍 Nashville, TN 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

At Lyft, our purpose is to serve and connect. We aim to achieve this by cultivating a work environment where all team members belong and have the opportunity to thrive. Flexdrive is a subsidiary of Lyft that works together with Lyft teams to realize our shared mission. Flexdrive builds the operational backbone for the future of mobility. As a leader in fleet management and a Lyft subsidiary, we're extending our expertise into autonomous vehicle (AV) infrastructure: managing the physical operations that keep AV fleets safe, available, and performing at scale. Our Nashville depot is the first proof point in a multi-market expansion, and we're building the team to sustain and scale it. Reporting to the Program Manager, AV Technical Services, Flexdrive is seeking a Training Lead to own technical training and qualification for the team who service and repair our autonomous vehicle fleet. These are electric, sensor-equipped vehicles that run around the clock, so every repair, inspection, and return-to-service decision has to be done right the first time. You'll train and certify Flexdrive associates to our AV partner's standards, keep them qualified, and use quality data to find and close skill gaps on the shop floor. Responsibilities: Training Program Ownership Run day-to-day technical training and qualification for Flexdrive team members, executing the training plan set by AV Technical Services leadership. Complete the AV partner's Train-the-Trainer certification, then serve as Lead Instructor. Deliver training per partner requirements: web-based modules, hands-on demonstrations, OJT checklists, and practical skills assessments. Build and maintain curriculum, SOP-based job aids, and quick-reference guides covering: high-voltage safety, de-energization, and lockout/tagout preventive maintenance and multi-point inspections tires, brakes, suspension, and steering sensor inspection, cleaning, replacement, and calibration diagnostics, scan tools, and partner-directed software procedures torque specs, critical fasteners, and quality checks before return to service accurate repair documentation in work orders Qualification Compliance Maintain all training records in the partner LMS. Run monthly verification reviews so the team stays 100% current and audit-ready. Make sure no Flexdrive associate performs a task without an active qualification. Manage expirations, recertifications, and remediation. Track and report time-to-qualify, first-attempt pass rate, and qualification coverage by shift and task. Field Performance & Continuous Improvement Use quality data to find skill gaps and deliver targeted retraining. Relevant signals include: rework and comebacks failed quality checks repeat repairs on the same vehicle safety incidents and near misses audit findings CAPAs Spend regular time on the shop floor across shifts, demonstrating repairs, observing associates and technicians, and coaching in the moment. Keep your own hands-on skills current, and step in on complex repairs as needed to model the standard. Roll out training for new procedures, vehicle platforms, hardware, and software changes on partner timelines. Support root cause investigations where training is a contributing factor, and update materials to close the gap. Flag unclear or missing repair procedures to the AV partner and push for updates. Cross-Functional Partnership Align with service partner leads, Repair Coordinators, and fleet operations so the whole depot works from the same process. Partner with Recruiting on technician skills benchmarks and hands-on hiring assessments. Champion a culture of safety, including high-voltage PPE, lift safety, and safe vehicle movement, working closely with EHS. Contribute to a repeatable technical training playbook Schedule & Travel Based at our Nashville depot, with flexibility to cover day and overnight shifts for training and observations. Some weekends as needed. Travel up to 25-50% to support training at other Flexdrive and partner sites. Travel volume varies with business needs. Physical Requirements Work on the shop floor for extended periods, including standing, bending, kneeling, and working under vehicles on lifts. Lift and carry up to 50 lbs. Wear required PPE, including high-voltage protective equipment. Experience: 4+ years of hands-on automotive technician experience in a dealer, fleet, or OEM service environment. 1+ years training technicians, whether formally (trainer, instructor) or as a lead or master tech responsible for onboarding and certifying others. Strong diagnostic skills with scan tools, wiring diagrams, and OEM service information. Experience building OJT checklists, skills assessments, and qualification standards. Experience maintaining training records in an LMS or similar system. Clear communicator who can teach complex technical content to technicians at every experience level. Valid driver's license with a clean driving record. Preferred: EV or hybrid repair experience , including high-voltage system service and safety procedures. This is a strong plus. ASE certifications, especially L3 (Light Duty Hybrid/Electric Vehicle Specialist) or Master Technician status. OEM EV certifications or high-voltage safety training. ADAS calibration or sensor experience (cameras, radar, lidar). AV experience is a plus. A Train-the-Trainer or instructor certification, and grounding in adult learning or instructional design. Experience in audited environments such as ISO 9001, OEM warranty, or aviation MRO. Experience launching a new site, service center, or market.
