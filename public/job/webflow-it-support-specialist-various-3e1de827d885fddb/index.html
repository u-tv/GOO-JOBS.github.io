Webflow: IT Support Specialist

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Argentina Remote Webflow is the agentic web marketing platform for modern marketing teams, helping organizations build, manage, and optimize high-performing web experiences that drive predictable growth and strengthen brand trust. Building at Webflow will need grit, because we move fast, without ever sacrificing craft or quality. Webflow is looking for an IT Support Specialist to be the hands-on technical presence in our Buenos Aires office. You'll be the first line of support for employees, resolving tickets through our help desk using state of the art, AI powered support tooling, while also owning the technology that makes our office run and our biggest moments land. You'll partner closely with our Workplace Experience team for on site builds or modifications. This is a role for someone who's energized by variety and has a desire to help create valuable experiences for Webflow employees.
