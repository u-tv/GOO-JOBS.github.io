About the Role: Gusto Internal Controls function is an AI native control function - improving traditional ICFR programs and processes with AI-native workflows that a single high-leverage owner can run end-to-end. We're looking for an AI Control Lead to embark on this exciting journey of reimagining the future of financial controls. In this role, you will be a hands-on technical contributor and key executor of our System Controls compliance program, with deep focus on our IT General Controls, IT Application Controls, and system risk assessments, especially with our homegrown systems and how AI is changing the inherent risk and testing approach. This role reports to the Head of Gusto Controls and is well-suited for someone who is comfortable with ambiguity, naturally curious, and enjoys solving problems that don't yet have industry-standard answers. This is a high-impact opportunity to collaborate with first and second lines of defense to help establish a control environment suitable for an AI-first company! You will act as a trusted advisor to Engineering, GRC, Security, Finance and Accounting teams on the technology controls adapted to a fast growing and rapidly changing organization. You'll bring technical depth, a bias toward automation, and the ability to partner effectively with both technical teams and Accounting teams as the organization rapidly grows. Here’s what you’ll do day-to-day: Partner with Engineering, Security, IT, and Product teams to assess the design and implementation of scalable and sustainable controls Assess new system implementations and its impact on Financial Reporting Assist in scoping and planning for annual Control assessments Execute system control testing across IT General Controls and IT Application Controls, including access management, change management, and IT operations Scale the System Control program as the company grows - rationalizing scope, standardizing testing approaches, and building repeatable processes that keep pace with new systems and entities Guide and direct the day-to-day work of internal team members and co-sourced partners: setting priorities, reviewing workpapers, and ensuring quality and consistency across the testing program Lead detailed remediation design with internal Engineering, AIT, and Security teams for identified ITGC findings, and build AI automation skills to perform remediation testing Track and assist with remediation of audit findings; communicate status to the Head of Gusto Controls and relevant stakeholders Build strong working relationships with process and control owners across the organization, translate technical control requirements for non-technical stakeholders and vice versa Coordinate with external auditors on system control matters, including evidence requests, walkthroughs, and testing schedules Own the integrity of the source of truth for Gusto controls (Optro Admin), manage documentation efforts, and evaluate homegrown alternatives where existing tooling falls short Own control design review for Accounting Control AI use cases, and build/refine second-line review agents so Control review scales without becoming the bottleneck Identify automation and tooling opportunities to improve control efficiency and monitoring, and contribute to the development of continuous monitoring capabilities, including ITAC testing, ITGC monitoring, and AI SDLC. Co-design integrated second-line risk and control agents with Legal and Security second-line risk partners You will work across: Accounting, Engineering, AIT, Security, Legal, Internal Audit, and external auditors/consultants. Here’s what we're looking for: 10+ years of progressive experience in a Big 4 accounting firm, internal audit, or SOX compliance role program in a complex, scaling organization with professional certifications like CISA. Experience in a technology-driven environment with proprietary applications, automated controls, complex data flows, high transaction volumes, and evolving products. Experience in fintech, payments, payroll, or financial services is preferred. Hands-on IT audit or IT Controls compliance experience, ideally in a fast-paced technology environment Deep working knowledge of ITGCs, ITACs, and IT risk assessment methodologies Experience auditing highly automated DevOps environments (CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure-as-code, automated deployments) and adapting traditional change-management and access controls to them Experience designing, testing, and documenting controls across access management, change management, and computer operations Demonstrated comfort with ambiguity and the ability to operate effectively in a high-pace, rapidly changing environment The ability to influence senior leaders, translate technical and financial risks into practical decisions, and work credibly with Product, Engineering, external auditors, and the Audit Committee. Ability to work independently on complex, ambiguous workstreams while communicating proactively with senior stakeholders Strong project management and organizational skills with close attention to detail Clear, effective communicator - able to work across technical and non-technical audiences A builder who can establish a long-term strategy and operating model while remaining close enough to the work to resolve ambiguous risks, challenge conclusions, and guide remediation. An AI-forward mindset. You are already experimenting with AI and automation to improve how internal controls work, from risk identification and evidence evaluation to testing and monitoring. A practical understanding of how AI changes the control environment, including the risks introduced by AI-generated code and AI-enabled financial processes, and the ability to partner with Engineering and business teams to establish practical oversight.
