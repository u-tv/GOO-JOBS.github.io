BeyondTrust: Field & Partner Marketing Manager, Public Sector

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote Unites States | Atlanta, Austin, Boston, Chicago, Dallas, Denver, Phoenix BeyondTrust is a place where you can bring your purpose to life through the work that you do, creating a safer world through our cybersecurity SaaS portfolio. Our culture of flexibility, trust, and continual learning means you will be recognized for your growth, and for the impact you make on our success. You will be surrounded by people who challenge, support, and inspire you to be the best version of yourself. Priority Locations: Atlanta, Boston, Chicago, Dallas, Austin, Denver, Phoenix The Role We are seeking a high-impact and execution-focused Field & Partner Marketing Manager, Public Sector to support pipeline growth and brand awareness across State, Local and Education (SLED) verticals. In this role, you will act as the strategic marketing partner to the US SLED leadership team and our cybersecurity channel partners (including elite VARs, Integrators, and Managed Service Providers). Along with the Sr Manager, Field and Partner Marketing Manager, Public Sector, you will design and execute data-driven, account-based marketing (ABM) programs, regional field events, and joint channel campaigns that penetrate net-new customer accounts, accelerate active cycles, and maximize partner-sourced and partner-influenced pipeline. This role is ideal for a hands-on marketer who enjoys running events, supporting partners, and working cross-functionally in a fast-paced cybersecurity environment. You will play a key role in bringing regional and partner marketing initiatives to life and ensuring programs are executed efficiently and effectively. What You’ll Do Field & Partner Marketing Execution Support the execution of SLED regional field and partner marketing programs, including field events, local roundtables, ABM campaigns, virtual events, and industry conferences. Work closely with Sr Manager, Field and Partner Marketing, sales leadership, direct sellers and channel to support territory-based campaigns and event execution. Assist in coordinating joint marketing activities with partners and distributors, including scheduling, promotion, and follow-up. Partner & Alliance Marketing Ecosystem Growth Joint GTM Planning: Co-author and execute comprehensive joint marketing plans with national and regional channel partners to drive awareness and source pipeline. MDF & Budget Management: Strategically allocate, track, and optimize regional marketing budgets and Market Development Funds (MDF) to maximize return on investment (ROI). Enablement & Local Execution: Equip partner sales and marketing teams with localized 'campaign-in-a-box' assets, sales tools, and localized threat-intelligence content to drive self-sufficient demand generation. Campaign Measurement & Reporting Track, analyze, and report on regional pipeline metrics, including marketing sourcing, partner influence, conversion velocity, and campaign ROI using Salesforce and marketing automation platforms. Ensure accurate campaign setup, lead routing, partner attribution, and data hygiene in Salesforce and marketing automation tools. Work closely with Sales Development Team on event and campaign follow-up to ensure appropriate lead follow-up and conversion. Support post-campaign analysis and documentation of results and learnings. Skills Comfortable managing multiple events and deadlines simultaneously. Experience supporting co-marketing initiatives with channel or technology partners. Basic understanding of the cybersecurity market and security buyer personas. Strong organizational and project coordination skills with attention to detail. Collaborative, proactive, and adaptable in a fast-moving environment. What You’ll Bring 5+ years of experience in field marketing, partner marketing, demand generation, or related B2B marketing roles. Previous experience is public sector marketing, channel or sales strongly preferred Exposure to marketing for B2B SaaS, cybersecurity, or IT technology companies. Experience supporting multi-channel marketing campaigns and events. Familiarity with channel ecosystems (VARs, MSSPs, technology partners) is a plus. Working knowledge of Salesforce and marketing automation platforms (e.g. Eloqua). Strong written and verbal communication skills. Able to travel 30% to support field and industry events.
