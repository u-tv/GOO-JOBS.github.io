Role Overview We are looking for a Senior DevOps Integration Engineer to enhance our application’s architecture, development, and operational efficiency through innovative integration solutions. With a strong foundation in back-end development, operations, and full-stack implementations, you will be responsible for creating robust Continuous Integration and Continuous Delivery (CI/CD) systems and providing seamless continuous delivery solutions across diverse cloud environments. What To Expect In This Role (Responsibilities) Integration Implementation: Develop and implement new integrations, creating seamless connections between disparate systems, services, and APIs to improve operational efficiency. Refactoring & Optimization: Lead efforts to refactor and optimize existing application architectures, focusing on performance, scalability, and clean code principles. Back-End Engineering: Utilize extensive back-end development expertise to guide architectural decisions and ensure systems are reliable and optimized. CI/CD Pipelines: Enhance and maintain automated CI/CD pipelines to streamline testing, building, and deployment processes, significantly reducing manual efforts. Cloud Management: Provide continuous delivery solutions within cloud environments, managing infrastructure and optimizing resource utilization and cost-efficiency. Collaboration: Partner closely with software development, infrastructure, operations, security, and architecture teams to refine release processes. Mentorship: Mentor existing team members, sharing insights and best practices related to integration, automation, and application architecture. Requirements Experience: 7+ years of experience spanning technical architecture, application development, or IT operations, with a strong emphasis on back-end development and integration. Education: Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Systems Engineering, or a related technical field. Programming & Scripting: Proven expertise in programming with high-level languages (such as Ruby, Python, Java, C#, or similar) for automation and configuration. Cloud Infrastructure: Deep knowledge of cloud infrastructure management and automation technologies across major providers (e.g., AWS, Azure, Google Cloud). DevOps Tooling: Hands-on experience with CI/CD tools (e.g., Jenkins, Hudson, Bamboo, Artifactory) and configuration management tools (e.g., Chef, Puppet, Salt, Ansible). Architecture: Strong understanding of microservices and distributed architecture, focused on integrating disparate systems into cohesive applications. Language Proficiency: Advanced English fluency to communicate effectively across all organizational levels and with global teams. Nice to Have Advanced degree (Master's) in a related technical field. AWS Developer, AWS Engineer, or similar cloud certifications. Excellent presentation skills, capable of conveying complex technical concepts to non-technical audiences, IT management, and executives. Benefits 100% Remote work model. Long-term commitment with autonomy and impact on global, high-scale projects. Strategic, high-visibility role within a modern engineering culture.
