Field Enablement Manager

🏢 datadog 📍 Singapore, Singapore 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

The Team The APJ Enablement Team empowers our Sales, Customer Success, and Technical Solutions teams with the right information, skills, and tools throughout their tenure at Datadog in order to maximize their ability to land, expand, and drive Datadog’s next phase of growth across the region. We act as strategic partners to the APJ Leadership Team, ensuring they have the support they need to achieve their goals. We regionalize and contextualize global programs, custom-build regional programs, and provide just-in-time support in order to deliver quantifiable impact on go-to-market performance in APJ. The Opportunity Datadog continues to grow at a rapid rate and with this exciting growth, our onboarding and ongoing training is also quickly increasing in scale and complexity. This position is responsible for providing onboarding and ongoing enablement training/programs to all of our regional go-to-market teams. We are looking for a structured communicator with a knack for simplifying the complex to deliver the training content, curriculum, and programs that enable our sales people to be successful in their jobs. At Datadog, we place value in our office culture - the relationships and collaboration it builds and the creativity it brings to the table. We operate as a hybrid workplace to ensure our Datadogs can create a work-life harmony that best fits them. What You'll Do: Deliver engaging live and virtual enablement sessions for regional Sales and Customer Success teams, including onboarding, ongoing training, and reinforcement programs. Conduct ongoing field listening (shadows, 1:1s, syncs with first-line leaders) to maintain a strong pulse on business needs, gaps, and strengths across APAC markets; share insights with the broader enablement team to shape programs and priorities. Lead reinforcement and coaching to drive post-program adoption, including designing activities (role plays, deal clinics, skill practice sessions) that connect training to measurable behavior change in real, complex customer scenarios. Partner with Curriculum, Program Management, and Operations to share field insights and pilot or co-build content, and work closely with first-line Sales leaders to build trusted relationships, diagnose challenges, and deliver ad hoc training in response to emerging needs. Use AI tools to accelerate delivery and reinforcement — including synthesizing field feedback and surfacing patterns — and model effective AI use for the teams you train. Who You Are: 3–5 years of experience in sales enablement, field enablement, training, or a related GTM role, with a proven delivery track record supporting Sales and/or Customer Success teams in a SaaS or technology environment. Strong facilitator with experience delivering engaging live and virtual training sessions to senior, sophisticated audiences, and familiarity with sales methodologies/frameworks such as MEDDICC, Command of the Message, Challenger, Sandler, etc. (certification a plus). Strong business acumen and comfort using performance data, field feedback, and qualitative insights to inform enablement decisions; experience with Asana or similar project management tools is a plus. Foundational understanding of Datadog and/or the broader technology industry, including foundational knowledge of Datadog's core product pillars (Infrastructure, APM, Logs) and the ability to connect product capabilities to customer value. Highly collaborative and relationship-oriented — you build trust quickly with sellers, managers, and cross-functional partners. Organized, adaptable, and comfortable operating independently across a large, diverse region. Willing and able to travel approximately 25% across APAC. Datadog values people from all walks of life. We understand not everyone will meet all the above qualifications on day one. That's okay. If you’re passionate about technology and want to grow your skills, we encourage you to apply.
