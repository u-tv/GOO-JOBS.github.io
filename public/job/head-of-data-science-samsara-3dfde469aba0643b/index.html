Head of Data Science

🏢 samsara 📍 Remote - SF Bay Area 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are Samsara (NYSE: IOT) is the pioneer of the Connected Operations™ Cloud, which is a platform that enables organizations that depend on physical operations to harness Internet of Things (IoT) data to develop actionable insights and improve their operations. At Samsara, we are helping improve the safety, efficiency and sustainability of the physical operations that power our global economy. Representing more than 40% of global GDP, these industries are the infrastructure of our planet, including agriculture, construction, field services, transportation, and manufacturing — and we are excited to help digitally transform their operations at scale. Working at Samsara means you’ll help define the future of physical operations and be on a team that’s shaping an exciting array of product solutions, including Video-Based Safety, Vehicle Telematics, Apps and Driver Workflows, and Equipment Monitoring. As part of a recently public company, you’ll have the autonomy and support to make an impact as we build for the long term. About the role: Samsara's Data Science, ML, and Data Platform team brings together data science, production ML, data engineering, and data platform to turn emerging AI capabilities into reliable products. These products help customers run safer, more efficient physical operations. As Head of Data Science, ML, and Data Platform, you will lead about 30 people through experienced managers and senior technical leaders, and report to the SVP of Engineering. You will decide where agents, foundation models, classical ML, and statistical methods belong at Samsara, and you will turn what you learn into how the whole organization works. In this role, you will: Define an AI-native strategy and rethink the roadmap from first principles as capabilities change. Choose what to build, buy, stop, and scale. Partner with Product and Engineering to take intelligence from experiment to deployment, adoption, and continuous improvement in customer experiences. Build a trusted data foundation by making certified metrics, discoverable metadata, and traceable outputs available to people and agents across the organization, within clear permissions. Own production and economic outcomes, including reliability, latency, inference and infrastructure cost, and human oversight. Make evaluation a core capability, with failure modes, uncertainty, and regressions visible during development. Build a leadership team of managers and senior individual contributors and develop them. Hire people who combine scientific rigor, engineering ability, and a drive to keep learning. Lead the use of AI across the team. Raise the pace and technical ambition of every team through everyday use of AI-assisted development, and move recurring data science work, from data discovery to evaluation and monitoring, into reusable tools, agents, and workflows. Decide when greater autonomy is justified and when a simpler, more constrained system serves users better. Champion, role model, and embed Samsara's cultural principles (Focus on Customer Success, Build for the Long Term, Adopt a Growth Mindset, Be Inclusive, Win as a Team) as we scale globally and across new offices Hire, develop and lead an inclusive, engaged, and high performing team Minimum requirements for the role: Hands-on experience with modern AI: you have prototyped or contributed to work with foundation models, agents, or AI-assisted development, and can discuss the design decisions and limits Experience leading delivery of AI/ML beyond demonstrations and pilots, with evidence of adoption, quality, and customer or operational impact Technical judgment across the AI system: model selection, retrieval, tool use, data access, evaluation, and deployment, including knowing when a deterministic workflow beats an agent Strong foundations in statistical reasoning, measurement, and model evaluation Experience leading scientists and engineers through managers, holding a high technical bar outside your own specialty, and changing how teams work An ideal candidate also has: Experience with agentic analytics: building investigation workflows over enterprise data Experience creating evaluations for multi-step systems, including tool correctness, grounded answers, and task completion Experience with product intelligence: combining structured operational data, predictive models, and foundation models in customer-facing products, with feedback loops from production use and human review Experience running large-scale data or ML platforms with demanding reliability, cost, and governance requirements The compensation range shown spans approved pay zones for this role.
