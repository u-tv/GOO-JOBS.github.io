Thrive Market: Performance Creative Coordinator (Contractor)

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: URL: http://www.thrivemarket.com ABOUT THRIVE MARKET Thrive Market was founded in 2014 with a mission to make healthy and sustainable living easy and affordable for everyone. As an online, membership-based market, we deliver the highest quality healthy, and sustainable products at member-only prices, while matching every paid membership with a free one for someone in need. Every day, we leverage innovative technology and member-first thinking to help our over 1,700,000+ members find better products, support better brands, and build a better world in the process. We are also a Certified B Corporation, a Public Benefit Corporation, and a Climate Neutral Certified company. Join us as we bring healthy and sustainable living to millions of Americans in the years to come. THE ROLE A contractor (30-40 hrs per week) who helps keep the paid social performance creative engine running: first-round ad review, QA and delivery, performance data, builds and decks, and ad hoc support for our creative strategist. RESPONSIBILITIES 1. First-round creative review Review ads against the brief: does the concept, message, and offer answer what was asked? Assess engagement potential (hook, first 3 seconds, clarity of CTA, platform-native feel) Give team concise first-round feedback and flag what needs a closer look before it goes further 2. Creative QA & delivery Check aspect ratios (1:1, 4:5, 9:16, etc.) and file specs (format, size, video length, safe zones, captions) Check ad naming against convention Confirm all assets are in (nothing missing) and the correct, approved version is used Deliver to channel managers with a standard handoff note (batch contents, launch date, links) 3. Coordination & Workload Balance Keep track of what is due, what is ready, and what is at risk in Wrike and build out a 8-12 week rolling calendar Balance competing projects and flag blockers or slipping deadlines early Proactively contact the right people when a new project starts (designers, editors, copywriters, channel managers, brand/legal) Move work along on Sabrina's behalf (follow-ups, nudges), with signoff as described below 4. Ad hoc support Slot in last-minute asks and triage them by urgency, telling Sabrina what gets bumped Own small edit and resize requests end to end without Sabrina stepping in: no editing skills required, but coordinate with the project manager to find bandwidth on the editing team, brief the editor, and track the request through delivery 5. Performance data & reporting Pull performance data (Motion, Meta Ads Manager) and build recurring reports (top and bottom performers, spend by concept) Tag and categorize creatives (hook, format, angle, offer) Keep a testing learnings log Surface early takeaways to inform briefs and next rounds 6. Decks & strategy support Help build decks: test readouts, performance recaps, creative reviews Research competitor and inspiration ads Draft first-pass briefs or test summaries for Sabrina to review QUALIFICATIONS Core competencies Proactive mindset; balances strategic objectives with day-to-day tactical execution Maintains timelines and prioritizes tasks for smooth workflows and timely delivery of creative assets Comfortable wearing multiple hats and shifting between review, data, and coordination work Must-have Background in performance creative (paid social preferred), with a trained eye for what makes ads engaging and on-brief Strong attention to detail (specs, naming, completeness) Clear written communication, able to give concise, constructive feedback Organized and self-directed; comfortable with fast-changing priorities Basic data pulling and deck-building skills BELONG TO A BETTER COMPANY We're a community of more than 1 Million + members who are united by a singular belief: It should be easy to find better products, support better brands, make better choices, and build a better world in the process.
