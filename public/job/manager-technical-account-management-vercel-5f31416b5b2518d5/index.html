🏢 vercel 📍 Hybrid - San Francisco, New York City, Austin 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Vercel: Vercel is the agentic infrastructure company, freeing people and agents to ship what's next. For more than a decade we've helped builders move from idea to production with speed, security, and exceptional developer experience. Now we're scaling our products for both agents and people to ship and run software, built in the open and trusted by OpenAI, PayPal, Ramp, Supreme, and millions of developers worldwide. About the Role: As a Manager, Technical Account Management, you’ll lead the Technical Account Managers who serve as the persistent technical advisor embedded in Vercel’s marquee accounts, across AI agent platforms, large-scale migrations, and greenfield builds. This is an early, still-forming function: you’ll coach a team of senior technical advisors while helping build the operating model, metrics, and playbooks it runs on. You’ll stay close to the accounts yourself, stepping into P1 and P2 escalations, reviewing architecture, and carrying a small set of strategic logos. In your first year, success looks like a team driving real adoption depth on their accounts, strong retention on the book they cover, and an engagement model repeatable enough to hire into. This is a hybrid role with 3 in-office days per week in San Francisco, Austin, or New York City. What You Will Do: Lead and coach a team of Technical Account Managers, from hiring and ramp through career growth and the quality of their technical, written, and customer work Own adoption depth and retention across the team’s marquee accounts: proactive architecture reviews, platform health checks, upgrade planning, and expansion surfaced to the Account Executive Build the function alongside the Head of Professional Services: the engagement model, operating rhythm, QBR standards, and the metrics the team is measured on Run point with the team on P1 and P2 escalations, review architecture and success plans, and carry a small set of strategic accounts yourself Partner with Product, Engineering, Support, and Sales as the team’s internal advocate, and feed what the team learns in the field back into the roadmap About You: 10+ years in customer-facing technical roles, including 3+ years managing technical ICs Have hired, coached, and grown a post-sales technical team: TAM, Solutions
