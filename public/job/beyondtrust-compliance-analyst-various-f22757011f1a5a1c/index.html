BeyondTrust: Compliance Analyst

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote Canada BeyondTrust is a place where you can bring your purpose to life through the work that you do, creating a safer world through our cybersecurity SaaS portfolio. Our culture of flexibility, trust, and continual learning means you will be recognized for your growth, and for the impact you make on our success. You will be surrounded by people who challenge, support, and inspire you to be the best version of yourself. The Role This is a contract role with the BeyondTrust Trust & Assurance team for 3 months for a full time Compliance Analyst role. As a Compliance Analyst, you will support and enhance our security compliance programs, with a particular focus on vendor risk management, customer trust, and project coordination/management. This role will contribute to the continuous improvement and execution of the Trust & Assurance program, including documentation creation, project tracking, and day to day operational activities. The ideal candidate has a background in GRC, security, or vendor risk, and demonstrates strong oral and written communication skills. They are a critical thinker, highly organized, and adept at managing multiple projects in a fast-paced cybersecurity environment. This is a project-driven role that demands strong attention to detail, confidence in communicating with both internal and external stakeholders, and a commitment to understanding BeyondTrust technology as well as the evolving security compliance landscape. What You’ll Do Assess vendors for compliance with BeyondTrust security requirements Respond to external security questionnaires Track complex project statuses across internal and external stakeholders Collect compliance evidence to support a variety of legal, operational and security requirements Arrange communication and contacts with involved stakeholders. Coordinate and research requests from the organization regarding security, compliance, and privacy-related questions. Assist in the update and review of Policies, Standards, and Procedures in accordance with applicable compliance and/or regulatory requirements Assist in delivering compliance and security awareness training, education modules, and role-based training. Provides help with compliance audit and assurance activities as needed. What You’ll Bring Strong listening, written and verbal communication skills, with ability to communicate at all levels of the organization. Familiarity of industry regulations and frameworks such as NIST 800-53/800-171, ISO 27001/27701 and SOC 2. Ability to interpret compliance reports and white papers Experience in project management related activities including status tracking, artifact creation, and reporting. Technical skills working with MS Office Suite (Word, PowerPoint, Excel, OneNote, Visio). Ability to work both independently and collaboratively with other departments/teams to achieve goals and represent the business. Ability to work efficiently with cross-functional teams, managing multiple projects simultaneously while under firm deadlines. Nice To Have Bachelor’s degree 2 or more years of experience in project management, security, or compliance role Experience supporting Cloud Platforms (AWS, Azure) and/or Software-as-a-service (SaaS) applications.
