Job Description

We're transforming the grocery industry At Instacart, we invite the world to share love through food because we believe everyone should have access to the food they love and more time to enjoy it together. Where others see a simple need for grocery delivery, we see exciting complexity and endless opportunity to serve the varied needs of our community. We work to deliver an essential service that customers rely on to get their groceries and household goods, while also offering safe and flexible earnings opportunities to Instacart Personal Shoppers. Instacart has become a lifeline for millions of people, and we’re building the team to help push our shopping cart forward. If you’re ready to do the best work of your life, come join our table. Learn more about our flexible approach to where we work. Overview Instacart's AI Productivity team builds the AI-powered platforms and tools that help our engineers and the broader R&D organization move faster, reduce toil, and deliver higher-quality experiences for customers, shoppers, retailers, and brand partners. We have been at the forefront of applied AI for years, and we move quickly: we prototype, ship, learn, and retire what is no longer the best answer. As a Senior Staff Software Engineer, you will set the direction for how AI changes the way Instacart builds products and software, and you will drive that change across the whole company. You will stay close to where the AI industry is heading, anticipate where the industry and Instacart will be 18 to 36 months from now, and turn that view into strategy, pilots, and adoption at scale. That means more work handled in the background by orchestrated, cost-efficient, and compliant agents, so people can focus on the work that needs human judgment. This is a hands-on, high-visibility role. You will write code, partner with senior engineering and finance leadership, evaluate vendors and prototypes, and lead organizational change, including the cultural and people side of adoption. This position is remote-friendly within North America. About the Job Define and drive the company-wide strategy for AI-assisted software and product development, anticipating where the industry and Instacart are headed over the next 18 to 36 months. Lead adoption of AI tools and agentic workflows across engineering and R&D, including company-wide rollouts, and own the organizational change that comes with them: culture, learning, communication, and working through resistance. Increase engineering throughput by growing the volume of background and parallel agentic work each person can handle, with high quality, strong safeguards, and clear cost controls. Build and prototype hands-on: lead large features, pilots, and proofs of concept that change how work gets done, and scale the ones that prove their value. Define how we measure impact, moving beyond &quot;are people using it?&quot; to &quot;are they using it effectively?&quot;, and report outcomes clearly to executive audiences. Partner with engineering leaders, Security, Legal, and Finance to manage AI cost, security, and compliance as usage grows. Evaluate vendors and emerging tools, deciding what to buy, what to build, and what to retire. Mentor and influence senior engineers across all pillars, and represent AI productivity across the company through forums such as lunch-and-learns and brown bags. About You Minimum Qualifications 10+ years of professional software engineering experience building and operating production systems at scale. Production experience with large language models at the frontier: you have shipped LLM-powered capabilities in real products and understand what good inference engineering looks like. Using AI tools alone is not enough. A track record of scaling AI beyond yourself to an entire organization, with measurable outcomes and a candid account of the organizational challenges you faced. Experience navigating the business side of AI, including cost management, security and compliance, and vendor evaluation and procurement. Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. Preferred Qualifications Track record of building and driving adoption of AI tools or platforms across an engineering organization of 500+ engineers, not just a single team. Experience measuring and communicating the impact of AI on developer productivity and throughput (for example, DORA/SPACE metrics, agent task volume, cycle time) to executive audiences. Experience managing AI spend at scale, including cost modeling, budgeting, and optimization across models and vendors. Hands-on experience with agentic systems, including orchestrating background and parallel agents with strong safeguards. Deep knowledge of AI safety, security, privacy, and compliance practices (for example, guardrails, PII handling, and data governance), and experience partnering with Security and Legal on them. Experience fine-tuning or adapting foundation models, and building retrieval and evaluation systems around them.
