Senior Manager, CEO's Office

🏢 meesho 📍 Bangalore, Karnataka 💼 Full Time Employee 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About the Role Meesho's CEO's Office sits at the centre of how the company makes decisions. As Senior Manager, you'll work directly with the CEO and CXOs/GMs on company strategy, the operating rhythm, and the cross-functional bets that matter most. This is a leadership role, not a support role. You'll own outcomes, not just inputs. Scope will shift as company priorities shift, and you'll often work without formal authority. It suits someone who is part thinker, part builder, part operator. You'll get a front-row seat to building and scaling a public tech company, direct impact on the company's trajectory, and a launchpad for future leadership roles at Meesho. About the Team The CEO's Office works as a force multiplier for the CEO. We run the company's operating system: the reviews, planning cycles and performance tracking that keep leaders aligned and accountable. We also pick up the problems that don't sit neatly inside one function. These cut across business, product, tech and operations, and we drive them to closure with the owners. It's a high-trust team. We see the company's most important decisions early, and we're expected to handle that with discretion and sound judgement.
