Content Creator - YouTube

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Important: Applications without an intro video will not be considered. Job Overview Create engaging YouTube tutorials about software, digital marketing, and AI tools that reach millions of people around the world every month through our YouTube channel, Metics Media. About us Metics Media is a small, fully remote team creating high-quality YouTube videos about software, digital marketing, and AI tools for an audience of over 700,000 subscribers. Our mission is simple: to make technology easy to understand and help people get the most out of the tools available to them. We partner with leading platforms like Shopify, Hostinger, and Squarespace, allowing us to keep our content free and accessible to everyone. We're now looking for talented, motivated content creators to join our growing team, help us produce even more high-quality videos, and reach millions more people around the world. What you'll do Research each video's topic and tools in depth, and figure out what to cover and how to present it in a clear, engaging way. Write video scripts and outlines. Record videos, including talking-head and screen recordings. Your face will be visible in your videos. Perform basic video editing tasks and prepare footage for our in-house editor. What we're looking for Native-level written and spoken English Strong on-camera presentation skills and the ability to communicate clearly and confidently Ability to quickly understand complex topics and explain them clearly to beginners Strong research skills and the ability to verify information before presenting it AI-native and highly proficient with modern AI tools, using them effectively to work faster, improve quality, and maximize productivity High attention to detail and a strong commitment to producing high-quality work Ability to take feedback and continuously improve Reliable, organized, and able to work independently and take ownership of your projects Genuine interest in YouTube, online tools, software, and learning new topics A quiet, dedicated workspace where you can work without interruptions, with room to set up a small studio for recording talking-head videos with minimal background noise Fast internet connection with at least 50 Mbps download and 25 Mbps upload Legal authorization to work in the US or Canada Nice to have Previous experience creating educational video content Knowledge of topics such as website creation, AI tools, ecommerce, or other areas we regularly create videos about Equipment needed to produce high-quality videos Working at Metics Media At Metics Media, you'll have a lot of freedom in how you work. You'll be trusted to structure your own day, manage your projects independently, and take ownership of your work. We're a small, ambitious team with high standards, and we're always looking for ways to improve what we create and how we work. At the same time, you'll get hands-on support, feedback, and training from people with over 8 years of experience making YouTube videos. Compensation Base salary: $70,000 USD per year (US) / CA$80,000 per year (Canada) Profit sharing: Earn a percentage of the profit generated by every video you create, paid out quarterly For reference, our current creators have earned an average total compensation (base salary + profit-sharing bonuses) of $85,000 USD in their first year and $155,000 USD in their second year. These figures are based on the past performance of our current creator team and are not guaranteed. Benefits 15 days of paid vacation per year, plus public holidays 10 paid sick days per year Medical, dental, and vision coverage A 401(k) with employer contributions (US) How to apply Applications require a short intro video (max. 5 minutes) and your CV.
