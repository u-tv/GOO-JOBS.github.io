To learn more, visit www.inmobi.com Senior Manager - Product Marketing – Ads Representation Business About the Role As the Senior Manager - Product Marketing for the Ads Representation Business , you will drive the commercial success, market positioning, and scalable growth of Microsoft Advertising’s portfolio across represented regions. This is a highly strategic, B2B-focused role centered on building robust Go-To-Market (GTM) strategies , orchestrating sales enablement , and crafting sophisticated client solutioning frameworks . Position Overview We are looking for a seasoned and dynamic product marketer to head the marketing efforts for our strategic Microsoft Ads partnership. InMobi represents and resells Microsoft Ads to advertisers across multiple regions globally. This role will serve as the lead PMM position for this entire global business line. As the lead PMM, you will be the definitive thought leader and go-to-market (GTM) plan owner for the partnership. You will be the face of InMobi’s marketing efforts for this business, both internally within the InMobi organization and externally to our market of advertisers and agencies, and senior Microsoft stakeholders. The ideal candidate will deeply understand the search advertising ecosystem, lead with market insights, and partner closely with our sales teams to produce best-in-class sales enablement collateral that drives revenue. Furthermore, you will act as the critical hub for this business, collaborating closely with centralized marketing execution teams (for example, events, social, PR, web) to bring your GTM strategies to life across various channels.
