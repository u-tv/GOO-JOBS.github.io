Manager, Account Management

🏢 samsara 📍 Remote - UK 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are Samsara (NYSE: IOT) is the pioneer of the Connected Operations™ Cloud, which is a platform that enables organizations that depend on physical operations to harness Internet of Things (IoT) data to develop actionable insights and improve their operations. At Samsara, we are helping improve the safety, efficiency and sustainability of the physical operations that power our global economy. Representing more than 40% of global GDP, these industries are the infrastructure of our planet, including agriculture, construction, field services, transportation, and manufacturing — and we are excited to help digitally transform their operations at scale. Working at Samsara means you’ll help define the future of physical operations and be on a team that’s shaping an exciting array of product solutions, including Video-Based Safety, Vehicle Telematics, Apps and Driver Workflows, and Equipment Monitoring. As part of a recently public company, you’ll have the autonomy and support to make an impact as we build for the long term. About the role: You lead and scale our EMEA Mid-Market team of Customer Success Account Managers (CSAMs) who look after our Scaled and Digital customer tiers. Your team owns adoption, value delivery, renewal attainment and churn mitigation across a large book of customers, using AI tooling and structured, risk-based engagement to reach every account. You will work closely with Sales, Support, Deal Desk, Operations, Product and Finance to give customers a high-value experience through to and beyond renewal. You should apply if: You want to impact the industries that run our world : Your efforts will result in real-world impact, helping to keep the lights on, get food into stores, and most importantly, ensure workers return home safely. You are the architect of your own career : This is a new role on a team that is still shaping its future. You will have plenty of autonomy and opportunity to define how the role works and master your craft in a hyper-growth environment. You want to be with the best : At Samsara, we win together, celebrate together, and support each other. You will be surrounded by the best and brightest professionals out there. You build genuine relationships: the industries we serve value trust earned over time, and you coach your team to earn it. You are at your best: developing people, scaling a motion and winning as a team. In this role, you will: Hire, onboard, develop and lead an inclusive, engaged and high-performing team of Customer Success Account Managers Deliver renewal and retention targets at team and individual level, minimising financial attrition. Maintain and share accurate renewal results, scenario-based forecasts and customer trends with leadership. Embed a value-led renewals motion, coaching the team to lead the ROI conversation, negotiate favourable terms and close renewals that work for both sides. Shape and refine a 1:Many motions to engage with more than 2000 customers in our Basic and Digital tiers, utilizing AI-assisted prioritisation, risk-based engagement, customer workshops and programmes that drive adoption and value at scale. Step into at risk renewals, escalations and complex deals to support your team. Drive expansion via Customer Success qualified leads (CSQL); set and hit pipeline targets for your team, build a repeatable CSQL motion for the Scaled and Digital books, and feed customer insight back to Sales, Product and the wider business Partner with Sales, Support, Deal Desk, Operations, Product and Finance on account strategy and better customer outcomes. Champion and model our principles as we scale globally and across new offices. Minimum requirements for the role: 2+ years managing customer-facing, quota-carrying teams (customer success, account management, renewals or sales), and 6+ years of overall experience in such roles. A record of consistent over-achievement against retention, renewal or sales targets. Experience running high-volume, scaled or digital customer portfolios across EMEA markets, including UK/I, France and Germany. Proven ability to hire, coach and motivate talented teams in a performance-driven environment. Strong forecasting discipline and fluency with Salesforce, Gainsight, Gong or similar GTM tools. Strong interpersonal and influencing skills, including with executives, and real empathy for customers. Comfort in a fast-changing, high-growth environment, and a love of winning as a team. Renewals and SaaS experience preferred. A bachelor's degree is preferred, or equivalent practical experience. An ideal candidate also has: A strong bias towards action, big-picture thinking and high standards. An AI-first approach: you use AI tooling to drive efficiency and outcomes, and help your team do the same. Experience building scaled or digital customer success programmes. Industry Knowledge: Experience with routing, navigation, commercial trucking fleets, and logistics. Business fluency in German Total Rewards At Samsara, we build for the people who keep the global economy moving. We want owners, not passengers, which is why our rewards are designed to fuel high-impact builders. Our compensation program delivers above-market total compensation through a combination of base salary, performance-based bonus/variable pay, and equity (for eligible roles) in a high-growth public company. We meaningfully differentiate pay for our top performers, who have the opportunity to earn above-market compensation that can outpace the broader market over time. Beyond compensation, we provide the foundations that enable long-term success: a flexible, employee-led remote model, a professional development stipend, comprehensive health and parental leave plans, and more. If you’re ready to build for the long term and own the outcome, your journey starts here.
