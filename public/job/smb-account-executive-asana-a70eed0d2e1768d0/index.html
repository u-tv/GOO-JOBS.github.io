SMB Account Executive

🏢 asana 📍 Chicago 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

We’re looking for a driven, customer-centric Account Executive to join our SMB Sales team in Chicago and help small and mid-sized businesses unlock the full potential of Asana. Thousands of teams adopt Asana organically every day, and as they scale, they rely on our team to help them expand their usage and bring structure to their workflows. In this role, you will own a dedicated book of business—driving new customer acquisition, converting high-intent inbound demand, and partnering with existing customers to fuel long-term account growth. Working closely with Marketing, Customer Success, and Sales Programs, you will deliver a seamless customer experience in a fast-paced, high-velocity sales environment. This role is based in our Chicago office with an office-centric hybrid schedule. The standard in-office days are Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday. Most Asanas have the option to work from home on Wednesdays. Working from home on Fridays depends on the type of work you do and the teams with which you partner. If you're interviewing for this role, your recruiter will share more about the in-office requirements. What you’ll achieve Manage the full sales cycle from initial discovery and tailored product demonstrations through pricing, negotiation, and closing Partner with marketing, operations, sales, and IT leaders to understand their workflows, identify operational bottlenecks, and position Asana as a strategic solution Execute targeted outbound prospecting campaigns with personalized outreach tailored to each prospect's industry and role Qualify and convert inbound marketing leads into new revenue opportunities Analyze your existing book of business to identify expansion opportunities and execute strategic account growth plans Maintain accurate, transparent pipeline management and revenue forecasting Collaborate cross-functionally with Customer Success, Marketing, and Sales Programs to ensure a cohesive end-to-end customer journey Build a strong foundation for career progression into Corporate or Mid-Market Account Executive roles through continuous coaching and high performance About you Demonstrates curiosity about AI tools and emerging technologies, with a willingness to learn and leverage them to enhance productivity, collaboration, or decision-making 1+ years of experience closing deals in a fast-paced, transactional or high-velocity sales environment Proven track record of consistently meeting or exceeding revenue quotas Experience balancing both outbound prospecting and inbound lead conversion Clear, empathetic communicator skilled in navigating negotiations and addressing customer objections Proficiency with modern sales tools, including Salesforce, Outreach, Clari, and LinkedIn Sales Navigator Collaborative team player who thrives in a metrics-driven environment and demonstrates customer centricity in every interaction At Asana, we're committed to building teams that include a variety of backgrounds, perspectives, and skills, as this is critical to helping us achieve our mission.
