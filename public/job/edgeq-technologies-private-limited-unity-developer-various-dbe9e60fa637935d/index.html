Headquarters: United States Unity Developer Job Title: Unity Developer Location: United States (Remote) Work Mode: 100% Remote Experience: 2–8 Years Salary Range: $85,000 – $145,000 per year (USD) Employment Type: Full-Time Job Category: Software Development / Game Development Job Summary We are seeking a talented and experienced Unity Developer to join our growing development team in the United States. The ideal candidate will be responsible for designing, developing, and optimizing interactive applications, 2D/3D games, simulations, and immersive experiences using the Unity game engine. The candidate should have strong programming skills in C#, experience with Unity development, and knowledge of game physics, animation, performance optimization, and real-time interactive applications. This position offers a fully remote work opportunity for qualified candidates across the United States. Key Responsibilities Design, develop, and maintain interactive applications and games using Unity. Write clean, efficient, and reusable code using C#. Develop gameplay mechanics, user interfaces, and interactive features. Implement 2D and 3D graphics, animations, and visual effects. Integrate third-party APIs, SDKs, plugins, and external services. Work with designers, artists, developers, and product teams. Optimize application performance across different platforms. Implement physics, collision detection, and real-time interactions. Develop and maintain multiplayer functionality and networking features. Debug, test, and troubleshoot application issues. Participate in code reviews and maintain technical documentation. Deploy applications across platforms such as Windows, Android, iOS, and WebGL. Required Skills & Qualifications 2–8 years of professional experience in Unity Development or Game Development. Strong programming knowledge of C# and object-oriented programming. Hands-on experience with Unity Engine. Experience developing 2D and 3D applications or games. Knowledge of Unity UI, animation systems, and physics engine. Experience with asset integration, lighting, and rendering. Understanding of game optimization and memory management. Familiarity with Git or other version control systems. Experience with debugging, profiling, and performance optimization. Strong analytical, communication, and problem-solving skills. Preferred Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Game Development, or a related field. Experience with Unity URP or HDRP. Knowledge of multiplayer frameworks and networking technologies. Experience with AR/VR development using Unity. Familiarity with Photon, Mirror, or Unity Netcode. Knowledge of Shader Graph and visual effects. Experience with AI integration in interactive applications. Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines and automated build systems. Experience publishing applications to mobile, desktop, or VR platforms. Salary & Benefits Annual Salary: $85,000 – $145,000 USD. Competitive compensation based on experience and technical expertise. 100% remote work opportunity. Flexible working arrangements. Health, dental, and vision insurance. Paid time off and holidays. Retirement savings benefits, where applicable. Professional development and training opportunities. Career growth opportunities. Work Location United States – Fully Remote Candidates from eligible locations across the United States are welcome to apply. Must be legally authorized to work in the United States. Ability to collaborate with distributed teams across different time zones. How to Apply Interested candidates can submit their updated resume along with contact details, Unity projects, portfolio, and relevant development experience. Equal Opportunity Employer: We welcome qualified candidates from diverse professional backgrounds and encourage applications from eligible candidates across the United States.
