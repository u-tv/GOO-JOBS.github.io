About the Opportunity Gusto's Brand Studio is at an exciting inflection point. We’re scaling the team and building the systems to meet our growing ambition, from the brand refresh to increasingly complex, multi-channel integrated campaigns. We're looking for a Senior Creative Program Manager to be the connective tissue between Brand Studio and the cross-functional partners we build with: Marketing, PMM, R&D, and Legal. This role reports to the Manager of Creative Operations, who sets the operational roadmap and defines how the creative team interfaces with Marketing. You'll independently execute against that roadmap: unblocking projects, managing cross-functional stakeholders, and driving timelines across standard tier-1/tier-2 launches and complex, multi-channel integrated campaigns, each with its own approval checkpoint and parallel workstream. A core part of the role is helping stand up, optimize, and maintain the team's project infrastructure including the Brand Studio dashboard (an all-up project view for resourcing and capacity planning) and its task tracker, and driving consistent adoption and project management hygiene across the team and stakeholders. The Manager of Creative Operations leads the systems-level architecture; you'll help build it out and own ongoing optimization and maintenance. If you bring calm and clarity to fast-changing, multi-stakeholder work and you want to help shape, scale, and optimize the processes that let a growing in-house creative team meet this moment, this is your role.
