Senior Solutions Architect, APJC (Developer Platform) - India Market

🏢 cloudflare 📍 Hybrid 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Us At Cloudflare, we are on a mission to help build a better Internet. Today the company runs one of the world’s largest networks that powers millions of websites and other Internet properties for customers ranging from individual bloggers to SMBs to Fortune 500 companies. Cloudflare protects and accelerates any Internet application online without adding hardware, installing software, or changing a line of code. Internet properties powered by Cloudflare all have web traffic routed through its intelligent global network, which gets smarter with every request. As a result, they see significant improvement in performance and a decrease in spam and other attacks. Cloudflare was named to Entrepreneur Magazine’s Top Company Cultures list and ranked among the World’s Most Innovative Companies by Fast Company. At Cloudflare, we’re not looking for people who wait for a polished roadmap; we’re looking for the builders who see the cracks in the Internet that everyone else has simply learned to live with. We value candidates who have the instinct to spot a &quot;normalized&quot; problem and the AI-native curiosity to create a solution using the latest tools. Our culture is built on iteration, leveraging AI to ship faster today to make it better tomorrow, while ensuring that every improvement, no matter how small, is shared across the team to lift everyone up. If you’re the type of person who values curiosity over bureaucracy, and that AI is a partner in solving tough problems to keep the Internet moving forward, you’ll fit right in. Location: Singapore About Developer GTM The Developer GTM organization is a highly collaborative startup within Cloudflare, dedicated to attracting, inspiring, and growing the next generation of builders. We are redefining developer acquisition in a world where AI and distributed agents are reshaping the stack. Our mission is to create accessible and exciting pathways for developers to discover Cloudflare—whether through ecosystem partnerships, high-impact content, or creative field engagement. We are a diverse team of builders, storytellers, and connectors united by one goal: to make Cloudflare the most empowering and exciting place for developers to build. About the Role As a Senior Solutions Architect for the Developer Platform, you will shape and lead the regional technical strategy for our customer and partner engagements across APJC. In this role, you will partner closely with engineering leaders, architects, developers, security teams, and executives to define architectures, validate technical approaches, and confidently transition production workloads onto Cloudflare. Rather than focusing on a single account, you will operate across a dynamic portfolio of engagements, ensuring broad impact. A central part of your role involves translating your field experience into reusable organizational knowledge. You will create architectural frameworks, provide technical guidance, and champion the needs of regional customers directly to our product teams. This is a senior, individual-contributor role that beautifully balances hands-on architecture, executive collaboration, and regional technical leadership. You will report directly to the Director of Developer GTM, APJC. What You’ll Do Shape Strategy: Guide the technical strategy for Developer Platform customers and partners across the region. Design & Translate: Transform business, application, security, and operational requirements into secure, scalable, and highly maintainable architectures on Cloudflare. Collaborate & Advise: Partner with engineering executives and senior stakeholders to advise on application modernization, distributed systems, AI architecture, and platform strategy. Facilitate Discovery: Lead architecture discovery sessions, technical workshops, proofs of concept, design reviews, and production-readiness discussions in a supportive, consultative manner. Create Frameworks: Establish reference architectures, architectural standards, and governance patterns that empower our customers and the broader APJC field organization. Stay Hands-On: Build, review, and troubleshoot representative applications and integrations. Champion the Region: Serve as a Developer Platform technical leader through presentations, written guidance, and internal enablement. Mentor & Grow: Support and mentor fellow solutions architects and technical colleagues navigating complex customer engagements. Bridge Teams: Build effective, continuous feedback loops between customers, field teams, and product leaders, translating regional needs into actionable product and go-to-market recommendations. What Success Looks Like By the end of your first year, you will have: Become a trusted technical advisor and collaborative partner to customers, partners, and internal leaders. Established clear, actionable technical strategies and adoption plans for priority engagements. Helped customers successfully validate and launch their production workloads on Cloudflare. Published reusable reference architectures and field guidance that elevate the entire team. Improved the field organization’s ability to position, architect, and deliver Cloudflare’s developer and AI solutions. Cultivated the technical leadership of your colleagues through active mentorship and enablement. Provided product teams with consistent, evidence-based insights into regional customer requirements and adoption barriers. Become recognized as a supportive, authoritative Developer Platform leader across APJC. What We’re Looking For Meaningful Experience: A strong background in solutions architecture, software engineering, technical consulting, or a related customer-facing technical role. While candidates often bring 10+ years of experience, we care most about your demonstrated scope, impact, and ability to deliver results. Language Skills: Professional fluency in English and a major language spoken in India, with the ability to comfortably lead technical and executive discussions in both languages, to support clients in that region. A significant portion of your portfolio will consist of clients based in India. Strategic Collaboration: A track record of owning complex technical strategies involving developers, infrastructure teams, security stakeholders, and senior executives. Technical Depth: Deep working knowledge of distributed application architecture (compute, storage, networking, security, reliability, and observability). Hands-On Capability: Software-development experience, including the ability to build or review production-quality applications and proofs of concept (preferably in JavaScript or TypeScript). Ecosystem Knowledge: A strong understanding of modern web technologies, serverless/cloud-native architectures, modern software-delivery practices, and at least one major public cloud platform. Influential Leadership: A proven ability to influence without direct authority, thoughtfully mentor colleagues, and turn individual solutions into shared organizational capabilities. Communication: Excellent written, presentation, and facilitation skills tailored for developer, architectural, and executive audiences. Travel: Ability to travel up to 30%, primarily within APJC, including India (supported by flexible planning). Nice-to-Have (Bonus Skills) Experience building and operating production applications on Cloudflare’s Developer Platform. Experience designing AI inference, agentic, retrieval-augmented generation, or data-intensive applications. Familiarity with customers, technology partners, or developer communities in India. A background in public speaking, technical writing, open-source contribution, or community building. Relevant cloud or architecture certifications. A degree in computer science, engineering, or a related discipline, OR equivalent practical experience.
