Hyland: Senior Human Resources Advisor

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: United States URL: https://www.hyland.com Overview Senior Human Resources Advisor 📍 Location: Remote, US 🖥️ Work Model: Remote About the Role As a Senior Human Resources Advisor , you will serve as a trusted partner to employees and leaders across the organization, delivering expert HR guidance and driving seamless employee lifecycle experiences. You bring a solutions-oriented mindset, a deep understanding of HR practices, and the ability to navigate complexity with confidence and discretion. Your Role Responsibilities? Here's What You'll Do. Serve as a primary point of contact for employees and leaders, providing guidance on HR policies, programs, and employee lifecycle processes while resolving moderately complex to complex inquiries and coordinating specialized support as needed. Advise leaders on performance management practices, feedback, coaching, and documentation to support employee growth and organizational performance. Execute employee lifecycle processes — including onboarding, offboarding, organizational changes, and employment updates — while continuously identifying opportunities for improvement and greater efficiency. Partner with HR Business Partners and Centers of Excellence to execute HR programs, contribute to key initiatives, and drive continuous process improvements. Interpret and apply HR policies and practices with a strong understanding of local employment requirements; proactively identify risks and recommend appropriate action. Collaborate with HR Advisors to drive consistency and alignment in HR support processes and knowledge sharing across regions and lines of business. Role Essentials You'll be set up for success if you have: Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience in Human Resources or a related field. Demonstrated experience providing HR support and guidance to employees and managers. Experience working with HR systems and maintaining accurate employee data and records. Experience administering employee lifecycle processes and applying HR policies and programs. Strong knowledge of local employment requirements, including works councils, employee representative bodies, and collective bargaining or consultation obligations in the countries supported. What We'd Like to See Bonus points if you bring: Strong oral and written communication skills with the ability to interact with others with professionalism, discretion, and tact. Strong critical thinking and problem-solving skills with a practical, solutions-oriented approach. Fluency in English, plus professional proficiency in the primary language(s) of the countries supported (Spanish/Portuguese for LATAM; applicable local languages for EMEA and APAC) . Demonstrated ability to handle sensitive information with discretion and maintain confidentiality. Strong ability to establish rapport, gain the trust of others, and effectively build consensus across all levels of the organization.
