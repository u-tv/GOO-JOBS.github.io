Job Description

Airbnb was born in 2007 when two hosts welcomed three guests to their San Francisco home, and has since grown to over 5 million hosts who have welcomed over 2 billion guest arrivals in almost every country across the globe. Every day, hosts offer unique stays and experiences that make it possible for guests to connect with communities in a more authentic way. The Difference You Will Make: We're looking for a thoughtful, analytically rigorous, and strategically minded finance professional to partner with our Community Support leadership team. This role sits at the intersection of strategy, product, and operations: evaluating the impact of what we build, shaping the Airbnb customer experience, and connecting Community Support initiatives to Airbnb's broader company priorities. You'll be a trusted advisor to leadership and a key voice in cross-functional conversations across Product, Engineering, Finance, and Operations, working on some of the most consequential technology and investment decisions at Airbnb. A Typical Day: Build frameworks to measure ROI and cost-effectiveness of technology deployed within Customer Support, surfacing where investments are working and where spend should be reallocated Develop financial analyses evaluating AI initiatives, differentiated Customer Support models, unit economics, customer retention, and contribution margin Provide financial analysis and deal support for negotiations with operations partners to secure optimal pricing and contract terms Ensure Customer Support proactively and efficiently supports new business initiatives such as Hotels on Airbnb, Experiences, and other partnerships Monitor Customer Support industry trends and emerging technologies, including AI in customer service, to identify risks and opportunities Own core financial planning for CS, including forecast accuracy, the annual planning process, and long range planning Your Expertise: 9+ years in strategic finance, investment banking, or management consulting, ideally with meaningful exposure to customer support or operations at enterprise scale Resilient under pressure, with a track record of prioritizing and delivering in fast-paced, ambiguous environments Executive presence and exceptional communication skills; proven ability to translate operational concepts into clear financial insights and influence senior stakeholders Superb financial modeling skills; comfortable building frameworks from scratch, stress-testing assumptions, and sizing complex opportunities with incomplete data Familiarity with SQL or willingness to learn; able to independently extract, manipulate, and analyze large datasets and develop strategic narratives for an executive audience Preferred Qualifications Experience at a scaled marketplace, platform, or technology company with a global CS operation Familiarity with data visualization software Familiarity with customer support economics Your Location: This position is US - Remote Eligible.
