Together, we help more than 300 million learners and 12,000+ enterprise customers build the skills they need for a world being reshaped by AI. Read more about the combined company by visiting our blog . Why join us now? AI is transforming how people learn, work, and grow, and the need for new skills has never been greater. Coursera brings trusted content and credentials from leading university and industry partners, while Udemy brings a dynamic skills marketplace and global network of real-world experts. By combining these strengths, we can connect more people and organizations to the skills they need, when they need them. Shape what comes next By joining our team, you’ll have the opportunity to reshape how the world learns and applies skills—and help millions of people participate in the new economy. Bring your ideas, expertise, and perspective to meaningful work that can make a difference at global scale. About your skills Communication & Stakeholder Management – Ability to articulate technical concepts to both technical and non-technical audiences, including customers, sales teams, and internal stakeholders. Collaboration & Cross-Functional Coordination – Works effectively across sales, customer success, engineering, and product teams to drive successful customer implementations. Adaptability & Fast Learning – Thrives in a fast-paced environment, quickly learning new technologies and adjusting to evolving business and customer needs. About the role We are looking for a Solution Engineer to partner with our sales teams in engaging our top enterprise customers. This role is responsible for helping customers map the Udemy platform to their business outcomes, guiding them through discovery conversations, and delivering compelling presentations to audiences ranging from small executive groups to large-scale rooms of 100+ stakeholders. This is not a programming role. Instead, it requires the ability to tell a story, uncover the “why” behind customer needs, and translate those insights into meaningful value with Udemy’s platform in a solution consulting manner. Key Responsibilities Partner with Account Executives & Account Directors on strategic accounts to lead discovery, understand customer goals, and identify how Udemy can drive measurable outcomes. Deliver compelling presentations and demos that resonate with diverse audiences, from frontline managers to C-level executives. Ask insightful discovery questions to uncover root challenges and the &quot;why&quot; behind a customer’s initiative. Translate business challenges into a tailored Udemy solution narrative. Serve as the trusted advisor on Udemy’s learning solutions, guiding prospects and customers on best practices. Participate in workshops, webinars, and events as a thought leader to showcase the value of Udemy to larger audiences. Collaborate cross-functionally with product, marketing, and customer success teams to ensure feedback loops from the field. Support RFPs/RFIs by providing clear, value-focused responses aligned to customer objectives. Proof of Concepts: Develop and handle effective Proof of Concepts (PoCs) for executives and decision-makers. Understanding of sales process/ methodology (MEDDPICC etc.) What Success Looks Like You consistently uncover the real business drivers behind customer interest in Udemy. You deliver high-impact presentations that inspire confidence and action. You help close deals by mapping Udemy’s capabilities to customer priorities. Customers view you as a trusted advisor who understands both their industry challenges and how Udemy can solve them. You become an expert on understanding the Udemy technology stack What you'll have 4+ years in a pre-sales, solution engineering, solution consulting, or similar role at a SaaS or enterprise software company. Exceptional presentation skills; comfortable presenting to executive audiences and large groups (100+ people). Strong discovery skills — ability to uncover, clarify, and articulate business drivers. Proven ability to craft compelling stories that connect customer needs to platform value. Strong business acumen and ability to connect technology to measurable business outcomes. Experience partnering closely with enterprise sales teams. Excellent communication, relationship-building, and influencing skills. Experience with AI prompt engineering and foundational AI agent configuration is required, with the expectation that candidates will actively deploy these capabilities to extend presales capacity and improve deal-cycle efficiency Ability to understand and talk through APIs and API workflows, particularly where cross-application integrations are concerned. Fluent Arabic and English, with the ability to present and run discovery conversations in both languages. Nice to Have Experience in learning technology, HR tech, or related SaaS solutions. Familiarity with enterprise learning and development challenges. Multilingual presentation capability.
