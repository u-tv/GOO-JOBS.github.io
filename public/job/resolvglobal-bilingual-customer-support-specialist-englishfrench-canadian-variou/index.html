Resolv.Global: Bilingual Customer Support Specialist (English/French-Canadian)

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Saguenay, Quebec, Canada URL: https://resolv.global We are seeking an experienced Bilingual Customer Support Specialist fluent in English and French-Canadian to join a customer support team handling general customer inquiries, product questions, and warranty claims. This is a fully remote opportunity for candidates currently residing in Canada who are confident communicating professionally and naturally in both English and French. Authentic Quebecois French is strongly preferred. Candidates should be comfortable with the Quebecois dialect, including regional expressions, conversational language, and commonly used Quebec slang. The ability to communicate naturally with French-speaking customers in Quebec is particularly important. The ideal candidate is personable, articulate, customer-focused, and experienced in providing support across phone, email, and live chat. Key Responsibilities Handle customer inquiries through inbound phone calls, email, and live chat in both English and French. Assist customers with warranty claims, product-related questions, and general support requests. Communicate clearly, professionally, and empathetically with customers in both languages. Understand customer concerns, identify appropriate solutions, and escalate issues when necessary. Maintain accurate records of customer interactions and case updates. Manage multiple customer inquiries while maintaining service quality and response standards. Compensation $14 CAD per hour Paid weekly in arrears Independent contract Full-time, fully remote opportunity Paid training Technical Requirements (Essential) Candidates must have their own computer and meet the following minimum technical requirements: Windows 11 PC (Mac not supported) Minimum 16 GB RAM Dual monitors Webcam and headset Intel i5 processor or higher High-speed internet with a wired Ethernet connection Backup power solution Microsoft Teams / Office 365 Applications that do not meet the minimum technical requirements will not move forward in the process. Hiring Process Our hiring process includes multiple stages to ensure the right fit for both the role and the client environment. The process includes: English Resume Review Pre-Screen Questionnaire One-Way Video Interview Pre-Screen Interview Live Interviews Important Information Candidates must complete both the pre-screen questionnaire and one-way video interview to be considered. All resumes must be submitted in English. Live interviews will be conducted via Microsoft Teams using a laptop or desktop computer. Only candidates currently residing in Canada will be considered. Requirements Requirements Must currently reside in Canada. Native or near-native fluency in both English and French-Canadian. Strong preference for candidates with an authentic Quebecois accent and familiarity with Quebecois expressions and slang. Previous customer service or customer support experience is required. Experience handling customer interactions across phone, email, and live chat. Excellent verbal and written communication skills in both languages. Strong listening, problem-solving, and interpersonal skills. Ability to manage multiple customer interactions and work independently in a remote environment. A reliable internet connection and a professional remote working environment. Preferred Qualifications Experience handling warranty claims, product support, or after-sales customer service. Previous call center or contact center experience. Experience working with North American customers, particularly those based in Quebec. Familiarity with CRM platforms, ticketing systems, and customer support software.
