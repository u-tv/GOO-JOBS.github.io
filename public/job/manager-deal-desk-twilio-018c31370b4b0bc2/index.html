About the job Twilio is looking for a curious, action-oriented Manager for the Deal Desk Team who lives the Twilio Magic to support our ISV and MarTech NAMER Sales team. This is an exciting role for the individual to gain and expand their broad spectrum of cross functional interactions and lead non-standard commercial agreements or Order Forms (OF) from end-to-end. This role will serve as a leader in the organization and will work to enforce and define process shaping the NAMER selling motion, appropriately structuring deals to be a ‘Win-Win’ for both the customers and Twilio! You will be passionate, dedicated and have the ability to understand complex problems while providing explanations and solutions which are sound and clearly communicated. Responsibilities In this role, you’ll: Build and maintain trusted working relationships with aligned Sales leaders on the NAMER team and serve as strategic advisor to all sellers. Relationships should be structured with consistent mechanisms for deal prioritization and review, in order to ‘own’ the total deal flow under aligned leaders. Oversee the team’s partnership with Sales to ensure commercially sound deals are structured by analyzing tradeoffs across price, volume, and risk, ensuring all structures align with Twilio’s financial guardrails. Lead the architecture, governance, and optimization of complex financial models and pricing frameworks, ensuring absolute accuracy and strategic alignment for high-value, non-standard commercial deals requiring executive approval. Act as a ‘Player-Coach’ for a team of analysts, helping to both manage deal flow across the team and serve as an owner of complicated deal work yourself. Supervise end-to-end, quote-to-signature support for strategic deals, including: pricing and financial analysis, order form creation, evaluating redlines, Docusign execution and opportunity closure. Collaborate with and establish solid trusted working relationships with other sales supporting teams, including: Product Management, Commercial Legal, Billing Operations, Order Management, and Finance, with the ability to influence policy, process and tooling automation requirements.
