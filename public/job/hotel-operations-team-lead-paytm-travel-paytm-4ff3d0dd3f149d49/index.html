About Us Paytm is India’s leading mobile payments and financial services distribution company. Pioneer of the mobile QR payments revolution in India, Paytm builds technologies that help small businesses with payments and commerce. Job Summary We are looking for a CST Team Lead to join our Customer Experience team within the CST Travel business. You don’t need deep flights experience on day one. What matters is strong customer handling, ownership of escalations, and the ability to learn travel workflows quickly. Key Responsibilities Customer Experience Lead exceptional service across phone, email, chat, and social channels Handle escalated and complex customer issues with empathy, clarity, and closure Spot trends in complaints and feedback; drive fixes with the team and stakeholders Use CRM and customer-service tools confidently; coach others on quality and tone Improve moments that raise CSAT / NPS and cut repeat contacts Team leadership & operations Guide day-to-day floor/team performance: quality, productivity, escalations, and SLAs Run huddles, feedback, and simple process improvements that lift team output Partner with Product, Engineering, Growth, and Finance to resolve customer issues Build and refresh SOPs / playbooks the team can actually use Travel platform support (trainable) Support booking and post-booking journeys on our travel platform (with training) Learn and apply processes for modifications, cancellations, refunds, and related checks Work with tools and systems used by the travel CST team (CRM + travel ops tools) Qualifications (must-have) Hands-on customer support / guest service experience, preferably from hotels or hospitality (front office, reservations, guest relations, hotel contact centre, hotel OTA / travel-hotel support, or similar) Experience leading, mentoring, or acting as a senior / Team Lead for support agents Strong communication, escalation handling, and problem-solving under volume Comfort with CRM tools and multi-channel support (phone / email / chat) Willingness to learn travel-domain processes and tools quickly Key service metrics like CSAT, Team productivity, escalation management and service level adherence. Good to have Prior OTA, travel marketplace, or hotels customer-support exposure Familiarity with ticketing, refunds, cancellations, reissues, or PNR checks Exposure to GDS tools (Amadeus, Galileo, or similar) Hotel booking / modification / cancellation / supplier-coordination experience on digital platforms.
