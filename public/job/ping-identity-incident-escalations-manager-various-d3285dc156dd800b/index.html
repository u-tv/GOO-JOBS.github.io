As a Ping Incident Escalations Manager, you will provide Level 2 incident response and escalation support within the Cyber Security Engineering team. You will help coordinate complex security incidents, improve incident readiness, and ensure that lessons learned are converted into practical improvements. This is an individual contributor role, combining technical incident response experience with strong operational coordination. You will work closely with CSE engineers and cross-functional teams to maintain momentum during incidents, support tabletop exercises, and improve our incident response capability. You will Act as a Level 2 escalation point for CSE engineers during complex or high-risk security incidents Coordinate incident response across Cyber Security, Engineering, Infrastructure, Cloud, IT, Product, and other relevant teams Establish incident priorities, ownership, actions, dependencies, and communication cadence Support investigation, containment, eradication, recovery, and validation activities Maintain accurate incident timelines, decision records, action logs, and stakeholder updates Prepare and support cyber security tabletop exercises, including scenarios, injects, facilitation, and follow-up actions Develop and improve incident response playbooks, runbooks, escalation paths, and readiness procedures Lead or support post-incident reviews and ensure lessons learned are converted into tracked improvements Identify recurring response gaps, operational risks, and opportunities for automation or process improvement Participate in the Cyber Security Engineering on-call or escalation rota, where required Collaborate cross-functionally to improve incident response readiness and security outcomes Requirements Experience in cyber security
