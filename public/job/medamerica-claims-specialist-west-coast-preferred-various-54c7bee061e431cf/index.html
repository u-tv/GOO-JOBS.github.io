MedAmerica: Claims Specialist - (West Coast Preferred)

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Sacramento, California, United States URL: https://www.vituity.com/about-us/practice-management The Mutual RRG Leadership Search *This opportunity is for The Mutual RRG. Vituity is supporting the executive search process on behalf of the organization.* Remote, Nationwide – Seeking a Claims Specialist This is an exciting opportunity to join The Mutual Risk Retention Group's Claims team , supporting healthcare professionals through the complexities of medical professional liability claims. We are seeking a curious, confident, and motivated professional who brings strong analytical skills, sound judgment, and a genuine interest in medicine and law. While previous medical professional liability (MPL) experience is valued, it is not required. We welcome candidates with experience in insurance claims, workers' compensation, personal injury, litigation, or related legal and medical fields. Reporting to the Assistant Vice President of Claims, this individual will help manage claims across the Western United States while delivering the personalized, high-touch service that distinguishes The Mutual's approach to supporting its insureds. The Opportunity: Claims Specialist Claims Investigation and Management Investigate, evaluate, and manage medical professional liability claims, including litigated and pre-litigated matters. Review medical records, legal documents, investigative reports, and other relevant information to assess liability, causation, damages, and potential exposure. Maintain accurate and thorough documentation of claim investigations, evaluations, and recommendations. Establish and maintain appropriate claim reserves in accordance with company guidelines. Monitor claim expenses, review legal invoices, and support responsible claims management. Legal Collaboration and Case Strategy Partner closely with local defense counsel to develop and execute effective case strategies. Participate in case evaluations, settlement discussions, mediations, and other legal proceedings as appropriate. Assist in obtaining settlement authority and negotiating resolutions consistent with company guidelines. Attend trials and court proceedings when necessary. Prepare required reports and documentation for regulatory and internal purposes. Policyholder Relationships and Service Build and maintain strong relationships with physicians, advanced practice providers, and other insured healthcare professionals. Provide clear, compassionate, and timely communication throughout the claims process. Guide policyholders through complex and often sensitive situations with professionalism, empathy, and sound judgment. Collaborate with internal team members and external partners to ensure thoughtful and effective claim resolutions. Deliver a high level of personalized service while balancing the legal, medical, and financial considerations of each claim. Required Experience and Competencies Bachelor's degree in a field relevant to medicine, law, insurance, or a related discipline. Two or more years of relevant professional experience in insurance claims, litigation, workers' compensation, personal injury, legal services, or a closely related field. Medical professional liability experience is preferred but not required. Working knowledge of, or demonstrated ability to develop proficiency in, medical and legal terminology and concepts. Strong analytical, investigative, organizational, and decision-making skills. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to explain complex information clearly and professionally. Demonstrated ownership, accountability, and confidence in making decisions while recognizing when additional information or guidance is needed. Intellectual curiosity, adaptability, and a genuine enthusiasm for continuous learning. Strong interpersonal skills and a service-oriented approach to working with healthcare professionals and their families. Ability to manage competing priorities, maintain detailed records, and work both independently and collaboratively in a remote environment. Proficiency with Microsoft Office applications and the ability to learn new software and claims management systems. What Will Help You Succeed The ideal candidate is someone who enjoys solving complex problems, asks thoughtful questions, and takes pride in supporting others through challenging situations. You do not need to come from a traditional medical malpractice insurance background to be successful. We value professionals who bring transferable experience, a willingness to learn, and an interest in the intersection of medicine, law, and insurance. Compensation and Work Environment We are excited to share that the base salary for this position is $95,000 - $125,000 , exclusive of benefits or potential bonuses. Final base salary compensation will be determined based on factors such as skills, education, experience, and internal equity. This is a fully remote position within the United States, primarily supporting claims across the Western region, including California, Oregon, Washington, Idaho, Nevada, Colorado, and Texas. The position requires occasional business travel, generally one to two trips per quarter, including mandatory quarterly meetings and attendance at court proceedings or other business events as necessary. This is an opportunity to join a collaborative team that values professional development, thoughtful decision-making, and meaningful relationships with the healthcare professionals it serves.
