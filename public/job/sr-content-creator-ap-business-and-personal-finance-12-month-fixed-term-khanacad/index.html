ABOUT KHAN ACADEMY Khan Academy is a nonprofit with the mission to deliver a free, world-class education to anyone, anywhere. Our proven learning platform offers free, high-quality supplemental learning content and practice that cover Pre-K - 12th grade and early college core academic subjects, focusing on math and science. We have over 181 million registered learners globally and are committed to improving learning outcomes for students worldwide, focusing on learners in historically under-resourced communities. THE ROLE We're looking for an experienced AP or high school business and finance educator to join Khan Academy as a Sr. Content Creator, AP Business and Personal Finance (12-Month Fixed Term) . Your focus will be helping us build a free, high-quality course aligned to College Board's new AP Business with Personal Finance framework. Khan Academy's Financial Literacy course already reaches students and adult learners across the country. The AP Business with Personal Finance course goes deeper than our current foundational content, and about half of it covers business and entrepreneurship. You'll bring the subject expertise and AP classroom perspective to help us get there. You'll work side by side with our Senior Content Creator for Financial Literacy, who will scope the course with you and guide the work. You'll be a primary creator of content for the AP course, and you won't be working alone: you'll collaborate with other members of the content team, and possibly with external reviewers. WHAT YOU'LL DO Partner with our Senior Content Creator to map the AP Business with Personal Finance course framework against existing Khan Academy content, and shape a scope and sequence of units, lessons, and assets. Design and write AP-aligned exercises, articles, and lesson video scripts across business, entrepreneurship, and personal finance topics, building on our current Financial Literacy content where it fits. Write practice questions that reflect the style and rigor of AP assessments, including the misconceptions and reasoning students need to work through. Review and edit content from teammates and reviewers, and help with peer review of other Financial Literacy projects as timing allows. Learn and use our content management system to build and publish the course. Keep work on schedule against shared milestones, and share progress clearly with the content team and our Philanthropy partners. Bring your classroom perspective to our course planning, so the course works well for AP teachers and their students. WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE IN YOUR YEAR Over your 12-month term, you and our Senior Content Creator will publish at least four units of the AP course, giving teachers and students a strong start on the full course. First 30 days: Learn our content tools and style standards, study the AP framework, and help finalize the course scope and sequence. First 3 months: Publish your first lessons and exercises, with a clear plan and timeline for the rest of the year. By the end of your term: Four units are complete, published, and available to the public. Throughout: Content you create meets our quality bar for accuracy, rigor, and accessibility, and leaves a clear foundation for the remaining units. WHAT YOU BRING At least 3 years of classroom experience teaching high school business, entrepreneurship, marketing, economics, or personal finance, with AP experience strongly preferred. Deep knowledge of both business and personal finance concepts, from business planning and marketing to budgeting, credit, investing, and taxes. Familiarity with College Board's AP course and exam design, and an understanding of how students prepare for AP-style questions. Proven ability to create clear, rigorous learning materials such as lessons, assessments, or curriculum. Strong intuition for how students learn and where they commonly get stuck. Excellent writing skills and careful attention to detail. Comfort working independently against deadlines, while collaborating closely with a small team. Fluency with Google Docs and Sheets, and a willingness to learn new tools, some of which leverage AI for scaling. A working knowledge/1-2 years experience with LLM-enabled workflows A passion for education and for helping every student take charge of their future. Motivated by the Khan Academy mission “to provide a free world-class education for anyone, anywhere.&quot; Proven cross-cultural competency skills demonstrating self-awareness, awareness of other, and the ability to adopt inclusive perspectives, attitudes, and behaviors to drive inclusion and belonging throughout the organization. NICE TO HAVE Experience teaching or piloting AP Business with Personal Finance, or completing College Board professional development for the course. Experience as a DECA, FBLA, or similar advisor, especially leading students to state or national competitions. An advanced degree in business, finance, economics, or education, such as an MBA. Relevant teaching experience at an undergraduate level. Professional experience in finance, business, or entrepreneurship outside the classroom. Experience writing assessment items for AP, state exams, or published curriculum. Experience creating digital or online learning content. ABOUT THE TERM Term: This is a full-time, salaried, fixed-term role for 12 months, expected to begin in January 2027. Reports to: Director, US Content, working day to day with the Senior Content Creator for Financial Literacy. Location: Remote, within the United States. Renewal: Extension beyond the 12-month term depends on funding and business needs. Fellows are welcome to apply for other open roles at Khan Academy during their term.
