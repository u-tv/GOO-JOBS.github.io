Celonis: Account Executive - Cross Industries

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote, Switzerland Celonis is the trusted platform to industrialize Enterprise AI. At our core is the Celonis Context Model — which combines process data, business knowledge, and intelligence into a living digital twin of the enterprise that AI can actually understand, turning AI's operational blind spots into operational clarity. World's leading companies trust Celonis and its global ecosystem of partners to make their AI agents, people, systems, and processes work together, achieving transformational outcomes. We believe there's a massive opportunity to unlock global productivity and sustainability by giving AI the context to understand how every business process really works. Join our mission to make processes work for people, companies, and the planet. The Team: You will join our high-performing Swiss sales team. We help leading enterprise organizations in Switzerland optimize their end-to-end processes, increase operational efficiency through our Process Intelligence platform, and provide the contextual layer to industrialize Enterprise AI. The Role: Applying a value-driven consultative sales approach, you will build and grow relationships to drive sales cycles from discovery to value realization. You will be responsible for a portfolio of enterprise accounts headquartered in Switzerland, combining existing customers, where you will expand our footprint, with new / greenfield accounts, where you will win new logos for Celonis. Navigating complex organizations from operational leaders up to the C-suite, you will collaborate cross-functionally to understand your accounts' needs and drive the end-to-end sales cycle to a successful close. The work you'll do: ● Manage a balanced portfolio of existing customers (land-and-expand) and new / greenfield accounts (new logo acquisition) ● Build pipeline in greenfield accounts through targeted prospecting and account planning ● Engage stakeholders across lines of business, from operational leaders up to C-level ● Drive Celonis Process Intelligence sales motions across the core enterprise value chain, translating business challenges into compelling, AI-enabled use cases that deliver measurable execution capacity ● Hold an individual revenue target for closing six-figure+ deals within your accounts ● Engage closely with partners e.g., consulting firms and SaaS vendors ● Drive sales cycles through orchestrating virtual teams including business development representatives, marketing managers, partner managers, sales engineers / pre-sales, customer success managers, professional services, implementation partners... ● Grow your skills through structured enablement, coaching and close collaboration with experienced Strategic Account Executives and your Sales Manager The qualifications you need: ● 3+ years of relevant experience in either one - or ideally both - of the following domains: ○ SaaS Sales: Track record of applying a consultative sales approach to sell complex B2B software solutions to enterprise accounts, ideally with experience in both expanding existing customers and winning new logos. ○ AI / Management Consulting: Experience in a (boutique) consultancy, delivering AI or digital transformation projects with exposure to account management, client relationships, and business development. ● A track record of meeting or exceeding targets, commitment, and successful career progression with your previous companies ● A hunter mentality: proven ability to prospect, open doors and build pipeline in new accounts ● Strong business acumen and a solid understanding of core processes and KPIs across the enterprise value chain (e.g., Finance, Procurement, Supply Chain) ● Curiosity and the ability to quickly understand different industries and business models ● Ability to build trusted relationships with Business and IT stakeholders and the confidence to engage up to the C-level ● Grit, a hands-on mindset, strong discovery skills and a structured approach to running sales processes ● Excellent communication and presentation skills in German (fluent) and English ● Eagerness to learn and develop, open to feedback and coaching ● Ability to adapt to a fast moving, ever changing, high growth environment and approach challenges with positivity, creative thinking, and a growth mindset ● Willingness to be collaborative, inclusive, to embrace diversity and to have strong interpersonal skills #differentmakesusbetter ● Ability to leverage internal resources and work as a team #bestteamwins Zurich area location preferred ; candidates based elsewhere in Switzerland must be available for weekly in-person presence and customer visits (primarily in German-speaking Switzerland ). #LI-SS6 What Celonis can offer you: Pioneer Innovation: Work with the global leader in Process Mining and the Process Intelligence Graph to shape the future of AI-driven business operations. Ownership from Day 1: Every full-time "Celonaut" is an owner, receiving Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) and merit-based refresh grants. Unrivaled Family Support: Benefit from our inclusive parental leave policy—24 weeks of fully paid leave for primary carers and 12 weeks for supporting carers, available from your first day of employment. Work-Life Integration: Enjoy Unlimited PTO (in applicable regions) and generous PTO globally, as well as a flexible hybrid work model that balances remote focus with vibrant office collaboration. Continuous Growth: Elevate your skills through our 70-20-10 learning framework, mentorship programs, and access to a dedicated learning platform.
