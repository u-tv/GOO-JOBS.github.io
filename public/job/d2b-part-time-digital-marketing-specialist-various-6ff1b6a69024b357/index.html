D2B: Part-Time Digital Marketing Specialist

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Manila, Metro Manila, Philippines URL: https://jobs.workable.com/company/dcaVtKQqXr9FwBNppNCBH9/jobs-at-d2b Position : Digital Marketing Specialist Salary : Up to $2,400 NZD per month Working Hours & Conditions : Part-Time, aligned with New Zealand business hours Work Arrangement : Remote ABOUT THE COMPANY: An innovative B2B SaaS platform serving the Allied Health clinic sector. By integrating directly with leading Patient Management Systems, their software provides branded, web-based patient intake forms and dedicated check-in kiosk solutions that streamline clinical operations and enhance the patient journey. Backed by an established strategic roadmap and marketing leadership, the organization is scaling its multi-channel footprint and market reach. ROLE OVERVIEW: Working directly alongside the Marketing Growth Lead, the Marketing Specialist acts as the core tactical execution engine for the brand. This role bridges high-level strategy and day-to-day operations by managing content output, web pages, email nurture sequences, and multi-channel campaigns. It is designed for an autonomous, execution-focused professional who excels at turning strategic roadmaps into measurable pipeline momentum in a fully remote setup. RESPONSIBILITIES: Content Production: Write, edit, and publish blog posts, resources, and case studies that speak to Allied Health clinic owners and practice managers. Manage the content calendar and maintain a consistent publishing cadence. Website & Landing Pages: Build and update pages in Webflow — including landing pages, feature pages, and conversion-focused content — to support campaigns and improve the buyer journey. Email Marketing: Set up and execute email campaigns including nurture sequences, onboarding flows, and re-engagement campaigns using our email marketing platform. Campaign Execution: Deploy and monitor marketing campaigns across B2B channels, including paid acquisition when required. Social Media: Maintain a consistent, high-quality presence across relevant social platforms. Plan, create, and schedule posts that build awareness and drive traffic. Review & Reputation Campaigns: Drive customer review campaigns to build social proof on relevant platforms. Performance Tracking: Monitor campaign and channel performance, pull basic reporting, and flag what's working and what isn't. You don't need to be a data analyst, but you should be comfortable reading dashboards and reporting on key metrics. Momentum Building: Proactively identifying the next steps needed to keep our growth roadmap on track. What We're Looking For Execution Focus: You love the "doing" part of marketing and take pride in seeing a plan come to life. Eye for design: Good instincts for layout, imagery, and visual consistency. You can create professional-looking assets without needing a designer for every task. Self-Starters: You are proactive and comfortable working independently within a defined strategy. Reliability: You are a consistent "engine" who can be counted on to deliver high-quality work. Comfortable with marketing tools like Slack, Webflow, G2, Gartner, Plausible Analytics, Google ecosystem B2B Experience: Ideally some marketing experience in the B2B SaaS landscape. Highly Desirable Direct background or familiarity with the Allied Health, healthcare, or clinic management software industry. Tech-forward mindset with an appetite for adopting modern AI tools to augment workflows, accelerate output, and maintain high operational leverage. Prior experience managing multi-channel paid acquisition campaigns. To apply: https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/d2b-part-time-digital-marketing-specialist
