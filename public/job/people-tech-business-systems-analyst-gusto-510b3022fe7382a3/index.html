We handle the hard stuff — payroll, health insurance, 401(k)s, and HR — so owners can focus on their craft and their customers. With teams in Denver, San Francisco, and New York, we support more than 500,000 small businesses nationwide and are building a workplace that reflects the people we serve. AI experience requirements vary by role and will be assessed during the interview process. About the Role As a Business Systems Analyst, you'll own the systems that power our recruiting experience, including our recruiting tools and Sana. You'll work with Recruiting, People Operations, and Engineering to understand how hiring work gets done today, find where it can be faster and simpler, and turn that into configured systems and AI agents. You'll partner with engineers to design, build, and test agents that remove manual steps from recruiting processes. You'll independently lead projects from discovery through launch, set your own priorities within the team's roadmap, and serve as a trusted advisor to Recruiting leaders on systems and automation. About the Team You'll join the People Technology team within People Operations, Services & Technology, which builds and runs the systems and automation behind Gusto's People programs. The team works horizontally across People functions, partnering with Recruiting, HR operations, and Engineering to turn process improvements into tools Gusties and hiring teams use every day. Here's what you'll do day-to-day: Own the administration, configuration, and roadmap for recruiting tools and Sana, including integrations, access, and change management Lead requirements gathering with Recruiting and write clear specs, user stories, and acceptance criteria Partner with engineers to design, build, test, and launch AI agents and automations that streamline recruiting processes, and help decide when to configure, build, or buy Map current-state workflows, identify friction, and drive improvements end to end, measuring their impact Manage multiple projects at once, communicate status and trade-offs to senior stakeholders, and keep documentation and training current Share best practices and support less experienced teammates as the team's recruiting systems and automation work grows Here's what we're looking for: 4–6 years in HRIS, recruiting systems, business systems analysis, or a related role Hands-on experience owning and configuring an ATS or HR system (Greenhouse, Workday, or similar), including integrations Track record of leading projects from requirements through launch with measurable results Strong requirements gathering, process mapping, and documentation skills Experience partnering with engineers on design, testing, and launch, and comfort with light scripting or automation tools Experience with, or strong interest in, building AI tools and agents to improve a process Clear communication, including presenting to senior stakeholders, and the ability to prioritize competing requests Our cash compensation range for this role is $107,000/yr to $130,000/yr in Denver, and $130,000/yr to $160,000/yr in San Francisco, New York. Final offer amounts are determined by multiple factors, including candidate experience and expertise, and may vary from the amounts listed above.
