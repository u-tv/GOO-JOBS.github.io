Administrative Coordinator

🏢 stripe 📍 Bengaluru 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world's largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team The administrative team works with Stripe leaders to support their day-to-day administrative and operational needs. What you'll do In this role, you'll be a critical part of supporting our business function. Responsibilities • Provide complex and proactive calendar support for multiple leaders • Make recommendations for your leaders with regard to their time management, prioritization, delegation, and organization • Coordinate domestic and international travel and manage all related details • Prepare and submit expense reports on behalf of your leaders Who you are We're looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement. Minimum requirements • You are organized, with excellent attention to detail and a strong ability to execute • You are excellent with time management and able to shift tasks and priorities as needed • You have a knack for working across various roles and teams in a changing environment while remaining flexible, proactive, resourceful, and efficient • You communicate, perform, and react well under pressure or ambiguity • You are adept at handling sensitive information and situations with care and confidence • You are able to anticipate and respond to the needs of others before they arise • 1+ years of administrative experience • Business proficiency in written and spoken English Preferred qualifications • Experience with Google Workspace • Experience supporting multiple complex calendars • Experience booking and managing both domestic and international travel arrangements • Experience with expense reporting
