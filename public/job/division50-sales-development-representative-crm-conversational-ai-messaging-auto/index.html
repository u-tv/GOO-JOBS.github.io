division50: Sales Development Representative - CRM, Conversational AI & Messaging Automation

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Egypt URL: http://division50.com Description Target markets: UAE and Saudi Arabia Languages: Fluent English and Arabic Division50 is hiring a full-time SDR for a client offering a customer communication and conversational AI platform. The platform brings channels such as WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook Messenger into one shared inbox, helping businesses manage customer conversations, organize contacts, automate follow-ups, and deploy AI agents. You will identify businesses with customer communication and sales challenges, qualify their needs, and book discovery meetings or product demonstrations for the client’s sales team. Responsibilities Research businesses in the UAE and Saudi Arabia that generate leads or serve customers through messaging channels. Identify decision-makers across sales, marketing, customer service, operations, and business ownership. Generate opportunities through cold calling, LinkedIn, and email outreach in English and Arabic. Explore challenges such as missed inquiries, slow responses, inconsistent follow-ups, and conversations spread across multiple channels. Explain the value of a unified inbox, contact management, messaging campaigns, and AI-assisted customer engagement. Qualify prospects based on their communication channels, inquiry volume, team structure, current tools, and buying timeline. Book 20 qualified meetings per month and follow up to support attendance. Maintain accurate CRM records and share clear discovery notes with the sales team. Requirements Proven B2B SDR or outbound sales experience, preferably selling CRM, SaaS, marketing automation, WhatsApp Business solutions, or customer engagement platforms. Fluent spoken and written English and Arabic. Experience prospecting into the UAE or Saudi market. Understanding of messaging-based sales, lead management, customer service workflows, and automation. Ability to connect software features to sales and customer experience outcomes. Strong cold-calling, objection-handling, research, and follow-up skills. Experience managing an outbound pipeline using CRM systems. Availability for a full-time schedule aligned with UAE and Saudi business hours. Benefits $500 USD monthly base salary + commission. Fully remote, part-time role. Opportunity to move into a full-time role based on performance and business needs.
