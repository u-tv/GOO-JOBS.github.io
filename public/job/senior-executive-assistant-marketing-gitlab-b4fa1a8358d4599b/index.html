An overview of this role As an Executive Assistant in GitLab’s Marketing organization, you’ll support the Vice President (VP) of Growth Marketing and the VP of Product and Technical Marketing, reporting to the VP of Growth Marketing. You’ll help both leaders focus on their highest-impact work across teams spanning digital experience, marketing operations, events, community and developer relations, campaigns, product marketing, and launches. Beyond calendar management, you’ll join leadership discussions, keep projects and recurring meetings moving, and connect the leaders with their organizations through a monthly launch rhythm. In your first year, you’ll build trusted relationships with both leaders, their teams, and the other executive support partners, and establish reliable processes for scheduling, meeting preparation, travel, events, recruiting coordination, and expenses. You’ll anticipate needs four to six weeks ahead and follow commitments through to completion. You’ll move quickly, build AI-powered workflows to automate recurring work, and be a warm, approachable partner to the people on both leaders’ teams. What you’ll do Own high-volume calendars across time zones for both leaders, connecting meeting priorities to quarterly goals and business rhythms. Protect focus time, recommend how to prioritize or delegate requests, and provide inbox triage when requested. Prepare leadership meetings, all-hands, and the monthly in-person launch review: coordinate agendas, pre-reads, and attendance, keep time, and capture and follow up on action items. Maintain the leadership project tracker and weekly leadership, monthly goals, and quarterly business reviews. Build work-back plans, follow up with owners, and flag delays, deadlines, and decisions so the right people stay involved and informed. Partner with senior directors and team leads across both organizations, helping when it moves the leaders’ priorities and bringing the leaders in when it matters. Support team connection by remembering milestones and getting notes, gifts, or flowers out quickly. Coordinate domestic and international travel, including transportation, lodging, security needs, and documentation. Arrange alternatives when plans change, and prepare accurate monthly expense reports with receipts and reconciliation. Plan team offsites, leadership working sessions, and company kickoffs from start to finish, working with event partners and confirming reservations, deposits, and logistics through completion. Partner with Recruiting and hiring teams on interviews, candidate communication, and onboarding logistics, and with People, information technology (IT), and Security on team needs. Provide coverage for executive support partners, help onboard new support partners, and take on Marketing special projects. Build and maintain artificial intelligence (AI) workflows, with Claude as our standard, to automate research, drafting, meeting summaries, and recurring administrative work. Check outputs for accuracy and context rather than relying on automated summaries alone. Identify recurring friction in how the teams work, and propose and implement improvements to processes and documentation. What you’ll bring Experience supporting two or more VP-level leaders simultaneously at a fast-growing company, ideally SaaS or AI-forward, adapting to distinct working styles and business priorities. A bias for action and ownership: you move from ask to done quickly, act independently within agreed priorities, and communicate progress proactively, with an organized, reliable approach to balancing priorities. Comfort figuring things out on your own in a fast-changing environment with limited resources. Experience managing complex calendars across time zones, using judgment to balance competing requests and checking scheduling details and attendees. Project management skills, including building work-back plans, following up with owners, and keeping multiple workstreams moving with minimal oversight. Experience coordinating international travel and team events from start to finish, anticipating dependencies, responding to disruptions, and confirming arrangements through completion. Proficiency with Google Workspace, Slack, Zoom, Okta, Navan, Claude, and GitLab, alongside experience with expense reporting, recruiting coordination, and onboarding logistics. Clear, approachable communication across functions and levels, and a track record of building trust with executives, peers, and other executive support partners. Sound judgment and discretion with confidential information. Experience in a remote, asynchronous environment where documentation and written communication are central. Flexibility to support meetings across time zones outside standard hours on occasion, with up to quarterly travel for team meetings and all-hands (about four trips a year, three to four days each). Located in the US or Canada, with strong overlap with Pacific time. About the team You’ll join GitLab’s Marketing organization and support two leaders who together lead about 120 people in a fast-moving, AI-forward organization. Their teams work closely together on campaigns, launches, and events. You’ll work alongside a globally distributed group of executive support professionals who collaborate asynchronously through clear documentation and shared processes. The focus is dependable support: keeping priorities clear, coordinating global travel and events, and ensuring commitments are completed and communicated as plans change.
