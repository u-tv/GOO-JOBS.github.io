If you’re the type of person who values curiosity over bureaucracy, and that AI is a partner in solving tough problems to keep the Internet moving forward, you’ll fit right in. About the Team The Developer GTM organization is driving how we attract, inspire, and grow the next generation of builders. About the Role Scale-ups are where developer platform bets become durable at scale. These are companies that have found product-market fit, are growing fast, and are building the platform and infrastructure teams that will define their architecture for years. The choices they make now about where to run workloads, how to serve users globally, and which platforms to build on will compound. Cloudflare needs to be in the room when those decisions are made. We are hiring a Head of Scale-ups to own Cloudflare's Developer Platform GTM strategy for the scale-up segment. This role is the strategic GTM leader who sets the vision for how Cloudflare wins with the fastest-growing technology companies in the world, drives executive engagement with their engineering leadership, and builds the commercial trajectory from early adoption through large-scale committed spend. You will identify the highest-potential accounts, lead the engagement strategy, and work with the field to turn usage into lasting commercial relationships. We are looking for someone who has operated inside or worked closely with high-growth category leaders (e.g. Figma, Notion, Canva, Atlassian, OpenAI, or Anthropic). You understand what it means to scale an engineering organization from Series B/C through enterprise maturity, stand up a platform team, and make infrastructure bets under rapid growth. You bring developer fluency and commercial rigor in equal measure. You can sit with a VP of Engineering and discuss workload architecture in the morning, then work through a multi-year commit structure with Deal Desk in the afternoon. What You Will Do Define and execute the scale-ups GTM strategy. Build the operating model for how Cloudflare acquires, activates, and grows scale-up accounts. Set goals, scorecards, and cadences to prioritize high-potential targets and optimize field resource allocation. Lead executive engagement. Build trusted relationships with CTOs, VPs of Engineering, and Heads of Platform at scale-up companies. Run impactful executive business reviews and strategically loop in Cloudflare leadership into the conversation at the right moments. Drive activation, expansion, and commercial structure. Use usage telemetry to map next-best actions, expansion paths, and risks. Work with the account team, Deal Desk, Pricing, Finance, and Legal to design commit structures and consumption-based agreements that match how scale-ups actually build and grow. Identify and prioritize high-potential accounts. Combine usage data, market signals, and ecosystem knowledge to build a target list of scale-ups where Cloudflare's Developer Platform can become deeply embedded. Track their growth trajectories and time engagement to inflection points. Mobilize cross-functional account teams. Align Sales, Solutions Architects, Forward Deployed Engineers, and Product on account priorities. Establish clear owners and drive follow-through. Run GTM motions in step with the workloads where Cloudflare has proven fit. Enable the field on scale-up motions. Ship playbooks, talk tracks, deal patterns, and expansion frameworks tailored to hyper-growth accounts. Run enablement sessions with Sales leadership and measure adoption and lift. Close the loop with Product. Translate what you see in scale-up accounts into clear, quantified recommendations on positioning, packaging, pricing, limits, and roadmap. Scale-ups push products to their limits earlier than most customers. Make sure Product hears those signals. Partner with Marketing and BDR Teams. Drive scale-up-targeted campaigns, events, and content in coordination with Marketing and BDR. Build Cloudflare's presence in the communities and channels where scale-up engineering leaders spend their time. Who You Are Experience working with or inside scale-up ecosystems and hyper-growth technology companies. You understand the challenges, pace, and decision-making patterns of companies scaling from hundreds to thousands of engineers. Developer fluency: you understand developer workloads, platform engineering, and infrastructure decision-making. Executive presence and credibility with VP of Engineering, CTO, and Head of Platform audiences at high-growth technology companies. Background in platform engineering, developer tools, or infrastructure, whether as a practitioner or in a technical GTM role. Hands-on familiarity with Cloudflare's Developer Platform.
