Internet properties powered by Cloudflare all have web traffic routed through its intelligent global network, which gets smarter with every request. As a result, they see significant improvement in performance and a decrease in spam and other attacks. Cloudflare was named to Entrepreneur Magazine’s Top Company Cultures list and ranked among the World’s Most Innovative Companies by Fast Company. At Cloudflare, we’re not looking for people who wait for a polished roadmap; we’re looking for the builders who see the cracks in the Internet that everyone else has simply learned to live with. We value candidates who have the instinct to spot a &quot;normalized&quot; problem and the AI-native curiosity to create a solution using the latest tools. If you’re the type of person who values curiosity over bureaucracy, and that AI is a partner in solving tough problems to keep the Internet moving forward, you’ll fit right in. Available Locations: London, New York City, Denver, Lisbon, Seattle, or Austin About the Role Millions of websites, applications, APIs, and AI agents run on Cloudflare, and the traffic we handle for them produces a lot of data. Some customers want to debug applications and investigate security events in Cloudflare. Others want that data in a stack they already trust, such as Splunk, Datadog, Grafana, or their own data lake. Many want both. We're hiring one Senior Product Manager for each of two areas. You apply once, and we'll work out together which role fits you best. Observability Platform brings logs, traces, analytics, alerts, and dashboards together in one place, so customers can see what's happening and why. Today most observability ends with a notification, and the customer does the rest. We want a platform that understands the problem, takes action where it can, and asks before doing anything risky. Telemetry Export delivers Cloudflare's data to the tools customers choose. Logpush handles most of that delivery today, and Workers Observability sends logs and traces over the OpenTelemetry Protocol (OTLP). Customers want OTLP for the rest of Cloudflare's data, metrics pushed to their own tools, and a way to send us telemetry from workloads that run elsewhere. Your mission is to make reliability and security something customers rarely have to worry about, with the data they need in Cloudflare or in the tools they already use. What You'll Do Talk with customers every day and deeply understand their needs. They include developers building on Cloudflare, security teams who feed our logs into a SIEM, and platform teams who run OpenTelemetry pipelines. Own the strategy, roadmap, and execution for your area, and make hard trade-offs between speed, retention, reliability, and cost. Build on open standards like OpenTelemetry, W3C Trace Context, and SQL so teams can keep using the tools they already have. Own the business, including pricing, packaging, and revenue. Measure progress by adoption and reliability. In Observability Platform , you'll also: Bring every Cloudflare product into one observability experience, so customers can follow a problem from one product to the next without switching tools. Move beyond notifications to workflows that investigate, roll back, or fix problems, with guardrails customers control. Give people and their agents the context they need to act safely, including how services connect and depend on each other. Own the experience end to end, from first setup to the middle of an incident or an audit. In Telemetry Export , you'll also: Bring Logpush, Workers Logpush, and the OpenTelemetry destinations in Workers Observability together as one product, and keep existing integrations working. Ship metrics export and extend OTLP delivery to more of our datasets. Own the intake path for telemetry from outside Cloudflare; Observability Platform will store and query it. Make delivery something customers can trust, so they can see whether their data arrived, get a useful alert when it didn't, and know how to recover. Help customers filter and transform data before it's sent, so they export only what they need. Who You Are You've shipped products people depend on, as a PM, engineer, engineering manager, SRE, or founder, and you can point to what changed because of your work. You move fast and own outcomes. You find the most important problem, get the team behind it, ship, and measure. You are a builder at heart. You love getting to a deep understanding of customer needs, and shipping things that help them accomplish their goals. You are passionate about the Observability industry and want to be on the forefront of innovating what it can turn into. You understand how telemetry systems fail, whether that's a delivery pipeline under backpressure or a query that scans far more data than it needs, and you can explain the reliability and cost trade-offs to engineers and to customers. AI is already central to how you work. You use it to prototype ideas and dig through data, and you have a view on how agents will produce telemetry and act on it. Examples of Desirable Skills, Knowledge, and Experience You've relied on observability yourself, on call during an outage or in a security investigation, and you know where today's tools fall short. You've run or built on telemetry pipelines such as the OpenTelemetry Collector, Vector, Fluent Bit, or Kafka, or you've ingested someone else's data at scale at a vendor. You notice friction, like a confusing error message or a query that takes too many steps, and you fix it. You've priced a usage-based product, and you know what happens when existing customers start paying for something that used to be included. You're comfortable with enterprise customers, whether you're talking about compliance with a security team or OTLP with a platform engineer. You write clearly enough to get teams across Cloudflare to agree on a plan, and you can translate between what customers need and what engineering builds.
