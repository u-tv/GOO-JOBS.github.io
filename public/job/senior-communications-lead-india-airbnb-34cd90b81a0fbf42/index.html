Senior Communications Lead, India

🏢 airbnb 📍 Gurugram, India 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Airbnb was born in 2007 when two hosts welcomed three guests to their San Francisco home, and has since grown to over 5 million hosts who have welcomed over 2 billion guest arrivals in almost every country across the globe. Every day, hosts offer unique stays and experiences that make it possible for guests to connect with communities in a more authentic way. The Community you will join: The communications team is responsible for telling the Airbnb story around the world. Whether we are briefing press on inspiring travel trends, highlighting unique experiences led by local hosts, or explaining how travel on our platform benefits local families and communities, we are the voice of Airbnb on a broad range of topics. We're a fast growing and high energy team with a passion for people, places and great stories. The difference you will make: Based in India, we're looking for a Senior Communications Lead, India, to own Airbnb's communications strategy in one of our most important growth markets. Reporting to the APAC Communications Director, you'll lead the India communications team and agency partners. You'll drive the work behind our consumer, policy and corporate goals, and act as a senior advisor to India leadership. You'll shape how Airbnb is seen in India, making sure local plans meet business needs while staying in line with regional and global priorities. We're looking for someone who can lead Consumer, Product, Policy and Corporate Communications under one roof, thinking holistically about strategy across all of them. You're an experienced, strategic communicator who is at ease with senior executives, government stakeholders and top-tier media. You can handle complexity, ambiguity and pressure, and you bring sound judgment to high-stakes moments.
