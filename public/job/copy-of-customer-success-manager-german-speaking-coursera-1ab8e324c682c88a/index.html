About this role As a Customer Success Manager, you will partner with valued enterprise customers across the DACH market to ensure successful adoption of Udemy Business and help them achieve their strategic learning and development goals. You will build trusted relationships with customer stakeholders, from programme managers and learning champions to senior executives, acting as a strategic advisor throughout the customer lifecycle. You will take a consultative approach to understanding customer needs, developing learning strategies, driving platform engagement, and demonstrating measurable business impact. Working closely with Sales and cross-functional teams, you will take ownership of customer retention and renewals while identifying opportunities for account growth and expansion. You will bring strong commercial awareness, a proactive mindset, and the ability to engage confidently with customers in both German and English.
