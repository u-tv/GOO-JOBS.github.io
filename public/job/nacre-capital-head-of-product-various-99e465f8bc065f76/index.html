Nacre Capital: Head of Product

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Chile URL: https://jobs.workable.com/company/8rWSxRWEU6b9TEpdx1KtdS/jobs-at-nacre-capital FDNA is a pioneer in AI-powered phenotyping and precision health, dedicated to expediting the diagnosis and care management of rare genetic and developmental disorders. Used across more than 10,000 medical centers in over 130 countries, our ecosystem and products bridges the gap between concerned parents, healthcare providers, and life sciences organization. About the Role We are seeking a strategic, execution-driven Head of Product based in Latin America to own the end-to-end product vision, roadmap, and delivery across FDNA’s clinical, patient-facing, and interoperability product lines. Reporting directly to the executive leadership team (working closely with our Chief Product & Technology Officer, Chief Medical Officer, and AI Research leads), you will translate cutting-edge multimodal AI capabilities into intuitive, compliant, and clinically impactful products tailored primarily for the US healthcare ecosystem. Key Responsibilities Product Strategy & Lifecycle Ownership: Define and execute the multi-product roadmap across Face2Gene, Family Health Checker, and FDNA’s Provider/Pharma Data Clearinghouse, balancing clinician utility, patient engagement, and B2B/life sciences monetization. US Clinical Workflow & EHR Integration: Architect seamless product experiences that fit naturally into US pediatric, genetic, and primary care clinical workflows, driving HL7/FHIR-based EHR integrations, digital intake, and specialist referral pathways. Regulatory & Compliance Governance: Partner with Legal, Clinical, and Security teams to ensure all product features strictly adhere to HIPAA privacy/security mandates, de-identification protocols, and FDA Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) / Clinical Decision Support (CDS) regulatory boundaries. AI-to-Product Translation: Collaborate closely with AI/ML engineering and medical genetics teams to productize novel multimodal models (facial analysis, video, audio, and clinical notes) while maintaining high standards for clinical transparency, bias mitigation, and explainability. Cross-Functional & Nearshore Leadership: Lead, mentor, and scale a distributed product management and UX/UI design function across time zones, fostering tight alignment between LATAM-based teams, US-based commercial/clinical stakeholders, and global R&D centers. Customer & Market Discovery: Conduct continuous discovery with US health systems, pediatric specialists, genetic counselors, patient advocacy groups, and pharma/CRO partners to validate product-market fit and measure clinical and commercial outcomes. - Experience: 6+ years of progressive product management experience, including at least 2+ years in a senior leadership role (Director or Head of Product) owning multiple interconnected software products. - Dedicated US Healthcare Exposure (Mandatory): ≥50% of your career (3+ years minimum) must be directly dedicated to building and scaling products for the US healthcare market, demonstrating hands-on mastery of: HIPAA Compliance & Data Privacy: Managing PHI/PII, BAAs, SOC 2 standards, and de-identified real-world data (RWD) exchange. FDA & SaMD Parameters: Navigating FDA guidance on Clinical Decision Support (CDS) software, SaMD classifications, and non-diagnostic informational labeling. US EHR Integrations: Hands-on experience shipping integrations with major US EHRs (e.g., Epic, Oracle Cerner, athenahealth) utilizing HL7, FHIR, SMART on FHIR, or clearinghouse APIs. US Clinical Workflows: Deep familiarity with US provider intake, referral management, care coordination, and specialty clinical environments. - Location & Alignment: Residing in any Latin American country with strong overlap with US Eastern/Central business hours and fluent, executive-level English communication skills. - AI / Data-Intensive Product Track Record: Proven experience bringing data-heavy, algorithmic, or AI/ML-powered healthcare applications from concept to commercial adoption. Preferred Qualifications Prior experience with rare diseases, clinical genomics, pediatrics, digital diagnostics, or life sciences/CRO real-world data platforms. Background working in high-growth healthtech startups or distributed international R&D organizations. Familiarity with consumer-to-provider ("digital front door") patient acquisition and engagement funnels. CV/Resume submissions MUST be in English.
