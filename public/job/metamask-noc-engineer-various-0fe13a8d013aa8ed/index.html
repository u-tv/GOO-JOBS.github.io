MetaMask: NOC Engineer

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: EMEA - Remote Please note that we are unable to consider applications from candidates based in France, Italy, or Germany for this role. Money is moving onto the internet, and the shift has a name: Open Money. This is money that is open, portable, agentic, and owned by you. MetaMask spent the last ten years building it; with 100M+ downloads, users in ~190 countries, and trillions in cumulative transaction volume, it's the most trusted self-custodial financial platform on the internet. Now we're building the operating system for your money: one place to hold, move, grow, and use anything you own. Join a remote-first, global team rebuilding how money works: a problem that touches everyone, every day. Our mission is to unlock the collaborative power of communities by making Web3 universally easy to use, access, and build on. Working with MetaMask puts you at the forefront of an evolving paradigm , transforming our society for the better. We believe blockchain is the next generation of technology that can lay the foundation for a more just and equitable society. Blockchain tech is just over 10 years old. Ethereum itself is still a toddler, and we’re far from reaching our full potential. You’ll get to work on the tools, infrastructure, and apps that scale these platforms to billions of users. You’ll be constantly exposed to new concepts, ideas, and frameworks from your peers, and as you work on different projects — challenging you to stay at the top of your game . You’ll join a network of entrepreneurs and technologists that reaches the edge of our ecosystem . About NOC As a NOC Engineer at MetaMask, you will be responsible for the proactive monitoring of our production services serving millions of users, troubleshooting and solving issues as effectively as possible to minimize customer impact, and working closely with our SRE engineers to maximize the platform's stability, reliability, availability and security. What you’ll do Monitor our production services availability, serving millions of users Troubleshoot and resolve infrastructure and security issues with our production environments Participate in incident management when dealing with critical incidents Partner with SRE & DevOps engineers to improve our monitoring, alerting, and remediation solutions Partner with SRE, developers, and DevOps engineers to develop and enhance our operational runbooks to continuously improve the NOC effectiveness Would be great if you brought this to the role Software development or automation experience Experience working with cloud providers such as AWS and Azure Experienced in troubleshooting techniques and problem-solving in a 24x7x365 production environment Understanding of monitoring concepts and solutions Knowledge of blockchain technology and the Ethereum ecosystem Don't meet all the requirements?
