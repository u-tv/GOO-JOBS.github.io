LUX Infusion: Director of Referral Services

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: United States URL: http://www.luxinfusion.com See your work in a new light. At LUX Infusion, we’re reimagining infusion care to be more human, supportive, and connected. Inspired by lux —meaning light - we guide patients, providers, and partners through complex therapies with clarity, compassion, and confidence. As a clinician-led, U.S.-based organization, we put people first - helping patients feel seen and supported through faster therapy starts, dedicated care coordination, and meaningful collaboration. Our commitment to inclusion, diversity, equity, and advancement (IDEA) is central to our culture, ensuring every team member feels valued and empowered to make a difference. We’re looking for an Associate Director of Referral Services who wants their work to mean something—someone who brings care, accountability, and purpose to how referrals become treatments and how systems become services. Why This Role Matters The Associate Director of Referral Services plays a critical role in delivering a patient‑first infusion experience by ensuring referrals move efficiently, accurately, and compassionately from intake to care delivery. The Associate Director of Referral Services will be responsible for the oversight and management of the front end referral process including, Benefit Verification and PA processes for LUX Infusion Neurology, Immunology, Transplant and Specialty Infusion areas. The Associate Director will understand the specific BV and PA roles and support the day-to-day department activities. This position will work closely and collaboratively with other internal department leads, including Intake Coordination CRT, Nursing Services and Pharmacy Services. In this role, you’ll contribute to work that: Ensures patients experience timely, coordinated access to infusion therapy Supports clinicians and referral partners through clarity, responsiveness, and reliability Strengthens the foundation of care delivery through strong reimbursement, documentation, and intake workflows Every referral represents a real person navigating a complex healthcare moment—and your leadership helps that journey begin with confidence and trust. What You’ll Do ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES: Set standard metrics and goals for Referral Management team members for pull through of referrals, documentation management, BV, PA processing Sets the tone for the Referral Management departments, develops and supports LUX Infusion’s culture in the work environment, and ensures there is opportunity for team professional Establish training protocols for new employees and evaluate performance against metrics/goals; and determine corrective action or training plans to support performance improvement when needed Establishes communication processes and procedures for Referral Management team members to engage necessary internal and external stakeholders to gather missing or inaccurate information Work cohesively with internal department leadership to ensure continuity of patient referral activities are performed optimally. Provide administrative and/or leadership support for accreditation related process or data collection activities that entail information from the Intake or Referral departments. Work collaboratively with referral and pharmacy operations management peers to support successful outcomes overall end to end processes to support getting referrals to their proper destination Daily & Weekly (or as needed) – Review department alignment with stated SLAs for the department, identify trends among reporting periods, and take action with team or peers as needed Support staffing and resource adjustments as found necessary to support referral volumes and overall growth goals for the organization Maintains necessary and thoughtful communications to the intake and Patient Services specialist team members delivering information supplied by leadership with impact to people, process, and technology updates within a given location. Serves as a point of escalation for issue management and resolution support related to services provided by Intake & Referral Specialists NON-ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS & RESPONSIBILITIES Triages work and assigns duties to staff Perform employee management tasks, including, but not limited to review of productivity, management of job descriptions, timely performance reviews, disciplinary process/corrective action, and all associated personnel paperwork.
