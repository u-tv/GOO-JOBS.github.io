Montu: Martech Platforms & Data Manager (Braze)

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Melbourne, Victoria, Australia URL: https://www.montu.com.au Company Description Montu is Australia's largest healthtech business with a focus on alternative healthcare. Founded in 2019, it supports patients, doctors and pharmacies through its Alternaleaf clinic, offers accredited healthcare education to clinicians via its SAGED platform, and provides dispensing solutions to pharmacies across Australia through Leafio. Montu is also the founding member of Cannabis Council Australia, a not-for-profit advocacy body that advances legislative change across the healthcare landscape. Guided by our Culture of Care, our mission is to make alternative healthcare more affordable and accessible for the millions who could benefit. This commitment was recently recognised at the Cannabiz Awards, where Montu took home Best Use of Technology for Alternaleaf's IDC and Distributor of the Year for Leafio. Job Description Sitting at the heart of CRM architecture and data engineering, you will support the technical platform function, directing the design, scaling, and integration of internal martech infrastructure across Montu Group’s ecosystems. Your remit spans platform architecture, data flow integrity, technical roadmaps, and the delivery of complex integrations to minimise engineering reliance. In this role, you will build the robust technical foundation that powers scalable, automated, and hyper-personalised communications. You will support the configuration and governance of the martech stack, ensuring operational resilience and enabling advanced segmentation through sophisticated event and attribute schemas and seamless data orchestration. Hands on experience with Braze is essential. Key Responsibilities: Platform Architecture & Technical Strategy: Integration Leadership: Lead the independent delivery of complex technical integrations (e.g., Zendesk, Litecard, Survicate) into the CRM environment to accelerate campaign delivery and business impact. System Architecture: Drive strategic oversight of data architecture, managing pipelines and performing complex queries to power audience targeting and the definition of core business metrics. Operational Resilience: Maintain high standards of implementation and QA across the tech stack, ensuring the reliability of data flows and the integrity of event-driven automation at scale. Platform Ownership & Innovation: Platform Strategy: Act as Braze platform owner, overseeing configuration, health, and performance. Drive adoption of advanced capabilities including dynamic personalisation, modular content, and journey automation. Feature Enablement: Champion innovation by identifying, testing, and rolling out new platform features, working closely with Lifecycle, Product, and Data teams to maximise impact. Channel Management: Lead CRM delivery across email, SMS, in-app messaging, content cards, landing pages, web forms, and mobile experiences. Automation, Testing & Platform Integrity: Lifecycle Orchestration: Develop and optimise patient lifecycle automations using custom events, attributes, and data triggers to deliver timely and relevant communications. Issue Resolution: Diagnose and resolve logic, data, and platform issues that impact journey execution or personalisation accuracy. Act as a key escalation point for the team. QA & Testing Standards: Establish and maintain robust testing frameworks, including UAT, journey validation, and ongoing performance monitoring. Data Architecture & Personalisation: Data-Driven Targeting: Work with the Data team to activate behavioural and transactional signals to support intelligent segmentation and personalisation at scale. Attribute & Event Management: Maintain a structured and scalable CRM data architecture, including user attributes, tags, and custom events. Integration Oversight: Ensure reliability and integrity of data flows across systems (e.g. Segment, Hightouch, APIs), collaborating with engineering and product stakeholders where needed. Platform Knowledge & Continual Development: Trends & Innovations: Stay abreast of emerging CRM trends, martech advancements, and patient engagement strategies. Translate insights into actionable improvements across CRM programs. Operational Excellence: Identify and implement process enhancements to increase the efficiency, scalability, and resilience of CRM operations. Team Enablement & Cross-Functional Collaboration: Training & Capability Building: Upskill internal teams on platform features, logic frameworks, and best practice execution. Share documentation and promote technical excellence across the CRM team. Stakeholder Alignment: Serve as the key CRM technical liaison across Product, Marketing, Tech, Data, and Compliance. Provide regular updates on platform performance, risks, and roadmaps. Compliance, Data Governance & Sensitivity: Privacy and Compliance Management: Ensure all CRM initiatives adhere to the Privacy Act, SPAM Act, and other relevant data protection regulations. Follow best practices to safeguard patient data, and ensure all communications are compliant with regulations. Legislation Awareness: Stay informed on changes to relevant legislation (such as updates from the Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA) and Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC) and ensure all campaigns comply with updated guidelines. Communicate these updates to stakeholders as necessary. Consent & Personal Data: Capture patient consent at key stages of the communication journey, and ensure data is used in accordance with privacy laws. Use this data to enhance profiling and personalise communication to better meet patient needs. Qualifications Education: Bachelor’s degree in Marketing Technology, Information Systems, Communications, or a related field. Experience: 6+ years in CRM operations, marketing automation, or MarTech platform roles. Experience working in healthcare or other regulated industries is advantageous Platform Expertise: Hands-on experience with CEPs such as Braze (mandatory), Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Adobe Campaign, or Iterable. Skilled in managing user attributes, custom events, Liquid personalisation, journey logic, content cards, and multichannel enablement Technical Skills: Proficiency with scripting and markup languages including Liquid, HTML/CSS, JSON, and SQL. Comfortable navigating Braze’s developer tools, audience logic, and debugging environments. Experience with AI platforms such as Gemini and Claude. System & Data Integration: Familiarity with CDPs (e.g. Segment, Hightouch), event/attribute schemas, and data flows that support lifecycle automation. Understanding of API interactions and cross-platform sync issues. Channel Enablement: Strong understanding of how to optimise channels like email, SMS, in-app messaging, content cards, and webhooks for lifecycle communications Issue Resolution & Troubleshooting: Experience diagnosing and resolving platform issues, campaign misfires, or automation bugs through structured testing and collaboration with technical teams. Training & Collaboration: Ability to upskill others on platform features and champion best practices in naming conventions, tagging, and QA. Confident working cross-functionally with Product, Data, and CRM stakeholders Governance & Compliance: Working knowledge of privacy legislation (Privacy Act, SPAM Act), data protection practices, consent frameworks, and unsubscribe handling. Mindset: Curious, detail-oriented, and delivery-focused. Comfortable in fast-paced, agile environments with a passion for building infrastructure that supports scale, accuracy, and improved patient experiences.
