Account Director (Network Testing & Planning)

🏢 Jobgether 📍 UK Only 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for an Account Director (Network Testing & Planning) based in the United Kingdom. We are seeking an experienced Account Director to drive revenue growth and expand strategic customer relationships across the UK, Ireland, and selected major European accounts. In this senior commercial role, you will lead sales strategy and business development for a portfolio of mobile network testing, planning, and optimisation solutions. You will be responsible for growing existing accounts, securing new customers, and building a sustainable pipeline across the telecommunications ecosystem. Working closely with technical, marketing, product, and sales teams, you will develop compelling value propositions and deliver solutions that address complex customer challenges. You will engage with mobile network operators, service providers, infrastructure companies, and other key industry stakeholders. This is an exciting opportunity to influence the evolution of next-generation networks, including 5G, IoT, and emerging connectivity technologies, within a collaborative and globally distributed environment. Accountabilities: Take full ownership of territory revenue targets, sales pipeline development, and forecasting across the UK, Ireland, and assigned strategic European accounts. Develop and execute comprehensive territory and account plans aligned with business growth objectives and commercial priorities. Expand existing strategic customer relationships through account penetration, upselling, cross-selling, and the development of new business opportunities. Identify, prospect, and acquire new customers across the mobile network testing, planning, optimisation, and broader telecommunications ecosystem. Build and maintain strong relationships with senior commercial, technical, and operational stakeholders within target accounts. Understand customer business objectives, technical challenges, and operational requirements, translating them into compelling commercial opportunities and tailored value propositions. Develop robust business cases demonstrating measurable customer benefits, supporting larger-scale engagements and long-term partnerships. Manage the complete sales lifecycle, from prospecting, qualification, and discovery through solution development, proposal preparation, negotiation, and contract closure. Identify competitive displacement opportunities and position alternative network testing and planning solutions to increase market share. Explore new market segments and addressable opportunities, including private networks, neutral hosts, infrastructure providers, engineering service companies, and adjacent telecommunications markets. Develop and leverage strategic partnerships and channel relationships to strengthen market coverage and create additional routes to market. Collaborate with Solution Engineering teams on technical qualification, product demonstrations, customer evaluations, and proof-of-concept (PoC) initiatives. Work closely with Inside Sales and Demand Generation teams to identify target accounts, generate qualified leads, build pipeline, and accelerate sales opportunities. Maintain accurate CRM records, ensuring clear visibility of opportunity status, pipeline coverage, customer engagement, next steps, and revenue forecasts. Apply disciplined sales methodologies and opportunity qualification processes to ensure predictable growth and consistent commercial performance. Strengthen customer and partner relationships to generate repeat business, increase engagement value, and establish sustainable revenue streams. Requirements: Demonstrated success in B2B technology sales, with a proven track record of managing and achieving revenue targets and sales quotas. Significant experience selling to telecommunications operators, network equipment vendors, or organisations operating within the broader mobile network ecosystem. Strong understanding of mobile network technologies and the processes involved in planning, deploying, testing, optimising, and operating mobile networks. Proven ability to develop existing strategic accounts while successfully identifying and acquiring new customers. Experience managing complex, consultative sales cycles involving multiple commercial, technical, and operational stakeholders. Excellent account planning, business development, opportunity qualification, negotiation, and closing capabilities. Strong commercial acumen and the ability to build compelling business cases, demonstrating measurable customer value beyond product features or pricing. Experience working closely with technical presales and solution engineering teams while maintaining ownership of commercial strategy and sales outcomes. Strong pipeline management, revenue forecasting, CRM discipline, and sales performance reporting skills. Ability to work autonomously across a geographically distributed territory while collaborating effectively within an international organisation. Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English, with the ability to influence senior decision-makers and build trusted customer relationships. Previous experience in network testing, drive testing, network planning, RF optimisation, network performance, or related telecommunications software and solutions would be a significant advantage. Experience gained within telecom software providers, network equipment manufacturers, test and measurement companies, engineering consultancies, service organisations, or technology suppliers serving mobile network operators is particularly relevant. Knowledge of the competitive landscape for network testing, planning, and optimisation solutions would be highly desirable. A proactive, results-oriented, and entrepreneurial mindset, with strong relationship-building skills and the ability to identify and pursue new commercial opportunities. Benefits: Competitive compensation package and benefits. Fully remote working arrangement within the UK. Flexible working hours and adaptable working locations. Inclusive and collaborative international working environment that values diverse perspectives. Opportunities to contribute to innovative technologies shaping next-generation telecommunications networks. Career development opportunities and structured professional growth plans. Access to a global network of experienced professionals across multiple countries and technical disciplines. Commitment to sustainability through environmental, social, and governance (ESG) initiatives. A workplace culture built around transparency, innovation, integrity, collaboration, and employee well-being. Ongoing IT security training and awareness initiatives to support a secure working environment.
