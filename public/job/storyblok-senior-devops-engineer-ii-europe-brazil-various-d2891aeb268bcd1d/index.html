Storyblok: Senior DevOps Engineer II - Europe, Brazil

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote Storyblok is a headless CMS that enables marketers and developers to create with joy and publish with confidence. It empowers you to deliver structured and consistent content everywhere: websites, apps, AI search, and beyond. Legendary brands like Virgin Media O2, Oatly, and TomTom use Storyblok to make a bigger, faster market impact. WHAT IS IN IT FOR YOU You will be joining a growing company where you can contribute to many “firsts”. Plus these benefits: Monthly remote work stipend (home internet costs, electricity). Home office equipment package right at the start (laptop, keyboard, monitor…) Home office equipment upgrade (furniture, ear plugs …) or membership to a local co-working space after your onboarding Sick leave benefit, parental leave and 25 days of annual leave plus your local national holidays Personal development fund for courses, books, conferences, and material The annual international team-building trip, quarterly and monthly online get-togethers As a fully remote company, with work-life balance at its core, you’ll enjoy flexible schedules An international team that loves to have fun at work and works hard together to accomplish shared goals JOB SUMMARY The Senior DevOps Engineer II operates beyond individual feature ownership and takes responsibility for major infrastructure subsystems, multi-cycle initiatives, and complex technical challenges that impact multiple teams. This role works closely with Engineering Managers, software engineering, Security, and fellow infrastructure engineers to drive scalable, reliable, and secure platform solutions and is expected to clarify ambiguous problems, lead technical design efforts, and elevate engineering standards across the Infrastructure pillar. Success in this role is measured not only by the delivery of high-quality systems but also by the ability to create technical leverage, strengthen reliability, and increase the organization's execution capacity. ESSENTIAL JOB FUNCTIONS Be accountable for the technical health, scalability, and long-term sustainability of a defined infrastructure domain or platform area. Lead complex, multi-cycle infrastructure initiatives with end-to-end ownership — from problem framing and architecture design through to measurable delivery and stabilization. Design robust, scalable, and resilient infrastructure architectures and implementation plans that reduce operational risk and improve platform performance. Break down ambiguous or loosely defined infrastructure challenges into clear technical strategies with explicit trade-offs, and communicate these effectively to both technical and non-technical stakeholders. Collaborate closely with Engineering Managers, Product, and cross-functional teams to align infrastructure work with business priorities and engineering roadmaps. Improve platform reliability by proactively identifying systemic risks, technical debt, and architectural bottlenecks within the domain and drive remediation before they impact teams or customers. Contribute hands-on to infrastructure code and configuration, delivering high-quality, secure, well-structured, and maintainable work (Terraform, CI/CD pipelines, platform automation). Contribute to the evolution of CI/CD pipelines, Infrastructure-as-Code practices, and platform automation standards, pushing for improvements that benefit teams beyond your immediate scope. Apply and advocate for cloud infrastructure best practices (primarily AWS), covering cost efficiency, scalability, security compliance, and operational excellence across your domain. Partner with software engineering, security, and QA teams to ensure infrastructure considerations are embedded early in the development lifecycle and architectural decisions. Drive security posture improvements within your domain using cloud security and vulnerability management tools (such as Aikido), moving the team from reactive remediation toward proactive risk reduction. Improve monitoring, observability, and incident response practices within your domain — contributing to better detection, root cause analysis, and long-term remediation. Raise engineering standards through thoughtful code and design reviews, documentation, and technical guidance. Mentor mid-level and senior engineers across the Infrastructure pillar, supporting their growth toward greater technical ownership. Support production stability by actively participating in debugging, root cause analysis, and long-term remediation efforts. Act as a technical reference point within a defined infrastructure domain, fostering alignment and reducing cross-team friction. Demonstrate AI literacy by actively applying AI tools to improve infrastructure workflows and drive measurable productivity gains across the team. EDUCATION AND EXPERIENCE 9+ years of relevant experience as a DevOps, Infrastructure, or Site Reliability Engineer, with demonstrated ownership of complex systems or subsystems. Proven experience designing and operating large-scale applications on AWS (GCP or Azure a plus). Strong expertise with Infrastructure-as-Code, primarily Terraform, including reusable modules, state management, and governance patterns. Experience designing secure, scalable CI/CD pipelines and release automation for production-grade systems (e.g. GitHub actions) Experience leading technical initiatives, platform migrations, or large refactors across teams is highly valued. Deep understanding of cloud-native architectures, including compute platforms such as ECS, EKS, Fargate, Lambda or equivalents. Strong Linux fundamentals, networking, and fluency in scripting languages such as Bash, Ruby, Python, or Go. Experience with PostgreSQL operations and familiarity with caching and queueing systems such as Redis or Sidekiq. Strong experience with observability tooling (e.g., Prometheus, Grafana, ELK, OpenTelemetry, Datadog), SLOs, and incident response practices. Proven ability to apply zero-downtime deployments, autoscaling, and capacity planning in production environments. Strong knowledge of security best practices, including IAM, secrets management, network segmentation, and vulnerability management. Hands-on experience with cloud security and vulnerability management platforms (such as Aikido, Snyk, or Wiz), including triage, remediation workflows, and integration into CI/CD. Familiarity with supporting modern AI/ML services or data-intensive workflows is desirable. Excellent knowledge of how to identify and fix performance bottlenecks across compute, storage, and network layers. AWS Certified DevOps Engineer – Professional (or equivalent) is a strong plus. Strong problem-solving skills and ability to operate effectively in complex, evolving environments. Excellent written and verbal communication skills in a collaborative, remote-first setting. Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience.
