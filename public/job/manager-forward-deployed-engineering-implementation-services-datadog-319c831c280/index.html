Manager, Forward Deployed Engineering - Implementation Services

🏢 datadog 📍 New York, New York, USA 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Datadog’s Implementation Services team leads end-to-end deployment and optimization projects. Our Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) own the discovery, design, and build of the Datadog platform in customer environments, helping customers get to value faster. As a Manager for FDE, you’ll lead the engineers behind our most complex engagements: deploying Datadog at scale, migrating customers off legacy platforms, and optimizing existing observability setups. You’ll set the bar for what great build looks like, review architectures and code alongside your team, and work shoulder to shoulder with Datadog Engineering and Product. You’ll also stay close to customers, supporting scoping and showing up in the engagements that matter most. At Datadog, we place value in our office culture - the relationships and collaboration it builds and the creativity it brings to the table. We operate as a hybrid workplace to ensure our Datadogs can create a work-life harmony that best fits them. What you’ll do Coach and Develop your team: Hire, manage, develop, and mentor a fast-paced team of Forward Deployed Engineers, giving direct, actionable feedback and holding a high bar for technical quality Define what great build looks like across the SDLC: design review, code review, testing, CI/CD, release, and operational readiness for customer deliverables Own high quality delivery for your portfolio: prioritize work across your team and run regular inspection on deliverables and outcomes to ensure quality at scale Build relationships with the customer to scope engagements, cultivate trust, and evaluate the impact of our work on their business Collaborate cross functionally: with Sales, Customer Success, and Technical Account Management to deliver an exceptional customer experience Who you are You have 8+ years in software engineering, SRE, DevOps, platform engineering, or customer-facing technical roles, and 3+ years of people management experience leading engineers or technical consultants You collaborate credibly with Engineering and Product teams, weigh in on architecture and design tradeoffs, and can read, review, and write production-grade code You have a strong grasp of SDLC fundamentals and what great build looks like (design reviews, testing strategy, CI/CD, release management, production readiness) You know at least one major cloud well (AWS, GCP or Azure), along with strong fundamentals in Kubernetes, Terraform or Ansible, and CI/CD, and have hands-on observability experience with Datadog or a comparable platform You’re comfortable in front of customers: you can run technical discovery, build trust with engineering leaders, present to technical and executive audiences, and turn field experience into sharp, actionable feedback for Product and Engineering Able to sit up to 4 hours, traveling to and from client sites. Able to travel via auto, train or air up to 30% of the time. Datadog values people from all walks of life. We understand not everyone will meet all the above qualifications on day one. That’s okay. If you’re passionate about technology and want to grow your skills, we encourage you to apply.
