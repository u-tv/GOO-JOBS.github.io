The Sales Strategy & Operations team partners with advertising sales leaders to improve business performance and help the sales organization make informed decisions. As a Sales Analytics Intern, you’ll help turn data into insights that support sales performance, planning, and day-to-day decisions. You’ll contribute to reporting, analysis, and process-improvement projects while building your skills in communication and cross-functional collaboration. What you’ll do: Analyze data related to pipeline health, sales performance, coverage, productivity, forecasting, and planning. Build or improve recurring reports and dashboards, check data quality, and make insights clear and useful for business partners. Explore performance trends across regions, products, segments, or sales motions; identify possible drivers, data gaps, and follow-up questions. Turn analyses into concise summaries, presentations, and recommendations that help teammates understand findings and decide on next steps. Support straightforward planning or scenario analyses using established assumptions, templates, and guidance from the team. Partner with Sales, Finance, Marketing, and other teams to clarify business questions, align on metrics, and respond to analytical requests. Document reporting workflows, identify manual steps, and suggest practical process improvements. Thoughtfully use approved AI tools to support research, analysis, documentation, or workflow improvements and validate outputs before sharing them. What we’re looking for: Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in business, economics, statistics, data analytics or a related field, or equivalent experience. Ability to analyze data, identify patterns and explain findings and implications clearly. You can structure analytical questions, break work into manageable steps, ask clarifying questions and communicate conclusions. Proficiency with Excel or Google Sheets to organize data, conduct analysis or build basic models. Familiarity with SQL and at least one analytics or visualization tool such as Tableau, Looker or Power BI gained through coursework, projects or internships. Clear written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to summarize findings for technical and non-technical audiences. Interest in sales analytics, digital advertising, revenue operations or go-to-market strategy. Capacity to collaborate, manage agreed upon priorities and make progress on assigned projects. Ability to use AI tools responsibly to support research or analysis, check outputs against source data and business context and take ownership of the final work.
