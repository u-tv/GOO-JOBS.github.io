Creative Director

🏢 Luxury Presence 📍 US Only 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Luxury Presence is building the AI growth platform for real estate. Backed by Bessemer Venture Partners and other top investors, we're a Series C company that has hit $100M in annual recurring revenue. More than 100,000 real estate professionals, including over 30% of the WSJ Real Trends top 100 agents in the United States, use us to run and grow their business. The Opportunity We build the platform that helps high-performing real estate agents stand out in their market. Our own brand has to be the best example of an agent's aspiration for brand design. As we enter our next stage of growth, we're looking for a Creative Director to lead our in-house brand design team and set the creative standard for everything our customers and the market see from us. You'll lead the design team and decide what great looks like on every surface. You'll have the authority to say a piece isn't ready, and the judgment to explain why in terms a designer can act on. You'll report to the SVP of Partnerships, Brand, and Community, and you'll work with our CEO on the work that carries the brand. You'll do well here if you can spot the gap between good and great, close it with clear feedback, and still ship on the committed date. You use AI to get more out of a small team, and you hold every asset to the same standard whether an employee, a freelancer, or an agency made it. What You'll Own The creative standard Write and publish a quality standard with real examples for each surface, including web, email, video, social, and events, within your first 90 days. Review work and give specific feedback that raises it in a single round of revisions. By day 90, the CEO and SVP, Brand review your team's output and agree that 90% of randomly selected pieces meet the bar. A brand system that morphs Own a brand system that flexes to the standards of each channel and context, so what good looks like differs by surface while the brand remains unmistakably one brand. Bring every surface up to the new standard by month 6, from website and product imagery to newsletters, YouTube, and our offices. The team Lead, coach, and grow a small design team of fewer than five direct reports. Decide where in-house talent, freelancers, and outside firms each fit, and hold all of them to the same bar. Predictable, well-run delivery Run design intake and delivery so requesters always know what is coming and when. By month 6, at least 90% of requests ship by the committed date. Get at least 85% of assets approved within one round of revisions, and end day-before creative notes on delivered work. Keep the team aligned to business priorities, and flag requests that are not aligned early and often. How You'll Work You'll work closely with Brand, Growth Marketing, Product Marketing, and Product. You'll take feedback from senior leadership and partner teams, and you'll give it back just as directly. Politics have no place here, and the best work wins the argument. What We're Looking For Experience 8+ years in design, with experience leading design teams. A portfolio of luxury or premium brand work that spans web, brand, and motion. Leadership and mindset Exacting taste and the judgment to tell great work from good work. The ability to define a quality bar, explain it, and coach others to it. Full ownership of the output of the entire design function, including vendors and freelancers. Comfort influencing senior stakeholders, including the CEO. Technical foundation Command of brand systems across web, email, video, social, and events. Strong process and operational habits for intake, prioritization, and review. Location This is a remote role open to candidates in the United States, with a preference for New York, Los Angeles, or Austin.
