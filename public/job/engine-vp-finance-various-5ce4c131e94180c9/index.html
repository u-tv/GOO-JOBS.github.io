Engine : VP, Finance

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: United States URL: https://engine.com/ About the Role We're looking for a VP, Finance to lead Finance at Engine and serve as a strategic partner to our CEO and executive leadership team. You'll own the financial strategy and operating cadence of the company, from planning and forecasting to capital allocation, business performance, financial reporting, and long-range strategy. You'll help leadership understand what is driving the business, where we should invest, and what we need to do next. You'll also be the architect of Engine's financial story, shaping how the company is understood by its Board, investors, and partners. You'll build the Finance organization and infrastructure to support Engine through its next phase of growth. That means creating clarity where there is ambiguity, building scalable processes before they become bottlenecks, and using data, systems, and AI to make Finance increasingly leveraged. Success in this role will be measured by business impact and the results of the Finance organization. What You'll Do Strategic Financial Leadership Serve as the CEO's financial thought partner on growth strategy, investment priorities, resource allocation, and profitability. Drive capital allocation decisions across the executive team — where Engine invests people, capital, and resources, and where we make tradeoffs. Bring financial rigor to pricing, monetization, new products, markets, and partnerships. Capital Strategy & Transactions Own fundraising, debt and liquidity strategy, M&A, and other strategic transactions. Build investor relationships, lead the Board story on performance, and prepare Engine for future capital events — including the reporting, controls, and governance a public-ready company requires (SOX readiness, audit committee partnership, external guidance frameworks). Financial Planning & Business Insight Own the financial plan — quarterly forecasting, long-range modeling, scenario analysis, and the operating cadence across Engine. Develop a deep understanding of revenue, margins, customer economics, pricing, payment economics, and cash flow. Build the metrics and frameworks that make the business legible to leadership. Narrative & Communication Tell Engine's financial story to the Board, investors, lenders, and partners — turning complex numbers into a narrative leadership can act on and external audiences believe. Lead financial reporting and help the CEO communicate performance, outlook, risks, opportunities, and key decisions with clarity. Build Finance for Scale Build and lead a high-performance Finance team — attracting exceptional talent, modernizing through AI and automation, and establishing systems, processes, data infrastructure, and controls that scale without creating bureaucracy. Partner with Accounting, Treasury, Legal, and external advisors to ensure Engine has the financial foundation required to scale responsibly. What You'll Bring Experience and Background 12+ years of experience across investment banking, private equity, growth equity, corporate finance, FP&A, strategic finance, or a related field, with significant experience in high-growth technology. Investment banking or private equity background, with hands-on experience in valuation, transaction execution, and capital markets. Strongly preferred. Experience leading Finance, or a major component of Finance, at a high-growth tech, SaaS, marketplace, fintech, or similarly complex company. Financial Expertise Exceptional financial modeling skills and a strong understanding of three-statement modeling, unit economics, cash flow, and capital allocation. A track record of building financial planning, forecasting, reporting, and analytical infrastructure in environments that are scaling quickly. Experience evaluating pricing, monetization, new products, investments, headcount, and other strategic decisions. Communication and Partnership A track record of communicating complex financial stories to investors, boards, and executive teams in ways that drive decisions and build confidence. Experience partnering directly with a CEO, executive team, and Board. Leadership and Team Building Experience building and developing high-performing Finance teams. Comfort operating without a fully defined playbook and building structure where it doesn’t exist. Judgment and Execution Strong commercial instincts. You understand that Finance exists to help the business make better decisions, not simply to explain what happened last quarter. The ability to operate at both the strategic and tactical level and an usually high bar for execution and bias towards action Strong conviction and judgment. You bring a point of view vs just presenting options. Compensation Our compensation packages are based on several factors, including your experience, expertise, and location.
