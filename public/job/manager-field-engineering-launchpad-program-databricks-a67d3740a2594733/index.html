Manager, Field Engineering (Launchpad Program)

🏢 databricks 📍 Singapore 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

FEQ427R506 Team Overview & Mission The FE Launchpad program builds a high-performing early-career Solutions Engineering team that delivers customer value at scale while developing the next generation of Solutions Architects at Databricks. Our team exists to strengthen the SA talent pipeline, expand impactful SA activities across broad customer use cases, and feed the field with fully ramped, high-impact SAs ready to drive immediate revenue results. The Impact You Will Have As the Program Lead for the FE Launchpad team, you will lead, coach, and mentor a team of early-career Solutions Engineers (SEs) through a 9-month learning and execution journey. You will balance hands-on talent development with operational leadership, partnering closely with Field Engineering (SA), Account Executive (AE), and Specialized Technical Specialist (STS) leadership to ensure seamless collaboration and tech win delivery via the ASQ (Architecture Support Queue) process. Key responsibilities include: People Leadership & Talent Pipeline Development: Hire, coach, and mentor L3 Solutions Engineers. Build structured development plans to guide them from early-career professionals into independent, high-performing Senior Solutions Engineers. Operational & Technical Execution: Oversee team performance using the FE Launchpad Operational Dashboard. Manage funnel progression, activity tracking, account coverage, and technical evaluation quality across customer engagements. Cross-Functional Partnership: Establish tight alignment with SA, AE, and STS leadership in your territory to optimize ASQ workflow, match L3 capacity with field demand, and drive customer tech wins at scale. Technical & Pre-Sales Coaching: Conduct 1:1 coaching on technical discovery, requirement gathering, proof-of-concept (PoC) delivery, and communicating the business value of the Databricks Data Intelligence Platform. Program Execution: Oversee the execution of the 9-month FE Launchpad curriculum, tracking ramp milestones, technical certifications (AWS, Azure, GCP, Databricks), and readiness for promotion into regional field roles. What We Look For People Management Experience: 2+ years leading pre-sales/solutions architecture teams, or 4+ years as a Senior/Principal Solutions Architect with a proven track record of mentoring early-career technical talent. Passion for Talent Development: Passionate about early-career mentorship, onboarding, and creating structured environments where junior engineers thrive. Operational Rigor: Ability to manage metrics, track team velocity via dashboards, and continuously refine pre-sales operational processes (e.g., ASQ scoping and evaluation).
