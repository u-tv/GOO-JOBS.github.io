Spiralyze: Video Editor (LinkedIn Content)

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Atlanta, Georgia URL: https://www.spiralyze.com/ We’re looking for a video editor to help create sharp, premium LinkedIn videos about A/B testing, CRO, landing pages, and design. This is founder-led content. Most videos will include talking-head footage, screen recordings, website examples, captions, charts, and simple motion graphics. Key Responsibilities Edit short LinkedIn videos, usually 30 seconds to 3 minutes Turn raw footage into polished, engaging social content Add captions, callouts, zooms, screenshots, charts, and simple motion graphics Apply clean, consistent color grading to maintain a polished look Enhance audio with basic sound design and leveling Make business and data-heavy topics feel clear and visually interesting Create strong openings that hook viewers in the first few seconds Repurpose longer recordings into short clips Maintain a clean, premium, data-driven brand style Communicate clearly with a U.S.-based team and respond well to feedback Skills, Knowledge and Expertise 2+ years of professional video editing experience Strong portfolio of social media videos Excellent sense of pacing, storytelling, captions, and visual hierarchy Experience editing talking-head, educational, or business content Strong skills in Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut, or similar Good written English Reliable, organized, and able to work independently Nice to Have Experience with B2B, SaaS, marketing, design, or tech content Motion graphics or After Effects skills Familiarity with LinkedIn and YouTube editing styles Interest in A/B testing, CRO, UX, or digital marketing Benefits Growth path. We invest heavily in developing our people and promote almost exclusively from within. Success in this role leads to building and leading an entire content and brand team. In-demand skills. You’ll work alongside one of the most experienced experimentation teams in the world and learn how data, psychology, and creative intersect. Tech reimbursement. Need a better computer, monitor, or setup? We’ll help. Time to recharge. 20 days of paid vacation plus holidays.
