About ShatterproofShatterproof is a national nonprofit working toward a world where addiction never defines or ends a life. Through evidence-based programs, advocacy, and public education, Shatterproof works to transform systems for tomorrow, support families today, and end stigma forever. Learn more at shatterproof.org. Who Succeeds at Shatterproof Shatterproof is in a period of transformative and rapid growth. We are building new systems, reimagining how work gets done, and creating more cross-functional alignment to accelerate our mission. To thrive here, you should be energized by building, not just maintaining, and excited to bring structure, clarity, and innovation to evolving spaces. We're looking for team members who: Build systems and processes from the ground up and improve them continuously as we scale. Take ownership and act with a builder's mindset, bringing forward solutions rather than waiting for direction. Influence and collaborate across teams, shaping work through strong communication and relationship-building. Move quickly, learn fast, and adapt with resilience, especially in a high-growth, high-impact environment. Are mission-driven and motivated to leave their mark on an organization working to reverse the addiction crisis. The RoleReporting to the Chief Financial & Administrative Officer, the Vice President, Technology & Digital Strategy serves as Shatterproof's senior technology executive and leads the organization's technology, digital operations, cybersecurity, data, artificial intelligence, enterprise applications, digital platforms, and technology-enabled products. This leader will create and execute a comprehensive technology vision that advances organizational strategy, enables operational excellence, strengthens security and resilience, improves the employee experience, supports fundraising and advocacy, and drives innovation across mission-focused platforms. This role is ideal for a builder who wants the rare opportunity to shape not only an organization's technology strategy, but also how technology, data, AI, and digital platforms accelerate mission impact at scale. The Team The Vice President leads a multidisciplinary team spanning enterprise technology, digital operations, data, and product management. Current direct reports and functional oversight areas include IT Operations, Digital Operations, enterprise CRM and database capabilities, Treatment Atlas Product Management, and Treatment Atlas Data Management. The role is responsible for developing these capabilities into a cohesive, high-performing technology function that provides exceptional organizational support while advancing Shatterproof's digital, data, and platform strategy. The team supports and manages a broad portfolio that includes enterprise technology and workplace operations, cybersecurity and privacy, Salesforce and enterprise applications, website and fundraising technology, business intelligence and reporting, artificial intelligence initiatives, and the current and future technology architecture supporting Treatment Atlas. In addition to leading the internal team, the Vice President oversees a network of managed service providers, software vendors, implementation partners, and technology consultants, ensuring that internal and external resources are aligned to organizational priorities and positioned to scale with Shatterproof's evolving needs.
