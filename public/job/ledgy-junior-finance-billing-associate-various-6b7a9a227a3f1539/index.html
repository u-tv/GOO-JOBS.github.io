Ledgy: Junior Finance & Billing Associate

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Germany (Remote); London, United Kingdom; Poland (Remote); Romania (Remote); Spain (Remote) At Ledgy, we’re on a mission to make Europe a powerhouse of entrepreneurship by building a modern, tech-driven equity management and financial reporting platform for private and public companies. In 2026, we aim to be the leading provider for European IPOs and reporting for share-based payments. We are a value-based company with a core focus on being humble, transparent, ambitious and impactful, all in order to delivery the best experience for our customers and end users. We are proud to partner with some of the world’s leading investors. New Enterprise Associates led our $22m Series B round in 2022, with Philip Chopin joining Sequoia’s Luciana Lixandru on our board. We were founded in Switzerland in 2017 and today we operate globally from offices in Zurich and London. We encourage diversity and are an international team coming from 26 different countries and speaking 25 different languages. About the role As Junior Finance and Billing Associate you own billing operations, pre-accounting, and day-to-day finance tasks for our four entities across Ledgy. You report to the Finance Lead and work closely with Revenue Operations, Sales, Customer Success and our external accounting partners. This is a role with real ownership: billing is yours from day one and you will grow to own key work streams in international accounting and finance operations. What you'll do Billing operations Own the full billing cycle end to end: set up subscriptions, coordinate invoices and credit note issuing, and manage renewals on time and accurately through our billing platform (currently Stripe) Make sure billing data, contracts and invoicing terms are kept in sync with tools used by Sales and Customer Success Follow up on open receivables, reconcile incoming payments and coordinate dunning Answer customer billing questions from the Customer Support team and resolve disputes and corrections in a timely manner Document billing processes and spot where automation and improvements can reduce manual work Pre-accounting support Prepare and match supplier invoices, expense receipts and bank transactions for the monthly close of 4 entities Perform basic reconciliations and prepare clean documentation for our external accountants Support month-end tasks, such as accruals and deferrals preparation Finance team support Help with ad hoc finance tasks: reporting inputs, audit and tax filing requests, credit cards management, and document collection Support payroll queries from the team Keep finance records organized and audit-ready What you bring 2–3 years of experience in billing, accounts receivable, bookkeeping or accounting, and a degree in finance, accounting, business or similar Familiarity with billing, payment or accounting tools (for example Stripe, Rillet or a similar system) Strong attention to detail and a habit of double-checking numbers Solid Excel or Google Sheets skills, and familiarity with standard AI tools such as Claude Cowork Basic understanding of double-entry bookkeeping and VAT Excellent written and spoken English; you are comfortable explaining billing questions to customers and colleagues A structured, reliable way of working: you meet deadlines and flag issues early Curiosity and willingness to learn, and to take ownership of a process Interest in a professional path within startup operations Nice to have Experience with a SaaS or subscription business and recurring billing Exposure to cross-European accounting systems, such as DATEV or Abacus Exposure to multi-entity or multi-currency accounting German, or another European language Interest in automating repetitive finance work Per the EU Pay Transparency Directive, the pay range for this role is listed below for all legally required countries.
