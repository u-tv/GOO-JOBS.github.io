Learn more about our flexible approach to where we work. OVERVIEW The Developer Experience team at Instacart is the force multiplier for our 1000+ engineers. We define and build the platforms, tools, and practices that enable Instacart engineers to ship high-quality software faster — from AI-powered code review to build infrastructure, developer onboarding, and local development environments. Our success is measured in developer velocity: how quickly an engineer can take an idea from concept to production. As a Staff Software Engineer on Developer Experience, you will lead the technical direction for the developer infrastructure that every Instacart engineer relies on. That spans local and remote development environments, CI pipelines and the feedback loop on every commit, pre-production environments that engineers and AI agents can trust, version-control infrastructure, and the AI-powered developer tooling woven through all of it. This is a hands-on technical leadership role. You'll write code, set architectural direction, and partner across the org to move engineering-wide metrics on developer velocity, CI reliability, and the time it takes to get a change safely into production. About the Job Lead the technical direction for Instacart's local and remote development environments, including ephemeral environments, image-based provisioning, health checks, and the developer-facing ergonomics of the inner development loop. Lead the technical direction for our CI pipeline infrastructure and the commit-to-feedback loop, CI reliability, failure diagnostics, flaky-test reduction, and shift-left validation. Own the architecture and evolution of our pre-production environments, including staging stability, service-owner accountability models, and environments that let engineers and AI agents validate changes end-to-end in parallel. Drive the reliability and resilience of our git infrastructure, git operations at monorepo scale, and implement disaster recovery plans. Advance AI-powered developer tooling across the workflow, AI-assisted code review, agentic development workflows, and the platforms that let engineers run many AI sessions in parallel with humans on the loop. Design and drive multi-quarter initiatives that measurably improve developer velocity, CI reliability, pre-production health, and the local development loop. Own the developer experience metrics for the systems you lead: define what &quot;good&quot; looks like, instrument it, and use the data to prioritize investment. Partner with infrastructure, platform, and product engineering teams to roll out platform changes with minimal disruption Set the technical bar for the systems you own on reliability, performance, upgrade cadence, and developer ergonomics. Mentor engineers in the team and the broader engineering org; grow the community of developer infrastructure experts at Instacart. Represent the team in cross-org technical discussions on local development experience, CI/CD, pre-production, and AI-augmented development.
