Art Director - Brand

🏢 datadog 📍 New York, New York, USA 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Datadog’s brand is entering a new chapter, and we’re looking for an Art Director who can help bring it to life at scale. You’ll build the foundational brand systems behind each of our major areas of work, including events, campaigns, product marketing, and digital, so our team, vendors, and partners across the company can move quickly and independently while staying unmistakably Datadog. You’ll take a brief, develop strong concepts, and pitch them with clarity and confidence, then turn the chosen direction into the components, templates, and guidelines that let it scale. This is a highly collaborative role that is equal parts art director and hands-on designer: you’ll set art direction for your area of work, lead a team of brand designers, and do the work alongside them. You’ll partner closely with fellow art directors and creative directors across the brand team to keep the work cohesive. You bring deep expertise in creating brand systems with a strategic, business-problem-solving approach, and you elevate the craft of your team and the partners you work with. AI is already part of how our team designs, and we want an Art Director who has it built into their own process and workflow. You’re tool-curious, hungry to learn, and constantly evolving how you work as the technology changes. You’ll use AI tools to speed up research, ideation, and prototyping, and to take on repetitive production work, so you and your team can spend more time on concept, craft, and storytelling. You’ll also take the brand systems you build and implement them in AI tools that enable teams across the company to produce on-brand work on their own, with confidence and compliance. At Datadog, we place value in our office culture - the relationships and collaboration it builds and the creativity it brings to the table. We operate as a hybrid workplace to ensure our Datadogs can create a work-life harmony that best fits them. What You’ll Do: Shape and build the system: Build scalable brand systems in Figma that work across print, digital, and environmental formats. These systems include components, variables, templates, asset libraries, documentation, and governance. The goal is for teams and vendors to produce consistent, on-brand work on their own across events, campaigns, product marketing, and digital channels. Lead integrated work: Concept and direct full-funnel, integrated campaigns, guiding the work from brief to final execution. Collaborate across the org: Partner closely with Brand, Creative Operations, Marketing, Events, Product, and Sales, to co-create from initial brief through production, ensuring the work is cohesive, on-brand, and ready to scale across every touchpoint. Push the brand forward: Honor the foundational principles of the Datadog brand while finding fresh, confident ways to evolve its visual identity. AI is a core part of the workflow: Use AI-assisted tools in your process for research, ideation, prototyping, and production, and build them into our systems and processes so the whole team can use them effectively. Lead and develop designers: Mentor and grow a team of brand designers through clear direction, thoughtful critique, and growth opportunities, and stay hands-on to model the standard you expect. Present and persuade: Articulate and defend your creative choices to stakeholders at every level, from fellow creative leaders to cross-functional partners, through strong visual storytelling. Manage external partners: Direct agencies, freelancers, and production vendors when the work calls for it, and build the systems and guidelines that let partners run our work at scale without losing quality. Who You Are: You have 10+ years of experience at an agency or world-class in-house creative team with a standout portfolio showcasing strong integrated work. B2B SaaS or technology experience is a plus. You have 3+ years in an art direction or design leadership role, managing and mentoring designers, including setting direction, giving feedback, and growing talent. You’re equally comfortable directing the work and doing it yourself. You have deep experience designing for live events and experiential work, such as keynotes, environments, signage, and event content, ideally at global scale. You think in systems and have deep, hands-on Figma expertise, including component architecture, style and variable systems, and documentation other designers will use. You’ve built or significantly shaped a large-scale brand system or Figma library, not just maintained one, and you know how to make it flexible enough to serve many teams, formats, and regions. You have a strong foundation in typography, design, layout, and motion graphics. You know what looks good, and more importantly, why it works. You have strong taste and a clear point of view. You know how to translate brand strategy into scalable, well-governed design systems. You’re experienced at directing work across channels, including campaign, events, digital, video, and motion, and at working with external partners. You thrive in fast-paced environments with evolving priorities and ambiguity, and you’re comfortable balancing high-volume, systemized work with bespoke, high-craft moments. You use AI tools daily in your creative workflow and have a point of view on where they help and where they don’t. Zero-ego, highly collaborative, and deeply invested in pushing the creative work forward. Datadog values people from all walks of life. We understand not everyone will meet all the above qualifications on day one. That's okay. If you’re passionate about technology and want to grow your skills, we encourage you to apply.
