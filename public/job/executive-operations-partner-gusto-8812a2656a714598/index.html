Increasingly, our AI products are reshaping what’s possible for tax compliance infrastructure when it comes to accuracy and speed. Symmetry is the heart of the entire payroll industry, providing best-in-class software support stemming from our nearly four decades of experience seeing the most complicated tax and payroll scenarios across the country.
