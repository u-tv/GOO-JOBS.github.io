## About Outsite &#160; Outsite is a global coliving company for remote workers &#8212; hotel-quality service, extended-stay flexibility, and a built-in community, at the intersection of hospitality and lifestyle. We operate 40+ properties across 21+ cities on 5 continents, with 6,000+ verified members and an NPS of 65. &#160; ## About Outsite for Teams &#160; Outsite for Teams is Outsite's B2B expansion &#8212; giving companies, teams, and communities access to our global network of coliving and coworking spaces for their employees. Rather than a bulk discount, we position it as a meaningful perk: founders and leaders purchase memberships on behalf of their team, and a dedicated account manager keeps them active and engaged. This is a genuine growth priority for the business, and you'll be one of the first dedicated sales hires driving it. &#160; ## Role Overview &#160; We're hiring a Business Development Representative to build and run outbound sales for Outsite for Teams. You'll identify and prospect the right companies, run discovery calls, and close new business &#8212; turning remote-first startups, distributed teams, and growth-stage companies into long-term Outsite for Teams accounts. &#160; This is a highly autonomous, build-it-yourself role. You'll help shape the outbound playbook, not just execute someone else's. &#160; ## Key Responsibilities &#160; ### 1. Prospecting & Outbound - Build and work target lists of remote-first and hybrid companies, using signals like team size (50&#8211;500), remote/hybrid language in company descriptions, and titles such as Head of People, Chief of Staff, VP of Operations, or Founder - Source leads via LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Wellfound/AngelList, Crunchbase, remote job boards (We Work Remotely, Remote.co), and Outsite's own member base - Run cold outbound via email, LinkedIn, and calls, and continuously test and refine messaging - Reach out to local companies near Outsite locations to sell business travel and corporate stay packages - Build partnerships with travel agencies and tour operators to generate referral and bundled-stay opportunities &#160; ### 2. Sales & Pipeline Management - Run discovery calls to understand a prospect's team, travel patterns, and remote work culture - Own the full sales cycle from first touch to close - Keep the CRM clean and current &#8212; every deal staged, every next step logged - Forecast pipeline accurately and hit monthly/quarterly new business targets &#160; ### 3. Reporting - Report weekly on pipeline: volume, stage, conversion, and blockers - Track and share what's working in outbound so it can be systematized &#160; ## Required Skills & Experience &#160; - 1+ years of B2B sales or business development experience (SDR/BDR background a plus) - Confident, consultative communicator &#8212; comfortable running discovery calls with founders and People leaders - Self-starter who's built pipeline from scratch, not just worked warm leads - Able to work autonomously and independently&#160; - Comfortable with taking initiative and being proactive&#160; - Comfortable working European timezone hours - Strong written communication &#8212; outbound copy is part of the job - Organized; keeps a CRM honest without being told to - Comfortable using AI in workflow &#160; ## Nice to Have &#160; - Experience selling to remote-first or distributed teams - Familiarity with Pipedrive or HubSpot - Existing network in the startup, remote work, or travel/hospitality space - Experience selling a membership, subscription, or perk-style product &#160; ## What Success Looks Like (First 90 Days) &#160; - A working, documented outbound playbook (lists, messaging, cadence) - A full and accurately-staged pipeline in the CRM - First qualified meetings booked and first deals in late-stage or closed - Clear, weekly reporting rhythm established with Marketing - Sharpened point of view on which ICPs convert fastest &#160; ## Benefits &#160; - Remote, travel encouraged - Credits for Outsite locations&#160; - Salary - $45,000 - $50,000 USD + commission &#160;
