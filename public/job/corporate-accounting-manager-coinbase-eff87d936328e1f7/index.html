Ready to do the most impactful work of your career? At Coinbase , we are uncompromising on our mission to increase economic freedom. The bar is high, the environment is intense, and we like it that way. This isn't a place for complacency, it’s a place to be pushed past your perceived limits. If you're ready to build the future of finance alongside people who refuse to settle for &quot;good enough,&quot; you belong here. Coinbase is a remote-first, but not remote-only company. Expect to get together quarterly for intense in-person working sessions called “surges.” Learn more about working at Coinbase . The Controllership team is seeking a Corporate Accounting Manager to lead core accounting operations across global month-end close, operating expenses, operating cash, and capitalized software. Reporting to the Assistant Controller, you will own end-to-end close activities across global entities, strengthen internal controls, and partner with FP&A, Tax, Treasury, Product, and Engineering to turn complex product launches into close-ready operational processes. What you’ll do: Lead month-end and quarter-end close activities across global entities, including journal entries, reconciliations, account analysis, foreign-currency revaluation and remeasurement, and consolidated reporting. Own accounting operations for key operating expense categories, including G&A, accruals, prepayments, amortization, vendor spend, and capitalized software in partnership with FP&A; deliver clear balance-sheet reconciliations and P&L fluctuation analysis. Oversee operating cash reconciliations, aging of reconciling items, and close-related partnership with Treasury. Design, implement, and document SOX-compliant controls across corporate accounting and close workflows, and support internal and external audits with schedules and account substantiation. Drive automation across the close, partnering with Finance Systems and Engineering to streamline reconciliations, invoice-to-pay, and reporting workflows, and to deliver NetSuite enhancements that support them. Lead the automation of the accruals process, increasing automated accrual coverage and reducing manual journal entries in partnership with FP&A and Engineering. Coach and develop staff, fostering accountability, sound judgment, and continuous improvement. Required Skills and Experience: CPA and a bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field. 9+ years of progressive accounting experience, including 2+ years leading or managing people or accounting work. Experience in corporate accounting, including leading multi-entity month-end close, operating expenses, variance analysis, cash reconciliation, FX, and consolidated reporting, at a publicly traded or high-growth technology company. Strong knowledge of U.S. GAAP and internal controls, with direct experience implementing and maintaining SOX 404 documentation and compliance. Demonstrated ability to resolve complex accounting and operational issues and improve close processes. Experience implementing or optimizing an enterprise ERP; NetSuite or a comparable system preferred, with strong spreadsheet skills; SQL or analytics-tool experience (Snowflake, Looker, or similar) a plus. Strong communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to adapt in a changing environment. Utilizes generative AI responsibly, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality.
