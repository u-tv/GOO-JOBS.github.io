Partner Solutions Engineer, EMEA

🏢 vercel 📍 Hybrid - London, Berlin 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Vercel: Vercel is the agentic infrastructure company. We free people and agents to ship what’s next. For more than a decade, Vercel has shaped how the web is built. As the team behind Next.js, v0, and AI SDK, we create products that help builders move from idea to production with speed, security, and exceptional developer experience. Now, software is entering a new era, and the next generation of products will not just be used by people. They will be built, extended, and operated by agents. We are building the platform for that future, trusted by companies like OpenAI, PayPal, Ramp, Supreme, and millions of developers worldwide . Whether you’re building our products, supporting our customers, growing our community, or shaping our story, you’ll help define what comes next. About the role: We are looking for a Senior Partner Solutions Engineer to join our partnerships team in EMEA. This role will lead enablement and pre-sales motions with Vercel's solution and consulting partners (SIs and GSIs) across the region, with a primary focus on the DACH market. Partner Solutions Engineers work with partner engineering, sales, and product teams to grow adoption of Vercel's products and build strong technical relationships. A key focus in this role will be growing partner-delivered capacity in the DACH region: running Vercel's partner certification program, creating and delivering technical enablement and workshops, and supporting pre-sales motions where partners are engaged. Fluency in German and/or French is essential; this is a spoken, partner-facing role. This role will serve as a developer advocate for Vercel across solution and consulting partners. You will be the expert on building and delivering with Vercel products, including AI and agent workloads, and will establish best practices, author guides, lead workshops and hackathons, and build solutions for partner communities and Vercel teams. Partner Solutions Engineers require a strong web development background, especially in frontend development, cloud infrastructure, networking, and modern application development. Strong coordination skills across multiple partners and time zones are essential, as is building lasting relationships with practice leads, delivery teams, and executives. This is an in-person (hybrid) position that will require you to be based in Vercel's London or Berlin office with a required 3 days per week in office. What you will do: Serve as subject matter expert on Vercel and Next.js to partner engineering teams and leadership across EMEA. Run Vercel's partner certification program in the region: deliver certification workshops, review certification projects, and guide partner engineers through the program. Create and deliver technical training and enablement on Next.js and Vercel best practices, including building agents and AI applications on Vercel. Deliver one-to-many technical workshops for partner engineering teams, and author the workshop content behind them. Run and support partner hackathons that build partner skills and surface joint solutions. Provide pre-sales support where partners are engaged: proofs of concept, technical deep dives, and deal unblocking alongside Vercel's sales and sales engineering teams. Serve as the main technical contact for SIs and GSIs in the region, building lasting relationships with their engineering teams and practice leadership. Build templates and solutions that equip partner communities with the best practices on delivering with Vercel. About you: You are passionate about building first-class user experiences on the web. You have a strong understanding of web architecture, frontend development, and serverless computing. You have 4+ years of experience as a solutions engineer advising or consulting engineering teams. You have 6+ years of experience building and launching frontend applications using Next.js or React. You speak German and/or French; spoken fluency in at least one of them, alongside professional English, is essential for this role. You have worked with or within agencies, systems integrators, or consulting firms, and understand how they staff, deliver, and grow a technology practice. You are experienced in developer relations, from training sessions and talks to producing technical content. You have a results-driven mindset and are experienced working in a fast-paced environment. You are comfortable working with remote, globally distributed cross-functional teams. You have excellent communication skills and experience building and maintaining strong relationships with stakeholders. You have experience driving the adoption of web technology or previous pre-sales experience. You are based in EMEA and able to travel 20% of the time. Bonus if you: Have built and shipped AI-powered or agentic applications, ideally with the AI SDK. Speak both German and French. Have run certification, training, or enablement programs at scale. Have experience with composable architectures (headless CMS, commerce, search, analytics) common in partner-delivered projects. Benefits: Competitive compensation package, including equity. Inclusive Healthcare Package. Learn and Grow - we provide mentorship and send you to events that help you build your network and skills. Flexible Time Off. We will provide you the gear you need to do your role, and a WFH budget for you to outfit your space as needed.
