The Opportunity As an IT Automation and AI Engineer at Okta, you will be a key contributor to the team building the platforms and services that drive our internal AI transformation. We are moving beyond simple automation to build sophisticated, &quot;agentic&quot; workflows—utilizing LangGraph and Gemini Enterprise to create stateful, reasoning-capable systems that support our global workforce. This is a hands-on role for a professional engineer who excels at writing production-grade Python and is eager to bridge the gap between AI research and enterprise-scale applications. You will own the implementation of core features, orchestrating complex LLM interactions while ensuring performance, security, and reliability. What You’ll Be Doing Build Agentic Workflows: Develop and maintain stateful multi-agent systems using LangGraph to solve complex, non-linear business tasks. Gemini Integration: Implement and optimize LLM interactions using Gemini Enterprise , focusing on prompt engineering, function calling, and structured outputs. State & Memory Management: Design and manage agent state, persistence, and &quot;human-in-the-loop&quot; checkpoints to ensure reliable automated decision-making. Backend Development: Write high-quality, production-ready Python code to build the APIs and services that power our internal AI ecosystem. RAG & Grounding: Work on Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines to ensure agents have access to accurate, real-time enterprise context. Infrastructure & Deployment: Deploy and manage services on GCP using containerized environments (Docker/Kubernetes) and Infrastructure as Code (Terraform). Operational Excellence: Promote engineering best practices around testing AI logic, security, and CI/CD to ensure services are production-ready. What You’ll Bring to the Role Professional Python Core: 2–4 years of software engineering experience with a deep command of Python and its asynchronous ecosystem. AI Framework Mastery: Hands-on experience with LangGraph (or similar frameworks like LangChain). You understand how to build cycles, nodes, and conditional edges in a stateful application. LLM Practicality: Experience working with frontier models like Gemini or GPT-4. You know how to manage prompt versioning, token constraints, and model behavior. System Design Foundations: Solid understanding of distributed systems, cloud-native architectures, and building/consuming RESTful APIs. Problem-Solving Autonomy: The ability to take a defined workflow or prototype and drive it to production with minimal oversight, knowing when to escalate complex trade-offs. Collaborative Spirit: Strong communication skills and a desire to work across team boundaries to deliver cohesive, high-impact AI solutions. Why Join Okta Build With Purpose: Work on meaningful problems that simplify and secure how Okta operates at a global scale. Cutting-Edge Stack: Work with the latest from Google’s AI suite (Gemini) and the leading edge of agentic orchestration (LangGraph).
