What You Will Do Service Development: Build, maintain, and operate platform security services and controls using Python, Node.js, or Golang. Container Security: Provide technical support and engineering solutions for securing Kubernetes (K8s) and containerized workloads, including runtime security, network policies, and admission controllers. Engineering Quality: Write clean, testable, and well-documented code while driving technical designs and participating in code reviews to maintain high engineering standards. Vulnerability Management: Support remediation plan for vulnerabilities in partnership with Auth0 engineering teams, including development and commit of patches to remediate issues. . What You Bring Software Development Experience: 5+ years of hands-on experience designing, building, and maintaining production-grade software services. Core Language Proficiency: Hands-on mastery of Python, Node.js, or Golang. Expertise in using GitHub, and CI/CD systems (GitHub Actions, Jenkins) from an architectural and engineering perspective. Expert-level proficiency in Software Composition Analysis (SCA) and hands-on experience in the practical remediation of third-party and open-source dependency vulnerabilities at scale. Cloud & Containers: Solid experience with cloud-native environments (AWS or Azure) and container technologies (Docker, Kubernetes). Security Mindset: Practical experience building platform security controls, managing service permissions, and applying secure coding practices. Nice to Have Experience with high-throughput streaming platforms or log processing tools (e.g., Kafka, Kinesis, Pub/Sub, Vector). Experience with Infrastructure-as-Code (Terraform) and Policy-as-Code tools. Familiarity with compliance standards (FedRAMP, SOC2) and security frameworks in multi-cloud environments.
