We’re looking for a Senior Product Manager, Core Saving Experience, to lead how people save, build and organize boards across our core surfaces. You’ll own the end-to-end save experience for new and existing Pinners, from first-session saving to ongoing curation and action. You’ll partner with cross-functional teams to build delightful, AI-powered saving and curation experiences that help Pinners turn inspiration into action. What you’ll do: Own the product strategy and roadmap for the Core Saving Experience, including new user saving, ongoing saving and adjacent organizing actions across key Pinterest surfaces. Define and drive the strategy for new user saving, improving first-session save conversion, early repeat saves and early board creation to build durable habits. Lead the product strategy for new and sparse boards so Pinners quickly see value from creating and curating boards even when they have very little content. Use data, research and experimentation to understand how Pinners save, organize and act on content, and translate those insights into simple, powerful product flows. Run an experiment-driven roadmap that reduces friction in save flows, improves save quality and grows downstream engagement. Partner with AI/ML teams to design and ship AI-powered features that make saving and boards more useful and actionable, like intelligent organization, summarization and planning helpers. Use AI to accelerate analysis, ideation and iteration while applying judgment and verification to ensure correctness and quality. What we’re looking for: Experience building and scaling consumer products, with experience in activation, engagement or retention funnels. Strong product sense and user empathy, with experience in shipping high-impact, user-facing features on core surfaces such as saving, collecting, feeds or similar experiences. Experience in defining product strategies, setting goals and metrics, and running experiment-driven roadmaps that move key business outcomes. Data-driven mindset, with experience in partnering with data science, instrumenting metrics, interpreting A/B tests and making tradeoffs based on evidence. Demonstrated ability to use AI to improve speed and quality in your day-to-day workflow, with a strong track record of evaluating and verifying AI-assisted work for accuracy and quality. High integrity and ownership; you protect sensitive data, avoid over-reliance on AI and remain accountable for final decisions and deliverables. Excellent cross-functional leadership and communication skills; you bring clarity, alignment and momentum across Engineering, Design, Research and other partners. Bachelor’s degree in a relevant field such as Computer Science, Engineering, Design, Data, Business, or equivalent experience.
