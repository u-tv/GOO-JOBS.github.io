Director, Strategic AI Partnerships

🏢 dropbox 📍 Remote - US: San Francisco, CA 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Role Description The Dropbox Business Development team builds strategic partnerships that expand product adoption, create new distribution channels, and drive long-term customer and revenue growth. We are hiring a Director of Strategic AI Partnerships to own a focused portfolio of Dropbox’s highest-priority AI relationships, including OpenAI, Anthropic, and Meta. You will define and execute multi-year strategies across product integration, distribution, enterprise GTM, commercial agreements, and joint marketing. The opportunity goes beyond maintaining individual integrations: you will identify where Dropbox and each partner can create differentiated customer value, align executives and product leaders around a small number of high-impact workstreams, and move those workstreams from concept through agreement, launch, adoption, and growth. This role requires enough technical fluency to shape product discussions involving AI assistants and agents, native content experiences, connectors and MCP, authentication and account linking, model and platform capabilities, and governed access to customer content. You will work closely with Product, Engineering, Sales, Marketing, Finance, Legal, Communications, and senior executives to translate strategic ambition into clear owners, milestones, commercial terms, and measurable outcomes. The role is based in the San Francisco Bay Area to support frequent in-person engagement with partner teams and Dropbox leadership. Success requires executive presence, product and commercial judgment, and the ability to create momentum in relationships where the opportunity is large but the path is not predetermined. Responsibilities Own executive and operating relationships with a small portfolio of Tier 1 AI partners, including OpenAI, Anthropic, and Meta. Build partner-specific, multi-year strategies that connect Dropbox priorities with each partner’s product roadmap, enterprise motion, distribution, and strategic objectives. Identify and structure high-impact opportunities across native integrations, AI-agent workflows, enterprise identity and administration, model or platform collaboration, joint GTM, distribution, and co-marketing. Lead complex commercial and strategic negotiations, balancing customer value, product feasibility, reciprocal economics, data and security requirements, and long-term positioning. Drive cross-functional execution from opportunity definition through technical scoping, agreement, launch, adoption, and expansion, with clear internal and external owners. Establish executive governance, operating cadences, escalation paths, joint roadmaps, and scorecards that keep the most important workstreams moving. Provide market intelligence and a clear point of view on the AI ecosystem, using partner and customer evidence to influence Dropbox product, GTM, and corporate strategy. Requirements 10+ years of experience in strategic partnerships, business development, product partnerships, enterprise technology, cloud platforms, AI, or a related field. Demonstrated success owning complex, high-value relationships with major technology platforms and engaging directly with senior executives. Track record of creating and negotiating strategic agreements that combine product, technical, commercial, distribution, and marketing components. Technical fluency across AI models and agents, APIs, connectors, MCP, enterprise identity and administration, content workflows, and cloud or developer platforms. Strong product and commercial judgment, including the ability to prioritize opportunities, build business cases, evaluate reciprocal value, and make clear tradeoffs. Ability to lead through influence across Product, Engineering, Sales, Marketing, Finance, Legal, Communications, and executive stakeholders. Based in the San Francisco Bay Area and able to travel for partner and company engagements. Preferred Qualifications Direct experience working with OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta, or comparable AI and technology platforms. Experience launching native integrations or strategic platform experiences used by enterprise and consumer customers. Experience building enterprise AI partnerships involving identity, administration, security, distribution, or model consumption. Strong public-speaking and executive-presentation skills, including experience representing a company in high-visibility partner settings. Demonstrated use of AI and automation to improve research, partner preparation, operating cadence, and decision quality. Durable Skills AI fluency means using these tools to amplify human judgment, not replace it.
