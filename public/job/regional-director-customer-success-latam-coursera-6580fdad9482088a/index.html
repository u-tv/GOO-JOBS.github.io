You'll partner cross-functionally with Sales, Renewals, Channel Partners, Product, Marketing, and Support teams to ensure customers realize measurable value and become long-term advocates. The job requires fluency in English, Spanish and Portuguese. About your skills Trilingual Executive Communication (English, Spanish, Portuguese) The ability to present, negotiate, and build trust seamlessly with executive sponsors across LATAM and global stakeholders in all three required languages.
