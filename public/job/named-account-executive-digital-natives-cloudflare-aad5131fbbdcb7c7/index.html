Named Account Executive, Digital Natives

🏢 cloudflare 📍 Hybrid 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Us At Cloudflare, we are on a mission to help build a better Internet. Today the company runs one of the world’s largest networks that powers millions of websites and other Internet properties for customers ranging from individual bloggers to SMBs to Fortune 500 companies. Cloudflare protects and accelerates any Internet application online without adding hardware, installing software, or changing a line of code. Internet properties powered by Cloudflare all have web traffic routed through its intelligent global network, which gets smarter with every request. As a result, they see significant improvement in performance and a decrease in spam and other attacks. Cloudflare was named to Entrepreneur Magazine’s Top Company Cultures list and ranked among the World’s Most Innovative Companies by Fast Company. At Cloudflare, we’re not looking for people who wait for a polished roadmap; we’re looking for the builders who see the cracks in the Internet that everyone else has simply learned to live with. We value candidates who have the instinct to spot a &quot;normalized&quot; problem and the AI-native curiosity to create a solution using the latest tools. Our culture is built on iteration, leveraging AI to ship faster today to make it better tomorrow, while ensuring that every improvement, no matter how small, is shared across the team to lift everyone up. If you’re the type of person who values curiosity over bureaucracy, and that AI is a partner in solving tough problems to keep the Internet moving forward, you’ll fit right in. Available Locations Germany, Munich About the Role We are seeking a commercially sharp and technically curious Digital Natives Account Executive to join our Germany & Austria Sales Team. Your goal is to grow Cloudflare's business with digital native companies. You'll win new customers, either from a named list of high-potential accounts or from companies that don't work with us yet, and you'll expand our business with existing customers. Your target universe includes SaaS platforms, Fintechs, AI-native Scaleups and other comparable companies that are born in the cloud. Your customers move fast, make infrastructure decisions at the engineering level, and expect their vendors to keep up. The ideal candidate understands how digital-native companies actually buy: bottom-up product adoption, engineering influence, founder-driven decisions, and rapid procurement, and engages as a credible peer in conversations with CTOs, VPs of Engineering, and technical founders. As a Digital Natives Account Executive, you don't chase the next transaction, but are able to build a path on a strategic level with your customers to transform whole infrastructures. You will build your own pipeline through outbound prospecting, community engagement, and ecosystem presence, convert developer-led adoption into commercial new logo wins, and position Cloudflare as the infrastructure partner of choice for the next generation of technology companies. Above all, we are looking for true team players. We win together, not as lone wolves with sharp elbows. You share what works, ask for help when you need it, and celebrate your colleagues' wins as much as your own. You see your BDR, Solutions Engineers and specialists as real partners, not as support functions, and you bring the same care to working with your colleagues as you do to working with your customers. If you believe the best results come from people who make each other better, you'll feel right at home on our team. Critically, you will use AI tools as an active and integral part of how you sell. Many of your buyers build AI products themselves and speaking their language is the key to being successful. In line with Cloudflare's AI-native culture, you are expected to leverage AI tools daily as a genuine force multiplier across your prospecting, pipeline, and preparation and not as an occasional aid. Responsibilities Account strategy: For each key account, work out where the customer could be with Cloudflare in 6 to 18 months, and build a plan with them to get there. Look for the bigger infrastructure changes we can support, such as consolidating vendors or moving workloads to our network, rather than the next single product sale. New customers: Win new digital native customers, both from your named list of high-potential accounts and from companies that don't work with Cloudflare yet. Bring new logos onto the platform every quarter and hit or beat your targets. Pipeline generation: Own your pipeline. Build it through direct outreach, VC and accelerator networks, partners, and the startup and developer communities across DACH. Spot buying signals early and get to the right people quickly. Account growth: Grow existing customers by finding new teams, use cases and products where Cloudflare can help. Make sure new customers get off to a strong start, so they're set up to grow with us. Product-led growth: Find accounts where developers or teams already use Cloudflare on their own, and turn that usage into a commercial relationship before a competitor does. Technical buyers: Build trusted relationships with CTOs, VPs of Engineering, platform leads and technical founders. Talk confidently about infrastructure, APIs, cloud architecture and security. Explain what Cloudflare changes for their business in a way that makes sense to engineers and to the people who sign the budget. AI infrastructure: Position AI Gateway, Workers AI, Vectorize and our security products for AI workloads with companies building AI products. Understand the problems they face, such as securing AI APIs and keeping inference costs under control as they scale. Teamwork: Work closely with your BDR, Customer Engineers and product specialists. Plan accounts together, bring them into customer conversations at the right moment and keep everyone up to date on where each deal stands. Work with Sales Ops and Deal Desk to keep deals moving. Full-cycle sales: Run the whole sales process across deals of different sizes and lengths. Qualify carefully, involve the right people and close with confidence, whether a deal takes two weeks or six months. Pipeline and forecasting: Keep your pipeline accurate and up to date, and give sales leadership forecasts they can rely on. AI in your daily work: Use AI tools every day for account research, personalised outreach, meeting prep, pipeline management and follow-ups. In the interview, we'll ask you how AI helps you get better results. Market insight: Share what you learn in the field with the wider team, such as how digital native companies build, buy and scale. This helps Cloudflare keep pace with a fast-moving market. Desirable Skills, Knowledge, and Experience Sales experience 3 to 5 years in B2B technology sales with a quota, for example in SaaS, cloud, security or developer tools Met or beat your annual quota in at least two of the last three years Closed deals worth six-seven figures a year, with sales cycles of several months Won new customers through your own outbound work, and grown existing accounts with additional products or usage Sold usage-based contracts and kept growing them after signature by selling developer-centric use cases. Working directly with engineering teams to bring new applications, workloads and products onto the platform, so spend grows from real adoption. Built multi-year account plans together with customers Sold to CTOs, VPs of Engineering or technical founders, ideally at digital native companies, startups or scale-ups Worked with a structured sales method such as MEDDPICC Technical knowledge You can explain the basics of how the internet works: DNS, CDN, TLS, HTTP and APIs You understand common threats such as DDoS attacks, bots and API abuse, and how companies protect themselves You know the basics of modern cloud setups, such as public cloud, Kubernetes and serverless You have solid public cloud know-how. You understand how companies use AWS, GCP, Azure, including Kubernetes and serverless. What drives their cost and where Cloudflare complements or replaces them. You know how modern engineering teams ship software, including CI/CD and developer tooling such as GitHub, GitLab and AI coding tools like Claude Code. Knowing Cloudflare (Workers, R2, AI Gateway, Zero Trust) or a competitor such as AWS, Akamai, Fastly or Vercel is a plus Tools and ways of working You use Salesforce or a similar CRM every day and keep your pipeline and forecast accurate You already use AI tools in your sales work, for example ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini for research and writing, or Clay for account research. Be ready to show us how. You've worked in a team with BDRs and pre-sales engineers Other Startup or VC experience is a plus, either working at a startup or selling into VC-backed companies Fluent German and English, spoken and written Able to travel within DACH and to industry events Compensation For Germany based hires: Estimated annual salary is between €169,000 - €232,000. This role is eligible to earn incentive compensation under Cloudflare's Sales Compensation Plan. The estimated annual salary range includes the on-target incentive compensation that may be attained in this role under the Sales Compensation Plan.
