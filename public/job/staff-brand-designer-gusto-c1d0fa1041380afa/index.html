Staff Brand Designer

🏢 gusto 📍 Denver, CO - Hybrid; New York, NY - Hybrid; San Francisco, CA - Hybrid 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Gusto At Gusto, we're on a mission to grow the small business economy. We handle the hard stuff — payroll, health insurance, 401(k)s, and HR — so owners can focus on their craft and their customers. With teams in Denver, San Francisco, and New York, we support more than 500,000 small businesses nationwide and are building a workplace that reflects the people we serve. All full-time employees receive competitive base pay, benefits, and equity (RSUs) — because everyone who helps build Gusto should share in its success. Offer amounts are determined by role, level, and location. Learn more about our Total Rewards philosophy . AI is a fundamental part of how work gets done at Gusto. We expect all team members to actively engage with AI tools relevant to their role and grow their fluency as the technology evolves. AI experience requirements vary by role and will be assessed during the interview process. About the Role Brand design is how Gusto earns trust, builds recognition and familiarity, and makes people feel something before they ever read a word. Our Brand is about how we show up in every moment, and this team owns some of its most important attributes, from how we use language to typography, from color and composition to illustration and layout. These aren't finishing touches. They're critical to how we show who we are and why we're different. We're looking for a Brand Designer who is a hands-on maker: someone who loves the craft of visual design, takes real ownership of creative output end to end (from concept through the actual production tools) and wants to help define how Gusto looks and feels across formats. This is a senior individual contributor role focused on brand craft, production fluency, and the kind of deeply considered visual work that makes a B2B brand feel genuinely human. About the Team Design at Gusto is made up of over 80 creative, collaborative people who care deeply about our mission to empower small businesses and their employees. We're a cross-functional group that is always looking for opportunities to build understanding and empathy for the people who use Gusto. We don't care much about swim lanes and though we care deeply about quality and craft, we are never precious about it. We work closely with cross-functional partners in Product, Engineering, Data, and Marketing to design, build, and ship experiences that make a real difference. We believe great design can make hard things possible, even delightful. We are building an AI-native design organization, and we mean that seriously. We're looking for designers who are already working this way: using AI to prototype ideas quickly, shorten customer feedback loops, and ship quality UX directly to customers. This isn't a future aspiration: it's how we work now, and it's changing what the design role looks like at Gusto and across the industry. Gusto is expanding our in-house creative studio inside a fast-moving, late-stage startup. We launch products, educate customers, run brand campaigns, and show up at events, and we do all of it with a consistent visual language and voice that needs to work across web, print, digital channels, and everything in between. In the same week, you might be designing a landing page for a product launch and building a direct-mail campaign that ships to thousands of small businesses. Here's what you'll do day-to-day Design and execute visual work across product launches, product education, brand campaigns, events, mailers, and digital channels Build directly in production tools (Figma, CMS platforms like Contentful, InDesign), taking work from comp to shipped asset Partner closely with marketing, product, and growth teams from early concept through final delivery, often on tight timelines Direct and evaluate AI-generated creative work, setting the standard for what's on-brand and what shouldn't ship Use AI tools to compress the distance between concept and execution, iterating faster and raising the quality bar Build repeatable templates and components that enable faster turnaround without sacrificing quality Maintain brand integrity across a wide range of deliverables (including work where AI tools played a role in production), ensuring everything feels cohesive, human, and unmistakably Gusto Here's what we're looking for 6+ years of experience in brand or graphic design for in-house studios, agencies, or content teams A portfolio that demonstrates strong visual craft and range across web, print, campaigns, and digital content, including examples where you can speak to the role AI played in the work Strong design instincts across typography, color, composition, and layout: you know what makes work feel distinctive versus generic Comfort working hands-on in production tools (Figma, CMS platforms, Adobe Creative Cloud/InDesign), not just handing off comps A demonstrated point of view on AI in creative work: how to direct it, when to override it, and what it means to maintain a high creative standard when AI tools are part of the process Comfort moving between big, brand-defining work and fast-turn executional needs, and a maker mindset: you enjoy improving how work gets made, not just making the work itself Strong collaboration skills across creative, production, marketing, and product partners If you're energized by being close to the work, care deeply about craft & taste, and enjoy improving both the output and the process, this role will be an excellent fit.
