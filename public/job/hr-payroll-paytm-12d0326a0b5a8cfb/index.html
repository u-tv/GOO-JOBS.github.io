Job Description

About the Role We are looking for a detail-oriented and enthusiastic HR Payroll Intern to support our HR team in payroll operations and employee-related processes. This internship will provide hands-on exposure to payroll management, HR operations, employee data, and HR systems. Duration - 6 Months Key Responsibilities ● Assist in the monthly payroll processing and related HR activities. ● Maintain and update employee records and payroll data. ● Support attendance, leave, and other payroll-related inputs. ● Assist in preparing and validating payroll reports and MIS. ● Coordinate with relevant teams to resolve payroll and employee data discrepancies. ● Support documentation and other HR Operations activities. ● Ensure accuracy and confidentiality of employee information. ● Assist the HR team with other payroll-related tasks as required. Requirements ● Pursuing BBA/MBA/PGDM in HR, Finance, or a related field. ● Strong attention to detail and numerical aptitude. ● Good working knowledge of MS Excel. ● Good communication and coordination skills. ● Ability to handle confidential employee information responsibly. ● Candidates who can commit to a 6-month internship are preferred. What You'll Gain ● Hands-on experience in Payroll & HR Operations. ● Exposure to real-world employee data and payroll processes. ● Opportunity to work in a professional corporate environment. ● Practical understanding of HR systems, processes, and reporting.
