Director, Brand & Creative Marketing

🏢 mongodb 📍 United States 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

As a Director of Brand & Creative Marketing, will own the evolution of MongoDB’s global brand identity and creative expression. MongoDB isn’t just about logos or design systems—it’s about driving real business outcomes, translating our complex technical offerings into stories and experiences, and fostering pride and loyalty among all audiences. This is your chance to shape the creative future of one of the fastest-growing enterprise companies in the world. You will lead and influence all aspects of MongoDB’s brand visual expression. You are an articulate champion for design, passionate about quality and craft, and an experienced leader of diverse teams who can elevate ambitious work while delivering outstanding business results. Reporting to the VP of Corporate Marketing, you will work with peer leaders in Marketing and Product to transform the MongoDB brand to drive mindshare and market share with developer and executive audiences. You are responsible for leading a team of creative professionals and collaborating with marketing, product, and sales to develop marketing plans, analyze results, and identify opportunities. You are also responsible for meeting budget requirements by forecasting and managing expectations. What you’ll do: Strategic Impact & Vision Develop and articulate a bold, clear, and compelling brand strategy that enhances MongoDB’s market differentiation, emotional resonance, and business impact with audiences ranging from developers to senior executives Translate MongoDB’s business priorities into powerful creative concepts and brand campaigns. Make technical complexity feel engaging, empowering, and relatable Stay ahead of emerging creative technologies, including AI, generative design, motion, and interactive storytelling. Leverage these tools not just for efficiency, but to unlock bold, new creative possibilities Build for digital-first experiences across platforms.
