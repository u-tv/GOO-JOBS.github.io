Job Location - Bengaluru, India (2 Days WFO) Title - Contract Negotiator What You'll Do As a Contracts Negotiator at Cloudflare, you will manage agreements from initial intake through signature and beyond. You will act as the main contracting point of contact within Procurement, working side by side with Strategic Sourcing, Commercial Legal, and outside vendors to clear roadblocks and bring deals to a close. A big part of this role is bringing a fresh, practical approach to our daily work by using AI tools to handle repetitive tasks faster and improve how our team operates. Because our team is expanding, you will have the freedom to take ownership of your work and grow with the role. Who You Are We are looking for a driven, independent professional who loves taking ownership and genuinely wants to make the team better. You are approachable, adaptable, and comfortable leading live meetings with both internal stakeholders and external vendors to work through redlines and resolve issues. You are also AI friendly and naturally curious about using modern tools to simplify everyday tasks like reviewing clauses, summarizing agreements, and spotting bottlenecks. You enjoy tracking metrics, pay close attention to the details, and stay calm and professional when deadlines get tight. Key Functions Draft, review, redline, and negotiate a wide variety of commercial agreements, including SaaS, technology, internet infrastructure, security, and data privacy contracts. Lead meetings and live negotiation calls with internal business owners, Commercial Legal, and external vendors to work through sticking points and get contracts signed. Use AI tools and workflow automations to speed up daily contracting work, improve accuracy, and share new ways to make the broader team more efficient. Spot and reduce contractual, legal, and commercial risks by reviewing terms carefully and partnering with Legal, Privacy, and Security. Work with internal teams and suppliers to resolve contract issues after signature, manage renewals and amendments, and support vendor performance reviews. Manage contracts from intake through execution in our contract management system, keeping records clean, following company policies, and tracking key metrics. Qualifications At least 6+ years of experience drafting, reviewing, and negotiating commercial contracts in Procurement, Sourcing, or Commercial Legal. Bachelor's degree required; a Juris Doctor (JD) or equivalent legal background is a plus. Comfort and enthusiasm for using AI tools and contract management platforms to simplify daily work and reduce manual effort. Proven ability to manage a busy contract pipeline independently, lead productive stakeholder and vendor meetings, and drive deals to completion. Solid understanding of commercial contract law, data privacy, and security terms, ideally with exposure to SaaS, cybersecurity, internet infrastructure, or OEM vendors. Strong organizational skills, sound judgment under pressure, and a proactive mindset focused on helping the team improve. What Makes Cloudflare Special?
