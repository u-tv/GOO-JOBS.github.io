California Virtual Academies: CAVA - Instructional Paraprofessional Educator, English Lang Dev

Headquarters: Simi Valley, California, United States URL: https://workforcenow.adp.com/mascsr/default/mdf/recruitment/recruitment.html?cid=8e98f044-e925-4d2a-8482-5e9f34ae1350 Application Deadline: Friday, December 4th, 2026 at 4:00pm ABOUT THE CALIFORNIA VIRTUAL ACADEMIES AND THE INSTRUCTIONAL PARAPROFESSIONAL EDUCATOR, ENGLISH LANGUAGE DEVELOPMENT POSITION: The California Virtual Academies (a network of 9 public charter schools) delivers online/virtual home based instruction to students in grades TK-12 in California. We use K12, inc. as our curriculum provider for the basis of instruction and teachers provide live instruction via the Engageli platform. While our schools do offer some scheduling flexibility, students are still required to log into the online school daily, complete coursework, and attend instructional sessions. The ELD Instructional Paraprofessional Educator provides supplemental support to English learner students and reclassified students during their four-year monitoring period. Working under the direction of ELD leadership and credentialed ELD Specialists, this role supports new student orientations, reinforces language skills through individual and small-group activities, manages supplemental program accounts, tracks student engagement, and organizes assessment data and program records. Through collaboration with staff and families, the paraprofessional helps strengthen English language development, academic success, and student participation. HOURS AND SALARY INFORMATION: Monday-Friday work hours between 9:00am-3:30pm (Part-Time, up to 25 hours per week). You are not allowed to have another job during these contracted hours. Hourly Rate: $24.25 We do participate in CalPERS. Please contact your CalPERS counselor about how employment with California Virtual Academies may affect your personal PERS account. Only full time employees are offered health, dental, and vision benefits. Benefits information is only given out upon offer. We do provide training for all programs and we have a great staff to serve as your support system. Job Description Job Title: Instructional Paraprofessional Educator, English Language Development Reports To: Director of Intervention and Special Programs and/or ELD Coordinator FLSA Status : Non-Exempt Department: Intervention and Special Programs Work Year: 180 instructional days Monday-Friday work hours between 9:00am-3:30pm (Up to 25 Hours per week) Overview: Under the direction of the Director of Intervention and Special Programs and the ELD Coordinator, and in collaboration with credentialed ELD Specialists, this position provides supplemental support services to promote the success of active English learner students and students who have been Reclassified Fluent English Proficient (RFEP) during the state-required four-year monitoring period. This position is paid at an hourly rate. This position is funded through Title III funds and is contingent upon the continued availability of those funds. The position supports the implementation of supplemental programs and services designed to increase English language proficiency, improve academic achievement, strengthen family engagement, and support the successful participation of English learner students in the educational program. Responsibilities: Conduct Welcome and Orientation Meetings for new English Learner students as they enroll Download and organize English learner assessment reports from Pear Assessment and California's Test Operations Management System (TOMS) to support instructional planning, differentiated groupings, and supplemental interventions. Set up and manage student accounts within ELD supplemental/intervention programs, document engagement minutes in the program(s), and run program reports for ELD Specialists to drive intervention content and delivery Upload and maintain English learner program documentation within the school's Student Information System (SIS) to support program implementation and compliance requirements. Prepare surveys, forms, and related documentation for English learner program implementation and maintain records in accordance with program requirements. Send out and collect eligibility and monitoring surveys Support implementation of supplemental English language development activities and interventions for English learner students under the direction of credentialed ELD staff. Work with individual students or small groups to reinforce English language development skills through supplemental activities planned by credentialed ELD Specialists. Provide supplemental language acquisition and instructional support to English learner students during assigned instructional activities. Participate in required meetings, professional learning, and training sessions to support effective implementation of assigned responsibilities. Motivate students through effective communication and evaluative feedback Model a personal code of ethics aligned with CAVA’s policies Use directions from the Intervention and Special Programs administrative team to carry out duties Maintain high expectations for all students and a deep belief that all students can learn Perform other related duties as assigned that support the implementation of supplemental English learner programs and services. Work Year Instructional days are during the regular school year and will be utilized for direct student support, as well as related activities. Non-instructional days will be scheduled throughout the school year, aligned with our school calendar. Paraprofessionals will not work on non-instructional days for students.
