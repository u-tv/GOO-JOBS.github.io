Technical Solutions Engineer

🏢 stripe 📍 Singapore 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team Stripe is beloved by developers for the simplicity of our APIs, the thoroughness of our documentation, and our focus on developer experience. The Technical Solutions Engineering (TSE) team supports our developer-users and champions developer experience as central to the overall user experience. TSE interacts directly with developer-users more than any other team at Stripe, so your role will have a significant impact on what Stripe is known for and values most. When we do our job well, developers all over the world can smoothly launch and grow their businesses on Stripe, whether they’re integrating payments for the first time or building complex financial systems. What you’ll do TSEs understand the fundamentals of programming incredibly well and eagerly familiarize themselves with others’ code. You’ll explain technical concepts about Stripe’s products, build and debug integrations with developers, and produce and improve guides that help our users solve problems and succeed with Stripe. You’ll interact with developers of all skill levels through a variety of channels. Early on, current team members will devote time to teaching you and helping you develop expertise in Stripe, its services, and how we interact with users. Later, we’d love for you to take on this role with others on the team and Stripes broadly, as teaching is a great way to learn and reinforce what you know. If you enjoy working with all kinds of code, researching quickly, writing clearly, and helping others build products their businesses depend on, we want to hear from you! Responsibilities Communicate with external developers based in Asia pacific and China region to help them build applications using Stripe’s infrastructure across programming languages and frameworks, including Java, Python, Ruby, PHP, Go, Node.js, .NET, React, React Native, Swift, Objective-C, and Kotlin Unblock external developers facing complex integration challenges through support channels, including email, ticketing software, internal issue trackers, community platforms such as Stack Overflow, and real-time chat platforms Develop and maintain internal and external documentation, examples, integration demos, and code snippets that demonstrate usage patterns for Stripe’s infrastructure Identify points of friction developers encounter and collaborate with engineering teams to improve Stripe products, including participating in planning sessions, design reviews, and development meetings Develop and maintain internal support tooling for TSE and Stripe Support more broadly, such as a retrieval-augmented generation copilot powered by a large language model or the Invoice Explainer Admin tool Act as a developer advocate and educational resource within Stripe by preparing training for non-developers and responding to product integration questions in real time Who you are We’re looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement. Minimum requirements At least 4 years of full-stack software development experience, with a proven track record of building web or mobile applications that deliver high-quality user experiences Direct experience explaining technical concepts and the ability to explain how Stripe works to both technical and non-technical audiences Ability to write clean code in multiple programming languages and read code that may be less well structured Ability to support users during working hours in your region, with occasional weekend coverage to monitor or support critical infrastructure. The team is largely remote and supports working arrangements that accommodate family care and other needs. High professional fluency in spoken and written Mandarin and English to explain technical concepts, troubleshoot integrations, and answer questions over video calls and through written support channels for English- and Mandarin-speaking users across Asia-Pacific, including China Preferred qualifications Ability to write clearly and quickly General software engineering skills and an understanding of concepts that support learning new languages and frameworks Experience teaching or coaching developers Comfort working with substantial autonomy and limited supervision Degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related analytical field of study
