Senior Oracle Functional Lead - Record to Report

🏢 dropbox 📍 Remote - US: Select locations 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Role Description As the Oracle Functional Lead for Record to Report, you will own the design, configuration and evolution of the systems behind how Dropbox records, reconciles, consolidates and reports its financial results. You'll work hands-on across Oracle Fusion General Ledger, Cash Management, Fixed Assets, Intercompany and Subledger Accounting, the custom Oracle PaaS applications that automate our close, and the integrations that bring in data from treasury, banking, payroll, expense, lease and revenue systems. This is a senior, hands-on role. You'll be the functional DRI for Record to Report: leading design on complex initiatives, configuring Oracle, and partnering with Controllership, Treasury, Tax and FP&A to make the close faster, more automated and better controlled. Responsibilities Propose and shape the multi-quarter RTR strategy and roadmap in partnership with leadership — where to invest, what to transform, what to retire — and translate it into delivery, using automation and AI to reduce manual work in the close while strengthening SOX controls Own Oracle Fusion RTR configuration hands-on across General Ledger, Cash Management, Fixed Assets, Intercompany and Subledger Accounting, including period close, journal approvals and bank reconciliation rules Own the wider close platform estate — Oracle ARCS for account reconciliation, EDM for master data and hierarchies, and the reporting and planning integrations supporting EPM and statutory reporting Direct offshore delivery partners on R2R build and support work Design custom integrations into Oracle from treasury, banking, HR and payroll, expense, lease and revenue systems using Oracle Integration Cloud, Workato, REST APIs and file-based FBDI and UCM patterns Design and support custom Oracle PaaS solutions that underpin close: automate journals, accruals, reconciliations, foreign currency translation, and intercompany processes . Partner with Controllership, Treasury, Tax, FP&A and vendors to translate accounting requirements into scalable solutions, and explain trade-offs in business terms Requirements Deep h ands-on Oracle Fusion Financials configuration across Record to Report including General Ledger, Cash Management, Fixed Assets, Intercompany, Subledger Accounting and period close . Experience supporting international and statutory reporting requirements across multiple entities Experience designing and supporting integrations into a core ERP from upstream systems — using an integration platform such as Oracle Integration Cloud, Workato, REST and SOAP APIs, and file-based patterns. Comfortable supporting custom-built applications that extend the ERP, including reading and troubleshooting SQL Strong knowledge of journals, accruals, intercompany, reconciliations, FX revaluation and consolidation Led full-lifecycle Oracle implementations or major enhancements, and supported a live close cycle at a public company in a SOX-controlled environment Drive requirements conversations with stakeholders such as Accounting and FP&A in their language — what the close needs, what the accounting outcome has to be, what the deadline is. Design the technical solution behind it and explain the trade-offs back in business terms . Bring a point of view on where a process or platform should go rather than only delivering what was asked. Build a case for investment and influence a roadmap.
