WE NEED YOU TO BE ABLE TO DO: Apply your interpretation and artistic skill to translate concept art, environment designs into models to a fully 3D world. Ability to create or adapt models within different styles. Ability to understand proportions, spaces and volumes and transfer it to the 3D model. Good understanding of form along with textures and how it influences the final model. Deep knowledge making UVs under the direction of the supervisor and the surface department. Have a thorough knowledge of all aspects of the animation production process Strong artistic eye, who can successfully interpret a 2D design into a 3D model. Organizational skills to manage large environments and all their elements. Ability to find a balance between a quality model over time defined for it. Ability to develop the entire process of creating an environment, being able to solve the problems that appear both artistic and technical level. Look for solutions that optimize daily work when creating big sets. If needed, help and teach any of your colleagues with less experience. Excellent communication skills. Ability to work autonomously and independently in a fully remote environment, with that comes the supreme individual responsibility to prioritize and manage multiple tasks and maintain your own initiative. Great team player, create a collaborative and welcoming environment. Proven ability to meet deadlines and prioritize workload. Align with the style, sensibility of the project, Director vision, department and Supervisor demands. WE WILL MAKE THE PERFECT MATCH IF YOU HAVE: 5+ years of professional experience from either animation TV series or feature animation movie. Experience with Maya, SpeedTree and zBrush is a plus. Knowledge of organic, modular and procedural modeling. Knowledge of rendering engines and texturing programs is a plus. Experience with traditional drawing, sculpting is a plus. Ability to take feedback constructively and implement it effectively, to reach the quality desired. Anticipation and quick reaction for problem solving. Great attention to detail.
