Headquarters: Elvoro URL: https://elvoro.com/ Python AI Creative Engineer (Social Media) – Elvoro Ltd Engagement: Project-based contract with planned expansion to Full-time Location: Remote, Worldwide Compensation: To be agreed About & Mission Elvoro is a founder-led AI social media marketing and advertising company. We are on an important mission to deliver the world’s best AI-native marketing solutions to our clients for remarkable business growth (without AI slop). The AI revolution has changed what’s possible and our proprietary system, built around 100+ API’s, makes it work. We are now looking for an expert engineer with exceptional creative taste to lead this system. Responsibilities Lead the codebase and creative output of Elvoro’s proprietary social media engine Turn client briefs into large-scale short-form videos, carousels, static posts and ads Use your exceptional creative taste, knowledge on what type of content goes viral and converts well on social media, and large amounts of performance data to continuously, independently refine and improve the codebase to produce better content output. Schedule-post our high-quality content across numerous accounts using social media APIs Capture performance data via official APIs and use your creative taste and content conversion knowledge to refine content quality and test new creatives Maintain and extend the production codebase. Integrate numerous media and publishing APIs, test changes and monitor failures and cost per approved post Fix weak creative hooks, ai slop and pacing and product inaccuracies. Turn successful results into reusable yet unique systems Refine, manage and improve our code pipelines that produce thousands of different social media posts using unique processes Tech Stack Qualifications Professional software engineering experience building and maintaining large production codebases. Advanced Python REST API integrations OAuth 2.0 (eg: Anthropic, Runway, SpaceXSI, Instagram, ...) Video, audio and image processing (FFmpeg, MoviePy, Pillow, ...) Google Cloud Storage (GCS) Git, GitHub, GitHub Actions Preferred: SQL, HTML/CSS, JavaScript (React) AI-Native Workflow Qualifications Strong prompt engineering skills. Proactively using AI for efficiency, speed, and quality. Agents such as: Claude Code, Cursor/xAI, Codex, etc. Judgment to manually review AI output and full accountability for all AI-generated code. Benefits You get to lead Elvoro’s proprietary AI marketing engine and shape its technical and creative direction alongside the founder. An engineer previously working with Elvoro's founder has now gained a high-level job at Meta. Build a track record leading both engineering and creative production. You’ll lead campaigns for many established consumer brands and learn firsthand which creative decisions build brand awareness with hundreds of millions of views across social media and what turns attention into conversions such as sales and signups. The advantages of an ambitious, early stage startup: Work remotely from anywhere, at any time, on your own terms. Join a straightforward, no-nonsense environment focused on efficiency and results. Impact the world of marketing, get things done with the founder as your only approval point and see your ideas positively impact client campaigns. The codebase you are leading in this job has been built by engineers from TikTok and Stanford. To apply: https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/elvoro-ai-creative-engineer-social-media
