Regional Marketing Manager

🏢 okta 📍 Bengaluru 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Secure Every Identity, from AI to Human Identity is the key to unlocking the potential of AI. Okta secures AI by building the trusted, neutral infrastructure that enables organizations to safely embrace this new era. This work requires a relentless drive to solve complex challenges with real-world stakes. We are looking for builders and owners who operate with speed and urgency and execute with excellence. This is an opportunity to do career-defining work. We're all in on this mission. If you are too, let's talk. The Opportunity We are seeking an experienced, data-driven, and AI-savvy Regional Marketing Manager to own and execute high-impact field and demand marketing programs across India. Working closely with the India Country Manager, APJ Regional Marketing leadership, Sales, xDRs, and the broader Go-to-Market (GTM) organization, you will serve as the primary marketing quarterback on the ground—driving new logo acquisition, accelerating enterprise pipeline, and deepening customer expansion across both the Okta and Auth0 platforms. What you’ll be doing Drive New Logo & Pipeline Growth: Design and execute localized, full-funnel marketing programs tailored to the Indian enterprise and B2B SaaS market to achieve ambitious new business and expansion targets. Execute Account-Based Experience (ABX): Partner with Sales and xDRs to penetrate Target Account Lists (TAL) and Global 2000 accounts, engaging multi-threaded buying committees across technical and executive personas. Accelerate Deal Velocity: Move accounts from initial engagement through late-stage pipeline (Stage 2 to Stage 4) with high-touch executive programs, technical workshops, and targeted follow-up plays. Amplify Ecosystem & Partner Co-Marketing: Leverage India’s Global System Integrators (GSIs), Cloud Service Providers (CSPs), ISVs, and regional solution providers to scale reach and partner-influenced pipeline. Localize Brand & AI Security Positioning: Adapt global campaigns, thought leadership, and customer reference stories to resonate with Indian CISOs, CIOs, architects, and developers. What you’ll bring to the role Experience: 7+ years of B2B enterprise field or regional marketing experience in India, demonstrating measurable impact on pipeline and revenue across complex, multi-stakeholder sales cycles. End-to-End Campaign Ownership: Proven ability to manage full-funnel campaign plans from audience targeting and asset localization to execution, budget management, and post-campaign ROI reporting. Sales & xDR Partnership: Strong track record of partnering with Field Sales and Inside Sales/xDR teams to drive account-based engagement and improve lead-to-opportunity conversion. MarTech & Analytical Fluency: Strong working knowledge of Salesforce (SFDC), Marketo, ABM/intent platforms (such as 6sense), and analytics dashboards; comfortable using data and third-party buyer research to guide decisions. AI-Savvy Mindset: Hands-on curiosity and proficiency in leveraging Generative AI tools to accelerate content localization, campaign workflows, and marketing productivity. Execution & Communication: High sense of urgency, ability to multitask in a fast-paced environment, strong budget discipline, and exceptional verbal and written communication skills. Education: Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Business, or an equivalent field of study. Regional Strategy & Field Execution Serve as the primary marketing point of contact in India, acting as the strategic liaison between India Field Sales, APJ Regional Marketing, Global Demand, and Product Marketing. Plan, build, and execute an integrated mix of field marketing programs—including CxO roundtables, executive briefings, targeted field events, industry conferences, developer workshops, digital campaigns, direct mail, and webinars. Tailor messaging and campaign execution across Okta’s core use cases (operational efficiency, identity lifecycle governance, threat detection/response, and securing AI agents) and Auth0’s B2C and B2B SaaS priorities. Manage local agency relationships, vendor contracts, and regional marketing budgets to maximize program return on investment (pROI) and cost-to-pipeline efficiency. Sales Alignment, ABX & Pipeline Management Align closely with regional Sales leadership, Account Executives, and xDRs to track account engagement across the total addressable market (TAM) and orchestrate signal-driven sales plays. Monitor and optimize the lead-to-conversion funnel, ensuring timely xDR/Sales follow-up and strong conversion from qualified leads into Stage 2+ pipeline. Communicate upcoming campaigns, events, customer wins, and enablement assets to the regional sales team, while relaying field feedback back to Product and Global Marketing. Deliver regular data-driven performance readouts during pipeline reviews, Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs), and APJ planning sessions. Partner Co-Marketing & Customer Advocacy Collaborate with the APJ Partner Marketing and Channel/GSI teams to execute joint GTM initiatives, executive events, and co-branded demand programs with strategic GSIs, AWS/CSPs, and regional partners. Partner with Customer Growth and Account teams to develop localized customer reference stories, case studies, PR opportunities, and customer speaker participation at Okta and third-party events. Share regional best practices, campaign learnings, and innovative approaches across the broader APJ and Global Demand teams.
