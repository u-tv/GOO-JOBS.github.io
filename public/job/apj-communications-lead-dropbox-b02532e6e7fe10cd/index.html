APJ Communications Lead

🏢 dropbox 📍 Remote - Australia: Select locations 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Role Description Dropbox is looking for an Australia-based APJ Communications Lead to build our reputation and support our business priorities across Asia Pacific and Japan, with a focus on Australia, Japan, South Korea, Singapore, and India. You’ll help shape how customers, media, and industry leaders understand Dropbox as we expand beyond file storage into AI-powered work. Working closely with regional leadership and global communications, marketing, and product teams, you’ll develop a regional communications strategy that connects Dropbox’s global story to local customer needs and market priorities. Your work will span product and customer storytelling, media relations, executive visibility, and reputation management. We’re looking for someone who can set a clear direction and deliver the work themselves, from advising executives and developing campaigns to writing briefing materials and pitching journalists. You’ll bring strong editorial judgment, a deep understanding of the region, and the ability to turn complex ideas into clear, relevant stories. Responsibilities Own and execute Dropbox’s APJ communications strategy, aligning regional programmes with business priorities and global communications objectives. Lead regional product announcements and launches, adapting global narratives to reflect local customer needs, market conditions, and cultural context. Build and maintain relationships with business and technology journalists, influential voices, and relevant industry stakeholders across priority markets. Partner with customer, sales, and marketing teams to identify compelling customer stories and bring their perspectives into communications programmes. Advise regional leaders on external communications, prepare them for media and speaking engagements, and partner with global communications on issues that could affect Dropbox’s reputation. Lead agency partners across APJ, setting clear priorities, managing budgets, and ensuring consistent, high-quality execution. Bring regional insights into global planning, measure results against communications objectives, and use those findings to refine priorities and resource allocation. Requirements 10+ years of relevant communications experience, including responsibility for programmes across multiple APJ markets including Australia, Japan, South Korea, Singapore and India. Excellent writing and editing skills with the ability to make complex business and technology topics accessible and engaging. A track record of developing communications strategies and delivering measurable results. Strong understanding of the region’s media landscape, business environment and cultural differences, with established relationships in key markets. Experience advising senior executives and preparing them for external engagements. Sound judgement and experience managing corporate issues and reputational risk. Ability to influence and collaborate across functions, markets and time zones. Experience managing agencies, budgets and competing priorities. Preferred Qualifications Proficiency in an additional language relevant to the region, particularly Japanese or Korean highly regarded. Experience developing earned and owned content, with a strong understanding of how they work together within the broader communications mix.
