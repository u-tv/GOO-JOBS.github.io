Okta is looking for an experienced, self-motivated team player who will be a vital component for Okta’s sustainability (1) analytics and reporting, and (2) automation. This person will drive accurate and timely reporting and insights to meet the expectations of external stakeholders (customers, investors, and regulators) and internal stakeholders (executives, program leads, etc). This person will identify opportunities to automate data collection, analytics, reporting, and dashboards across various programs to streamline and simplify this work, and to enable our teams to scale to meet the moment and the growing stakeholder requirements. The role will drive impact as reporting is a customer business requirement, and data insights are foundational to building sustainability programs that drive business value and reduce environmental impacts. The ideal candidate for this role has 4+ years of data analytics, visualization, reporting, and automation experience, as well as sustainability, GHG accounting and carbon analytics experience. We are looking for highly motivated team players who have strong analytical skills, excellent attention to detail, and the ability to work cross functionally, as well as independently. This is a great opportunity for candidates seeking experience within a dynamic Finance organization deeply embedded in the teams we support. Prepare audit ready data sets and documentation; maintain traceable audit controls and trails; implement any feedback to strengthen internal controls and improve future assurance readiness; and manage third party assurance processes. Carbon analytics : Own the design and maintenance of models to track and analyze, forecast, and reduce greenhouse gas emissions and environmental impact. Support quantifying the value of this work in terms of cost savings from reducing energy consumption, revenue supported by meeting customer expectations, etc. Support key teams with related analytics and reporting, such as IR team with investor ESG questionnaires/surveys and data analysis and visualization for investor disclosures and presentations. Finance SEC Accounting, Legal, & Sustainability teams with sustainability data for SEC and other public disclosures . Finance to integrate and maintain sustainability metrics in T&E dashboard , and to conduct analysis and provide recommendations and visualizations for each team/org. Design, build, automate and maintain user-friendly dashboards, analytical tools, and reports to track metrics and performance indicators, and prepare slides and briefs, such as Quarterly sustainability metrics/dashboards for Okta’s Executive Committee (CFO, CLO, COO) and Board of Directors.
