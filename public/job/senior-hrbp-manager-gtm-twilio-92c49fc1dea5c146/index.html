About the Job We are looking for a Senior HRBP, Manager who turns people's strategy into business impact. You thrive at the intersection of Go-To-Market strategy, partner ecosystems, sales enablement, operations, strategic growth, and carrier relations. You are energized by complex global organizations and see yourself as a trusted advisor to senior executives—partnering directly with 1 SVP and 4 VPs across Industry Relations & Strategic Growth (IR&SG), Partners, and GTM Operations and Sales Enablement (GTM SOE)—driving workforce strategies that scale teams, enhance operational performance, and shape culture across a globally distributed organization of ~175 employees. This role is for someone who goes beyond traditional HR operations. You understand how specialized talent across IR&SG, Partners, and GTM SOE fuel business growth, and you have a proven track record of designing strategies that attract, develop, and retain high-impact professionals across global teams (spanning NAMER, APJ, LATAM, and EMEA). You are fluent in leveraging data, analytics, AI, and automation insights—using them to anticipate talent needs, guide organizational design, and influence systemic change. You thrive in fast-moving, evolving environments, translating complex workforce insights into clear action and aligning executive stakeholders across regions to achieve measurable business outcomes. This is an Individual Contributor role with high executive visibility and global influence, perfect for someone who excels at cross-functional collaboration, shaping high-performing cultures, and driving organizational effectiveness at scale. If you see yourself as a strategic partner, business-savvy advisor, and driver of workforce transformation, this is the role for you!
