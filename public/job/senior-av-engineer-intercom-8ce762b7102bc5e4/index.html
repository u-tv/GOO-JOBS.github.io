What's the opportunity? We’re looking for a proactive, technically strong Senior AV Engineer to own and improve AV services in our Dublin or London office and contribute to consistent, reliable AV support across our global offices. As part of the IT Operations team, you’ll be responsible for the systems and processes that support meeting rooms, digital signage and AV events. You’ll lead complex technical work from planning through delivery, build and improve scalable support processes and help colleagues develop the skills and documentation they need to support AV confidently. You’ll work closely with teams including Workplace Experience, Communications, Operations and Executive Assistants to deliver a seamless experience for employees, leaders and event audiences. This is a hands-on role for someone who combines strong technical judgement with a high standard of service and a desire to lead complex company events. What will I be doing? Own and improve AV operations in the office, including meeting room support, digital signage and event production. Contribute to the reliability and consistency of AV services across our global offices, working closely with AV colleagues in other locations. Lead AV projects and technical changes from requirements and planning through implementation, testing, documentation and handover. Design, configure and improve AV systems for meeting rooms and events, making sure solutions are reliable, supportable and suited to how people use them. Lead in-office AV events from preparation through live delivery and follow-up, including technical setup, live mixing, troubleshooting and post-event reporting. Plan and test event workflows, identify risks early and prepare appropriate contingencies for high-profile or complex productions. Act as a trusted AV partner to senior leaders and key stakeholders, providing personable, responsive support and clear communication. Build strong working relationships with cross-functional teams so AV services reflect business needs. Proactively identify reliability issues and opportunities to improve the employee and event experience. Develop and refine AV tools, processes, documentation and support models to make services more consistent and easier to maintain. Enable IT Support colleagues and other AV engineers to support users and deliver events through practical training, clear runbooks and knowledge sharing. Coordinate with vendors and internal partners to resolve technical issues and deliver AV work effectively. Stay current with AV technologies and production practices and recommend improvements where they can benefit the business. Strong broadcast engineering background, with experience designing and operating reliable live production and streaming workflows, including signal flow, audio, video and redundancy. What skills do I need? Significant experience supporting and improving professional AV environments, including meeting rooms and live events. Strong technical knowledge of integrated AV systems and the ability to configure, validate and troubleshoot them. Experience leading AV projects or system changes through planning, implementation, testing, documentation and handover. Demonstrated experience running live events, including technical leadership, setup, live operation and troubleshooting under pressure. Experience with live production and streaming workflows, including multi-source mixing and complex audio and video feeds. Strong troubleshooting skills and the ability to diagnose complex issues across AV hardware, software and networked systems. Experience improving AV services through monitoring, preventative checks, incident follow-up or other reliability practices. Ability to create clear technical documentation and processes that colleagues can use to support systems independently. Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with experience working effectively with senior stakeholders and cross-functional teams. Ability to manage competing priorities, maintain a high standard of delivery and stay calm in high-pressure situations. A proactive approach to identifying problems and delivering practical improvements without waiting for direction. A collaborative, service-oriented mindset and enthusiasm for AV technology and continuous learning. Experience using Automation or AI tools to improve AV workflows, documentation, troubleshooting or internal tooling. Experience with the following technologies and workflows: vMix, including multi-source mixing, live streaming and Stream Deck / Companion configuration. Q-SYS configuration and optimisation. Crestron control systems. Allen & Heath audio consoles and live sound. Shure microphone systems and DPA headsets. Digital signage platforms. AV over IP technologies, including Dante and NDI. Meeting room platforms such as Google Meet and Zoom. Windows and macOS troubleshooting. Bonus Skills & attributes Crestron or Q-SYS control system programming. Experience with cloud-based or hybrid AV production environments. Experience supporting AV office buildouts, commissioning, or room refresh projects. Relevant AV certifications, such as CTS. Location This is an in-office role based in Dublin or London. Regular onsite presence is important for hands-on room support, project delivery and live event responsibilities. You’ll report to the Senior Manager, AV and collaborate with a globally distributed AV team. Occasional inter-office travel and out-of-hours event support may be required.
