Senior Industry Consultant, Healthcare

🏢 mongodb 📍 Toronto 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

We are looking for passionate technologists to join our growing Industry Solutions Team (IST), a remote/flexible, globally-dispersed team. Our objective is to align MongoDB AI platform with industry specific solutions. We understand our enterprise clients and their needs to modernize software legacies, transition to modernized solutions, and guide them through continuous data-driven modernization. MongoDB Industry Solutions Consultants communicate the direction of MongoDB’s platform to customers and industry partner communities (i.e. webinars, client presentations, blog content , video content , live/virtual events, demos , account support, etc.), while keeping up with continuous data trends. A key component is direct account engagement with the clients’ senior executives, to help business leaders see the vision of how MongoDB is a solution for their industry’s needs, wants, and pain points. This will require a technical background, foundational DBMS/DBaaS understanding, programming knowledge, an interest in customer-facing interactions, and a keen interest to continue learning and digging into how this applies to Healthcare industries. You'll work closely with multiple teams, such as marketing, product, pre-sales, sales enablement and sales teams, as well as play a key role in winning deals and driving the business forward, as subject matter experts. Our team is an exciting, diverse group, made up of ambitious, innovative thinkers that range from software thought leaders and entrepreneurs to young professionals. Learn more about #LifeAtMongoDB here ! This role will primarily focus on the North and Latin American market but you also support other regions such as Europe and Middle East (EMEA). As such, the location for this role is based in Toronto, Canada, and includes about 30% of traveling across the North American region and to some extent globally. What you do at MongoDB You translate technical concepts and patterns into business benefits for senior management and executives as well as enterprise architects and product owners. This includes the creation of demos, training materials, slide decks, whitepapers, blogs and YouTube videos that communicate the business value of MongoDB as a leading industry solution You partner with our sales team to help ensure success in accounts ranging from small startups to large enterprises (i.e customizing solutions, client presentations, etc.). We are NOT a sales team, but we work closely with sales You support sales through enablement, with activities such as technical discovery, demos, training, proof of values, presentations, and documentation of technical decision criteria; working across a number of sales opportunities in parallel. You will take initiative in creating more opportunities for sales support You present MongoDB’s position at various events - either in person or digitally You represent MongoDB in international organizations, in workgroups, committees, and standardization bodies You are tightly aligned with our product management organization and communicate field requirements, changes you see in industry requirements to the product management leadership You proactively seek opportunities to enable other functions such as Sales, Pre-Sales or Marketing You help our team continue building processes in how we work with each other and other teams throughout MongoDB, to make ourselves as scalable and efficient as possible Enjoy the ride because we’re growing fast As a candidate, you should have More than 5 years in the Healthcare Sector (Lifesciences, clinical data, insurance) directly or in a consultative form in multiple areas; candidates with less years of experience but with exceptional expertise can be considered accordingly Strong programming/software engineering skills for demo development Deep expertise in clinical data structures (openEHR, Snomed, FHIR), and the US healthcare system Expertise in technical architecture, solution design, and delivering complex technical solutions at scale Excellent presentation and communication skills with the ability to visualize and communicate complex information to all levels of an organization - both internally and externally Ability to discuss a broad range of data technologies (non-exhaustive list: RDBMS, document, key-value, unstructured, noSQL, JSON,...)
