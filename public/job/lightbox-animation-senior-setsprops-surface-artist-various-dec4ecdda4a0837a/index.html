Paint textures with a high level of accuracy and high quality, pay attention to detail. Work closely with the modeling team to ensure UVs are positioned efficiently. Collaborate with Light team, to execute the required look. Ability to work autonomously and independently in a fully remote environment, with that comes the supreme individual responsibility to prioritize and manage multiple tasks and maintain your own initiative. Great team player, create a collaborative and welcoming environment. Proven ability to meet deadlines, prioritize workload and within budgets, adhere to the production schedule and deliver completed assignments on time. Align with the style, sensibility of the project and the Director vision. WE WILL MAKE THE PERFECT MATCH IF YOU HAVE: 5+ years of professional experience from either animation TV series or feature animation movie. Extensive knowledge of Substance Painter and Maya. Extensive knowledge of Substance Designer. Excellent 2D painting skills. Understanding of image color space relating to texture painting. Knowledge of 3D modeling is a plus. Experience with traditional drawing, and/or painting is a plus. Knowledge of UVs, texture, lighting and optimization is a plus. Ability to take feedback constructively and implement it effectively, to reach the quality desired. Anticipation and quick reaction for problem solving.
