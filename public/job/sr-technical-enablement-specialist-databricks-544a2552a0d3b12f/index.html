Sr. Technical Enablement Specialist

🏢 databricks 📍 Bengaluru, India 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

FEQ426R542 As a Sr. Enablement Specialist at Databricks, you will apply your expertise in enablement to lead the design, rollout, and adoption of enablement programs for Databricks Technical Support Engineers, including collaboration with Support leaders and SMEs and/or Technical Curriculum Developers. Programs in your portfolio can include onboarding and everboarding motions focused on developing and maintaining technical and soft skills for Technical Support Engineers. As a member of the Databricks Support Enablement Team, you’ll be responsible for (1) keeping up to date with the latest and greatest that Databricks has to offer when it comes to Support, (2) work with our enablement team to fully understand the business needs and requirements for Support, (3) create high-impact programs for Support, and (4) work with a team of technical enablement specialists to curate and design content needed for programs. The impact you will have: Use your Databricks and Enablement expertise to lead the design, curate, and maintain enablement programs for Databricks Technical Support Work with subject-matter-experts and technical enablement specialists to scope needs for new courses and updates to existing ones Drive improvements in Support KPIs through successful enablement programs (lagging indicators) What we look for: Experience with enablement programs for technical audiences Experience with technical support best practices Experience with cross-functional stakeholder management Strong analytical skills and data-driven decision-making [Preferred] Experience designing and developing highly technical training About Databricks Databricks is the Data and AI company.
