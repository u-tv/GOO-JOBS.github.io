About Pinterest Millions of people come to Pinterest to find inspiration and make their ideas a reality. Our mission is to bring everyone the inspiration to create a life they love—and the partnerships we build play an important role in making that possible. We are looking for a strategic, commercially minded Business Development Lead to identify, structure, and scale partnerships that create meaningful value for Pinners, customers, partners, and Pinterest. What you’ll do Develop partnership strategies, with guidance from your manager, that define the highest-priority partnership areas, target partners, and value-exchange frameworks aligned to Pinterest’s business goals. Research and evaluate new partnership opportunities by analyzing customer needs, market dynamics, strategic fit, operational feasibility, and financial impact; translate findings into clear business cases and recommendations. Lead the commercial development of partnership opportunities, including shaping proposals, aligning internal stakeholders, negotiating terms, and driving deals to close with support from relevant subject-matter experts and leadership. Manage a portfolio of partnerships and/or partnership programs, owning key execution deliverables, operating rhythms, performance monitoring, and issue resolution to realize agreed value. Build trusted, productive relationships with external partners and cross-functional teams across Product, Engineering, Sales, Marketing, Legal, Finance, Privacy, Policy, Operations, and other relevant functions. Identify partner and stakeholder needs, articulate how Pinterest can address them, and present solutions that connect directly to shared business objectives and challenges. Establish clear success metrics, milestones, and feedback mechanisms for the partnerships you lead; surface risks early, hold stakeholders accountable to commitments, and adapt plans based on results. Make thoughtful, timely recommendations in ambiguous situations by synthesizing quantitative analysis, market context, stakeholder input, and experience; raise and address difficult tradeoffs directly when needed. Represent the voice of partners and customers internally, bringing forward insights that can improve Pinterest’s products, commercial strategy, and partner experience. Contribute functional expertise to the broader Business Development team by sharing negotiation, market-analysis, account-management, and stakeholder-management best practices with peers. What we’re looking for Minimum 10 years of experience in business development, strategic partnerships, corporate development, management consulting, investment banking, strategy, or a related commercial function. Demonstrated experience independently evaluating, structuring, negotiating, and managing complex strategic or commercial partnerships. Strong strategic and analytical skills, including the ability to assess market and customer conditions, build a compelling business case, and convert analysis into practical recommendations. Proven ability to manage external accounts or partners while balancing partner objectives with Pinterest’s business interests. Excellent executive communication and presentation skills, with the ability to explain complex commercial, strategic, and operational issues clearly to diverse audiences. Strong cross-functional leadership skills and a track record of influencing without direct authority across senior internal and external stakeholders. Sound judgment and comfort making decisions amid incomplete information, competing priorities, and evolving conditions. A resilient, accountable, and low-ego approach: you stay calm through setbacks, own outcomes, give direct feedback constructively, and respectfully seek out different perspectives. Experience in consumer internet, advertising, commerce, creator ecosystems, platform partnerships, or technology is preferred.
