Product Management - Senior Product Manager / PM-2 – Risk Product

🏢 paytm 📍 Bangalore, Karnataka 💼 Full-time Employment 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Us: Paytm is India's leading mobile payments and financial services company and the pioneer of mobile QR payments. Our mission is to bring half a billion Indians into the mainstream economy through technology. About the role: Paytm is expanding into merchant payments in Europe, and we're looking for a Product Manager to build its risk function from day one. You'll own the risk product strategy for our European merchant acquiring business: deciding which merchants we onboard, how we monitor them, and how we stop fraud and losses as volume grows. This is a 0→1 individual-contributor role with a lot of ownership. You'll lead through influence across engineering, operations, compliance, banking partners and leadership. We want an AI-first mindset: designing risk systems that use automation and predictive signals from the start, rather than adding them on later. Key Responsibilities: - Own the risk product vision and roadmap for the Europe merchant business ahead of launch, and through launch and scale. - Design the merchant risk lifecycle end to end: onboarding and underwriting, KYB, ongoing monitoring, transaction risk, and chargeback and dispute management. Define risk policies, rules and limits for new markets. Calibrate risk appetite with business, finance and compliance stakeholders. - Identify where AI/ML, automation and predictive signals can improve merchant screening, fraud detection and investigations. Turn those into PRDs and shipped features. - Work with engineering and data teams to adapt Paytm's existing risk platform for European requirements and to build what's missing. - Partner with compliance, legal, acquiring banks, card schemes and third-party vendors (KYB, sanctions screening, fraud tools) to meet regulatory and scheme obligations. - Set up risk metrics and reporting, including fraud rates, chargeback ratios, loss rates and approval rates. Use data to drive decisions and experiments. - Build a deep understanding of the European payments ecosystem (merchants, competitors, regulation, fraud patterns) and use it to shape strategy. - Travel to Europe occasionally and work across time zones with local teams and partners. What We're Looking For: - 3–5 years of hands-on product management experience. - B.Tech/B.E. from a premier institute, with strong technical depth. - Experience in payments or fintech, ideally merchant acquiring, payment gateways or cards. - An AI-first mindset, with a record of shipping ML/LLM-driven features or automation. - Comfort owning ambiguous, 0→1 problems with little structure in place. - Comfort with data and analytics for decision-making. - Strong communication and stakeholder management, including with external partners and senior leadership. - Nice to have: Familiarity with European payments regulation and scheme rules, such as PSD2/SCA, EU AML directives and Visa/Mastercard risk programmes. - Nice to have: Experience in fraud, risk, merchant underwriting or compliance products. Why join us: Work with a high-performing and passionate product, design, and engineering team. Shape the future of credit for millions of users. Build at scale in one of India’s most dynamic and regulated spaces. Flexible and inclusive work environment with fast decision-making and ownership
