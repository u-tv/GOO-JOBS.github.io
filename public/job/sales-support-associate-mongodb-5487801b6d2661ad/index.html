Job Description

The SalesOps Associate provides front-line support to our rapidly-growing sales organization, including answering inquiries, maintaining data health, performing research and working on tasks that enable Account Executives to take data driven decisions, and maintaining the health of our sales tools and systems. MongoDB’s SalesOps Associates are mentored and coached by highly-skilled Sales Operations leaders with shown track records. SalesOps Associates will gain practical, real-world sales operations experience, along with exposure to the best sales methodology in the industry. This experience provides an enviable foundation for your business career, whether you are passionate about a future in leadership or sales. We are looking to speak to candidates who are based in Gurugram for our hybrid working model.
