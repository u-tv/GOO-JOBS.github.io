D2B: Estimator and Production Administrator

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Manila, Metro Manila, Philippines URL: https://jobs.workable.com/company/dcaVtKQqXr9FwBNppNCBH9/jobs-at-d2b Position : Estimator and Production Administrator Salary : 1,900 AUD (Php 75,000 - 81,000) Working Hours & Conditions : Monday to Friday AU Business Hours ROLE OVERVIEW: Approximately 75% of this role focuses on estimating, quotations and professional sales proposals, with the remaining 25% supporting production and business administration. As a bespoke build company with an extensive hire inventory, we require someone who can confidently prepare and manage quotations combining date-based hire stock with custom fabrication and joinery builds for each project. You will cost hired items against project dates and availability, while estimating bespoke components from designs, drawings, specifications, material quantities, fabrication methods and supplier pricing. You will consolidate these elements into complete, accurate and commercially viable project quotations.Hands-on experience using software such as Rentman, Odoo, Current RMS, Booqable, HireHop, Tibr or a similar platform is essential. You will work directly with our Designers to understand and cost each concept, and closely with our Producers, who manage client relationships and project delivery. You will report to the Producers and Managing Director. About You You are exceptional with numbers, highly organised and commercially aware. You enjoy translating a creative concept into a practical, accurate and clearly documented quotation. You understand how hire inventory and custom-built components contribute to the total cost of delivering a project. You check quantities, rates, supplier pricing and margins carefully, and raise missing information or cost concerns early. You are a proactive self-starter who can work independently, take ownership and follow tasks through to completion. You remain calm under pressure and manage competing priorities without compromising accuracy. You take pride in getting the details right while working efficiently and meeting urgent deadlines. Estimating and Quotations – 75%  Review client briefs, 3D designs, floor plans, construction drawings, specifications and finish schedules.  Prepare detailed project estimates combining date-based hire inventory, bespoke fabrication, custom joinery, purchased materials and subcontracted services.  Quote hire stock against delivery, installation, event, dismantling and return dates, accounting for hire duration, stock availability, turnaround time and external hire requirements.  Estimate custom-built joinery, display structures, signage, graphics and other fabricated components.  Calculate material quantities, wastage, hardware, finishes, fabrication labour, assembly, freight, installation and dismantling costs.  Obtain and compare quotations from fabricators, joiners, signage suppliers, installers, freight providers and other production partners.  Check supplier quotations for scope completeness, quantities, exclusions, lead times and delivery requirements.  Build and maintain quotations using item catalogues, hire rates, packages, supplier costs and project-specific fabrication estimates.  Distinguish owned hire stock, externally hired equipment, purchased items and bespoke fabrication within internal cost breakdowns.  Calculate selling prices, mark-ups and gross margins accurately.  Work with Designers to assess buildability, material choices, fabrication methods and practical cost saving alternatives.  Confirm project scope, budgets, inclusions, exclusions, assumptions and quotation deadlines with Producers.  Prepare professional client quotations, sales proposals and tender submissions.  Identify missing information, scope gaps, commercial risks and potential variations before quotations are issued.  Manage quotation revisions as designs, quantities, dates and supplier costs change, checking their effect on availability, budgets and margins.  Maintain current item catalogues, supplier rates, material pricing and estimating templates.  Review estimated costs against actual project costs to improve future pricing.  Manage multiple quotations and urgent deadlines while maintaining accuracy. Production and Office Administration – 25%  Support Producers with purchasing, supplier coordination, project documentation, logistics and production administration.  Prepare purchase orders and track supplier confirmations, lead times and delivery dates.  Ensure approved materials, finishes, graphics, equipment and fabrication requirements are accurately ordered and documented.  Maintain current project information as approved quotations move into production.  Assist with hire schedules, equipment lists, packing lists and warehouse documentation.  Record approved changes and communicate updated information to Designers, Producers, suppliers and warehouse staff.  Assist with freight bookings, deliveries, collections, installation and dismantling schedules.  Organise supplier invoices and project cost records in Xero, checking them against purchase orders and approved costs.  Maintain CRM records, supplier details, project folders and outstanding action lists.  Provide regular updates on quotation progress, supplier pricing, project costs, deadlines and commercial risks.  Communicate professionally with suppliers and contractors, while Producers retain primary responsibility for client management and project delivery. Essential Skills and Experience  Demonstrated experience estimating and quoting projects that combine date-based hire inventory with bespoke fabrication or custom joinery.  Relevant experience in exhibitions, events, commercial joinery, shopfitting, signage, interior fit-outs or a comparable custom production environment.  Hands-on experience using Rentman, Odoo, Current RMS, Booqable, HireHop, Tibr or a similar quotation, rental or business management platform.  Practical experience using software to prepare quotations, maintain item catalogues and pricing, manage inventory information and track project costs.  Ability to manage hire dates, stock availability, hire rates and external hire costs alongside project specific fabrication estimates.  Advanced Microsoft Excel skills for estimating, calculations, pricing schedules and cost analysis.  Practical experience using Xero for purchase orders, supplier invoices and project financial administration.  Strong numerical skills, including a clear understanding of mark-up versus gross margin.  Ability to interpret construction drawings, floor plans, specifications and 3D designs.  Practical understanding of joinery and fabrication materials, construction methods, labour allowances, finishes and installation requirements.  Experience obtaining and assessing supplier and subcontractor pricing.  Ability to prepare professional quotations, proposals and clearly written scope descriptions.  Excellent attention to detail, organisation and written and verbal communication.  Demonstrated ability to work independently and complete accurate work within tight and changing deadlines. Highly Regarded  Direct experience in exhibition stand construction, event production or branded environments.  Experience setting up or improving item catalogues, hire packages, pricing structures and quotation templates.  Experience coordinating stock shortages, external hire, equipment substitutions and warehouse requirements.  Experience tracking estimated versus actual costs and project profitability.  Familiarity with modular exhibition systems such as Octanorm, Maxima, Aluvision or beMatrix.  Knowledge of timber and sheet materials, laminates, aluminium, steel, acrylic, signage, graphics, AV, lighting and flooring.  Experience using CRM platforms and production scheduling software.  Experience improving estimating processes and the handover from approved quotation to production. Remote Working Requirements  Ability to work independently and effectively in a full-time remote offshore role.  Reliable internet access and a suitable workspace for focused estimating work and video meetings.  Availability for agreed working hours that provide regular overlap with Colab’s Australian team.  Confidence using online meetings, shared files and digital collaboration tools to work with Designers, Producers, suppliers and warehouse staff.  Consistent communication about quotation progress, outstanding information, supplier responses and deadlines.  Disciplined file management and secure handling of client information, supplier pricing and commercial records.
