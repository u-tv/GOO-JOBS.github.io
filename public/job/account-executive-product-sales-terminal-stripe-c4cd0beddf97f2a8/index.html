Account Executive, Product Sales (Terminal)

🏢 stripe 📍 US-SF, US-NY, US-Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team Product Sales is a team of deep functional and technical specialists who both lead and advise on deals for the most strategically important products in Stripe’s portfolio. The Terminal Product Sales is tasked with attacking a $15T market opportunity for unified online and offline commerce. The team is lean, high visibility and highly cross-functional! What you’ll do As an Account Executive, Product (Terminal) focused on Enterprise Retail, you will use your sales and product expertise to drive growth of Stripe's Unified Commerce suite of hardware, software and payments solutions among North America's largest retailers. You will partner with product, engineering and marketing teams to build deep product and industry knowledge, and you will lead strategic, multi-stakeholder deals with Fortune 100-500 retail brands. You will lead in-depth product discussions with existing and prospective customers and help build and execute our go-to-market strategy for the retail vertical. You are an adept salesperson, able to hold business-level and technical conversations at every level of a large organization, up to the C-suite (CEO, CFO, CIO and CTO). You are used to building value in competitive, long-cycle enterprise situations and enjoy working on products that require deep product understanding combined with technical knowledge. You are as comfortable working with clients as with internal stakeholders, including executives. You are excited to become an expert and share your knowledge. You are an organized self-starter and an excellent communicator who is highly analytical and has a track record of improving the processes and organizations around you. Responsibilities Drive new business growth by identifying, prospecting and closing Stripe Terminal opportunities with Fortune 100-500 retailers in North America. Own the full enterprise sales cycle, from lead generation through close, with a strong focus on expanding Stripe Terminal's footprint across large retail estates (stores, regions and banners). Lead strategic sales conversations with retail executives and buying committees (e.g., Payments, IT, Store Operations, Digital/Ecommerce, Finance and Procurement), positioning Stripe Terminal's hardware, software and payments as the best unified commerce solution. Navigate complex enterprise buying processes, including RFPs, security and compliance reviews, pilots and phased multi-store rollouts. Develop and execute account strategies and go-to-market plans to displace incumbent POS and payments providers and accelerate adoption. Manage and expand relationships with existing enterprise customers, driving upsells and incremental revenue growth. Act as a trusted advisor and Terminal expert, both internally and externally, to drive customer success and influence product direction. Collaborate with Product, Marketing and User Operations teams to refine our approach and fuel Terminal's growth in retail. Who you are We’re looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement. Minimum requirements 10+ years of sales or prospecting experience at a technology company, with a track record of top performance. Experience selling hardware and/or software solutions into large enterprise accounts. A proven ability to build strong, collaborative working relationships with business partners and multiple stakeholders. The ability to deal effectively with ambiguity and thrive in an unstructured, fast-moving environment. A proven ability to be a self-starter who drives business results without significant supervision. Excellent verbal and written communication skills. Strong presentation skills, particularly for in-person meetings with multiple stakeholders. A proven ability to lead complex negotiations involving bespoke commercial agreements. The ability to understand complex technical requirements and craft solutions across multiple stakeholders. Preferred qualifications 3+ years of experience selling online and offline payments or commerce solutions directly to large enterprise merchants. Experience selling to or working for large POS or commerce technology businesses. Experience selling into large, strategic clients in retail, hospitality, travel or banking. Proven ability to sell complex solutions that combine hardware and software. Familiarity with the North American enterprise retail landscape, including omnichannel and unified commerce trends, in-store payments, and the POS and payments competitive environment.
