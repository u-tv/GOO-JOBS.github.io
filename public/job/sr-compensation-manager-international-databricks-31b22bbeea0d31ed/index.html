Sr Compensation Manager, International

🏢 databricks 📍 London, United Kingdom 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

GAQ327R301 Mission Databricks is looking for a strategic compensation leader who likes to build. As the first dedicated international member of the Compensation team, you'll be a trusted partner to founders, senior leaders, People Partners, Recruiting, and the broader Compensation team, supporting our expanding EMEA and APJ regions. You'll create compensation solutions that help us attract, retain and motivate world-class talent, and you'll use AI to build the tools that make those solutions scale. This role reports to the Director of Compensation. At Databricks, compensation is more than a number. It's how we show that every employee is an owner and part of our success. We're looking for someone with a strong foundation and a track record of doing exactly that. Databricks is a deeply technical, fast-moving company, with a global footprint. You'll partner with dynamic, data-driven leaders who value rigorous analysis, clear perspectives, and collaborative problem-solving. In this role, priorities can evolve quickly, and you'll often navigate multiple important initiatives at once. We set high standards for this role to support our fast-paced growth. Successful team members in this environment tend to: Build trust through analytical rigor. You present well-reasoned models and effectively articulate complex analysis to engineering leaders who will test your assumptions. Navigate fast-paced priorities with resilience. You maintain focus, sound judgment, and positivity when balancing competing deadlines and feedback. Offer constructive perspectives. You confidently partner with senior stakeholders to propose data-backed alternative solutions and drive alignment to finalize decisions. Embrace feedback as a growth tool. You extract actionable insights from feedback and respond with clarity and openness. Create order from ambiguity. When there's no playbook, you build one, and you're comfortable acting on 80% of the information. If you are excited by high standards, technical depth, and collaborative strategic discussions, you will find this work deeply rewarding. The impact you will have This is a hands-on, high-impact role. Expected time on each area: International compensation solutions and strategic advice (40%) Help evolve Databricks’ international compensation strategy as we scale, including our approach to geographic differentiation, market positioning, salary structures, and equity, while working directly with international leaders to diagnose talent challenges and design attraction and retention solutions. Partner closely with HR teams and business executives across global divisions to translate US compensation practices internationally, offering localized strategies, pay equity assessments (including EU pay transparency compliance), key talent retention initiatives, and macro-level guidance as Databricks enters and scales in new markets. Serve as the main compensation advisor on strategic hires and high-stakes retention situations for niche international talent. Turn vague or urgent requests into clear options with explicit tradeoffs, then help leaders reach a decision. Regularly coordinate and partner with US-based Compensation Partners to ensure seamless continuity of support for business leaders in a globally matrixed setup. AI-enabled lifecycle compensation (30%) Run merit, refresh equity and promotion cycles for global teams. Design and build AI tools and workflows that reduce manual work and improve decisions. Examples include offer modeling, retention-risk analysis, cycle quality checks, and self-serve insights for leaders. Spot what's repetitive or error-prone in today's processes and automate it. Enterprise compensation frameworks (30%) Spearhead ongoing and upcoming strategic rewards initiatives, helping build the infrastructure, principles, and enablement that will allow Databricks' international compensation framework to scale alongside job architecture, market pricing models, pay-for-performance design, and equity strategy. Build frameworks that solve today's problems and anticipate the ones coming as we grow. Partner with the Compensation Programs team as they lead, manage, and support enterprise-wide programs, collaborating closely on international considerations and matters. What we look for Quantitative and technical skill is the foundation of this role. Everything else we look for builds on it. 1. Compensation expertise You know your craft. Job architecture, compensation frameworks, market pricing, year-end and mid-year pay cycles, global compensation practices, and pay-for-performance design. You know the competition. Deep knowledge of competitor compensation, especially new-hire and refresh equity strategy at high-growth and public tech companies with global footprints. You know the market. You formulate and conduct detailed benchmarking studies to monitor market movements, bridge data deficits, and offer validated recommendations to the business. You reason from first principles. You balance compensation best practice with what the business actually needs, and you can rebuild a framework from its underlying logic when the standard approach doesn't fit. 2. Quantitative and technical foundation Expert-level analytical modeling. You build compensation models from scratch, including cost-of-program scenarios, pay-positioning analysis, attrition and retention-risk modeling, and sensitivity and tradeoff analysis. Built with AI. You've personally used AI tools (for example LLMs, AI assistants, or automation and agent platforms) to build compensation tools, models or workflows that others rely on. You don't need to be a software engineer, but you should be able to explain how it works, what you'd improve, and how you checked that its outputs were accurate. Equity fluency. You bring deep equity expertise to translate Databricks' equity philosophy across markets where candidate expectations and familiarity with US-style equity compensation vary significantly, grounded in a solid understanding of RSU and option mechanics, vesting design, and refresh strategy. Statistical judgment. You know when a market data cut is credible, when a sample is too small, and how to present ranges and market pricing without false precision. 3. Strategic partnership Trusted advisor to senior leaders. You have a track record of advising executives on sensitive, high-stakes compensation decisions (offers, promotions, leveling, retention, transfers, org changes) and influencing the outcome through data and judgment rather than authority. Operational excellence. You connect the dots between systems, stakeholders and programs to deliver compensation programs end to end, accurately and on time. Clear communicator. You turn complex analysis into a crisp recommendation an executive can act on in one conversation. About Databricks Databricks is the Data and AI company.
