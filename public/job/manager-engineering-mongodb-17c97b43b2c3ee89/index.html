Manager, Engineering

🏢 mongodb 📍 Dublin, Ireland 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

We are seeking an Engineering Manager to oversee a team, guide the technical direction, and support the growth of the Atlas Growth Platforms team at MongoDB. This is a management role where you will be responsible for building and maintaining the infrastructure behind experimentation, service configuration, and user behavior tracking that shape key moments in the Atlas user journey and drive measurable impact across our growth funnel. As an Engineering Manager, you’ll be responsible for ensuring your team delivers high-quality, scalable backend services and intuitive frontend experiences that empower teams across MongoDB to experiment rapidly and build winning data-driven products. You will own both the success of the systems your team builds and the people you lead - striking the balance between project delivery, mentoring, long-term architectural investment, and individual career development. We are looking to speak to candidates who are based in Dublin for our hybrid working model. Responsibilities Support the career growth of your direct reports through coaching, feedback, and opportunity creation. Model MongoDB’s core values & leadership principles while fostering psychological safety, inclusive culture, and accountability Identify and prioritize the most impactful projects, and lead the delivery of high-impact infrastructure. Set realistic and ambitious goals for your team while helping proactively manage scope, dependencies, and risks Provide technical design reviews and balance hands-on contributions to complex systems involving A/B testing, data pipelines, feature flags, and user behavior tracking Partner closely with Product, Analytics, Engineering teams, and other stakeholders to help define and drive a roadmap aligned with business outcomes Ensure observability, scalability, and maintainability of your team’s systems. Lead retrospectives, support incident response, and cultivate a culture of continuous improvement. Support the team’s 24/7 on-call rotation by being in the escalation path Represent your team’s needs and priorities in organization-wide discussions. Foster strong partnerships across engineering, product, and GTM functions Hire, onboard, and retain exceptional engineers. Assess team capacity regularly and ensure knowledge sharing and load balancing across the team Basic Qualifications 5+ years of professional software development experience and 2+ years of engineering management or team leadership of >3 engineers Expertise in building backend systems in compiled languages (Java, Go, C#) and fluency with frontend technologies (React/TypeScript preferred) Solid understanding of distributed systems, data pipelines, and service-oriented architecture Track record of delivering complex projects with cross-functional impact Proven ability to mentor and grow engineers while holding a high bar for quality and collaboration Preferred Skills Experience running or building frameworks for large-scale A/B testing Familiarity with analytics platforms (e.g. Segment, Amplitude) and data-driven product development Proficiency with Kubernetes and cloud-native infrastructure Strong product intuition and a passion for experimentation infrastructure Managing feature request intake and tear-out+ roadmapping Success Measures You’ll know you’re successful in this role if within: 3 months: You’ve earned your team’s trust, started guiding and learning from engineers, and delivered an initial enhancement or fix to our experimentation, configuration, and/or partner integrations systems. You are leading team syncs and retrospectives 6 months: You’re leading strategic planning and designing quarterly plans, while unblocking your team on technical and execution challenges.
