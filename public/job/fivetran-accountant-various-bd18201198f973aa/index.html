Headquarters: SRB - Remote From Fivetran’s founding until now, our mission has remained the same: to make access to data as simple and reliable as electricity. With Fivetran, customer data arrives in their warehouses, canonical and ready to query, with no engineering or maintenance required. We’re proud that more organizations continue to leverage our technology every day to become truly data-driven. About Us Fivetran and dbt Labs are bringing together two industry-leading companies with a shared mission: helping organizations unlock the full value of their data. Together, we’re delivering the data infrastructure layer that helps organizations move, transform, and trust their data — from the moment data moves, through every transformation, to the context teams and AI systems rely on. Fivetran helps organizations automate data movement across the systems, clouds, engines, and tools they rely on. dbt Labs pioneered analytics engineering, helping teams transform data into reliable, governed insights. Together, we support thousands of organizations as they build a trusted foundation for analytics, AI, and better business decisions. As we bring our teams and technology together, we’re building on the strengths of both companies while continuing to deliver the products and experiences our customers know and trust. It’s an exciting time to join us: we’re creating a company with the scale, talent, and technology to help more organizations put their data to work with greater speed, confidence, and impact. During this transition period, you may see references to both Fivetran and dbt Labs throughout our recruiting process as we integrate our teams, systems, and career sites. About the Role Fivetran is building data pipelines to power the modern data stack for thousands of companies. Fivetran is seeking an exceptional Sr. International Accountant, to join its fast growing Accounting team. This role will be a significant contributor to the success of the accounting department. The ideal candidate has in-depth knowledge of generally accepted accounting principles, proficiency in accounting systems, and strong motivation to contribute to the achievement of efficient financial close and reporting processes. You pay great attention to detail, deliver results under deadlines and work effectively cross-functionally in a rapidly changing environment. What You’ll Do Be responsible for our Serbian entity by Executing effective procedures to ensure accuracy, completeness, and reliability of financial information Prepare and post journal entries in Netsuite for these entities as well as monitor postings done by other accounting functions Hands-on performance of month-end close activities. Make sure that all deadlines are met and proactively review balances before closing Oversee the preparation of financial statements in accordance with applicable local GAAP, along with preparation of US - local GAAP reconciliations Support the Statutory Audit process and ensure timely and accurate preparation of audit deliverables Review the work of our outsourced partners and closely manage service levels Identify and drive process improvements and automation initiatives to reduce manual effort and drive toward Global standardisation Manage processes related to invoicing, tax payments, statistical filings and other obligatory filings Demonstrate strong technical accounting acumen to be able to conclude on accounting treatment of complex transactions; Partner with key stakeholders including Tax, FP&A, Legal and Sales & Marketing teams Support ad-hoc projects as required Comply with federal, state, and local legal requirements by studying requirements; enforcing adherence to accounting-related, tax-related requirements; filing reports; advising management on needed actions; Manage the Serbian SEF (System of Electronic Invoices) portal, including the review and monitoring of all accounts payable invoices received, ensuring accurate processing, compliance with local regulations, and timely resolution of any discrepancies. Skills We’re Looking For Recognised accounting qualification (ACA, CIMA, ACCA) Minimum 3 years of PQE, preferably gained within a Big Four firm and / or a multinational organization Solid knowledge of IFRS and local GAAPs, US GAAP a plus Strong computer and analytical skills Excellent planning, analytical, interpersonal and communication skills Demonstrable experience as a team player, who is comfortable working as part of a larger team located across multiple geographic locations Ability to work independently, to work under pressure and to handle several projects at once A desire to work in a hyper-growth, quick paced, exciting business environment #LI-LA1 #LI-HYBRID Perks and Benefits 100% employer-paid medical insurance * Generous paid time-off policy (PTO), plus paid sick time, inclusive parental leave policy, holidays (including a year end Global Week of Rest), and volunteer days off RSU stock grants* Professional development and training opportunities Company virtual happy hours, free food, and fun team-building activities Monthly cell phone stipend Access to an innovative mental health support platform that offers personalized care and resources in areas such as: therapy, coaching, and self-guided mindfulness exercises for all covered employees and their covered dependents. * May vary by country and worker type - please reach out to your recruiter for more information Click here to learn more about Fivetran's Benefits by Region.
