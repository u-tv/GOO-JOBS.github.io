Smartsheet: Principal Pricing Program & Operations Manager (Remote Eligible)

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: -REMOTE, USA- For over 20 years, Smartsheet has empowered teams to manage work seamlessly and scale solutions smarter. Now, in our most ambitious chapter yet, we are uniting human teams with AI agents. By orchestrating the work agents do best, automating manual tasks and uncovering insights at scale, we create the space for people to focus on what truly matters: judgment, creativity, and big thinking. That is magic at work, and it’s what we show up for every day. Smartsheet is looking for a Principal Pricing Program & Operations Manager to join our Pricing & Packaging team. This individual connects pricing strategy to execution. Our pricing analysts and strategists develop the research, analysis, and recommendations. You will take those recommendations and drive them to realization: building the plan, aligning the stakeholders, and landing the change across Product, Sales, Finance, Marketing, Legal, Billing, and Operations. You will work with guidance from the Sr. Director of Pricing & Packaging, but you will own the execution of your programs end to end. You will keep multiple initiatives moving at once, adapt quickly as priorities shift, and make sure that what we decide in the pricing team is what customers and the field actually experience. This is a high-impact role for someone who combines strong B2B SaaS pricing acumen with the discipline of a program and operations leader. You understand why a pricing change is the right call, and you know how to get it done. You will report to our Sr. Director of Packaging & Pricing, and you may work remotely from anywhere in the US where Smartsheet is a registered employer. What You Will Do: Program execution Own the end-to-end execution of pricing and packaging initiatives, from the handoff of a recommendation through launch and post-launch measurement. Examples include new packaging constructs, list price changes, rate card updates, AI and new-capability monetization, and customer migrations. Build and manage detailed program plans, timelines, dependencies, risk registers, and RACI structures across multiple concurrent projects. Translate pricing recommendations into clear requirements and action plans for the teams that implement them, including Product, Engineering, Sales Ops, Finance, Billing, Legal, Marketing, and Customer Success. Run program cadences, including stand-ups, steering committees, and executive updates. Surface risks and decisions early, and escalate with clear options and trade-offs. Re-plan and re-prioritize quickly as business priorities, market conditions, and leadership direction change. Cross-functional leadership and communication Build trusted partnerships across the organization and secure stakeholder buy-in, often without direct authority. Write clear, executive-ready documents, including program briefs, decision memos, launch plans, FAQs, and status reports. Present program status, outcomes, and recommendations to senior stakeholders and leadership. Partner with Sales Enablement and Marketing to develop field-facing materials such as pricing guides, discounting guidelines, talk tracks, and training, so that changes are understood and adopted. Pricing operations and governance Operationalize pricing policy by partnering with the right cross-functional partners for critical changes to prices, discount guidelines, approval workflows, quoting and CPQ configuration, billing and systems changes, and exception handling. Contribute to pricing governance, including intake, prioritization, decision rights, approvals, and deal-desk escalation paths. Build repeatable playbooks, templates, and processes for pricing launches, migrations, and changes so the team can scale. Partner with Finance, Revenue Operations, and Business Systems to ensure pricing changes are accurately reflected in systems, reporting, and forecasts. Analytics and performance tracking Define success metrics for each program and track post-launch performance, including ARR, NRR, ARPU, attach rate, price realization, discount levels, and migration performance. Use data to monitor adoption, identify issues, and recommend corrective actions or follow-on work. Work with the pricing team to understand the research, models, and assumptions behind each recommendation so you can execute with context and flag when results diverge from the plan. Support competitive pricing and packaging monitoring that informs ongoing program decisions. You Have: 7 to 10+ years of experience in pricing, monetization, revenue operations, strategy and operations, program management, or a related commercial function, with at least 5 years in B2B SaaS pricing or monetization. A proven track record of driving pricing or packaging changes to execution in a multi-product, multi-segment SaaS business, not only recommending them. You can point to launches or migrations you delivered and the outcomes they produced. Strong program and project management skills. You are skilled at managing complex, cross-functional initiatives with many dependencies and stakeholders. Operations experience, such as pricing operations, deal desk, revenue operations, sales operations, or business operations, and familiarity with how pricing flows through quoting, billing, and finance systems. Strong business and pricing strategy acumen. You understand value metrics, packaging architecture, unit economics, and how pricing decisions affect revenue, margin, and customer experience. Excellent verbal and written communication. You can distill complex topics into clear narratives for executives, partners, and the field. Comfort working cross-functionally, with the ability to influence and build consensus across teams with different priorities. The ability to adapt and pivot quickly. You stay organized and focused as priorities change, and you bring structure to ambiguity. Analytical skills sufficient to size impacts and track results: advanced Excel or Sheets, working knowledge of SQL, and experience with a BI tool (e.g., Tableau). A collaborative, low-ego working style that fits Smartsheet's culture. Nice to Have: Experience in work management, collaboration, productivity, or analytics SaaS, ideally with AI-enhanced products. Experience launching or operationalizing pricing for AI features. Exposure to PLG plus enterprise "hybrid GTM" environments. Experience managing customer migrations, grandfathering, or renewal-based price changes. Hands-on familiarity with CPQ, billing, and CRM systems and the processes that surround them. Background in pricing consulting or strategy consulting. Experience with pricing governance forums. Global pricing experience (US plus EMEA/APAC).
