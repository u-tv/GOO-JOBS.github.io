Robinhood’s Security Team is looking for a stellar Engineering Manager to build and lead our Insider Threat team. The Insider Threat team is focused on protecting, detecting, and responding to and from internal threats to Robinhood data and systems. The team identifies malicious and accidental misuse of otherwise legitimate access to data from people inside the company. We investigate across a broad spectrum of threats and abuse including abuse of personal data, intellectual property, and leaks of sensitive information. We collaborate across our security engineering and business teams to build detection capabilities and analyze how abuse happens so that we can stay ahead of those who are interested in misusing access. This role is based in our Menlo Park, CA office, with in-person attendance expected at least 5 days per week. At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.
