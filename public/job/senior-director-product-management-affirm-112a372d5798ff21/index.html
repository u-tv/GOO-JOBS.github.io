Senior Director, Product Management

🏢 affirm 📍 Remote US 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

At Affirm, we exist for the moments that matter—giving people a clear, predictable way to pay over time, with no hidden fees, no surprises, and no tradeoffs on what matters most. Affirm is building the next generation of consumer finance, and the Affirm Card sits at the center of that vision—blending debit, pay-over-time, and network-native experiences into a single product. We’re looking for a Senior Director of Product to lead the Affirm Card and Card Platform—owning both the end-user experience, the business outcomes of every card product, and the underlying platform that powers card issuance, authorization, servicing, and partner integrations. This is a pillar-level leadership role responsible for defining strategy, building a high-performing team, and delivering durable business outcomes across one of Affirm’s most strategic product areas. What you’ll do Own the Card domain end-to-end Define and drive the multi-year vision for Affirm Card across consumer experience, growth, and economics, inclusive of new card products that complement the one we have in market today. Lead the US direct to consumer card products (Affirm Cards, and in app virtual cards) Oversee our Card Platform (authorization, ledgering, servicing, controls) which powers the D2C products both domestically and internationally, along with a number of merchant and partner integrations. Translate company strategy into a coherent roadmap spanning multiple teams and systems Build and scale a high-performing product organization Manage and develop a team of PMs and PM leaders Establish product standards, operating cadence, and decision frameworks Build a durable org that delivers high-quality outcomes without direct involvement in every decision Drive business outcomes at scale Own GMV, engagement, unit economics, and credit outcomes for the Card domain Shape investment strategy, tradeoffs, and resource allocation across multiple product areas Partner with Finance and Credit to optimize profitability and risk Lead platform strategy and execution Drive platform capabilities that unlock velocity across Affirm (APIs, controls, servicing systems) Partner deeply with Engineering to make cost vs. value tradeoffs at the system level Ensure the Card Platform scales reliably across use cases, geographies, and partners Operate as a cross-functional leader Align senior stakeholders across Engineering, Design, Credit, Legal, Partnerships, and GTM Build strong relationships with external partners (networks, processors, wallet providers) Represent the Card domain in executive forums and company planning cycles What we look for Product leadership at scale 15+ years in product management, with experience leading multi-team product areas or platforms Track record of owning large domains or business lines with meaningful financial impact Org-building capability Experience managing managers and building high-performing PM teams Proven ability to create systems, processes, and culture that scale Strategic and systems thinker Ability to operate at portfolio-level altitude—connecting product decisions to company strategy Strong judgment across second- and third-order platforms Execution and influence Ability to drive alignment across senior stakeholders and functions Strong product instincts paired with rigorous, data-driven decision-making Why this role matters Affirm Card is one of the fastest-growing parts of the business and a key driver of long-term differentiation.
