Jadeclass Education: Online English Writing and Analysis Course Developer - All Levels

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Canada URL: https://jobs.workable.com/company/579freWZJFcubEizieQPrD/jobs-at-jadeclass-education Overview JadeClass Education Singapore Writing and Analysis courses provide enrichment education to bright students who already have a good command of the English language and are focusing on their written English with the goal of continuing their education abroad. Our students vary in age from 6-16 and are carefully screened for English level, behavior, and motivation. We focus on teaching concrete skills that will help students in their learning endeavors. IMPORTANT: We will not consider your application without a video introduction!!! Job Responsibilities Our Writing and Analysis courses place one teacher with a group of up to eight students for a semester length course of study covering a topic such as story analysis, story writing, persuasive writing, narrative writing, analytical writing, and literature analysis. Lesson materials are provided by our design team and are classroom-ready: all teachers have to do is review them and teach! Grading and student support outside of the classroom is provided by our in-house staff – everything from presentations and in-class activities to homework, tests and keys with standardized comments are created during the development process so that the course can be taught and supported independently of its creators. · Develop and deliver well-planned, engaging online classes in a 1 on 8 format · Create, test, and revise lesson materials conforming to JadeClass standards · Commit to developing a full course of 15 lessons Applicant Requirements · Able to guarantee preparation for and attendance at each scheduled meeting · Fun personality, talkative · Logical, able to explain concepts clearly and simply · A good imitator, able to emulate our style · Neat and professional appearance · Positive attitude · Malleable, willing to change to meet standards · Perfect oral grammar and clear enunciation Schedule Development meetings can take place any day of the week online on the ClassIn (classin.com) platform between the hours of 08:00 and 20:00 UTC +8 (Singapore time). Lessons take place on the weekends during the fall and spring, and Monday-Saturday during the summer. For the Spring 2027 semester, classes will run from late February through late June with teaching days on Friday evenings, Saturdays, and Sundays. · February – June: Fri 16:30 – 20:30 (UTC+8) Sat, Sun 08:30 – 20:30 (UTC+8) Compensation · 360 USD per newly developed lesson – includes in-class materials as well as homework, grading keys, and supplementary materials · Teaching hours are paid separately – 75 USD per 90 minute lesson · Remuneration is through Wise (wise.com) between the 10th and the 12th of each month. Technology Requirements · Fast & stable internet connection (³ 20 Mbps) · Modern, reliable computer (~10,000+ on www.cpubenchmark.net/cpu_list.php) Application Process If you are interested in applying, please send the following to us at teaching@jadeclass.com: · Your resume · A link to your introduction video (Vimeo, YouTube, Google, or other safe platforms). The video can be 1-2 minutes in length, telling us a bit about your education and teaching background. If you send us a video file instead of a link, please make sure it is smaller than 20MB. · Where you saw our job posting · The name of the position for which you wish to apply Please understand that due to the high volume of applications, we are only able to consider applicants who submit all the information listed above. Thank you for your interest in JadeClass Education! To apply: https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/jadeclass-education-online-english-writing-and-analysis-course-developer-all-levels
