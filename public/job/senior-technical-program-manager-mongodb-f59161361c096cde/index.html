Senior Technical Program Manager

🏢 mongodb 📍 Gurugram 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

The HR Technology organization is looking for a seasoned Senior Technical Program Manager to help enable the efficient delivery of technology solutions to meet stakeholder needs. TPMs in the organization enable IT to accelerate business impact and strategically transform how we work with technology and AI. As a Senior Technical Program Manager, you will own the successful delivery of complex, cross-functional efforts. You will partner closely with stakeholders across the organization from Human Resources, Legal, Finance and other teams across the business as needed to develop and execute against our overall product vision and business objectives, uncover solutions to technical challenges, and provide data-driven insights that power strategic decision-making for our organization. We are looking to speak to candidates who are based in Gurugram for our hybrid working model. Position Expectations: Execute and improve the processes and tools that inform the daily life of the HR Technology delivery teams Drive coordination between product development teams and internal stakeholders to prioritize the team's backlog and plan its release roadmap Identify risks to the team's delivery, mitigating them where possible Own the data around the health of each team's project and ticket backlog Know when a project/team requires more hands-on management and know when it is time to operationalize and develop a process framework for the project and/or team(s) Promote a culture of continuous improvement by conducting retrospectives to help identify areas of growth and learnings within and across teams Recognize patterns in a sea of information and take action accordingly Expand knowledge into new domains by consistently working to gain the requisite technical knowledge required to skillfully manage the complexities, dependencies, and risks associated with HR technology enabled projects The right candidate for this role will be Experienced with 7+ years working in an PMO and/or engineering organization, whether in technical program management delivery, support, or an analytics role. Skilled at managing HR related technical programs end-to-end and working with engineering teams using a variety of program management and agile delivery techniques Capable of deep user empathy and adept at balancing product goals and engineering constraints to deliver impactful experiences Proficient in grasping the technologies of the HR Technology portfolio, and skilled at identifying the technical interfaces between teams Proficient in HR related processes and capabilities, including but not limited to: Talent Management, Learning and Development, Core HCM, Benefits/Compensation and more. Able to maintain a strategic view of our engineering processes while also supporting and optimizing the operations of the team Inspired to innovate in areas where automation can replace manual work.
