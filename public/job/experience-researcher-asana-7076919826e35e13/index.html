Experience Researcher

🏢 asana 📍 Warsaw 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Our work informs teams at every level of Asana and drives key roadmap decisions. We partner with Product Design, Product Management, Data Science, and Engineering, as well as business and marketing teams, to develop valuable insights about customer behaviors and attitudes, translating them into actionable recommendations. We think deeply about new areas, help develop and test concepts, and improve products and services once they've rolled out. As a Senior Experience Researcher, you will be a strategic partner who not only executes high-impact research but also shapes the research culture to elevate our collective craft. This role is based in our Warsaw office. You will split your time across two areas. The first is our mobile team, a locally based Warsaw team. The second is Command, a new product area with a team split between Warsaw and New York City. We operate with an office-centric hybrid schedule. In-office days are Monday, Tuesday and Thursday. Most Asanas have the option to work from home on Wednesdays. Working from home on Fridays depends on the type of work you do and the teams with which you partner. If you interview for this role, your recruiter will share more about in-office requirements. In this role, you will work in two very different research contexts. On mobile, you'll help steer a mature product using user feedback and build a durable research practice. You’ll also work on a new 0→1 product for software developers, Command. Together, these give you the chance to shape both the everyday experience of Asana on mobile and entirely new ways our customers work. What you’ll achieve Design the research process for mobile: Establish how the mobile team gathers, synthesizes and acts on research, from feedback loops to planned studies. Partner closely with Design and PM: Work side by side with your Warsaw teammates to shape roadmap decisions and the quality of the mobile experience. Research a 0 → 1 product: Lead exploratory and evaluative research from the earliest concepts, helping the team decide what to build and for whom. Make sense of a fast-moving space: Understand a rapidly changing technology area with emerging behaviors. Design new ways to research those behaviors, including how the software development lifecycle (SDLC) is changing and how Command can support it. Unlock new workflows and use cases: Uncover opportunities that open up entirely new ways of working for our customers. Work across locations: Collaborate with a team split between Warsaw and New York City, keeping research insights flowing across both. About you 6+ years of experience: practicing UX Research for digital products, with a proven track record of leading large-scale projects. Strong proficiency in mixed-methods research : seamlessly combining qualitative deep-dives (contextual inquiry, prototype testing) with quantitative validation (survey design, behavioral data analysis) to triangulate insights and build high-confidence recommendations. Comfort with ambiguity, including 0 → 1 work: experience researching new or emerging product areas where there is no established playbook, and designing research methods from scratch. Mature product judgement with the ability to distill messy signal into clear, &quot;low-polish&quot; deliverables that drive immediate decision-making. Strong organizational leadership and the ability to manage multiple complex workstreams while building deep trust and rapport with senior stakeholders. Persuasive communicator who can influence product strategy through storytelling and evidence-based recommendations. Actively experimenting with AI tools and emerging technologies, with a willingness to learn and leverage them to enhance productivity, collaboration, or decision-making. We're looking for researchers who can harness AI to extend and deepen the ways in which we develop insights and leverage them across the org. We know that the best ideas and solutions come from multi-dimensional teams. That's because these teams reflect a variety of backgrounds and professional experiences.
