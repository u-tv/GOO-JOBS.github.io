UCM Practice Growth Systems: 70-100K Potential for Practice Growth Advisor Sales Position (Central or Eastern Time Zone)

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Southern California URL: https://ucmpracticegrowthsystems.com/ Summary NOTE: Applicants MUST read this entire job posting and complete all steps outlined below to be considered for this position. Our Mission At UCM Practice Growth Systems, we're a team on a mission. Dedicated to the glory of God, we aim to bring hope and healing to sick and suffering people through our partnership with upper cervical chiropractors. We help chiropractic practices grow with proven patient acquisition systems, training and business growth consulting that let doctors focus on what matters most: helping more patients. We're looking for a driven, relationship-oriented sales professional who shares our passion for helping doctors grow their practices and is excited about the opportunity to build meaningful relationships while generating new business. Your Role in Our Mission As a Practice Growth Advisor, your primary responsibility is to generate new revenue by identifying, engaging, and converting qualified upper cervical chiropractic practices into UCM clients. This results-driven sales position requires someone who can confidently prospect, build relationships, conduct effective sales consultations, and close new business. You won't simply wait for leads to come to you. You'll take ownership of developing your own pipeline (we provide the leads for you), creating sales opportunities, and consistently turning qualified conversations into new clients. We believe great salespeople don't pressure prospects into buying. They identify real needs, build trust, provide valuable solutions, and guide prospects toward decisions that help their businesses succeed. What Success Looks Like Success in this position means consistently generating new business, maintaining a healthy sales pipeline, and converting qualified opportunities into paying clients. New deals may include Practice Growth Memberships, Associate Recruitment Programs, Start Right Programs, website designs, and other UCM solutions. A successful Practice Growth Advisor consistently closes 5 or more new deals per month, maintains a 40% conversion rate on qualified sales consultations, schedules at least 13 qualified outbound appointments, and successfully completes at least 12 of those appointments each month. Your Key Responsibilities New Revenue Generation Consistently generate new business by identifying qualified prospects, conducting sales consultations, and closing deals to achieve monthly revenue and sales goals. Outbound Prospecting & Lead Generation Proactively identify and engage potential clients through touchpoints; phone calls, texts, emails, social media messages, and other outreach strategies to build a consistent flow of qualified opportunities. PGA’s that consistently achieve 500 touchpoints per week get the best results. Sales Pipeline Management Take full ownership of your sales pipeline by tracking prospects, maintaining accurate CRM records, scheduling follow-ups, and moving opportunities efficiently through the sales process. Consultative Selling & Closing Build trust with prospective clients, identify their business challenges, recommend appropriate solutions, overcome objections, and confidently convert qualified opportunities into new clients. Product & Service Expertise Develop a thorough understanding of UCM's growth memberships, products, services and pricing to effectively communicate their value and recommend solutions that align with each practice's needs. Sales Performance & Accountability Consistently meet or exceed established KPIs, including new deals closed, consultation conversion rates, qualified appointments scheduled, and appointments completed. Relationship Building & Brand Representation Establish meaningful relationships with chiropractic professionals while representing UCM's mission, core values, and commitment to ethical, relationship-based selling. Industry Events & Networking Represent UCM at chiropractic conferences, college events, and industry gatherings to develop relationships, generate new opportunities, and expand the company's reach when needed. Sales Training & Continuous Improvement Participate in sales role play coaching, call reviews, and professional development to continuously improve prospecting effectiveness, objection handling, and closing performance. What We're Looking For Sales Experience and Skills Proven experience in B2B sales, business development, or consultative selling. Demonstrated success generating appointments through outbound prospecting. Strong ability to build rapport and establish trust with business owners or decision-makers. Experience conducting qualifying calls, demo consultations, and closing conversations. Confidence in overcoming objections and guiding prospects through purchasing decisions. Strong written and verbal communication skills. Experience using CRM systems to manage leads, track activity, and maintain an organized pipeline. Ability to work independently, prioritize opportunities, and consistently achieve measurable sales goals. Personal Characteristics Self-motivated: You take initiative and don't need constant supervision to generate opportunities. Results-oriented: You enjoy working toward clear performance goals and being accountable for measurable outcomes. Relationship-focused: You genuinely care about understanding people and helping them succeed. Persistent: You understand that successful sales require consistent outreach, follow-up, and resilience. Coachable: You welcome feedback, embrace training, and continuously improve your approach. Organized: You take pride in maintaining accurate records, reporting results daily and following through on commitments. Mission-aligned: You're excited about helping chiropractic practices grow so they can serve more patients. Compensation We offer a performance-based compensation structure that rewards successful business development and sales results. First 90 Days – Paid Training and Performance Period $3,000 monthly base compensation. 20% commission on deals personally generated and closed. Structured training and guidance from our Director of Sales. Opportunity to demonstrate sales ability, master our services, and build a productive pipeline. After 90 Days – Base + Commission Upon successfully completing the initial performance period: $1,500 monthly base compensation. 50% commission on first-month membership revenue for eligible sales. 25% commission on eligible one-time purchases. Significant earnings potential based on individual sales performance with our sales career path. The initial 90-day evaluation includes successfully learning UCM's offerings, independently sourcing opportunities, closing at least 5 personally generated deals, maintaining accurate CRM records, and demonstrating alignment with company standards. Benefits Opportunities for professional growth and leadership advancement. Company-sponsored professional and personal development training. 50% contribution toward an eligible major medical insurance policy premium. 100% reimbursement for eligible upper cervical chiropractic care after 90 days. Company-paid travel expenses for approved business events. Paid time off after 90 days, subject to company PTO policy. Complimentary Financial Peace University participation after 180 days. Eligibility for monthly profit sharing bonuses after 365 days. 401(k) with a 4% company match after 365 days. Employment is contingent upon confirmation that the candidate’s location is supported by the company’s payroll, insurance, benefits, and employment arrangements. Applicants must also be consistently available during Central or Eastern Time Zone business hours. Flexible Hours: Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday 8 AM to 5 PM Eastern time or 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern time & Tuesdays 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM Eastern time. 60-minute lunch each day.
