Principal Analyst Relations Manager

🏢 datadog 📍 Boston, Massachusetts, USA; New York, New York, USA; San Francisco, California, USA 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Datadog is seeking an Analyst Relations Principal to help shape and scale our global Analyst Relations (AR) program. In this role, you will lead strategic engagement with industry analysts across observability, AI, and adjacent cloud technologies, ensuring they have a clear understanding of Datadog’s platform, innovation, and long-term vision. You will partner closely with Product Management and Product Marketing to translate complex technical capabilities into compelling narratives, while bringing analyst insights back into the business to inform strategy and positioning. This is a highly cross-functional role that blends strategic thinking with operational excellence and offers strong visibility across senior leadership. At Datadog, we place value in our office culture - the relationships and collaboration it builds and the creativity it brings to the table. We operate as a hybrid workplace to ensure our Datadogs can create a work-life harmony that best fits them. What You’ll Do: Develop and drive the global analyst relations strategy across Datadog’s observability and AI portfolio Build and maintain trusted relationships with key analysts across firms such as Gartner, Forrester, and IDC Partner with Product Management and Product Marketing to craft clear, differentiated narratives aligned to market trends Lead strategy, preparation and execution of analyst evaluations and research cycles, ensuring high-quality submissions Prepare senior leaders for analyst briefings with structured messaging and executive-ready materials Synthesize analyst insights into actionable recommendations for product strategy and go-to-market alignment Who You Are: 8+ years of experience in Analyst Relations, Product Marketing, or a related role within the technology industry Proven experience building and scaling analyst engagement programs tied to business objectives Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to simplify complex technical concepts Strong executive presence with experience working with and advising senior executives in high-visibility external engagements Highly organized with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment Demonstrated collaboration with cross-functional teams including Product Management and Marketing Datadog values people from all walks of life. We know not everyone will meet all the above qualifications on day one. That’s okay. If you’re passionate about technology and want to grow your experience, we encourage you to apply.
