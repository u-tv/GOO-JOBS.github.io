Manager, Field Engineering (Greater China)

🏢 databricks 📍 Singapore 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

As a Manager, Field Engineering, you will lead a diverse team of technical pre-sales Solutions Architects covering the Greater China (Taiwan region). You’ll play a key leadership role at the intersection of technology and business—guiding your team to design impactful solutions and drive growth through collaboration and innovation. You’ll empower your team to communicate complex ideas with clarity, support strategic enterprise customer engagements, and build lasting partnerships with clients and internal stakeholders. The impact you will have: Build, lead, mentor, and support a high-performing, inclusive pre-sales team. Drive your team to meet and exceed consumption and pipeline targets through rigorous forecasting, QBRs, and data-driven inspection cadences. Foster an environment of belonging, continuous learning, and psychological safety that reflects Databricks’ values of customer focus, teamwork, and diversity. Work closely with Sales and other teams to identify opportunities and deliver data-driven, value-focused solutions. Co-own the territory plan, support strategic deal cycles, and resolve cross-functional friction. Establish best practices that improve team efficiency, collaboration, and business impact. Lead by example in using AI tooling, encourage rapid experimentation, and ensure your team delivers customer-centric solutions at the pace the market demands. Build trusted relationships with customers and partners, acting as a strategic advisor in their digital transformation journey. Personally engage with senior customer stakeholders (VP/CxO level) as a trusted technical advisor, framing Databricks' value in terms of business outcomes and ROI Represent Databricks as part of the regional leadership team, contributing to our local presence and inclusive culture. What we’re looking for: You do not need to fulfil every single requirement to be a strong candidate. If you are excited about this role and have related experience, we encourage you to apply. Proven experience leading or managing technical teams in Big Data, Cloud (AWS, Azure, or GCP), or SaaS environments—or equivalent experience gained through hands-on engineering or consulting work. 5+ years of experience in the data and analytics space with a technical product (data warehousing, big data, ML, or data science) Familiarity with pre-sales processes and sales frameworks. A track record of building and leading technical pre-sales teams — hiring, onboarding, enabling, building a territory, and exceeding targets Demonstrated ability to own a business number and run it with operational rigor (forecasting, pipeline management, capacity allocation) Strong collaboration skills and the ability to partner effectively with Sales, Marketing, and other cross-functional leaders. Excellent communication and interpersonal skills Enthusiasm for data, AI, and cloud technologies, and the ability to connect these to real business outcomes. Demonstrated use of AI tools to improve team productivity and customer outcomes Business proficiency in English and Mandarin. Experience in the Greater China Region is required to enable effective collaboration and understanding of client needs. The successful candidate will engage with the Greater China Region internal teams and customers in Mandarin for technical sales discussions, address technical challenges, and articulate clear technical solutions and value propositions.
