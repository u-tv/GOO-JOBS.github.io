Product Engineer - Ads Bidding

🏢 faire 📍 Toronto, ON 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Faire Faire is a technology wholesale platform built on the belief that the future is local. Independent retailers around the globe collectively represent a multi-hundred-billion-dollar wholesale market that has historically been fragmented and offline. At Faire, we're using the power of tech, data, and machine learning to connect this thriving community of entrepreneurs across the globe. Picture your favorite boutique in town — we help them discover the best products from around the world to sell in their stores. With the right tools and insights, we believe that we can level the playing field so businesses can grow and local communities can thrive. We’re looking for smart, resourceful and passionate people to join us as we power the shop local movement. If you believe in community, come join ours. About this role: Faire's Ads team builds the systems that decide which ads brands show, at what price, and how their budgets are spent. We own the bidding side of the Ads business: auction and bidding logic, budget pacing, spend accuracy, and the infrastructure that keeps all of it fast, correct, and trustworthy. As an infrastructure engineer on the team, you'll own systems end-to-end: design them, ship them, run them in production, and measure whether they made bidding better for advertisers and retailers. You'll partner with Product, Data Science, ML, and the product engineers who build on top of our platform to shape what we build, not just how it gets built. This is an ideal role for engineers who enjoy hard systems problems, want to see their work show up directly in revenue and advertiser outcomes, and bring genuine excitement about AI. You'll go deep on how ads bidding works in a wholesale marketplace, where the advertisers are independent brands and the buyers are the retailers who stock them. What you'll do: Infrastructure ownership: Design, build, and operate the services behind bidding, auction, and budget pacing, from problem to launch and through on-call. Reliability and performance: Build systems that stay low-latency, high-throughput, and correct under failure. Spend accuracy affects advertiser money directly, so you'll treat correctness as a product requirement. Data and feedback loops: Build and maintain the streaming and batch pipelines that feed bidding with fresh, trustworthy signals such as impressions, clicks, conversions, and spend, and contribute to the technical strategy that lets them scale. AI fluency: Use AI tooling as a core part of how you deliver impact, from prototyping and coding to debugging, reviewing, and exploring ideas faster. Observability and experimentation: Instrument what you ship with monitoring, alerting, and SLOs, and partner with Data Science and ML to run experiments and evaluate how changes to bidding systems affect advertiser outcomes. Qualifications: Must-haves 2+ years of full-time software engineering experience building and operating backend or distributed systems in a production environment. Strong systems fundamentals. You design services for latency, throughput, correctness, and failure handling, and you can explain the tradeoffs you made and the impact of systems you've built. Has integrated AI tools into your regular engineering workflow; we want to see that AI has become part of how you work, not just something you've tried. Background in at least one of: ads / ad tech (auction, bidding, pacing, or serving), real-time or high-throughput systems, or data engineering (streaming, pipelines, ETL, warehousing). Ownership mindset: comfortable with on-call, debugging production issues, and ambiguity. A clear communicator who works effectively with PMs, data scientists, ML engineers, and product engineers, with product sense for how infrastructure choices affect advertisers and retailers. Preferred Experience with ad auctions, bid optimization, budget pacing, ads ranking, or ads serving. More than one of the domain backgrounds above (ads + real-time systems, real-time systems + data engineering, etc.). Experience with streaming or batch data systems (such as Kafka, Flink, or Spark) and large-scale data stores. Experience with experimentation infrastructure, ML model or feature serving, or observability tooling. Salary range: Canada: the pay range for this role is $129,500 - $ 178,000 per year.
