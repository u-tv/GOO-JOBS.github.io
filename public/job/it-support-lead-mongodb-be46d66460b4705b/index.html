IT Support Lead

🏢 mongodb 📍 New York City 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

What You'll Do Strategic IT leadership & stakeholder alignment. Partner with business leaders, Executive Assistants (EAs), Workplace/Facilities, and Audio/Visual (AV) teams to translate organizational needs into seamless IT solutions. Manage regional IT budgets, optimize cloud costs (FinOps), negotiate vendor contracts, and drive multi-site hybrid work People leadership & operational rigor. Hire, mentor, and direct a hybrid team of ~15-20 L2/L3 IT support leads and specialists across multiple Americas offices (NYC HQ, Austin, SF, Palo Alto). Establish systems of record and operational workflows using Jira and the ITSM ticketing system to streamline ticketing, project tracking, and lifecycle management. Requires up to 5-10% travel and availability for after-hours escalation management during major operational incidents or critical office deployments Incident management & service standards. Serve as the primary escalation point for major operational outages. Enforce quantifiable KPIs, including Customer Satisfaction (CSAT), Mean Time to Resolution (MTTR), and First Time Fix Rate (FTTR), through trend analysis and Root Cause Analysis (RCA). Practical automation & operational reporting. Integrate standard workflow automation tools and practical AI capabilities into daily IT service delivery. Use ticketing data and operational metrics to make data-driven decisions on capacity planning, service scaling, and office tech deployments Baseline risk management & asset governance. Ensure corporate infrastructure adheres to baseline compliance, hardware lifecycle standards, and access management best practices, delivering secure and productive environments for all employees What office deployments, and daily operations Demonstrated success coaching and mentoring L2/L3 support personnel across both local HQ and remote/hybrid team settings Calm, empathetic, and decisive
