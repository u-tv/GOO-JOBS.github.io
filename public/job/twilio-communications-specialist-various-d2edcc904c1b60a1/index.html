About the job This position is needed to lead the external communications for Twilio’s CIO and our Speaking and Awards program. The role will also support the North America PR team across product launches, external campaigns, thought leadership content, and reporting & measurement. The role sits within Twilio’s Corporate Communications team, which is responsible for shaping and delivering Twilio’s story to media, customers, industry analysts, and employees. You will collaborate closely with partners across Analyst Relations, Government Relations & Public Policy, Internal Comms, Social Media, and R&D and Enterprise Technology leadership to craft compelling external narratives that generate press coverage and awareness. Responsibilities In this role, you’ll: Lead External CIO Communications: Design and execute a comprehensive external communications strategy for Twilio’s Chief Information Officer, inclusive of media opportunities, thought leadership, speaking engagements, and LinkedIn social presence. Own Speaking & Awards Program: Partner closely with Twilio’s R&D and Enterprise Technology leadership, Events, Executive Communications, and external PR agency partners to develop a comprehensive speaking and awards program, driving thought leadership and awareness. Support North America PR Programs & Product Launches: Collaborate cross-functionally with members of the Corporate Communications team, Product Marketing, and Product Management on thought leadership content and external company and product announcements. Manage External Comms for AR, Government Relations, & Public Policy Teams : Serve as the PR team lead for ad hoc external facing moments and announcements coming from Twilio’s Analyst Relations, Government Relations, and Public Policy teams. Lead Reporting & Measurement: Partnering with Twilio’s PR agency and International PR Team, lead PR team reporting and measurement, including AEO visibility, daily coverage tracking, and quarterly QBR reporting. Own AI tools for the PR team: Own the PR team’s AI tool stack—ensuring all training data and prompt libraries remain current. Proactively identify new AI use cases to streamline workflows and drive team efficiency. Track record of directly supporting and advising C-suite leaders in high-stakes, fast-moving environments. Strong writing, editing, and storytelling skills with executive judgment and the ability to capture a specific leader's authentic voice. Systematic thinker who can establish high-level narratives and execute them with tactical precision. Exceptional organizational skills with a calm-under-pressure demeanor and the ability to manage competing priorities on tight timelines. Desired: Active practitioner of AI: Strong interest in how artificial intelligence is transforming public relations and communications, with a willingness to lead change and build innovative workflows using AI tools. Familiarity with enterprise technology: A solid baseline understanding of cloud communications, API technologies, data platforms, and the customer engagement landscape. Experience managing executives’ social media presence (e.g., LinkedIn) with an eye for digital engagement trends. Experience working inside a fast-paced agency environment.
