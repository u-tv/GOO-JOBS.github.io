Taste Labs: Operations Manager, Community

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: United States URL: https://tastelabs.com/ About the company Taste Labs is building the data and infrastructure layer for taste. Our goal is to end AI slop. To make AI feel right, not just be correct. We raised $18.5M in seed co-led by Amplify and CRV, and most frontier labs are already customers. AI has nailed objective domains and can generate anything. The hard part left is judgement: what fits, what feels like you, what's actually GREAT. We're turning that into something measurable, starting with design. We do it on two sides: building the post-training data and RL environments that teach taste to frontier models, and the context and verification tools agents need to produce work that's more creative, more on-brand, more right. If that problem excites you, you'll like it here! About this role The TasteMakers community is at the core of what we do. Your role is to build the operating system, processing and check-ins for the community to run smooth. This is not a community management role, it's an ops one - you need to be a phenomenal operator who's great at turning things into machines, while being great with people. DAY TO DAY You analyze the end to end funnel weekly, pull metrics and flag risks or areas of concern You run onboarding meetings and training with experts, you build training materials and create the process around it You run reactivation campaigns, engaging experts that have dropped off or not been active. You'll schedule sessions, email campaigns chase stalls, move experts through different steps in the funnel, answer questions, and more You'll run "bridging": the between project touchpoints to keep experts engaged and warm, so that retention stays high. YOU'LL BE A GREAT FIT IF YOU: Are a doer, you know how to get stuff done. The goal is not just spotting problems, it's fixing them end to end. You need to be a true owner and not be afraid to get hands dirty. Are comfortable with ambiguity. We love process, but we won't always have answers for everything and need to start moving with imperfect information. You need to be comfortable navigating these scenarios while trying to bring structure. Are very organized, meticulous and detail oriented Love this mission and problem we're working on. This domain of "taste" and "design" is creative and subjective, and it's part of the fun of it. WHERE YOU MIGHT COME FROM: We are intentionally open. A handful of profiles match the shape of this work, none of which are obvious by title. Ops or production coordinators at design-first agencies, studios, or production companies Talent coordinators at agencies, casting operations, or fellowship and residency programs Marketplace or community ops at companies where supply quality mattered more than supply volume Program coordinators who ran cohorts, bootcamps, or apprenticeships end to end Ops generalists or EAs at fast companies who built the tracker everyone ended up using Freelance producers who have kept many-headed projects on schedule without authority OTHER NOTES: This is a contractor position, fully remote US or Global. We have team members from several countries. To apply: https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/taste-labs-operations-manager-community
