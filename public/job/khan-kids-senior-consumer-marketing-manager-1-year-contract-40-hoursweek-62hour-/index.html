ABOUT KHAN ACADEMY Khan Academy is a nonprofit with the mission to deliver a free, world-class education to anyone, anywhere. Our proven learning platform offers free, high-quality supplemental learning content and practice that cover Pre-K - 12th grade and early college core academic subjects, focusing on math and science. We have over 181 million registered learners globally and are committed to improving learning outcomes for students worldwide, focusing on learners in historically under-resourced communities. ABOUT KHAN ACADEMY KIDS Khan Academy Kids is a free and fun learning program for children ages 2 to 8. Kodi Bear and a cast of animated characters lead children on a personalized education journey filled with fun, standards-aligned activities in early literacy, math, executive functioning and social emotional skills. Built by a small but mighty team, Khan Academy Kids is used by millions of children in homes and classrooms around the world. OUR COMMUNITY Our students, teachers, and parents come from all walks of life, and so do we. Our team includes people from academia, traditional/non-traditional education, big tech companies, and tiny startups. We hire great people from diverse backgrounds and experiences because it makes our company stronger. We value diversity, equity, inclusion, and belonging as necessary to achieve our mission and impact the communities we serve. We know that transforming education starts in-house with learning about ourselves and our colleagues. We strive to be world-class in investing in our people and commit to developing you as a professional. THE ROLE The Khan Academy Kids team is excited to add a contract Marketing Specialist to our team. Working closely with the Marketing Lead, this role runs the channels where families and teachers discover Khan Academy Kids in support of the launch of our new English language learning product. The Marketing Lead sets overall strategy and priorities. From there, the Marketing Specialist builds the channel-level plans and executes them across app store marketing, consumer digital advertising, and organic social media, and serves as our hands-on analyst for all three. We're looking for someone who is curious about what the numbers are telling them and who finds real joy in getting high-quality learning into the hands of more kids.. Responsibilities: Working with the Marketing Lead, who sets overall strategy and priorities, you will: Manage app store marketing across the Apple App Store and Google Play, including listing optimization, creative testing, promotional events, and seasonal updates. Run consumer advertising across Apple Ads, Google Ads, and Meta. Build campaigns, manage spending within approved budgets, test audiences and creative, and develop ad materials with the Marketing Lead's review. Run organic social across Instagram, Threads, Facebook, and TikTok, and manage our creator and ambassador programs. Own the publishing calendar, community engagement, and partnerships from outreach through delivery. Create content that sounds like us: our characters, our warmth, and learning that feels like play. Support the US and international launch of our new English language learning product. Coordinate localization of store listings and creative, and adapt campaigns and social content for each market. Own performance reporting across channels and markets, including app store impressions, conversion, and installs. Share regular readouts and recommend where to invest next. Work with Khan Academy Kids' design, content, and product teams and Khan Academy's marketing team to keep messaging consistent. Support website updates, content promotion, and occasional school and district marketing. The role may expand to include lifecycle and family email. Use AI tools responsibly to work faster without sacrificing quality, accuracy, or brand voice. WHAT YOU BRING 4–6 years of digital marketing experience, including hands-on consumer app marketing. You understand app store discovery, install conversion, and the difference between a download and an engaged user. Direct experience with at least two of Apple Ads, Google Ads, or Meta Ads, including writing copy, briefing creative, running campaigns, and reading test results. Experience creating organic social content and managing creator, influencer, or ambassador relationships. Experience adapting marketing for international audiences through localized store listings, creative, or campaigns. You know what it takes to make copy land in a market that isn't your own. Strong analytical skills. You can pull your own numbers, explain what they mean, and turn them into recommendations. Familiarity with GA4, Google Tag Manager, Semrush, or Brandwatch is a plus but not required. Clear communication and strong project management. You can write for families and teachers, present recommendations, and keep multiple channels and collaborators moving without dropping details. Real investment in the mission. You care about making high-quality learning accessible and reaching the kids who need it most. Experience marketing to families, teachers, or in edtech is a plus. Experience using AI responsibly in marketing, with good judgment about where it helps and where a person needs to own the output. This is a 1 year contract at 40 hours/week for $62/hour.
