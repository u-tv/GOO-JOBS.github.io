Secure Every Identity, from AI to Human Identity is the key to unlocking the potential of AI. This work requires a relentless drive to solve complex challenges with real-world stakes. We are looking for builders and owners who operate with speed and urgency and execute with excellence. This is an opportunity to do career-defining work. We're all in on this mission. If you are too, let's talk. We build the platform that gets Okta's code tested, merged, built, and shipped. Bacon, our CI system, runs tens of thousands of tests on every commit. Around it we own the services that make CI observable and self-healing, and the release tooling that takes a green commit to a production release: code freeze, backporting, release assembly, artifact promotion, and release auditing. We are a small team, we ship weekly, and engineers own services end to end. Our goals are to cut feedback time from hours to minutes, keep mainline green, and make every release predictable, auditable, and boring. Our engineering team is fast, innovative and flexible; with a weekly release cycle and individual ownership. We expect great things from our engineers and reward them with stimulating new projects, emerging technologies and the chance to be part of a company that is changing the cloud computing landscape forever. You will get an opportunity to work in scaling our infrastructure to the next generation. Our scale is already huge in running tens of thousands of tests for every commit automatically. This comes with challenges in speed by reducing compute time from days to a few minutes. Responsibilities: Release engineering Own and evolve the release toolchain: code freeze, backporting to release branches, release assembly, artifact promotion, and release auditing. You'll build the automation and Slack workflows (for example in Baconbot) that engineers rely on. Keep weekly releases on schedule. That includes release branch health, cherry-pick/backport gaps, and promotion of build artifacts through environments. Build controls and audit trails that prove what shipped, from where, and who approved it, supporting compliance requirements without slowing teams down. Run the release process during freezes and incidents. You'll triage blocked releases, retry or unstick commits, and write the post-incident follow-ups. CI platform Design, build, and operate the services behind Bacon: REST/GraphQL APIs, data pipelines, and the React frontend, in Java, Python, Node/TypeScript, and Go. Improve CI speed, reliability, and cost across runner infrastructure on AWS, host images, queue and job allocation, and flaky-test detection and quarantine. Make CI and releases observable with telemetry, metrics, and dashboards (OpenTelemetry, Grafana, time-series stores) that show why a build or release failed and how healthy the fleet is. Apply LLMs to developer workflows, such as automated log triage and failure root-causing, with sensible guardrails. Across both Automate everything. Everybody automates here, so you'll write tools and scripts that remove toil for thousands of internal users. Partner with product engineering, QA, and Ops teams on rollouts, adoption, and feedback. Share operational ownership: on-call participation, runbooks, alerting, and incident follow-up for what you build.
