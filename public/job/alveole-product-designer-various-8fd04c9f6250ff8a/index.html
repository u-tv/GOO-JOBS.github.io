Alveole: Product Designer

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada URL: https://www.alveole.com About us 🐝 Alvéole is on a mission to connect people with nature in cities, one building at a time. As a certified B Corporation since 2018, we believe business can be a force for good. We partner with the world’s leading real estate companies to turn sustainability into an experience that tenants can see, touch, and feel. Through rooftop beehives and educational biodiversity programs, we help our clients meet their environmental goals while engaging communities and strengthening their brands. You can find our work in (and on) thousands of properties across North America and Europe, driven by a belief that business and biodiversity can thrive together. Learn more at alveole.buzz ✨ The Role We're looking for a Product Designer to join our Product team and lead the design of our two digital products: MyHive , our customer engagement platform, and Aura , our biodiversity data and insights platform. Until now, design has been handled by a mix of engineers, product team members, and an external designer. It's worked, but as our products grow, we need someone who can take full ownership of design, make decisions, and help us build better experiences for our users. You'll work closely with our Product Director and Engineering team, getting involved in everything from understanding user needs and improving existing features to designing new ones. We're a small team, so you'll have plenty of autonomy, a direct impact on both products, and the opportunity to shape how we approach design at Alvéole. 📍 Key Responsibilities Own end-to-end UX/UI design across MyHive and Aura, from understanding user needs and mapping flows to wireframes, high-fidelity designs, and implementation. Translate complex product requirements, customer feedback, and business needs into intuitive, user-friendly experiences. Collaborate closely with Product and Engineering to shape solutions, challenge assumptions, and make thoughtful design decisions. Build, maintain, and evolve a consistent design system across both products, improving usability and reducing design inconsistencies. Conduct or contribute to user research, customer interviews, and usability testing to validate ideas and continuously improve the experience. Design responsive experiences across desktop and mobile, balancing usability, accessibility, technical feasibility, and business priorities. Support occasional visual design needs, including customer-facing materials and product-related communications, while keeping product design as your main focus. 🎯 You're the Ideal Fit If You Have Must-Haves Around 3+ years of experience in Product Design, UX/UI Design, or a similar role . Experience designing B2B digital products , ideally SaaS platforms or web applications. Hands-on experience designing for both desktop and mobile , with a strong understanding of responsive design principles. A strong portfolio showcasing your design process, problem-solving skills, and examples of products or features you've helped bring to life. Solid understanding of UX principles, user flows, information architecture, interaction design, and visual design. Ability to work independently, take ownership of design decisions, and collaborate effectively with engineers and product stakeholders. Strong communication skills, curiosity, and openness to feedback. Professional proficiency in English. Nice-to-Haves Experience designing data-heavy products, dashboards, or complex B2B workflows. Experience building or scaling a design system. Experience working in a startup or small product team where you've had significant autonomy. Interest in sustainability, biodiversity, or environmental technology. 🌍 Location & Work Setup Location: Fully remote within Canada Type of contract: Permanent, Full-time (40h/week) Salary: 85,000–110,000 CAD annually 🐝 What's in It for You? 🌱 Join a mission-driven company creating meaningful environmental impact across North America and Europe. 🎨 Take real ownership of product design across two growing digital platforms, with the freedom to influence how our products look, feel, and evolve. 🚀 Work closely with a small, collaborative Product and Engineering team where your ideas and decisions will have a visible impact. 💰 Competitive compensation package. 🏥 Comprehensive health benefits from Day 1. 🌴 Generous and flexible PTO policy. 🏡 Fully remote work within Canada. ⚙️ Our Hiring Process Intro conversation with Talent Acquisition (15 min) Interview with the Hiring Manager Work Sample Presentation + Debrief Offer 🌐 Language Requirements Some roles at Alvéole require proficiency in English and/or French due to collaboration with clients, partners, and team members across North America.
