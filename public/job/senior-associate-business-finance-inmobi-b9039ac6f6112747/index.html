About the Role The Senior Associate – Business Finance will work as part of the Business Finance team, supporting revenue operations, planning cycles, and commercial decision-making for the unit. This is a high-exposure role suited for someone early in their career who wants to build a strong foundation in commercial finance within a fast-paced ad-tech environment. Key Responsibilities Prepare monthly accruals, maintain revenue files, and assist in building and updating the revenue and cost forecast with accuracy and timeliness Support the annual planning process by building data packs, maintaining models, and conducting research on market/competitive trends Review cost and revenue contracts to identify key commercial terms, flag risks or inconsistencies, and suggest improvements that drive better financial outcomes for InMobi Assist in evaluating deal structures by preparing deal-level P&L templates, running scenario analyses, and documenting adherence to pricing and margin guidelines Ensure completeness and accuracy of financial data across systems — own periodic reconciliations between internal records, contracts, and actuals Identify and document process inefficiencies in recurring finance workflows; propose potential improvements and help implement approved changes Prepare data-driven briefs and supporting materials for agency discussions on incentive structures, including historical performance summaries and benchmarking What We're Looking For CA qualified, or MBA (Finance) from a reputed institute; 2–3 years of experience in BFM, FP&A, commercial finance; freshers with strong internships considered Strong Excel skills; comfort with large datasets and financial modelling Familiarity with SQL or BI tools (Looker, Tableau, Power BI) is a plus Sharp analytical thinking with attention to detail Clear written and verbal communication — ability to summarize findings crisply Proactive, curious, and comfortable operating in ambiguity The InMobi Culture At InMobi, culture isn’t a buzzword; it's an ethos woven by every InMobian, reflecting our diverse backgrounds and experiences.
