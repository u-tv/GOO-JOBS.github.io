Job Description

MongoDB's Application Modernization Platform (AMP) helps organizations modernize legacy applications and move their workloads and associated data from relational databases to MongoDB. Our customers need a dependable way to bring analysis, transformation, testing, and migration capabilities into their modernization workflows. The Platform team builds the shared foundations that make AMP a cohesive product. We connect specialized tools and services through consistent interfaces, integrate them with AI agents and development environments, and make them secure, observable, and straightforward to deploy and operate. Our work enables product teams to deliver new capabilities without repeatedly solving the same integration and infrastructure problems. We are looking for a Staff Engineer to set technical direction and lead delivery of this platform. You will combine deep systems engineering with product judgment, turning ambiguous requirements into reusable services and working integrations. You will define clear boundaries between platform, product teams, and external partners; make deliberate build-versus-integrate decisions; and contribute foundational code. Your impact will be measured by how reliably teams and customers can adopt and use AMP capabilities, and how much engineering effort the platform removes from their path. We are looking to speak to candidates who are based in Gurugram for our hybrid working model.
