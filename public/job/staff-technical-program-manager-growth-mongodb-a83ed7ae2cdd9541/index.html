Job Description

As a Staff Technical Program Manager, you will own the successful delivery of complex, cross-functional efforts. This role can be based out of our Toronto office or remotely in the EST Canadian region. Position Expectations Leverage technical acumen and analytical skills to drive engineering programs forward and maximize business value delivery Recognize patterns in a sea of information and take action accordingly Develop a strategic approach to program management that balances agility and process.
