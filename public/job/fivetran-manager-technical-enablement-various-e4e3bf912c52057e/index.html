Fivetran : Manager, Technical Enablement

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote, New York, United States, AMER From Fivetran’s founding until now, our mission has remained the same: to make access to data as simple and reliable as electricity. With Fivetran, customer data arrives in their warehouses, canonical and ready to query, with no engineering or maintenance required. We’re proud that more organizations continue to leverage our technology every day to become truly data-driven. About Us Fivetran and dbt Labs are bringing together two industry-leading companies with a shared mission: helping organizations unlock the full value of their data. Together, we’re delivering the data infrastructure layer that helps organizations move, transform, and trust their data — from the moment data moves, through every transformation, to the context teams and AI systems rely on. Fivetran helps organizations automate data movement across the systems, clouds, engines, and tools they rely on. dbt Labs pioneered analytics engineering, helping teams transform data into reliable, governed insights. Together, we support thousands of organizations as they build a trusted foundation for analytics, AI, and better business decisions. As we bring our teams and technology together, we’re building on the strengths of both companies while continuing to deliver the products and experiences our customers know and trust. It’s an exciting time to join us: we’re creating a company with the scale, talent, and technology to help more organizations put their data to work with greater speed, confidence, and impact. During this transition period, you may see references to both Fivetran and dbt Labs throughout our recruiting process as we integrate our teams, systems, and career sites. About the Role As Manager, Technical Enablement, you will be the founding people leader of a dedicated technical enablement function, built to support our large and growing global Sales Engineering team. Our Sales Engineers are central to how we win, and as the team scales, it needs enablement that matches the depth of the work. You will hire, lead, and develop a team of enablement professionals, and you will serve as a player-coach, contributing directly to the programs your team delivers. Working in partnership with the Director of Revenue Enablement and global Sales Engineering leadership, you will own the strategy, roadmap, and execution of advanced technical enablement. You will identify capability gaps, establish proficiency standards, and build the technical onboarding, hands-on workshops, certifications, and ongoing coaching that take our field well beyond foundational product knowledge. You will partner closely with Product and Product Marketing to turn each release into field-ready technical content, and you will measure readiness and program effectiveness so that the function earns its place as a trusted partner to the field and to leadership. Technologies You’ll Use Notion, Salesforce, Gong, Thought Industries, Claude, Sumble, Hex, Nook, Jira, Navan, Nooks, Sales Navigator, AI role play tools What You’ll Do Own the strategy, roadmap, and execution of advanced technical enablement in partnership with the Director of Revenue Enablement for the global Field Engineering team Lead and develop a team of enablement professionals while serving as a player-coach, contributing directly to the execution of team priorities. Partner with global Field Engineering leadership to identify capability gaps, establish proficiency standards, and prioritize readiness programs across regions. Build and maintain deep technical product training, hands-on workshops, certifications, and ongoing coaching that go beyond foundational seller education. Define technical onboarding curriculum, ramp milestones, and proficiency benchmarks in partnership with the existing onboarding team. Partner closely with Product and Product Marketing to translate releases and product differentiation into field-ready technical content and practical exercises. Collaborate with Sales and Revenue Enablement to connect seller-facing technical fluency with advanced solution discovery, demonstrations, evaluations, and technical sales processes. Build a structured feedback loop between field teams and Product, and measure technical readiness, training adoption, demo environment health, and program effectiveness. Lead cross-functional programs through influence, clear stakeholder alignment, and disciplined execution in a global matrix environment. Monitor completion rates, assessment results, field feedback, and ramp outcomes, and use these insights to refine programs and report progress to leadership. Skills We’re Looking For 4+ years of experience in technical pre-sales, solutions architecture, field engineering, or related technical go-to-market roles, with demonstrated leadership of complex programs or teams. Previous people management experience, including hiring, coaching, and developing team members Deep working knowledge of modern data platforms, data integration, analytics engineering, cloud data architectures, and technical evaluation practices. Experience designing enablement programs, such as and delivering advanced technical training, coaching, and developing on-demand learning modules Ability to partner credibly with Product, Product Marketing, Sales, and senior Field Engineering leaders across a global organization. Strong program management, communication, facilitation, and stakeholder influence skills; ability to drive results through a matrix operating model.
