Protective Life: Reinsurance Accountant II - Specialty P&C

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Work From Home The work we do has an impact on millions of lives, and you can be a part of it. We help protect our customers against life’s uncertainties. Regardless of where you work within the company, you’ll be helping provide protection and peace of mind when our customers need it most. The Reinsurance Accountant II will support Obsidian, Protective’s specialty property and casualty (P&C) business, which operates in the excess and surplus (E&S) insurance market. Obsidian supports specialty P&C insurance programs and provides the carrier infrastructure, oversight, and access to reinsurance capacity needed to operate and grow these programs. In this role, you will support the financial and operational administration of Obsidian’s reinsurance programs, with responsibility for reinsurance cessions and settlements, collateral monitoring, account reconciliations, statutory reporting, and the financial administration of reinsurance agreements. This is an excellent opportunity for an analytical, detail-oriented professional who is interested in the specialty P&C and E&S insurance market and enjoys understanding how contractual terms translate into financial activity. The successful candidate will bring strong problem-solving skills, intellectual curiosity, and the ability to independently manage detailed financial processes while building a deeper understanding of reinsurance and specialty insurance. Work Arrangement This is a primarily remote position. Candidates should reside within reasonable driving distance (~2 hours) of Middletown, Connecticut and be available to travel to the Middletown office approximately 3–5 times per year for team meetings and other business needs. Key Responsibilities Prepare reinsurance cession statements and support related remittances and settlements. Partner with reinsurers and reinsurance brokers to support cessions, settlements, receivables, and payables. Monitor collateral requirements, including letters of credit and related funding requirements. Support the timely collection and payment of reinsurance receivables and payables. Calculate adjustable reinsurance features, including contingent and sliding-scale commissions. Prepare and post general ledger entries and complete balance sheet reconciliations for reinsurance-related accounts. Assist with Schedule F preparation and other statutory and management reporting requirements. Review reinsurance agreements to understand contractual terms and their impact on accounting and operational processes. Support internal and external discussions related to new and renewal reinsurance agreements. Execute established internal controls accurately and in accordance with company policies. Identify opportunities to improve reporting, processes, controls, and the use of financial data. Support additional reinsurance accounting projects and initiatives as needed. Core Competencies (skills, knowledge and abilities): Understanding of accounting principles and their application to insurance or reinsurance transactions. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to work accurately with detailed financial data. Strong Microsoft Excel capabilities, including experience with pivot tables and lookup functions such as XLOOKUP and VLOOKUP. Ability to interpret financial information and understand the accounting implications of contractual terms. High degree of attention to detail and commitment to financial accuracy. Intellectual curiosity and willingness to investigate discrepancies, ask questions, and develop a deeper understanding of underlying business activity. Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to work effectively with internal stakeholders and external reinsurance partners. Ability to organize competing priorities, meet deadlines, and work independently in a remote environment. Required Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field. 2+ years of relevant professional experience in accounting, finance, insurance, reinsurance, or insurance brokerage. Experience with general ledger accounting, account reconciliations, financial reporting, or related accounting processes. Proficiency with Microsoft Excel, including pivot tables and lookup functions. Preferred Qualifications Property & Casualty insurance accounting experience. Reinsurance accounting or reinsurance operations experience. Experience supporting program insurance or program accounting. Familiarity with ceded reinsurance structures, including quota share and excess-of-loss reinsurance. Experience with reinsurance cessions, settlements, recoverables, collateral, or broker relationships. Experience preparing or supporting Schedule F or other statutory insurance reporting. Experience interpreting reinsurance agreements or applying contractual provisions to accounting processes.
