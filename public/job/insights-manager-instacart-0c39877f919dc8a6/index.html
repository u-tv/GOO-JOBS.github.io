Insights Manager

🏢 instacart 📍 United States - Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

We're transforming the grocery industry At Instacart, we invite the world to share love through food because we believe everyone should have access to the food they love and more time to enjoy it together. Where others see a simple need for grocery delivery, we see exciting complexity and endless opportunity to serve the varied needs of our community. We work to deliver an essential service that customers rely on to get their groceries and household goods, while also offering safe and flexible earnings opportunities to Instacart Personal Shoppers. Instacart has become a lifeline for millions of people, and we’re building the team to help push our shopping cart forward. If you’re ready to do the best work of your life, come join our table. Instacart is a Flex First team There’s no one-size fits all approach to how we do our best work. Our employees have the flexibility to choose where they do their best work—whether it’s from home, an office, or your favorite coffee shop—while staying connected and building community through regular in-person events. Learn more about our flexible approach to where we work. Why this role is on the menu Instacart's Brand Insights team sits at the intersection of our retail data, our most important CPG partnerships, and category-level consumer trends. We are the engine behind the sales motion, and as our partnerships grow, so does the demand for insight that helps brands win on Instacart. We're hiring an Insights Manager to turn that data into clear, actionable recommendations that help partners grow, help our sales team drive more advertising investment, and make Instacart a trusted advisor on how to win in ecommerce. You'll work on a team where no two days look alike, and where building something new from scratch is part of the job. What you'll cook up in your first year Clients, internal and external, rate your work as high quality and come to you as their trusted source for ecommerce insight. You ramp quickly: you're in external meetings within your first 30 days and operating with growing independence by 180 days. Your scorecards, dashboards, and insight narratives give sales and account teams a story that helps partners grow and drives advertising investment. You build strong working relationships across sales, account management, measurement science, and our brand partners. You spot gaps, bring proactive ideas, and build tools or automation that make the team more efficient and benefit clients beyond your own accounts. The secret ingredients we're looking for Must-have pantry staples (Minimum Qualifications) 3+ years of hands-on experience producing data and insights, ideally at a CPG company or retailer Strong analytical skills, with the ability to draw conclusions from large amounts of data and summarize them concisely Ability to communicate and present findings confidently to senior executives Business acumen and the ability to craft a compelling storyline that goes beyond the data A proactive, go-getter mindset and the ability to work independently Proficiency with Excel or Google Suite and dashboarding tools such as Tableau or Mode Optional garnishes (Preferred Qualifications) SQL experience Ecommerce experience Experience at a data consultancy or with third-party data providers such as Nielsen, Numerator, or dunnhumby A track record of building strong relationships with clients and cross-functional partners Located in or near New York City, Chicago, or Toronto #LI-Remote Instacart provides highly market-competitive compensation and benefits in each location where our employees work.
