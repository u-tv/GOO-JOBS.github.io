About this Position We are looking for a mid-level Software Engineer II to design, build, and scale our AI powered product features with agentic experiences using AI agents and multi-agent workflows. Operating at the crossroads of Agentic AI and full-stack product engineering, you will harness the latest foundation or purpose-fit models and modern agent frameworks to resolve intricate business challenges. What you will do: Design, build, and operate production AI features across the stack (APIs, services, and product UI) using modern cloud architectures Design and implement agentic systems: tool-calling agents, multi-step workflows, and multi-agent orchestration patterns with guardrails, authz, rate limits, retries, fallbacks, and safe human-in-the-loop paths Own quality loops: offline/online evals, benchmarks, regression sets, prompt/version management, A/B or canary rollouts, and continuous improvement of evals, accuracy, latency, and cost Instrument AI systems for production: tracing, token/cost metrics, failure taxonomy, grounding/hallucination signals, and operational runbooks Collaborate with Product and UX to translate ambiguous use cases into shippable, measurable outcomes Contribute to shared AI platform patterns (agent frameworks, RAG/retrieval, memory, tool registries, evaluation harnesses) and evolve the SDLC for AI delivery What we're looking for: 3+ years building Agentic / SaaS / enterprise software in distributed teams Strong full-stack foundations: Python, TypeScript, React, Node.js, APIs, and modern databases Hands-on experience with AWS Bedrock, AgentCore, Strands Agents SDK (or comparable frameworks like LangGraph, CrewAI, or Claude Agent SDK), including function/tool calling, structured outputs, and streaming responses. Deep practical understanding of multi-agent orchestration, context management, and API integrations across models. Hands-on experience shipping, scaling and improving AI applications in production (LLM apps, RAG, copilots, or agent workflows) Working knowledge of agent design: tools/function calling, state/memory, orchestration graphs, and failure handling Familiarity with cloud architectures (AWS preferred) and shipping services with CI/CD, monitoring, and secure defaults Startup / high-growth experience desirable Our ranges are established using objective market benchmarking data for this level of work.
