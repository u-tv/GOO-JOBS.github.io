Job Description

About the Role We are seeking a Staff Enterprise Architect, Data to lead the strategy, design, and modernization of our enterprise data landscape. This role operates at the intersection of data architecture, engineering, and AI enablement, defining solutions to integrate our Data Lake and Data Warehouse across multi-cloud platforms. Over the next 12-18 months, you will enable self-service data access and natural language query capabilities for business users. You will architect Master Data Management and data lineage frameworks ensuring AI models operate on high-quality, governed data. You will also evaluate and implement AI-powered tools to automate data quality monitoring and enhance data security. We're looking to speak with candidates based in the San Francisco Bay Area for our hybrid working model. Define POC criteria and scoring models. Update quarterly or as needed.
