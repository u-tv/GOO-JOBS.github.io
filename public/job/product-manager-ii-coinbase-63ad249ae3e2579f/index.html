Job Description

Ready to do the most impactful work of your career? Coinbase is building the future of finance for people, institutions and their agents, with one place to trade crypto, equities, derivatives and prediction markets, use stablecoins, and connect AI agents with clear controls. The bar is high and the pace is intense, and we like it that way. This isn’t a place for complacency. It’s a place to be pushed past your perceived limits and do the best work of your career. If you’re ready to build the future of finance with a championship team who refuse to settle for “good enough,” you belong here. Coinbase is a remote-first, but not remote-only company. Expect to get together quarterly for in-person working sessions Team/ Role Paragraph: As a Product Manager II on the Prime Onchain team within the Institutional group, you'll define the next generation of onchain yield products for institutional clients. This team builds the infrastructure that enables asset managers, financial institutions, and crypto-native funds to earn onchain rewards and access DeFi yield through Coinbase Prime. You'll own product strategy across curated DeFi vaults, staking, and institutional reporting, working at the intersection of client experience, DeFi protocol dynamics, and enterprise-grade infrastructure with direct exposure to our largest clients. What you'll do (ie. job duties): Own the end-to-end product strategy and roadmap for institutional onchain yield, including curated DeFi vaults, staking, and reporting tailored for institutional clients Drive cross-functional delivery of compliant, scalable, and performant onchain yield and staking solutions across multiple chains and DeFi protocols Engage directly with institutional clients, including asset managers, financial institutions, and crypto-native funds, to gather feedback, validate product direction, and ensure the highest bar for security and operational excellence Lead prioritization of product investments by translating client needs, protocol risks, and market dynamics into a clear, defensible roadmap Partner with engineering, design, protocol specialists, sales, and legal teams to ship products that meet qualified custody, reporting, and regulatory standards Required skills and experience: 5+ years of product management experience with a track record of shipping enterprise or B2B crypto products from concept through scale Deep understanding of DeFi and onchain yield mechanics, including lending, vaults, LSTs, staking, and the protocols and risks behind them Demonstrated ability to engage directly with institutional clients, translate their operational pain points into product requirements, and close the loop with measurable product improvements Prior experience navigating reporting and regulatory considerations for onchain products, including qualified custody and compliance standards Familiarity with staking ecosystems such as Ethereum and Solana, and hands-on experience interacting with DeFi protocols Utilizes generative AI responsibly, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality.
