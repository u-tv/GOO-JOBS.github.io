Webflow: Associate General Counsel, Corporate

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: U.S. Remote Webflow is the agentic web marketing platform for modern marketing teams, helping organizations build, manage, and optimize high-performing web experiences that drive predictable growth and strengthen brand trust. Building at Webflow will need grit, because we move fast, without ever sacrificing craft or quality. We’re looking for an Associate General Counsel, Corporate to spearhead Webflow's corporate legal function, with an emphasis on corporate governance, public company readiness, and strategic transactions. You’ll play a critical role supporting Webflow’s continued high-rate growth and maturation, balancing legal compliance with a calibrated business mindset. About the role: Location: Remote-first (United States) Full-time Permanent Exempt The cash compensation for this role is tailored to align with the cost of labor in different geographic markets. We've structured the base pay ranges for this role into zones for our geographic markets, and the specific base pay within the range will be determined by the candidate’s geographic location, job-related experience, knowledge, qualifications, and skills. United States (all figures cited below are in USD and pertain to workers in the United States) Zone A: $240,000 - $300,000 Zone B: $225,000 - $282,000 Zone C: $211,000 - $264,000 This role is also eligible to participate in Webflow's company-wide bonus program. Target amounts are a percentage of base salary and vary by career level. Payouts are based on company performance against established financial and operational goals. Please visit our Careers page for more information on which locations are included in each of our geographic pay zones. However, please confirm the zone for your specific location with your recruiter Application Information: Application deadline: applications accepted on an ongoing basis until position is closed and filled This posting is for an existing vacancy Reporting to the Chief Legal Officer As the Associate General Counsel, Corporate you’ll be responsible for: Board Administration: Manage preparation for board and committee meetings, prepare materials, maintain corporate records and minutes, advise on governance practices Entity Management: Oversee corporate structure, subsidiary governance, and ensure compliance with regulatory requirements across all jurisdictions M&A Support: Lead legal due diligence, negotiate transaction documents, and manage post-acquisition integration from a legal perspective Corporate Transactions: Negotiate and draft complex commercial agreements, financing documents, and other strategic transactions Public Company Readiness: Drive Webflow’s public company readiness, including due diligence coordination and working with external counsel. Employment and Litigation: Lead our internal employment function, as well as our litigation efforts as they may arise Securities and SOX Compliance: Develop and implement securities law compliance program and insider trader policies. Develop and implement SOX compliance framework, if and when applicable, including internal controls, disclosure procedures, and certification processes Leadership: Build and lead Webflow’s corporate legal team, manage outside counsel relationships, and optimize and create scalable corporate legal processes and operations About you: Requirements: JD degree with at least one active bar membership 10+ years of relevant experience, with experience at a nationally-recognized law firm (required), and ideally a mixture of late stage private and public company experience Experience with securities laws, corporate governance, M&A transactions, SEC reporting requirements, and SOX requirements Experience with driving public company readiness, or managing public company compliance You’ll thrive as an Associate General Counsel, Corporate if you: Possess excellent leadership, communication, and project management skills Operate with a strategic, pragmatic mindset with ability to balance legal risk management with business objectives Have experience in technology or SaaS industry (preferred) Are a creative thinker, collaborative, and excited to contribute to a lean but growing Legal team on a variety of projects which may fall outside of your core expertise Have a track record of forming and maintaining strong cross-functional relationships Are accountable, adaptable and an effective multi-tasker. You are able to manage multiple, sometimes competing, demands for your time, and you manage expectations and anticipate needs Stay curious and open to growth — actively building fluency in emerging technologies like AI to unlock creativity, accelerate progress, and amplify impact Our Core Behaviors: Build lasting customer trust. We build trust by taking action that puts customer trust first. Win together. We play to win, and we win as one team. Success at Webflow isn't a solo act. Reinvent ourselves. We don't just improve what exists, we imagine what's possible. Deliver with speed, quality, and craft. We move fast because the moment demands it, and we do so without lowering the bar.
