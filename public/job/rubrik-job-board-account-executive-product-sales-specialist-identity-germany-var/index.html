Rubrik Job Board: Account Executive, Product Sales Specialist (Identity) - Germany

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Germany, Remote About the Team & Role Identity is now the front door for attackers, and organisations across government, financial services, healthcare and critical infrastructure rely on Rubrik to recover quickly when identity systems like Active Directory, Entra ID, Okta and Ping Identity are compromised. As an Account Executive, Identity Resilience in Germany, you'll take a category-defining product to customers in some of the most regulated markets in the world, building a new revenue stream alongside the Identity Sales Specialist team and shaping how Rubrik wins in identity from the earliest stage of the motion. What You'll Do Own and exceed your identity resilience quota across Germany, turning identity into a core part of every strategic security conversation with your customers. Build and execute territory plans for Identity Resilience, prioritising the accounts and verticals that feel identity risk most acutely, so Rubrik is in front of the right buyers at the right moment. Lead executive conversations with CISOs, CIOs and identity and infrastructure leaders, creating urgency around Active Directory and Entra ID recovery and closing complex, multi-stakeholder deals. Generate and develop pipeline through outbound prospecting, existing Rubrik relationships and joint account planning with Account Executives and Sales Engineers, so identity becomes part of the conversation from the first meeting. Work with channel partners, GSIs and specialist resellers to build joint plays that extend Rubrik's reach beyond what the direct motion can achieve alone. Partner with Sales Engineers to run discovery, demonstrations and proofs of value that translate technical identity attack paths into clear business risk and measurable resilience. Maintain accurate forecasting and disciplined pipeline hygiene, and share what you learn from the field with Product and Marketing so competitive insight and regional requirements shape the roadmap and messaging. Share your identity expertise with colleagues across the field and help enable the wider sales team to lead with identity, growing your own influence as the motion scales. Experience You'll Need Substantial experience selling identity security, IAM, privileged access or adjacent cybersecurity solutions into enterprise and public sector customers, with a track record of consistently meeting or exceeding quota. Demonstrated success leading complex, executive-level sales cycles with CISOs and senior security and infrastructure stakeholders. Strong understanding of the identity threat landscape, including Active Directory and Entra ID attack paths, and the ability to translate technical risk into business impact. Experience building pipeline in a new or emerging product category, with the self-direction to create momentum where no playbook yet exists. Experience working with partners, resellers or GSIs to influence and co-sell into target accounts. Comfort operating within the regulatory environment and buying culture of the German market. Language: professional fluency in German and English, written and spoken. Education: bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience. Willingness to travel regularly for customer, partner and field engagements. Preferred Qualifications Experience selling in the cyber resilience, data security or identity recovery space, including familiarity with identity security and AD recovery vendors. Established relationships with GSIs, identity-focused partners and security-focused resellers in Germany and the wider DACH region. Experience with channel-led and partner-influenced selling models. #LI-remote Join Us in Securing and Accelerating the World's AI Transformation Rubrik (NYSE: RBRK) is the Security and AI Operations Company, delivering one platform built at the intersection of data, identity, and AI. The platform enables agentic cyber resilience and trusted AI operations for enterprises worldwide, built to recover before disruption strikes and stay a step ahead, so security is never the reason ambition stalls.
