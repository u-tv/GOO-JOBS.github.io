Senior Director, Growth Marketing

🏢 datadog 📍 New York, New York, USA; San Francisco, California, USA 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Datadog is seeking a strategic, visionary, and results-driven Senior Director, Growth Marketing to lead customer acquisition and conversion across the full product-led growth funnel - from initial discovery and website engagement through free trial, activation, and conversion to a paying customer. This leader will define the vision, strategy, and operating model for Datadog’s growth marketing programs, spanning organic search (SEO), AI and LLM discoverability (AEO/GEO), trial experience optimization, and lifecycle marketing. They will establish investment priorities, identify new growth opportunities, and drive measurable improvements in customer acquisition, engagement, and conversion. You will shape Datadog’s long-term organic growth strategy, build a culture of data-driven experimentation, and translate insights into scalable programs that accelerate customer acquisition and revenue growth. At Datadog, we place value in our office culture - the relationships and collaboration it builds and the creativity it brings to the table. We operate as a hybrid workplace to ensure our Datadogs can create a work-life harmony that best fits them.
