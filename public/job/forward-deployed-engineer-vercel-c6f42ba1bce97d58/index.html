Forward-Deployed Engineer

🏢 vercel 📍 Hybrid - San Francisco, New York City, Austin 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Vercel: Vercel is the agentic infrastructure company, freeing people and agents to ship what's next. For more than a decade we've helped builders move from idea to production with speed, security, and exceptional developer experience. Now we're scaling our products for both agents and people to ship and run software, built in the open and trusted by OpenAI, PayPal, Ramp, Supreme, and millions of developers worldwide. About the role As a Forward Deployed Engineer on the Professional Services team, you’ll work hands-on-keyboard inside strategic enterprise customers, modernizing legacy frontends to Next.js and building production AI agents. You’ll own the work from technical discovery to shipped TypeScript, whether that’s moving a React app to App Router, auditing a codebase for performance, or deploying agents with Vercel’s eve framework and AI SDK. The role has two profiles: Next.js experts focus on code optimization, performance audits, and SEO and AEO (answer engine optimization), while AI builders scope, design, and ship agents that prove their value. This is a hybrid role with 3 in-office days per week in San Francisco, New York City, or Austin. What you’ll do Build production AI agents, MCP servers, and agentic workflows with Vercel’s eve framework, AI SDK, and AI Cloud Lead migrations from legacy React, Vue, and other frameworks to Next.js, including Pages Router to App Router, and automate them where possible Audit customer codebases for performance bottlenecks and anti-patterns, and deliver roadmaps with measurable improvements Architect Next.js applications with App Router, Server Components, streaming, and ISR for enterprise scale Run workshops and pair programming so customer teams can maintain what you ship What you need 6+ years of software engineering experience, including 2+ years in a customer-facing technical role (consulting, solutions architecture, forward deployed engineering, or technical founder) Built and deployed web applications, backend systems, or agentic workflows using AI tooling such as harnesses, skills, and MCP servers Expert-level TypeScript and React experience shipping production applications Next.js experts: Led a legacy frontend migration to Next.js, or shipped a production Next.js application using App Router and Server Components AI builders: Built production LLM agents, including sandbox and filesystem agent patterns and human-in-the-loop flows Ran technical discovery and presented to executives at enterprise customers, with occasional travel to customer sites Bonus if you Led an enterprise AI initiative that shipped production agents replacing manual processes or legacy systems Diagnosed and fixed LCP, CLS, and INP issues at scale Contributed to Next.js, React, or AI SDK open source Benefits: Competitive compensation package, including equity. Inclusive Healthcare Package. Learn and Grow - we provide mentorship and send you to events that help you build your network and skills. Flexible Time Off.
