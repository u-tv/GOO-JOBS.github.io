About Frontline Wildfire Defense Frontline Wildfire Defense is the fastest-growing company in the wildfire protection industry, serving as the premier manufacturer and installer of exterior wildfire defense systems for residential and commercial structures. Operating across 12 Western states, our organization is dedicated to safeguarding residential properties, commercial sites, communities, and critical infrastructure from the destructive impact of wildfires. As wildfires rapidly intensify across the West, they threaten communities and destabilize property insurance markets—creating a vital need and a powerful market opportunity for proven structural defense solutions. Beyond our core technology, Frontline is strategically positioning itself to capture high-growth adjacent opportunities, including strategic insurance partnerships and municipal fire service integration. Our proprietary defense platform integrates real-time wildfire tracking and data fusion with an advanced hydration system designed to increase moisture levels on and surrounding structures to prevent ignition. The efficacy of this technology is well-documented: during the 2025 Los Angeles fire events, Frontline successfully defended 59 of 61 equipped properties, representing a 96% success rate. This performance has earned notable industry honors, including recognition as one of TIME Magazine’s Best Inventions, the National Association of Homebuilders IBS Best Home Technology of 2025, and recognition in Inc. Magazine’s ranking of the fastest-growing enterprises in the Pacific Region. Frontline was founded by Harry Statter and is headquartered in Jackson Hole, Wyoming. The company is backed by Norwest Venture Partners. Frontline maintains a modern, flexible corporate culture supported by a predominantly distributed workforce. We welcome inquiries from qualified professionals seeking to contribute to a high-growth enterprise dedicated to preserving lives and property. About This Opportunity Frontline is seeking a strategic, operationally minded Chief Financial Officer to lead our finance function and help drive the company’s next phase of growth. Frontline is scaling quickly, and wildfire risk is reshaping the availability and affordability of property insurance across the West. This creates a large market for effective structure defense, along with strategic opportunities in the insurance sector, recurring-revenue services, and fire department and public-sector partnerships. As a member of the executive team, you will partner closely with the CEO, COO, leadership team, and Board to turn strategic priorities into clear plans and measurable results. You will bring financial rigor, operational discipline, and a commercial mindset to company-wide decisions, and you will help identify, evaluate, and execute new growth opportunities, including strategic insurance partnerships and financial offerings. This role reports directly to the CEO and leads the existing finance organization, as well as Frontline’s legal function. We’re looking for a finance leader who has helped a company through this kind of growth before, and who can build the foundation for our next stage, including a potential IPO and/or other strategic growth opportunities. Key Responsibilities Financial Leadership & Planning Lead financial planning, forecasting, budgeting, cash management, and reporting. Give the CEO, COO, and leadership team clear analysis of business performance, risks, investment needs, and the financial impact of key decisions. Establish financial models, performance KPIs, and cross-functional processes that support disciplined growth. Develop the processes, systems, and leadership capacity needed to support growth while keeping the organization practical and focused. Strategy, Growth & Corporate Development Partner with the CEO, COO, and leadership team to identify, evaluate, and prioritize strategic growth opportunities. Help sequence strategic initiatives, set the operating plan for each, connect them to commercial and operating plans, and hold teams accountable to outcomes and ROI. Develop the economics, value proposition, and execution plan for new initiatives such as strategic insurance opportunities and other expansion areas. Lead corporate development, including partnerships, M&A screening, and strategic investments. Support insurance and mortgage-market partnerships, including conversations with insurance carriers. Support recurring-revenue strategy, including pricing, packaging, service contracts, and monitoring. Lead public-private partnerships and the company’s non-profit affiliates. Lead select initiatives from evaluation through completion, aligning internal teams and external partners around milestones, ownership, and measures of success, then assess results and refine or scale them. Capital, Board & Investor Relations Prepare the company for future capital events, including M&A, debt financing and credit facilities, and potential IPO or other strategic equity opportunities. Prepare and present financial and strategic materials to the Board, investors, and other external stakeholders. Build relationships with external partners, lenders, and stakeholders, representing Frontline with sound judgment and commercial credibility. Legal, Risk & Compliance Lead the legal function, overseeing state operating licensure and tax, intellectual property, vendor and customer contracts, recurring-revenue contract structures, and lender relationships. Team Leadership Build and lead the finance organization, including the existing VP of Finance and accounting team, and develop a high-performing team as the company scales. Qualifications Senior finance leadership experience as a CFO or comparable executive position. Experience leading financial planning, analysis, and company-level decision support, including resource allocation that balances growth investment with financial discipline. Experience shaping commercial or strategic initiatives from evaluation through implementation, and turning broad priorities into business cases, operating plans, milestones, and clear ownership. Experience leading work beyond the finance function and partnering closely with a CEO, COO, and executive team on company priorities. Demonstrated ability to engage effectively with a Board and investors. Experience building and developing a high-performing finance team. A pragmatic, low-ego leadership style: willing to push back on assumptions and strategy, comfortable working hands-on as a builder, and decisive in a lean, high-growth environment where priorities can shift quickly. Experience in growing a profitable hardware and software business or a company with complex operating economics. Experience in insurance, construction, home services, property technology, climate resilience, data, or a related field. Direct experience taking a company public or leading IPO readiness, including audit readiness, public-company reporting, and internal controls. Experience evaluating and structuring new commercial models, including strategic partnerships, new products and channels, pricing, and recurring revenue or lease-based models, ideally including a shift from one-time to recurring revenue. Experience with debt financing (including credit facilities), investor relations, and/or M&A transactions. Experience with consumer financing or asset-backed financing, and/or consumer-finance regulations and compliance. Experience with project-based accounting. Experience implementing and optimizing financial systems and using data and technology to improve reporting and decision-making. Experience in a private equity or venture-backed business.
