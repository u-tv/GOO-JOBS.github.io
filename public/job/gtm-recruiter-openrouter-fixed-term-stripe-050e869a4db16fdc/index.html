GTM Recruiter, OpenRouter (Fixed Term)

🏢 stripe 📍 SF, NYC, CHI, US-Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies - from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups - use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career. To help support that mission, in August we announced that Stripe had agreed to acquire OpenRouter, a leading AI model gateway and routing platform. While that acquisition is under regulatory review and has not yet closed, our intent is that this role will support the OpenRouter team following the closing of the acquisition. About the team OpenRouter helps developers and businesses access and use a broad range of AI models through a unified platform. This role sits at the intersection of OpenRouter and Stripe, supporting hiring across Tech, GTM, and Core teams. You’ll serve as a bridge between the 2 organizations, bringing Stripe’s recruiting standards, talent networks, and resources to OpenRouter while protecting its speed and minimizing operational overhead. You’ll align to Tech, GTM, or Core, but you won’t be dedicated to a single job profile within that function. For example, a GTM Recruiter may support Sales, Solutions Architecture, Marketing, or other GTM roles as priorities shift. The same model applies across Tech and Core. What you’ll do Own full-cycle recruiting for your aligned function, moving across job profiles based on business priority, from intake and market mapping through offer acceptance and onboarding handoff. You’ll combine direct execution with consultative partnership, working closely with hiring managers to clarify needs, advise on talent markets, build and engage qualified pipelines, and move candidates through structured, timely processes. You’ll operate with startup speed while leveraging Stripe’s infrastructure and reach. You’ll use metrics to identify risk and guide action, while keeping recruiting outcomes, not reporting or process for its own sake, at the center of the work. Responsibilities Own full-cycle recruiting across the full range of roles within your aligned function, Tech, GTM, or Core, moving between job profiles as business priorities shift Build qualified and inclusive pipelines through hands-on sourcing across communities, referrals, events, market mapping, relationship-based outreach, and other creative channels beyond inbound applications and standard LinkedIn outreach Partner consultatively with hiring managers to translate business priorities into clear role requirements, search strategies, structured assessments, and timely decisions Use funnel metrics, market evidence, and candidate feedback to identify risks, recommend actions, and communicate progress without allowing reporting or process to substitute for recruiting Deliver a thoughtful candidate experience and maintain relationships with prospective candidates for immediate and future needs Serve as an operational bridge between OpenRouter and Stripe by absorbing coordination where possible, maintaining continuity, and reducing the time OpenRouter leaders spend navigating recruiting processes Flex across job profiles within your aligned function when priorities shift or additional coverage is needed Who you are We're looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement. Minimum requirements 5+ years of full-cycle recruiting experience, including direct ownership of sourcing, assessment, stakeholder partnership, and closing Experience recruiting in a high-growth technology startup, preferably during a stage below 200 employees Demonstrated recruiting depth in at least 1 of the following areas: Tech, GTM, or Core, with experience recruiting across multiple job profiles or clear evidence you can move between profiles as business priorities shift. Demonstrated interest in and ability to personally conduct hands-on sourcing and build qualified pipelines through creative channels beyond inbound applications and standard LinkedIn outreach Experience partnering consultatively with hiring managers, managing multiple searches, and using recruiting metrics and market evidence to identify risk and guide action Ability to work through ambiguity, adapt quickly as priorities change, and balance direct execution with longer-term pipeline building Preferred qualifications Experience recruiting across both startup and larger-company environments and helping teams work effectively across different operating models Experience supporting structured, competency-based hiring processes Experience recruiting across multiple geographies or advising on location strategy Practical experience using recruiting technology, talent intelligence, or AI tools to improve sourcing and candidate experience
