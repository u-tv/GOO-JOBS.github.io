Edgewater Federal Solutions, Inc.: Full-Stack Platform Engineer

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: United States URL: https://www.edgewaterit.com Overview Edgewater Federal Solutions is seeking an experienced Full-Stack Platform Engineer . The Full-Stack Platform Engineer provides senior technical leadership and hands-on engineering for a secure enterprise data analytics platform supporting a federal environment. The role designs and delivers scalable web applications, APIs, data integrations, cloud solutions, and secure development practices while guiding technical decisions and resolving complex platform issues. *Due to the nature of the work and contract - US Citizenship is required. *This is a remote role. Responsibilities Responsibilities include, but are not limited to the following: Lead architecture, design, and development of full-stack solutions using React, Node.js/Express, Python (Flask/FastAPI), REST APIs, and advanced SQL. Design and support cloud-based data integrations, ETL/ELT pipelines, Databricks or equivalent analytics services, and data quality capabilities. Establish secure engineering and DevSecOps practices, including CI/CD, automated security testing, containerization, infrastructure as code, and code reviews. Integrate AI/ML capabilities, including LLM APIs, RAG patterns, prompt engineering, and secure handling of sensitive data. Architect solutions within AWS using appropriate IAM, networking, secrets management, encryption, logging, and security controls. Provide technical leadership, mentor engineers, support production troubleshooting, and communicate technical recommendations to stakeholders. Qualifications US Citizenship Required Must be able to obtain and maintain a Public Trust Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Systems, or a related technical field. 10+ years of software/platform engineering experience, including senior technical leadership or architecture responsibilities. Strong experience with React, Python and/or Node.js, REST APIs, advanced SQL, AWS, and modern data platforms. Hands-on experience with CI/CD, Docker/Kubernetes or ECS, Terraform, Git, and automated security tooling. Knowledge of application security, IAM/RBAC, OWASP practices, observability, and secure cloud architecture. Experience supporting federal or regulated environments and familiarity with FISMA, NIST 800-53, FedRAMP, and ATO processes. Preferred certifications include AWS, CISSP/Security+, Databricks, CKA/CKS, or comparable technical credentials. Physical Demands: The physical demands described here are representative of those that must be met by an employee to successfully perform the essential functions of this job. Specific vision abilities required by this job include close vision requirements due to computer work. The employee must occasionally lift and/or move up to fifteen (15) pounds. Fine hand manipulation (keyboarding).
