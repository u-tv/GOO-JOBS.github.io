Antora Energy: Senior Power Procurement Manager

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: United States URL: https://www.antora.com Antora Energy delivers affordable, reliable energy to industry, data centers, and the grid. Our thermal batteries turn low-cost electricity into always-on heat and power, without supply-constrained critical minerals or multi-year construction timelines. We are growing our company with people who put team and mission first, value connection through laughter and joy, and work with humility and openness. We are committed to building a diverse, passionate, and creative team dedicated to a future powered by abundant, clean, low-cost energy. Position Summary The Senior Power Procurement Manager will lead utility tariff and power purchase negotiations from first engagement through execution, owning a portfolio of medium- to high-complexity deals across one or more ISOs/RTOs. This is a hands-on individual contributor role with no direct reports: the person will draft and negotiate term sheets and definitive agreements, build and pressure-test deal models, coordinate cross-functional inputs, and serve as the day-to-day point of contact with utility counterparts. They will lead complex, multi-site negotiations, pilot new commercial constructs, and are expected to become Antora's recognized expert for a region. Roles & Responsibilities Deal Execution & Negotiation Lead day-to-day negotiations and the overall close plan for utility tariffs, energy service agreements, and PPAs. Draft and negotiate term sheets; lead definitive agreement negotiations alongside counsel; negotiate standard tariff rate changes per playbook. Support capacity and power sales origination, including preparing and negotiating MOUs, term sheets, and off-take agreements with utilities, hyperscalers, and other large-load customers. Help shape deal structures that fit Antora's unique load profile. Coordinate Finance, Legal, Policy, Business Development, and Engineering inputs; run cross-functional working sessions to align positions, timelines, and technical constraints. Own financial model architecture for deals: select modeling approaches, set key assumptions, run sensitivities and stress tests, validate inputs, and QA models built by others. Utility & Counterparty Relationships Serve as the primary point of contact with utility managers and directors. Build deep regional knowledge of priority ISOs/RTOs, utilities, and tariff landscapes, growing into a go-to resource for at least one region. Partner with Business Development to qualify and advance opportunities, ensuring smooth handoffs from early engagement to active negotiation. Regulatory Support & Team Contribution Support regulatory work by drafting comments, exhibits, and data responses; may represent Antora in staff workshops and by testifying in regulatory forums. Surface patterns across deals (common counterparty objections, emerging tariff structures, regulatory shifts) and propose playbook language updates for senior review. Informally mentor junior team members on models, redlines, and negotiation prep. Key Qualifications 6–12+ years of experience in power procurement, energy origination, utility negotiations, or energy commercial roles. Demonstrated deal track record of leading successful Power Procurement Agreement or Electric Service Agreement negotiations. Solid working knowledge of utility tariff design, wholesale power markets, and the commercial mechanics of how large loads contract for energy and grid services, with depth in at least one region or ISO. Hands-on experience drafting and negotiating term sheets and definitive agreements alongside counsel. Strong financial modeling skills, ability to build, stress-test, and QA deal models and translate results into negotiation positions. Sound commercial judgment within guardrails: knowing which terms to hold, which to trade, and when a deviation needs to be escalated. Clear written and verbal communication; able to write decision memos and present recommendations to internal leadership with minimal editing. Additional Qualifications Desired Experience with energy storage, thermal storage, or flexible load commercial structures. Exposure to capacity sales or hyperscaler energy procurement. Experience in high-growth or start-up environments where commercial processes are still being built. Working relationships with utility procurement/rates teams and ISO/RTO stakeholders in MISO, SPP, ERCOT, or PJM. Experience supporting regulatory proceedings (comments, data responses, staff workshops). The actual salary offered will be within the posted range and determined based on several factors including but not limited to a candidate's experiences, credentials and expertise, as they pertain to the position's requirements.
