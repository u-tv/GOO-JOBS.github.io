To accomplish this, you will: Manage queue distribution in our chat support system and workload across the US support team to maintain target response times and SLAs. We're aiming for a first response time of Coach and develop team members through regular 1:1s, performance reviews, and real-time feedback. Handle technical troubleshooting and empower the team to resolve issues that require strong product and systems knowledge, as well as managing customer escalations and high-priority issues.
