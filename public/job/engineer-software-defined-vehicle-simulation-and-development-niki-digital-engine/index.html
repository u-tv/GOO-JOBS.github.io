Engineer Software Defined Vehicle Simulation and Development

🏢 NIKI Digital Engineering 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

NIKI is looking for graduates of Technical University Departments/Schools of Sciences/Engineers, or similar disciplines to support and develop complex system simulation processes of software-defined vehicles. Become a member of NIKI Digital Engineering! If you have completed your studies or doctorate and are looking for new challenges, interdisciplinary and diverse teams, and projects, send us your resume. Our strategy is to transfer the know-how into practice. TWT is our trusted long-term partner, sharing and supporting our vision. Through close, daily collaboration, we design and deliver innovative solutions for clients and partners across the energy, health, and automotive industries. Combining interdisciplinary expertise in IT, digital engineering, and consulting, we turn ideas into impactful results that reflect our shared vision for progress. What to expect Develop and optimize process and simulation methods for complex vehicle development in collaboration with international industry partners and research organizations. Continuously monitor industry trends and advances in simulation technology to improve NIKI's services and drive innovation. Coordinate our interdisciplinary projects in close cooperation with our customers. Task Description Modeling and simulation of complex systems and software functions. Focus on areas such as driving dynamics and autonomous driving. Active cooperation with our customers. Programming of functions and algorithms for modern vehicle functions. Accompanying the process from the first idea to delivery. Forward-looking development processes. Functional and vehicle development using the latest methods and tools, such as graphic modeling languages. Empowering and coaching our clients. Protection of software functions. Efficient implementation of technical security measures and criticality analysis of software functions. Creation of test concepts. Development of automated test cases according to existing customer requirements. Test execution on the test bench or in road tests hedging concepts. Development of new concepts for safeguarding networked systems (MIL/SIL/HIL) and also autonomous driving functions. Development, commissioning, and testing of complex, multifunctional test benches. Collaboration with cross-functional teams to ensure the timely delivery of high-quality technical solutions. Basic Qualifications Knowledge of modeling and numerical simulation of mechanical systems. Familiarization with the application of simulation or design software tools. Good knowledge of at least one of the languages: Python, Matlab or Java. Excellent communication skills. Self-motivated, with the ability to work independently and as part of a team. Strong problem-solving and analytical skills. Spirit of innovation, initiative, and enjoyment of learning and tinkering. Excellent command of English and technical terminology. Preferred Qualifications Experience in the automotive industry, with a focus on vehicle development and simulation. Initial knowledge of semi-formal modeling languages such as UML, SysML or Matlab/Simulink or initial experience in handling measurement technology or test benches, such as Vector, dSPACE, ETAS, or initial experience with control units and software in the embedded area. Interest in interdisciplinary collaborations. Good command of the German language. What we offer Structured learning on the job, professional feedback, and a mentor open to all your questions. Innovative, highly technically demanding industry and research projects, interdisciplinary teams, and highly qualified colleagues. Open doors, and the chance to take over responsibility quickly - participate, shape, and leave your fingerprint. Discover new talents and shape your career path: Become a specialist, project manager, or manager. Simultaneous, collaborative work using modern digital platforms. Workplace flexibility. Flexible working hours thanks to our attractive flextime model - only compatible projects. Enjoy working with great colleagues in your day-to-day work.
