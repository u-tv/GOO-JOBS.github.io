Senior Account Executive - ANZ

🏢 dropbox 📍 Remote - Australia: Select locations 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Role Description As an Senior Account Executive in A NZ , you will grow customer relationships and revenue with Dropbox Core as your primary product. You will be developing existing accounts, and also creating and closing new-business opportunities across your assigned territory. You will understand how customers work, identify where they can gain more value from Dropbox, and build a clear business case for expansion. You will also sell across the wider Dropbox portfolio when additional solutions address a genuine customer need. You will bring customer insight into account planning and prospecting, develop relationships across buying teams, and share what works with the wider AP J team. This is a hands-on Account Executive role within an assigned territory. You will own your opportunities, maintain a disciplined pipeline and forecast, and work with your manager and cross-functional colleagues to deliver revenue and customer outcomes. Responsibilities Own and execute territory and account plans to deliver revenue targets, growing existing customers into additional teams, buying centres and use cases, with Dropbox Core as the primary product. Generate new-business opportunities through targeted prospecting, account research and collaboration with partners and internal teams, taking responsibility for building a qualified pipeline. Lead complex sales cycles from discovery and qualification through negotiation and close, building clear business cases and securing alignment across senior decision-makers and buying teams. Identify and develop opportunities across the wider Dropbox portfolio, connecting each recommendation to a customer problem, measurable business value and a realistic adoption plan. Contribute to growth in the media sector by developing priority accounts, tailoring outreach to relevant workflows and sharing customer insight and successful approaches with the wider APJ team. Maintain accurate account plans, CRM records, pipeline and forecasts, identifying deal risks early and taking action to progress opportunities and close gaps against revenue targets. Coordinate Sales Engineering, Customer Success, Marketing and other relevant partners to progress deals and support customer outcomes, aligning account and renewal activity with the relevant owners. Requirements Typically 7+ years of B2B SaaS sales experience, or an equivalent track record, with individual quota ownership and evidence of delivering against revenue targets. Demonstrated success growing existing accounts through strategic account planning, developing senior stakeholder relationships and expanding into additional teams, buying centres and use cases. Proven ability to generate and close new-business opportunities, including building a qualified pipeline through your own targeted prospecting. Strong discovery, qualification and value-selling skills, with the ability to understand customer workflows, uncover business problems and build a clear, measurable business case. Experience independently leading complex sales cycles through negotiation and close, engaging senior decision-makers and aligning operational, technical and commercial stakeholders. Disciplined pipeline management, forecasting and CRM use, with sound commercial judgement about opportunity quality, deal risk and actions needed to progress a sale. Ability to develop and execute a territory plan, prioritise account expansion and new-business opportunities, and coordinate cross-functional teams to deliver revenue and customer outcomes. Preferred Qualifications Experience selling eSignature or digital agreement solutions, with an understanding of customer workflows, buying stakeholders and commercial priorities. Experience selling B2B SaaS solutions to customers in Australia and New Zealand. Success expanding existing customers into additional products, teams or use cases. Experience selling into media organisations, with insight into their content and collaboration workflows.
