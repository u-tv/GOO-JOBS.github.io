True Talent Solutions Partners: Software Engineer, AI Agents

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: United States URL: https://truetalentsolutionspartners.com About the Role Join a product engineering team building AI-powered tools for data and marketing workflows. You will help explore new product opportunities, shape the underlying AI architecture, and bring prototypes into production. What You'll Do Rapidly prototype ideas that apply AI to data and marketing workflows. Work with a small team to develop and ship product concepts, including foundational work for new product areas. Choose AI architectures and frameworks suited to each use case. Evaluate where different language models and AI methods work well within the product. Build production data agents that use company data to answer analytics and data science questions. Understand user workflows and create seamless AI-assisted experiences. What We're Looking For Five to twelve years of software engineering experience building core products, ideally in an AI or machine learning context. Strong backend architecture skills and product intuition, with experience taking ideas from prototype through production. Experience building complex AI applications and applying machine learning in product or engineering settings, beyond simple retrieval-augmented generation. Product engineering experience in a fast-moving startup or growth-stage environment, with evidence of increasing scope or responsibility. Experience contributing as an early engineering hire or founder is valuable. TypeScript experience is relevant; prior LLM experience is not required. Compensation & Benefits Base salary: $200,000 to $320,000 annually. Visa sponsorship is available. Location Remote within the United States.
