Senior Sales Compensation Manager

🏢 databricks 📍 Mountain View, California; San Francisco, California 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

(GAQ128R87) The Senior Sales Compensation Manager (L7) is the senior systems expert for Databricks' commissions platform. You will co-own how commissions get calculated: how the Xactly Incent plans are built, and the data integrations and pipelines that feed every calculation. You'll be the trusted co-owner of the system design, the technical roadmap, and the systems controls in a fast-growing, consumption-based SaaS business. You’ll take on ambiguous, cross-functional problems that span several systems. You'll influence senior leaders in Finance, GTM, and Engineering, and raise the bar for the team through documentation, enablement, and AI-first automation. The impact you will have Plan configuration and calculation engine Co-lead annual (and mid-year if applicable) plan builds in Xactly for every major plan family, including AE, sales leaders, xDR, field engineering, and partner plans. This covers rule logic, rate tables, accelerators, quotas, crediting hierarchies, and tuning the calculation engine. Partner with Plan Design to turn plan design decks into system specifications. Configure, run UAT on, and test new plans before they go to production. Co-own the system implementation timeline and the go-live schedule for the new fiscal year. Be the responder when calculations break, data sync fails, or configuration has defects. Integrations, data pipelines and system design Co-own the integration layer between Salesforce and Xactly and the data loads from the Databricks lakehouse into Xactly. This covers object mappings, the API integration user, OAuth and IP restriction upkeep, and the schedules and dependencies of the pipelines that feed plan crediting. Collaborate and influence the future-state design and technical roadmap for the commissions platform. That includes evaluating the commission engine strategy and moving manual files onto governed pipelines with change management and SOX-aligned logging. Collaborate and influence vendor escalations and support cases, and lead day-to-day work with the Xactly account team. Controls, close, and audit Co-own the commissions systems SOX controls: plan configuration and change management, system-to-system validation, plan reconciliation, quarterly user access reviews. Act as a subject-matter expert in walkthroughs with external auditors. Run the systems side of the monthly and quarterly close calendar with high precision. Make change requests to the change-approval board (CAB) audit-ready from the start, with full descriptions, approvals, and test evidence. AI, automation and insight Collaborate and influence AI adoption. Use it to turn plan design into configuration, automatically generate documentation, and validate data with AI agents. Use AI-driven anomaly detection to find payout discrepancies before they reach payroll. Use predictive modeling to estimate commissions impact of proposed plan changes/adjustments or for accrual purposes. Turn prototypes into tools the team actually uses, and track the time saved and quality gained. Cross-functional influence Represent Commissions Systems to Engineering, IT, Finance Data & Strategy, GTM Strategy & Ops, Accounting, FP&A, and Partner teams. Turn business requirements into engineering terms and the other way around, and agree on SLAs and intake processes. Be the voice telling downstream teams about system changes that affect their reporting and analysis. Leadership & culture Help make the team resilient by design. Turn undocumented rules and configuration knowledge into playbooks, wikis, and recorded walkthroughs so no one person is a single point of failure. Mentor and cross-train other team members, including offshore resources. Help build a culture of continuous learning and AI adoption. Turn concerns into recommendations with clear trade-offs that win senior leaders' support. What we look for Bachelor's degree in Finance, Business, Economics, Information Systems, or a related field. 12+ years in sales compensation, commissions systems, or sales/finance operations, including deep hands-on administration and configuration of an SPM platform. Expert skills in Xactly Incent and Connect. Experience with other SPM tools is a plus. Strong grasp of Salesforce data models and integrations, and of data pipeline concepts. SQL or lakehouse experience (e.g., Databricks) is strongly preferred. A track record of running SOX controls and ITGCs, and of leading audit walkthroughs. Fluency in SaaS go-to-market comp models: quotas, OTI, accelerators, overlays, SPIFFs, MBOs, and consumption-based crediting. A proven record of efficiency gains from automation and AI. You're comfortable prompting and evaluating LLMs, building practical tools, and judging where people still need to make the call. Experience in a fast-paced SaaS or tech company, plus strong communication and presentation skills for senior audiences.
