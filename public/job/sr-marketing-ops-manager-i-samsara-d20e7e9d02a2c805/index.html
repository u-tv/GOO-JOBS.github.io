Sr. Marketing Ops Manager I

🏢 samsara 📍 Remote - CA; Remote - Chicago; Remote - FL; Remote - Houston; Remote - PA 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are Samsara (NYSE: IOT) is the pioneer of the Connected Operations™ Cloud, which is a platform that enables organizations that depend on physical operations to harness Internet of Things (IoT) data to develop actionable insights and improve their operations. At Samsara, we are helping improve the safety, efficiency and sustainability of the physical operations that power our global economy. Representing more than 40% of global GDP, these industries are the infrastructure of our planet, including agriculture, construction, field services, transportation, and manufacturing — and we are excited to help digitally transform their operations at scale. Working at Samsara means you’ll help define the future of physical operations and be on a team that’s shaping an exciting array of product solutions, including Video-Based Safety, Vehicle Telematics, Apps and Driver Workflows, and Equipment Monitoring. As part of a recently public company, you’ll have the autonomy and support to make an impact as we build for the long term. About the role We're looking for a technically strong, forward-thinking specialist to help shape the next generation of Campaign Operations. This role focuses on innovation, AI-driven capabilities, and technical solutions that make our campaign operating model more intelligent, scalable, and efficient. You'll spend most of your time designing and building: prototyping AI agents, automations, and integrations, then turning the ones that work into reusable capabilities the whole team can adopt. You'll also bring enough Campaign Operations expertise to support campaign execution when needed. This is a remote position open to candidates residing in the US except the San Francisco Bay Metro Area, NYC Metro Area, and Washington, D.C. Metro Area. You should apply if: You want to impact the industries that run our world: Your efforts will result in real-world impact—helping to keep the lights on, get food into grocery stores, reduce emissions, and most importantly, ensure workers return home safely. You are the architect of your own career: If you put in the work, this role won't be your last at Samsara. We set up our employees for success and have built a culture that encourages rapid career development, countless opportunities to experiment and master your craft in a hyper growth environment. You're energized by our opportunity: The vision we have to digitize large sectors of the global economy requires your full focus and best efforts to bring forth creative, ambitious ideas for our customers. You want to be with the best: At Samsara, we win together, celebrate together and support each other. You will be surrounded by a high-calibre team that will encourage you to do your best. In this role, you will: Drive innovation: Explore emerging technologies, identify opportunities, and build next-generation Campaign Operations capabilities that go beyond traditional automation. Build AI and technical solutions: Design, prototype, and implement solutions using AI agents, automation, APIs, and scripting, taking scoped projects from concept through implementation. Own internal tools and agents: Maintain and improve the team's intake agent and other internal tools, including issue triage, stakeholder feedback, and a roadmap for what comes next. Optimize operations: Find opportunities to improve scalability, efficiency, quality, and reliability across campaign workflows and systems, and measure the impact. Lead cross-functional technical initiatives: Serve as the Campaign Operations point person for platform and workflow projects, such as marketing automation platform migrations, owning project plans, timelines, and stakeholder alignment while partnering with MarTech, MDA, Web Dev, Engineering, and vendor teams. Own shared processes: Document, standardize, and automate capabilities such as list uploads and landing-page creation, building repeatable processes and clarifying dependencies with partner teams. Shape the technical roadmap: Help shape and own the technical and AI roadmap for Campaign Operations: decide which capabilities to bring in-house, integrate, rebuild, or automate, and serve as the Campaign Ops subject matter expert and owner for the semantic layer and standardized skills that power our agentic workflows. Provide lead flow backup coverage: Act as a backup point of contact for critical lead flow outages and technical troubleshooting (e.g., audit logs, vendor support), in partnership with the lead flow owners. Enable the team: Share knowledge, drive adoption of new capabilities, and provide hands-on campaign execution support when needed. Champion, role model, and embed Samsara's cultural principles (Focus on Customer Success, Build for the Long Term, Adopt a Growth Mindset, Be Inclusive, Win as a Team) as we scale globally and across new offices. Minimum requirements for the role: 7+ years of hands-on campaign operations experience at a fast-paced B2B technology company, including hands-on execution of multi-channel campaigns (email, landing pages, webinars) in a marketing automation platform such as Marketo, Iterable, and/or HubSpot. Proficiency building AI agents and automated workflows with tools like Claude Code, Cursor, Gumloop, n8n, or similar. Proficiency in Python or JavaScript, plus working experience with APIs, integrations, and data manipulation. Experience experimenting with emerging technologies and turning concepts into functional, maintainable solutions. Strong project management and process orientation: able to scope, plan, and drive cross-functional initiatives from intake to delivery, and to document and standardize repeatable processes. Strong analytical thinking, problem-solving skills, curiosity, and a builder mindset. Ability to work independently, collaborate cross-functionally, and adapt quickly in a fast-paced environment. Good written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to translate operational needs into practical technical solutions. An ideal candidate also has: Experience with Marketo and/or Iterable. Familiarity with AI-native or AI-forward marketing automation platforms, and experience evaluating or migrating between MAPs. Experience with SQL, Salesforce, Hightouch, Databricks, Airtable, Zapier, Workato, or Splash. General marketing operations exposure to lead flow concepts such as routing, scoring, and lifecycle stages. Familiarity with MCP, agent frameworks (e.g., LangChain), and semantic-layer concepts for agentic workflows. Familiarity with email compliance regulations (e.g., GDPR, CAN-SPAM, CASL) and data privacy best practices. Basic familiarity with HTML and CSS.
