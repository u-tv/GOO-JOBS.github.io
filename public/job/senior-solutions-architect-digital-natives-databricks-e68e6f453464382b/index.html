Senior Solutions Architect - Digital Natives

🏢 databricks 📍 Finland 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

(FEQ128R9) The role: At Databricks, our core values are at the heart of everything we do; a culture of proactivity and a customer-centric mindset guide us in creating a unified platform that makes Data Engineering, Data Science, Analytics & GenAI accessible to everyone. We aim to inspire our customers to make informed decisions that push their business forward. We provide a user-friendly and intuitive platform that makes it easy to turn insights into action and fosters a culture of creativity, experimentation, and continuous improvement. As a Senior Solutions Architect, you are an expert-level technical leader responsible for the organisation's most complex customer engagements. You drive consumption strategy, own executive-level relationships, and serve as the definitive technical authority for your accounts. You have declared your archetype - a recognised technical specialisation that makes you a force multiplier across your team and region. You operate at the intersection of deep technical expertise, strategic account planning, and executive influence. This role will report to a Field Engineering Manager in the Emerging Enterprises team. The impact you will have: Drive the technical and consumption strategy in partnership with Account Executives for Startup & Digital Native customers in the Nordics region, directly influencing platform adoption and revenue growth. You will influence stakeholders at all levels through complex engagements with the broader cloud ecosystem and 3rd-party applications, ensuring they are excited by the Databricks vision and solution strategy. Be a 'champion’ for both customers and colleagues, operating as an expert architect and trusted advisor for significant data analytics architecture, design, and adoption of the Databricks Data Intelligence platform. Lead the most complex architecture engagements: multi-workload platforms, enterprise-wide migrations, real-time systems, and AI/ML at scale. Contribute to Field Engineering thought leadership through customer-facing content, workshops, and community engagement Coach junior Solutions Architects and teams on use-case prioritisation and on building technical champions. What we look for: (Note: it is not expected that you “tick every box” below and encourage you to apply if you are meeting several of the expected criteria and can demonstrate a strong ability to transfer skills and develop quickly in the role!) 8+ years in solutions architecture, principal engineering, technical pre-sales leadership, or a senior customer-facing technical role A strong &quot;AI-Builder&quot; mindset; Expert-level coding proficiency — Python, SQL, and ideally Spark/PySpark. You will complete a live coding assessment and a platform build during the interview process. Proven track record of driving consumption growth and platform adoption with customers. Experience working with and/or within Startups and Digital Native organisations will be advantageous for this role. Strong technical architecture skills with deep expertise in enterprise data technologies: distributed systems at scale, streaming/real-time, lakehouse design patterns, data governance frameworks, and cloud-native platforms Expert-level cloud platform knowledge (Databricks, AWS, Azure, GCP or equivalent depth in competing modern data platforms with demonstrated rapid learning ability) Declared technical specialisation with recognised depth — e.g., real-time architectures, ML/AI platforms, data governance, large-scale migrations, or industry-specific solutions Executive presence and strong strategic thinking - ability to influence Directors, VPs, and C-level stakeholders on long-term data and AI strategy - connecting technical architecture decisions to business outcomes and revenue impact Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a quantitative discipline (or equivalent experience); advanced degrees are valued Note: the contract for this position will be formalised with a global Employer of Record (EOR).
