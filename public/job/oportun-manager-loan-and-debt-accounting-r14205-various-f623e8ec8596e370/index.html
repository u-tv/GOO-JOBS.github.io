Oportun: Manager, Loan and Debt Accounting (R14205)

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote - US Who We Are Oportun (Nasdaq: OPRT) is a mission-driven financial services company that puts its members' financial goals within reach. With intelligent borrowing, savings, and budgeting capabilities, Oportun empowers members with the confidence to build a better financial future. Since inception, Oportun has provided more than $22.7 billion in responsible and affordable credit, saved its members more than $2.5 billion in interest and fees, and helped its members set aside an average of more than $1,800 annually. The Opportunity The Manager, Loan and Debt Accounting will oversee the day-to-day accounting activities related to Oportun’s loan portfolio and debt, ensuring accurate and timely financial reporting in accordance with applicable accounting standards and company policies. This role will lead key aspects of the monthly close process, including account reconciliations, financial analysis, and review of loan- and debt-related accounting activities. You’ll also lead and develop accounting team members, strengthen processes and internal controls, and partner across Finance, Technology, and other teams to improve the scalability, accuracy, and efficiency of Oportun’s accounting operations. At Oportun, we move fast and we hold each other to a high bar. If you care about doing your best work while actually moving the needle for real people, you'll fit right in. What You'll Do Your day-to-day You’ll oversee accounting activities for Oportun’s loan portfolio and debt, ensuring transactions are recorded accurately, completely, and in accordance with applicable accounting standards and company policies. You’ll lead the month-end close process for loan- and debt-related accounts, including journal entries, account reconciliations, supporting schedules, flux analysis, and other close activities. You’ll supervise, coach, and develop accounting team members, providing clear priorities, feedback, review, and ongoing support. You’ll strengthen accounting processes and internal controls by identifying opportunities to reduce manual work, improve data quality, streamline workflows, and improve close and reporting efficiency. You’ll use AI-enabled tools and emerging technologies thoughtfully to enhance accounting analysis, documentation, workflows, and operational efficiency while maintaining appropriate review and controls. You’ll partner with Finance, Technology, and other Oportun teams to implement process improvements, automation solutions, and scalable accounting practices supporting loan and debt activities. Who You Are The right fit looks like this You’re a detail-oriented accounting leader who combines strong technical judgment with a practical approach to execution. You know how to balance accuracy, controls, deadlines, and team development while continuously looking for better ways to operate. You're comfortable navigating complexity and don't wait for perfect information to move forward. You communicate clearly, give direct feedback, and expect the same in return. What You Bring Skills & experience Required Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field. 6+ years of progressive accounting experience. Strong knowledge of U.S. GAAP and demonstrated ability to research, interpret, and apply accounting guidance. Experience with loan, lending, debt, or other financial-services-related accounting activities. Demonstrated experience leading, coaching, and developing accounting professionals and reviewing the work of others. Experience with internal controls and accounting process improvements. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to investigate complex accounting issues, identify root causes, and develop practical solutions. Ability to manage multiple priorities and meet strict deadlines in a dynamic environment. Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to partner effectively across Finance and other functions. Willingness to use AI-enabled tools and emerging technologies responsibly to improve accounting analysis, documentation, and processes while maintaining appropriate review and controls. Nice to Have CPA or progress toward CPA certification. Experience in consumer lending, financial services, fintech, banking, or a related industry. Experience implementing accounting process improvements, workflow automation, or financial-system enhancements. Familiarity with AI-enabled accounting, finance, analytics, or productivity tools in a controlled environment. Our AI Mindset We build with AI. We lead with judgment. AI isn't a feature here. It's how we work. We're excited about what AI makes possible, and we're looking for people who share that curiosity. You don't need to be an expert. You need to be open to experimenting , willing to learn, and able to apply new tools in ways that actually move the needle. In this role, you'll be encouraged to explore AI tools, find practical use cases for your function, and bring ideas for working smarter. We believe the best outcomes come when human judgment and AI capability work together.
