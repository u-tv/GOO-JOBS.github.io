Job Description

At Affirm, we exist for the moments that matter—giving people a clear, predictable way to pay over time, with no hidden fees, no surprises, and no tradeoffs on what matters most. About the Engineering team The Engineering team builds systems that power Affirm’s mission. We take pride in our craft, building reliable, performant, and scalable solutions that touch every part of the business. Whether we’re enabling new ways to pay, ensuring our platforms can scale to the most demanding levels in the industry, or developing seamless servicing experiences, our focus is on solving complex problems through exceptional engineering. About the role Lead the Code Standards team within Developer Productivity, helping Affirm engineers build on current, reliable languages, libraries, and tooling. You’ll grow a team spanning Python and Kotlin, guide technical strategy, and make upgrades routine through shared frameworks and automation, improving developer speed and the reliability of Affirm’s products. What you'll do Lead, hire, and develop a team of engineers across Python and Kotlin tooling. Set clear expectations, provide regular feedback, and build shared ownership and knowledge across the team. Set the technical strategy and roadmap for getting Affirm’s languages and dependencies current and keeping them current. Translate that direction into achievable quarterly goals, balancing migration risk, developer needs, operational work, and team capacity. Drive runtime and dependency upgrades across a large production codebase. Guide compatibility testing, staged rollouts, and recovery plans with service owners and security partners so teams can adopt supported versions safely. Evolve shared libraries, dependency management, and build tooling for Python and Kotlin services. Make code easier to build, maintain, and extend through clear package boundaries, versioning, reusable configuration, and automated standards. Turn repeated migration work into reliable upgrade workflows and self-service automation. Help engineers discover supported dependencies, understand required changes, and validate upgrades with less manual coordination and fewer disruptive catch-up efforts. Partner with CI/CD, Developer Environments, Test Platform, and service teams to deliver tooling that fits how engineers work. Guide architecture and code reviews, investigate difficult failures, and use developer feedback to improve adoption and usability. Own the reliability and support of the team’s tools and services, including monitoring, on-call, incident response, and documentation. Measure progress through supported-version coverage, dependency age, upgrade lead time, and reduced engineering toil. What we look for 7+ years of software engineering experience, including 2+ years of engineering management or equivalent people leadership. A track record of coaching engineers, supporting career growth, and leading a team through complex delivery. Strong experience building backend systems or developer infrastructure, with depth in Python or Kotlin/Java. You understand language runtimes, package management, dependency resolution, and build systems, and can guide engineers working across both ecosystems. Experience leading migrations, dependency upgrades, or shared library changes across a large codebase. You understand compatibility, transitive dependencies, testing, and safe rollout practices, and have used automation to make repeated changes easier. Experience treating internal tools and platforms as products: understanding developer needs, setting priorities, and driving adoption across teams. You can connect technical investments to measurable improvements in productivity, reliability, and maintenance cost. The technical judgment to work closely with senior engineers on architecture, review code, and help debug complex build or runtime failures. You balance immediate delivery with maintainability and reliable operations. Clear written and verbal communication, with experience aligning distributed teams on ownership, tradeoffs, and delivery plans. You can lead through ambiguity, make decisions with incomplete information, and adjust as you learn. This posting is for an existing vacancy.
