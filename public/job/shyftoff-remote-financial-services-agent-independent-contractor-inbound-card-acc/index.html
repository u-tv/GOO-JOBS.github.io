ShyftOff: Remote Financial Services Agent (Independent Contractor) – Inbound Card & Account Support

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Florida URL: https://www.shyftoff.com/ Set your own hours, take inbound banking card calls from home, and earn up to $350 in certification bonuses. The contract has a pool of 20,000+ hours of work available every month. Earnings at a Glance 15.00–17.00 per hour, plus performance incentives $300 certification bonus once you complete 200 productive hours +$50 bonus if you certify within 6 weeks Choose your own hours. Coverage runs 24/7, every day Remote, U.S.-based, 1099 independent contractor The Opportunity Help cardholders manage their money with confidence. You'll take inbound calls from people who rely on their cards for everyday life. They might be activating a new card, replacing a lost one, sorting out a disputed charge, or getting a time-sensitive payment through. These moments matter, and you're the person who makes them go smoothly. What the Work Involves Inbound calls on card activation, replacements, lost or stolen cards, balances, and account questions Payments, transfers, and disputes, handled accurately and securely Identity verification and strict security and compliance protocols on every call Spotting potential fraud and helping resolve it with composure Accurate documentation of every interaction You're a Fit If You Bring Clear, empathetic phone communication, even when callers are stressed Care for protecting sensitive financial information Comfort moving between multiple systems at once Composure and problem-solving in a fast-paced environment Strong plus: banking, card, financial services, or contact center experience Your Path to Starting Work Week 1: Apply. Create your ShyftOff account and apply to the Financial Services campaign. Weeks 2–3: Screening & credentialing. Federal background check, fingerprinting, drug screening, and credential issuance. Weeks 4–5: Certification. Simulations and scored mock calls. Standards and examples of required work product are demonstrated. Week 6: Practice calls & go-live. Show that your work meets the program's accuracy and quality standards, then start choosing open hours. Screening Cost: Please Read Before Applying This is a federally backed program, so every contractor must complete third-party credentialing before taking live calls. As an independent contractor, you pay these providers directly: Extensive federal background check Fingerprinting Drug screening YubiKey security device (yours to keep) Total: $299, paid to third-party providers. It's due only when you move into credentialing, not at sign-up. What You'll Need Ability to pass federal background, fingerprint, and drug screening A quiet home workspace and reliable high-speed internet Commitment to completing self-paced certification (about 6 weeks) How to Apply Create your agent account at https://www.shyftoff.com/agents Apply to the Financial Services campaign from your dashboard This is an independent contractor (1099) opportunity. You set your own schedule, work remotely, and provide your own workspace and equipment. To apply: https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/shyftoff-remote-financial-services-agent-independent-contractor-inbound-card-account-support
