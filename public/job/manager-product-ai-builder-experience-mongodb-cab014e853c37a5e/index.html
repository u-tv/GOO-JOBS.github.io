🏢 mongodb 📍 Dublin, Ireland 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

We are seeking a high-impact product leader to join us as Manager of Product and lead Frameworks within MongoDB’s AI Builder Experience organization. In this role, you will champion bold execution, drive impactful growth outcomes, and prioritize our product strategy across MongoDB’s AI framework integrations, developer SDKs, and cutting-edge application libraries. As a first-line manager, you’ll operate as a hands-on player-coach—leading a team of talented IC Product Managers while frequently diving into IC or staff-level work yourself to shape how MongoDB shows up in the frameworks builders use every day. You’ll decide where to place the next bets, build partnerships across the AI ecosystem, and work closely with engineering, marketing, developer relations, and partners to make MongoDB the natural foundation agents run on. Your leadership in this role will be instrumental not just in maintaining, but in increasing and intensifying builders’ love of MongoDB. We are looking to speak to candidates who are based in Dublin for our hybrid working model. What You'll Do Grow PMs into the best versions of themselves. Lead a high-performing team of IC Product Managers, coaching them on product craft, strategy execution, and career growth. Meet every PM where they are and build a culture of creativity and ownership in line with MongoDB's Core Values Act as a player-coach: guide day-to-day product judgment, while stepping in directly on the highest-priority product questions, toughest trade-offs, and sensitive stakeholder situations without taking ownership away from your team Build the operating engine that makes the team great. Set the product standards the team runs on with clear decision-making mechanisms, building a culture of rapid hypothesis testing and iterative experimentation so the team moves quickly, and everyone knows what matters most and why Set goals, track the right metrics, and drive outcomes. Builder tooling has a profound impact on activation, retention, and churn. Optimize the developer journey and reduce time-to-value for integrations. You'll build a strong partnership with product analytics to identify the right signals, formulate meaningful metrics, and use data to drive decisions Champion agentic development. Builders are using AI to write their code and build agent-native applications, and MongoDB is the data platform at the center of that shift. Make sure our framework integrations are purpose-built for the way agents actually work, with memory, state, and context as core capabilities. Bring real enthusiasm and a clear point of view on where agentic development is going, and set ambitious goals for the builder impact that follows Place smart, ambitious bets on where MongoDB shows up next. Shape a portfolio investment strategy that looks beyond adoption numbers to where the ecosystem is heading, balancing the integrations builders rely on today with rising frameworks and early bets that could define what comes next. Recommend where to build, where to partner, and where to step back and work with leadership and partner teams to land those calls Be a credible technical voice. You’ll engage deeply with engineering on decisions involving client libraries, agentic workflows, and developer tooling. You won’t write the code, but you’ll need to understand it well enough to help the team make the right tradeoffs, communicate confidently with technical customers, and be a strong advocate for the builders out there who are creating with MongoDB Win the trust of the ecosystem, inside and outside MongoDB. Frameworks is partnership work at its core. Build relationships with framework maintainers and AI platforms that trust MongoDB to ship quality and stay committed after launch.
