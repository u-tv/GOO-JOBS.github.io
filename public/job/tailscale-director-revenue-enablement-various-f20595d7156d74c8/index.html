About Tailscale Tailscale is making safe connection effortless by delivering software that makes it easy to securely interconnect people and their devices, no matter where they are. From hobbyists to multinational corporations, teams of every size use Tailscale each day to protect their networks, share access to internal tools, and more. We're building a future for the Internet that's easy, sensible, and safe, like it used to be. Founded in 2019 and fully distributed, we're backed by Accel, CRV, Insight, Heavybit, and Uncork Capital. About the Role We're looking for a Director of Revenue Enablement to lead and scale our enablement function as Tailscale enters its next phase of growth. You'll own the strategy, team, and systems that help our revenue organization ramp faster, develop deep technical and commercial expertise, and consistently deliver exceptional customer experiences. This is an opportunity to build a world-class enablement function at a high-growth, product-led company. You'll partner closely with Sales, Customer Engineering, Customer Success, Product, Marketing, and Revenue Operations to ensure our customer-facing teams have the knowledge, skills, and tools to effectively engage technical buyers, communicate the value of Tailscale's platform, and drive meaningful customer outcomes. We're looking for someone who thinks beyond traditional training programs. As AI transforms how teams learn, engage customers, and work, you'll define the next generation of enablement at Tailscale, building scalable, AI-powered systems that accelerate productivity, deepen technical expertise, and keep pace with our rapidly evolving product and GTM motions. The ideal candidate combines strategic leadership, strong commercial instincts, technical aptitude, and a builder's mentality. You've successfully scaled enablement in a high-growth environment, understand what great customer-facing execution looks like across both sales and technical roles, and are energized by rethinking how enablement operates. Key Responsibilities Build and Lead Revenue Enablement Define and execute a multi-quarter enablement strategy aligned with Tailscale's revenue priorities and growth objectives. Build, lead, and develop a high-performing enablement team, establishing scalable processes, clear priorities, and accountability. Serve as a strategic advisor to revenue leadership, identifying capability gaps and connecting enablement investments to measurable business outcomes. Build scalable enablement systems that support rapid headcount growth, increasing product complexity, and the evolving needs of our customer-facing organization. Accelerate Onboarding and Continuous Development Own role-based onboarding and ongoing development across Sales, Sales Development, Customer Engineering, Customer Success, and other revenue-facing teams. Build scalable learning paths, competency frameworks, and certifications that accelerate ramp time and drive continuous skill development. Partner with frontline managers to establish coaching frameworks and reinforce learning through everyday workflows. Drive Sales and Technical Excellence Develop programs that strengthen sales execution, including discovery, value selling, competitive positioning, negotiation, and account development. Partner with technical leaders to build enablement programs covering product architecture, technical discovery, solution design, demonstrations, and proof-of-concept execution. Establish role-specific proficiency standards, playbooks, and certifications that improve commercial and technical effectiveness across the customer lifecycle. Connect Product Innovation to Field Readiness Partner with Product, Product Marketing, and technical leaders to establish a repeatable product launch readiness framework. Translate complex technical capabilities into relevant learning experiences, messaging, and resources tailored to commercial and technical audiences. Maintain scalable content and knowledge systems while establishing feedback loops to continuously improve field readiness. Reimagine Enablement Through AI and Automation Define and execute an AI-first enablement strategy that transforms how teams learn, practice, access knowledge, and improve performance. Implement AI-powered workflows across onboarding, coaching, technical learning, and knowledge management to personalize development and accelerate productivity. Partner cross-functionally to embed intelligent guidance into everyday workflows and drive adoption of emerging technologies. Measure Impact and Drive Continuous Improvement Establish metrics connecting enablement programs to ramp time, role proficiency, quota attainment, win rates, and customer-facing productivity. Leverage performance data, conversation intelligence, and qualitative feedback to identify gaps and continuously improve programs. Own the enablement technology strategy and lead readiness initiatives for key revenue events, including Sales Kickoff and technical bootcamps. What We Are Looking For 10+ years of experience in revenue enablement, sales enablement, technical enablement, sales leadership, or related GTM functions at B2B technology companies, including experience building and leading teams. Proven success scaling enablement in high-growth environments , supporting both commercial and technical customer-facing organizations. Strong technical aptitude , with the ability to understand complex infrastructure, networking, security, or developer-focused products and translate them into effective learning experiences. Deep expertise in adult learning, instructional design, and competency development , with a track record of driving measurable improvements in performance. Strong commercial acumen , including experience reinforcing sales methodology, developing customer-facing skills, and improving revenue productivity. An AI-first, systems-oriented mindset , with experience leveraging AI, automation, and scalable processes to improve learning and productivity. A data-driven approach , with demonstrated success measuring enablement effectiveness and connecting programs to business outcomes. Exceptional leadership, communication, and cross-functional influence , with the ability to engage technical and nontechnical audiences, advise executives, and drive organizational change. A builder's mentality , combining strategic thinking with hands-on execution, high ownership, and comfort operating in a fast-paced, remote-first environment. Bonus Points Experience in product-led growth environments or with developer-focused products. Background in technical presales, Customer Engineering, or quota-carrying sales roles. Experience building technical certification programs or hands-on learning environments. Experience implementing AI-powered coaching, learning, or productivity solutions. Experience supporting both high-velocity commercial and complex enterprise sales motions.
