Support Engineer, AI Infrastructure & Tooling

🏢 figma 📍 San Francisco, CA • New York, NY • United States 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Figma is growing our team of passionate creatives and builders on a mission to make design accessible to all. Figma’s platform helps teams bring ideas to life—whether you're brainstorming, creating a prototype, translating designs into code, or iterating with AI. From idea to product, Figma empowers teams to streamline workflows, move faster, and work together in real time from anywhere in the world. If you're excited to shape the future of design and collaboration, join us! Figma is evolving the Customer Support experience, powered by AI, automation, and integrated systems. As a Support Engineer, AI & Automations on the Support Technology Team, you'll design, build, and operationalize integrations across systems like Decagon, Zendesk, Figma admin tooling, and adjacent Customer Support platforms, bringing the right context into customer conversations and automating complex workflows. You'll turn ambiguous support problems into working technical solutions, combining applied AI engineering with strong software development to connect conversational systems to the context, tools, and backend capabilities they need to act. Automated Actions is one of Product Support's most important initiatives for 2027, and your work will help resolve requests that currently require manual intervention, giving our Support Specialists more time for complex customer needs. This is a full-time role that can be held from one of our US hubs or remotely in the United States. What you'll do at Figma: Build and operate automated actions that AI agents can invoke to resolve customer issues and complete support tasks, using clear tool definitions, structured inputs and outputs, and reliable execution logic. Develop reusable integrations connecting Decagon and other support platforms to Figma's internal systems, relevant customer and account data, and approved backend capabilities. Design permission-aware actions with authentication, input validation, policy checks, appropriate human approval, and safeguards that prevent unintended or duplicate actions. Define expected behavior and failure paths, and validate the experience end to end; contribute to conversational design and workflow testing when needed. Own technical testing, deployment, monitoring, troubleshooting, and maintenance. Evaluate and debug AI behavior across tool selection, action inputs, execution, and results, and turn what you learn into improvements. Work with Support, Analytics, Engineering, and Security to prioritize opportunities, resolve dependencies, and measure successful task completion, resolution quality, customer effort, and realized capacity gains. We'd love to hear from you if you have: Hands-on experience shipping AI-powered applications or agents that use tools and APIs to execute actions and complete tasks in production. Strong programming skills in Python, TypeScript, or a comparable language, with experience applying software development principles, software design practices, and technical team processes, and owning production software from implementation through ongoing operation. Experience building secure, reliable integrations, including authentication, authorization, data validation, retries, and error handling. A practical understanding of LLM behavior, tool calling, and structured outputs, with sound judgment about where deterministic logic, policy enforcement, or human approval belongs, and experience evaluating and debugging AI workflows, including incorrect tool selection, invalid inputs, failed actions, and unexpected outputs, alongside strong software testing and monitoring practices. The initiative to turn ambiguous customer needs into reusable technical solutions, communicate tradeoffs clearly, and collaborate across technical and operational teams. You care about whether what you build solves the customer's problem and delivers measurable impact. While it's not required, it's an added plus if you also have: Experience building customer-facing AI support experiences with Decagon, Sierra, or similar platforms, or integrating with Zendesk and other support systems. Experience with agent frameworks, orchestration libraries, Model Context Protocol (MCP), retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), or context engineering. Experience building automated AI evaluations, regression suites, and agent tracing, or implementing actions involving account administration, billing, or refunds. Familiarity with customer support operations, conversational design, or Figma's products and customer workflows.
