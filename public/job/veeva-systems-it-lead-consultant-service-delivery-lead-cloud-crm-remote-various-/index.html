The Role Are you looking for a place where your work makes an impact? We are seeking a talented Cloud CRM Consultant to join our amazing, fast-paced team of experts in Hungary. As a Cloud CRM Lead Consultant in our Commercial Managed Services team, you will drive customer engagements and lead project delivery for live Veeva commercial solutions (primarily Vault CRM). You will serve as the strategic bridge between business stakeholders and technical teams, leading delivery teams, managing executive client relationships, and ensuring technical solutions are scalable, secure, and aligned with industry best practices. This is a remote, full-time role based in Hungary (must be legally authorized to work in Europe). What You'll Do Team & Project Leadership: Lead project delivery teams, guide junior/senior consultants, and oversee the successful execution of configuration changes, feature activations, and system enhancements Strategic Client Communication: Serve as the primary trusted advisor for customer stakeholders, managing expectations, leading workshops, and presenting strategic recommendations Architecture & Solutioning: Translate complex business requirements into scalable technical designs on the Vault CRM platform Best Practice Advocacy: Proactively guide clients toward Veeva and Life Sciences industry best practices while ensuring seamless integration and high client satisfaction Requirements 8+ years of experience configuring, supporting, or implementing enterprise CRM solutions (e.g., Veeva, Salesforce, MS Dynamics) Proven leadership experience managing project teams and taking ownership of end-to-end client deliverables Exceptional client communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to navigate complex business conversations Strong organizational skills and the ability to work independently in a remote environment Fluency in English (written and verbal) Nice to Have Direct experience with Veeva Commercial applications or Salesforce.com Experience in the Life Sciences / Pharmaceutical industry Interviewing with Veeva We value your time and believe in a transparent hiring process.
