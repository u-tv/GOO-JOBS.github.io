Technical Accounting Manager I We’re looking for a Technical Accounting Manager I to join our Technical Accounting and SEC Reporting team. In this role, you’ll research and advise on complex accounting matters, develop accounting policies and support Pinterest’s external reporting. You’ll join a small, highly collaborative team with broad exposure across the business and have opportunities to leverage AI and automation to build scalable accounting processes and internal resources. What you’ll do: Independently review contracts and analyze complex, material or confidential transactions to establish the appropriate accounting treatment under US GAAP Research technical accounting matters and prepare clear, well-supported accounting memoranda, policies and practical guidance across topics such as leases, share-based compensation, capitalized software, infrastructure and hosting arrangements, long-lived asset impairment, contingencies and restructuring Serve as a technical accounting resource to internal stakeholders, translating complex accounting requirements into practical guidance and providing training to internal teams Partner with Accounting Operations, Finance, Legal, Real Estate, and other teams to advise on transactions and resolve accounting matters Monitor new accounting standards and SEC reporting requirements, assess their financial and operational implications and support the implementation of related policies, processes and controls Leverage AI to accelerate research, synthesize accounting guidance and develop scalable internal resources while applying judgment and verification to maintain accuracy, confidentiality and quality Support the preparation and review of financial statements and related disclosures included in Pinterest’s SEC filings, including Forms 10-Q, 10-K and 8-K and the proxy statement What we’re looking for: Bachelor’s degree in accounting, a related field or equivalent experience and an active CPA certification Minimum of 5 years of progressive accounting experience, including experience in public accounting and/or at a publicly traded company Strong knowledge of US GAAP, including experience researching and applying complex accounting guidance such as ASC 842, Leases; ASC 718, Stock Compensation; ASC 350, Intangible; ASC 360, Property, Plant, and Equipment; and ASC 450, Contingencies Experience preparing technical accounting memoranda and policies that clearly document accounting conclusions, supporting guidance and relevant judgments Understanding of SEC reporting requirements, internal controls and SOX 404 Clear written and verbal communication skills, including experience explaining complex accounting topics to stakeholders with varying levels of accounting knowledge Experience managing multiple priorities and collaborating across teams in a fast-paced environment with firm reporting deadlines Demonstrated experience using AI to improve the speed, quality or scalability of accounting work, with a thoughtful approach to validating outputs, protecting confidential data and maintaining ownership of final conclusions and deliverables Relocation Statement: This position is not eligible for relocation assistance.
