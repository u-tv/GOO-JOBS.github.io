About the job As a Senior Engineering Manager in Communication Identity Domain, you will lead the engineering teams behind Porting, Hosting, and A2P Messaging. Your teams own the products that let customers bring their own numbers onto Twilio (Porting), enable existing numbers for messaging without a full port (Hosting), and register and operate compliant Application-to-Person (A2P) messaging campaigns at scale — the gateway that determines whether a customer's messages actually reach their end users' handsets. Porting and Hosting are high-value, white-glove transactions: each one touches an existing customer's live production number, or a prospective customer's number that already works elsewhere and needs to keep working seamlessly through the transition. That makes operational maturity a core part of the value we deliver — precise execution, clear communication, and dependable outcomes are what earn a customer's trust in the Communication Identity Domain. Delivering on that requires deep, reliable integration with a broad ecosystem of carriers, aggregators, industry registries and the expertise to orchestrate that complexity into a smooth, trustworthy experience for the customer. It also means investing in efficient, well-instrumented Ops Tooling that gives your team and our internal support organizations clear visibility into every port, hosting activation, and campaign — so issues are caught and resolved quickly, and operational excellence scales with the business. You will own the people strategy, delivery execution, and stakeholder relationships for a team responsible for the trust-building work of moving and activating phone numbers for existing and prospective customers. You will be expected to champion AI-driven development practices, ensuring your engineers adopt LLMs and agentic tools to accelerate delivery, improve quality, and stay at the forefront of modern software engineering. Your mission is to build a high-performing team that consistently ships reliable, customer-obsessed products - while balancing the competing demands of external product commitments, internal platform obligations, and operational tooling investments. Responsibilities In this role, you’ll: DRAW THE OWL: Own the delivery roadmap for the Communication Identity Domain, including customer-facing services, internal platform APIs, and Ops Tooling. Partner with Principal and Staff Engineers to translate long-term technical strategy into executable quarterly plans and measurable outcomes. ACCELERATE WITH AI: Drive the adoption of AI coding assistants (GitHub Copilot, Anthropic Claude, or internal LLMs) within your team. Set expectations for AI-augmented workflows, measure the impact on team velocity, and create the psychological safety for engineers to experiment with new tools. BE AN OWNER: Take full accountability for your team’s operational excellence, including system reliability, incident response, on-call health, and security posture. Make high-judgment calls that balance speed, quality, and risk. WEAR THE CUSTOMER’S SHOES: Serve two customer bases with equal rigor. For external customers, partner with Product Management to ensure communication identity workflows are seamless and developer-friendly. For internal customers, proactively engage with dependent engineering teams to understand their needs, gather feedback on your team’s APIs and Ops Tooling, and ensure your platform enables them to move faster. Ground every decision in customer data and feedback from both audiences. EMPOWER OTHERS: Recruit, develop, and retain a world-class engineering team. Build a culture of psychological safety, candid feedback, and career growth. Coach your engineers-from mid-level to staff-to reach their full potential and take on increasing ownership. Champion diversity, equity, and inclusion in every hiring and development decision. RUTHLESSLY PRIORITIZE: Navigate competing priorities across external product features, internal platform needs, and Ops Tooling investments. Balance requests from multiple stakeholders-Product Management, internal engineering teams, and operations-with a clear framework for saying “no” to protect focus. Ensure resources are aligned to the highest-impact work across all three pillars. WRITE IT DOWN: Communicate team strategy, progress, and risks clearly and consistently to engineering leadership and cross-functional partners. Author team plans, post-mortems, and decision documents that create transparency and alignment. BUILD BRIDGES: Act as the primary point of contact between the Communication Identity Domain and its stakeholders. Manage relationships with Product Management, external-facing teams, and internal engineering teams that depend on your services and Ops Tooling. Proactively communicate roadmap plans, surface trade-offs, negotiate priorities, and ensure stakeholders have clear visibility into what your team is delivering and why. NO SHENANIGANS: Model integrity in every interaction. Create an environment where engineers feel safe raising concerns, surfacing problems early, and challenging the status quo. Hold yourself and your team to the highest standards of ethical behavior.
