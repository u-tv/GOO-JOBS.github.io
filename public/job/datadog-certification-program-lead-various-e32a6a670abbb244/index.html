Datadog: Certification Program Lead

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: California, USA, Remote; Massachusetts, USA, Remote; New York, USA, Remote; Washington, USA, Remote As Certification Program Lead, you will join the Customer Education and Training team and will take ownership of the strategy, execution, and expansion of our current certification initiatives. Your core mission will be to elevate the Datadog Certified practitioner program into the leading industry credential for individuals and enterprises across observability, security, and the diverse technical ecosystems that Datadog supports. What You'll Do Own program direction, roadmap, and long-term planning. Define growth targets for increasing practitioner and enterprise adoption. Manage the program budget and vendor relationships. Direct the production and publication of new exams, including maintenance and localization of existing content. Partner with engineering and product teams to keep certification criteria accurate as Datadog evolves. Work with Datadog Partner teams on the development and delivery of certification exams for the Datadog Partner Network. Envision, plan, and build promotion and engagement programs to recognize multiple-certified individuals. Empower the sales team to sell certifications to enterprise customers. Collaborate with Customer Education and other teams as they create certification prep workshops and learning resources. Manage a small team of certification coordinators. Who You Are You have 7+ years of experience leading a certification program for developer-focused tools and products. You have managed budgets, vendors, and complex project timelines. You understand the lifecycle of exam development, from role scopes to subject matter expertise to working with psychometric consultants, to publication and localization. You have worked with cloud engineering professionals and understand the professional skills required for devops, software development, and infrastructure monitoring. You have managed a certification program that features both multiple choice and performance-based exams. You are comfortable using AI tools to plan, validate, and accelerate business processes.
