Senior Manager, Accounting

🏢 mongodb 📍 Gurugram 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

MongoDB is seeking a Senior Manager, Accounting to join the Corporate Accounting team within Controllership in Gurugram. This role will lead core corporate accounting activities, global close execution, balance-sheet integrity, reconciliations, accounting operations, and process improvement across MongoDB’s multi-entity environment. The Senior Manager will partner closely with General Ledger, SEC Reporting, Technical Accounting, FP&A, Legal, Tax, Internal Audit, and external auditors. The role is designed for an accounting leader who can combine strong operational judgment with disciplined execution, clear communication, and a process-improvement mindset. What you will do Corporate accounting and global close Lead and review corporate accounting activities across the monthly, quarterly, and annual close cycles Own the quality and timeliness of balance-sheet reconciliations, journal entries, account analyses, flux explanations, and other close deliverables Coordinate with global accounting teams to resolve variances, strengthen close procedures, and maintain consistent accounting practices across entities Support consolidation activities, intercompany accounting, accruals, prepaids, fixed assets, leases, and other corporate accounting areas as assigned Partner with SEC Reporting and Technical Accounting to provide accurate close data, schedules, and supporting documentation for external reporting and accounting conclusions Controls, audit, and process improvement Maintain and strengthen accounting controls and support SOX activities, including control execution, evidence quality, process narratives, and remediation Serve as a key point of contact for internal and external auditors during walkthroughs, quarterly reviews, annual audits, and requests related to corporate accounting processes Identify opportunities to improve data quality, reduce manual effort, and increase automation across close and reconciliation processes Help implement scalable accounting policies, procedures, and documentation as MongoDB continues to grow Leadership and partnership Lead, coach, and develop accounting professionals while setting clear expectations for quality, accountability, and continuous improvement Coordinate work across global accounting teams and help establish consistent processes across entities and reporting cycles Translate accounting issues into clear, practical guidance for Finance leadership and cross-functional stakeholders Build trusted relationships across the organization and represent Controllership with sound judgment and a service-oriented approach What you bring Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field 8+ years of progressive accounting experience, including significant experience in corporate accounting, global close, or a related controllership function Strong knowledge of U.S. GAAP and corporate accounting processes in a public-company or similarly complex environment Experience with multi-entity accounting, consolidations, intercompany activity, balance-sheet reconciliations, and close management Experience working in a SOX-controlled environment and partnering with internal and external auditors Experience leading, mentoring, or managing accounting professionals in a global organization Strong analytical, organizational, written, and verbal communication skills Ability to manage multiple priorities, operate effectively during close and reporting cycles, and make sound decisions with incomplete information Experience with NetSuite, FloQast, Workiva, or similar accounting and reporting systems is preferred
