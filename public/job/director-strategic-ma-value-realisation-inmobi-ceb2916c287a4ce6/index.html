Role Overview We are seeking a Director/VP– Strategic M&A Integration & Value Realisation to own and lead the People charter across global mergers, acquisitions, and strategic investments . The role owns the end-to-end integration and value delivery lifecycle for large acquisitions and strategic transactions—starting pre-LOI and extending through Day-1, Day-90, and Year-1 outcomes. This role exists to ensure that acquisitions are: Assimilated fast Organisationally and culturally integrated Cost- and value-disciplined Measurably accretive to the core business The role does not own deal execution. Instead, it ensures that every deal delivers its intended strategic, organisational, and people outcomes, with clear accountability for value realisation. Operating at the intersection of People, Strategy, Finance, and Business , this is a high-impact role critical to making inorganic growth a repeatable, disciplined value engine for the organisation. Key Responsibilities Pre-LOI Integration & Org Due Diligence (Strategic Advisory) Partner closely with SIG Finance, and the CHRO’s office before LOI to assess: Leadership quality, depth, and succession risk Org structure, role overlaps, and decision rights Culture, performance norms, and operating cadence Workforce cost structures, location leverage, and scalability Shape integration hypotheses that directly influence: Valuation ceilings Retention vs redundancy strategies Day-1 operating model decisions Provide clear, decision-oriented inputs that inform deal structure—not retrospective commentary. Integration Strategy & Execution Ownership Design and own the end-to-end integration strategy across people, org, cost, systems, and culture Lead Day-1 / Day-30 / Day-90 / Year-1 integration plans Drive fast, decisive integration choices; escalate trade-offs when required Ensure integration momentum is sustained without creating prolonged disruption or fatigue This role acts as the single-threaded owner for integration outcomes. Org, Talent & Culture Assimilation Lead future-state org design and leadership mapping Make strategic calls on: Role redundancies Leadership transitions Retention of truly critical talent Drive cultural assimilation in close partnership with PBPs, focusing on: Decision speed Accountability and ownership Performance bars and execution rigor Ensure acquired leaders and teams are fully embedded, not operating as parallel structures. Synergy & Cost Value Realisation(Core to the Role) Architect and deliver robust synergies across: Workforce and leadership layers Vendors, platforms, and infrastructure Operating model simplification Partner with Finance to track real P&L and cash impact, not just synergy narratives Embed cost discipline from Day-1, ensuring value is realised—not deferred Hold integration teams accountable to committed vs realised value. Senior Stakeholder & Governance Leadership Act as a trusted extension of the CHRO in driving enterprise-wide change Partner closely with CEO, CFO, Business Heads, and SIG Provide transparent, executive-level updates on: Value realised vs committed Integration risks and blockers Org, leadership, and talent implications Ensure governance is tight, decisions are documented, and trade-offs are explicit.
