Siemens: Senior Power Systems Consultant

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Berlin, Germany URL: http://www.siemens.com Who are we? Here at Siemens Grid Software, our mission is to accelerate and secure the energy transition in a sustainable and profitable way. And for that we need you! We are paving the way for autonomous grid management empowering grid operators to accelerate their digital transformation easier, faster, and at scale. By leaving behind traditional ways of tackling the net zero challenge and embracing the powerful capabilities of software and digital technology, we turn the complexity of grids into competitive advantage. No matter where power comes from or where it goes, we make sure it makes its way at every step. Find out how Siemens Grid Software is decoding the future of energy . Join our team, get inspired, and help us re-imagine the world! Siemens Grid Software’s consulting team PTI (Power Technologies International) is seeking a Senior Power Systems Consultant . This is a remote role located within the contiguous U.S. What You Will do: Lead and perform consulting engagements related to power system analysis and design, power system dynamics and transients, generator interconnection, and grid code compliance. Manage project execution, track progress, and make adjustments as needed to maintain scope, schedule, budget, and quality objectives. Build and maintain client relationships to ensure customer satisfaction, identify client needs, and develop opportunities for additional services. Support business development activities through proposal preparation, technical approaches, project plans, budgets, schedules, and statements of work. Mentor junior consultants and support their technical and professional development. Develop automation tools and scripts to streamline repetitive tasks, improve quality, reduce errors, and increase overall efficiency. Prepare technical reports and present study results, recommendations, and technical findings to clients and industry stakeholders. Required Qualifications: Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering. 8+ years of experience in transmission planning, power system operations, generator interconnection, or power system studies. Experience performing power flow and dynamic stability analyses using PSS®E, TSAT™, TARA, PSLF™, PowerWorld, or similar software tools. Knowledge of NERC Reliability Standards and familiarity with RTO and regional transmission planning processes, criteria, and practices. Strong communication, customer engagement, presentation, and technical report writing skills. Ability to travel up to 10%. Preferred Qualifications: Master's degree or Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering with a specialization in Power and Energy Systems. Consulting experience within the electric utility or power industry. Experience with ERCOT and/or SPP transmission planning and generator interconnection processes. Experience with scripting and automation using Python or similar tools. Successful Performance in this Role: In your first 12–18 months, you will: Deliver high-quality power system studies on time and within budget. Build trusted advisor relationships with clients by providing clear, practical solutions to complex technical challenges. Successfully manage multiple consulting engagements while maintaining strong client satisfaction. Contribute to business growth through proposal development, identification of follow-on work, and expansion of existing client relationships. Identify and implement process improvements, automation tools, and best practices that improve team efficiency and quality. Provide technical guidance and mentorship to junior consultants and engineers.
