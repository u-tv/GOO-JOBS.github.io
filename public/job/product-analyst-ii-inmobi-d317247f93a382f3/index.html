Job Description

InMobi (Corporate) InMobi Group is a global technology company shaping the future of agentic commerce and advertising. Through its ecosystem of businesses — including InMobi Advertising and flagship consumer platform Glance — InMobi leverages data, machine learning, and generative AI to help brands reach audiences more precisely and consumers discover products more intuitively. Glance, which is pioneering new models of agentic commerce, is owned and operated by Glance InMobi Pte. Ltd., a non-consolidated subsidiary of InMobi Pte. Ltd. InMobi Advertising InMobi Advertising, part of global technology company InMobi, is an agentic advertising platform helping brands and merchants achieve their business outcomes. Through its proprietary intelligence, AI-led solutions, and vast consumer reach — including flagship consumer platform Glance — InMobi Advertising delivers the omnichannel performance defining what's next in advertising and commerce. Glance is owned and operated by Glance InMobi Pte. Ltd., a non-consolidated subsidiary of InMobi Pte. Ltd. To learn more, visit advertising.inmobi.com. Glance Glance is an intelligent shopping agent, redefining the commerce experience. Powered by proprietary agentic intelligence and generative AI, Glance delivers a hyper-personalized consumer experience across mobile and TV — shaping the new era of shopping. Glance is operated by Glance InMobi Pte. Ltd., a non-consolidated subsidiary of global technology leader InMobi, and is backed by Mithril Capital, Google and Jio Platforms. To learn more, visit glance.com. What You’ll Own End-to-end ownership of analytical projects , from problem definition and data exploration to analysis, solution development and adoption. Deep understanding of product and business context , translated into scalable analytical solutions that teams can rely on. Reporting systems that surface signal, not noise , simplifying complex data into clear answers, trends and actions for stakeholders and leadership. Dashboards and self-serve analytics that give Business, Product and Leadership a clear view of performance and the next action to take. Analytics that drive business and product decisions , identifying opportunities, diagnosing problems and measuring the impact of initiatives. Predictive and prescriptive analytics using statistical modelling and ML to anticipate trends, identify risks and support better decisions. Automation of recurring analytical workflows , replacing manual reporting and repetitive analysis with reliable, scalable solutions. Cross-functional delivery , partnering closely with Business, Product, Tech, Finance and Operations to translate business questions into analytical solutions and drive them through to execution. Leverage AI as a force multiplier , using AI models and tooling to accelerate analysis, automate workflows, identify patterns and anomalies, improve forecasting and decision-making, while applying strong analytical judgment to validate and act on the outputs. What We’re Looking For Skills & Attributes Strong stakeholder management , able to align Business, Product, Tech, Finance and Operations around a shared objective and drive initiatives to completion. A strong bias toward execution , turning analysis and recommendations into measurable outcomes rather than stopping at insights or presentations. The ability to manage long-horizon projects and roadmaps while continuing to deliver visible wins along the way. Strong project management skills , capable of independently driving multi-month analytical projects and integrations from problem definition through launch and adoption. Comfort working with large, messy and disconnected datasets , with the ability to identify the patterns that matter for a business decision. The ability to communicate complex analysis clearly and simply , enabling stakeholders to act on findings. A knack for identifying opportunities where one scalable solution, such as an automation, dashboard, data pipeline or model, can solve problems across multiple teams . Genuine curiosity about products, users and business problems , with the ability to quickly understand context and translate it into analytical questions. Strong problem-solving mindset with the ability to move between strategic questions and detailed data investigation . Experience & Tools 4+ years of hands-on experience with SQL, Python, advanced Excel and Tableau or Power BI. 3+ years of experience in a Product Analyst, Business Analyst, Data Analyst, Statistical Analyst or similar analytical role. 3+ years of overall experience in consumer tech, internet, technology, analytics or a data-driven business. A track record of shipping at least three end-to-end analytical solutions , from framing a business problem as a data problem through to a working and sustainable solution. Experience building dashboards, automated reporting pipelines, analytical frameworks or data products used by business stakeholders. Experience with statistical analysis, experimentation, forecasting or predictive modelling is a strong plus. Hands-on experience with Databricks, Tercept, SQL-based data platforms and BI tools is a strong plus. Experience working with large-scale data environments and multiple data sources is preferred.
