As the technical leader for our backend platform, you will define the architecture and technical direction that powers our multi-modal, conversational experiences at scale. You’ll drive foundational investments across orchestration, memory and personalization, context management, tool integrations, APIs, and platform infrastructure, enabling the next generation of AI experiences. Working closely with Product, Design, Frontend, Infrastructure, and Machine Learning teams, you’ll shape the long-term technical strategy, raise the engineering bar across the organization, and build reliable, high-quality experiences that delight millions of Pinners. What you’ll do: Define and drive the technical vision and architecture for the backend systems powering Pinterest Assistant, ensuring they scale reliably as the product reaches millions of users. Lead the design and implementation of the core Assistant platform including orchestration, memory and personalization, context management, APIs, tool integrations, and backend infrastructure. Drive technical strategy, identifying foundational platform investments that unlock new Assistant capabilities while improving developer velocity, reliability, and scalability. Partner closely with Product, Design, Frontend engineers, ML engineers, and Infrastructure teams to build a deeply personalized, multimodal conversational experience. Shape the architecture for AI-powered applications, including retrieval, context assembly, tool execution, memory, and model orchestration, in partnership with ML teams. Establish engineering best practices for production LLM and agent systems, including observability, logging, evals, safety mechanisms, rollout strategies, and incident response. Mentor engineers, raising the technical bar through architecture reviews, technical guidance, and thoughtful engineering leadership. Navigate ambiguity and make pragmatic architectural decisions that balance rapid product iteration with long-term maintainability. Influence technical direction beyond the immediate team by collaborating across teams and helping shape Pinterest’s broader AI platform strategy. Stay current with advances in LLMs, agentic systems, and backend platform design, and apply them pragmatically to consumer product development.
