Technical Business Development Representative

🏢 vercel 📍 Hybrid - San Francisco, New York City, Austin 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Vercel: Vercel is the agentic infrastructure company, freeing people and agents to ship what's next. For more than a decade we've helped builders move from idea to production with speed, security, and exceptional developer experience. Now we're scaling our products for both agents and people to ship and run software, built in the open and trusted by OpenAI, PayPal, Ramp, Supreme, and millions of developers worldwide. About Vercel: Vercel gives developers the tools and cloud infrastructure to build, scale, and secure a faster, more personalized web. As the team behind v0, Next.js, and AI SDK, Vercel helps customers like Ramp, Supreme, PayPal, and Under Armour build for the AI-native web. Our mission is to enable the world ship the best products. That starts with creating a place where everyone can do their best work. Whether you're building on our platform, supporting our customers, or shaping our story: You can just ship things. About the Role: As a Technical Business Development Representative, you'll be the first point of contact for companies that submit inbound leads, and you'll qualify them with personalized outreach to engineering and product leaders. You'll work closely with Account Executives to turn those conversations into pipeline in the commercial segment. In your first 6 to 12 months, you'll learn the SaaS sales cycle directly from sales and product leaders and build the foundation for a career in go-to-market. This is a hybrid role with 3 in-office days per week in San Francisco, New York City, or Austin. What You Will Do: Respond to inbound leads as the first point of contact and qualify them through personalized outreach Research prospects and tailor outreach to engineering and product leaders Partner with Account Executives to build pipeline and drive adoption in the commercial segment Work with GTM Engineering to improve our inbound agent project Collaborate with Marketing, Sales Engineering, and Customer Success to refine messaging and improve outreach effectiveness What You Need: Working knowledge of web development and current web technologies Can explain complex technical concepts to both technical and non-technical audiences Can manage your own prospect pipeline independently while working with cross-functional teams Bonus If You Have: Background in computer science, web development, or a related technical field Hands-on experience with Vercel, Next.js, or another modern web framework Prior sales experience, including Salesforce, Outreach, or similar tools Benefits: Competitive compensation and stock options Inclusive Healthcare Package Learn and Grow - we provide mentorship and send you to events that help you build your network and skills Unlimited PTO - 4 weeks recommended per year. Take time when you need it.
