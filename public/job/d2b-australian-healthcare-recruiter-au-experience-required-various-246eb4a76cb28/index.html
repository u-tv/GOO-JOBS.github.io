D2B: Australian Healthcare Recruiter (AU Experience required)

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Philippines URL: https://jobs.workable.com/company/dcaVtKQqXr9FwBNppNCBH9/jobs-at-d2b Position : Australian Healthcare Recruiter Salary : Up to $1,200 AUD per month Working Hours & Conditions : Standard Australian Business Hours Work Arrangement : Remote ABOUT THE BUSINESS: Join an established, multidisciplinary Australian healthcare provider dedicated to making a genuine difference in the community. With an expanding network of specialized clinics across speech therapy, physiotherapy, NDIS services, and broader allied health disciplines, the group is passionate about delivering exceptional care and building world-class clinical teams. As the organization continues its dynamic growth, we are looking for a proactive Australian Healthcare Recruiter to take full ownership of end-to-end talent acquisition—connecting top-tier clinical professionals with rewarding careers. ROLE OVERVIEW: The Australian Healthcare Recruiter will manage recruitment from vacancy briefing through to candidate placement. Working closely with internal stakeholders, the recruiter will assess hiring needs, attract and evaluate candidates, coordinate selection, and keep all parties informed throughout the process. This role requires proven Australian healthcare recruitment experience, with a strong focus on allied health, NDIS, or clinic-based roles within Australia for Australian operations. RESPONSIBILITIES: Full-Cycle Recruitment Manage end-to-end recruitment for internal healthcare vacancies. Meet with hiring managers to understand role requirements, timelines and candidate priorities. Develop recruitment plans for each vacancy and provide regular progress updates. Source, screen and shortlist suitable candidates. Coordinate interviews and support reference checking, offer coordination and pre-employment steps. Present opportunities clearly to candidates and support them through to acceptance. Maintain responsive communication with candidates and internal stakeholders. Candidate Sourcing & Attraction Source candidates through SEEK, LinkedIn and other relevant job portals. Build and maintain talent pools for current and future vacancies. Use direct sourcing, referrals and professional networks to reach qualified candidates. Develop relationships with universities, colleges and training providers. Explore alternative sourcing channels to reach experienced and emerging talent. Create effective job advertisements and candidate outreach messages. Healthcare & Clinic Recruitment Recruit across relevant healthcare specialties, including speech therapy, physiotherapy, NDIS and other clinic-based roles. Apply knowledge of the Australian healthcare recruitment market and candidate expectations. Check candidate qualifications, experience and relevant registrations or clearances in line with company processes. Escalate qualification, compliance or suitability concerns to the appropriate stakeholder. Stakeholder Service & Recruitment Outcomes Build strong working relationships with clinic managers and internal hiring teams. Provide a responsive, professional recruitment service to stakeholders. Advise hiring teams on candidate availability, sourcing progress and recruitment challenges. Keep recruitment activity, outcomes and follow-up actions accurately recorded. Promote roles effectively and help secure candidate interest throughout the recruitment process. Recruitment Administration & Reporting Maintain accurate candidate, vacancy and recruitment records in the relevant systems. Track vacancy stages, interview outcomes and outstanding actions. Provide recruitment updates and basic pipeline reporting. Handle candidate information confidentially and in accordance with company procedures. Key Requirements Australian healthcare recruitment experience is essential. Demonstrated experience managing the full recruitment lifecycle. Experience using SEEK and multiple job boards or sourcing platforms. Strong candidate screening, interviewing and selection skills. Excellent written and spoken English. Confidence building relationships with candidates and hiring stakeholders. Strong organization and follow-up skills, with the ability to manage multiple vacancies. Proactive approach to sourcing and willingness to use non-traditional channels. Professional judgement and discretion when handling candidate information. Ability to work independently and communicate recruitment progress clearly. Highly Desirable Experience recruiting speech therapists, physiotherapists or NDIS professionals. Previous recruitment experience for healthcare clinics or allied health providers. Relationships with Australian universities, colleges or healthcare training providers. Experience using applicant tracking systems and maintaining talent pools. Confidence presenting roles and engaging candidates through to acceptance. Tools & Systems The recruiter should be comfortable working across multiple sourcing platforms and able to learn the company’s chosen systems. Examples include: Job boards: SEEK, Indeed, Jora, CareerOne and Workforce Australia. Professional sourcing: LinkedIn and LinkedIn Recruiter. Healthcare-specific channels Social and community sourcing: Facebook Groups relevant to allied health, speech pathology, physiotherapy, NDIS and local professional communities. Applicant tracking and recruitment CRM systems: Candidate coordination: Microsoft Teams, Zoom or Google Meet; reference-checking and candidate assessment tools. Recruitment administration and reporting: Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets, Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace.
