Senior Product Designer

🏢 okta 📍 Bengaluru, India 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Secure Every Identity, from AI to Human Identity is the key to unlocking the potential of AI. Okta secures AI by building the trusted, neutral infrastructure that enables organizations to safely embrace this new era. This work requires a relentless drive to solve complex challenges with real-world stakes. We are looking for builders and owners who operate with speed and urgency and execute with excellence. This is an opportunity to do career-defining work. We're all in on this mission. If you are too, let's talk. The Overview At Okta, we're building a world where anyone can safely use any technology, and we're specifically focused on protecting the future of work. We bring simple and secure access to people and organizations everywhere, promising to not only protect the identities of our customers' workforce and users but to ask, “what more can we make possible?” As a Senior Product Designer for Okta, you’ll work with a globally distributed design team to apply your craft and expertise on a range of products and services. Our culture provides the opportunity to explore the technologies we’re researching and investing in. Some of your work will be functional, practical user experience design, where you will help our design team drive cohesive user experience across Okta’s portfolio of products. Other work will be speculative, generative explorations that you carry out in collaboration and partnership across the organization to flesh out, frame, and help prioritize solutions in an ever-evolving and complex, often ambiguous, problem space. Finer print You will join a team of AI first designers and researchers supported by leadership who work collaboratively to deliver delightful solutions to complex identity problems. As a centralized design team, we have a high degree of autonomy and ownership towards the outcomes we drive for our customers. We also work closely with our Product Management and Engineering counterparts to ensure our solutions are not only user-centric but also technically feasible and aligned with business needs. Together, we collaborate to define problems, explore innovative solutions and deliver seamless experiences that enhance security and usability for our customers. What you will do Drive and execute an ambitious product design strategy for your team ensuring a meaningful impact on both user experience and business outcomes. Bring a systems-thinking lens to the conversation and connect the dots between user insights and business goals to create experiences that keep millions of customers safe every day. Drive impact by framing problems and exploring solutions in collaboration with user research, UX writing, product management, engineering, marketing, and data science to deliver best in class experiences. You will have the opportunity to lead through influence. Design end-to-end flows that involve multiple personas – IT Admin, Security admins, End-user – and consider complex use cases that span the Okta portfolio ensuring that every user of Okta can do their work efficiently while staying secure. Ensure a cohesive, responsive experience across all form factors (desktop, mobile web, and dedicated applications), rigorously applying platform-specific design patterns and accessibility standards. Perfect the details to design scalable and usable design patterns that drive a lasting impact, ensuring they not only meet a high bar for design quality and efficiency but also enhance efficiency and consistency across the product. Regularly present work, concepts, and new ideas to cross-functional team members, adjacent teams, and senior leadership to drive alignment, showcase design impact and influence key product decisions. Champion the thoughtful, secure use of AI throughout the design process, including advanced vibe-coding for generative prototyping and efficient cross-functional collaboration. What we are looking for You easily move between big picture thinking and being detail-oriented. You’re curious and have experience incorporating AI into your design process, including the ability to craft tools, iterate on ideas collaboratively with AI, and document sophisticated prompts for scaled use. Evaluate AI-generated outputs for technical accuracy, design system adherence, and ease of use to serve as a necessary quality bar before designs move to production You demonstrate strong expertise in designing responsive, accessible, and inclusive experiences that scale across all device types, from mobile applications to large-scale desktop interfaces. You keep user goals at the heart of your solution and can also balance business objectives and engineering considerations. You thrive in ambiguous environments and know when to lead versus when to lean on your team members. You build and share empathy with people in order to deeply understand them and their needs. You are someone who sees design as a partner with PM and Engineering in shaping strategic and operational product and UX outcomes You are comfortable owning all stages of design for multiple teams within a given product area. You are passionate about upholding design UX principles You showcase story telling skills to communicate your design ideas to a variety of stakeholders. Your portfolio will show that you've worked on complex and challenging technical projects across all aspects of the design process (generative and evaluative research, discovery, interaction design, information architecture, visual design, etc.) You share our values , and work in accordance with those values Qualifications Bachelor's degree in a related field or relevant work experience as a product designer. Proficient in Figma, Miro, Balsamiq and/or similar design tools. 5+ years of experience as a Product Designer or UX Designer, with a portfolio showcasing shipped B2B or enterprise products. Can provide a digital portfolio that showcases solid skills across a variety of projects, including work that has been seen and used successfully in actual products.
