Consultant Relations Manager The Role: Modern Health is seeking an experienced Consultant Relations Manager to build and grow relationships with benefits consulting and brokerage firms across the Southeast Region (USA). In this highly visible role, you will report to and partner closely with the VP of Consultant Relations, serving as the primary connection between Modern Health and the consultant community. As the face of Modern Health to key consulting partners, you will develop trusted relationships that position us as a go-to mental health partner and translate engagement into qualified pipeline. This role is ideal for someone who is proactive, relationship-driven, and comfortable creating opportunities from the ground up, whether through cold outreach or an established network within the consulting space. You will lead both 1:1 and group conversations with consultants, bringing forward insights on mental health trends, client challenges, and how Modern Health can deliver meaningful impact. What You’ll Do: Build and manage strategic relationships with consultant partners across the Southeast region. Proactively generate qualified sales opportunities by driving consultant engagement Serve as the primary liaison between Modern Health and consultant stakeholders, including leaders, consultants, and subject matter experts Develop and execute tailored engagement plans for each consulting firm to drive consistent pipeline and long-term partnership value Conduct targeted outreach, including cold outreach, to establish new relationships and expand Modern Health’s footprint across the region Lead 1:1 meetings and facilitate group sessions with consultants on market trends, client needs, and Modern Health’s capabilities Partner closely with Account Executives and Client Management teams to align on opportunities and strengthen key relationships Collaborate cross-functionally (Growth, Product, Clinical, Operations) to surface insights and enhance consultant engagement Deliver compelling, customized presentations that position Modern Health as a thought leader in mental health and employer benefits Represent Modern Health at industry events and within the consultant ecosystem to build brand presence and credibility Who You Are (Requirements): 10+ years of experience in the employer healthcare/benefits ecosystem, ideally within a consulting/broker firm or in a consultant relations or sales role supporting consultants. Proven track record of building and managing high-impact relationships with benefits consultants and generating measurable pipeline Comfortable with proactive outreach, including cold calling and developing relationships from scratch Strong executive presence with the ability to influence and engage senior stakeholders in both 1:1 and group settings Skilled facilitator, able to lead strategic conversations on mental health trends and client challenges Highly relationship-driven, with strong emotional intelligence and exceptional communication skills Strategic thinker with a deep understanding of the healthcare and benefits landscape, including market dynamics and consultant influence models Highly organized and detail-oriented, with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced, evolving environment. We have a strong preference for applicants who currently live within the Southeast sales Territory, Ideally based in Atlanta, Raleigh, or Charlotte.
