THE ROLE: As a Project Manager, you will manage a full workload of creative projects from inception through to completion, primarily standard-complexity, well-defined work. This includes identifying project scope, clarifying deliverables, anticipating needs, understanding timelines, and managing client and internal communications. You’ll need to be comfortable in the weeds, specifically helping to drive day-to-day deliverables, and ready to flag or hand off projects that grow beyond standard complexity. WHAT YOU’LL DO: Own and manage a workload of standard-complexity projects Scope projects and gathering requirements Managing projects end to end, flagging anything that grows in complexity or risk Following and reinforcing established project management processes Building, maintaining, and executing on timelines Setting up projects and managing them within Asana Vetting and analyzing project feedback Collaborating on detailed creative briefs Updating and maintaining project tracking Act as day-to-day liaison with clients on assigned projects Tracking of each project’s status; maintaining an intimate knowledge of the project’s health WHAT YOU BRING: 2-4 years experience in the project management field for an agency or on a creative team Strong working knowledge of digital creative and social media creative Great communication, people, leadership, organizational, and project/time management skills The ability to work both independently and in a team environment The ability to prioritize and consistently handle multiple tasks across multiple clients and teams Flexibility when it comes to changing priorities, while still meeting deadlines Attention to detail, while simultaneously seeing the bigger picture A willingness and ability to learn A positive attitude when executing tasks across accounts Additional things that will impress us: Experience with Asana (or similar project management software) a plus Strong Working knowledge of PowerPoint, Google Suite Experience in Slack or similar platform Proficiency in the following programs/tools: Google Suite (Slides, Sheets, Docs) Microsoft Suite (PowerPoint, Word, Excel) Mac/PC platform literate* Asana Software (not required but a plus) WHAT DO WE OFFER?
