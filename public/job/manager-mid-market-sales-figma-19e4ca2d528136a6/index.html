Figma is looking for a Manager, Mid-Market Sales to lead a team of Account Executives responsible for renewing, growing, and expanding a large book of Mid-Market customers. Mid-Market at Figma focuses on organizations with fewer than 5,000 employees on Figma's Organization plan. These customers have meaningful spend, real whitespace, and a big opportunity to adopt Figma's AI products. Mid-Market is a newly dedicated segment, and you'll help build it: refining a repeatable sales motion, coaching Account Executives early in their careers, and turning customer signals into revenue. You will report to the Director, Mid-Market Sales. This is a full time role that can be held from one of our US hubs, either San Francisco or New York. What you'll do at Figma: Lead, coach, and develop a team of Account Executives, each managing roughly 50 Mid-Market customers, building a culture of high standards, care, and direct feedback Run weekly forecasting, pipeline reviews, and deal inspection with a high bar for forecast accuracy and data-informed decisions Partner with the Director to test, refine, and scale the Mid-Market sales motion, from qualifying opportunities to navigating technical evaluations Turn product usage, AI adoption, and account health signals into timely account engagement that protects renewals, reduces churn and contraction, and drives expansion Drive adoption of Figma's AI products by helping Account Executives connect new workflows to meaningful business value Coach consultative, value-based selling and MEDDICC qualification, joining key customer conversations and deal cycles alongside your team Hire, onboard, and ramp new Account Executives, developing their skills and preparing them for opportunities in Emerging Enterprise and Enterprise sales Partner with Solutions Consulting, Customer Success, Marketing, Sales Operations, and Deal Desk to align account plans and go-to-market execution, sharing field insights with leadership to improve the segment's approach We'd love to hear from you if you have: 6+ years of B2B SaaS sales experience, including a track record of closing new business and expansion 2+ years managing quota-carrying Account Executives, ideally in Mid-Market or Commercial, with consistent achievement of team revenue targets Experience leading a team that owns both renewals and expansion across a large book of customers Strong command of forecasting and pipeline management, with comfort using data to guide decisions Experience selling to technical audiences, including Product, Design, and Engineering teams Fluency in MEDDICC or a similar qualification framework, with a practical approach to coaching consultative, value-based selling Experience hiring and developing early-career Account Executives and preparing them for their next role A builder's mindset, comfort testing and improving a playbook, and hands-on use of AI tools, with curiosity about how AI is changing the way software is built and sold While it's not required, it's an added plus if you also have: Experience selling usage- or consumption-based products alongside seat-based subscriptions Familiarity with Figma, or with design, UX, or developer tooling Experience helping stand up or reshape a sales segment Training in Command of the Message or a similar value-selling methodology At Figma, one of our values is Grow as you go.
