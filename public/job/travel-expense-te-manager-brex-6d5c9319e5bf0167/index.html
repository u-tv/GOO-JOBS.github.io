Travel & Expense (T&E) Manager

🏢 brex 📍 United States 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Why join us Brex is the intelligent finance platform that enables companies to spend smarter and move faster in more than 200 markets. By combining global corporate cards and banking with intuitive spend management, bill pay, and travel software, Brex enables founders and finance teams to accelerate operations, gain real-time visibility, and control spend effortlessly. Brex’s AI-native automation and world-class service eliminate manual expense and accounting tasks for customers so they can focus on what matters most. Tens of thousands of the world's best companies run on Brex, including DoorDash, Coinbase, Robinhood, Zoom, Plaid, Reddit, and SeatGeek. Working at Brex allows you to push your limits, challenge the status quo, and collaborate with some of the brightest minds in the industry. We’re committed to building a diverse team and inclusive culture and believe your potential should only be limited by how big you can dream. We make this a reality by empowering you with the tools, resources, and support you need to grow your career. Accounting at Brex The Accounting team at Brex ensures we produce accurate and timely financial results to stakeholders to guide the business forward. We go beyond closing the books — we automate, iterate, and improve processes in close collaboration with partners across the business. The team also serves as power users and thought leaders for Brex's own products. We bring precision and curiosity to everything we build, combining deep ownership with a product mindset to help shape the future of finance. What you’ll do We are seeking a detail-oriented and analytical Travel & Expense (T&E) Manager to lead, optimize, and scale our global corporate travel and expense program. In this role, you will be the absolute owner of our T&E policies, corporate card management, and automated expense systems within the Brex product. Your primary goal will be to balance strict financial compliance and cost control with a seamless, user-friendly experience for our employees .The T&E Manager is expected to serve as the primary subject matter expert for all internal inquiries regarding travel policies and expense guidelines. You will also partner closely with many cross functional teams, including our Engineering, Product, and Design teams on Brex product features. Where you’ll work This role will be based in our San Francisco office. We are a hybrid environment that combines the energy and connections of being in the office with the benefits and flexibility of working from home. We currently require a minimum of three coordinated days in the office per week, Monday, Wednesday and Thursday. As a perk, we also have up to four weeks per year of fully remote work! Responsibilities Oversee employee access to the Brex platform (onboarding, offboarding, and limit provisioning) Maintain the company expense policy, related FAQ documents, and employee education regarding the T&E program Review, audit, and approve employee expenses to ensure compliance with the company expense policy Oversee employee travel bookings in the Brex Travel product Partner with the People team on Brex employee events where travel is required Partner with the Procurement team regarding allowable non-T&E spend on Brex Work with the FP&A team and budget owners bi-annually to update the budget structure within Brex and educate owners on best practices Partner closely with the AP (Accounts Payable) Sr. Accountant on accounting rules for Brex transactions Work with internal Engineering, Product, and Design teams to test and provide feedback on Brex product features Requirements 5+ years of experience in corporate finance, accounting operations, or procurement 2+ years managing T&E programs Exceptional communication skills with the ability to firmly enforce policy while maintaining a supportive, customer-service-driven attitude. Solid understanding of IRS corporate travel regulations, global VAT/GST reclaim processes, and SOX compliance controls Ability to work independently and manage deadlines effectively Ability to work in a fast-paced environment and address difficult situations in a professional manner Strong interpersonal and written communication skills Bonus Points Prior experience with Brex Knowledge and experience using AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude and/or Google Gemini Knowledge and experience using NetSuite or Workday ERPs Compensation The expected salary range for this role is $150,968 - $188,710. However, the starting base pay will depend on a number of factors including the candidate’s location, skills, experience, market demands, and internal pay parity.
