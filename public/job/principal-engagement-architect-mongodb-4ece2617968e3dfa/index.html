Principal Engagement Architect

🏢 mongodb 📍 New York City 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About the role Principal Engagement Architects decide how MongoDB delivers a modernization, migration, or AI application program, before the SOW is signed. You’ll partner with Engagement Managers and account teams to choose the delivery path, size the work, and make the technical case to customer architects and executives. You’ll own the shape of the solution: the architecture, delivery model, scope, and estimate behind it. You’ll also turn what works into standards, so more customers move forward faster without reinventing the wheel. You’ll work across Sales, Professional Services, and Customer Success to help customers adopt MongoDB, modernize applications, and move into the AI era with confidence. Every customer has a different goal. You’ll sit with their engineers, architects, and executives, work out what they’re really trying to do, design a path to get there, and win the work. We are looking to speak to candidates who are based in New York City, NY for our hybrid working model. What you’ll do Partner with software sellers on account strategies and plays that drive MongoDB adoption and growth across a broad set of customers Learn each customer’s applications, data, IT and business priorities, and success measures, then design modernization and transformation approaches built on MongoDB Own services deals from first conversation to close: turn use cases and pain points into scoped projects and SOWs, and negotiate them Explain the business value of services credibly, including delivery approach, challenges, and risk reduction Build and share repeatable assets such as estimates, proposals, SOWs, templates, and case studies. Use regional performance data to decide where to focus next Manage the regional Professional Services pipeline and forecast bookings and revenue accurately Coach sellers to run stronger discovery and bring services into more deals Hand off scope and expectations cleanly to the delivery team Get the most out of AI tools, while still deeply connecting with customers on you Mentor teammates and share what works What you bring Experience with the software development lifecycle, application modernization, AI and agentic development, and databases (RDBMS, SQL, NoSQL), so you can talk with customer engineers and explain technical goals to business buyers A track record of solutioning and selling technical solutions in enterprise software, influencing deals from design through kickoff and beyond Working knowledge of cloud and on-premises architecture (AWS, GCP, Azure, Kubernetes, OpenShift) and delivery methodologies (Agile, Waterfall) Proven ability to scope and estimate projects, build internal champions, and work with account teams on complex sales plays The ability to cast a vision that gets customers ready to say yes and gets delivery teams ready for a smooth onboarding Strong presentation and storytelling skills, in slides and in writing. SOW authoring experience is a plus A BS in Computer Science, Mathematics, Engineering, or equivalent professional experience Willingness to travel for key engagements and team offsites Why this team If you’re tired of working the same problem day in and day out, this is for you. You’ll work with more customers, see more complex problems, and help them set a path to their goals.
