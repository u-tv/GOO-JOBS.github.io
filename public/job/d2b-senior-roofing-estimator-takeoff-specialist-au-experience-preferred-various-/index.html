D2B: Senior Roofing Estimator & Takeoff Specialist (AU Experience preferred)

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Manila, Metro Manila, Philippines URL: https://jobs.workable.com/company/dcaVtKQqXr9FwBNppNCBH9/jobs-at-d2b Position : Senior Roofing Estimator & Takeoff Specialist Salary : Up to AUD 2,200 - 2,500 per month Working Hours & Conditions : Full time, aligned with Australian business hours Work Arrangement : Remote ABOUT THE COMPANY: Join a well-established Australian roofing company specializing in high-quality metal roofing solutions across Adelaide and the wider South Australian region. With a strong reputation in the residential and commercial roofing sector, the business has grown from a founder-led operation into a trusted partner for homeowners, builders, private developers, and commercial clients. Supported by a close-knit team of skilled roofing professionals, the company takes pride in delivering reliable workmanship, quality service, and practical roofing solutions. Fully licensed and insured, and affiliated with Master Builders South Australia, this is an opportunity to become part of a growing business with a solid industry foundation and a commitment to continued growth. ROLE OVERVIEW: We are seeking an experienced Senior Roofing Estimator & Takeoff Specialist to join an established Australian roofing business. This is a full-time, long-term opportunity for a skilled estimator who understands Australian roofing systems, construction plans, materials and pricing requirements. You will be responsible for reviewing construction drawings, completing accurate roofing takeoffs and preparing detailed cost estimates and quotations. The business owner will provide plans and quote requests, and you will independently prepare estimates for review and final submission to clients. We are looking for someone who can hit the ground running, work with minimal supervision and take ownership of the estimating process. RESPONSIBILITIES: Roofing Estimating & Takeoffs Review architectural drawings, roofing plans, specifications and tender documentation. Complete accurate roofing measurements and material quantity takeoffs. Prepare detailed cost estimates for residential and commercial roofing projects. Calculate roofing materials, labour requirements and associated project costs. Identify roofing specifications, materials, flashing, guttering and other relevant components. Prepare clear, accurate and professional quotation documentation. Identify discrepancies or missing information in drawings and clarify requirements before finalizing estimates. Project Coordination & Communication Manage incoming quote requests and prioritise estimating workloads. Liaise directly with the business owner to clarify project scope and requirements. Deliver estimates within agreed deadlines. Maintain organized records of takeoffs, estimates and project documentation. Potentially assist with client communication and quote follow-ups as the role develops. What We're Looking For: We are looking for a senior, experienced roofing estimator rather than a junior candidate requiring extensive training. The ideal person will: Have hands-on experience estimating roofing projects for Australian clients. Be confident in interpreting plans and producing accurate takeoffs independently. Understand roofing materials, installation requirements and the importance of accurate quantities. Be proactive in identifying missing details and asking the right questions. Take ownership of quotations from initial plan review through to completed estimates. Be reliable, organized and comfortable working directly with an Australian business owner. Key Requirements: Minimum 2 years of proven Australian roofing estimating experience. Strong understanding of Australian roofing materials, systems, terminology and construction practices. Demonstrated experience completing roofing takeoffs from architectural and construction plans. Ability to prepare accurate material quantities, cost estimates and quotations. Experience with estimating software, digital takeoff tools and Microsoft Excel. Strong attention to detail and numerical accuracy. Excellent written and spoken English. Ability to work independently with minimal supervision. Strong time management and ability to manage multiple quotations simultaneously. Highly Desirable: Experience estimating metal roofing, roof replacements and new roofing installations. Familiarity with Australian roofing standards and industry practices. Experience using Bluebeam, Plan Swift or similar digital takeoff software. Previous experience working directly with Australian roofing contractors. Client-facing experience, including communicating with builders, contractors or customers. Experience estimating both residential and commercial roofing projects. What We Offer: Full-time, stable remote employment. Competitive monthly salary of AUD $2200 - $2500, depending on experience. Opportunity to work directly with an established Australian roofing business. Autonomy and responsibility in managing the estimating workload. Potential to expand into client communication and broader project support over time.
