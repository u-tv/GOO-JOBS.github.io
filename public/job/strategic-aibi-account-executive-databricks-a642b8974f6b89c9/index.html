FEQ327R126 Databricks is seeking a Genie + AI/BI Sales Specialist to help enterprise customers transform how business users interact with data. This high-impact role sits within the AI Go-To-Market team and partners closely with Enterprise Account Executives to drive adoption of Databricks AI/BI and Genie in APJ. You will help organizations move beyond static dashboards to governed, conversational, AI-powered analytics at the center of the convergence of business intelligence, data platforms, and generative AI. Enterprise analytics is rapidly evolving from dashboards and static reporting to conversational, AI-driven decision platforms. Databricks AI/BI and Genie empower business users to securely interact with governed data using natural language, transforming the data platform into a true decision platform. If you want to be at the forefront of AI-powered analytics transformation at one of the fastest-growing data and AI companies in the world, this is your opportunity.
