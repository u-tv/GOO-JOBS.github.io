Come and See: Product Engineer, Full Stack

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Colorado Springs, Colorado, United States URL: https://comeandsee.bamboohr.com/careers JOIN THE COME AND SEE TEAM Product Engineer, Full Stack Great stories only matter when they reach people, and you build the way they get there. If you love shipping software that holds up in production and see engineering as a way to serve people well – we’d love to meet you. ABOUT US Come and See is the faith-based nonprofit media ministry behind The Chosen . Our mission is inviting everyone to encounter Jesus through exceptional storytelling that brings the Bible to life. Our Team Members work together as collaborators in the greatest mission on earth. ABOUT THE ROLE The Product Engineer, Full Stack will build and ship features across our streaming apps on web, mobile, and connected TV, the platform services behind them, and the tools our partners rely on, working with Product and Design from user problem to production. This is a full-time, remote role open to candidates in the USA. WHAT YOU’LL DO Start and end with the user. Partner with Product, Design, and stakeholders to understand user problems before building, and watch how features perform after launch so you can improve them. Shape what gets built. Contribute to discovery, refinement, and technical planning, and break features into small deliveries that put value in front of users early. Build across the stack. Design, build, and maintain features across web, mobile, connected TV, and backend services, building depth in new areas along the way. Write code others can change. Deliver clear, well-tested, maintainable code to our standards, and give and receive code review feedback that raises the whole team’s work. Own delivery and release. Run CI/CD pipelines, automated tests, deployment, and release readiness so changes ship with confidence. Make problems visible early. Build observability into what ships, and join incident response and root-cause analysis through to the fix. Pay down technical debt. Address debt that affects product quality, reliability, or delivery speed. Own your AI-assisted work. Use AI tools across coding, testing, debugging, and documentation, validate the output as rigorously as hand-written code, and share with the team where AI helps and where it doesn’t. WHAT YOU’LL BRING 3–5 years building and shipping production software Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field, or 4+ years of equivalent professional experience Strong TypeScript experience across client applications, backend services, and the pipelines that ship them Depth in frontend, mobile, or backend development, with willingness to work across the full range Familiarity with cloud infrastructure, containerized deployments, and CI/CD tooling Comfort across the full product and software life cycle, and ownership of work after it ships A personal faith in Jesus and alignment with our mission, values, and Statement of Faith BONUS POINTS: Mobile development (Kotlin or Swift), connected TV platform experience, nonprofit or ministry/faith-based organizational experience BENEFITS: Medical, dental, vision, retirement match, HRA, disability and life insurance, generous and flexible time off, holidays, parental leave, and more. OUR BELIEFS, CULTURE, AND COMMITMENT At Come and See, every staff member is a critical and valuable part of our mission and ministry. We consider ministry readiness and an individual’s capacity to represent our culture and Christian beliefs during the selection process for all staff positions. An essential function within every position held by a staff member at Come and See is to uphold and represent Jesus in how we live and behave. While we unite around our mission, we know unity doesn’t mean uniformity. Our calling is too great, and our mission is too important not to be intentional about strengthening our team with people from all backgrounds. We believe that varied perspectives—across race, ethnicity, life experience, age, and gender—enrich our ability to share the story of Jesus with the world and help everyone, everywhere, experience the authentic Jesus. Come and See is an equal opportunity employer and as a religious organization, lawfully exercises its right under Title VII and applicable state law to employ individuals who share its religious beliefs.
