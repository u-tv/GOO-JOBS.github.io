Celebrations Speech Group Inc.: School Staffing Coordinator

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: United States URL: https://www.celebrationspeechgroup.com Benefits: Flexible schedule Opportunity for advancement School Staffing Coordinator / Consultant Independent Contractor (1099) | Commission-Based Opportunity Celebrations Speech Group Inc. is a pediatric therapy and education organization providing services to children and school communities throughout California. Our School Staffing Division partners with school districts to connect students with qualified special education and related-service professionals. We are seeking an experienced School Staffing Coordinator / Consultant to independently develop school district staffing opportunities and support successful placements. This opportunity is best suited for an established recruiting, staffing, education, or business development professional who already understands school district operations and can independently manage relationships and placement activity from initial outreach through successful assignment launch. What You'll Do As an independent School Staffing Consultant, services may include: • Identify and develop staffing opportunities with public, charter, and other school partners. • Establish and maintain professional relationships with school district administrators, special education departments, and other decision-makers. • Develop prospective school partnerships and support the process of securing staffing agreements. • Recruit qualified professionals for school-based opportunities, including speech-language pathologists, SLPAs, occupational therapists, physical therapists, special education professionals, and other related-service providers. • Source, screen, and present qualified candidates for available school assignments. • Coordinate placement information between candidates, school partners, and CSG. • Partner with CSG Human Resources to facilitate credentialing and onboarding requirements for selected candidates. • Monitor placement progress and communicate issues that may affect a successful assignment launch. • Maintain appropriate records regarding active opportunities, candidate activity, placements, and school partnerships. • Support continued communication with school partners and placed professionals as reasonably necessary for successful staffing relationships. Compensation This is an independent contractor opportunity with commission-based compensation . Compensation is tied to successful school staffing placements and qualifying active assignments. There is no guaranteed salary, hourly wage, minimum number of placements, or minimum level of compensation. The specific commission structure, payment milestones, eligibility requirements, and payment terms will be provided in writing as part of the independent contractor agreement before services begin. Earning potential will vary based on successful placement activity, contract value, and continuation of qualifying assignments. What We're Looking For Required: • Prior experience working within a school district is required. Applicants without direct school district experience will not be considered. • Strong understanding of how school districts identify and fill staffing needs. • Demonstrated ability to develop professional relationships and independently move opportunities forward. • Strong communication, organization, and follow-through. • Ability to independently manage recruiting and business development activity. • Ability to maintain confidential candidate, district, and business information. Strongly Preferred: • Experience in special education, related services, school staffing, or educational recruiting. • Existing professional relationships within California school districts or educational organizations. • Experience recruiting licensed or credentialed clinicians and educators. • Familiarity with school-based credentialing, compliance, and onboarding requirements. • Experience with district contracts, staffing agreements, proposals, or similar business development activities. Independent Contractor Relationship This opportunity is structured for an independent professional providing services to Celebrations Speech Group as a 1099 contractor and is not an employee position. The contractor will maintain discretion over the manner and means of providing contracted services, subject to agreed-upon deliverables, placement requirements, contractual obligations, and applicable legal and confidentiality requirements. The contractor may provide similar services to other businesses and is responsible for maintaining their own business operations, equipment, expenses, taxes, and applicable registrations or licenses. No minimum number of hours, school contracts, candidates, placements, or assignments is guaranteed. Ideal Fit You may be a strong fit if you: • Already understand school district operations, staffing processes, and special education environments, with direct prior experience working within a school district. • Are comfortable with both relationship development and recruiting. • Enjoy building partnerships and turning staffing needs into successful placements. • Are highly organized and responsive without needing day-to-day supervision. • Have an established professional network or know how to independently develop one. • Want compensation tied directly to successful results rather than a traditional hourly schedule. If you have experience building school partnerships and placing qualified professionals into educational settings, we would love to hear from you! This is a remote position.
