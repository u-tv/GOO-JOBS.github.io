Natera: Principal Software Engineer in Test

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: US Remote POSITION SUMMARY Natera is seeking a highly technical, hands-on Principal Software Engineer in Test to define and own the end-to-end test automation strategy across all products in the Women's Health, Organ Health, and EB (WH/OH/EB) vertical. In this role, you will design, build, and maintain scalable backend automation suites to validate critical workflows across distributed microservice architectures, guaranteeing data integrity from order entry and lab processing to clinical reporting. You will drive innovation by leveraging AI-assisted practices and large language models to automate test case generation, streamline log analysis, and enhance CI/CD execution speed. As a Principal level individual contributor, you will set technical direction for quality engineering across multiple teams. You will partner with engineering, bioinformatics, laboratory operations, and Quality Assurance and Regulatory Affairs (QA/RA) leaders, and mentor engineers to build quality into the development process instead of inspecting it at the end. As a principal-level individual contributor, you will set technical direction and serve as a key organizational voice across teams. You will leverage strong interpersonal skills, clear communication, and deep technical credibility to align engineering groups, establish architectural standards, and elevate quality practices through peer leadership and technical influence. PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITIES Architect, build, and maintain a shared, reusable end-to-end test automation framework covering UI, API, event-driven/messaging, data pipeline, and integration testing across complex distributed systems. Integrate automated testing into the GitLab CI/CD pipelines so that tests run on every change, report clearly, and block releases on regression. Design and stand up ephemeral and persistent test environments, along with service virtualization and mocking for upstream and downstream dependencies. Implement tools and procedures to maintain persistent test environments including, but not limited to, configuration settings, infrastructure updates and removal of deprecated services or tools. Establish test data management practices, including synthetic and de-identified data, version-controlled fixtures, and automated loading of golden datasets into pipelines under test. Partner with bioinformatics scientists and verification and validation teams to automate execution of golden-dataset regression suites against bioinformatics pipelines and to track concordance over time. Ensure automated testing produces objective evidence that satisfies design control and regulatory requirements, including requirements-to-test traceability, verification reports, and audit-ready records. Drive non-functional testing strategy: performance, load, reliability, and security testing in collaboration with platform and security teams. Set coding standards, review practices, and patterns for test code, and evaluate and introduce new tools where they materially improve speed or coverage. Mentor Software Engineers in Test and developers; lead communities of practice and help teams adopt shift-left testing. Analyze defect trends and escaped defects, lead root cause analysis, and drive systemic improvements. Act as the senior technical voice for quality in planning, design reviews, and release decisions. Perform debugging and root cause analysis of product issues or defects. Perform system analysis, assess risk, and develop strong test strategies by analyzing product design and technical specifications, and by collaborating with product managers and developers. QUALIFICATIONS BS in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related discipline, or equivalent work experience. 15+ years of hands-on experience in software development or test automation, with a strong track record of building and scaling test frameworks for enterprise-grade distributed systems.. Demonstrated track record of designing and leading adoption of a test automation framework across multiple teams or products, not just contributing to one. Experience testing complex, distributed, multi-service systems end to end, including asynchronous and event-driven architectures. Experience working in a regulated environment (CLIA/CAP laboratory, medical device, pharma, or similar) is strongly preferred. KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS, AND ABILITIES Expert-level programming skills in Java and/or Python, and the ability to write production-quality test code. Deep hands-on experience with modern automation tools and libraries, such as Playwright, Selenium, JUnit/TestNG, pytest, REST Assured, and contract testing (for example, Pact). Experience testing Kafka or other messaging systems, REST/gRPC APIs, and data pipelines. Strong CI/CD experience (GitLab CI preferred), plus Docker and Kubernetes, and infrastructure-as-code for test environments. Experience with AWS and cloud-native test environment design. Strong SQL skills and experience validating data integrity across systems. Strong knowledge of Linux and command-line scripting. Familiarity with IEC 62304, ISO 13485, FDA 21 CFR Part 11 and Part 820, and computer system validation concepts. Experience with test management and traceability tools (for example, Jama, Jira/Xray, TestRail). Exposure to bioinformatics pipelines, LIMS, or genomic data formats (FASTQ, BAM, VCF) is a plus; no biology background required. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to influence engineering leaders and explain quality risk to non-technical stakeholders. Pragmatic judgment about where automation pays off and where it does not. Solid understanding of QA methodologies, test planning, system dependencies, and product integration phases. #LI-DNI The pay range is listed and actual compensation packages are based on a wide array of factors unique to each candidate, including but not limited to skill set, years & depth of experience, certifications and specific office location.
