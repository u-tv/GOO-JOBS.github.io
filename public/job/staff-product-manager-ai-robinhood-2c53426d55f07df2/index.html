Staff Product Manager, AI

🏢 robinhood 📍 Menlo Park, CA 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Join us in building the future of finance. Our mission is to democratize finance for all. An estimated $124 trillion of assets will be inherited by younger generations in the next two decades. The largest transfer of wealth in human history. If you’re ready to be at the epicenter of this historic cultural and financial shift, keep reading. About the team + role We are building an elite team, applying frontier technologies to the world’s biggest financial problems. We’re looking for bold thinkers. Sharp problem-solvers. Builders who are wired to make an impact. Robinhood isn’t a place for complacency, it’s where ambitious people do the best work of their careers. We’re a high-performing, fast-moving team with ethics at the center of everything we do. Expectations are high, and so are the rewards. Our AI team builds the models, agents, and platforms behind Robinhood's products and the tools our employees use every day. We work in small groups where product, engineering, and research sit together and ship in weeks, not quarters. We partner closely with design, data science, legal, and compliance, because financial products need to be useful and safe. We value clear writing, direct feedback, and decisions backed by data. It's an exciting time to join us! As a Staff Product Manager, AI, you will set the product direction for a core AI area, from the first problem statement through launch and measurement. You will decide which problems are worth solving, write clear specs, and define how we know a model or agent is working, including quality, speed, cost, and safety. Every day, you will work with engineers, researchers, designers, and data scientists to turn early prototypes into products customers trust. You will also coach other product managers and raise the bar on how the team plans and evaluates AI work. We can't wait to see what you build! This role is based in our Menlo Park, CA office, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week. At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams. What you’ll do Own the strategy and roadmap for an AI product area, and explain the plan clearly to senior leaders in writing and in person Turn open-ended problems into specs with goals, success metrics, and launch criteria Define how AI features are evaluated, including accuracy, response time, cost per request, and safety, and work with engineers and researchers to improve each one Ship in small steps with engineering, design, and data science, and learn from how customers use each release Partner with legal, compliance, and security so AI features meet financial regulations and Robinhood's standards Track product metrics and run experiments, then use the results to decide what to build, change, or stop Mentor other product managers and help set shared standards for AI
