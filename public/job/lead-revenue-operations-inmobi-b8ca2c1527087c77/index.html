InMobi Advertising is a global technology leader helping marketers win the moments that matter. Trusted by 30,000+ brands and leading publishers, InMobi is where intelligence, creativity, and accountability converge. By combining lock screens, apps, TVs, and the open web with AI and machine learning, we deliver receptive attention, precise personalization, and measurable impact. Through Glance AI, we are shaping AI Commerce, reimagining the future of e-commerce with inspiration-led discovery and shopping. Designed to seamlessly integrate into everyday consumer technology, Glance AI transforms every screen into a gateway for instant, personal, and joyful discovery. Spanning diverse categories such as fashion, beauty, travel, accessories, home décor, pets, and beyond, Glance AI delivers deeply personalized shopping experiences. With rich first-party data and unparalleled consumer access, it harnesses InMobi’s global scale, insights, and targeting capabilities to create high impact, performance driven shopping journeys for brands worldwide. Recognized as a Great Place to Work, and by MIT Technology Review, Fast Company’s Top 10 Innovators, and more, InMobi is a workplace where bold ideas create global impact. Backed by investors including SoftBank, Kleiner Perkins, and Sherpalo Ventures, InMobi has offices across San Mateo, New York, London, Singapore, Tokyo, Seoul, Jakarta, Bengaluru and beyond. At InMobi Advertising , you’ll have the opportunity to shape how billions of users connect with content, commerce, and brands worldwide. To learn more, visit www.inmobi.com Key Responsibilities 1. Strategic Planning & Forecasting Lead the top-down and bottoms-up planning cycles (AOP/AFP) for Media Flow (MF), Net Revenue (NR), and Take Rate across products (Contracted OX, PX, Auction Packages, Brand IO). Build and maintain dynamic forecasting models leveraging Data Cloud and Einstein AI for predictive insights, highlighting gaps to targets and driving corrective actions. Partner with Finance and Product to translate business strategy into actionable revenue plans by region, deal type, and vertical. 2. Revenue Intelligence & Insights Own the design and execution of the Revenue Intelligence pyramid (Input → Leading Indicators → MF/NR outcomes). Deliver account-level and deal-level intelligence dashboards for GTM teams, integrating data from Salesforce, OMS, and external signals. Build playbooks for Hunter–Farmer–CSM segmentation , new business vs. expansion, and win-loss analysis. 3. GTM Process Design & Governance Own the lead-to-cash process mapping (MECE framework), ensuring clean lead capture, MQL/SQL definitions, and pipeline hygiene. Champion Salesforce discipline and process adoption — “If it’s not in Salesforce, it doesn’t exist.” Drive deal desk governance to ensure pricing discipline, margin protection, and accelerated deal cycles. Qualifications & Experience 5-7+ years of experience in Revenue Operations, GTM Strategy, or Sales Operations in AdTech, SaaS, or Media. Proven success leading end-to-end revenue planning and forecasting for a global P&L. Deep expertise with Salesforce (Sales/Service Cloud) , BI tools (PowerBI, Tableau), and financial modeling. Strong understanding of digital advertising business models – Open Exchange, Private Exchange, Managed Service, and Programmatic. Exceptional stakeholder management skills, with the ability to influence C-level executives. Data-driven, systems thinker with bias for action and a “no manual Excel” mindset . Experience building and leading small but impactful RevOps/Analytics teams.
