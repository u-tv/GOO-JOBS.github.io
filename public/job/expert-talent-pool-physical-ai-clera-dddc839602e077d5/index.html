Job Description

About the Role Lead physical AI data capture programs that help robots learn from human movement and manipulation. You will set up capture systems, guide demonstrations, and uphold data quality across projects ranging from one week to several months. What You'll Do Set up and maintain teleoperation rigs using VR controllers, leader arms, and ROS or ROS 2, ensuring consistent demonstrations. Design egocentric and manipulation capture systems, including camera placement, task scripts, and quality assurance protocols. Operate motion capture and sensor systems such as OptiTrack, Vicon, IMUs, and LiDAR, and troubleshoot hardware in the field. Plan scene recording, calibration, and data collection across multiple sites. Design robot evaluation tasks, define success criteria, and develop scoring systems. Train, mentor, and review operators and data capture technicians. What We're Looking For At least five years of hands-on experience in robotics operations, teleoperation, motion capture, sensor capture, lab work, autonomous vehicle testing, or precision hands-on trades. Practical robotics or physical AI data capture experience, such as teleoperation, egocentric or manipulation recording, or robot evaluation. Experience setting up and troubleshooting capture rigs, including ROS or ROS 2, VR teleoperation, motion capture systems, cameras, LiDAR, or IMUs. Ability to design or run capture sessions with defined protocols and quality checks. Fine manipulation skills and the ability to record remotely with provided equipment or travel for on-site projects; experience leading or training operators and running robot evaluations is useful. Compensation & Benefits $40 to $90 per hour, depending on project, field, and experience. Pay is for hours worked. Location Remote projects, with some on-site work. Some projects can be completed from home or your workplace using provided equipment. On-site locations vary by project.
