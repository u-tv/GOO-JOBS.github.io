Jobgether: Email & CRM Marketing Specialist

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Estonia URL: http://jobgether.com This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, which manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for an Email & CRM Marketing Specialist based in Estonia. As an Email & CRM Marketing Specialist, you will take ownership of outbound and lifecycle marketing communications, helping drive customer engagement, retention, and long-term loyalty across B2C audiences. You will manage and optimize multi-channel campaigns using Customer.io, including email, SMS, push notifications, and in-app messaging. Working with customer behavior data, you will develop targeted segmentation strategies and personalized communication journeys adapted to different markets. You will continuously experiment with messaging, creative formats, and delivery channels to improve campaign performance and customer experience. Collaborating with marketing, design, and content teams, you will turn customer insights into effective communication strategies. This is an exciting opportunity to join an international, collaborative, and autonomous CRM team where experimentation, innovation, and professional growth are encouraged. Accountabilities: Campaign Management: Plan, build, execute, and optimize multi-channel CRM campaigns across email, SMS, push notifications, and in-app messaging to strengthen customer engagement, activation, and retention. Lifecycle Marketing: Develop and manage communication strategies for existing customers, creating relevant touchpoints throughout the customer journey. Customer Segmentation and Personalization: Analyze customer behavior and segment databases to deliver targeted, personalized communications based on user preferences, activity, and engagement patterns across different markets. Testing and Optimization: Continuously conduct A/B tests and experiments involving messaging, copywriting, design, audience segments, timing, and communication channels to improve campaign effectiveness. Performance Monitoring and Reporting: Track and analyze key marketing performance indicators, including open rates, click-through rates, engagement, and retention metrics. Prepare regular performance reports and recommend actionable improvements. Deliverability Management: Monitor email deliverability, maintain sender reputation, and implement best practices to ensure successful campaign delivery. CRM Platform Management: Use Customer.io and other customer engagement technologies to manage automated workflows, campaign execution, audience segmentation, and lifecycle communications. Cross-Functional Collaboration: Work closely with marketing, design, and content teams to develop consistent messaging and deliver coordinated CRM initiatives. Continuous Improvement: Stay informed about digital marketing trends, emerging technologies, and AI-powered marketing solutions to identify opportunities for innovation and improved campaign performance. Requirements: At least 3 years of professional experience as a CRM Manager, Email Marketing Manager, or in a similar B2C CRM marketing position. Previous experience in the iGaming industry is considered a strong advantage. Strong understanding of marketing performance metrics and customer engagement indicators across the entire customer lifecycle. Hands-on experience with customer engagement platforms, preferably Customer.io, and the ability to manage automated campaigns and communication workflows. Good understanding of customer segmentation, audience targeting, logical operators, and personalized communication strategies. Strong technical knowledge of HTML and APIs, with the ability to support CRM integrations and campaign implementation. Experience planning, executing, and optimizing multi-channel campaigns across email, SMS, push notifications, and in-app messaging. Ability to analyze campaign performance data, identify improvement opportunities, and translate insights into effective marketing actions. Knowledge of email deliverability best practices and sender reputation management. A customer-centric and results-oriented mindset, with a strong focus on engagement, retention, and continuous optimization. Excellent analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills, combined with attention to detail. Strong communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work effectively in an international team. Upper-intermediate English proficiency or higher. Familiarity with AI-powered marketing tools and technologies is a significant advantage. Curiosity about emerging digital marketing trends and a proactive approach to learning and professional development. Benefits: Competitive Compensation: Attractive salary package aligned with experience and expertise. Remote Work: Flexibility to work remotely, offering greater freedom over your working environment. Flexible Working Hours: Adaptable working arrangements that support autonomy and work-life balance. Generous Time Off: Comprehensive vacation and sick leave policies. Health Insurance: Health coverage designed to support employee well-being. Professional Development: Financial assistance for professional development and access to educational allowances. Career Growth: Regular career development reviews and opportunities to expand your skills and responsibilities. Personal Activity Allowance: Monthly financial support for personal interests, hobbies, and activities outside work. Referral Program: Rewards for recommending talented professionals to join the team. Positive Work Environment: A collaborative, supportive, and international workplace that encourages initiative, independence, and open communication. Team Activities: Opportunities to participate in team-building events and corporate celebrations.
