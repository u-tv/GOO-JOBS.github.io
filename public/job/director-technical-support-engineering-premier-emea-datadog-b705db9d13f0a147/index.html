Director, Technical Support Engineering, Premier - EMEA

🏢 datadog 📍 Dublin, Ireland; Paris, France 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Datadog is seeking a highly motivated, results oriented Director to join our high-growth organization and cutting-edge team. Our Technical Solutions team shares technical and product expertise with customers through ongoing guidance, presentations, technical evaluations, and demos. Premier Support Engineers (PSEs) support Datadog's Premier Customers with any technical questions or issues related to Datadog, engaging via asynchronous technical support channels as well as relationship-building cadence calls, technical troubleshooting calls, business reviews, conferences, and projects. This is a senior leadership role at the intersection of customer experience, technical support, people leadership, and business strategy. You will establish the regional vision, build leadership depth, strengthen operational rigor, and ensure Premier Support continues to scale without compromising technical quality or customer trust. You will also serve as an executive sponsor for strategic customers and a senior partner to Sales, Customer Success, Technical Account Management, Product, Engineering, and Global Support leadership. At Datadog, we place value in our office culture - the relationships and collaboration it builds and the creativity it brings to the table. We operate as a hybrid workplace to ensure our Datadogs can create a work-life harmony that best fits them. What You'll Do: Own the vision, strategy, and operating plan for Premier Support Engineering across EMEA, translating global priorities into regional objectives, organizational plans, and measurable outcomes. Lead, coach, and develop a team of Premier Support Engineering managers and senior individual contributors, building a strong leadership bench and an organization that operates with clarity and autonomy. Own regional KPIs (CSAT, time to resolution, responsiveness, customer outcomes) and the regional performance and culture. Advise managers on performance plans and reviews, and partner with the People team on talent and employee relations. Act as executive sponsor for key Premier accounts. Build trusted senior relationships, set expectations, and turn customer feedback into action. Provide strategic guidance and oversight to Managers on regional projects and OKRs, ensuring their successful and timely completion. GTM Partnership: Partner with Sales, Customer Success and TAM leadership on Premier onboarding, renewals and expansion, and represent Premier Support in executive business reviews. Own regional hiring plans and quarterly hiring targets, and partner with Recruiting to attract, close and approve offers for top technical and customer-centric talent. Ensure the successful onboarding, technical training, certification, and ongoing development of Premier Support Engineers across the region. Build a proactive support model that uses customer context, support trends, product usage, upcoming launches, migrations, and business priorities to identify and mitigate risks before they escalate. Create scalable operating systems, playbooks, automation, and AI-enabled workflows that improve productivity, scalability, consistency, decision-making, and the customer experience across EMEA. Serve as the executive escalation point for critical Premier customer issues and incidents in the region, supporting your leaders through urgent customer situations. Connect product trends and roadmap signals to team readiness, and bring the Premier customer voice to Sales, Customer Success, Product and Engineering to improve supportability. Work from the Datadog office 3 - 5 days per week. Who You Are: Experienced leading leaders (a team of managers) in technical support, premier/enterprise support or technical solutions. Accountable for enterprise customer outcomes such as CSAT, time to resolution, responsiveness and retention. Proven at scaling an organization through growth: building process, structure and leadership depth without losing quality. Customer-obsessed and data-driven, using analytics to make decisions and improve operations. Skilled at developing talent across the full performance range, from growing high performers to addressing underperformance with clarity Someone with a high EQ and strong written and verbal communication skills, comfortable in front of senior customer stakeholders Curious and excited by technology, with some programming experience and a working knowledge of Linux Able to work a rotating schedule that requires occasional weekend and out-of-hours availability to support Premier customers Bonus Points: Knowledge of current monitoring solutions, DevOps tooling, cloud platforms, and related technologies Experience operating a support or services team across multiple EMEA locations, languages, or time zones Experience partnering with go-to-market teams to position, sell, or grow technical services. Datadog values people from all walks of life. We understand not everyone will meet all the above qualifications on day one. That's okay. If you're passionate about technology and want to grow your skills, we encourage you to apply.
