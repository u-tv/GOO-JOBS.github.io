Montu: Telehealth Medical Practitioner

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Melbourne, Victoria, Australia URL: https://www.montu.com.au Company Description Alternaleaf is Australia’s leading and fastest-growing network for seeking alternative healthcare, dedicated to improving patient outcomes through accessible, evidence-based treatments. We believe that modern medicine shouldn't mean modern burnout. By combining cutting-edge telehealth technology with robust clinical governance and compassionate care, we are reshaping how specialist and alternative care is delivered across Australia. When you join Alternaleaf, you aren't just filling a roster slot, you are joining a forward-thinking clinical community that values your expertise, protects your time, and eliminates the heavy administrative burdens of traditional clinic management. Job Description Escape the burnout of traditional face-to-face clinics and reclaim your work-life balance with Alternaleaf. We are seeking dedicated, Ahpra-registered Medical Practitioners (PGY4+) to join our growing national network on a 100% remote, work-from-home basis. At Alternaleaf, we empower doctors to practice modern, evidence-based plant medicine and integrative care without the crushing administrative overhead of brick-and-mortar clinics. Thanks to our streamlined technology, comprehensive nursing support, and automated admin workflows, you can focus entirely on what you do best: caring for patients. What Your Week Looks Like: Flexible Rostering: Choose from flexible shift options across our operating hours (Monday to Friday, 9am – 9pm AEST) to suit your lifestyle. Full-time and part-time arrangements are available. Focused Consultations: Conduct thorough, structured 10-minute telehealth consults (averaging 5–6 patients per hour) backed by robust pre-screening and clinical pathways. Clinical Autonomy & Support: Practice within a collaborative, highly governed multidisciplinary team environment with regular peer support, without sacrificing your individual clinical judgment. Qualifications Qualifications & Requirements Registration: General or Specialist Registration with Ahpra with no conditions, undertakings, or reprimands. Experience: Minimum PGY4 (Post-Graduate Year 4) with solid clinical experience in General Practice, Emergency Medicine, or Urgent Care. Telehealth experience is advantageous, but a passion for patient-centric integrative care is essential. Communication & Care: Exceptional remote bedside manner, with the ability to build strong rapport and deliver high-quality, focused consultations via video/phone. Technology & Setup: A secure, private home office environment with a reliable high-speed internet connection and a professional setup for telehealth. Regulatory Mindset: A strong commitment to clinical safety, ethical prescribing standards, and adherence to state and federal regulatory frameworks. Training: Successful applicants must be available within core business hours Monday - Friday to complete an initial training program of approximately 60 hours (around 3 weeks at 20 hours per week). Training is flexible to suit both full-time and part-time schedules. Completion time may vary. Additional Information Perks & Benefits We look after our clinicians the same way we expect them to look after our patients. Enjoy a comprehensive suite of benefits designed to support your wellbeing, professional growth, and lifestyle: True Flexibility: 100% remote, work-from-home setup with full-time or part-time options and customisable shift hours to fit your lifestyle. Zero Brick-and-Mortar Overhead: Say goodbye to late-night paperwork, billing friction, and practice management headaches; our automated workflows and nursing support handle the heavy lifting. Family Support: Up to 8 weeks of paid parental leave to support you and your family when it matters most. Continuous Professional Development: Access to dedicated learning platforms, SAGED courses, and specialised clinical education to support your annual CPD requirements. Home Office Setup: A home office equipment allowance to ensure your remote workspace is ergonomic, secure, and professional. Wellness & Lifestyle: Free subscription to Unmind for mental health support, plus retail discounts and wellness perks through our employee benefits program. Join us and make a real difference in patient care while embracing a modern, digital-first practice. #LI-NT1 #LI-REMOTE We are committed to facilitating a barrier-free recruitment process and work environment.
