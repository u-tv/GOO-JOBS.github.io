About Paytm: Paytm is India's leading mobile payments and financial services distribution company. Pioneer of the mobile QR payments revolution in India, Paytm builds technologies that help small businesses with payments and commerce. Paytm’s mission is to serve half a billion Indians and bring them to the mainstream economy with the help of technology. About the Role Paytm Ads is building the next generation of advertising infrastructure by combining high-intent first-party commerce data, AI-driven decisioning, and a scalable programmatic ecosystem. As a PMII / SPM, you will own key components of Paytm's monetization infrastructure, enabling seamless integration with DSPs, SSPs, exchanges, SDK partners and measurement providers. You will build scalable platform capabilities that maximize advertiser demand, improve publisher yield, and expand Paytm's programmatic ecosystem. This role is ideal for someone passionate about solving complex AdTech problems and building products at scale in a fast-moving environment. What You'll Drive Programmatic Ad Stack * Own the roadmap for Paytm's programmatic monetization platform. * Build and enhance capabilities across OpenRTB, auction infrastructure, demand routing and inventory monetization. * Improve platform scalability, latency and auction efficiency. Partner Integrations * Drive integrations with leading SSPs, DSPs, exchanges and demand partners. * Build scalable APIs and onboarding frameworks for external monetization partners. * Work closely with business teams to accelerate partner launches. SDK & Monetization Infrastructure * Own SDK integrations across internal and third-party monetization platforms. * Enable access to premium demand through SDK and server-side integrations. * Improve monetization performance across app inventory while maintaining user experience. Measurement & Attribution * Build integrations with MMPs, DCR providers, fraud detection and measurement partners. * Improve attribution accuracy and campaign measurement capabilities. * Enable privacy-first measurement solutions. Platform Execution * Own the end-to-end product lifecycle—from discovery and design through launch, experimentation and scale. Partner closely with Engineering, Architecture, Data Science, Sales, Partnerships and Operations teams. * Define success metrics and continuously optimize platform performance. What We're Looking For * Experience3-7 years of Product Management experience. * 2+ years in AdTech, Programmatic Advertising or Marketing Technology. * Experience building B2B platforms, advertising products or marketplace products. * Demonstrated ownership of products from concept through launch and scale.
