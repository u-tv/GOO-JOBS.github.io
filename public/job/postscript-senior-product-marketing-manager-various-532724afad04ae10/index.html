Postscript: Senior Product Marketing Manager

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote Postscript is the AI-powered, all-in-one retention platform trusted by 20,000+ Shopify brands, including Brooklinen, Ruggable, True Classic, and Dr. Squatch. Our mission is to maximize the lifetime value of every shopper. We built the best-in-class SMS platform, expanded into email, and launched revenue-driving AI products you won’t find anywhere else. Now we’re building the most AI-forward team in ecommerce, and we’re just getting started. Backed by Greylock and Y Combinator. Fully remote since 2018. As Senior Product Marketing Manager , you will lead product marketing activities for a portfolio of product areas that will grow and shift as Postscript scales. This includes established product areas such as Billing and partner integrations, as well as new product lines. Your responsibilities will span positioning and messaging, GTM strategy and execution, product launches and campaigns, competitive intelligence, and partnering with Enablement to get the field ready. This role works cross-functionally at the intersection of Product, Marketing, and our customer-facing teams. This is a fully remote position. Primary duties Develop clear, differentiated positioning in the marketplace for your product lines. Ensure that we tell a consistent and memorable story across our website, sales assets, content, and in customer communications. Partner with Product to bring new products and features to market and translate them into revenue. Serve as the Marketing lead for major launches in your portfolio. Run a repeatable launch process for smaller releases. Contribute to our competitive intelligence program with battlecards and competitive updates for your product areas that keep the field sharp on how we win. Become a subject matter expert in the domain of your product lines. Partner with Enablement on training materials, answer questions from the field team, and help maintain our internal knowledge base. Be a strong advocate for customer needs by joining customer-facing calls, reviewing sales calls, reading product feedback, talking to the field team, and doing industry research. What We’ll Love About You 5+ years of experience in product marketing, preferably within a B2B SaaS context Experience working across teams to launch significant features and products. An expert project manager who has a track record of leading cross functional projects Strong written and verbal communication skills: experience writing short form copy and/or long form content You’re a believer in SMS and email marketing and can make the case for why owned channels win for DTC brands. Time spent in ecommerce or startups is a plus What You’ll Love About Us Salary range of USD $125,000-$146,000 base plus significant equity (we do not have geo based salaries) High growth startup - plenty of room for you to directly impact the company and grow your career! Work from home (or wherever) Fun - We’re passionate and enjoy what we do Competitive compensation and opportunity for equity Flexible paid time off Health, dental, vision insurance For information about how we use your personal data, please see our U.S. Job Applicant Privacy Notice You are welcome here. Postscript is an ever-evolving place of equal employment for talented individuals. To apply: https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/postscript-senior-product-marketing-manager
