The Payments team is looking for Product Managers to create high impact and critical business products. What We're Looking For:- * 5+ years of product management experience with strong exposure to payments infrastructure. * Prior experience in merchant payments and global fintech environments. * Exposure to launching new products in a new market is a strong advantage. * Strong execution focus with the ability to operate in complex, high-scale environments.
