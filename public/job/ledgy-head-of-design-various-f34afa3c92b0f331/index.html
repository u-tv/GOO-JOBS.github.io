Ledgy: Head of Design

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Germany (Remote); London, United Kingdom; Poland (Remote); Romania (Remote) At Ledgy, we’re on a mission to make Europe a powerhouse of entrepreneurship by building a modern, tech-driven equity management and financial reporting platform for private and public companies. In 2026, we aim to be the leading provider for European IPOs and reporting for share-based payments. We are a value-based company with a core focus on being humble, transparent, ambitious and impactful, all in order to delivery the best experience for our customers and end users. We are proud to partner with some of the world’s leading investors. New Enterprise Associates led our $22m Series B round in 2022, with Philip Chopin joining Sequoia’s Luciana Lixandru on our board. We were founded in Switzerland in 2017 and today we operate globally from offices in Zurich and London. We encourage diversity and are an international team coming from 26 different countries and speaking 25 different languages. About the role We're looking for a Head of Design who still loves to design. You'll lead a small, strong team of product designers, set the bar for craft and for how we work with AI, and spend about half your time designing with some of our teams. Ledgy's problems are dense and high-stakes: finance, legal and HR teams use us to manage real ownership and real money across many countries. Design here has to be precise and still feel simple. As the company grows into new markets and new products, you'll shape what design at Ledgy becomes. Design at Ledgy is AI-first. Work starts with AI models, moves into working prototypes in our codebase, and lands in Figma for the system. Every team ships agent features. You'll make sure the whole team does this well, consistently, and with judgement. Reports to: VP of Product Location: Hybrid from our London office, remote in the UK, Spain, Germany, Poland or Romania What you'll do Lead the team (~50%) Manage and grow three product designers working across our product teams: regular 1:1s, honest feedback, clear growth paths. Hire: help shape roles, run interviews and grow the team as Ledgy grows. Partner with product and engineering leadership on planning, staffing and the design vision across all teams. Own the direction of our design system and shared AI patterns (how agents ask, act, explain and undo), together with engineering. Define how we design with AI: tools, workflows, prototypes in code, shared skills and prompts. Make it easy for every designer to work this way. Run design critique and design reviews, and own the quality bar across the product. Design hands-on (~50%) Be the designer for one of our teams (for example Tax, Trading, AI Systems - to be decided together): contribute to Ledgy’s product experiences, from agents and AI-assisted workflows to the our trading integrations and compliance reporting Prototype in code in our real codebase, and open small front-end PRs when it's faster than a handoff. Set the example: work in the open, show your thinking, ship. You'll thrive here if you Have 10+ years in product design on complex B2B or data-dense products, including 2+ years leading or managing designers. Still design and prototype yourself, and want to keep doing it. Are AI-native: you know when to prototype, when to stop and think, which tool fits which job, and how to turn that into habits a team can share. Understand how AI agents work well enough to design for them and argue with engineers about them: the model, the harness, tool calls, APIs, audit trails. Build your own tools: prompts, skills, small automations, and help others build theirs. Raise quality through people, not just through your own work: you've grown designers and improved how a team works. Communicate clearly with leadership and influence decisions across product and engineering. Fluent English. Nice to have Experience in equity, fintech, tax, payroll or accounting software. Led design for AI or agent products that shipped to real customers. Built or evolved a design system in code. What your journey could look like First 30 days: Meet every designer, PM and EM; learn the product and our customers; review how the team works today. First 3 months: Run the team's rituals, ship your first work, and propose how design should work with AI across teams. 6–12 months: Grow the team, raise the craft bar visibly, and set the design vision for the next stage of Ledgy. Our interview process Recruiter call (30 min): you, us, the role, logistics. Hiring manager interview (45 min): how you work, lead and decide. Portfolio presentation (60 min): two projects, at least one showing how you work with AI, and one story about building or leading a team. AI working session (3.5h, live): frame a problem with a PM and a designer, build it with your own AI tools, and critique a piece of work as you would with your team. Final interview (45 min): with one of our founders. You'll get a short guide before each step. To apply: https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/ledgy-head-of-design
