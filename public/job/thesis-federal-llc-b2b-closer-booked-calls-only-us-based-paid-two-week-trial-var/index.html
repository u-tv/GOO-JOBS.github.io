Thesis Federal LLC: B2B Closer, Booked Calls Only (US-based, paid two-week trial)

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Gainesville, Florida, USA URL: https://thesisfederal.com/ We are a small US firm that writes federal funding proposals for technology companies, for a fixed fee. We are looking for one US-based closer for booked calls. The role starts with a paid two-week trial. The work Our sales are written-first. A prospect replies to our email, receives a free one-page written plan, and picks a call time in writing. You take those booked calls, return calls from prospects who phoned us, and rebook missed calls. No cold dialing and no lists. On each call you qualify, present our written fixed-fee offer (mid four figures to low five figures), confirm who signs, and ask for the decision. We send every agreement and payment link. You send a short written report after each call, the same day. Calls are in US business hours (Eastern Time). The work is remote. Who fits You are based in the United States. You have personally closed B2B service sales of US$5,000 or more with business owners or technical founders, on the phone. Experience selling to companies that pursue federal, SBIR or defense work is a strong plus. You are comfortable following a written call guide and firm rules: no promises about outcomes and no discounts. You do not currently write or sell federal proposal, white paper, SBIR or capture services for anyone else. How we screen and what we pay Your written answers to the three questions below. Finalists record a 3 to 5 minute drill on a short script we send. The drill is paid: US$40. Every drill is scored on the same written scorecard. The strongest candidates are offered a paid two-week trial: US$45 an hour, up to 10 hours a week, plus 15% of the fees we actually collect on deals you close. The exact terms are in a written agreement you see before you start. The role after the trial depends on results. We do not promise a drill, a trial, a hire, or any number of hours, calls, deals or earnings. Call volume is modest today. Part-time to start; the best performer moves to more hours as booked calls grow. To apply, email these three answers. Everything is in writing. Describe one B2B service sale of US$5,000 or more that you personally closed: what the service was, the price, and who signed. Your city and state, and the hours you could work each week between 9 am and 6 pm Eastern Time. A prospect says, "Send me something and I'll think about it." What do you say next? Use your own words. To apply: https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/thesis-federal-llc-b2b-closer-booked-calls-only-us-based-paid-two-week-trial
