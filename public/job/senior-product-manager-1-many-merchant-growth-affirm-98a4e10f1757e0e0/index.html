Senior Product Manager, 1: Many Merchant Growth

🏢 affirm 📍 Remote US 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Affirm is reinventing credit to make it more honest and friendly, giving consumers the flexibility to buy now and pay later without any hidden fees or compounding interest. Consumers across the globe need safe, transparent financing while merchants want to provide users with their preferred way to pay and convert more buyers. To help achieve this vision, we are hiring a Senior Product Manager for our 1:Many Growth team. Affirm's Distribution Partnerships program - internally called &quot; 1:Many &quot; - is one of the highest-leverage growth levers at the company. Instead of integrating merchant by merchant, we integrate once into a platform merchants already use, and every merchant on that platform becomes reachable through that single integration. This role is responsible for: Own and grow Affirm's partner portfolio - making it easier and more frictionless for merchants to onboard and offer Affirm as a payment method to their customers. This includes taking product ownership of Affirm's existing vertical software partnerships, and growing the broader portfolio of platform partners still being stood up. Partner Acceleration Strategy - building the durable capabilities that make every future partner integration faster to launch and easier to scale, rather than solving the same problem one partner at a time. As a Senior Product Manager, you will work closely with cross-functional teams including commercial, merchant risk, design, product analytics, and engineering to define, launch and grow integrations with platform partners. Effective stakeholder management and ability to successfully navigate organizational dynamics are a must for success. You should be outcomes-driven with a very high bias for action. What You'll Do Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define, launch, and grow integrations with payment and platform partners. Build durable, repeatable capabilities through a Partner Acceleration Strategy that makes future partner integrations faster to launch. Build relationships with platform partners to gather feedback and insights on product performance, and use it to drive merchant growth. Define and prioritize product features and requirements to improve the customer, partner, and merchant experience. Create and manage the product roadmap for the broader 1:Many growth team. Develop success metrics/OKRs to measure the effectiveness of these initiatives. Partner with commercial and product marketing teams to develop go-to-market strategies for new partner integrations and features. What We Look For 5+ years of product management experience Proven ability to multiple technical software products from kickoff to post-launch, driving meaningful business impact you can clearly articulate. Skilled in cross-functional collaboration — diving into technical details with engineers, iterating with designers in Figma, and analyzing funnel performance with data analysts. Strong organizational and communication skills to navigate complex dynamics and drive alignment. Execution-oriented with a proactive approach to overcoming obstacles and blockers. Adept at transforming incomplete or conflicting inputs into clear, actionable plans. Demonstrated track record of shipping high-quality products on time. Comfortable engaging in the commercial aspects of product development, including representing the product externally, setting expectations, and influencing outcomes that benefit both Affirm and its partners.
