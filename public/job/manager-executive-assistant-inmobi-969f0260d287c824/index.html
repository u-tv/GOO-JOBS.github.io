Title: Manager - Executive Assistant Position Summary: We are looking for a highly experienced and proactive Executive Assistant to provide comprehensive administrative and strategic support to senior leadership. This is a high-visibility role that demands exceptional discretion, sharp organizational instincts, and the ability to operate independently in a fast-paced, dynamic business environment. The ideal candidate will bring at least 10 years of EA experience, ideally supporting C-suite or senior leadership, and will be comfortable managing competing priorities while maintaining confidentiality at all times. Experience working directly with Founders and/or CEOs is a strong advantage. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES 1. Calendar & Schedule Management Own and manage complex, multi-time zone calendars for one or more senior leaders Proactively anticipate and resolve scheduling conflicts Coordinate internal/external meetings, calls, and events across time zones and departments 2. Communication & Correspondence Draft, review, and manage professional correspondence and memos on behalf of leadership Act as primary point of contact — screening and triaging high-volume email and requests Liaise confidently with internal stakeholders, external partners, clients, and senior executives 3. Administrative & Operational Support Prepare, format, and organize reports, presentations, and business documents Maintain organized filing systems for sensitive records and correspondence Coordinate with vendors and service providers, tracking key deliverables and timelines Manage contracts, invoices, and expense workflows through approval and signature processes 4. Travel & Logistics Arrange comprehensive domestic and international travel — flights, hotels, visas, and itineraries Prepare detailed trip briefings aligned with meeting objectives and business priorities 5. Project & Operations Support Drive coordination across cross-functional projects and initiatives Track action items and deliverables from leadership meetings Prepare agendas, presentations, and board-level materials as required Support budget tracking, invoicing, and expense reporting for the leadership office QUALIFICATIONS & EXPERIENCE Prior experience in a corporate, professional services, or fast-paced business environment strongly preferred Bachelor's degree in any discipline Demonstrated ability to manage sensitive, confidential information with discretion Advanced proficiency in Microsoft 365 (Word, Excel, Outlook, PowerPoint, Teams) KEY COMPETENCIES Discretion & Confidentiality Organizational Excellence Proactive Communication Stakeholder Management Attention to Detail Adaptability & Initiative What does InMobi do?
