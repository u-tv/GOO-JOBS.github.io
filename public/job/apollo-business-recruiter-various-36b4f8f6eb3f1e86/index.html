Apollo: Business Recruiter

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: URL: https://www.apollo.io/es About the Role Apollo is hiring a Business Recruiter to own full-cycle recruiting across our G&A, Sales and Marketing organizations . This is a high-ownership role on a lean, high-performing team. You will manage a portfolio of mid and senior-level requisitions, build and execute sourcing strategies, and serve as a genuine business partner to your hiring managers. This role requires someone who operates with low oversight , self-diagnoses problems before they escalate , and brings a talent advisor posture to every stakeholder conversation. The best person for this role brings their own operating system and makes the team around them sharper. What You Will Do Own full-cycle recruiting for mid and senior-level roles across G&A, Sales and Marketing, with consistent delivery across multiple verticals simultaneously Build and execute sourcing strategies that generate qualified, diverse pipelines, and adapt those strategies based on funnel performance , not just instinct Conduct structured screens and interviews, design interview plans , and lead calibrated debriefs that result in clear, defensible hiring decisions Manage 20+ phone screens per week and drive 360+ outbound contacts per quarter Hold the bar on candidate quality , give direct and honest feedback, and avoid passing weak profiles to sidestep an uncomfortable conversation Maintain accurate, real-time pipeline data in Greenhouse and self-report on progress to leadership without being asked Track funnel metrics, identify root causes of slowdowns, and bring data-informed recommendations to stakeholder conversations Partner with hiring managers and business leaders as a talent advisor : shape role scoping, set expectations on candidate profiles, and influence as a subject matter expert. Work cross-functionally with Finance, HR, and Compensation to navigate offer approvals and headcount processes with operational integrity Build or improve multiple workflows, processes, or tools that create efficiency beyond your own req load What We Are Looking For 7+ years of full-cycle recruiting experience with a focus on Sales, Revenue, or Marketing roles in SaaS Demonstrated delivery in high-growth environments where you managed complexity and ambiguity Comfort and credibility working with stakeholders at all levels, including the ability to challenge and influence , not just execute Strong data fluency : you monitor your own funnel health, diagnose issues before they surface in a check-in, and use data to drive stakeholder conversations Proven AI and process efficiency capability : you have built repeatable workflows or automation that measurably improved your output, and you can speak to what you built, why it works, and what changed Familiarity with Revenue teams, high-performing marketing orgs, and product-led growth models Hands-on experience with Greenhouse and Gem , with clean data hygiene as a non-negotiable operating habit Clear, direct communicator who can influence without authority across functions High agency : you identify problems, generate solutions, and drive progress without waiting for direction The listed Pay Range reflects the total cash compensation inclusive of annual base salary and annual bonus as applicable.
