Headquarters: Florida, United States URL: https://www.airlockdigital.com Location:Florida, United States - Remote Who Are We? About Airlock Digital: Airlock Digital is a global leader in application control and allowlisting. We seek to empower every organization to run only what they trust and operate free from malware and ransomware. With rapid growth across Australia, North America, and EMEA. We are committed to our core values, respect, determination, and integrity. We support a diverse and expanding global customer base. At Airlock, we pride ourselves on being a team of humble, collaborative, and driven professionals who support one another and share a passion for cybersecurity. What We Are Looking For: Airlock Digital is seeking a high-impact Channel Account Manager to help scale our North American partner ecosystem and drive meaningful partner-led revenue. This is an individual contributor role with both strategic and hands-on responsibilities. You will own partner relationships across a defined territory or portfolio, develop joint business plans, drive pipeline creation, and ensure strong execution across enablement, co-selling, and go-to-market initiatives. The ideal candidate brings deep channel experience, strong commercial instincts, and thrives in fast-growing environments where building structure, accountability, and momentum go hand-in-hand. Key Responsibilities: Channel Strategy & Partner Growth Own and execute the regional channel strategy for North America, aligned to Airlock Digital’s global partner vision Manage and grow a portfolio growth Develop and manage joint business plans, quarterly business reviews (QBRs), and mutual success metrics with priority partners Revenue Ownership & Pipeline Execution Own a measurable partner-sourced and partner-influenced pipeline and revenue target Drive high-quality deal registration, accurate forecasting, and consistent operating cadence with partners Partner closely with direct sales teams to support co-selling motions, unblock deals, and accelerate enterprise opportunities Meet or exceed quarterly and annual channel revenue objectives Enablement & Go-to-Market Lead partner onboarding, sales enablement, and ongoing training to ensure strong product knowledge and sales readiness Collaborate with Marketing on joint campaigns, events, MDF execution, and demand-generation initiatives with clear ROI Drive field execution including partner-led workshops, events, executive briefings, and regional activities Cross-Functional Leadership Act as the internal advocate for partner needs across Sales, Marketing, Product, Solutions Engineering, and Customer Success Provide feedback on market trends, competitive insights, and partner sentiment to influence program evolution Maintain strong CRM and PRM hygiene to ensure visibility, scale, and data-driven decision-making Representation & Market Presence Represent Airlock Digital at regional partner
