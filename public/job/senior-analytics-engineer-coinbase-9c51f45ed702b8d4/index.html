Ready to do the most impactful work of your career? At Coinbase , we are uncompromising on our mission to increase economic freedom. The bar is high, the environment is intense, and we like it that way. This isn't a place for complacency, it’s a place to be pushed past your perceived limits. If you're ready to build the future of finance alongside people who refuse to settle for &quot;good enough,&quot; you belong here. Coinbase is a remote-first, but not remote-only company. Expect to get together quarterly for intense in-person working sessions called “surges.” Learn more about working at Coinbase . The Compliance Data team owns the foundational data layer that every regulatory obligation at Coinbase depends on. We are a centralized team within Coinbase's Platform organization, and we build the trusted, certified data that the rest of the company relies on to answer regulators with confidence. At the center of our work is the Compliance Data Mart (CDM), Coinbase's canonical source of truth for user, transaction, and compliance data. Our focus is the data foundations beneath every regulatory report, making sure they are accurate, consistent, well-modeled, and trustworthy. When a number is wrong in a regulatory filing, the root cause usually traces back to the data layer. We own that layer, and we make sure it holds. While building durable foundations is our core mission, we also support live regulatory exams, audits, and time-sensitive data requests. You should be comfortable balancing long-term foundational work with occasional reactive, deadline-driven asks. Regulators operate on their timelines, not ours. Analytics Engineer is a hybrid role that blends data engineering and data science. You'll bring the engineering discipline to build durable, production-grade pipelines and data models, and the analytical instincts to understand what the data means, where it breaks, and how stakeholders will use it. The details matter here, because everything built on top of our foundations inherits our quality. What We Do: Own the Compliance Data Mart (CDM): Build and certify Coinbase's single source of truth for user, transaction, and compliance data across retail and institutional entities Build Foundational Data Models: Design the user, transaction, and compliance data models that downstream reporting, analytics, and AI workflows all build on Keep Foundations Current: Evolve our core data models as Coinbase's products, entities, and regulatory requirements change, proactively absorbing upstream changes before they break anything downstream Drive Data Quality at the Source: Monitor quality from source system into the CDM, catching and fixing issues before they ever reach a report Support Live Regulatory Needs: Provide accurate, timely data for regulatory exams, audits, and ad hoc regulator requests when they come up Enable Self-Serve and Automation: Build the foundations and tooling that let downstream teams and AI agents answer their own questions, instead of depending on manual data pulls Pay Transparency Notice: The target annual base salary for this position can range as detailed below.
