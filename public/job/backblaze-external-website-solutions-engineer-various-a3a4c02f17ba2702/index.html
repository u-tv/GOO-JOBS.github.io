Backblaze External Website: Solutions Engineer

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: United States URL: https://www.backblaze.com Solutions Engineer Backblaze (Nasdaq: BLZE) | Remote, US | AI and application storage Solutions Engineering | Reports to the Solutions Engineering Manager (the VP of Solutions Engineering until the manager is hired) | Works with Account Executives, Product, Support, and the Solutions Engineering team Salary range: $128,000-$144,000 plus variable pay and RSUs. How We Got Here We started by building storage servers from scratch in a Palo Alto apartment to save 90% on hardware. Turns out hard constraints make for pretty good engineering. That discipline forged a platform that performs as well or better than competitors, scales to multi-exabyte workloads, and costs a fraction of what AWS or Google charge. AI infrastructure runs on data. Storage gets hard at scale. We've been breaking, rebuilding, and improving that storage layer for 20 years. Today, the world's leading AI infrastructure providers, neoclouds, and data-intensive businesses run on Backblaze. Our platform includes: B2 Overdrive - high-throughput object storage engineered for AI infrastructure, GPU workloads, and massive-scale data pipelines B2 Neo - white-label enterprise cloud storage powering neocloud platforms for AI, HPC, and media B2 Cloud Storage - S3-compatible object storage with predictable pricing, free egress, and no lock-in Computer Backup - automatic, continuous endpoint backup for Macs and PCs 500,000+ customers. 175 countries. Nasdaq: BLZE. backblaze.com The Role Backblaze sells a technical product, so Solutions Engineers join the deal at the first discovery conversation, before it is qualified. You will partner with Account Executives to learn what a customer needs, run the proof of concept that shows Backblaze fits, and stay close after the deal closes. This role focuses on AI and application storage customers. Nearly every customer is replacing something they already run. Your job is to show where Backblaze slots into what they have already built, not to design their stack from scratch. You will earn trust by being curious, hands-on, and straight with customers about what the test shows. This is not a demo-and-handoff role. If your instinct is to open a terminal, test a customer's real workload, and write down what you find, keep reading. What You Will Do Join discovery early. Work with the AE from the first conversation to learn the customer's workload, success criteria, and timeline. Run proofs of concept. Define success with the customer, test Backblaze against what they run today, and report results plainly, including ones they didn't hope for. Show where Backblaze fits. Explain how Backblaze slots into the customer's AI data pipeline alongside the compute, networking, and storage they already use. Hand off cleanly. Take the lead through the technical win, then step back so the AE can carry the deal through negotiation. Stay engaged after the sale. Answer questions in shared customer channels, help customers adopt the platform, and work with the AE on expansion. Carry the field back to Product. Capture gaps and requests with evidence. Share what you learn. Contribute benchmarks, technical content, and events. What We Are Looking For Curiosity. You dig into technical workflows and pick apart what customers tell you. This is the trait that matters most. Programming fluency. You are comfortable in at least one language, any language, and use it to script tests and automate work. Customer-facing experience. You have worked directly with customers in solutions engineering, support, technical account management, consulting, or a similar role. A sales background is not required. Working knowledge of cloud infrastructure. You can explain how compute, storage, and networking fit into an AI data pipeline and where a storage layer sits. Experience on any cloud counts, whether AWS, Google, Azure, or CoreWeave. You don't need to have built these systems yourself. Strong communication. You can explain a technical result clearly to an engineer or an executive, and you listen well. Hands-on habits. You test things yourself and write down what you find. Also True of the Right Person Experience across a whole deal at a smaller company, from discovery through post-sale Experience with object storage or the S3 API, or with AI and GPU workloads such as training, inference, and checkpointing Familiarity with a sales qualification method such as MEDDPICC Experience supporting customers after close, including expansions This Is Not the Role for You If You want to stay at the demo layer rather than test real workloads You wait for an AE to ask before you engage You see the job as finished at signature rather than after the customer is live How We Will Interview You will have four conversations: a recruiter screen, the hiring manager, our principal solutions architect, and a member of the Solutions Engineering team. We will ask you to walk us through a proof of concept you ran, including a result the customer did not hope for and how you reported it. We will ask about a technical problem you dug into yourself, and how you work with customers and Account Executives.
