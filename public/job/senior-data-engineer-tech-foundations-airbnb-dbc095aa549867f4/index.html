Senior Data Engineer Tech Foundations

🏢 airbnb 📍 US - Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Airbnb was born in 2007 when two hosts welcomed three guests to their San Francisco home, and has since grown to over 5 million hosts who have welcomed over 2 billion guest arrivals in almost every country across the globe. Every day, hosts offer unique stays and experiences that make it possible for guests to connect with communities in a more authentic way. Senior Data Engineer, Enterprise Core Datasets, Tech Foundations The Community You Will Join At Airbnb, trustworthy data powers business decisions, product innovation, and operational efficiency. The Enterprise Core Datasets team builds the technical data foundations that help Airbnb understand how the business operates, where resources are invested, and how new technologies create value. Our scope spans high-impact datasets across the organization: measuring AI adoption, usage, and cost; understanding revenue, costs, and profitability through Unit Economics; integrating non-platform data from Jira and other enterprise applications; and powering critical company metrics such as Gross Booking Value and Revenue. You will join a team that values thoughtful architecture, engineering rigor, and operational ownership. We collaborate with engineering, product, analytics, finance, and business teams to turn complex requirements into dependable, reusable data assets. We welcome strong engineers who are curious about the business and eager to solve problems across diverse domains. The Difference You Will Make As a Senior Data Engineer, you will design, build, and operate datasets that shape decisions across Airbnb. Your work will help the organization measure AI adoption and spending, understand the economics of its products, and connect enterprise application data to meaningful business and operational insights. You will own complex data engineering projects from requirements gathering and technical design through implementation, validation, and production operations. Working closely with stakeholders and engineering partners, you will build maintainable data models and pipelines that accommodate new AI tools, products, cost categories, and enterprise systems while preserving clear definitions, traceability, and reliable historical data. Operating in an ecosystem that includes petabyte-scale datasets, you will improve correctness, freshness, performance, and cost efficiency. You will build reusable components that simplify source onboarding and reduce duplicated logic. Through design reviews, knowledge sharing, and mentorship, you will help strengthen the team’s engineering practices and contribute to Airbnb’s broader data community. A Typical Day Design, build, and operate high-impact data pipelines across AI usage and cost, Unit Economics, enterprise applications, and critical financial metrics. Partner with stakeholders to clarify how AI adoption, consumption, and spending are measured, and implement consistent data models that support those definitions. Evolve Unit Economics datasets to connect revenue and diverse cost drivers, enabling teams to understand product profitability and resource efficiency. Build reliable ingestion and transformation pipelines for non-platform systems such as Jira and other BizTech enterprise applications. Translate complex requirements into clear source contracts, reusable fact and dimension models, and practical technical designs. Develop configurable pipelines and shared components that simplify onboarding new data sources, products, and business requirements. Implement automated testing, reconciliation, anomaly detection, and monitoring to protect data accuracy, completeness, and availability. Plan and execute safe migrations and historical backfills through dependency analysis, validation, controlled cutovers, and recovery planning. Optimize distributed processing, storage, and query performance to improve freshness and reduce compute costs. Own production reliability for your datasets and pipelines through on-call participation, incident response, and improvements to systems and runbooks. Contribute to technical planning and design reviews, mentor fellow engineers, and improve Airbnb’s Data Engineering Paved Path. Your Expertise 5+ years of relevant industry experience, with a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. A track record of independently delivering complex data engineering projects, from requirements and technical design through implementation and sustained production ownership. Strong expertise in SQL and programming skills in Python or Scala, with experience building maintainable, well-tested data systems. Hands-on experience with distributed processing technologies such as Apache Spark and large-scale analytical storage systems. Strong data modeling skills, including fact and dimension design, slowly changing dimensions, incremental processing, and historical data management. Experience integrating heterogeneous sources, including APIs, enterprise applications, event data, and transactional systems, while managing schema changes and source reliability. Proven ability to build reliable pipelines and execute migrations and backfills while preserving correctness and downstream compatibility. Experience implementing data quality controls, observability, lineage, and governance practices, and troubleshooting production data issues. The ability to diagnose performance bottlenecks and optimize distributed computation, storage, and query execution. Strong collaboration and communication skills, including the ability to clarify requirements with business partners, explain technical tradeoffs, and coordinate delivery across teams. Experience contributing to design and code reviews, sharing knowledge, and supporting the development of other engineers. Curiosity and sound judgment when learning new domains and balancing delivery needs with long-term system health. Experience with AI usage or cost measurement, cloud cost attribution, Unit Economics, enterprise application data, or financial and payments datasets is a plus. Familiarity with workflow orchestration, SQL transformation frameworks, and modern table formats such as Apache Iceberg is also valuable. Your Location This position is US - Remote Eligible.
