Req: SLSQ427R362 With the most complete data & AI stack on the market, Databricks is well suited to be the strategic partner for our customers’ AI transformations. This role will lead in expanding Databrick’s mindshare in the non-technical C-suite and overall share of wallet, leading to owning both the platform conversation and transformation mandate.
