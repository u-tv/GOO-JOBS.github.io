University Recruiting Manager

🏢 stripe 📍 Singapore, Dublin 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies, from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups, use Stripe to accept payments, grow revenue, and create new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet. The work ahead is substantial, which gives you the opportunity to help put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing some of the most meaningful work of your career. About the team Early careers is a critical part of Stripe’s long-term talent strategy. The University Recruiting team helps shape the company we become by building the systems, programs, and hiring partnerships that bring exceptional interns, apprentices, and new graduates to Stripe. As University Recruiting Manager, you will lead and develop a team of University Recruiters while owning the strategy and delivery of intern and new graduate hiring across Stripe. This is a high-visibility leadership role with a wide stage: you will work across every line of business and role type, partner with leaders throughout the company, and turn ambitious hiring goals into thoughtful, durable programs. You will also help lead Stripe’s global internship and apprenticeship programs, including onboarding, intern experience, and employee-relations escalations. What you’ll do University Recruiting at Stripe is not a transactional recruiting function. This role goes well beyond attending a career fair or filling individual roles. You will set direction for an early careers strategy that supports Stripe’s business needs today and builds the talent pipeline we will need next. You will bring strategic judgment, a builder’s mindset, and a deep commitment to craft. You will balance the operational rigor required to deliver at scale with the creativity to design programs and experiences that are clear, useful, and memorable. You will use data to understand what is working, where we are falling short, and what needs to change. And you will build credibility with leaders by making complex recruiting challenges easier to understand and act on. Responsibilities Lead, coach, and develop a team of full-cycle University Recruiters. Set a high bar for judgment, execution, and candidate and hiring-team partnership. Develop and deliver a clear early careers hiring strategy across Stripe’s lines of business and role families, translating business needs into practical recruiting plans and measurable outcomes. Partner with senior leaders and hiring teams across the company to understand workforce needs, influence decisions, and meet ambitious hiring goals. Lead global internship and apprenticeship programs, including onboarding, intern experience, program operations, and employee-relations escalations. Build and improve the end-to-end candidate experience in partnership with Finance, People Operations, Benefits, Onboarding, Legal, and other cross-functional teams. Develop thoughtful university, event, employer-brand, and employee-referral strategies that strengthen Stripe’s early careers talent pipeline. Use people analytics, recruiting data, and operational insights to identify patterns, measure performance, inform decisions, and improve the systems your team relies on. Raise the bar for how University Recruiting works at Stripe by bringing rigor, care, and a strong point of view to both the strategy and the details. Who you are You are a strategic recruiting leader who sees early careers as a company-building function. You pair high standards with a practical work ethic, and you are comfortable moving from long-range planning to solving the immediate problem in front of you. You care about the quality of the work, the experience it creates for people, and the decisions behind it. We're looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. Minimum requirements Experience designing and delivering a university recruiting strategy or major early talent initiative at a high-growth or complex organization. A proven record of leading and developing University Recruiting professionals. You give clear feedback, build strong teams, and help people grow their judgment and capability. Strong strategic thinking and the ability to bring clarity to ambiguous, multi-stakeholder problems. The communication and influencing skills to earn trust with leaders at every level and turn ideas into aligned action. A high degree of ownership, a rigorous work ethic, and the ability to operate effectively in a fast-moving environment with shifting priorities. An appreciation for craft and beauty in the work. You care about the quality of the strategy, the programs, the candidate experience, and the operational details that make each of them work. A deep interest in people analytics, with experience using recruiting metrics, tools, and process data to improve decisions and outcomes. Experience partnering closely with hiring teams throughout the recruiting process as a trusted advisor.
