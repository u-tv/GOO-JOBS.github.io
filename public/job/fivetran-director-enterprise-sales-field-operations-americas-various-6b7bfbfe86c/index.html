Fivetran : Director, Enterprise Sales Field Operations, Americas

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote, California, United States, AMER From Fivetran’s founding until now, our mission has remained the same: to make access to data as simple and reliable as electricity. With Fivetran, customer data arrives in their warehouses, canonical and ready to query, with no engineering or maintenance required. We’re proud that more organizations continue to leverage our technology every day to become truly data-driven. About Us Fivetran and dbt Labs are bringing together two industry-leading companies with a shared mission: helping organizations unlock the full value of their data. Together, we’re delivering the data infrastructure layer that helps organizations move, transform, and trust their data — from the moment data moves, through every transformation, to the context teams and AI systems rely on. Fivetran helps organizations automate data movement across the systems, clouds, engines, and tools they rely on. dbt Labs pioneered analytics engineering, helping teams transform data into reliable, governed insights. Together, we support thousands of organizations as they build a trusted foundation for analytics, AI, and better business decisions. As we bring our teams and technology together, we’re building on the strengths of both companies while continuing to deliver the products and experiences our customers know and trust. It’s an exciting time to join us: we’re creating a company with the scale, talent, and technology to help more organizations put their data to work with greater speed, confidence, and impact. During this transition period, you may see references to both Fivetran and dbt Labs throughout our recruiting process as we integrate our teams, systems, and career sites. About the Role The Director of AMER Enterprise Sales Field Operations is the primary operations partner to the VP of AMER Enterprise Sales. You will own the operating rhythm of the region: forecasting, pipeline generation and inspection, and business reviews. You will also shape the region's go-to-market strategy, expansion plans, and capacity model. In this role, you will act as the COO to the VP of AMER Enterprise Sales, ensuring our business is growing efficiently and effectively. You need to think strategically about the Enterprise business, understanding the implications of changing market and team dynamics and proactively developing solutions that enable us to win. You will lead a team of three field operations managers and analysts. Success in this role means a more accurate forecast, a healthier pipeline, measurable gains in rep productivity, and increasing ARR across AMER Enterprise. The work you do will shape how Fivetran grows in the Americas. This role sits within the GTM Strategy and Operations organization. What You’ll Do Forecasting and pipeline Own the AMER Enterprise forecast process, including call methodology, roll-up, and accuracy tracking against commit and best case, giving leadership a number they can commit to with confidence. Drive pipeline generation planning: with the marketing, partner, and sales leadership teams set coverage targets by segment and source, track progress, and flag gaps early enough to act, so each quarter starts with the pipeline needed to hit plan. Run pipeline inspection with sales leadership, focusing on deal quality, stage progression, and slippage, so risk surfaces while there is still time to save the deal. Operating cadence and performance management Design and run the region's operating cadence: weekly forecast calls, pipeline reviews, monthly business reviews, and quarterly business reviews. Continuously refine those cadences so they drive decisions rather than reporting. Run AE performance reviews with frontline and second-line managers, using consistent metrics to identify coaching needs and performance risk, so managers act on facts rather than anecdotes. Identify and prioritize the metrics the field uses to run the business, and partner with GTM Analytics to build and maintain the supporting dashboards, giving every leader in the region a shared view of performance. Lead deep-dive investigations into performance questions, and turn findings into specific recommendations that change how the region sells. Regional strategy Shape GTM strategy and org structure for AMER Enterprise by region and sales motion, setting the team up to win as enterprise AI demand grows. Build the analysis behind regional expansion decisions, such as which territories to enter next and in what sequence, so we invest where the opportunity is largest. Design region- and motion-specific SPIFFs, then measure whether they changed behavior and results. Annual planning and capacity (in partnership with Strategy and Planning) Support quota setting for the region, bringing field context to top-down targets so quotas are both ambitious and attainable. Support and refine territory carving so territories are balanced and match the coverage model. Pressure-test assumptions in the bottoms-up headcount model and coverage ratios, so hiring plans reflect real field performance. Validate rep productivity and ramp assumptions against actual performance data. Team leadership Lead, hire, and develop a team of field operations managers and analysts and grow them into strategic partners to sales leadership. Set clear priorities for the team and allocate its capacity against the needs of AMER Enterprise leadership. Who you'll work with VP of AMER Enterprise Sales and their leadership team: your primary stakeholders and day-to-day partners. Strategy and Planning: annual plan, quota, territory, and capacity modeling. Revenue Operations: CRM, systems, process, and data integrity. GTM Analytics: dashboards, reporting, and analytical deep dives. Enablement: onboarding, ramp, and skill gaps surfaced through performance reviews. Skills We’re Looking For 8+ years in sales operations, sales strategy, management consulting, or a related analytical role, including 4+ years supporting enterprise sales teams directly. 4+ years managing people, ideally analysts or operations managers. Track record of owning a forecast process and improving its accuracy. Experience running sales operating cadences and business reviews with senior sales leaders. Working knowledge of annual planning: quota setting, territory design, capacity and coverage modeling, and ramp assumptions. Strong analytical skills, including building models in spreadsheets and working with CRM and BI data (for example, Salesforce, Tableau, Looker, or SQL). Ability to turn analysis into a clear recommendation and defend it with sales leaders. Bonus Skills​ Experience in B2B SaaS, ideally with a consumption-based or usage-based pricing model. Experience designing sales incentive programs or SPIFFs. Experience supporting regional expansion or new-segment launches. Prior experience as the dedicated operations partner to a regional sales leader.
