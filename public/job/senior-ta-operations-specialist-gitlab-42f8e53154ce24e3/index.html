AN OVERVIEW OF THIS ROLE Talent Acquisition at GitLab is evolving and you'll be at the center of it. As a Senior TA Operations Specialist, you own the full technology infrastructure that powers how GitLab recruits at scale. That means everything from ATS configuration and integrations to AI agent development and automation — you're the technical authority across the entire TA tech stack. This isn't a 'run the tools' role. You'll architect intelligent workflows, maintain the systems recruiters depend on every day, and build the AI layer that makes those systems smarter over time. You'll serve as the lead TA AI liaison, ensuring every AI tool and agent we deploy meets our standards for security, compliance, and fairness. If you're energized by the challenge of owning complex technical infrastructure while simultaneously pushing what's possible with AI, and you want to do it at a company that ships fast and trusts you to lead then this role is built for you. Manage all tools configuration, maintenance, and core setup across the technology ecosystem Own all integrations across the TA stack — including build, monitoring, and incident response when integrations fail; serve as the primary point of escalation for integration errors and outages AI Agent & Automation Development Design, build, and deploy end-to-end AI agents and automation workflows for recruiter-facing and enterprise TA use cases Own automation architecture and workflow mapping across the TA infrastructure layer Lead AI proposal triage — assessing feasibility, process impact, and candidate experience implications before approvals are granted Drive prompt engineering, model management, and ongoing performance monitoring for all deployed agents Tools Ownership, Roadmap, & Planning Lead the TA systems roadmap — owning evaluation, RFPs, and tech stack planning for the TA team Manage vendor relationships for all TA technologies Own tools configuration, maintenance, and Enablement in evaluating non-AI tool needs Governance, Compliance & Enablement Own AI governance framework and policy for the TA function — including bias auditing and fairness reviews for all AI tools and agents Lead data privacy and security compliance reviews for AI tools in partnership with Legal and Security Build and deliver AI-specific enablement curriculum and adoption programs for recruiters and TA stakeholders Author and maintain all AI-specific documentation, playbooks, and SOPs within the TA knowledge library Contribute AI agent performance data to TA dashboards and own AI performance reporting WHAT YOU'LL BRING Demonstrated experience owning and configuring enterprise ATS platforms and HR/TA tech stacks including stages, fields, integrations, and user access management Hands-on experience building and deploying AI agents or automation workflows in an enterprise environment Strong understanding of prompt engineering, LLM behavior, and model performance monitoring Experience managing integrations across platforms — including diagnosing and resolving integration failures Familiarity with AI governance, bias auditing, and compliance frameworks A genuine curiosity about emerging recruiting technology — you bring ideas before you're asked Experience in recruiting operations, HR tech, or a related domain required ABOUT THE TEAM The TA Operations team at GitLab is built around two complementary functions: TA Technology, and Process & Enablement. We're a small team with a large scope, which means you'll have real ownership and real impact from day one.
