Job Description

Secure Every Identity, from AI to Human Identity is the key to unlocking the potential of AI. Okta secures AI by building the trusted, neutral infrastructure that enables organizations to safely embrace this new era. This work requires a relentless drive to solve complex challenges with real-world stakes. We are looking for builders and owners who operate with speed and urgency and execute with excellence. This is an opportunity to do career-defining work. We're all in on this mission. If you are too, let's talk. The ISV Ecosystem team sits within the Okta Products and Technology organization and is focused on expanding our breadth and depth of SaaS integrations across both Okta Workforce and Auth0 product areas, a C-level priority for the company. Our team ensures SaaS ISVs (independent software vendors) have the knowledge, tools, and incentives to build integrations that extend our core product functionality. We are driving the formation and adoption of new identity standards focused on building an interconnected and secure AI ecosystem. Okta’s ecosystem is a core differentiator, and developing a scalable approach to ecosystem development will be a critical driver of our next stage of growth. As an ISV Success Manager , you will nurture ISV relationships to expand the Okta Integration Network. Balancing internal strategy with external technical guidance, you will support ISVs through their integration journey: from discovery and build support to roadmap influence. You will act as a bridge between integrators and cross-functional stakeholders (Product, Engineering, Product Marketing, Partnerships, etc.) to improve the integrator experience and shape partner programs. Your success will directly impact ISV satisfaction, ecosystem growth, and platform adoption across Okta and Auth0. The ideal candidate combines strong communication, analytical, and technical skills to influence ISV roadmaps, identify integration requirements, and assist through technical roadblocks. You must thrive in cross-functional environments and treat ISVs as true customers, solving strategic business and technical challenges to scale the Okta ecosystem. We look for a deep interest in identity and security, and the ability to evangelize Okta’s value to both builders and buyers. As the voice of Okta, you will also represent us at industry events and drive thought leadership.
