Rubrik Job Board: GSI Account Executive - LTM/HCL

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote - New Jersey Serve as the day-to-day business development lead for Rubrik's partnerships with LTIMindtree and HCLTech in the Americas. Drive partner-sourced and partner-influenced pipeline by connecting each GSI's account teams, practices, and delivery organizations with Rubrik's field and specialist teams. Turn executive alignment into repeatable execution: joint account plans, active deals, enabled sellers, and published wins. Key Responsibilities : Pipeline Generation and Deal Support Own pipeline targets for LTIMindtree and HCLTech, covering both partner-sourced expansion (PSX) and new-logo (PSNL) opportunities. Co-develop target account lists with each GSI and Rubrik AEs and Regional Directors; prioritize by customer fit, partner presence, and deal potential. Drive opportunities from first conversation to close: deal registration, partner engagement, executive sponsorship, and escalation of blockers. Run weekly pipeline and deal reviews with each partner and maintain clean Salesforce data (partner attribution, account categorization, owners, deal stages). Surface deal risks early, including stalled or early-stage deals and misaligned account ownership, and drive corrective action. Partner Relationship Management Build and maintain relationships with each GSI's alliance leads, sales leaders, practice heads, and delivery and managed services teams. Maintain a joint business plan for each partner with goals, target accounts, executive sponsors, and quarterly milestones. Lead regular business reviews, win/loss reviews, and joint account mapping sessions. Act as the single point of coordination between the partner and Rubrik's AEs, SEs, CDMs, Regional Directors, and specialty sellers. Enablement and Joint Solutions Plan and run sales and technical enablement for LTIMindtree and HCLTech sellers, architects, and delivery teams on Rubrik's Cloud Resilience and Identity Resilience value. Support joint offerings and managed services built around Rubrik, working with Product Marketing, Partner Programs, and the GSI practices. Make sure partner teams have current collateral, pricing and packaging guidance, and access to the right Rubrik specialists. Help partners shape services attach and SOW content so Rubrik is scoped into proposals early. Go-to-Market and Visibility Plan joint campaigns, workshops, executive briefings, and events with each partner, in coordination with Marketing. Capture and publish win stories, references, and internal win announcements, with proper credit to partner and Rubrik contributors. Prepare weekly and quarterly updates for GSI leadership covering partner highlights, risks, pipeline, and upcoming activities. Relay field and partner feedback to Product, Sales Leadership, and Partner Programs, and align with global partner counterparts. Required Qualifications : 6+ years in enterprise software, cybersecurity, or data protection, including 3+ years in partner, alliance, or business development roles with GSIs or large services firms. Track record of generating pipeline and supporting six- and seven-figure deals through partners. Working knowledge of how GSIs operate: account teams, practices, delivery, and services-led deal structures. Strong Salesforce discipline, forecasting, and pipeline management skills. Excellent communication and relationship skills, with comfort engaging both senior partner executives and field teams. Ability to work across time zones with partner teams in the US and India. Travel of approximately 20-30%. Preferred Qualifications Existing relationships at LTIMindtree or HCLTech. Background in data protection, cyber resilience, or cloud infrastructure. Familiarity with MEDDPICC, partner deal registration, and co-sell motions with Microsoft, AWS, or Google Cloud. Experience building joint solutions or managed service offerings with partners. Success Metrics (First 12 Months) : Partner-sourced and influenced pipeline coverage against target and closed-won bookings through each partner. Active joint business plan and named target account list for each partner. Number of partner sellers and architects enabled, and joint offerings or reference wins published.
