🏢 databricks 📍 Singapore 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

CSQ427R518 About the role Databricks Learning & Enablement trains customers, partners, and Databricks employees on the Data Intelligence Platform. As a Staff Technical Instructor, you deliver our most strategic technical training and set the instructional quality bar for a global community of instructors and training partners. You partner with curriculum, operations, and account teams to translate real-world delivery experience into better courses, stronger instructors, and measurable learner outcomes. This is a senior individual contributor role reporting to the Director of Global Delivery Excellence. You are a thought partner to Senior Director+ stakeholders on training delivery. What you will do Lead associate- and professional-level instructor-led training across most of the Databricks catalog, virtual and in person, including tailored programs for strategic customers and large-scale events such as Data + AI Summit. Partner with curriculum teams on new course development and curriculum revamps so content reflects real delivery needs. Mentor Databricks Certified Instructors training partner instructors globally with timely, candid, actionable feedback. Use CSAT, NPS, and skill-progression data to identify and implement improvements in learner outcomes, instructor operations, tooling, and scheduling. Advise delivery leadership and account-facing teams on training plans, priorities, and emerging trends. Build AI-based tools to evolve training delivery readiness and in-class experiences. What we look for Deep subject-matter expertise in at least one Databricks domain: Data Engineering, Data Analysis, Machine Learning, or Generative AI Most Databricks certifications completed, at both associate and professional levels Hands-on proficiency with the Databricks workspace, Databricks SQL, Lakeflow Declarative Pipelines, Lakeflow Jobs, Git folders, and Unity Catalog A track record of influencing training products, delivery processes, or tooling beyond your own classroom Experience mentoring and tailoring your coaching style to the individual Comfort presenting to and advising senior technical and business stakeholders Continuous study of industry trends and active investment in depth across additional technical domains Hands-on experience using AI to speed up course preparation, demo building, or delivery operations Preferred qualifications ~8+ years in technical instruction, solutions architecture, data engineering, or data science, including ~4+ years delivering technical training to enterprise audiences Experience as a lead instructor or curriculum contributor for a technical course Experience designing or running train-the-trainer programs Experience running large-scale or event-based training programs About Databricks Databricks is the Data and AI company. More than 20,000 organizations worldwide — including adidas, AT&T, Bayer, Block, Mastercard, Rivian, Unilever, and 70% of the Fortune 500 — rely on the Databricks Data + AI Platform to build and scale data and AI apps, analytics and agents. Headquartered in San Francisco with 30+ offices around the globe, Databricks offers a unified platform that includes Genie, Lakebase, Agent Bricks, Lakeflow, Lakehouse, and Unity Catalog.
