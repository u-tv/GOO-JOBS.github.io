Rubrik Job Board: Global Head of GSI Solutions Architecture

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote - United States Global Partner Engineering is the technical engine behind Rubrik's partner business. We work with global systems integrators, channel partners, managed service providers and technology alliances to help them build, sell and support Rubrik at scale. Our GSI team works with the largest integrators in the world. We co-create joint solutions with their practices, enable their practitioners through certification, and partner with our field teams to win large, complex deals. Partners are a core part of how Rubrik grows, and this team makes that real. What this role will do : You will lead Rubrik's global GSI technical organization, with two integrated charters that work as one system. Global Solutions - Architects build the play for named global GSIs: joint offers, reference architectures, blueprints and certification strategy with partner COEs. They work with Product on what is coming next and feed roadmap gaps back. Global Go-to-market - Architects run the play in the region. They pair with partner sellers and Rubrik account teams to build joint pipelines, shape deals, lead demos and POCs through close, and activate Black Belts. In this role, you will : Own the global GSI technical strategy. Define the KPIs, targets and investment priorities that connect team effort to partner-sourced and influenced pipeline and revenue. Shape the joint solution portfolio. Decide which GSIs get which offers, and run a repeatable pipeline that takes each offer from idea to adoption across our four product lines: enterprise and cloud data, identity, SaaS and AI agents. Build the operating system for the business. Run on cadence, clear ownership and one source of truth, so leaders spend their time on strategy and outcomes, not status. Drive deal excellence across every GSI motion, whether GSI-led, co-led or field-led. Bring the visibility and discipline to prioritize the right opportunities and win the technical decision. Be the senior technical voice with GSI executives and COE leaders. Partner with Rubrik Product, Marketing, Sales and Alliances to turn what GSIs need to sell into what we build next. Scale the partner field. Enable and certify GSI practitioners so partners can position and deliver Rubrik on their own. Attract, develop and retain high-agency leaders across the Americas, EMEA and APJ, and build a deep bench. Lead the team's AI transformation, with a clear productivity baseline, targets and new ways of working. Why you will love this job : Growth at scale. Rubrik has passed $1.6B in subscription ARR and is growing over 30% year over year. A category we created. Cyber resilience started here, and the market now follows our lead. Partners are a strategic priority, with executive sponsorship from the top and a seat at the table. A real build. A strong foundation and a clear mandate to scale and shape a global organization. The most exciting product shift in the industry. Identity resilience, SaaS data protection and Rubrik Agent Cloud give GSIs new services to build around. Direct impact. Your offers and relationships will show up in pipeline and closed deals you can point to. A team that moves fast, tries AI first, and rewards people who own outcomes. Requirements : 12+ years in solution architecture, pre-sales engineering, partner engineering or a related technical go-to-market role. 5+ years leading teams, including managing managers, with a global footprint across regions. Proven track record of building repeatable solutions, blueprints or offers that the field and partners adopt at scale. Experience designing KPIs, scorecards and operating cadences that drive behavior and outcomes. Executive presence. You can earn credibility with senior leaders at GSIs and inside Rubrik. Strong grasp of enterprise data, cloud or security. You will ramp quickly on Rubrik's platform. Comfort with Salesforce and data-driven management of pipeline and deals. Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience. Nice to have : Experience working directly with GSIs or in alliance and partner organizations. Background in cyber resilience, backup and recovery, identity, SaaS or AI and agentic systems.
