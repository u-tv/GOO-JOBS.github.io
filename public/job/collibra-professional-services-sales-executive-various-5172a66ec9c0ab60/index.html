Collibra: Professional Services Sales Executive

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote, Netherlands Joining Collibra's Professional Services Team Collibra helps organizations take command of data and AI. Our Professional Services team plays a critical role in helping customers realize value faster, accelerate adoption, and build the foundation for long-term success. As a Professional Services Sales Executive, you will be responsible for growing Collibra's Professional Services business across new and existing customers. You will partner closely with Account Executives and territory sales leadership, Solution Architects, Customer Success, and Services Delivery teams to position and sell services that help customers achieve their business objectives. You will own a Professional Services bookings target tied to aligned regions and serve as a trusted advisor, helping customers understand how strategic services engagements can accelerate outcomes and maximize the value of their investment in Collibra. This is a remote role based in Amsterdam.
