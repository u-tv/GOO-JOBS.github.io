Second-Line Manager, Enterprise Sales

🏢 stripe 📍 London 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team The GTM team is a highly consultative sales team focused on winning new customers and helping businesses grow with Stripe. We work closely with colleagues across Stripe and with Ent C-Level, funded founders, business leaders, investors and ecosystem partners to make Stripe a strategic partner for ambitious companies in the UK. What you’ll do As a Sales Leader, you’ll play an instrumental role in driving Stripe’s commercial growth and strengthening our presence in the UK. You’ll lead and coach high-performing account executives, set the market strategy, and work directly with customers and prospects to help them make payments and financial infrastructure a competitive advantage. Alongside leading the sales team, you’ll be the face of Stripe’s GTM organisation in the UK: building the local ecosystem, developing trusted senior relationships, and representing Stripe in internal and external engagements. This role combines disciplined sales execution with broader market leadership. You’ll be willing to take on critical strategic activities beyond the day-to-day remit where they advance Stripe’s mission and long-term opportunity. Responsibilities Recruit, develop and lead a high-performing team of account executives, fostering a culture of accountability, collaboration and customer impact. Develop and execute the long-term vision and GTM strategy in-region, translating local market insight into clear priorities and managing day-to-day performance against key sales metrics. Be accountable for new customer acquisition and the team’s commercial targets, partnering across Stripe to support product adoption, successful activation and long-term customer growth. Drive deal strategy and execution, including pipeline development, opportunity management, complex negotiations and accurate forecasting; take a hands-on role in strategic opportunities. Coach the team in consultative, value-led and solution-based selling through every stage of the sales cycle, helping customers connect Stripe’s capabilities to their business goals. Build and maintain enduring relationships with customers and prospects up to C-level, acting as a trusted senior point of engagement for key accounts. Inspire, motivate and support team members’ personal and career development, strengthening both individual impact and the team’s leadership pipeline. Build and strengthen Stripe’s ecosystem in-region, developing relationships with founders, investors, accelerators, industry associations, technology partners and other influential organisations that support business growth. Partner with Marketing and GTM Partnerships to turn ecosystem relationships into joint initiatives, community engagement and sustainable commercial opportunities. Be the face of Stripe GTM in the UK, building market awareness and credibility through relevant industry events, executive forums, customer engagements and speaking opportunities. Represent the UK’s GTM priorities internally, bringing customer needs, market developments and growth opportunities into regional and global discussions, and aligning stakeholders around action. Represent Stripe in external engagements with business leaders, ecosystem organisations and relevant public-sector stakeholders, in coordination with the appropriate Stripe teams. Collaborate closely with Sales, Product, Solutions Architecture, Customer Success, Sales Operations, Strategy & Operations, Marketing and GTM Partnerships to remove blockers and deliver a joined-up customer experience. Take on critical strategic activities beyond the team’s day-to-day sales responsibilities where they create meaningful value for Stripe in the UK. This may include serving on relevant government or industry advisory boards, participating in working groups, or leading cross-functional initiatives, where appropriate and subject to relevant Stripe approvals. Minimum requirements 8+ years of relevant sales experience, including sales management experience and a track record of delivering results in a high-growth business. Demonstrated ability to recruit, develop and coach a high-performing sales team, with a strong commitment to people development. Experience consistently forecasting and managing a business against ambitious targets over multiple quarters. Ability to create strategy, direction and structure with a high level of autonomy, balancing immediate execution with long-term market development. Experience leading consultative sales cycles, shaping deal strategy and navigating complex organisations and senior-level negotiations. Ability to build trusted executive relationships with customers, prospects and ecosystem partners, with a strong understanding of the UK business landscape. Credibility and confidence representing an organisation in internal leadership discussions and external customer, industry and public-sector engagements. Ability to understand and articulate technical concepts, translating them into relevant business outcomes. Strong cross-functional collaboration and sound judgement, including the ability to recognise when broader representation or commitments require specialist input and approval. Willingness to contribute beyond the core sales-management remit and take ownership of critical strategic initiatives that advance Stripe’s opportunity in the UK. Professional fluency in English.
