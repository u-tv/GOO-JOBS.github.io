Enterprise Account Lead

🏢 airbnb 📍 United States 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Airbnb was born in 2007 when two hosts welcomed three guests to their San Francisco home, and has since grown to over 5 million hosts who have welcomed over 2 billion guest arrivals in almost every country across the globe. Every day, hosts offer unique stays and experiences that make it possible for guests to connect with communities in a more authentic way. The Community You Will Join Every day, Airbnb hosts provide unique Stays and Experiences that connect guests with local communities in authentic and meaningful ways. These offerings are the core of what makes Airbnb special, creating connections and unforgettable memories for travelers worldwide. The Enterprise Account team represents the largest and most strategic supply partnerships within Airbnb and plays a vital role in growing the business. This role goes far beyond traditional account management. The Difference You Will Make As an Enterprise Account Lead, you will own the commercial performance of Airbnb’s largest property commercial partners. You will design and cultivate strong, collaborative relationships to shape their strategic roadmap, negotiate commercial terms, and optimize their listings at scale so Airbnb is competitive and the preferred distribution partner. You will own the end to end full relationship and commercial growth of C-Suite partners. You will shape the strategy for how these partners perform on Airbnb, driving outcomes that matter for hosts, guests, and Airbnb's business. A Typical Day Act as a strategic partner to relevant stakeholders to inform Airbnb enterprise account strategy, and communicate impact to relevant teams clearly Partner and influence key internal leaders on their roadmaps, ensure their teams support these key partnerships, and unlock revenue through product and policy enhancements Build and maintain trusted relationships with founders, board members, and executive stakeholders from partner accounts Navigate complex commercial conversations — multi-stakeholder deals, evolving usage patterns, and competitive evaluations in partnership with internal stakeholders Serve as the voice of the customer by gathering feedback and conveying market needs to inform our product roadmap Proactively identify and mitigate retention and competitive risks while maintaining high customer satisfaction Own and influence the business outcomes for assigned high valued accounts, strategically planning for the next phases of commercial growth Have a deep understanding of Airbnb policy, processes, and procedures to provide strategic decisions to (balancing long term success of Airbnb and our partners) Provide data driven insights quickly and accurately to inform business decisions that support commercial growth. This includes monitoring and analyzing over and underperformance, market trends, growth opportunities, and anomalies, and regularly communicate performance to executives to influence desired outcomes Present strategy, business performance, and recommendations directly to senior leadership and executive stakeholders, and drive alignment on the path forward Your Expertise Required qualifications 10+ years of experience leading business development, account management, business operations, or partnership initiative with large enterprise clients. Experience analyzing, structuring, and negotiating complex deals that align across multiple business units. Experience developing and selling complex business solutions at the C suite level. Ability to analyze data to uncover trends and inform decisions. Excellent verbal and written communication and presentation skills. Ability to travel at least 30% of the time for client and internal meetings. Track record of setting strategy and delivering measurable results. Comfort negotiating commercial deals that balance short term needs and long term implications. Ability to balance time between strategic initiatives and hands-on execution when needed. Experience leveraging AI tools for data analysis and deck development. Interest in travel, hospitality, and working with a high performing team. BA and MBA preferred Key Skills: Strategic thinking & Analytics Define and execute a win-win commercial strategy for a portfolio of top tier partnerships Translate market and performance data into a clear roadmap for growth Ability to problem solve and solution large scale challenges Partner and internal influence Shape partner behavior, not just manage it. Earn deep trust with partners while driving the outcomes Airbnb needs. Build and maintain relationships at the C-suite level clients Drive pricing, availability, and booking strategies. Advocate for roadmap prioritization and maintain alignment with stakeholders Identify gaps and address issues in partner facing workflows Execution and ownership Drive projects from strategy through execution, holding yourself and cross functional partners accountable for deliverables and timelines.
