Dremio: Software Engineer - Developer Experience

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Portugal - Remote Be Part of Building the Future Dremio is the unified lakehouse platform for self-service analytics and AI, serving hundreds of global enterprises, including Maersk, Amazon, Regeneron, NetApp, and S&P Global. Customers rely on Dremio for cloud, hybrid, and on-prem lakehouses to power their data mesh, data warehouse migration, data virtualization, and unified data access use cases. Based on open source technologies, including Apache Iceberg and Apache Arrow, Dremio provides an open lakehouse architecture enabling the fastest time to insight and platform flexibility at a fraction of the cost. Learn more at www.dremio.com. About the role In this role you will get opportunities to implement and maintain CI/CD systems and developer productivity solutions. You will be responsible for both regularly scheduled work as well as on-demand break-fix work to support Dremio’s CI/CD system, identifying areas of improvement, and implementing changes to increase efficiency and effectiveness of those systems. Bring your industry experience to improve the efficiency and scale of Dremio’s infrastructure via innovation on tooling and methodologies. You should have empathy for developers and a passion for engineering productivity. What you’ll be doing Solve problems for fellow Dremio engineers by: Leveraging and contributing to playbooks to implement solutions to unblock them Identifying short term workarounds and documenting them Automating processes where manual processes exist. Working on infrastructure across all major clouds (AWS, GCP, Azure). Managing access to key infrastructure. Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines and associated systems to increase organizational efficiency. Automate local development workflows. Analyze performance bottlenecks, root cause issues, file defects, follow up with developers, and document resolutions. Clear focus on keeping our development branches ‘green’. Release engineering - automating processes to push software to delivery endpoints. Report out on the status, reliability and robustness of our CI/CD systems. Actively participate in code reviews. What we’re looking for B.S. or M.S in Computer Science or in a related technical field 1+ years of experience on large scale distributed systems Comfortable navigating the fast moving, unstructured nature of a hyper-growth startup. Demonstrable Knowledge and Experience with at least one cloud platform (AWS, Azure, GCP) Basic understanding of cloud networking and cloud architecture. Proficiency in any of Java/C++/Python/Go Knowledge of Maven or similar build environments Hands on experience in pipeline automation using CI/CD tools like Jenkins Experience in Infrastructure as Code Experience with Terraform, BASH, Docker, Jenkins, and Kubernetes Ability to write technical reports, effectively summarizing key data Passion for learning and then delivering solutions using latest technologies Strong Affinity for collaboration and teamwork Bonus points if you have Working knowledge in data lakes and data warehousing Proficient with SQL What we value At Dremio, we hold ourselves to high standards when it comes to People, Thinking, and Action. Our Gnarlies (that's what we call our employees) communicate with clarity, drive accountability, and are respectful towards each other. We confront brutal facts and focus on results while operating with a sense of urgency and building a "flywheel". People who like to jump in and drive momentum will thrive in our #GnarlyLife.
