Regulatory Operations Program Manager

🏢 lyft 📍 Toronto, Canada 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

At Lyft, our purpose is to serve and connect. We aim to achieve this by cultivating a work environment where all team members belong and have the opportunity to thrive. We’re looking for a quick study, who is no stranger to working with shifting rules and regulations, to join our team. You’ll operationalize business and regulatory requirements, advocate for process improvements, and collaborate with Legal, Product and Engineering to solve problems with solutions that scale across platforms. You will balance growth objectives with regulatory requirements, but that won’t phase you as you’re no stranger to challenge or ambiguity. You have a deep sense of empathy for our users (both internally and externally) and excel in asking probing questions, detecting unstated assumptions, driving consensus, resolving conflicts, and prioritizing competing objectives. You enjoy learning new things and working with many people to achieve success. You consistently own the work, focus on delivering impact, and reimagine what’s possible. In short, you make it happen, and you do it with a great sense of humor and an excitement to change the face of ridesharing.
