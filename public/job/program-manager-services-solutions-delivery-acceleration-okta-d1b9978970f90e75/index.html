Role Summary Reporting to the Sr. Manager, Strategic Programs and Delivery Governance, the Program Manager, Services Solutions & Delivery Acceleration is a key operational and technical enablement leader within our Global Professional Services and Delivery Success organization. This role treats &quot;services as a product&quot;—transforming custom, highly complex implementation workflows into standardized, packaged service solutions and reusable delivery acceleration toolkits. Working alongside Product Management, Engineering, and Delivery teams, you will manage the full lifecycle of packaged services solutions (e.g., QuickStarts, migration packages, specialized health checks). You will identify, define, and launch both technical accelerators (API scripts, automation tools, code repositories) and methodological accelerators (workshop kits, intake templates, automated documentation) designed to drastically reduce customer time-to-value and boost implementation efficiency. As a member of the Strategic Programs team, you will also support the adoption of Generative AI to scale delivery acceleration—using AI to automate code sample generation, synthesize configuration schemas, and build intelligent delivery templates that empower both internal teams and partner ecosystems. Key Responsibilities Services Delivery Acceleration (Technical Toolkits) Technical Accelerators: Collaborate with technical delivery SMEs to harvest, standardize, and package technical IP (e.g., reusable API scripts, automation code, configuration templates, and connector modules). Field IP Submission & Governance: Establish and operate a global IP harvesting mechanism—incentivizing and vetting contributions from internal delivery architects and external partner engineers into centralized repositories (GitHub, Highspot, Confluence). Central Repository Management: Maintain a centralized technical asset repository (e.g., GitHub, Highspot, Confluence) ensuring that internal consultants and partner delivery teams can easily access up-to-date acceleration tools. Partner Acceleration Alignment: Package acceleration toolkits specifically for external Service Delivery Partners and GSIs to ensure partner-led implementations achieve equal velocity and quality as direct engagements. New Product Introduction (NPI) Services Readiness Product & Engineering Alignment: Serve as the primary Professional Services liaison to Product Management and Engineering for incoming product features, ensuring services solution packages and delivery accelerators are launched in lockstep with core product releases. Collaborate with the Strategic Team for reporting and executive communications. Early Adopter & Alpha/Beta Services Validation: Partner with early-adopter customer delivery teams to validate initial service patterns, converting custom project learnings into standard commercial service offerings. Acceleration Adoption, Time-to-Value & Outcome Analytics Measure Adoption & Impact: Define and track critical performance metrics, focusing on packaged service sales volume, accelerator adoption rates, attach rates, time-to-value metrics. Continuous Improvement Loops: Collect structured feedback from internal consultants, service delivery partners, and customer surveys to continuously refine existing acceleration assets and prioritize new investments. AI-Powered Delivery Acceleration AI-Assisted Code & Asset Generation: Leverage Generative AI tools to curate the creation of technical code samples, configuration scripts, and standard technical documentation. Solution Opportunity Mining: Apply AI tools across to systematically identify repeatable custom builds and establish priorities for new accelerator development. AI-Assisted Quality Verification : Design and deploy AI validation utilities to provide a layer of assurance for services delivery projects.
