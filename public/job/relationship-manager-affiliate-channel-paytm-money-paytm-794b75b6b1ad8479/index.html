Relationship Manager - Affiliate Channel - Paytm Money

🏢 paytm 📍 Mumbai, Maharashtra 💼 On-roll 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Paytm Money Paytm Money is a SEBI-registered investing and trading platform for stocks, F&O, mutual funds, ETFs, IPOs and NPS — built for long-term investors and active traders. It offers tools such as Stock SIP, GTT & basket orders, portfolio insights and Pay Later (MTF), with no account-opening fee and zero AMC for life. Paytm Money cites 6M+ investors and operates as a trading and clearing member across NSE, BSE, MCX and other Indian exchanges. About the role You will be part of the Alliances & Partnerships (Affiliate) team at Paytm Money, reporting to the Team Lead / Manager, Affiliate Partnerships. You will identify, onboard, and manage affiliate partners who bring customers to the Paytm Money platform, and own each relationship end to end, from the first pitch to account-opening and traded-account targets. The role offers direct exposure to Product, Marketing, and senior leadership, with a clear path to Team Lead. Key responsibilities : Partner sourcing and onboarding • Source, pitch, and onboard new affiliate partners, including marketing agencies, influencers, finance communities, fintech platforms, and training institutes. • Explain Paytm Money products (stocks, mutual funds, F&O, IPOs) clearly so partners can promote them with confidence. Partner management and targets • Manage key partner accounts through regular performance reviews, quick issue resolution, and steady engagement. • Deliver monthly account-opening and traded-account targets for your partner portfolio. Campaigns, tracking, and attribution • Work with Product and Tech to turn partner needs into deep links, tracking, creatives, and payout setups. • Plan and run campaigns with Business Development and Marketing, and track results by partner (installs, KYC completions, first trades, CAC). • Check tracking and attribution in the MMP (AppsFlyer) and flag discrepancies before they affect payouts. Reporting and compliance • Keep partner documentation, reporting, and payout reconciliation accurate. • Follow SEBI and company compliance guidelines on partner communication. • Share weekly performance reports and partner insights with the Team Lead and senior management. Requirements • Bachelor's degree in any stream; MBA or PGDM preferred. • 1–3 years of experience in business development, partnerships, affiliate or performance marketing, or B2B/solution sales, ideally in fintech, broking, BFSI, or payments. • Proven record of sourcing and engaging corporate, SME, or creator partners and meeting Paytm Money. • Strong written and spoken communication, and confidence pitching to partners and internal stakeholders. • Analytical, consultative approach; comfortable with Excel and campaign data. • Willingness to travel within Mumbai for partner meetings. Why join us • Shape India’s digital economy — help consumers and merchants pay, get paid, and access financial services every day. • Build across Payments, Marketing Services, and Financial Services — QR & Soundbox, Payment Gateway, UPI, recharges & bills, ads & commerce, lending (consumer & merchant), Paytm Gold, and Paytm Money. • Work on products used across India’s daily commerce — consumer and merchant surfaces at national scale. • Own outcomes that matter — reliability, growth, risk, and experience on the rails and products that move the country’s payments and financial services. • Work ownership-first — ship fast, think scale, stay customer-obsessed; if you are not the customer, don’t build it. • Grow with the platform — collaborate across businesses and AI-forward agendas in a culture of accountability, empowerment, and ownership.
