Contingent Workforce Program Lead

🏢 lyft 📍 Nashville, TN 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

At Lyft, our purpose is to serve and connect. We aim to achieve this by cultivating a work environment where all team members belong and have the opportunity to thrive. Lyft is looking for a strategic partner to the business to support our Global Contingent Workforce Program. The Program Lead will balance strategic priorities while helping to manage day-to-day administrative tasks, working cross functionally with teams like Legal, IT, Procurement and our hiring managers. You’ll report to the contingent workforce program manager and help the team prepare to grow their global footprint while automating and creating education around the CW programs ability to support. Responsibilities: Work cross-functionally to support day-to-day operations of the contingent workforce program Help manage the MSP and VMS teams including process, supplier management and automation Identify areas of risk proactively and partner with cross-functional teams to drive solutions to mitigate such risks Identify operational opportunities and help improve existing or develop new processes Consult with business leaders to better understand their workforce needs and advise on the appropriate strategy Establish and develop relationships with all key stakeholders: suppliers, internal customers, internal People team, Procurement, Legal, IT, and other business stakeholders to drive and understand global operational needs Ensure compliance among contingent workforce and suppliers Experience: 3+ years work experience in project management, managing complex and business critical projects Comprehensive understanding of contingent workforce guidelines and best practices, as well as contingent workforce risk and compliance strategies Experience documenting processes and creating step-by-step guides or SOPs, with the ability to translate complex workflows into clear, repeatable documentation Experience working in any Vendor Management System (VMS) Self-directed, detailed oriented problem solver with demonstrated experience in developing and improving processes Strong communication and interpersonal skills Bonus points: 2+ years of experience in workforce management and Managed Service Provider (MSP) operations Experience in data analysis, with the ability to review complex datasets and extract key insights to inform strategic decision-making Experience with automation tools such as Claude Benefits: Great medical, dental, and vision insurance options with additional programs available when enrolled Mental health benefits Family building benefits Child care and pet benefits 401(k) plan with company match to help save for your future In addition to
