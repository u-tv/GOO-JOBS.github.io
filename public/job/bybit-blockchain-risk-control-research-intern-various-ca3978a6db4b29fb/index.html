Job Summary The role seeks a skilled researcher with a background in security or risk control. You will be responsible for analyzing blockchain data, leading forensic investigations into crypto-related crimes (theft, scams, market manipulation), and studying vulnerabilities within blockchain protocols and smart contracts. Main Responsibilities Data Analysis: Analyze blockchain data to identify and flag abnormal cases. Forensics & Investigation: Trace and lead forensic investigations into real-world crypto theft, scams, and market manipulation. Security Research: Study vulnerabilities and security issues related to blockchain protocols and smart contracts. Reporting: Write high-quality industry or academic research reports and papers. System Optimization: Collaborate with development teams to improve blockchain data collection and threat identification. Incident Response: Partner with internal and external stakeholders to handle accident/incident responses. Key Requirements Education: Bachelor’s, Master’s, or Ph.D. in Blockchain Security, Computer Science, Data Science, or a related field. Publication Record: A strong track record of publications in top-level industry or academic conferences is highly valued. Programming: Proficiency in languages used for blockchain analytics (e.g., SQL, Python, JavaScript). Tools: Hands-on experience with blockchain monitoring/forensic tools and analytics platforms. Domain Knowledge: Deep understanding of blockchain protocols, smart contracts, consensus mechanisms, and cryptography. Soft Skills: Effective communication and strong collaboration abilities.
