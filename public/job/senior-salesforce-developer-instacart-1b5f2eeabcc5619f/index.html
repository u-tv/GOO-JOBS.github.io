Overview We are hiring a Senior Salesforce Developer to join the IT Business Systems team, which owns the CRM platform that every business unit at Instacart depends on. You will design, build, and ship solutions across Sales Cloud, Salesforce CPQ, Service Cloud, and Contact Center, with an immediate focus on the CPQ Phase 2 migration, the HighRadius integration, and the migration of our contact center to Agentforce Contact Center. These are multi-million dollar initiatives touching Sales, Finance, and Customer Experience, and you will present your work to VP-level stakeholders. You will also use AI-assisted tooling such as Claude Code and Cursor to deliver faster and at higher quality, and to raise the engineering bar for the team.
