Storyblok: Senior Global Partner Specialist - USA

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote Storyblok is a headless CMS that enables marketers and developers to create with joy and publish with confidence. It empowers you to deliver structured and consistent content everywhere: websites, apps, AI search, and beyond. Legendary brands like Virgin Media O2, Oatly, and TomTom use Storyblok to make a bigger, faster market impact. WHAT IS IN IT FOR YOU You will be joining a growing company where you can contribute to many “firsts”. Plus these benefits: Monthly remote work stipend (home internet costs, electricity). Home office equipment package right at the start (laptop, keyboard, monitor…) Home office equipment upgrade (furniture, ear plugs …) or membership to a local co-working space after your onboarding Sick leave benefit, parental leave and 25 days of annual leave plus your local national holidays Personal development fund for courses, books, conferences, and material The annual international team-building trip, quarterly and monthly online get-togethers As a fully remote company, with work-life balance at its core, you’ll enjoy flexible schedules An international team that loves to have fun at work and works hard together to accomplish shared goals Job Summary The Senior Global Partner Specialist owns the growth and commercial performance of Storyblok's global Longtail partner ecosystem, including New, Nurturing, and Emerging partners of all sizes. This role develops and delivers scalable programs that enable partners, increase partner-sourced and partner-influenced revenue, and move partners through the lifecycle from onboarding to sustained commercial activity. Operating primarily through one-to-many engagement, automation, self-service, and data-driven segmentation, the role ensures Storyblok can support and grow a broad partner base efficiently. The role also identifies practical opportunities to use AI and emerging technologies to improve partner experience, efficiency, and scale. The Senior Global Partner Specialist identifies high-potential Longtail partners that are ready to progress into the Focused segment and works cross-functionally to continuously improve Storyblok's global partner growth model. Essential Job Functions Own and continuously improve the global growth strategy and engagement model for New, Nurturing, and Emerging partners. Drive partner-sourced and partner-influenced pipeline and revenue across the Longtail partner ecosystem. Design and deliver scalable one-to-many programs that support partner activation, enablement, engagement, and commercial growth. Manage partner lifecycle communications and campaigns across relevant channels and partner cohorts. Use partner data and performance insights to improve segmentation, prioritization, lifecycle progression, and program effectiveness. Identify barriers to partner growth and lead cross-functional initiatives to improve the partner experience and commercial outcomes. Own the process for identifying, assessing, and recommending high-potential Longtail partners for progression into the Focused segment. Collaborate with Partner Management, Sales, Marketing, Partner Enablement, Solutions Engineering, Developer Experience, and Operations to deliver a connected partner journey. Identify opportunities to use AI and automation to reduce repetitive work, improve partner self-service, and scale partner engagement while preserving human involvement for high-value commercial and strategic moments. Define and monitor success measures, share insights with Partner Leadership, and continuously optimize programs based on measurable outcomes. Education and Experience 4-5 years of relevant experience in partnerships, channel management, partner growth, partner programs, business development, revenue programs, or a related SaaS environment. Demonstrated experience owning complex programs or business initiatives with measurable commercial outcomes. Experience managing or influencing a large portfolio of partners, customers, or accounts through scalable programs. Strong understanding of SaaS sales cycles, partner ecosystems, and lifecycle or growth programs. Strong analytical skills and experience using CRM, PRM, engagement, enablement, and pipeline data to guide decisions. Experience working globally and cross-functionally with commercial, marketing, enablement, operational, and technical stakeholders. Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English. Demonstrated ability to work independently and lead complex initiatives with limited oversight. Professional Competencies Commercial orientation: Connects partner activity to pipeline and revenue and prioritizes work based on business impact. Program strategy and management: Translates strategic objectives into scalable programs and leads complex initiatives from concept through optimization. One-to-many partner management: Creates relevant partner experiences across a large portfolio without relying on continuous individual account management. Lifecycle, segmentation, and prioritization: Uses partner maturity, behavior, capability, and commercial signals to guide engagement and investment. Analytical problem solving: Uses qualitative and quantitative insights to diagnose challenges and develop effective solutions. Cross-functional leadership: Aligns stakeholders across functions and influences decisions without relying on direct authority. Technology and automation mindset: Identifies practical opportunities to use automation, AI, and digital tools to improve partner experience, operational efficiency, and program scalability. Autonomy and ownership: Takes accountability for outcomes and makes sound decisions in an evolving, global environment.
