Senior Connectivity Partner Manager

🏢 airbnb 📍 United States 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Airbnb was born in 2007 when two hosts welcomed three guests to their San Francisco home, and has since grown to over 5 million hosts who have welcomed over 2 billion guest arrivals in almost every country across the globe. Every day, hosts offer unique stays and experiences that make it possible for guests to connect with communities in a more authentic way. The Community You Will Join: Every day, Airbnb hosts provide unique Stays and Experiences that connect guests with local communities in authentic and meaningful ways. These offerings are the core of what makes Airbnb special — creating connections and unforgettable memories for travelers worldwide. The Connectivity team sits at the heart of Airbnb’s Supply strategy, managing the API Partners that power a significant share of our global inventory. Our connectivity partners — property management software providers, channel managers, and distribution platforms — unlock access to millions of listings and directly shape the quality and reliability of the host and guest experience. This team plays a critical role in growing, deepening, and shaping Airbnb’s partner ecosystem. The Difference You Will Make: As a Senior Connectivity Partner Manager, you will run the program for Airbnb’s scaled connectivity partners, including the long tail of API partners who don’t have dedicated coverage today, and build our ecosystem partner program from the ground up. You are not managing a handful of accounts. You are designing and running the program that lets a small team credibly cover a big portfolio, and you are shaping how those partners show up on Airbnb. You will bring a global view of the partner ecosystem, define success through Airbnb’s outcomes, and act as the connective tissue between partners, product teams, and internal stakeholders. You are someone who earns trust, moves fast, and holds yourself accountable for results. This role goes beyond traditional account management. You will operate at the intersection of: Program design Commercial incentives Ecosystem partnerships, including third-party tools that Hosts use AI-assisted coverage Marketplace dynamics A Typical Day: Strategic Thinking Hold a portfolio view of our scaled connectivity partners and the long tail, including which are growing, which are stalling, and which levers move the most nights. Set coverage based on where the impact is, rather than by relationship depth. Define success through Airbnb's outcomes, not activity: partner productivity, feature adoption and program retention across the portfolio. Commercial Acumen Design incentive mechanics for long-tail API partners that move portfolio goals at scale Structure what we ask of ecosystem partners (co-marketing, adoption data). Partner Influence Build and run the ecosystem partner program. Understand what each one wants from Airbnb, support them with a light touch, and set up cross-functional partnerships so the program stays aligned with their strategy. Shape partner behavior through plays and programs rather than one-to-one management. Build trusted relationships with founders and decision makers at ecosystem partners and represent Airbnb in external meetings and at industry events. Product & Technical Educate partners on the product roadmap and significant product changes to accelerate feature adoption, drawing on a working understanding of API and technical integrations. Use AI tooling as part of the coverage model itself: triage, drafting, trigger handling and readouts that let one person hold a large book. Execution & Ownership Drive outcomes with minimal oversight. Own a full book of business end-to-end. Act with speed and extreme ownership. This role is for someone who sees problems and fixes them and identifies opportunities. Operate cross-functionally Partner with Product, Engineering, Finance, Legal, and Regional teams. Drive alignment across regions to ensure consistency in partner strategy. Your Expertise: Core Experience 8+ years in partner programs, platform or marketplace partnerships, or ecosystem management , including several years running a scaled partner program. Program Design: You have built the tiers, triggers and operating rhythm for a partner program and lived with the consequences: who gets a cadence, who gets an automated touch, what gets handed off. You turn what works into a playbook others can run. Commercial Fluency: Proven ability to structure, negotiate, and close deals — from term sheets to signed contracts. Comfortable in the room with senior commercial stakeholders. AI-Native Operator: You have used AI in the running of a program (triage, drafting, trigger handling, readouts), not as a side tool, and you have a strong point of view on what it lets one person cover. Deep interest in AI is a requirement for this role. Ownership & Execution: High bias for action. You don’t wait to be told — you see what needs to happen and make it happen. Track record of delivering results in ambiguous, fast-moving environments. Influencing Skills: You know how to shape behavior through trust, not just authority. Strong communicator with the ability to advocate internally and externally, Cross-Functional Collaboration: Experience working across product, analytics, legal, finance, marketing and sales operations teams to deliver outcomes for partners. Preferred Background from a marketplace or platform partner program (app ecosystems, developer or integration partner programs, seller or merchant programs). Hospitality technology (PMS, channel manager, OTA connectivity, pricing or data tools) is a plus, not a requirement. Working understanding of API integrations and the ability to work credibly with product and engineering. Your Location: This position is US - Remote Eligible.
