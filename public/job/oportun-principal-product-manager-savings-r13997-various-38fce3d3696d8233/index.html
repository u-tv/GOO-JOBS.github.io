Oportun: Principal Product Manager, Savings (R13997)

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote - US Who We Are Oportun (Nasdaq: OPRT) is a mission-driven financial services company that puts its members' financial goals within reach. With intelligent borrowing, savings, and budgeting capabilities, Oportun empowers members with the confidence to build a better financial future. Since inception, Oportun has provided more than $22.7 billion in responsible and affordable credit, saved its members more than $2.5 billion in interest and fees, and helped its members set aside an average of more than $1,800 annually. The Opportunity We’re looking for a Principal Product Manager to lead the strategy, roadmap, and execution for Oportun’s flagship, AI-powered Savings product. Savings is an important driver of member growth and retention, giving this role significant influence over the future of our financial health platform and how we help members build stronger financial futures. You’ll lead the ideation, development, launch, and scaling of new products and features while partnering closely with Engineering, Design, Data, Marketing, Legal, and Customer Support. This role reports to the Vice President & General Manager for Savings and Mobile. Candidates located in Central or Eastern time zones are strongly preferred to support team and stakeholder collaboration. At Oportun, we move fast and we hold each other to a high bar. If you care about doing your best work while actually moving the needle for real people, you'll fit right in. What You'll Do Your day-to-day You’ll lead the long-term product strategy and roadmap for Oportun’s Savings product in partnership with leadership and cross-functional stakeholders. You’ll scope and lead the development of new products and features designed to improve members’ financial health and strengthen growth and retention. You’ll use customer research, experimentation, data, and product metrics to build a deep understanding of member needs and behaviors and translate those insights into product decisions. You’ll lead a high-performing cross-functional product team across Engineering, Design, Data, Marketing, Legal, and Customer Support to bring new capabilities to market. You’ll drive alignment across teams and leadership through clear communication, thoughtful documentation, and strong product judgment. You’ll navigate complex legal and regulatory considerations while keeping the team focused on member outcomes and execution. You’ll bring ideas for how we can do things better, proactively, not just when asked. You’ll look for ways to use AI tools thoughtfully to accelerate product discovery, improve decision-making, reduce manual work, and create stronger member experiences. Who You Are The right fit looks like this You’re a curious and creative product leader who enjoys solving ambiguous problems and turning customer needs into clear product strategies. You combine strong product judgment with data, move comfortably between strategy and execution, and build trust across a wide range of partners. You’re customer-focused, solution-oriented, and comfortable navigating complexity without waiting for perfect information. You communicate clearly, build alignment, and keep teams moving toward meaningful outcomes. What You Bring Skills & experience Required 7+ years of product management experience, including experience building and scaling products in high-growth environments. A strong track record of defining product strategy, building roadmaps, and shipping products that deliver measurable customer and business outcomes. Strong customer orientation and a passion for building products that improve financial health. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, including experience with experimentation, product metrics, data analysis, and data-informed decision-making. Demonstrated success leading cross-functional product teams and building alignment across Engineering, Design, Data, Marketing, Legal, Customer Support, and senior leadership. Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to communicate complex product decisions clearly and concisely. Experience navigating ambiguity, removing blockers, and driving execution across complex product initiatives. Nice to Have Experience in financial services, fintech, digital banking, or another highly regulated industry. Experience launching products within complex legal or regulatory frameworks. Experience using SQL or similar tools to support product analysis and decision-making. Experience working with globally distributed or multinational teams. Experience using AI tools such as ChatGPT, Copilot, or similar tools to improve product discovery, analysis, or productivity. Our AI Mindset We build with AI. We lead with judgment. AI isn't a feature here. It's how we work. We're excited about what AI makes possible, and we're looking for people who share that curiosity. You don't need to be an expert. You need to be open to experimenting , willing to learn, and able to apply new tools in ways that actually move the needle. In this role, you'll be encouraged to explore AI tools, find practical use cases for your function, and bring ideas for working smarter. We believe the best outcomes come when human judgment and AI capability work together.
