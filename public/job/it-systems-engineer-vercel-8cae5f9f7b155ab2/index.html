About the Role: We're hiring an IT Systems Engineer to help us improve the way our tools, systems, and processes work together. This role is hands-on and technical, with a focus on automation, integrations, and cleaning up some of the complexity that’s built up as we’ve grown. In the near term, you’ll spend most of your time building scripts and improving workflows while leveraging AI, and helping us reduce tool sprawl and vendor noise. Over time, we’re looking for someone who can help bring more structure and long-term thinking to how our team operates. If you like solving real problems, working across teams, and turning messy systems into something that just works, we’d love to talk. This is an in-person role based at our San Francisco headquarters. The successful candidate will be expected to work onsite to effectively collaborate with our team and support our office's IT needs.
