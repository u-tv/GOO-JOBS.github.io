About the Role We are looking for a highly skilled Video Editor with a strong creative eye and expert-level editing skills. Someone who can take raw footage and turn it into sharp, engaging, high-quality content across formats and platforms. This is a hands-on creative role for someone with a strong sense of storytelling, pace, music, typography, visual design, and attention to detail. You should be equally comfortable editing a high-energy event film, leadership interview, podcast, keynote, brand film, or short-form social video. You should be able to take complete ownership of an edit, from understanding the brief and working through raw footage to delivering a polished final output. Key Responsibilities Expert Video Editing Edit raw footage into polished, high-quality long-form and short-form videos. Work across brand films, leadership videos, interviews, podcasts, events, keynotes, employer brand content, social content, and internal communications. Bring a strong understanding of storytelling, pacing, transitions, music, sound design, typography, and visual flow. Take complete ownership of edits from the first cut to final delivery. Short-form & Social Content Turn long-form content into engaging clips for LinkedIn, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, X, and other platforms. Motion Graphics & Design Create motion graphics, animated typography, titles, lower thirds, intros, outros, and transitions. Work confidently with After Effects for animation and motion design. Event & Fast-Turnaround Content Edit event content including highlights, speaker clips, attendee bytes, session videos, and aftermovies.
