Okta seeks a skilled NetSuite Developer to optimize, customize, and maintain our NetSuite ERP system. Job Duties and Responsibilities Develop, and maintain NetSuite customizations, including SuiteScripts (1.0/2.0), workflows, and saved searches. Leverage NetSuite's native AI capabilities and APIs, such as the SuiteScript Generative AI API, NetSuite Prompt Studio, and the AI Connector Service (MCP) to create custom AI-enhanced features. Closely work with the BT team to design, develop, test, implement & support system solutions primarily in NetSuite ERP. Support & resolve production issues Develop and maintain integrations with third-party applications via REST/SOAP APIs. Dedicate time to learning and experimenting with external AI frameworks like AWS Bedrock and RAG to identify opportunities for future NetSuite enhancements. Required Skills: 5 to 8 years of NetSuite development experience ( SuiteScript, SuiteFlow, SuiteTalk ). Experience with SuiteCloud tools, REST/SOAP APIs, and third-party integrations. Experience with the SuiteScript Generative AI APIs to integrate LLM capabilities for advanced scripting solutions. Good understanding of NetSuite ERP modules (Order Management, Billing, Vendor bill, etc.). Proficiency in JavaScript, SQL, and related web technologies. Configure NetSuite modules to align with business needs, including leveraging Prompt Studio to customize and manage NetSuite AI functions like Text Enhance .
