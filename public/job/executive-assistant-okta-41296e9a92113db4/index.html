Okta is hiring an Executive Assistant , based in Okta headquarters in San Francisco to provide a wide array of administrative, logistical, and project-related support. This role will report to the SVP, Digital and will also support the VP, Brand & Creative. The environment is fast-paced, and the right person has the ability to take initiative, is professional, quick, creative, and motivated. The ideal Executive Assistant is dedicated to the Executive and her/his success. This role will have a high amount of visibility and is pivotal in optimizing executive time, acting as a go-to connector between executives, the team, and external partners to drive priorities forward. The Executive Assistant is committed and has the ability to stay a step ahead of the Executive. What You'll Be Doing Calendar and Administrative Management: Proactively manage complex executive calendars with scheduling across time zones and competing priorities. Organize and manage travel logistics for high-stakes international trips, handling complex itineraries with multiple legs. Maintain the organizational activity calendar and align executive workflows to adhere to and support the broader rhythm of the business across planning cycles, QBRs, and key company milestones. Manage, process, and submit all timely and accurate expense reports, ensuring expenses adhere to company protocols. Meeting and Event Coordination: Coordinate high-stakes meetings, both internal and external customers, with a sense of urgency, ensuring all logistics are handled. Handle logistics for large team events, off-site meetings, and departmental All Hands, including assisting with slides and managing Q&A segments. Partner with the executive to define the goals, agenda, and overall vision for off-sites, managing the budget and confidential information. Take meeting minutes for key discussions, ensuring action points are clearly documented and followed up. Project and Communication Oversight: Coordinate tasks and track progress across multiple medium to large projects, ensuring timelines are met and maintaining detailed documentation. Act as the primary liaison between the executives, staff, and external clients, managing communications effectively. Resolve complex scheduling conflicts and manage multiple, competing executive priorities, ensuring timely execution of tasks. Proactively monitor executive calendars one month out, alerting managers of rolling conflicts. What You'll Bring to the Role: The ideal candidate will have the professional experience and behaviors that drive efficient execution and strategic thinking: A minimum of 3+ years of experience supporting Sr. level Executives with a strong preference for supporting more than one senior-level executive (VP/SVP). While you are a master of calendars and logistics, you also know how to bring energy, warmth, and fun to the team culture . Logistics Mastery: Extensive experience with complex schedules and logistics across multiple time zones, utilizing web-based scheduling and collaboration tools. Proactive Ownership & Solutions Focus: Dedicated and heavily solutions-focused, with the ability to anticipate next steps, solve problems before they escalate, and make sound judgment calls autonomously. Organization, Results & Accountability: Deep organizational skills, extraordinary time management, and sharp attention to detail bringing end-to-end accountability to manage multiple projects, lead priorities, and set clear expectations. Growth Mindset & Teamwork: A true team player with a strong growth mindset who thrives in ambiguity, adapts quickly to feedback and change, and brings positive, collaborative energy across diverse working styles. Communication & Composure: Excellent verbal and written communication skills and a proven ability to interact in a professional and composed manner with all internal and external stakeholders (Customers, Board Members, etc.). Confidentiality: Complete confidentiality on all business matters.
