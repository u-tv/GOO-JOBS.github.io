About the job This position focuses on designing end-to-end functionality for your product team. You will work alongside other product designers and in lock-step with product and engineering stakeholders, leveraging UX best practices to diverge and converge on solutions. You will be an owner of your work, prioritizing tasks to maximize impact while supporting design and business objectives. Skills & responsibilities Product Strategy : Use customer and product expertise to contribute early and often to your product team's roadmap. Advocate for how work gets prioritized by drawing on your understanding of business goals. Craft & Execution : Independently scope work and use a strong command of UI best practices to design multi-page or multi-state experiences. Utilize and help evolve the design system when needed. Research : Lead or co-lead research initiatives, using those insights to inform design and identify opportunities for your product team. Teamwork & Communication : Clearly document and articulate decision-making in a way that is easy for different audiences to understand. Independently manage feedback and escalate risks appropriately. Leadership : Design and facilitate team discussions repeatedly and across different contexts to help solve challenges on your team. Project Management : Establish necessary commitments with PM and Engineering partners, defining a plan to perform the work and delivering successfully.
