Job Description

Why Mozilla? Mozilla Corporation is the non-profit-backed technology company that has shaped the internet for the better over the last 25 years. We make pioneering brands like Firefox, the privacy-minded web browser. Now, with more than 225 million people around the world using our products each month, we’re shaping the next 25 years of technology and helping to reclaim an internet built for people, not companies. Our work focuses on diverse areas including AI, social media, security and more. And we’re doing this while never losing our focus on our core mission – to make the internet better for people. The Mozilla Corporation is wholly owned by the non-profit 501(c) Mozilla Foundation. This means we aren’t beholden to any shareholders — only to our mission. Along with thousands of volunteer contributors and collaborators all over the world, Mozillians design, build and distribute software that enables people to enjoy the internet on their terms. What is a Senior Staff Partnerships Lead at Mozilla? Senior Staff is a senior individual contributor level at Mozilla. People at this level are recognized leaders in their field. They take on complex, ambiguous work that spans several disciplines and organizations with little supervision, lead strategic external relationships, and are comfortable working with executives inside and outside Mozilla. About this team and role: Mozilla’s New Products organization is an internal incubator for high-potential ventures. We’re building a new Firefox product for PC gamers, and partnerships across the games industry are central to how it reaches players and grows. As the partnerships lead, you’ll be responsible for identifying, initiating, and closing the gaming partnerships that bring the product to more players. You will report to the Director of Product and build new gaming partnerships that advance the product roadmap. What you’ll do: Own the product’s partnerships strategy and pipeline across cloud gaming services, storefronts and launchers, publishers and studios, community platforms, and hardware makers. Originate, structure and close deals that bring players, product capabilities and new revenue, such as distribution, co-marketing, in-game rewards and revenue-share agreements. Plan and deliver partner launch moments with Firefox marketing that reach players where they already play. Identify and propose new product features in support of partnerships, and evangelize new product features with partners. Lead each deal across Mozilla: shape terms with Legal and Finance, scope partner integrations with product and engineering, and hold every partnership to Mozilla’s standards for privacy, trust and responsible design. Represent Mozilla with senior leaders across the games industry, and bring what you learn from partners and the market back into product strategy. What you’ll bring: 10+ years in business development, product marketing, or strategic partnerships, including 5+ years in the games industry. A track record of originating and closing multi-party commercial partnerships from first conversation to signed agreement, including revenue-share and co-marketing deals. Established relationships with decision-makers across the PC gaming ecosystem, and the standing to get meetings with senior leaders at platform holders and publishers. Strong commercial judgment: you can model deal economics, structure terms that work for both sides, and negotiate with senior counterparts. Experience taking deals through legal, finance, product and marketing teams to launch, without an established playbook. Clear, persuasive communication with executives inside and outside the company, and willingness to travel for partner meetings and industry events. Commitment to our values: Welcoming differences Being relationship-minded Practicing responsible participation Having grit Bonus points for: partnerships experience at a browser, operating system or consumer software company; distribution deals such as device pre-installs or app store placements; and a passion for PC gaming and its communities.
