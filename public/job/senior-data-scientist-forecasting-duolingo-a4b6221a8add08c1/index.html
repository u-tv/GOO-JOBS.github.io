Senior Data Scientist, Forecasting

🏢 duolingo 📍 New York, NY; Pittsburgh, PA 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Our mission at Duolingo is to develop the best education in the world and make it universally available. It’s a big mission, and that’s where you come in! At Duolingo, you’ll join a team that cares about educating our users, experimenting with big ideas, making fact-based decisions, and finding innovative solutions to complex problems. You’ll have limitless learning opportunities and daily collaborations with world-class minds — while doing work that’s both meaningful and fun. Join our life-changing mission to develop education for our half a billion (and growing!) learners around the world. Read our blog to learn more. About the role... Duolingo has over 500 million users and is rapidly expanding across new subjects and markets, meaning our growth trajectory is central to our business success. We're hiring a Senior Data Scientist to execute and improve our foundational monetization (revenue) forecast, used for planning, goal setting, and business strategy. This person will be part of our Monetization Data Science team, collaborating with peers and cross-functional partners in Finance, Product, and Strategy to continuously improve our forecasting capabilities. They will drive methodological advances and deliver trustworthy business-critical quarterly forecasts in close partnership with the Finance team. Built on a deep understanding of the dynamics of revenue generation across Duolingo’s product portfolio, the forecast will set the pace for monetization innovation and inform business operation and strategy. 🧠 You will... Co-own forecast development and presentation with Finance team peers. Recommend, develop and validate methodological advances to our forecasts, aligning methodological strengths with business priorities. Collaborate with fellow data scientists to compare forecasts against actuals throughout the quarter. Analyze performance to understand key drivers of deviations, and clearly communicate findings and recommended next steps to cross-functional partners to keep revenue on track. Work with diverse stakeholders to develop forecasts that reflect a deep understanding of our product development, big bets, and evolving business strategy. Translate complex analytical findings into clear narratives for senior leadership and cross-functional partners. Influence strategic decisions through a combination of technical depth and effective communication. Build systems and processes that ensure our forecasting process is robust, repeatable, and easily understandable across the company. ✅ You have... Applied industry experience building forecasts in messy real world settings where details, confounds, and the validity of assumptions matter. A graduate degree in Data Science, Statistics, Economics, or a related quantitative field, or equivalent demonstrated expertise in formal analytical techniques. Demonstrated ability to connect technical results to real-world recommendations, and to translate product and business changes into technical requirements. Strong command of SQL, Python, AI analysis tools, and data/analytics engineering ecosystems. Exceptional communication skills, both written and verbal, with the ability to distill complex quantitative work into crisp, actionable insights for diverse audiences. ⭐ Exceptional candidates will have... Experience operating in a financial analytics role at a high-growth consumer technology, finance/banking, or consulting company, including experience with revenue forecasting specifically. Experience at a public company, including the analytical demands that come with investor-grade reporting and forecasting. An impressive Duolingo streak.
