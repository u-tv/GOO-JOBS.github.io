remediation tracking.</p> <p>Maintain and enhance governance processes for UK compliance issues management, risk assessments, control oversight,and reporting.</p> <p>Support internal audit, external governance materials, and issue summaries for senior leadership and relevant governance forums.</p> <p>Partner with Legal to ensure clear delineation between legal advisory responsibilities and Compliance operational / oversight responsibilities, particularly in the privacy space.</p> <p>Partner with Product, Engineering, Operations, and other cross-functional stakeholders to ensure UK
