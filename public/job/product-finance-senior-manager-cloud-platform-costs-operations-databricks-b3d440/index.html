Product Finance Senior Manager, Cloud Platform Costs & Operations

🏢 databricks 📍 Mountain View, California; San Francisco, California; Seattle, Washington 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

(GAQ427R230) About the role As customers build their mission-critical data and AI workloads on Databricks, our shared cloud platform infrastructure is scaling at unprecedented speed. We are looking for a Product Finance Senior Manager to own the economics and financial operations of our multi-cloud platform spend. You will be a key finance partner to our Cloud Engineering and Infrastructure Operations leaders, with a mandate that spans driver-based cloud forecasting, infrastructure efficiency roadmaps, and scalable cost attribution across our product portfolio. The impact you will have Drive analytics and modeling to understand how platform spend scales Lead deep data analytics to identify the underlying drivers of shared cloud platform spend across products, cloud providers, and global regions. Build robust, driver-based forecasting models that accurately predict infrastructure spend across regional expansion, customer scale, and new product launches. Partner with Cloud Infrastructure Operations to design a forward-looking cost attribution model that accurately maps shared platform expenses to individual products. Establish robust KPIs and operations to achieve unit economics goals Partner with Engineering to define north star efficiency KPIs across core platform services and storage/compute layers. Build the infrastructure efficiency roadmap alongside Engineering and translate technical optimizations into committed operating and gross margin targets. Drive tight operational rhythms and reporting so product and engineering leaders have clear visibility to execute against their unit economic goals. Scale cloud finance operations and systems Bring systems-level thinking to cloud FP&A processes, building automated workflows that scale as our multi-cloud footprint and commitment structures become increasingly dynamic. Ensure cloud cost allocation frameworks, monthly accruals, and reporting pipelines remain rigorous, auditable, and scalable as the business compounds. Dogfood the Databricks platform to analyze massive cloud billing and telemetry datasets, delivering self-serve cost visibility to engineering partners. What we look for Required 8+ years of experience in Strategic/Product Finance, Cloud FP&A, or FinOps, complemented by foundational training in Investment Banking, Consulting, or high-growth technology. Deep fluency in consumption-based business models, public cloud infrastructure costs (AWS, Azure, GCP), unit cost analysis, and gross margin drivers. A proven track record of partnering directly with Engineering and Cloud Infrastructure Operations teams to drive cost efficiency and forecasting accuracy. Systems-level thinker with a demonstrated ability to build scalable financial processes, driver-based models, and auditable allocation frameworks in fast-moving environments. Preferred Functional expertise in hyperscaler cloud pricing, enterprise commitment structures, and infrastructure deal economics. Experience attributing shared platform or foundational infrastructure costs across a multi-product SaaS or PaaS portfolio. Hands-on proficiency with SQL, Python, or notebook environments (especially Databricks) to self-serve insights from massive cloud billing and usage datasets.
