UPO Team Lead (Operations)

🏢 stripe 📍 LOCATION 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies - from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups - use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team User Policy Operations evaluates businesses against Stripe’s Terms of Service and related policies. We help Stripe support businesses responsibly while protecting our users, financial partners, and the broader Stripe ecosystem. What you’ll do As a Team Lead in User Policy Operations, you’ll lead and develop a team of operations associates responsible for policy evaluations and risk assessments. You’ll combine people leadership with hands-on involvement in complex cases, helping the team make well-supported decisions and deliver consistent operational results. You’ll work with cross-functional partners to resolve challenging cases, improve processes, and build a team environment that supports strong performance and professional growth. Responsibilities Lead and coach associates conducting Terms of Service evaluations and risk assessments for complex and strategic users. Help the team assess ambiguous situations, consider risks beyond written policy, and develop clear, well-documented recommendations. Partner with account teams and other risk stakeholders to resolve complex cases and remove operational blockers. Balance timely case resolution with decision quality, rigor, and consistency. Manage team performance against operational goals and key performance indicators, and communicate results to stakeholders. Plan capacity, manage work allocation, and ensure complex or time-sensitive cases receive appropriate attention. Identify opportunities to improve policies, processes, and workflows based on operational experience. Set clear individual and team expectations, provide ongoing feedback, and conduct performance and career-development conversations. Ensure associates have the knowledge, resources, and support they need to succeed. Maintain hands-on involvement in core operational work. Support recruitment and hiring, and foster an inclusive, engaged team environment. Who you are We're looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement. Minimum requirements At least six years of experience in risk operations or policy operations focused on assessing whether businesses can be supported. Experience leading or coaching operations teams and managing performance. Experience mentoring colleagues and providing constructive, actionable feedback. Experience delivering operational metrics and reporting on weekly and monthly business performance. Strong subject-matter expertise in risk operations. Sound judgment in ambiguous situations, including the ability to assess risk and make clear, defensible recommendations. Experience collaborating with cross-functional stakeholders to resolve complex operational issues. Ability to manage competing priorities and respond effectively to time-sensitive work. Strong written and verbal communication skills. Strong planning, organizational, and time-management skills. Preferred qualifications Knowledge of risk reviews, policy or compliance operations, and fraud-detection tools. Experience managing risk events and identifying scenarios that require additional investigation or escalation.
