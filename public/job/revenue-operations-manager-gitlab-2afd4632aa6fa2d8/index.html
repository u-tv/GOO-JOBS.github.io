An overview of this role As a Revenue Technology Manager at GitLab, you'll lead how the tools we build and buy work together as we grow our consumption-based business. You'll own the vision, roadmap, and requirements for internal go-to-market applications, starting with Command Center, which helps field teams act on product usage and consumption data. You'll decide what those applications need to do and how they should fit with the rest of the stack. Engineering will own their technical architecture, code, and infrastructure. This is a senior individual contributor role with no direct reports, reporting to the Director, Revenue Technology, within Revenue Operations. Recommend when to build, buy, or consolidate tools based on cost, return on investment, and how well each option meets the needs of go-to-market teams. Own the roadmap for Command Center and other internal applications. Turn requests from Sales, Solutions Architecture, Customer Experience, Product, and Ecosystem into clear requirements, and explain what you won't prioritize and why. Partner with Engineering to deliver applications that fit the existing stack and limit technical debt. Lead user acceptance testing, launch, documentation, and enablement, then use adoption data to decide what to improve, expand, or retire. Set the long-term direction of the revenue technology stack with Revenue Strategy, Revenue Operations, and field leaders as the business and artificial intelligence capabilities evolve. Build artificial intelligence and automation workflows that save time for Revenue Technology and go-to-market teams, and help the team decide when to use them. Bring consumption metrics into the tools and workflows field teams rely on, and adapt them as GitLab's consumption model matures.
