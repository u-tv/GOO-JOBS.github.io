Overview We are looking for a Director of Technical Program Management to lead a senior team of TPMs driving Instacart's most critical Engineering Foundations initiatives across Data Infrastructure, Developer Infrastructure, Platform, AI enablement, Privacy, and Reliability. In this highly visible role, you will shape the strategy and direction of company-critical, cross-functional programs while staying hands-on in how they are planned and delivered. You will partner closely with VPs and Directors of Engineering, Finance, Legal, GRC, Data Science, and other cross-functional leaders. This is a terrific opportunity for a technical leader who is passionate about people, program management, and engineering, and who understands that our success hinges on our ability to deliver impact as a team. The Technical Program Management team leads Instacart's most impactful cross-functional efforts, bringing technical expertise, product sense, and business acumen to deliver highly complex initiatives at scale. About the Job Lead and grow a senior team of TPMs, building a vision for a high-performing TPM organization while actively mentoring and developing individuals to succeed. Shape the vision, strategy, and investment needs across Engineering Foundations, including data product strategy, data quality and governance, financial data transformation, AI enablement, privacy, and reliability. Drive a step change in how Engineering Foundations operates: clear reliability expectations, real-time visibility into top-line metrics, and proactive investment decisions owned by each pillar. Partner with VPs, Senior Directors, and Directors of Engineering and with leaders in Finance, Legal, and Security to enable effective strategic planning and timely execution of company-critical programs. Present regularly to the CTO and influence engineering leadership on key investments needed to hold a high bar on our operational health. Use your technical knowledge and product sense to drive data-driven decisions, negotiate prioritization, weigh long-term and short-term investments, and reduce ambiguity and risk. Enable the company to expand into new markets with privacy and regulatory compliance in strong shape. Build a team culture of curiosity, impact, and ownership that reinforces the core values of Instacart. About You Minimum Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related technical field. 12+ years of experience in technical program management, engineering leadership, product management in a technical domain, or a related discipline. Track record as both a strong individual contributor and a people leader, with experience leading and growing a team of TPMs or engineers. Experience in infrastructure domains (such as data infrastructure, developer infrastructure, platform, reliability, or privacy) at scale. Demonstrated influence on technical or business strategy, with a record of measurable outcomes delivered for the organization. Proven ability to influence senior engineering and executive leaders, resolve conflict, and build consensus while navigating ambiguity. Preferred Qualifications Experience in infrastructure at a leading technology company operating at scale, as both an individual contributor and a leader. Experience with data governance, privacy, or regulatory compliance programs.
