Storyblok: Senior Content Marketing Specialist - UK

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote Storyblok is a headless CMS that enables marketers and developers to create with joy and publish with confidence. It empowers you to deliver structured and consistent content everywhere: websites, apps, AI search, and beyond. Legendary brands like Virgin Media O2, Oatly, and TomTom use Storyblok to make a bigger, faster market impact. WHAT IS IN IT FOR YOU You will be joining a growing company where you can contribute to many “firsts”. Plus these benefits: Monthly remote work stipend (home internet costs, electricity). Home office equipment package right at the start (laptop, keyboard, monitor…) Home office equipment upgrade (furniture, ear plugs …) or membership to a local co-working space after your onboarding Sick leave benefit, parental leave and 25 days of annual leave plus your local national holidays Personal development fund for courses, books, conferences, and material The annual international team-building trip, quarterly and monthly online get-togethers As a fully remote company, with work-life balance at its core, you’ll enjoy flexible schedules An international team that loves to have fun at work and works hard together to accomplish shared goals JOB SUMMARY We're looking for a Senior Content Marketing Specialist to shape and create impactful, thought-leadership led content across our 6 monthly brand campaign cycles, plus additional ICP tracks as needed, while owning customer advocacy content - e.g. case studies. The right candidate understands what makes great B2B marketing content, is AI-native, takes initiative in a fast-paced scale up environment, and brings a data-driven mindset to connect strategy with business results. This person will sit within Storyblok's Brand and Communications team, supporting marketing across the buyer journey, driving case study development, and partnering with field marketing, social, and events on content needs. They'll also help build and launch a new UGC programme and support executive communications on LinkedIn. ESSENTIAL JOB FUNCTIONS Content strategy Shape and execute content creation efforts within the Brand and Communications Team, from long-form whitepapers and blog posts to short-form landing pages and other supporting assets that require content support Generate creative, storytelling-focused content ideas that produce engaging thought leadership, staying abreast of B2B social trends and thinking beyond the 'usual' types of content Support aligning content marketing efforts with the wider Brand and Communications team functions, including PR, company social media, and executive communications via LinkedIn Conduct in-depth keyword research using tools like Ahrefs and/or Semrush to identify relevant topics and keyword groups for Storyblok content Briefing videographers and motion graphics experts to create videos for campaign tracks, events, social to name a few Optimisation and monitoring of content health of all Storyblok content via the website Customer advocacy Ownership of the case study content process, from initial ideation and customer contact, to creation and publication of the assets Optimisation and monitoring of all case study pages on Storyblok's website Ensuring consistent quality across all case study pages by updating older case studies and coordinating with the Partner and/or Customer Success team Planning measurable outreach strategies to garner more customer success stories in response to business requirements Defining and tracking precise KPIs for all case study efforts EDUCATION AND EXPERIENCE Must haves 5+ years’ experience in content marketing or content strategy Knowledge of SEO best practices and familiarity with on-page and off-page SEO tactics Tech industry marketing experience Content reporting experience including analysing data and making recommendations Nice to haves Content marketing experience in a B2B tech scale up environment Experience developing and executing UGC programmes, particularly in B2B/tech settings Experience in a Brand and Communications team/similar with an integrated content function, having worked with PR and social media teams Demonstrable experience building content marketing support agents via the likes of Claude - e.g. to automate processes Experience working on event content PROFESSIONAL COMPETENCIES Highly organised, reliable, and self-driven in fast-moving ambiguous
