Job Description

FEQ427R574 Senior Manager, Field Engineering Enablement – Scaled Programs and Field Capability Databricks Field Engineering (FE) is a global organization of thousands of technical experts who help customers build on the Data Intelligence Platform, and how they learn, grow, and prove their expertise directly shapes how quickly our customers succeed. Inside FE Enablement, a strong team already runs the scaled programs that make this possible. These include communities of technical experts, programs that develop engineers into trusted advisors for customer leaders, ongoing learning that keeps experienced engineers current as the platform and AI change, and the skills standards and specialization paths that define what great looks like in the field. These programs have proven that scaled, field-led enablement works, and the opportunity now is much bigger than running them well. AI is changing what Field Engineers need to be good at, and we need a Senior Manager who can set the vision for what comes next, connect today's programs into one system that helps every engineer grow throughout their career, and design and launch the new programs the field will need. You will lead the team that built these programs and give them a clear direction and more room to build, working closely with FE leadership, product teams, the People team, and enablement partners in every region to bring that vision to life. The impact you will have: Set a multi-year vision for how Field Engineers build, apply, and prove their expertise in an AI-enabled world, and turn it into a roadmap that FE leadership understands, supports, and invests in. Connect expert communities, trusted advisor development, ongoing learning, and skills standards into one system, so that what an engineer learns, practices, shares, and demonstrates builds on itself instead of living in separate programs with separate audiences. Find the next capability gaps before they slow the field down, and design and launch new scaled programs to close them, taking each from first idea to a global program that thousands of engineers use and value. Build a repeatable way to create programs at scale, with shared design patterns, community-led delivery, and AI built into how programs are created and run, so that launching the next program is faster and easier than the last one. Lead, coach, and grow the team that owns these programs, giving each person clear scope, real ownership of what they build, and a visible path to grow their careers and impact, including leading new programs as the portfolio expands. Give the team's work more visibility and influence with FE leadership, so that program owners have a seat where decisions about field readiness are made and their results are recognized across the organization. Make demonstrated capability useful to the business, working with FE leadership and the People team to connect skills and specialization to development conversations, staffing, and career growth. Define clear measures of adoption, proficiency, capability coverage, and impact on customer outcomes, and use that evidence to decide where to invest, what to scale, and what to retire. What we look for: 10+ years of experience in technical enablement, solutions architecture or field engineering, learning and development, or program leadership at a high-growth technology company, including at least 4 years leading teams of senior individual contributors. A track record as a people leader who develops senior talent, gives people ownership and credit for their work, and advocates for their growth, along with references who would say they did some of their best work on your team. A track record of creating and launching new programs from ambiguity and scaling them to a large, global technical audience, not only running programs someone else designed. The ability to see how separate programs fit together, form a clear point of view on where they should go next, and bring senior leaders and your team along with that vision. Enough technical fluency in data and AI to earn credibility with Solutions Architects and product experts, ideally built through time in a customer-facing technical role. Strong strategic judgment and executive presence, with experience partnering with Directors and VPs to shape priorities, make tradeoffs, and secure investment for new work. A builder's mindset, regular hands-on use of AI tools, and the data fluency to define useful measures and turn data and field feedback into decisions about how programs are designed and run. Clear and thoughtful communication across a distributed organization, from a working session with your team to a readout for senior FE leaders.
