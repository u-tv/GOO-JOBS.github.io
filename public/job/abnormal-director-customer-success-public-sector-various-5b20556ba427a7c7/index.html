Abnormal: Director, Customer Success - Public Sector

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote - USA About the Role Abnormal AI is looking for a Director of Customer Success, Public Sector to lead the team that drives adoption, retention, and expansion across our federal, state, local, and education customers. Why Abnormal. We are an AI-native cybersecurity company protecting organizations from the most sophisticated email and identity-based attacks. We hold FedRAMP Moderate authorization and GovRAMP authorization, so agencies and public institutions can adopt our platform with confidence. Why this role. You will own Customer Success for one of the fastest-growing parts of our business, with real autonomy to shape the model. You will lead a high-performing team of Customer Success Managers, build executive relationships across agencies and public organizations, and set the standard for how Abnormal serves government customers. The mission is hard to match: you are helping protect the people and institutions that serve the public. Why now. As we expand our federal presence, we need a leader with deep public sector experience, an established network, and a long-term commitment to this space to take the team to its next level. You will partner closely with Sales, Renewals, Channel, Support, and Product to create clear ownership and a consistent customer experience. This role reports to the VP of Customer Success. What you will do Lead, coach, and grow a team of Customer Success Managers serving federal and SLED customers. Build trust with a strong existing team and create room for them to take on more. Define and execute the Public Sector Customer Success strategy, including coverage models, success planning, and playbooks tailored to federal and SLED customers. Own retention, adoption, and expansion outcomes for the Public Sector book, with accurate forecasting that accounts for fiscal year timing, funding cycles, and contract vehicles. Build and maintain executive relationships with agency and organization leaders, including CISOs, CIOs, and program and procurement stakeholders. Serve as a senior escalation point for critical customer issues. Partner with Public Sector Sales leadership to establish clear ownership and communication lanes after the close, so customers experience one coordinated Abnormal team. Work with Renewals, Channel, and resellers on compliant, predictable renewals and approved expansion motions such as true-ups and scope changes. Bring working knowledge of FedRAMP, GovRAMP or StateRAMP, and government procurement to how we onboard, support, and renew customers. Represent the public sector customer voice to Product and Engineering with prioritized, business-backed input rather than lists of requests. Champion AI-enabled workflows and data-driven health and risk management to free CSM capacity for high-impact customer work. Hire, onboard, and develop federal-ready Customer Success talent as the business grows. Travel as needed to meet customers and internal teams, up to 25% (confirm). Must Haves Significant experience serving U.S. Public Sector customers, with federal experience required and a demonstrated long-term commitment to the public sector (confirm minimum years). 10+ years of experience in Customer Success, Account Management, or Post-Sales leadership in B2B SaaS, including 5+ years leading Customer Success teams (confirm). Established relationships with federal, state, local, or education organizations that bring credibility with customers and Sales. Working knowledge of government procurement, fiscal year budgeting, contract vehicles, and channel or reseller-led buying. Familiarity with public sector compliance frameworks such as FedRAMP and GovRAMP or StateRAMP. Proven track record of driving retention and expansion outcomes and forecasting renewals in long, budget-driven cycles. Experience partnering with a Sales organization that stays closely involved after the sale, and defining clear ownership as a result. Strong executive presence and the ability to engage senior government stakeholders. Experience building or operationalizing AI-driven processes in post-sales functions. Based in, or willing to relocate to, the Washington, DC area (confirm). Nice to Haves Experience in cybersecurity, email security, or enterprise infrastructure software. Active security clearance, or the ability to obtain one (confirm whether required). Experience with DoD, Intelligence Community, or large civilian federal agencies. Prior experience building or scaling a federal Customer Success function from a small base. Familiarity with Salesforce and Gainsight. #LI-LM3 Actual compensation will be determined based on several non-discriminatory factors including skills, experience, qualifications, and geographic location.
