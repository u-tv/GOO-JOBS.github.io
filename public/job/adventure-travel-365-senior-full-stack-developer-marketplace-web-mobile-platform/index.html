Adventure Travel 365: Senior Full-Stack Developer - Marketplace Web & Mobile Platform

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Phoenix, Arizona URL: https://adventuretravel365.com/ Senior Full-Stack Developer - Marketplace Web & Mobile Platform We have a big idea, and we’re looking for a top-notch Senior Full-Stack Developer to help us bring it to life. We’re building a new contractor referral and services marketplace, think Thumbtack or Angi , where clients can easily find and connect with contractors and service providers. The vision includes both an interactive web platform and mobile app, with everything from user accounts and referrals to job tracking, payments, advertising, and more. This is a fully remote, full-time, ongoing position, and we’re open to applicants worldwide. There are a lot of moving pieces, which is exactly why we’re looking for someone who has built something like this before and gets excited about taking a platform from an idea to a working product, then continuing to grow it with us. What You’ll Help Us Build The platform will have two main sides: clients looking for services and contractors/vendors looking for business. Some of the functionality we’re planning includes: Client and contractor/vendor accounts and profiles Contractor listings, categories, search, and filtering Service requests, inquiries, referrals, and lead management Job and project tracking Client and vendor dashboards Online payments and transaction tracking Reviews and ratings Notifications and communications Banner advertising and paid promotional opportunities Admin tools for managing the platform Reporting and analytics Secure management of user, job, and transaction data And probably more! We have a strong vision for where we want this to go, but we also want the right developer to help us determine the smartest way to build it. This will be an evolving platform, so we’re looking for someone who wants to grow with it, not simply complete a checklist of features. Who We’re Looking For We’d love to find someone with strong experience in full-stack web and mobile development , especially someone who has worked on a marketplace, booking platform, service-provider network, or another multi-user platform. You should be comfortable with: Full-stack web development Mobile or cross-platform app development Strong front-end and back-end development Multi-user marketplace platforms User authentication and account management Payment gateway integrations Databases and APIs Third-party integrations Dashboards and reporting Secure, scalable application architecture Cloud hosting and deployment Testing, maintenance, and optimization Using modern AI-powered development tools and integrating AI capabilities into web and mobile applications If you’ve worked on something similar to Thumbtack, Angi, TaskRabbit, Upwork, or another two-sided marketplace , we definitely want to hear about it. More Than Just Coding We’re not looking for someone who simply waits for a list of instructions and starts coding. We want someone who can look at the big picture, ask the right questions, recommend the best technical approach, flag potential challenges, and help turn our ideas into a platform that’s intuitive, scalable, and built to grow. You’ll also be expected to study established platforms like Thumbtack and Angi , understand how their user journeys and marketplace functionality work from both the client and service-provider sides, and use those learnings to help us determine the best approach for our own platform. You should be able to take the project through planning, architecture, development, testing, launch, and beyond , while communicating clearly with a non-technical team along the way. Strong spoken and written English communication skills are important. You should be comfortable discussing ideas, explaining technical decisions clearly, and communicating regularly with a non-technical team. This is not a short-term build-and-leave project. We’re looking for someone who can stay with the platform as it grows, helping us continually improve, expand, and maintain it over time. Interested? Tell Us About Yourself Please send us: A quick introduction Your resume or LinkedIn profile Your portfolio and links to websites/apps you’ve built Examples of marketplaces, booking platforms, referral systems, or payment-enabled platforms you’ve worked on What you personally worked on for those projects Your experience using AI in development What technologies/frameworks you’d recommend for a project like this and why Your availability A short video introduction would be great, too! Nothing overly formal, just a quick introduction so we can get to know you, hear about your experience, and get a feel for how you communicate. Most importantly, show us what you’ve built. If you’ve helped create a multi-sided marketplace similar to Thumbtack or Angi, tell us about it. We’d love to see it. To apply: https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/adventure-travel-365-senior-full-stack-developer-marketplace-web-mobile-platform
