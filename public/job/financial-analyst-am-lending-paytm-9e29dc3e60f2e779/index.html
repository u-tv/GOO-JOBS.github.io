Job Description

About Us: Paytm is India's leading mobile payments and financial services distribution company. Pioneer of the mobile QR payments revolution in India, Paytm builds technologies that help small businesses with payments and commerce. Paytm's mission is to serve half a billion Indians and bring them to the mainstream economy with the help of technology. Role: Financial Planning & Analysis / Business Finance Job Summary We are looking for a detail-oriented and analytical Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) Analyst to support financial planning, budgeting, forecasting, business performance analysis, and strategic decision-making. The role will involve managing the Sales Channel P&L, monitoring financial performance, analysing business metrics, and developing data-driven insights to improve profitability and operational efficiency. Key Responsibilities 1. Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) Develop and maintain financial models, budgets, forecasts, and long-term business plans. Conduct variance analysis of actual financial performance against budgets and forecasts. Monitor key financial metrics, unit economics, profitability, and cash flows. Identify financial risks, business trends, and opportunities to improve operational efficiency. Prepare scenario analysis and sensitivity models to support business and strategic decisions. Manage and maintain the P&L for the Sales Channel, with a focus on cost tracking, expense management, and profitability improvement. Partner with business stakeholders to drive financial discipline and optimize costs. 2. Business Intelligence & Data Analytics Analyse large datasets to identify business trends, performance drivers, and actionable insights. Develop and maintain dashboards, MIS reports, and analytical frameworks to track business and operational KPIs. Work closely with business teams to identify performance gaps and recommend corrective actions. Track key business metrics, including customer acquisition, retention, revenue growth, and unit economics. Automate recurring reports and streamline financial reporting processes to improve accuracy and efficiency. Collaborate with Finance, Accounting, Operations, and Business teams to gather, validate, and analyse financial information. Required Skills & Qualifications Qualified Chartered Accountant (CA) is mandatory. Strong understanding of Financial Planning & Analysis, budgeting, forecasting, and financial modelling. Experience in P&L management, variance analysis, and cost optimisation. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to interpret complex financial data. Advanced MS Excel skills; experience with Power BI, Tableau, or other BI tools will be an added advantage. Ability to work with large datasets and prepare management dashboards and MIS reports. Strong stakeholder management and communication skills. Ability to translate financial data into meaningful business insights and recommendations.
