Mozilla Corporation is the non-profit-backed technology company that has shaped the internet for the better over the last 25 years. We make pioneering brands like Firefox, the privacy-minded web browser. Now, with more than 225 million people around the world using our products each month, we’re shaping the next 25 years of technology and helping to reclaim an internet built for people, not companies. Our work focuses on diverse areas including AI, social media, security and more. And we’re doing this while never losing our focus on our core mission – to make the internet better for people. Along with thousands of volunteer contributors and collaborators all over the world, Mozillians design, build and distribute open-source software that enables people to enjoy the internet on their terms. About this team and role: Mozilla’s Customer Experience team helps users, organizations, and partners successfully adopt, use, and get sustained value from Mozilla products. Historically, much of Mozilla’s support work has focused on consumers. As Mozilla expands its business and organizational offerings, we are building a support operation designed for the needs of B2B customers, including dependable service delivery, clear customer commitments, global coverage, and close coordination with Product and Engineering. As Staff Enterprise Support Operations Manager, you will be Mozilla’s first dedicated B2B support operations leader. You will not inherit a mature operation; you will build one. You will design and launch the operating model, vendor strategy, processes, tooling, training, metrics, capacity planning, and governance required to provide high-quality technical support to organizations deploying and managing Mozilla products. Beyond building the support operation, you will serve as the operational bridge between Enterprise customers, CX, Product, and Engineering, ensuring insights from customers and support teams inform the broader Enterprise experience. This is an individual contributor role with broad end-to-end ownership. You will be the day-to-day owner of Mozilla’s external B2B support vendor relationships and the operational processes connecting vendor agents with Mozilla teams. You will use customer demand, service performance, and cost data to determine when and how the operation should expand across geographies, languages, coverage models, and support offerings. As the business grows, you will also help define the structure and capabilities of Mozilla’s future B2B support team. You will participate in a defined incident coverage model and periodically lead the operational response to major customer incidents. In those moments, you will ensure vendors follow established procedures, coordinate escalation and handoffs to Mozilla engineers, drive cross-functional resolution, communicate clearly with customers and internal leaders, and oversee post-incident reviews and root cause analysis. This position requires a rare combination of support operations expertise, vendor management, program development, technical fluency, and calm leadership under pressure. Our ideal candidate is a practical builder who can move between strategy and execution, create structure where none exists, and earn the trust of customers, vendors, engineers, and senior leaders. What you’ll do: Build and launch Mozilla’s first B2B support operations capability, including its operating model, workflows, escalation paths, governance, and continuous-improvement processes. Own external support vendor relationships end to end, including future vendor selection and contracting support, onboarding, workforce planning, performance management, invoicing, renewals, and investment recommendations. Create and maintain vendor training, support playbooks, standard operating procedures, quality frameworks, knowledge materials, and coordination with product teams for release-readiness plans. Forecast customer demand and support capacity, balancing staffing, coverage, language needs, service quality, cost, and business growth; recommend when to expand into new geographies or service models. Define service objectives, metrics, KPIs, dashboards, and operating reviews for quality, speed, customer experience, productivity, and cost. Hold vendors accountable for performance, identify service gaps, and lead corrective-action and continuous-improvement plans. Own Mozilla’s operational enterprise escalation framework while designing and managing operational handoffs between vendor agents and Mozilla teams so complex issues reach the right Engineering, Product, Security, or business partners with sufficient context and urgency. Lead the operational response to major B2B incidents, coordinating vendor execution, engineering engagement, customer and leadership communications, resolution, and post-incident review. Partner with Product, Engineering, Customer Success, Sales, Legal, Finance, Procurement, and other teams to align customer commitments with operational capacity and improve the support experience. Participate in customer-facing meetings to understand operational challenges, explain support processes, gather feedback, and strengthen the customer support experience. Translate support trends and customer feedback into actionable insights, partnering cross-functionally to improve customer journeys, products, documentation, processes, tooling, automation, and responsible uses of AI, while helping define the future structure of Mozilla’s B2B support organization. What you’ll bring: 5+ years of experience in B2B or enterprise technical support operations, service delivery, customer operations, vendor management, or a related field. Demonstrated experience building a new support capability or substantially scaling or transforming an existing support operation. Experience managing outsourced support vendors or BPO partners, including training, workforce planning, quality assurance, performance governance, and corrective action. Experience with vendor selection, statements of work, contracts, budgets, forecasts, invoices, renewals, or related procurement and commercial processes. Experience with support platforms such as Zendesk/CRMs and issue-tracking systems such as Jira, including reporting, workflow design, and operational analytics. Strong knowledge of enterprise support and service-management practices, including service levels, severity models, escalations, incident management, problem management, and root cause analysis. Experience leading high-severity technical incidents and coordinating customers, support teams, engineers, executives, and other stakeholders under pressure. Experience defining operational metrics and using dashboards, reporting, and workforce planning to improve quality, customer outcomes, efficiency, and cost. Technical fluency sufficient to coordinate issues involving enterprise software, browsers, endpoint management, identity, certificates, proxies, networks, security tools, and managed customer environments; deep software engineering expertise is not required. Excellent communication and cross-functional leadership skills, with the ability to explain complex situations clearly and move work forward across distributed teams. Ability to operate independently across regions, cultures, and time zones, exercise sound judgment in ambiguous situations, and build support experiences worthy of long-term customer trust. Experience with multilingual or multi-region support operations is desired. Experience building and leading B2B or enterprise technical support operations teams is desired. Familiarity with GDPR, enterprise security requirements, regulated customer environments is desired.
