Bybit: [EU] Business Development Specialist, Poland

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Poland - Remote About Us Established in 2018, Bybit is one of the world’s leading cryptocurrency exchanges and digital financial platforms, serving over 90 million users across more than 200 countries and regions. Powered by world-class technology and a user-first mindset, Bybit delivers a seamless ecosystem across trading, payments, wealth management, custody, institutional services, and Web3 — connecting users to the future of digital finance. Our core values define how we build. We listen, care and improve to create products and experiences that put users first. Backed by a global team of ambitious builders, problem-solvers, and innovators, we foster a high-performance and fast-moving environment where talent is empowered to drive real impact at the global scale. Supported by 24/7 multilingual customer service and a strong commitment to innovation, we are shaping the future of finance through technology, collaboration, and bold execution. Today, Bybit is recognized as one of the most trusted and transparent platforms in the digital asset industry, continuing to expand its global presence while building the infrastructure for the next generation of financial services. The Role We’re looking for a Business Development Manager to grow Bybit EU’s presence in Poland and contribute to our wider European expansion. You’ll build and manage partnerships with local KOLs, affiliates, creators, crypto communities, and VIP networks—helping partners grow their audiences and delivering measurable results for Bybit. Key Responsibilities Identify and approach potential KOLs, affiliates and crypto communities in Poland. Build and manage a strong partnership pipeline, from market mapping and outreach through negotiation, onboarding, and activation. Develop trusted relationships with existing partners and help them grow their communities and partnership performance. Create localized partnership plans and campaigns that support user acquisition, user activity, trading volume, and revenue growth. Monitor partner and campaign performance, assess ROI , and identify ways to improve results. Work with Marketing, Product, Compliance, and other internal teams to develop and deliver effective campaigns and partner initiatives. Use OSOP to create and manage Campaign 2.0 campaigns. Identify local market trends, community needs, and competitor activity, and share insights with internal teams. Promote relevant Bybit EU products as part of partner initiatives, including Bybit Pay where appropriate. Represent Bybit at local industry events and build relationships across the Polish and European crypto ecosystem. Requirements Experience in business development, partnerships, affiliate marketing, or growth—ideally in crypto, Web3 , fintech, or a digital asset exchange. A track record of acquiring and developing partnerships that contribute to user growth, trading activity, volume, or revenue. Strong knowledge of the Polish crypto market, including its KOL , affiliate, VIPs, and community landscape. A good understanding of community dynamics and the local marketing environment. Strong communication, relationship-building, pitching, and negotiation skills. Ability to use performance data to evaluate campaigns and improve partner results. Understanding of Bybit’s platform and products, or the ability to build that knowledge quickly. Awareness of the European crypto market and regulatory environment, and the ability to work with Compliance on partner initiatives. Fluency in Polish and English. Nice to have: Familiarity with OSOP and experience creating Campaign 2.0 campaigns. Why Join Us At Bybit, we are committed to fostering a supportive and enriching work environment. Internal Events: Participate in regular team-building activities, workshops, and events designed to promote collaboration and innovation. Global Collaboration: Be part of a diverse, international team, working alongside colleagues from around the world. Career Advancement: Access opportunities for growth and advancement within a rapidly expanding global company. Internal Mobility: Grow with us- Your long-term development is important to us.
