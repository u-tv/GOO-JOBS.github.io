An overview of this role As a People Safety & Resilience Manager at GitLab, you'll help protect team members working from home, in Labs, and in coworking spaces. You'll turn GitLab's safety and resilience strategies into practical programs that work across countries, both day to day and when a crisis disrupts how people work. What you’ll do Run the occupational health and safety management system in line with ISO 45001. Put the global policy into practice through hazard identification, risk and ergonomic assessments, incident reporting and investigation, performance measurement, internal audits, and management reviews. Identify and meet each country's legal safety obligations with Employment Law, People Compliance, Benefits, and local vendors. Track changes in the law and keep records of required appointments, assessments, committees, training, and medical surveillance. Manage vendor agreements and keep safety and resilience services within budget. Coordinate required consultation with works councils and team members before GitLab makes a decision or contacts affected team members outside the consultation process. Prepare consultation materials, participate in health and safety committees where they exist, and retain the consent, hearing, and anonymized reporting records each jurisdiction requires. Run the workplace violence prevention program. Coordinate the threat management team, train managers, and keep case records and legally required local plans up to date. Coordinate crisis response and disaster relief. Keep the crisis plan and records current, with a lead and alternate for every role. During on-call shifts, follow escalation procedures and support the crisis team until the response ends. Work with the relief partner to track the Team Member Disaster Relief fund balance and handle communications and reporting. Maintain continuity plans for the People Group, assess the impact of disruptions, and prioritize recovery of essential services. Keep records for auditors and insurers, run crisis and continuity exercises, lead required reviews after incidents, and track follow-up actions until they're complete. Assess each requirement in ISO 45001, ISO 22361, and ISO 22301. Each week, review the scores produced by AI agents and correct any errors. Set deadlines for improvements and report quarterly on the safety and resilience measures in the People Safety & Security scorecard. Lead people safety and crisis response on site at major company events alongside security operations. Confirm emergency procedures and medical arrangements are in place, serve as the crisis team's on-site contact, and lead a review after each event. What you’ll bring Experience delivering safety and resilience programs across several countries, covering occupational health and safety plus either crisis management or business continuity. Knowledge of when to consult team members or works councils, hold hearings, or make decisions jointly, and how to document each step. To handle GitLab's legal obligations in Germany, you'll need direct experience applying the Betriebsverfassungsgesetz and Arbeitssicherheitsgesetz. Ability to apply ISO 45001, ISO 22361, ISO 22301, the ANSI/ASIS workplace violence prevention standard, and the European Union framework directive on safety and health at work when drafting policies and procedures with legal and compliance partners and external advisers. Experience managing programs across teams, including assigning owners and deadlines, tracking regulatory changes, and preparing records for audits. Experience using artificial intelligence (AI) agents to draft materials, score assessments, and update records, and checking their results against the standards. Ability to keep that work repeatable by tracking changes to prompts and workflows in GitLab. Ability to travel internationally up to 30% of the time to support company events, run exercises, and attend health and safety committee meetings in person. These commitments can fall in the evenings or on weekends. Availability outside standard working hours as part of a scheduled on-call duty rotation and when the crisis management plan is activated, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays. Experience managing consultations across Germany, the Netherlands, and France is strongly preferred because you'll work under each country's rules. Safety or business continuity credentials, ISO 45001 or ISO 22301 lead implementer or auditor qualifications, and works council or labor relations training are also helpful. About the team We work with Employment Law, People Compliance, Benefits, the crisis management team, team member representatives, and external partners. You'll bring these partners together through plans with named owners and deadlines. Our People Safety & Security scorecard tracks how well we meet safety standards and legal duties and which consultations, exercises, and incident reviews we've completed. It also tracks whether we've reviewed continuity plans in the past twelve months and closed audit findings by their deadlines.
