🏢 dropbox 📍 Remote - Canada: Select locations 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Role Description Dropbox is seeking a Head of Revenue Operations to lead and scale the operational foundation of the company's global B2B go-to-market organization. This role will be responsible for driving operational excellence across the revenue engine, enabling predictable, efficient growth across SMB, Mid-Market, and Enterprise segments. This role will also oversee Marketing Operations, ensuring alignment between demand generation, pipeline creation, and revenue outcomes across the full customer lifecycle. This leader will own revenue planning, forecasting, revenue operations, incentives, systems, analytics, and GTM infrastructure. Partnering closely with Sales, Finance, Product, Marketing, and Customer leadership, this role will play a critical role in strengthening execution rigor, improving forecast accuracy, and enabling Dropbox to scale across products, regions, and sales motions, including product-led, sales-assisted, and enterprise.
