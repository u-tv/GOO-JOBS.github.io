Veeam Software: Campaign Marketing Intern - Summer 2027

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote, Georgia, USA URL: http://veeam.com Veeam is the Data and AI Trust Company, specializing in helping organizations ensure their data and AI are fully understood, secured, and resilient to enable the acceleration of safe AI at scale. As the market leader in both data resilience and data security posture management, Veeam is built for the convergence of identity, data, security, and AI risk. Headquartered in Seattle with offices in more than 30 countries, Veeam protects over 550,000 customers worldwide, who trust Veeam to keep their businesses running. Join us as we go fearlessly forward together, growing, learning, and making a real impact for some of the world’s biggest brands. About Out Summer Internship Program Our Summer Internship Program is designed for students entering their final year of university who are eager to gain meaningful, real-world experience in a fast paced, collaborative, and professional environment. As a Summer Intern, you'll participate in a comprehensive onboarding experience led by our University Relations team to set you up for success from day one. Throughout the program, you'll also have the opportunity to participate in weekly professional development sessions, networking events, social activities, and other engaging experienced designed to support your personal and professional growth. The program takes place from June – August 2027 (10-week program). Your Role As a Campaign Marketing Intern, you will support the planning and execution of global marketing programs that drive prospect and customer engagement, sales signals, and pipeline. This role provides hands-on experience in campaign marketing and cross-functional coordination with product marketing, field marketing, execution teams, and agencies. You’ll learn how campaigns come together from strategy to execution, touching everything from messaging and segmentation to digital channels, analytics, and optimization. This internship is ideal for students interested in future roles in campaign marketing, demand generation, digital marketing, or broader gotomarket teams. Key Responsibilities Campaign Support Support development of targeted outreach campaigns tied to Veeam’s Go To Market (GTM) framework Assist with segmentation lists, project tracking, and campaign performance monitoring Create promotional Bill of Materials (BOM) to drive engagement and pipeline for our GTM Help ensure messaging aligns with global priorities and audience needs Digital Marketing Channels Work closely with Demand Gen execution teams to deliver campaign results, including email marketing, digital advertising, online events, and web Shadow execution team meetings to dive deeper into the various execution arms of Demand Gen Analytics & Insights Conduct analysis on campaign and segment performance, diving into what is working, not working, and compiling recommendations Propose A/B tests and optimizations to help improve performance Analyze content and channel patterns and present to campaign leaders Qualifications Rising Junior or Senior currently pursuing a degree in Marketing, Business, Communications, or related field Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple moving pieces Excellent written and verbal communication skills Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (especially Excel and PowerPoint) Interest in campaign marketing, digital marketing, or global marketing Comfortable working in a fast-paced, collaborative environment Detail-oriented with a proactive, problem-solving mindset Benefits As a paid intern at Veeam, you’ll receive: Paid Company Holidays during your internship Tech Stipend to help set up your workspace 8 Hours of Paid Volunteer Time through our Veeam Cares Program Personal and Professional Development through our Internship Program We’re committed to providing a supportive and rewarding internship experience.
