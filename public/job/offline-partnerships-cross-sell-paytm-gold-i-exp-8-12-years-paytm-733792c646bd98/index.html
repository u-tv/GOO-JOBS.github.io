About Paytm Paytm is a pioneer of digital payments in India, serving over 450 million consumers and 45 million merchants across payments, financial services, and commerce. Over the years, Paytm has built deep in-house capabilities across technology, data, and operations to operate at scale with high reliability. Role Overview We are looking for a high-ownership, execution-first General Manager to drive cross-sell initiatives by working closely with offline business teams and partners across locations. This role is critical in bridging central product and business teams with on-ground leaders, ensuring rapid execution, structured feedback loops, and strong MIS-driven decision making. The role demands significant travel, deep coordination with senior field leaders, and the ability to operate with an entrepreneurial mindset in ambiguous, fast-moving environments.
