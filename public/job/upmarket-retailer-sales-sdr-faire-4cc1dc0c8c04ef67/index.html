Upmarket Retailer Sales SDR

🏢 faire 📍 San Francisco, CA 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Faire Faire is a technology wholesale platform built on the belief that the future is local. Independent retailers around the globe collectively represent a multi-hundred-billion-dollar wholesale market that has historically been fragmented and offline. At Faire, we're using the power of tech, data, and machine learning to connect this thriving community of entrepreneurs across the globe. Picture your favorite boutique in town — we help them discover the best products from around the world to sell in their stores. With the right tools and insights, we believe that we can level the playing field so businesses can grow and local communities can thrive. We’re looking for smart, resourceful and passionate people to join us as we power the shop local movement. If you believe in community, come join ours. About this role We are hiring across several early-career and early-tenure revenue roles on our Brand and Retailer teams. Rather than run separate searches, we are running one process. You will interview once, and our hiring managers will match you to the open role where you are the strongest fit. Depending on experience and interview performance, that could be: Sales Development Representative, Brand Partnerships (L1a): Prospect and qualify brands to expand Faire's supply and feed pipeline for our Account Executives. Senior Sales Development Representative, Brand Partnerships (L1b): Run strategic outbound motions toward high-value and enterprise brands, and contribute to team process and mentorship. Sales Development Representative, Upmarket Retailer Sales (L1a or L1b): Run strategic outbound motions toward high-value and enterprise retailers, and contribute to team process and mentorship. Scaled Success Manager (L1-L2): Own outcomes across a portfolio of 1000+ active brand partners, driving adoption of Faire’s tools and programs to help them grow. All three roles share a common core: you are a self-starter who uses data, creative outreach and strong communication to help brands and retailers find and scale their wholesale businesses. What you’ll do Across all roles Manage a large book of brand or retailer accounts, prioritizing where your time drives the most impact Run outreach across multiple channels: cold and warm calls, email, social/ Linkedin, webinars and events, tailoring your message to each brand's goals, category and pain points Use Salesforce, Salesloft, Looker or similar tools to track pipeline and pacing, spot opportunities and gaps, and adjust your approach Partner with Account Executives, Product, Marketing and other teams, and share patterns, learnings, and solutions Contribute ideas for better playbooks and processes, and share best practices with teammates Make AI a core part of how you work. Continuously test new tools, experiment with different workflows, iterate based on what drives results, and share learnings to help raise the bar for the entire team. If you join Brand Partnerships (SDR / Sr. SDR) Prospect and identify high-value brands that fit Faire's retailer base, and qualify inbound leads and applicant brands Generate qualified pipeline for the Account Executive team (Sr. SDRs: manage territories and priorities in partnership with AEs, and focus on strategic and enterprise brands) Help build and maintain our brand directory If you join Brand Success (Scaled Success Manager) Own outcomes for a portfolio of 1,000+ brands, deciding where to focus and how to put the team’s playbooks to work for each one Advise brands on best practices, product adoption and pricing for Faire's tools and programs (such as Faire Direct) Hit your monthly goals by managing your pipeline and balancing competing priorities Help brands grow through scalable initiatives such as webinars, content and growth experiments If you join Retailer Sales (Upmarket SDR) Own top-of-funnel for ~200 enterprise retailers, partnering with 2 senior AEs to turn target accounts into qualified pipeline through segmentation, tailored multichannel outreach, and MEDDIC qualification Land early buy-in across complex retail orgs, multi-threading to build champions and speed up deals from the start Fuel lead gen through tradeshow support and event follow-up Qualifications Must haves Bachelor's degree or equivalent professional experience 0-3 years in no sales, business development, account management, customer success or another customer-facing role is a plus, but not required for all openings. We welcome recent graduates who are self-starters, bring a growth mindset and have an entrepreneurial spirit looking to grow their careers in sales Exceptionally strong verbal and written communication, including the ability to lead prospect conversations with confidence and turn data into a clear story A genuine hunger to learn, experiment, and push boundaries with AI and emerging tools. You’re naturally curious, constantly looking for new ways to work smarter, and energized by testing new approaches to find what works. Strong organization and prioritization skills for managing a large number of accounts at once Comfort with data: you run reports, read dashboards and use them to decide where to focus Strong judgment and attention to detail in assessing which brands are the best fit for Faire Motivated, curious and action-oriented, with integrity, and energized by a fast-moving environment with changing priorities Genuine passion for Faire's mission and for helping small businesses, artisans and entrepreneurs thrive Collaborative mindset: you share what you learn and help the team improve Nice to haves Experience with Salesforce, Salesloft, Looker or similar sales and reporting platforms Cold calling and objection handling experience Experience in SaaS, marketplace, ecommerce or startup environments Retail, merchandising, wholesale or small business experience Enterprise or strategic account prospecting experience is a plus (especially relevant for Sr. SDR) Entrepreneurial mindset and a desire to help shape a growing sales organization Salary Range L1A: San Fransisco : the OTE pay range for this role is $70,500 to $97,000 per year. L1B: San Fransisco : the OTE pay range for this role is $80,000 to $110,000 per year. L2A : San Fransisco : the OTE pay range for this role is $112,000 to $154,000 per year.
