About the Role This role focuses on sourcing and managing suppliers globally for Data Center Infrastructure services, including structured cabling, DC infrastructure installation, power system installation, cooling system deployment, cage/space fitout, and critical infrastructure commissioning. You will create sourcing plans for infrastructure categories and deliver these through supplier capability reviews, technical assessments, pre-qualification, onboarding, and master contract negotiations. This position requires deep technical knowledge of data center infrastructure design and build-out—you must understand power and cooling sizing calculations, cold aisle containment design principles, caging requirements, electrical distribution architectures, and the end-to-end process of a full-scale DC space/cage fitout. You will leverage this expertise to drive informed vendor discussions, evaluate technical proposals against Cloudflare's infrastructure standards, and negotiate competitive pricing that reflects true value and risk allocation. You will forge strategic partnerships with infrastructure vendors, evaluate their performance against clearly defined technical and commercial metrics, and balance the priorities of both Cloudflare and your suppliers to achieve mutually beneficial outcomes. This role complements the Global Vendor Manager, Data Center Services (focused on field services, IT asset disposal, logistics, and rack integration) by owning the infrastructure build, installation, and critical systems domain.
