Senior Influencer Manager

🏢 airbnb 📍 London, United Kingdom 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Airbnb was born in 2007 when two hosts welcomed three guests to their San Francisco home, and has since grown to over 5 million hosts who have welcomed over 2 billion guest arrivals in almost every country across the globe. Every day, hosts offer unique stays and experiences that make it possible for guests to connect with communities in a more authentic way. The Community You Will Join The Airbnb Communications team drives earned visibility and cultural relevance for the brand, delivering campaigns that support direct traffic, brand love and positive conversation. Our influencer function sits at the intersection of culture, content and community — leading social-first campaigns that inspire travel on Airbnb and strengthen its long-term reputation. The Difference You Will Make We're looking for a Senior Influencer Manager to lead Airbnb's influencer programme in the UK, localising regional priorities and delivering campaigns that connect the brand to culturally relevant conversations. The role covers our full influencer remit: earned and organic creator partnerships, and influencer work running through paid media. You'll own campaigns end to end — creator sourcing, briefing, contracting, measurement and reporting — and help position influencers as both a commercial lever and a long-term driver of brand health, while collaborating with agency partners. Sitting in the UK Communications team, you'll build influencer into campaign narratives from inception, and partner with Brand Marketing and Paid Media so creator content is designed for amplification from the outset, with usage rights, whitelisting and dark posting permissions secured up front. This is a hands-on role for someone who knows how to turn brand objectives into content that is authentic, thumb-stopping and earns attention rather than interrupting it.
