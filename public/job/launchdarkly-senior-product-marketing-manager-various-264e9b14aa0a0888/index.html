LaunchDarkly: Senior Product Marketing Manager

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote - US About the Job: Teams are putting AI agents into production everywhere, and almost no one owns controlling them once they’re there. Agents make decisions at runtime, change behavior with every model update, and touch systems that were never designed for them. The teams that figure out how to govern, observe, and control agents in production will set the pace for the AI era. That’s the category we’re building. LaunchDarkly is hiring a Senior Product Marketing Manager to own the story and go-to-market for how teams govern, observe, and control AI agents in production, a new capability within the LaunchDarkly platform. You’ll set the strategy, build the story, and be judged on pipeline and revenue, not assets shipped. You’ll work with Product and Demand Gen on go-to-market strategy, with Sales on live deals, with RevOps on pricing and packaging, and with partners and analysts on defining the category. It’s early, it’s moving fast, and you’ll have real ownership. This is a senior individual contributor role for someone who operates with minimal direction. You’ll thrive here if you’re data-oriented and a GTM operator at heart: curious about what’s happening in the market, quick to dig into the numbers, and able to identify and size new opportunities before anyone asks. You’d rather connect what product, sales, and marketing are each seeing into one strategic direction than optimize one part of the business, and you want to be measured on business outcomes, not deliverables. Responsibilities: Own the go-to-market plan for agent management — know what’s happening in the business and the market, build and deliver the GTM plan against company goals, and align product, sales, and marketing leaders behind it. Bring GTM input and recommendations into quarterly and annual planning. Build the category narrative — define the audience, positioning, and differentiation for agent management with product and PMM leadership, grounded in customer and market research. Differentiate against observability, LLMOps, and agent-framework vendors, and adapt the narrative across web, content, and sales conversations. Lead go-to-market for launches — define the story, audience, and launch plan for releases, events, and customer stories, and work with cross-functional partners so each ships with a clear message and enabled field teams. Enable sales to win — work with sales leaders and reps to build what they need, from enablement and discovery guidance to outbound messaging and competitive positioning. Track win-rate impact, and support strategic deals where the narrative is decisive. Optimize the customer journey — partner with marketing, sales development, sales, and customer success to find where buyers stall from first touch to closed-won, and recommend and implement fixes that improve conversion, velocity, and deal quality. Experiment and adapt the GTM plan — build a clear view of how agent management is performing, monitor market and funnel data, run tests with marketing and sales across audiences, messaging, and channels, and use customer conversations and win/loss analysis to decide what to scale, change, or stop. Be the voice of the market — share customer, field, and data insights with product and marketing leadership to inform roadmap, pricing and packaging, and investment decisions. Brief analysts on agent management, and report on GTM progress and outcomes. Qualifications: 8+ years in product marketing or a related GTM role at B2B software companies, including owning go-to-market for a segment or product tried to growth targets A track record of impact you can quantify: pipeline you built, win rate you moved, or revenue that grew because of work you led Fluency with technical buyers (developers, engineering leaders, AI teams) and the depth to hold your own with product managers and sales engineers Comfort in the data: you can read a funnel, find where it breaks, and turn that into a decision Strong writing and a high bar for quality, with examples that drove measurable business outcomes A history of building trust fast with sales and product, and influencing leaders without owning their teams Low ego and team-first: you care about the outcome more than the credit, and you step in where the team needs you Bonus: Experience marketing AI infrastructure, LLMOps, or agent tooling, or deep familiarity with the observability and DevOps categories we compete with Hands-on experience building AI-powered workflows Pay: Target pay ranges based on Geographic Zones* for Level 4: Zone 1: San Francisco/Bay Area or NYC Metropolitan Area, Boston, Seattle - $156,700 - $215,490 ** Zone 2: Irvine, LA, Monterey, Santa Barbara, Santa Rosa, Austin, Portland, Philadelphia, Chicago - $141,000 - $193,930 ** LaunchDarkly operates from a place of high trust and transparency; we are happy to state the pay range for our open roles to best align with your needs. About LaunchDarkly: Modern software delivery was supposed to be the foundation for a thriving digital business but reality has proven otherwise. Slow, inefficient development cycles, costly outages, and fragmented customer experiences are preventing developers from building their best software. The LaunchDarkly platform helps developers innovate on new features faster while protecting them with a safety valve to instantly rewind when things go wrong. Developers can target product experiences to any customer segment and maximize the business impact of every feature. And by gradually rolling out new application components, they escape nightmare "big-bang" technology migrations. The LaunchDarkly platform was built to guide engineers to the next frontier of DevOps by: Improving the velocity and stability of software releases, without the fear of end customer outages Delivering targeted experiences by easily personalizing features to customer cohorts Maximizing the business impact of every feature through the ability to experiment and optimize Coordinating the release and optimization of software to provide consistent experiences across mobile platforms and device types Improving the effectiveness and productivity of engineering teams, by providing insights into engineering cadence and stability At LaunchDarkly, we believe in the power of teams.
