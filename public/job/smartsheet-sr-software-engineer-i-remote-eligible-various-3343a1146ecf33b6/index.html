Smartsheet: Sr. Software Engineer I (Remote Eligible)

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: -REMOTE, USA- For over 20 years, Smartsheet has empowered teams to manage work seamlessly and scale solutions smarter. Now, in our most ambitious chapter yet, we are uniting human teams with AI agents. By orchestrating the work agents do best, automating manual tasks and uncovering insights at scale, we create the space for people to focus on what truly matters: judgment, creativity, and big thinking. That is magic at work, and it’s what we show up for every day. Are you looking to solve challenging engineering problems impacting Enterprise customers with 10,000+ employees? How about building a platform that contributes to mission-critical projects such as launching a rover to Mars, reducing carbon footprint by launching energy-efficient warehouses, launching critical life-saving medicines in International markets, or planning a brick-and-mortar store deployment? The Smartsheet Portfolio Management Pillar is looking for curious and passionate Frontend and Fullstack Software Engineers. You will help us build enterprise-grade applications within the Portfolio Collaborative Work Management space, an area that grew 4x year-over-year. Our applications manages three areas that serve our customers: A feature-rich config management tool that allows power users the ability to create their business processes; A fast and reliable orchestration engine that runs millions of distributed transactions per day; A global management service that alters the attributes of Project assets and ensures the state of the Portfolio is running and updates are managed at-scale. Our Engineering team members are passionate about working on multiple projects using several technologies. We are a self-operating service team and use a diverse set of Frontend (JavaScript, TypeScript, React), Backend (Ruby/Java), and Cloud-native services(AWS). In 2005, Smartsheet was founded on the idea that teams and millions of people worldwide deserve a better way to deliver their very best work. Today, we deliver a leading cloud-based platform for work execution, empowering organizations to plan, capture, track, automate, and report on work at scale, resulting in more efficient processes and better business outcomes. You will report to our Sr. Manager, Engineering, and may work remotely from anywhere in the US where Smartsheet is a registered employer. You Will: Design, implement, and deliver production complete solutions. Promote best practices in Software development and Software methodologies. Automate repetitive tasks to increase productivity and efficiency. Contribute concise documentation. Promote Agility as much as Reliability and Scalability. You Have: 5+ years of engineering experience in a team environment. 3+ years of experience in service-oriented architecture, deployment systems, and cloud infrastructure. Proficient in at least one high-level programming language, such as Ruby, Java, Go, Python, and TypeScript. Experience with modern client JS libraries, like React, and Redux. Experience in application migration patterns and tradeoff scenarios. You have a knowledge of OOP and REST design principles. Clear and objective writing and in-person communication. Curiosity to learn new technologies and systems. A degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field or equivalent practical experience. Legally eligible to work in the U.S. on an ongoing basis.
