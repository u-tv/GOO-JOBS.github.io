About The Team The MongoDB Customer Organization Portfolio Management Office (PMO) orchestrates strategic initiatives and programs that advance customer experience, functional excellence, and technology innovation. Through comprehensive portfolio governance, the PMO optimizes investments and drives value realization across MDB’s Customer priorities to accelerate enterprise growth and enable customer success. As trusted advisors to leadership, the PMO implements data-driven frameworks that strengthen decision-making and cross-functional alignment of our most business-critical initiatives. This systematic approach to benefit tracking and resource optimization positions MongoDB to capture emerging market opportunities, maximize stakeholder value, and set new industry benchmarks. In this role, you'll be the driving force behind a portfolio of programs and strategic initiatives, each aimed at realizing customer value, optimizing our operations, and advancing our capabilities. You'll thrive on complexity and make swift, thoughtful, data-driven decisions that balance outcomes across the function and our customer org. Your ability to anticipate obstacles, work through developing problem spaces, influence cross-functional alignment, and realize strategic outcomes will be crucial in maximizing our collective impact.
