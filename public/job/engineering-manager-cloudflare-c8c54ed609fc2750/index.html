Engineering Manager

🏢 cloudflare 📍 Hybrid 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Us At Cloudflare, we are on a mission to help build a better Internet. Today the company runs one of the world’s largest networks that powers millions of websites and other Internet properties for customers ranging from individual bloggers to SMBs to Fortune 500 companies. Cloudflare protects and accelerates any Internet application online without adding hardware, installing software, or changing a line of code. Internet properties powered by Cloudflare all have web traffic routed through its intelligent global network, which gets smarter with every request. As a result, they see significant improvement in performance and a decrease in spam and other attacks. Cloudflare was named to Entrepreneur Magazine’s Top Company Cultures list and ranked among the World’s Most Innovative Companies by Fast Company. At Cloudflare, we’re not looking for people who wait for a polished roadmap; we’re looking for the builders who see the cracks in the Internet that everyone else has simply learned to live with. We value candidates who have the instinct to spot a &quot;normalized&quot; problem and the AI-native curiosity to create a solution using the latest tools. Our culture is built on iteration, leveraging AI to ship faster today to make it better tomorrow, while ensuring that every improvement, no matter how small, is shared across the team to lift everyone up. If you’re the type of person who values curiosity over bureaucracy, and that AI is a partner in solving tough problems to keep the Internet moving forward, you’ll fit right in. About the department Cloudflare's Engineering team builds and runs the software that handles a large share of the Internet's requests. We also build the internal tools and platforms that software depends on. Teams own large areas with real impact and work autonomously within them, whether their customers are external or other Cloudflare engineers. About the team Developer Productivity builds the internal tools and platforms Cloudflare engineers use to take code from commit to production. We own developer infrastructure (CI/CD, build systems), developer insights and ADLC metrics, AI-assisted development, and Cloudflare-on-Cloudflare dogfooding. We're responsible for AI-assisted development across all of Cloudflare engineering, and adoption has grown 4 to 6 times over in the last year. We also own the tooling behind Cloudflare's Engineering Codex, the standards every engineering team is expected to meet. About the role This is a front-line engineering manager role leading a team of 6+ engineers in Bengaluru. The team's initial focus will be on two areas: AI tools for engineers. Agents, agentic systems, MCP servers, evals, and context engineering that change how Cloudflare engineers write, review, and ship code. Engineering standards and the tooling that enforces them. Continuing to transform our Engineering standards into automated guardrails, paved paths, and feedback engineers can act on, instead of checklists and manual review. You'll own the product direction for both areas. There are no dedicated product managers on the team. Our users are Cloudflare engineers, and our engineers work as product engineers who talk to users, watch how tools get used, and help decide what to build next. You'll lead that work, deciding what to build and why, and making sure it gets adopted and solves real problems. You don't need to write code every day, but you should be able to review a design, debug a broken pipeline, or prototype an agent when the team needs it. We move fast. AI tooling changes month to month, so a six-month plan is stale in six weeks. We ship small, put it in front of engineers early, and change course based on what we hear. We'd rather ship a rough version this week and improve it next week than spend a month polishing a design doc. We add process only when it solves a real problem. You'll partner closely with engineering managers and principal engineers in the US, UK, and Europe, and work with teams across Cloudflare's global organization. The team starts in Bengaluru. As it grows, it may include engineers in other regions. What you'll do Hire, onboard, and grow your team. Coach engineers to think like product engineers, run career conversations, give direct feedback, and handle performance. Own the product roadmap. Spend time with the engineers who use your tools, decide which problems matter most, and say no to the rest. Keep the team shipping. Release in small increments, get feedback from users quickly, and cut process that slows the team down without adding value. Deliver in both focus areas: agents, MCP servers, and evals the rest of engineering relies on, and Codex standards turned into CI/CD checks, templates, and policy-as-code, with remediation and exception workflows to match. Measure whether the work is landing: tool adoption, Codex compliance, and what engineers actually think of the experience. Own operations for the platforms your team runs, including on-call and incident response. What we're looking for 3+ years managing software engineers, including hiring, onboarding, and developing people. 8+ years of total software engineering experience, with enough recent hands-on work to hold your own in code and design reviews. Hands-on experience with AI in engineering organizations, such as building agents, running agentic systems in production, or governing how a company uses AI. This is not a model-training or ML research role. Experience building or running developer tools, internal platforms, or CI/CD systems. Product and user thinking. You've owned a roadmap, with or without a PM, and you can point to decisions you made because of what users told you or how they actually used what you built. A bias to action. You've led teams that shipped often and changed direction when the evidence called for it, while still delivering on commitments. Clear judgment and clear writing. You make sound trade-offs and explain the &quot;why&quot; as well as the &quot;how&quot;. Much of your work with partners will happen asynchronously across time zones. Bonus points You've built MCP servers or LLM evals, or worked on context engineering for agents. Experience with policy-as-code or compliance automation. Experience measuring developer productivity, using frameworks such as DORA or SPACE, or developer surveys. You've built a team from scratch or started an engineering presence in a new location. Familiarity with TypeScript, Go, or Kubernetes-based systems. What Makes Cloudflare Special? We’re not just a highly ambitious, large-scale technology company. We’re a highly ambitious, large-scale technology company with a soul. Fundamental to our mission to help build a better Internet is protecting the free and open Internet. Project Galileo : Since 2014, we've equipped more than 2,400 journalism and civil society organizations in 111 countries with powerful tools to defend themselves against attacks that would otherwise censor their work, technology already used by Cloudflare’s enterprise customers--at no cost. Athenian Project : In 2017, we created the Athenian Project to ensure that state and local governments have the highest level of protection and reliability for free, so that their constituents have access to election information and voter registration. Since the project, we've provided services to more than 425 local government election websites in 33 states. 1.1.1.1 : We released 1.1.1.1 to help fix the foundation of the Internet by building a faster, more secure and privacy-centric public DNS resolver. This is available publicly for everyone to use - it is the first consumer-focused service Cloudflare has ever released. Here’s the deal - we don’t store client IP addresses never, ever. We will continue to abide by our privacy commitment and ensure that no user data is sold to advertisers or used to target consumers. Sound like something you’d like to be a part of?
