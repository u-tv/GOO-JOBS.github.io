Property Accountant – Re-Leased Trust Accounting (REMOTE) What you’ll do Manage rent and outgoings invoicing, allocate tenant receipts, and code supplier invoices to the correct property and owner ledgers. Reconcile trust bank accounts in Re-Leased and investigate unmatched receipts, incorrect allocations and ledger discrepancies. Prepare supplier payment runs and owner disbursements in line with approval controls. Complete month-end close, reconcile owner balances, and prepare owner statements and property financial reports. Support annual outgoings reconciliations and work with the New Zealand property management team to resolve billing queries and arrears. What you’ll bring Proven New Zealand property accounting experience, including direct responsibility for trust accounting in Re-Leased. Experience personally managing a sizeable portfolio with multiple owners, properties and tenancies. Ability to independently complete trust reconciliations, prepare owner statements and disbursements, and close month-end. Commercial property accounting experience, including tenant outgoings, is strongly preferred. To apply: https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/d2b-ap-property-accountant-nz-experience
