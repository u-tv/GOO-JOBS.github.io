Joby: Customer Support & Product Demo Specialist

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Miami, FL, USA URL: https://joby.io/ About Joby Joby (joby.io) is an all-in-one CRM for home-service businesses - HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, cleaning, landscaping and 70+ other industries. Service teams use Joby to manage leads, schedule jobs, send estimates and invoices, take payments, and talk to their customers - all in one place. The role We are looking for an experienced Customer Support & Product Demo Specialist to be the face of Joby for new and existing customers. This is a full-time, fully remote role, open worldwide. What you will do Run live product demos of Joby over Zoom for prospective customers and show them how it fits their business Handle day-to-day customer support in Intercom (live chat and tickets), from quick how-to questions to troubleshooting Host onboarding and support calls over Zoom to get new customers set up for success Turn common questions into help-center articles and saved replies Pass customer feedback to the team so the product keeps getting better What we are looking for 2+ years in customer support or customer success at a SaaS company Hands-on experience with Intercom (or a similar tool such as Zendesk) and with running screen-share demos on Zoom Excellent spoken and written English, confident presenting to business owners Self-managed, reliable and empathetic - you will often be the first person our customers talk to Bonus: experience with CRMs or with home-service / field-service businesses What we offer Full-time remote position, open worldwide $25,000 - $48,999 per year, depending on experience and location Product training and a clear playbook for demos and support A small, fast-moving team where your feedback directly shapes the product How to apply Email info@joby.io with your CV, a short note about the SaaS products you have supported, and - bonus points - a short screen recording of you demoing any product you know well. To apply: https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/joby-customer-support-product-demo-specialist
