Wisevu: Web Development Project Manager

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Bosnia And Herzegovina URL: http://wisevu.com Description Wisevu™, along with our sister agencies Homevu™ and charitymarketing.com ™, has been providing industry-leading digital marketing services to medical practices, home service companies, and charities for over 17 years. Our multi-industry focus and diverse marketing approach create a dynamic and engaging work environment, supported by cutting-edge tools and collaboration with a highly skilled and experienced team of professionals. We are driven by a mission to harness our collective online marketing wisdom to help organizations that contribute to the betterment of our communities achieve their organizational objectives. Learn more at wisevu.com. Responsibilities Manage multiple website development projects from kickoff through launch, ensuring projects are delivered on time, within scope, and within budget. Coordinate cross-functional teams, including WordPress developers, designers, SEO specialists, copywriters, and QA, to ensure smooth project execution. Define project scope, priorities, timelines, deliverables, and resource allocation. Serve as the primary point of contact for clients by providing project updates, managing expectations, and resolving issues proactively. Review deliverables before client presentation to ensure quality, functionality, SEO best practices, accessibility, and alignment with project requirements. Identify project risks, remove blockers, and keep projects moving efficiently. Requirements Must-haves: Professional-level English proficiency (C1 or higher). Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, IT, Business, Management, Marketing, or a related field. A minimum of 1 year of experience managing WordPress website development projects within a digital marketing agency or web development agency. Strong understanding of the WordPress website development lifecycle, including discovery, design, development, QA, launch, and post-launch support. Experience managing multiple client projects simultaneously while meeting deadlines. Experience coordinating cross-functional teams, including developers, designers, SEO specialists, and content teams. Experience using project management tools such as Asana or similar. Nice-to-haves: Hands-on experience with WordPress, including basic site configuration and content management. Understanding of HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and PHP. Familiarity with website performance, Core Web Vitals, technical SEO, and accessibility best practices. Experience working with website builders such as Elementor, Divi, or Gutenberg. Project Management certification. Experience working in Agile/Scrum environments. Work-timings: Able to provide at least 7 hours of overlap with the 9 am-5 pm EST workday (Monday - Friday). Benefits What We Offer: Fully remote work with flexible working hours. Competitive salary, which will be negotiated during the interview based on competencies, skills, and experience. Annual salary review. Premium training programs and access to an internal knowledge base built across 17 years of hands-on experience. Professional equipment budget. Access to cutting-edge tools. Long-term and stable working relationship. Apply now and help us achieve exceptional digital marketing results—together! We kindly ask that you provide us with your resume in English.
