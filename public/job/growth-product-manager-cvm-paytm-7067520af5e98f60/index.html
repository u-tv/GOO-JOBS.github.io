Growth & Product Manager - CVM

🏢 paytm 📍 Noida, Uttar Pradesh 💼 On-roll 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Paytm Group: Paytm is India's leading mobile payments and financial services distribution company. Pioneer of the mobile QR payments revolution in India, Paytm builds technologies that help small businesses with payments and commerce. Paytm’s mission is to serve half a billion Indians and bring them to the mainstream economy with the help of technology. About the Team: Our Key Offerings are divided into 5 broad categories as follows: • Entertainment • Digital Platforms • CVM Solutions • Enterprise Services • Financial Platforms One97 has the widest and largest deployment of telecom applications on cloud platforms in India and has a myriad of VAS services that have helped operators augment their revenue even in complex markets like India, SAARC, Middle East, Africa and many more. Role Overview We are looking for a Growth & Product Manager with strong telecom domain experience to drive business growth, consulting engagements, and client relationships. The candidate will work closely with telecom operators to identify revenue monetization opportunities, develop customer engagement strategies, and recommend solutions leveraging CVM, analytics, and digital platforms. The role requires a combination of business consulting, growth strategy, product understanding, client management, and internal stakeholder coordination. Key Responsibilities Work closely with telecom operators to understand their business objectives, challenges, and revenue drivers, and identify opportunities for business growth and monetization. Develop and recommend growth strategies to improve ARPU, revenue, customer acquisition, retention, cross-sell, upsell, and customer lifetime value. Drive consulting engagements around Customer Value Management (CVM), campaign management, customer lifecycle management, segmentation, targeting, and personalized customer engagement. Identify and define business use cases across prepaid, postpaid, broadband, and digital services, leveraging subscriber behaviour, usage patterns, and customer analytics. Manage client relationships and act as a key point of contact for business discussions, requirement gathering, solution recommendations, and ongoing engagements. Conduct client workshops, business reviews, and strategic discussions with stakeholders across Marketing, CVM, Digital, Analytics, and Business teams. Translate client business challenges into actionable growth initiatives, product use cases, and functional requirements, working closely with internal product and technology teams. Coordinate with internal stakeholders across Product, Engineering, Analytics, Sales, and Delivery to drive client requirements, manage dependencies, and ensure timely execution. Monitor business performance and growth KPIs such as ARPU, revenue uplift, churn, campaign conversion, recharge frequency, customer engagement, and campaign ROI; provide actionable recommendations for improvement. Evaluate campaign performance and recommend optimization strategies around customer targeting, offer effectiveness, channel selection, and campaign journeys. Identify product enhancement opportunities based on client feedback, market trends, competitive benchmarking, and evolving telecom business requirements. Prepare business proposals, consulting recommendations, solution presentations, product demonstrations, and business cases highlighting potential value and revenue impact. Proactively manage client expectations, stakeholder alignment, business escalations, and delivery dependencies to ensure successful client engagements. Identify opportunities for account growth, new use cases, and expansion of existing engagements by understanding evolving client business needs. Stay updated on telecom industry trends, CVM platforms, digital engagement solutions, customer analytics, and emerging technologies to identify new business and product opportunities. Required Skills & Experience 5–9 years of experience in Telecom Consulting, Growth Management, CVM, Business Strategy, or Product Management. Strong understanding of telecom operator business models, customer lifecycle, and revenue monetization. Experience in client-facing consulting engagements and managing cross-functional internal stakeholders. Good understanding of CVM, campaign management, customer segmentation, targeting, and customer engagement strategies. Strong business acumen, analytical skills, communication, and presentation capabilities. Experience in translating business requirements into actionable solutions and driving execution with technology teams. Understanding of telecom KPIs such as ARPU, churn, revenue uplift, customer retention, and campaign ROI. Experience working with telecom operators across multiple geographies will be an added advantage.
