Minimum of 3 years of experience in a technical sales role, managing C-Level technical and business relationships Strong technical acumen with experience in conducting technical discovery and delivering high-impact value presentations. Ability to solve problems independently while thriving in collaborative team environments Proven time management skills in a dynamic team environment Demonstrated ability to quickly identify and communicate the value proposition throughout the sales cycle.
