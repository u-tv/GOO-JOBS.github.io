Head of Global Partnerships and Startup and Ecosystem Product, Finance & Strategy

🏢 stripe 📍 SF, NYC, SEA 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies - from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups - use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team Our team partners with Stripe’s Chief Business Officer and leaders across Global Partnerships and Startup and Ecosystem Product. Global Partnerships builds and manages relationships with banks, networks, payment methods, and other strategic partners that enable Stripe’s products and global expansion. The Startup and Ecosystem Product teams build tools and capabilities that help founders start and grow businesses and partners to extend Stripe products to better serve users across our entire ecosystem, respectively. The Finance & Strategy team brings financial rigor, strategic clarity, and analytical depth to the decisions that shape Stripe’s business and long-term growth strategy. We act as a strategic finance function for complex commercial partner negotiations and work with product leaders to shape their strategy, growth, investment priorities, and financial performance as it scales. We help leaders evaluate partner economics, allocate resources, establish goals, and understand performance in order to turn complex analysis into clear recommendations. What you’ll do We’re looking for a finance leader to lead Finance & Strategy for Global Partnerships and Startup and Ecosystem Product. You’ll serve as a trusted financial and strategic advisor to the Chief Business Officer and other senior business leaders, connecting commercial decisions to their financial implications. You’ll lead a team of strategic finance professionals to craft deal analysis and structure recommendations, investment frameworks, and strategic insights that drive long-term economic growth for Stripe. In addition, you will work cross-functionally to ensure disciplined financial planning and forecasting. You’ll develop the team, establish clear priorities, and balance time-sensitive deal support with recurring financial commitments and targeted strategic analysis. Responsibilities Lead the financial evaluation of complex product enablement partnerships. Partner with Global Partnerships leaders and Partner Development Managers to evaluate and execute commercial agreements with new and existing partners, bringing an independent financial perspective to negotiation strategy and decisions. Guide deal economics and investment analysis. Oversee financial modeling, commercial benchmarking, scenario analysis, and investment cases. Translate complex deal structures into clear assessments of financial impact, risks, returns, and commercial trade-offs. Improve partner economics and performance visibility. Develop data-driven insights to identify strategic and optimization opportunities for Stripe’s partner portfolio. Establish consistent frameworks and metrics to assess partner initiatives and deals. Build investment cases in support of new business initiatives. Craft financial models in support of Startup and Ecosystem Product initiatives and establish objectives and metrics in partnership with Business and Product leaders. Actively contribute to org building as part of the F&S leadership team. Help shape and execute F&S priorities, influence and drive cross-functional initiatives, and contribute to culture building of the organization. Coordinate cross-functional financial recommendations. Work closely with Product and Engineering, Pricing, Strategy and Operations, Treasury Finance, Accounting, and other Finance & Strategy teams to support planning and forecasting activities. Own the financial planning and forecasting cycle. Lead annual budgeting, forecasting, and business reviews for Global Partnerships and Startup and Ecosystem Product areas. Guide operating expense management. Maintain visibility into resource plans, assess investment and efficiency trade-offs, and surface material changes that require leadership decisions. Partner on goals, metrics, and performance. Help leaders establish financial objectives and key business metrics for priority businesses and initiatives. Explain material deviations from plan, identify underlying drivers, and recommend actions. Build and develop a high-performing team. Coach and develop direct reports, set clear expectations, and maintain high standards for analytical quality, business partnership, and execution. Scale the team’s effectiveness. Improve financial models, reporting, planning processes, and analytical infrastructure. Use AI, automation, and better tooling to reduce manual work and create capacity for higher-value analysis. Who you are We're looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement. Minimum requirements 15+ years in strategic finance, commercial finance, product finance, or FP&A, with demonstrated success leading and developing finance teams. Strong commercial judgment and experience evaluating complex partnership agreements, building investment cases, and supporting negotiations through rigorous financial analysis. Strong grounding in budgeting, forecasting, headcount and operating expense planning, and business performance management. A proven ability to build trusted relationships with executives and cross-functional leaders, provide an objective perspective, and influence consequential decisions. Exceptional analytical and financial modeling capabilities, with the ability to connect detailed economics to broader business strategy. A hands-on leadership style and the judgment to prioritize across urgent transactions, recurring operating commitments, and longer-term strategic work. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including experience presenting complex financial information and recommendations to senior leaders. Comfort working with large, complex datasets, a working knowledge of SQL, and the ability to create structure in an ambiguous, fast-moving environment. Preferred qualifications Experience at a high-growth payments company, fintech, or bank Deep understanding of payments unit economics, including card networks, banks, and the role of partnerships in enabling financial products. Prior experience in investment banking, private equity, or another transaction-oriented analytical role. Experience using AI, automation, and financial planning or analytics tools to improve the quality and scalability of finance work.
