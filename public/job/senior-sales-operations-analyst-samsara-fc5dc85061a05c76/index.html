Senior Sales Operations Analyst

🏢 samsara 📍 Remote - US 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are Samsara (NYSE: IOT) is the pioneer of the Connected Operations™ Cloud, which is a platform that enables organizations that depend on physical operations to harness Internet of Things (IoT) data to develop actionable insights and improve their operations. At Samsara, we are helping improve the safety, efficiency and sustainability of the physical operations that power our global economy. Representing more than 40% of global GDP, these industries are the infrastructure of our planet, including agriculture, construction, field services, transportation, and manufacturing — and we are excited to help digitally transform their operations at scale. Working at Samsara means you’ll help define the future of physical operations and be on a team that’s shaping an exciting array of product solutions, including Video-Based Safety, Vehicle Telematics, Apps and Driver Workflows, and Equipment Monitoring. As part of a recently public company, you’ll have the autonomy and support to make an impact as we build for the long term. About the role: Samsara is looking for a strategic and highly effective Senior Sales Operations Analyst to support our fast-growing partner sales organization. This role will partner directly with partner leadership and the partner programs team to support day-to-day partner operations, implement and manage all partner processes, and contribute to the strategic design of new partner programs. The ideal candidate has experience driving operational programs that bring together people, process, and technology to solve complex business problems — and is passionate about building scalable solutions in a fast-paced, cross-functional environment. The ideal candidate has experience at a high-growth B2B SaaS company, preferably public, with demonstrated success designing and scaling processes in an indirect or partner sales environment. This is a remote position open to candidates residing in the US except the San Francisco Bay Metro Area, NYC Metro Area, and Washington, D.C. Metro Area. You should apply if: You want to impact the industries that run our world: Your efforts will result in real-world impact—helping to keep the lights on, get food into grocery stores, and most importantly, ensure workers return home safely. You have an innate curiosity about how businesses work: One day you'll be working through a partner onboarding process, and the next you may be designing the operational framework for an entirely new channel program. You seek to understand the businesses you support in order to make a larger impact. You build genuine relationships: Partner sales runs on trust. You know how to build lasting relationships with internal and external stakeholders and show up as a reliable, strategic resource for the teams you support. You want to be with the best: Samsara's high-performance culture means you'll be surrounded by the best and challenged to go farther than you have before. You are a team player: At Samsara, sales is a team sport. We help each other out by sharing best practices and focusing on winning as a team. In this role, you will: Partner directly with partner leadership and the partner programs team to support day-to-day partner operations, serving as the primary Sales Ops point of contact for all partner sales needs. Implement and manage all partner sales processes, ensuring they are scalable, repeatable, and aligned with Samsara's global sales policies and systems. Contribute to the strategic design of new partner programs, including operational playbooks, cross-functional alignment, and change management execution as new programs are launched. Own project objectives and milestones with minimal guidance, driving smaller-scope projects or complex cross-functional workstreams end-to-end. Serve as the subject matter expert on all sales processes that involve channel partners, advising on solution design, system enhancements, and 'run-the-business' items. Devise and implement new solutions that solve complex project-level issues, including improvements and UAT for tools such as Salesforce used across the partner sales organization. Proactively identify and prioritize process and system improvements that enhance productivity for partner-facing sales teams. Champion, role model, and embed Samsara's cultural principles (Focus on Customer Success, Build for the Long Term, Adopt a Growth Mindset, Be Inclusive, Win as a Team) as we scale globally. Minimum requirements for the role: 4+ years of experience in a Sales Operations or related operational/strategic role supporting sales organizations in a dynamic, high-growth environment Demonstrated ability to design, implement, and scale sales processes and policies, with strong organizational skills and the ability to manage projects from end-to-end Proficiency in Salesforce and the broader sales technology ecosystem; ability to build and maintain reports, dashboards, and operational workflows Strong analytical skills with comfort performing quantitative analysis on large data sets to surface insights, track KPIs, and inform business decisions Proven ability to manage complex, cross-functional programs and coordinate across multiple stakeholders with competing priorities Strong written and verbal communication skills; experience partnering with and presenting to senior and executive leadership Proactive, adaptable, and able to operate independently with strong business judgment in a fast-moving environment Bachelor's degree or higher; business, finance, economics, or related field is a plus An ideal candidate also has: Experience supporting channel, reseller, or partner sales programs at a B2B SaaS company Familiarity with partner relationship management tools or indirect sales systems Experience working closely with deal desk, finance, or partner programs teams on cross-functional process design The range of annual base salary for full-time employees for this position is below.
