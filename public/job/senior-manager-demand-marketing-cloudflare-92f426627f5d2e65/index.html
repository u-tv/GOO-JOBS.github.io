About Us At Cloudflare, we are on a mission to help build a better Internet. Today the company runs one of the world’s largest networks that powers millions of websites and other Internet properties for customers ranging from individual bloggers to SMBs to Fortune 500 companies. Cloudflare protects and accelerates any Internet application online without adding hardware, installing software, or changing a line of code. Internet properties powered by Cloudflare all have web traffic routed through its intelligent global network, which gets smarter with every request. As a result, they see significant improvement in performance and a decrease in spam and other attacks. Cloudflare was named to Entrepreneur Magazine’s Top Company Cultures list and ranked among the World’s Most Innovative Companies by Fast Company. At Cloudflare, we’re not looking for people who wait for a polished roadmap; we’re looking for the builders who see the cracks in the Internet that everyone else has simply learned to live with. We value candidates who have the instinct to spot a &quot;normalized&quot; problem and the AI-native curiosity to create a solution using the latest tools. Our culture is built on iteration, leveraging AI to ship faster today to make it better tomorrow, while ensuring that every improvement, no matter how small, is shared across the team to lift everyone up. If you’re the type of person who values curiosity over bureaucracy, and that AI is a partner in solving tough problems to keep the Internet moving forward, you’ll fit right in. Available Locations: Remote, US About the Role Cloudflare’s Americas Marketing Team is searching for Demand & Field Marketing Leader to partner directly with our US Major Accounts segment. Aligning with sales leadership, sales operations, partner marketing and GTM counterparts you will design and drive high-impact pipeline generation programs, events and account focused marketing leading to pipeline creation and closed-won bookings across Global 2000 accounts. In this role, you will lead Majors Demand Marketing utilizing historical performance, data analytics, and intent intelligence to inform quarterly strategies and drive growth. You will hold end-to-end responsibility for developing and coordinating a comprehensive, omnichannel marketing approach for US Major Accounts. Your scope will encompass hosted field events, Account-Based Marketing (ABM), VIP and executive C-level events while providing strategic inputs for full-funnel digital programs (paid search/social, webinars, web strategy). Key Responsibilities Strategic Planning & Execution: Develop and execute multi-channel marketing plans that align directly with US Major Accounts sales and pipeline targets, holding ownership of budget management, execution, and reporting. Sales & BDR Partnership: Partner closely with enterprise sales leaders, account executives, and Business Development Rep (BDR) leadership to establish shared SLAs, conduct joint pipeline reviews, and enable sellers with high-converting account engagement programs. Full-Funnel Digital Demand Generation: Direct enterprise-focused digital marketing strategy working with our Digital Marketing team—ie. LinkedIn advertising, programmatic display, executive webinar series—to capture, engage, and nurture high-intent enterprise accounts. ABM: 1:1 ABM strategies opportunistically for our strategic accounts segment within Majors. VIP Field Events & Executive Experiences: lead the strategy, budget, vendor management, and execution for high-touch field programs, including C-suite dinners, regional roundtables. Partner & Ecosystem Marketing: Coordinate with the Partner Marketing team to align efforts as required for co-marketing initiatives, and alignment for strategic partner marketing plans. Qualifications & Requirements Minimum 10 years in enterprise B2B field marketing, demand generation, or account-based marketing in a $1B+ technology organization (cybersecurity, cloud, or networking preferred); minimum 5 years field marketing experience. Location Requirement: Remote - NYC, Atlanta, Austin, SF/Bay Area recommended Proven track record supporting sales teams focused on Global 2000 / Fortune 1000 enterprise accounts and driving quantifiable pipeline and conversion. Omnichannel Digital & Campaign Expertise: Experience across full-funnel digital strategy, paid & earned media. Tech Stack & ABM Proficiency: Advanced skills with enterprise CRM (Salesforce), analytics platforms, and ABM/intent intelligence platforms (6sense, Demandbase). Thorough knowledge and understanding of enterprise lead-management processes, revenue attribution models, and marketing SLAs. Demonstrated ability to manage budget with strong financial accountability and ROI optimization. Strong cross-functional collaboration skills with the ability to build relationships and operate effectively across organizational boundaries. BA/BS degree in Marketing, Business, Communications, Engineering, or a related field. Compensation Compensation may be adjusted depending on work location.
