Staff Software Engineer, ASM

🏢 asana 📍 Vancouver, BC 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Asana Service Management (ASM) is an AI-native IT and enterprise service management platform built on the Asana Work Graph. Employees ask for help in Slack, Teams, email or a portal. An AI agent answers what it can, runs end-to-end workflows through Stack AI, and hands the rest to service teams, who work each request as tracked, cross-team work. The share of requests the AI resolves correctly with no human touch is the number our customers, and our business, care about most. We’re looking for a Staff Software Engineer to own the technical quality of AI resolution across ASM. You’ll decide how we measure it, find where it breaks, and work with the teams that own each piece to fix it. This role is based in our Vancouver office with an office-centric hybrid schedule. The standard in-office days are Monday, Tuesday and Thursday. Most Asanas have the option to work from home on Wednesdays. Working from home on Fridays depends on the type of work you do and the teams with which you partner. What you'll achieve Define the metrics and quality bar for AI resolution (resolution rate, retrieval hit rate, response latency, cost per resolution), and build the eval framework that enforces it as a release gate other teams trust. Diagnose why the agent gets answers wrong across retrieval, prompts and knowledge content, and turn failure analysis into a prioritized fix list. Set technical direction for how the agent decides to answer, act or escalate, including its permission model and how it works with Stack AI workflows. Improve latency and reliability of the agent and retrieval pipeline. Work across intake, ticketing and knowledge teams to settle ownership boundaries and interfaces, so a wrong answer has a clear owner and a fast fix. Raise the technical bar for ASM engineering through design reviews, mentoring and candid feedback. Partner with Product, Design, Data Science and User Research to turn what customers experience into engineering priorities. Write and review production code. About you 8+ years of software engineering experience, including 3+ years leading complex technical work across teams. Hands-on experience shipping LLM or agentic features to production, including designing evals and handling failure modes such as hallucination, bad retrieval and unsafe actions. Working knowledge of retrieval and search quality (indexing, ranking, relevance measurement). Experience with latency, reliability and cost trade-offs in AI systems. Strong systems design and pragmatic judgment in ambiguous, fast-changing environments. Clear written communication. You can write a design doc that settles an ownership question. A record of growing other engineers. Curiosity about AI tools and emerging methods. Bonus: ITSM or service desk experience, workflow automation platforms, or enterprise permission and audit models. What we'll offer The estimated base salary range is $211,000-$240,000 CAD. Actual base salary varies by market and individual qualifications assessed during the interview process. Total compensation may include equity and benefits, including mental health, wellness and fitness benefits, career coaching, family-building benefits and long-term savings plans.
