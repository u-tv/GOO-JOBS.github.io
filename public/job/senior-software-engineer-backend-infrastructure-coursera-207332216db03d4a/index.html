Job Overview As a Senior Software Engineer , you will join a fast-paced innovation team at Coursera working on AI enablement for our organization and customers . As an internal focus area, this team builds and maintains the infrastructure & tooling required to drive meaningful AI adoption across the organization, providing a platform for non-technical team members to build and deploy tools and transforming entire business functions into AI-native operating models. As a customer-facing focus area, this team builds custom AI-powered solutions for our enterprise customers supporting them in their journey of AI adoption and upskilling beyond just the content on our platforms. You will have the opportunity to work on complex technical challenges, and influence the future roadmap, growth and success of Coursera's AI culture. You will work closely with a cross functional team of Software Engineers, AI Specialists and Tech Innovation Strategists - leading architecture, owning technical decisions and ensuring end-to-end delivery throughout the SDLC. Key Responsibilities Build, scale and maintain the infrastructure and platform required to deliver production-grade agentic AI solutions Lead architecture and technical design while ensuring high availability, reliability and scalability in our systems Analyze customer requirements, understand enterprise data landscape and ensure data privacy and residency compliance factors in our engineering framework Collaborate closely with Product Managers and Tech Innovation Strategists to scope out problem statements and narrow down requirements with a laser focus on impact delivered to customers and stakeholders Set a high standard of excellence for engineering culture and developer experience in the team by leading code reviews, mentoring junior engineers and building stronger processes Participate in sprint planning, technical grooming, and retrospective discussions Basic Qualifications 5+ years of experience in a software engineering role -
