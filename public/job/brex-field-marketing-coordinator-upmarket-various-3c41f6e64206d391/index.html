Brex: Field Marketing Coordinator, Upmarket

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote Why join us Brex is the intelligent finance platform that enables companies to spend smarter and move faster in more than 200 markets. By combining global corporate cards and banking with intuitive spend management, bill pay, and travel software, Brex enables founders and finance teams to accelerate operations, gain real-time visibility, and control spend effortlessly. Brex’s AI-native automation and world-class service eliminate manual expense and accounting tasks for customers so they can focus on what matters most. Tens of thousands of the world's best companies run on Brex, including DoorDash, Coinbase, Robinhood, Zoom, Plaid, Reddit, and SeatGeek. Working at Brex allows you to push your limits, challenge the status quo, and collaborate with some of the brightest minds in the industry. We’re committed to building a diverse team and inclusive culture and believe your potential should only be limited by how big you can dream. We make this a reality by empowering you with the tools, resources, and support you need to grow your career. Marketing at Brex Marketing tells the story of Brex to the world. From acquisition to activation, we translate product value into business results. Our team spans Revenue, Product, and Brand Marketing, and works closely with nearly every function at Brex. We move fast, experiment often, and think deeply about customer behavior. If you want your creativity to drive growth and shape perception, this is the place. What You'll Do As a Field Marketing Coordinator, Upmarket, you'll play an important role in helping Brex scale regional marketing programs that drive pipeline generation, accelerate opportunities, and strengthen relationships with senior finance, accounting, and procurement leaders across Enterprise and Mid-Market organizations. Reporting directly to the Field Marketing Lead, Upmarket, you'll support a diverse portfolio of revenue-driving initiatives, including executive experiences, regional events, account-based marketing (ABM), and digital campaigns. You'll take ownership of day-to-day program execution and operational coordination, working closely with Sales, Marketing, and cross-functional partners to bring programs to life. Your work will help expand our regional presence, improve program efficiency, and ensure consistent Sales activation before, during, and after each program. The ideal candidate is a proactive, highly organized marketer who thrives in a fast-paced environment, builds strong relationships with Sales, and understands how thoughtful execution drives business growth. You’ll bring attention to detail, creative problem-solving skills, and the ability to balance multiple projects and competing priorities while delivering high-quality work. Where you'll work This role will be fully remote and based in the United States. Responsibilities Own day-to-day planning and execution for Upmarket field marketing programs, including executive experiences, regional dinners, sporting events, and digital activations. Coordinate event logistics, including venue sourcing, vendor management, catering, gifting, shipping, registration, attendee communications, budgets, and onsite execution. Develop and maintain event timelines, registration pages, attendee trackers, run-of-show documents, and internal event briefs. Partner with AEs, SDRs, and Sales leadership to support account targeting, prospect outreach, attendee engagement, and pre- and post-event follow-up. Execute smaller, repeatable regional programs and sales-led experiences that strengthen customer relationships, support multi-threading, and accelerate pipeline. Support integrated digital marketing campaigns across email and paid media, coordinating audience lists, creative assets, landing pages, campaign launches, and performance tracking. Coordinate ABM and direct-mail campaigns, including prospect targeting, vendor management, gifting, fulfillment, and delivery tracking. Maintain the Field Marketing events calendar, campaign documentation, and performance reporting across Salesforce, registration platforms, and marketing automation tools, tracking meetings generated, qualified pipeline, and pipeline influence. Requirements 2+ years of experience in field marketing, event marketing, demand generation, project management or a related role. Strong project management and organizational skills, with the ability to manage multiple projects, deadlines, and stakeholders simultaneously. Strong communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work cross-functionally. Proactive, resourceful, and comfortable taking ownership of day-to-day execution with minimal supervision. Ability to adapt to changing priorities, solve problems quickly, and maintain high-quality execution under tight deadlines. Ability to travel up to 40% of the time to support regional events and onsite execution. What Success Looks Like Seamless program execution: Deliver high-quality field events and integrated campaigns on time, within budget, and with minimal operational friction. Increased program capacity and efficiency: Expand the team's ability to execute more regional and digital programs through repeatable processes, automation, and streamlined workflows. Stronger Sales activation: Improve the consistency and timeliness of prospect outreach, attendee engagement, and post-event follow-up. Measurable business impact: Support programs that generate qualified pipeline, strengthen customer relationships, and accelerate revenue opportunities. Compensation The expected salary range for this role is $73,600 - $105,800. However, the starting base pay will depend on a number of factors including the candidate’s location, skills, experience, market demands, and internal pay parity. Depending on the position offered, equity and other forms of compensation may be provided as part of a total compensation package.
