Principal Campaign Marketing Manager, Integrated Campaigns

🏢 databricks 📍 United States 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

MKTQ128R11 As a Principal Campaign Marketing Manager, you will lead integrated campaigns for Databricks' Data and AI products, from strategy through measurable pipeline results. You will set campaign strategy with product marketing leaders up to SVP and regional field marketing leaders up to VP across AMER, EMEA, and APJ. You will influence these leaders to reach trade-offs and align on priorities that align with the campaign objectives, ensuring the integrated campaign tactics map to the customer journey. You will own a portfolio of one to two integrated global campaigns and help build the next generation of personalized, AI-enabled customer journeys. The impact you will have: Own the strategy and results for full-funnel, global integrated campaigns, accountable to marketing-wide OKRs and global demand gen targets (net name acquisition, funnel stage lead metrics, conversion rates, pipeline, etc.). Deliver against campaign-level pipeline targets, and work with leadership to turn top-level targets into program plans using connected journey architecture to build full-funnel customer paths defined by journey stage and next best action. Run integrated campaigns across PMM, field marketing, digital, web, MOPs, and creative. Drive alignment, prioritization trade-offs and de-conflicting with very little oversight. Recommend program investments based on past performance. Map how personas engage with programs, channels, and offers so the team can invest where it gets the best return. Try new tactics, channels, creative, and AI-enabled workflows. Drive an optimization flywheel through continuous in-flight experimentation, structured test learnings, and reporting flywheels to significantly improve and scale campaign performance. Build reusable best practices, processes, and documentation for managing the campaign portfolio across the broader team. Step in to cover adjacent campaign portfolios when the team needs it, so strategic work continues, and execution stays on track. What we look for: 12+ years of B2B demand generation or integrated campaign marketing experience, including ownership of multiple large, multi-channel campaigns; or 8+ years of relevant experience when paired with an advanced degree. A proven record of influencing Director-to-SVP stakeholders without direct authority, and of driving trade-off decisions across functions and regions. Experience leading global or multi-region campaigns, in partnership with product marketing and field marketing teams. Experience launching products quickly in a high-growth business, re-planning scope and timelines while protecting results. Strong analytical skills. You turn campaign and funnel data into insights, optimization decisions, and investment recommendations tied to pipeline. Hands-on experience using AI or automation to change how campaigns are planned, personalized, and executed. Executive-level written and verbal communication.
