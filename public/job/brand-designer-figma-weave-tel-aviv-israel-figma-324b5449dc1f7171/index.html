Brand Designer - Figma Weave (Tel Aviv, Israel)

🏢 figma 📍 Tel Aviv, Israel 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Figma is growing our team of passionate creatives and builders on a mission to make design accessible to all. Figma’s platform helps teams bring ideas to life—whether you're brainstorming, creating a prototype, translating designs into code, or iterating with AI. From idea to product, Figma empowers teams to streamline workflows, move faster, and work together in real time from anywhere in the world. If you're excited to shape the future of design and collaboration, join us! We're building the next chapter of AI-native craft and creativity - where craft and technology meet. Our unified platform bridges the gap between AI capabilities and human creativity, and lets professional creatives and designers harness the power of the world’s leading AI models without losing precision, expression, or soul. Together, we’re building tools that help people move faster without sacrificing quality, precision, or craft — a platform where AI supports creativity instead of flattening it. We’re looking for a Brand Designer to help shape how Weave looks and communicates across marketing. You’ll generate images and videos, design marketing assets, and develop creative ideas with the studio team for brand films, feature announcements, new model launches, and campaigns. This is a hands-on role with room for both ideas and execution. You’ll need strong design foundations, excellent visual judgment, and deep practical experience with AI image and video generation. You should be comfortable taking a project through full production yourself, as well as creating assets for others to build on. The work spans presentations, email, ads, social, and web. Some briefs will need a new visual world; others will need a clear presentation or a well-designed email. You’ll bring care and judgment to the full range, with marketing images and videos as your first priority. You’ll report to the Brand Manager and work closely with the studio team. This is a hybrid role based in Tel Aviv, with at least two days per week in the office. What you’ll do at Figma Weave: Create and refine images and videos for Weave marketing, combining AI generation with hands-on design, editing, and finishing Own image and video projects from concept and visual exploration through final production and delivery Create assets that other studio members and collaborators can use in their work Design presentations, email, ads, social assets, campaigns, and web pages Brainstorm and develop creative ideas with the team for brand videos, feature announcements, new model launches, and other marketing moments Help evolve Weave’s visual language and apply it thoughtfully across formats Build useful templates, organize shared assets, and share production techniques with the studio Manage projects from brief to delivery, working independently and incorporating feedback along the way We’d love to hear from you if you have: A portfolio showing strong brand and marketing design, with clear examples of your personal contribution, and excellent typography, composition, layout, and image-making skills Very strong practical experience with AI image and video generation, demonstrated through finished work The ability to handle full image and video production yourself, including editing and finishing, and experience designing across presentations, email, ads, social, and web The ability to contribute creative ideas, explain your decisions, and develop work with a team Proficiency in the design, image, and video tools you use to deliver strong work, and the judgment and initiative to take projects from an open brief to a finished result While it's not required, it’s an added plus if you also have: Experience with Figma Weave or other workflow-based creative tools Advanced motion graphics, animation, or 3D skills Experience building brand systems, templates, and shared asset libraries Experience working with creative tools or software brands At Figma, one of our values is Grow as you go.
