Unexploded Ordnance Tech II

🏢 Alaka`ina Foundation Family of Companies 📍 South Andros, 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Bering-Alaka`ina Holdings, LLC is looking for an Unexploded Ordnance Tech II to support our government customer located at Andros Island in the Bahamas. Seeking an Unexploded Ordnance Tech II to support the Atlantic Undersea Test and Evaluation Center (AUTEC) Mission in Andros Island, Bahamas. AUTEC is a Major Range and Test Facility Base (MRTFB) and the Naval Undersea Warfare Center, Division Newport's largest single team project, providing both deep and shallow water test and training environments. The Unexploded Ordnance Tech II is responsible for performing handling, preparation, post run and transport of exercise torpedoes and associated equipment in accordance with NAVAIR/NAVSEA/NAVFAC guidelines on the AUTEC project. Must be able to obtain and maintain a Secret US Government Clearance. Note: US Citizenship is required to obtain a Secret Clearance. DESCRIPTION OF RESPONSIBILITIES: Perform mechanical and electrical maintenance and repair on exercise torpedoes (MK-46/MK-50 REXTORP's, MK-48, MOD 4, 5, 6, 7 and MK-46, 54 EXTORP's) and associated equipment in accordance with NAVAIR/NAVSEA guidelines. Operate tractor-trailer, forklift, and overhead cranes per NAVFAC guidelines. Conduct rigging operations of heavy equipment per NAVFAC standards. Handle, package, transport hazardous ordnance material up to 1.1 per NAVORD standards. Operate torpedo data extraction computer per NAVSEA standards. Operate and maintain the breathing air system in support of the torpedo operations. Operate and maintain breathing air system. Performs other duties as assigned.
