eLEND: Mortgage Servicing Analyst

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: URL: http://www.elendtpo.com 🏡 About Us At eLEND, we are committed to delivering an exceptional mortgage experience through innovation, technology, operational excellence, and client-focused partnership. Our Mortgage Servicing team plays a critical role in maintaining accurate loan records, supporting borrowers, managing servicing activities, and ensuring operational consistency throughout the servicing lifecycle. The Mortgage Servicing Analyst is responsible for executing daily servicing activities, including loan boarding, servicing data maintenance, reconciliations, borrower inquiries, servicing transfers, operational reporting, and exception resolution. This position works closely with Servicing leadership, Finance, Operations, internal departments, and third-party servicing partners to maintain accurate portfolio information, support regulatory compliance, and ensure servicing activities are completed efficiently and within established procedures. ✨ Why This Role Matters As a Mortgage Servicing Analyst, you will help maintain the accuracy, integrity, and operational efficiency of eLEND's mortgage servicing portfolio. While the AVP establishes priorities and monitors overall servicing performance, you will execute the core activities that keep our servicing operations running smoothly. From boarding new loans and maintaining servicing platform data to researching borrower inquiries, reconciling accounts, and supporting servicing transfers, your work directly contributes to accurate records, timely borrower support, and operational consistency. Success means maintaining reliable servicing data, resolving routine operational issues, meeting established deadlines, and supporting a compliant and efficient servicing environment. 🛠️ Key Responsibilities 🏡 New Loan Boarding & Data Validation Execute new loan boarding activities within the servicing platform and interim servicing environment. Validate loan boarding information against origination records to ensure accuracy and completeness. Identify, research, and resolve loan boarding discrepancies and exceptions. Maintain accurate loan-level information throughout the boarding process. Coordinate with internal departments and servicing partners to address outstanding boarding requirements. Escalate complex boarding issues or recurring discrepancies to senior servicing staff. 💻 Servicing Platform & Data Management Maintain accurate servicing information within the Origination Platform, servicing systems, and interim servicing data feeds. Review and update loan-level data to support accurate servicing records and portfolio reporting. Monitor data consistency across origination and servicing environments. Research discrepancies between servicing platforms and supporting documentation. Complete assigned data maintenance activities within established procedures. Support accurate portfolio analysis through timely and reliable servicing information. 📊 Accounting Reconciliations & Financial Accuracy Research and clear routine corporate accounting reconciliation items related to mortgage servicing. Review servicing invoices, escrow accounting records, and purchase advice discrepancies. Identify and document reconciliation differences and supporting findings. Coordinate with Finance and servicing partners to resolve routine discrepancies. Escalate complex, unresolved, or aged reconciliation items to senior servicing staff. Maintain organized documentation supporting reconciliation activities and resolutions. 🔄 Servicing Transfers & Portfolio Transitions Support the preparation and execution of mortgage servicing transfers. Prepare loan-level data files, borrower notices, transfer checklists, and supporting documentation. Validate servicing transfer information for completeness and accuracy. Complete assigned transfer responsibilities within established timelines. Coordinate with internal departments and servicing partners to address transfer-related requirements. Research and resolve routine servicing transfer exceptions. Maintain accurate records supporting transfer activities and operational readiness. 📈 Servicing Reporting & Portfolio Analytics Generate recurring mortgage servicing reports according to established schedules. Validate servicing data used in operational reports and portfolio analysis. Gather source information supporting the monthly Servicing Portfolio Report. Support the preparation of servicing key performance indicators (KPIs) and subservicer scorecards. Research reporting discrepancies and coordinate corrections when necessary. Maintain accurate supporting documentation for recurring reporting activities. Provide reliable servicing data to support leadership visibility into portfolio operations. 🔍 Subservicer Monitoring & Operational Oversight Monitor daily subservicer and interim servicing activities for operational exceptions. Review servicing activity for potential service-level concerns, discrepancies, and processing issues. Investigate routine exceptions and document findings. Track outstanding servicing issues and coordinate follow-up as appropriate. Escalate recurring concerns, operational risks, or complex servicing matters requiring additional review. Support consistent execution of servicing activities across internal and external servicing partners. 🏗️ Construction-to-Permanent Loan Monitoring Monitor construction-to-permanent loan servicing activity. Review loan-level information and complete required servicing updates. Identify discrepancies, outstanding requirements, or items requiring corrective action. Coordinate with appropriate departments or servicing partners to address routine issues. Maintain accurate documentation of servicing updates and monitoring activities. Escalate unresolved or complex matters to senior servicing staff. 💰 Interim Servicing & Transaction Processing Process routine interim servicing transactions within documented procedures. Research mortgage payment activity and related servicing discrepancies. Support escrow administration and account maintenance activities. Assist with payoff processing and related servicing updates. Prepare and process borrower correspondence associated with servicing transactions. Maintain accurate transaction records and supporting documentation. Identify and escalate processing exceptions requiring additional review. 🤝 Borrower Inquiries & Customer Support Research and respond to borrower inquiries involving mortgage payments, escrow accounts, payoff requests, servicing transfers, and other servicing activities. Provide accurate, timely, and professional information to borrowers. Coordinate with internal departments and servicing partners to research and resolve inquiries. Identify and document borrower complaints according to established procedures. Track outstanding borrower concerns and ensure appropriate follow-up. Escalate complex inquiries, unresolved issues, or potential compliance concerns. Support a consistent and positive borrower experience through clear communication and accurate servicing information. 🚨 Servicing Exception Management & Issue Resolution Identify and investigate routine mortgage servicing exceptions. Research discrepancies to determine root causes and appropriate corrective actions. Coordinate with internal stakeholders and servicing partners to resolve operational issues. Maintain accurate records of exceptions, findings, corrective actions, and resolutions. Monitor recurring exceptions and communicate trends to senior servicing staff. Escalate complex issues, operational risks, or matters outside established procedures. Support timely resolution while maintaining servicing accuracy and compliance. ⚖️ Audit, Investor & Regulatory Support Gather and organize servicing documentation requested for audits, examinations, investor reviews, and regulatory inquiries. Retrieve and validate loan-level servicing data and supporting records. Review documentation for accuracy, completeness, and consistency. Coordinate with internal departments to obtain requested servicing information. Maintain organized records supporting audit and examination readiness. Escalate discrepancies or potential compliance concerns identified during documentation reviews. 📚 Servicing Policies, Procedures & Documentation Execute servicing responsibilities in accordance with documented procedures and internal controls. Maintain accurate records of servicing activities, exceptions, and resolutions in designated systems. Identify procedures that may no longer align with current operational practices. Recommend updates that improve accuracy, consistency, and efficiency. Support the maintenance of organized and accessible servicing documentation. Follow established escalation protocols when servicing activities require additional guidance. Demonstrate accountability for completing assigned work accurately and on schedule. ⚙️ Process Improvement & Operational Efficiency Identify opportunities to improve servicing workflows and reduce recurring operational errors. Recommend improvements to data accuracy, reconciliation processes, reporting, and exception management. Support servicing process improvement initiatives and operational readiness activities. Contribute to servicing system conversions and other assigned projects. Assist with evaluating recurring servicing issues and potential process enhancements. Support consistent execution of servicing activities across platforms and servicing partners. Promote efficiency while maintaining established controls and servicing standards. 🤝 Cross-Functional Partnership Collaborate with Servicing leadership, Finance, Operations, and other internal departments. Coordinate with subservicers and interim servicing partners to address operational needs. Communicate servicing discrepancies, outstanding requirements, and resolution updates clearly. Support Finance with reconciliation research and supporting documentation. Partner with internal stakeholders to resolve borrower inquiries and servicing exceptions. Contribute to servicing transfers, reporting activities, audits, and operational projects. Maintain professional, responsive, and solutions-focused working relationships. 🔄 Cross-Training & Knowledge Sharing Participate in cross-training activities to strengthen knowledge of mortgage servicing operations. Share process knowledge, servicing findings, and operational best practices with team members. Support operational continuity through accurate documentation and collaboration. Remain informed about changes to servicing systems, workflows, and internal procedures. Assist colleagues with servicing activities as business priorities require. Perform additional duties aligned with business needs, competencies, and experience. 🌈 Team Culture & Accountability Demonstrate ownership of assigned servicing responsibilities and operational deadlines. Maintain high standards of accuracy, professionalism, and attention to detail. Communicate servicing issues proactively and follow established escalation procedures. Support collaboration, knowledge sharing, and continuous improvement. Remain adaptable as servicing priorities, portfolio needs, and business processes evolve. Demonstrate eLEND's core values through daily actions and interactions. 🎯 What You Bring 🏠 Mortgage Servicing & Operations Experience Must-Haves Minimum of two (2) years of experience in residential mortgage servicing or mortgage operations. Working knowledge of mortgage servicing processes and basic servicing regulations. Experience researching and resolving borrower inquiries, operational issues, or servicing exceptions. Understanding of routine mortgage servicing activities and operational workflows. Ability to execute assigned servicing responsibilities accurately and within established deadlines. 💻 Mortgage Systems & Data Management Proficiency with mortgage-related software, including loan origination systems (LOS) or mortgage servicing platforms. Strong organizational skills and ability to manage multiple servicing responsibilities. Ability to prioritize assigned work and maintain accurate records. Comfort working independently within documented procedures and controls. Consistent follow-through on outstanding servicing activities and operational issues. 🤝 Communication & Collaboration Strong written and verbal communication skills. Ability to communicate servicing information clearly to borrowers and internal stakeholders. Experience coordinating with internal departments or external servicing partners. Professional and solutions-focused approach to borrower inquiries and issue resolution. Ability to collaborate effectively while maintaining accountability for assigned responsibilities. 🔄 Adaptability & Continuous Learning Ability to adapt to changing servicing priorities, systems, and operational needs. Willingness to learn new servicing processes and expand mortgage operations knowledge. Comfortable participating in servicing initiatives, system conversions, and process improvement efforts. Ability to identify opportunities to improve accuracy and operational efficiency. Commitment to continuous learning and professional development. 🌈 Values & Professional Standards Ability to demonstrate eLEND's core values through daily actions and decisions. Strong sense of ownership, accountability, and integrity. Commitment to accuracy, compliance, borrower service, and teamwork. Professional judgment when handling borrower information and servicing records. Ability to contribute positively to a collaborative and service-focused environment. ⭐ Nice-to-Haves Associate or bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, Business, or a related field. Experience with new loan boarding, servicing transfers, or interim servicing activities. Experience supporting reconciliations, accounting activities, or operational reporting. Prior mortgage call center or borrower-facing servicing experience. 🪑 Physical & Work Environment Requirements Ability to perform desk-based responsibilities involving prolonged sitting and intermittent standing and walking.
