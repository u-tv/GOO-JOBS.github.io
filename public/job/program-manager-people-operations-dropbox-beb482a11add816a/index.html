🏢 dropbox 📍 Remote - US: All locations 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Role Description Dropbox is looking for a Program Manager within our People Operations, Rewards, and Technology team (PORT). You will join a talented team to deliver operational services, optimize people processes and ensure that Dropboxers have amazing experience throughout their career. Our best in class PORT team drives strategic HR program initiatives and delivers an excellent employee experience at a fast-growing global capacity. You will thrive working in a fast-paced and customer -focused environment to make an immediate impact and ensure best practices for compliance, effectiveness and efficiency. Responsibilities Oversee the day-to-day operations of the global Contingent Workforce program. Serve as a primary point of contact for CW processes, escalations, and program-related questions from MSP. Partner with the MSP, suppliers, hiring managers, HR, Legal, Procurement, and other stakeholders to ensure consistent program execution. Monitor program processes, identify gaps and opportunities, and implement improvements that increase efficiency and reduce risk. Maintain and continuously improve CW policies, processes, documentation, and governance. Support reporting, compliance, and program performance tracking. Support ServiceNow enhancements and updates. You’ll work with Dropbox vendors to troubleshoot and resolve day to day issues/inquiries. You’ll develop and ensure standard HR operating procedures are in place with clear SLAs You’ll collaborate with partners in HR, Recruiting, and Finance to conduct root cause analysis and develop corrective action plans when issues arise, ensuring the highest quality employee experience You’ll work closely with the HR Project Managers to support project execution and continuous process improvement and onboarding and Contingent workforce operations. You’ll maintain documentation for each part of the service, and drive communication outwards to stakeholders when necessary. You’ll identify and make recommendations on program/process changes and conduct cost/benefit analysis of potential operational improvements. You’ll support ad-hoc HR Ops tasks and reporting. Work cross collaboratively across all regions with the respective onboarding team. You’ll create SOPs and Job Aids when necessary for documentation and to streamline processes. Requirements 5 + years of experience in HR Operations/HR administration in a fast-paced environment Problem solving, critical thinking and troubleshooting abilities Experience in Workday and HR Operations service request management Strong customer-service skills with proven track record of customer satisfaction Proficiency in Office Suite (Word, Excel, Power Point), Gmail and Google Docs BA/BS degree or equivalent practical experience Experience with ServiceNow Durable Skills AI fluency means using these tools to amplify human judgment, not replace it.
