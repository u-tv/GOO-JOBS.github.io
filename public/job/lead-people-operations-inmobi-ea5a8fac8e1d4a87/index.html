People Operations Specialist – Compliance & Governance About the Role We are seeking a detail-oriented and proactive People Operations Specialist – Compliance & Governance to support efficient, compliant, and employee-centric People Operations in a fast-paced global technology environment. This role will play a key part in strengthening People Operations governance, supporting IPO readiness initiatives, ensuring audit preparedness, and driving operational excellence across the employee lifecycle. The individual will partner closely with People, Payroll, Finance, Legal, and Internal Audit teams to maintain strong controls, enhance compliance, and deliver a seamless employee experience. What will YOU be doing? Compliance, Governance & Controls Support compliance and governance initiatives across People processes, ensuring alignment with internal policies, regulatory requirements, and operational controls. Monitor adherence to policies, SOPs, and established governance frameworks while identifying opportunities for process improvement. Assist in implementing and maintaining effective controls to support a scalable and compliant People Operations function. Audit Readiness & IPO Support Prepare, maintain, and organize audit-ready documentation and supporting records. Coordinate and support internal and external audits, ensuring timely responses to requests and closure of remediation actions. Contribute to IPO readiness activities by supporting People-related controls, documentation, and governance requirements. Partner with cross-functional stakeholders to strengthen audit preparedness and compliance practices. Data Integrity & Reporting Ensure the accuracy, completeness, and integrity of employee data across HR systems. Support reporting requirements, compliance metrics, and governance-related analyses. Monitor and drive timely resolution of data discrepancies, compliance findings, and process gaps. Stakeholder Collaboration & Continuous Improvement Collaborate with People Business Partners, Payroll, Finance, Legal, and Internal Audit teams to standardize processes and improve operational efficiency. Identify opportunities to streamline workflows, improve service delivery, and enhance employee experience through process optimization. Support projects and initiatives focused on operational excellence, governance, and scalability. What do we expect from YOU? Required Skills & Competencies People Operations Employee Lifecycle Management Compliance & Audit Coordination Governance & Internal Controls Documentation Management Data Accuracy & Reporting Process Improvement & Operational Excellence Stakeholder Management Problem Solving & Attention to Detail Strong Communication & Collaboration Skills Preferred Experience 5–8 years of experience in People Operations, HR Shared Services, HR Compliance, or related functions. Experience supporting compliance, governance, audit, internal controls, or operational excellence initiatives. Exposure to IPO readiness, SOX controls, or public company compliance environments is highly desirable. Experience working in a global and fast-paced technology organization preferred. Demonstrated ability to work cross-functionally with HR, Payroll, Finance, Legal, and Audit teams. Systems Exposure SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central ADP Freshdesk or similar case management/ticketing platforms Strong proficiency in Microsoft Excel and reporting tools Qualifications Bachelor's degree required. HR, Compliance, Audit, or related certifications are an advantage. Success in This Role The ideal candidate is highly organized, process-oriented, and passionate about building scalable People Operations practices. They bring a strong governance mindset, attention to detail, and the ability to balance compliance requirements with an exceptional employee experience.
