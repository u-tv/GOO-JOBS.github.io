Field CTO, Public Sector

🏢 gitlab 📍 Remote, United States 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

GitLab is the intelligent orchestration platform for DevSecOps. GitLab enables organizations to increase developer productivity, improve operational efficiency, reduce security and compliance risk, and accelerate digital transformation. More than 50 million registered users and more than 50% of the Fortune 100* trust GitLab to ship better, more secure software faster. The same principles built into our products are reflected in how our team works: we embrace AI as a core productivity multiplier, with all team members expected to incorporate AI into their daily workflows to drive efficiency, innovation, and impact. GitLab is where careers accelerate, innovation flourishes, and every voice is valued. Our high-performance culture is driven by our values and continuous knowledge exchange, enabling our team members to reach their full potential while collaborating with industry leaders to solve complex problems. Co-create the future with us as we build technology that transforms how the world develops software. * Fortune 500® is a registered trademark of Fortune Media IP Limited, used under license. An overview of this role As a Field CTO, Public Sector at GitLab, you will serve as a trusted technical advisor for public sector customers and prospects, helping them connect DevSecOps strategy, security, and compliance goals to real business and mission outcomes. You will work closely with executive stakeholders during the pre-sales process, offering strategic guidance on how GitLab can support secure software delivery, modernization efforts, and platform adoption across complex environments. In this role, you will combine technical depth with executive communication. You will build relationships with leaders such as Chief Information Security Officers, Chief Security Officers, Chief Technology Officers, and chief security architects, while also partnering across GitLab with sales, marketing, and product teams. Your perspective from the field will help shape customer conversations, influence go-to-market strategy, and inform how GitLab continues to serve public sector organizations. Note: This is an individual contributor, customer-facing technical advisory role; it does not include direct people management responsibilities. This role is well suited for someone who enjoys operating at the intersection of technology strategy , customer engagement , and market leadership . You will help customers understand the value of a single DevSecOps platform, contribute to industry conversations around standards and guidance, and represent GitLab in a way that reflects our values of transparency, collaboration, and results. Some examples of our projects: Supporting strategic public sector opportunities with executive-level DevSecOps and security guidance Contributing to government working groups, standards discussions, and public sector thought leadership content What you'll do Engage with customers in a technical consulting and advisory role during the pre-sales process, providing technical assistance and strategic guidance. Deliver executive-level thought leadership on DevSecOps , security, and compliance for strategic public sector opportunities, including new and growth accounts. Build and maintain relationships with customer leaders such as Chief Information Security Officers, Chief Security Officers, Chief Technology Officers, and chief security architects. Partner with the marketing team to develop content and support media engagement opportunities that increase awareness of GitLab in the public sector market. Educate customers on the value of the GitLab platform and related technologies, and support conversations across multiple levels of the organization to help enable successful deployment. Assist with requests for information and responses related to government publications and public-private partnership opportunities. Participate in and contribute to working groups connected to government publications, standards, and demonstrations. Curate and communicate public sector field feedback, collaborate on prioritization with marketing and product teams, and contribute to sales strategy, playbooks, enablement, and reusable technical content for the field. What you'll bring Demonstrated expertise in DevSecOps strategy, application security, and compliance for regulated environments, including providing risk-based guidance. Experience selling to, supporting, or working within the public sector . Experience creating and delivering technical thought leadership content (presentations, demos, written materials) independently. Working knowledge of the end-to-end software development lifecycle, including CI/CD and modern engineering and operations practices. Experience with cloud computing and modern platform architectures that support secure software delivery. Strong verbal, written, and presentation skills, with the ability to communicate technical strategy effectively to audiences ranging from executives to engineers. Comfort working in a fully remote, distributed, and asynchronous environment. Ability to coordinate across time zones and adapt schedules to customer and internal stakeholder needs. Experience in technical pre-sales, consulting, solutions architecture, or comparable customer-facing IT roles, including using GitLab (and willingness to learn quickly) to support customer conversations and demonstrations. Living in the Washington D.C. Metro area preferred Employment is contingent upon successful completion of a background check, as permitted by applicable law. About the team The Field CTO, Public Sector role works across customer-facing and internal teams to help GitLab meet the needs of public sector organizations with clarity, credibility, and deep technical insight. This role partners closely with sales, solutions, marketing, and product stakeholders to represent customer priorities, strengthen technical strategy in the field, and support GitLab's position in a highly regulated and mission-driven market. The team environment is collaborative, remote, and asynchronous, with a strong focus on sharing knowledge and turning field insight into action. The work is especially meaningful because it brings together executive advisory skills, platform expertise, and an understanding of security and compliance expectations that matter in public sector environments. Success in this role comes from being able to translate complex technical topics into clear guidance, while also helping GitLab improve how we serve this market over time. At GitLab, you'll do this work in a values-driven culture built on collaboration , results , efficiency , diversity, inclusion and belonging , iteration , and transparency .
