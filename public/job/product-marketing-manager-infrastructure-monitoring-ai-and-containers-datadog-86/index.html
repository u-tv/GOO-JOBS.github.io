Product Marketing Manager - Infrastructure Monitoring (AI and Containers)

🏢 datadog 📍 New York, New York, USA 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Datadog Infrastructure Monitoring helps teams understand the health and performance of the infrastructure powering modern applications, including AI - from hosts and Kubernetes clusters to GPUs and other compute resources. AI applications are increasingly built and deployed in containers, making visibility into container health, GPU utilization, and underlying compute critical to reliable AI performance. As the Product Marketing Manager - Infrastructure Monitoring for AI and Containerized Workloads, you’ll own the go-to-market strategy for this portfolio. You’ll partner closely with Product Management, Sales, Customer Success, and Enablement to shape positioning, support product launches, increase adoption, and translate complex infrastructure capabilities into clear customer value. You will incorporate AI-enabled tools into your day-to-day work to improve productivity and execution. You’ll also help position Kubernetes and container monitoring capabilities within Datadog's Bits agentic AI offerings, showing how agentic workflows can help teams detect, investigate, and remediate infrastructure issues. At Datadog, we place value in our office culture - the relationships and collaboration it builds and the creativity it brings to the table. We operate as a hybrid workplace to ensure our Datadogs can create a work-life harmony that best fits them. What You’ll Do: Own the end-to-end go-to-market strategy for Infrastructure Monitoring for AI and containerized workloads, including positioning, launches, adoption, and field readiness. Develop clear, differentiated messaging that communicates the value of unified visibility across hosts, containers, Kubernetes clusters, GPUs, and the infrastructure supporting AI workloads. Position Kubernetes and containers within Datadog’s Bits agentic AI offerings, translating workflows for detecting, investigating, and remediating infrastructure issues into clear customer value. Create engaging content to support product and feature launches, including pitch decks, ebooks, case studies, blogs, webinars, short-form demo videos, and customer testimonials. Own the messaging and positioning used across digital marketing campaigns and industry events, including DASH, Datadog’s user conference, Kubecon, re:Invent, and more Partner closely with Product Management, Sales, Customer Success, and Enablement to translate product capabilities and customer insights into compelling narratives while enabling field teams to win new customers, increase product adoption, and identify expansion opportunities. Who You Are: 2–3 years of Product Marketing experience, specifically supporting enterprise B2B SaaS products. Experience developing product positioning, messaging, launch content, collateral, and sales enablement assets. Prior experience with infrastructure monitoring, AI infrastructure, containers, serverless technologies, Kubernetes, or related developer technologies is preferred. Able to communicate effectively with technical audiences while translating complex capabilities into clear customer value. A strong storyteller who can develop compelling narratives across a range of formats and channels. Passionate about SaaS, cloud computing, DevOps, AI infrastructure, and product-led growth. Datadog values people from all walks of life. We understand not everyone will meet all the above qualifications on day one. That's okay.
