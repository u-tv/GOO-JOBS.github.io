Headquarters: UK - Remote About Ping Identity: At Ping Identity, we believe in making digital experiences both secure and seamless for all users, without compromise. We call this digital freedom. As a Professional Services Engagement Manager, you will report to the Director of Professional Services and will play an essential role in Professional Services' commitment to deliver successfully on engagements striving for predictability, repeatability and financial responsibility. We are looking for someone who enjoys leading engagements for external clients and one who can manage a portfolio of complex projects. This is a hybrid role that will require 50% of your time to be spent in hands-on project management and 50% of the time to be spent in services sales. You will have several years of experience in project management best practices, consulting, and leading technology implementation projects. You will join a highly effective team with a focus towards team work, successful deployments and outstanding cooperation with our go-to-market teams. Our goal is not just to make our customers successful, but to take your project management skillset to the next level. You will: Be the primary point of contact overseeing engagements delivered by Ping Professional Services. Build relationships with customers, partners, and our go-to-market teams Manage a portfolio of projects for successful delivery outcomes, revenue and profitability. Manage outcomes to resolve issues, conflicts, risks, and dependencies Flourish in a collaborative environment, demonstrating teamwork and excellence Provide written/verbal status updates to customer, partners and leadership on key project metrics Support revenue forecasting, invoicing and resourcing efforts for the Professional Services team Be an active member of project governance setup Support new services sales effort by attaching professional services offerings to new deals. Establish, monitor and manage own services sales pipeline Participate in regional license and services sales forecasting Define and present business value of services deals Scope complex custom services engagements together with technical consultants Understand and apply meaningful commercial incentives for services deals Requirements: You have: 5 years of project management experience in consulting and technology implementation Motivated and comfortable adapting to change and competing demands with timelines Bachelor's Degree Exceptional skills around communications and delivery Can independently deliver results Commercial mindset Desired Qualifications German, French, Spanish or Italian language skills.
