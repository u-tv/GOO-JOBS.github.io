Senior Engineering Manager - Brand

🏢 faire 📍 Kitchener-Waterloo, ON 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Faire Faire is a technology wholesale platform built on the belief that the future is local. Independent retailers around the globe collectively represent a multi-hundred-billion-dollar wholesale market that has historically been fragmented and offline. At Faire, we're using the power of tech, data, and machine learning to connect this thriving community of entrepreneurs across the globe. Picture your favorite boutique in town — we help them discover the best products from around the world to sell in their stores. With the right tools and insights, we believe that we can level the playing field so businesses can grow and local communities can thrive. We’re looking for smart, resourceful and passionate people to join us as we power the shop local movement. If you believe in community, come join ours. Help shape the experience retailers have with every brand on Faire: Faire is building the operating system for local retail. Every day, hundreds of thousands of independent retailers and brands rely on Faire to discover products, grow their businesses, and compete with large marketplaces. When a retailer orders from a brand on Faire, they’re trusting that the brand will price fairly, ship on time, and deliver a good experience. Keeping that promise is a big part of what makes the marketplace work. We’re hiring a Senior Engineering Manager to lead two pods in our Brand pillar, about 10 engineers in total. One is Marketplace Quality, which owns the programs that set expectations for brands on Faire: consistent pricing, reliable shipping, and avoiding bad retailer experiences. Brands that meet the bar earn recognition like the Top Shop badge, which helps them get discovered and grow their sales. Depending on your background, your second pod will be either Brand Platform, which builds the internal platform other Faire engineering teams depend on, or our AI assistant pod. This is not just a people management role. You’ll be a close partner to product, making product and business decisions alongside your product managers and owning those decisions when they’re not in the room. You’ll stay close enough to the architecture and code to lead your teams well. You’re excited about this role because… You want to own a business problem that directly shapes how retailers and brands experience Faire You like making product and business calls, not just deciding
