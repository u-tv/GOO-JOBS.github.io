Collibra: Senior Customer Engineer - Collibra Public Sector

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote, USA J oining Collibra’s Public Sector team The Federal Customer Engineer (CE) serves as the guiding force behind bringing Collibra Public Sector's (CPS) product and vision to customers and prospects. Senior Customer Engineers are responsible for both net-new and expansion opportunities in existing accounts. They establish knowledgeable and trusted relationships from day one and serve as thoughtful technical advisors. The CE maintains a technical relationship with customers throughout the entire customer journey, both pre- and post-sale. You will fill an essential and highly respected role in the sales organization at CPS. As a Federal Customer Engineer at Collibra Public Sector, you will support our nation’s most critical agencies in achieving Data Sovereignty and Mission Intelligence . You will partner with Federal Account Executives to navigate the unique procurement and technical landscapes of the DoW, Civilian and the Intelligence Community agencies. Your goal is to show how Collibra’s Self-Hosted and FedRAMP-authorized platforms can break down agency silos, ensure regulatory compliance, and accelerate the secure adoption of AI. Senior Customer Engineers at Collibra are responsible for Presales Lead in-depth technical discovery sessions to uncover complex needs and environments and recommend solutions Owns delivery of technical evaluation efforts with input from specialists for (Data Quality, Privacy, AI Governance and the core Collibra Platform). Works closely with the Account Executive to craft win-win sales proposals and influence decisions. Participates in discovery and drives qualification of new opportunities, and expands Collibra solutions with existing customers. Prepares and owns functional and technical sections of RFPs/RFQs. Demonstration/Presentation delivery Deliver focussed demonstration, creating sample/custom assets and configure workflows as required to meet client needs. Deliver focused presentations and roadmaps to key stakeholders across both business and technology Deliver complex tailored demonstrations and discuss in detail customization capabilities for specific use cases. Present tailored product roadmap presentation based on customer's priorities and current/future use cases Deliver complex multi-disciplinary workshops from start to finish involving internal and external stakeholders. Lead and own workshops, be the key driver for the agenda, and ensure outcomes result in positive outcomes for client and Collibra. Lead product and technical sections of regular business reviews with key customers Prepare and conduct product demonstrations, highlighting how Collibra’s Platform can support our Federal Government customer requirements. Identifying, evaluating, recommending, and executing value-based workshops and proof of concepts/values. Deliver tailored demonstrations that address specific Federal mandates (e.g., OMB M-24-10 regarding AI Governance, CDO Council priorities, and Executive Orders on Data Sharing). Lead technical Proof of Value (PoV) engagements within agency environments. Ensure successful deployment of Collibra Edge behind agency firewalls. Provide thought leadership on best practices for adoption and technical optimizations. Influences product and service direction to enhance overall client experience. Advising and supporting customers’ technical stakeholders to accelerate Collibra adoption and value realization, i.e., be the primary point of contact for technical and product-related queries. Help agencies build a "Unified Data Language" to facilitate secure data sharing between departments while maintaining strict access controls and data labeling. Customer Support Address technical questions, mitigate risks, and overcome objections related to security, scalability, integration and implementation. You have 5+ years in Sales Engineering or Technical Consulting, specifically supporting U.S. Federal Government customers with 4+ years working in the Data/SaaS space. Proficiency in SQL and deep familiarity with Federal data stacks (e.g., Snowflake Government Data Cloud, AWS GovCloud, Azure Government, and Palantir) are a plus. Working knowledge of NIST 800-53, FISMA, and the Federal Data Strategy. Proven ability to navigate the Federal procurement cycle and build long-term relationships with Agency CDOs, CTOs, and Program Managers. Experience should include a broad range of disciplines, including value-based selling, cost-benefit analysis, ROI analysis, process and project management. Experience in regulatory compliance and or (financial/IT) audit is a plus. Experience working with leading PaaS providers, data lakes, databases, and integration platforms is required. Familiarity in enterprise security solutions such as LDAP or Kerberos. Experience working with Data Quality, Data Pipelines, Data Orchestration, and/or Job Control tools. Knowledgeable of at least least two or three of the following: data management processes, including data governance, data stewardship, master data management, data cataloging, and business rules management Experienced with Collibra or comparable technologies Demonstrated proficiency in leveraging AI tools (e.g., Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT, Copilot) to solve real-world business challenges, drive measurable outcomes, or streamline workflows. A bachelor’s degree or equivalent related working experience is required. Hold active security clearance (Secret/TS). Because this role supports the U.S. government business, it is required that you are a U.S. citizen who resides on U.S. soil. You are Exceptional in communication, presentation and interpersonal skills including the ability to communicate effectively and build relationships within government entities. Fast learner with an aptitude for grasping complex technical concepts. Excellent organization and stakeholder management skills. A proven collaborator and liaison with all levels and departments within an organization, with experience and success in both partner and contractor-heavy environments. Confident in dispensing knowledge to a highly skilled and experienced audience. Able to demonstrate a thorough understanding of business needs and revenue potential for accounts in the region. Measures of success Within your first month, you will become familiar with Collibra Public Sector and understand our customer landscape. Within your second month, you should confidently conduct discovery with customers and prospects, initial product demonstrations, and be able to present solutions aligned to customer value. Within your third month, you will become a voice for Collibra within your accounts and for your accounts back within Collibra - educating external and internal stakeholders on features, roadmap, and new use cases. Additionally you will drive operational excellence by ensuring the sales process runs smoothly and efficiently.
