Job Description

Headquarters: We're looking for a Product Manager to own roadmap, prioritization, and delivery for a digital product initiative, operating within Agile/Scrum frameworks. The role sits at the intersection of business strategy and technical execution — most engagements in this market are technical-delivery-adjacent (software, data, or AI products) rather than purely strategic, so comfort partnering closely with engineering is expected. Key Responsibilities Own the product backlog, prioritization, and roadmap using Agile/Scrum practices Translate business and stakeholder needs into clear epics, user stories, and acceptance criteria Partner closely with engineering teams to scope, sequence, and deliver iteratively Define and track success metrics/KPIs for the product area Facilitate or support Agile ceremonies (backlog refinement, sprint planning, reviews) alongside a Scrum Master where applicable (Common variant, ~1 in 5 postings) Own AI/Agentic feature delivery — defining how GenAI or agentic capabilities integrate into the product (Common variant, ~1 in 7 postings) Operate as a Technical PM — comfortable in architecture/API-level discussions, not just business requirements (Frequent industry context) CPG/enterprise data-product ownership — managing data platforms, catalogs, or analytics products feeding business stakeholders Required Skills (Must-Have) Agile Product Management — backlog ownership, iterative delivery, Scrum/Kanban fluency Proven Product Manager, Product Owner, or Technical PM experience Strong stakeholder management and cross-functional collaboration (engineering, data, business) Ability to translate ambiguous business needs into structured, actionable product requirements Nice to Have AI/GenAI or Agentic product experience (increasingly common — nearly 1 in 5 current postings) Data product management experience (catalogs, analytics platforms, BI) CPG, enterprise, or regulated-industry domain experience Technical fluency (APIs, data architecture) for Technical PM-flavored roles To apply: https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/toptal-agile-product-manager
