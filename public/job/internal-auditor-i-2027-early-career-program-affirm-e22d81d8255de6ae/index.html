At Affirm, we exist for the moments that matter—giving people a clear, predictable way to pay over time, with no hidden fees, no surprises, and no tradeoffs on what matters most. About Affirm’s Early Career Program Affirm’s Early Career Program (ECP) brings together interns and new graduates across Engineering, Business, and Risk & Analytics. You’ll do meaningful work alongside your team while becoming part of a community of peers who are learning and growing together. Through our structured one-year program, you’ll get dedicated onboarding, mentorship, and professional development to help support your growth and confidence. And with our hybrid-office approach, you’ll have opportunities to connect in person, collaborate with your team, and build relationships across Affirm. About Enterprise Risk & Internal Audit The Enterprise Risk & Internal Audit team protects Affirm’s operations and long-term success. We identify, assess, and monitor risk across the business. Our work includes internal audits, regulatory assurance, fraud prevention, and crisis planning. The team maintains strong controls, tracks key risk indicators, and ensures readiness for external reviews. We uphold accountability, transparency, and resilience to support responsible growth. About the role As an Internal Auditor I, you’ll support Affirm’s Internal Audit and Internal Controls teams by evaluating business processes, assessing financial reporting controls, and identifying opportunities to reduce risk. Your work will help strengthen accountability, build trust, and support Affirm’s mission to deliver honest financial products that improve lives. What you’ll do We are looking for a curious, collaborative, and detail-oriented Internal Auditor I to support Affirm’s Internal Audit and Internal Controls teams in identifying risks, strengthening controls, and helping the business operate with integrity and accountability. Contribute to internal audits from planning through reporting, working alongside experienced team members to complete assigned audit procedures, evaluate business processes, and identify opportunities for improvement. Perform assigned audit procedures to assess business processes and internal controls, document findings, and help identify risks that could impact operational effectiveness or compliance with company policies and regulatory requirements. Support financial reporting and SOX compliance activities by testing the design and operating effectiveness of key internal controls, documenting results, and helping maintain accurate and reliable financial reporting. Collaborate with teams across Affirm to conduct process walkthroughs, gather supporting evidence, and maintain audit documentation, including process narratives, flowcharts, risk and control matrices, and testing workpapers. Help identify and address control gaps by documenting findings, supporting root-cause analysis, and tracking corrective actions in partnership with audit managers and business stakeholders. Partner with internal teams and external auditors to coordinate information requests, communicate testing progress, and provide timely, accurate updates that keep audit and control activities on track. Bring curiosity and a continuous improvement mindset to your work by building knowledge of Affirm’s products, business operations, and evolving risks, while using data analysis and audit tools to make reviews more efficient and effective. What we look for We're looking for someone who's curious, detail-oriented, and eager to learn, with a strong foundation in analytical thinking and a collaborative mindset to help strengthen Affirm's internal audit and controls practices. On track to graduate in Spring 2027 with a degree in Accounting, Finance, Economics, Information Systems, Data Analytics, or a related field, with interest in applying this knowledge in an internal audit capacity. You have a foundational understanding of accounting, financial reporting, internal controls, or business processes and risk, developed through coursework, academic projects, or relevant experience. You have strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with attention to detail and curiosity to understand how processes work, identify risks, and explore opportunities for improvement. You communicate clearly and effectively, both verbally and in writing, and can organize complex information into straightforward, accurate documentation. You are organized and accountable, with the ability to manage multiple assignments, prioritize responsibilities, meet deadlines, and handle sensitive information with integrity and discretion. You are a collaborative teammate who builds positive working relationships, values different perspectives, and actively seeks and applies feedback to improve your work. You are adaptable and eager to learn, with the ability to navigate changing priorities, approach unfamiliar challenges with curiosity, and take initiative while knowing when to ask for guidance. You have familiarity with common productivity tools, such as Google Workspace or Microsoft Office, and are open to learning new technologies, including data analytics and AI tools, to support audit and internal controls activities.
