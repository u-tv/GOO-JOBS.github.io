Product Manager, Dashboard

🏢 vercel 📍 Hybrid - San Francisco 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Vercel: Vercel is the agentic infrastructure company, freeing people and agents to ship what's next. For more than a decade we've helped builders move from idea to production with speed, security, and exceptional developer experience. Now we're scaling our products for both agents and people to ship and run software, built in the open and trusted by OpenAI, PayPal, Ramp, Supreme, and millions of developers worldwide. About the role As a Product Manager, Dashboard, you’ll own the Vercel Dashboard, the control plane for every Vercel user. You’ll define how developers and teams interact with Vercel from first login through daily use, across individual accounts and enterprise organizations. You’ll lead the shift from a project listing page to a personalized, agent-powered surface that proactively helps users ship and discover value. This is a hybrid role based in San Francisco, in the office on Monday, Tuesday, and Friday. What you'll do Own the product vision, strategy, and roadmap for the Vercel Dashboard, the primary surface through which all Vercel customers interact with the platform Define how agents operate within the Dashboard, from surfacing errors and suggesting fixes to updating code and config on behalf of users, and move it from a passive “pull” experience to a proactive “push” experience with insights, notifications, and recommended actions Define the information architecture and design patterns that product area teams (Billing, CI/CD, Observability) build within, and keep their experiences consistent in the Dashboard shell Partner with Engineering on the Dashboard’s performance bar Run experiments and measure onboarding funnel performance, feature adoption, and engagement across user segments What you need 6+ years of product management experience, including building and shipping developer-facing products Worked on control planes, dashboards, or console products at infrastructure, cloud, or developer tools companies Defined the information architecture of a complex product Use AI tools in your own workflow Run experiments and used product analytics to drive roadmap decisions Bonus if you Have worked on platform or horizontal product teams where you set patterns that other teams adopt Have worked on onboarding, activation, or growth loops in a product-led growth context Have worked on personalization frameworks or systems that adapt to user segments and lifecycle stages Benefits: Competitive compensation package, including equity. Inclusive Healthcare Package. Learn and Grow - we provide mentorship and send you to events that help you build your network and skills. Flexible Time Off.
