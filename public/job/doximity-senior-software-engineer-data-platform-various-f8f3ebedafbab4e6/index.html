Doximity: Senior Software Engineer, Data Platform

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: San Francisco, CA or Remote (U.S.) Location : This role is remote in the United States, Canada, or some countries in South America. Visa sponsorship is not available for this role. Doximity is the leading clinical AI company with the largest network of U.S. clinicians. Doximity’s AI-powered workflow platform is trusted by over 85% of U.S. physicians and 300+ health systems. Doximity's mission is to help clinicians be more productive so they can provide better care for their patients. At Doximity, we value curiosity, ownership, inclusion, and a commitment to making a meaningful impact. If you're excited about solving complex problems that improve healthcare, we'd love to hear from you. How you’ll make an impact You will join the Data Services team - one of three teams in a data platform group of about ten engineers, alongside infrastructure and developer productivity. Work closely with data analysts, data engineers, and machine learning engineers to understand their needs - and evolve the Doximity data stack to meet them. Build the foundations that make client-facing data delivery safe and repeatable - per-client data isolation, access controls, and auditability. Build and operate the MCP-based tooling and agentic workflows through which analysts and clients interact with the data platform - and the data it manifests - by conversation rather than tickets. Work in a HIPAA-regulated environment where the right people and systems reach the data they need and nothing more - masking policies, scoped roles, audit trails, and approval gates that hold up to scrutiny from auditors and clients alike. Understand what our data product teams are trying to achieve, and design solutions that maximize the time they spend on their goals and minimize the time lost to technical hurdles. Help strengthen our software engineering culture within the data organization, leading by example. Diagnose development, performance, and stability issues in our core data pipelines and Python libraries, and help product data teams resolve problems that need platform-level expertise. What we’re looking for We’re seeking a strong Python engineer who brings deep professional experience in data engineering and internal tooling, with a track record of supporting multiple product teams simultaneously. Especially valuable experience: Snowflake beyond querying - governance features, cloning, Time Travel and Fail-safe, hybrid tables, streams, and data lifecycle capabilities, Cortex suite of AI tools, Snowpark Container Services, or data sharing. Kafka, change data capture (CDC) like Debezium, and database internals - a significant portion of our pipeline work lives here. Operating under HIPAA, SOC 2, or similar - least-privilege design, masking and de-identification, audit logging. Building or operating agentic and MCP-based tooling. Apache Airflow and AWS. Container-based development workflows (Kubernetes, Docker) - useful, but secondary to the above. We don’t expect all of these. Depth in Python, SQL, and data engineering is the requirement; the rest tells us where you’ll ramp fastest. The ideal candidate will demonstrate expert fluency in Python and an understanding of programming language internals. Advanced knowledge of software engineering practices and testing methodologies is a must; you are foremost an engineer, making you passionate about high-quality code, automated testing, and software engineering best practices. You demonstrate this both within your own team and with colleagues beyond it. You have deep fluency in SQL, using analytical databases, and are able to practically employ and orchestrate user defined functions, stored procedures, and other database-oriented solutions to deliver performant and cost-effective database-driven solutions. You have worked with data that carries real handling obligations - PHI, PII, or client-confidential data - under a framework like HIPAA, SOC 2, or GDPR. You design for least-privilege access by default, you think in terms of blast radius and auditability, and you treat these as engineering problems rather than paperwork. Your ability and drive to self-manage, prioritize tasks, and deliver functional solutions will be crucial to succeeding in this role. You treat AI-driven engineering as part of the craft, not a novelty - agentic coding tools, MCP-based tooling, and automation that removes toil - and you invest in keeping your practice current as the tools evolve. You communicate clearly in writing and in person, and you keep your work legible through practices like clear change management and well-documented code. You see your teammates as your first customer.
