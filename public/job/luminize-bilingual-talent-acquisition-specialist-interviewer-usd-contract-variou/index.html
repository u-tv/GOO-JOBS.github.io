Luminize: Bilingual Talent Acquisition Specialist / Interviewer (USD Contract)

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Belgrade, Serbia URL: https://luminize.com 🚀 Be the First Conversation in Every Great Hire 📍 Role: Bilingual Talent Acquisition Specialist / Interviewer (USD Contract) 📍 Location: Belgrade, Serbia Work From Home / Remote - Serbia Offshore, Full-time, US hours: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM Pacific Time At Luminize, we help brands win. As a top 5 Amazon seller in the US by volume, we're growing fast, and great hiring starts with a great first screen. We are looking for a Candidate Screening Specialist to own the qualification step between application and recruiter. You will review inbound applicants, run structured screening interviews, and keep clean, consistent records in our applicant tracking system (ATS). You will screen for roles across our San Diego corporate team and our warehouse and fulfillment operation in Fort Worth, Texas. This is a focused role. Your job is to hold a consistent, fair bar across high volume screens so our recruiters spend their time on candidates who are truly ready. If you take pride in sound judgment, clear notes, and doing the details right every time, we would love to meet you! 🎯 What You'll Be Doing Review inbound applications and resumes against role requirements, keeping the applicant queue current with no aged applications Conduct structured phone and video screening interviews using an approved screening guide and scorecard Write concise, evidence-based screen notes in ATS, working from meeting transcripts Make advance or decline decisions at the screening stage, and apply an accurate reason code to every candidate you disposition Flag strong candidates who may fit a different open role, and escalate patterns such as off-profile applicants or screening guides that don't reflect the role Support general upkeep of the ATS so job posts, application questions, candidate emails, and reporting stay accurate 🏆 What Success Looks Like Recruiters consistently agree with the candidates you advance. Quality matters more than volume. Every applicant is reviewed promptly, and every screen is documented the same day Every dispositioned candidate carries a clear reason code, giving our team reliable hiring data Candidates leave your screen with an accurate picture of the role and a professional impression of Luminize ✅ What You Bring 3+ years recent experience conducting candidate screens and structured interviews US-market recent recruiting experience Fluent professional English required, written and spoken with the ability to build rapport quickly Experience with an ATS (i.e JazzHR) Familiarity with AI and video interview platforms (i.e. Fathom) Ability to assess candidates against a defined scorecard rather than personal impression, and to accept calibration feedback Working knowledge of EEO and non-discrimination principles in screening, including topics that should not be asked Concise written assessment skills: you document evidence, not impressions Strong attention to detail and the discipline to keep records accurate under time pressure Discretion with confidential candidate information Ability to work 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM Pacific Time, Monday through Friday Reliable high-speed internet with backup power, and a quiet workspace suitable for video interviews Bachelor's degree in Business, Human Resources, Communications, or a related field, or equivalent work experience About Luminize Luminize is a full-service Amazon growth partner helping brands succeed in a competitive digital marketplace. From creative strategy to advertising and analytics, we combine expertise and hustle to drive results for brands in CPG, health, wellness, beauty, and beyond. Luminize also operates as Coastal Trading Ventures (CTV), our warehouse and fulfillment operation in Fort Worth, Texas. CTV keeps products moving from the dock to the customer, giving us end-to-end control of the brand experience from listing to delivery. We are not your typical agency, and we like it that way. We operate with transparency, celebrate wins often, and treat every brand like it's our own. If you are ready to work with great people, do work that matters, and keep growing, we would love to meet you! 👉 Apply now and let's grow together!
