If you love seeing people grow, and you’re excited by what AI makes possible in sales, this is a role where you’ll have real impact. What will I be doing? We’re hiring a Sales Development Manager, EMEA to develop our team of Inbound SDRs supporting the GTM Sales organization across the region. You’ll build, coach, and develop a high-performing team that turns inbound interest into qualified, high-quality pipeline. We are looking for a leader with proven sales experience and a strong curiosity for AI technologies, someone who can thrive in a complex and rapidly evolving environment. You will be responsible for hiring, onboarding, and coaching SDRs, establishing repeatable processes that generate high-quality pipeline and revenue, and fostering a collaborative and high-performance culture. Collaboration will be at the heart of your success. You will work closely with sales executives, marketing, and operations to craft and execute tailored strategies that move the business forward. By taking a data-centric approach to decision-making, you will ensure accurate forecasting, effective team management, and a healthy pipeline that empowers our sales teams to succeed. What skills do I need? People management experience in SaaS (2+ years preferred), with a passion for mentoring and developing others. 2+ years of B2B software closing experience with a consistent track record of achieving quota. Experience with inbound teams that engage prospects through a personalized, multi-channel, value-led approach. Excellent analytical skills: you’re comfortable interpreting metrics and turning them into insight and action. Strong operational rigor around activity metrics, conversion rates, pipeline quality, and execution consistency. A track record of navigating complex internal ecosystems and building strong cross-functional partnerships. Ability to translate market feedback and customer insights into input for the broader GTM organization. Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, dynamic environment with flexibility and agility. AI fluency and a passion for applying emerging technology to sales. We know that not everyone will tick every box.
