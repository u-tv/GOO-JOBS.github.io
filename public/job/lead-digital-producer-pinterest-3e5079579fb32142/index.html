As a Lead Digital Producer on Pinterest’s House of Creative team, you'll lead complex digital experiences with primary ownership of production for Pinterest’s business-facing web ecosystem. This role sits at the intersection of Creative, Technology, Marketing, and Operations, partnering closely with Digital Strategy, Design, and cross-functional teams to turn business and creative ambition into scalable, executable digital experiences. The Business Site will be the primary priority, but the remit is broader than a single website. This producer will also lead production across campaign and launch experiences, event and registration platforms, newsroom and other digital touchpoints that require strong creative, technical, and operational leadership. This is not a traditional website project-management role. We’re looking for a senior producer who combines strong digital production expertise with technical fluency, strategic judgment, and the ability to lead complex cross-functional teams through ambiguity. What you’ll do: Lead end-to-end production across the Pinterest Business Site and broader digital properties — including evergreen experiences, campaign initiatives, and site enhancements — from early planning through launch Partner closely with Digital Strategy and Design leadership to translate business priorities and creative ambition into clear production plans, balancing scope, feasibility, timelines, and available capacity Serve as a strategic thought partner to senior stakeholders, helping teams evaluate opportunities, make informed decisions, and navigate competing priorities and tradeoffs Assess existing production workflows, tools, and processes to identify inefficiencies and gaps — then build scalable systems, repeatable rhythms, and smarter ways of working that improve clarity, accountability, and speed without creating unnecessary overhead Identify and implement opportunities to integrate tools, leverage automation, and use AI to simplify repetitive tasks, improve visibility, and accelerate production workflows Apply strong working knowledge of web production, CMS platforms, and design systems to inform scoping and planning, partnering with Engineering to navigate technical considerations and balance creative vision with implementation feasibility. Serve as the connective tissue across Digital Strategy, Creative, Design, Engineering, Marketing, Analytics, SEO, Localization, Legal, and external partners — establishing clear ownership, milestones, dependencies, and decisions across complex initiatives Anticipate scope changes, resource constraints, and delivery risks, ensuring tradeoffs are visible and stakeholders make informed decisions before downstream teams absorb the impact Own production planning, including scopes, schedules, budgets, staffing, and dependencies; partner with leadership on resource forecasting and manage external agencies and vendors to ensure quality delivery. Coordinate QA, stakeholder reviews, and launch readiness to ensure digital experiences meet agreed-upon requirements; facilitate retrospectives to capture learnings and continuously improve how the
