EdgeQ Technologies Private Limited: Robotics Software Engineer Job Title: Robotics Software Engineer

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: United States Robotics Software Engineer Job Title: Robotics Software Engineer Location: United States (Remote) Work Mode: 100% Remote Experience: 3–10 Years Salary Range: $100,000 – $175,000 per year (USD) Employment Type: Full-Time Job Category: Robotics Engineering / Software Development / AI & Automation Job Summary We are seeking a skilled and experienced Robotics Software Engineer to join our innovative engineering team in the United States. The ideal candidate will be responsible for designing, developing, testing, and optimizing software solutions for robotic systems, autonomous platforms, and intelligent automation applications. Candidates should have strong programming skills in C++ and Python, experience with Robot Operating System (ROS/ROS2), and knowledge of robotics algorithms, motion planning, and sensor integration. This position offers a fully remote work opportunity for qualified candidates across the United States. Key Responsibilities Design, develop, and maintain software applications for robotic and autonomous systems. Develop robotics algorithms using C++, Python, and ROS/ROS2. Implement motion planning, navigation, localization, and control algorithms. Work with robotic sensors, cameras, LiDAR, GPS, and IMU systems. Develop and optimize real-time robotic software applications. Integrate AI, machine learning, and computer vision capabilities into robotic systems. Perform simulation, testing, debugging, and software optimization. Collaborate with robotics engineers, AI engineers, and cross-functional development teams. Develop software for robotic perception, path planning, and autonomous navigation. Maintain technical documentation and follow software engineering best practices. Participate in Agile development, code reviews, and continuous integration processes. Troubleshoot complex robotics software and system integration issues. Required Skills & Qualifications 3–10 years of professional experience in Robotics Software Engineering or a related field. Strong programming experience in C++ and Python. Hands-on experience with ROS or ROS2. Strong understanding of robotics algorithms and autonomous systems. Experience with motion planning, path planning, and robot control. Knowledge of Linux-based development environments. Familiarity with computer vision and sensor fusion. Experience with robotic simulation tools such as Gazebo, RViz, or NVIDIA Isaac Sim. Understanding of real-time systems and embedded software development. Strong analytical, debugging, and problem-solving abilities. Excellent communication and remote collaboration skills. Preferred Qualifications Bachelor's or Master's degree in Robotics, Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or a related field. Experience with SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping). Knowledge of AI/ML frameworks such as TensorFlow or PyTorch. Experience with OpenCV, PCL, and robotic perception systems. Familiarity with Docker, Git, CI/CD, and cloud technologies. Experience with autonomous mobile robots (AMRs), industrial robots, or robotic manipulators. Knowledge of robotics middleware and distributed systems. Experience with robotic hardware integration and testing. Salary & Benefits Annual Salary: $100,000 – $175,000 USD (Indicative range, depending on experience and technical expertise). 100% remote work opportunity. Flexible working arrangements. Competitive compensation and performance-based incentives, where applicable. Health, dental, and vision insurance. Paid time off and holidays. Retirement savings benefits, where applicable. Professional development and technical certification support. Career advancement opportunities. Work Location United States – Fully Remote Candidates from eligible locations across the United States may apply. Must be legally authorized to work in the United States. Remote collaboration with engineering and product development teams. Occasional travel or access to robotics testing facilities may be required, depending on project needs. How to Apply Interested candidates can submit their updated resume along with their contact details, relevant Robotics Software Engineering experience, and technical skills. Equal Opportunity Employer: We welcome qualified candidates from diverse professional backgrounds and encourage applications from eligible candidates across the United States.
