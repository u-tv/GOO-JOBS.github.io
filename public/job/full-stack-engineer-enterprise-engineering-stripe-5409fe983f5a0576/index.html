Job Description

Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team As part of the Enterprise Product team, the Enterprise Engineering team is responsible for bridging the gap between Stripe's core capabilities and the bespoke needs of the world's largest organizations. Our mission is to improve Stripe's product-market fit with enterprise users to win more deals and grow revenue, while giving every enterprise user an excellent experience as they onboard, activate, and grow their business with Stripe. Enterprise Engineering tackles ambiguous, complex problems in a cross functional manner to deliver an exceptional Enterprise experience on Stripe. The key missions of the team is to 1) develop specialized Enterprise-grade product features for industry verticals 2) provide a configuration platform that streamlines onboarding Enterprise users onto Stripe and 3) deliver a consistently great and smooth experience when migrating sensitive data onto Stripe. The team consists of full stack engineers who tackle this user goal through scalable and secure infrastructure, dashboard interfaces and internal administrative tools. What you’ll do Responsibilities Design, build, and maintain scalable, reliable and performant services and systems Collaborate with other engineers across Stripe, as well as stakeholders across product, design, risk, legal, and operations Build easy to use systems that streamlines adoption of Stripe and enable safe migration of data Make effective tradeoffs that consider business priorities, user experience, and a sustainable technical foundation Improve engineering standards, tooling, and processes Who you are We’re looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement. Minimum requirements 2+ years of industry experience shipping high-quality products Strong analytical capability and ability to quickly ramp up in a new area, and tackle ambiguous problems Enjoy and have experience building end-to-end solution, including full development lifecycle of deploying and maintaining this infrastructure Experience with web software development Hold yourself and others to a high bar when working with production systems Take pride in taking ownership and driving projects to business impact Preferred qualifications Strong collaboration and communication skills with both technical and non-technical counterparts Able to reason clearly and move with urgency in a fast-paced, ambiguous environment Able to think outside of the box and be creative in designing and delivering the best solutions for our users Enjoy a range of product development activities, from architecture design and planning, to user interviews, to writing code
