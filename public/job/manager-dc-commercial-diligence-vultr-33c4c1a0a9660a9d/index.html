This is a hands-on underwriting role: for every site Vultr pursues, whether an operator colocation lease, a powered shell, a lease-to-own structure, or a powered land purchase, you will work the data room directly, build out findings, and bring a well-supported point of view to the Senior Manager on whether the deal is what it claims to be. You will build fluency across power availability and energy commercial terms, counterparty and lease economics, and the deal model, and your work will directly inform the Go/No-Go recommendations and negotiating positions the Senior Manager brings to investment gates on transactions worth tens to hundreds of millions of dollars. Your job is to be constructively adversarial to every deal: the diligence function exists to find what the seller's materials do not say. Key Responsibilities Support commercial due diligence end to end for active acquisitions: review documents in the data room, maintain the findings register, and prepare evidence-based materials for investment gates alongside the Senior Manager Diligence power under guidance: interconnection queue position versus contracted capacity, utility and ESA terms, tariff structure, curtailment provisions, demand charge pass-throughs, and power-on forecast credibility, flagging where bankable capacity and marketing figures diverge Diligence counterparties: operator financial health and credit, guarantor structure, lease terms including minimum power obligations, escalations, expansion rights, termination provisions, and service level commitments Build and maintain components of the deal model: total development cost, fit-out and NRC comparisons, yield, tax and incentive assumptions, and sensitivity to power cost and ramp scenarios, under the Senior Manager's review Verify seller claims against primary sources: utility confirmations, interconnection studies, executed versus draft agreements, title and site control instruments Draft Go/No-Go packages, risk registers, and challenge questions for operators; run day-to-day RFI cycles through resolution Support the Senior Manager, Director, and Acquisitions Managers with diligence-derived data and analysis; do not carry deal negotiation Escalate findings transparently through the Senior Manager, including adverse findings that may be unwelcome to the deal team Coordinate with external counsel and consultants on legal, environmental, and specialty workstreams under the Senior Manager's direction Help pre-qualify pipeline sites toward proposal-ready status so sales never offers an unvetted site Apply and help refine repeatable diligence frameworks, checklists, and data room standards across deal types Track diligence performance metrics: cycle time per deal, findings volume and severity, and post-close issue rate Qualifications 3-6 years in data center, energy, or infrastructure transactions with hands-on exposure to due diligence: you have worked in data rooms and contributed to investment views, even if final sign-off sat with others Working familiarity with power markets: interconnection processes and queue mechanics, utility tariffs, energy services agreements, and power purchase agreement structures as applied to data centers Solid understanding of data center lease structures, operator economics, and mission-critical delivery commitments Strong financial modeling ability in Excel: comfortable building and maintaining components of a development cost, returns, and scenario model Demonstrated attention to detail in finding discrepancies in deal materials: capacity claims that did not hold, unexecuted agreements presented as commitments, terms with hidden exposure Able to synthesize a large document set into clear, evidence-cited findings for review by senior stakeholders Comfortable managing multiple concurrent diligences in a fast-moving deal environment, with guidance from the Senior Manager Bachelor's degree required;
