Join us in transforming the life sciences industry , committed to making a positive impact on its customers, employees, and communities. The Role Are you looking for a place where your work makes an impact? We are seeking a talented Cloud CRM Consultant to join our amazing, fast-paced team of experts in Hungary. The Cloud CRM Consultant is a member of our Commercial Managed Services team who provides ongoing professional service for customers live with Veeva’s commercial solutions. You will provide application consulting services to the Veeva Commercial Cloud product suite, primarily on the Vault CRM application, but will potentially develop expertise across other Commercial products. You will identify customer requirements, perform configuration changes to the system, activate new product features and act as an advising partner. This is a remote, full-time role with Veeva. Candidates must be legally authorised to be employed in Europe. Veeva Systems does not anticipate providing sponsorship for employment visa status for this employment position. There may be travel opportunities at select times of the year for internal events. What You'll Do Provide post-go-live consulting and administrative services Understand complex business processes and translate them into technical requirements Perform system configuration changes and data migrations Activate new product features and ensure seamless integration Troubleshoot system issues and provide timely solutions Collaborate with internal teams to ensure client satisfaction Requirements 3+ years of experience with configuring, supporting, and implementing software solutions (e.g. CRM) Experience working in a customer facing and/or consulting role and managing customer expectations Strong desire to continuously learn and develop Excellent verbal and written communication skills in English Ability to work independently Nice to Have Technical CRM experience, ideally Salesforce MS, Dynamics, SAP or equivalent Customer facing IT Consultancy Experience working in the Life-Sciences industry Interviewing with Veeva We value your time and believe in a transparent hiring process.
