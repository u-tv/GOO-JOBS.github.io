Available Locations: Austin, New York, San Francisco, Remote About the Team We manage an internal, multi-region PostgreSQL platform that enables engineers to rapidly build, deploy, and operate services. Our mission is to provide a highly available, stable, and self-service platform with clear operational visibility. As a Systems Engineer, you will collaborate with database engineers, developers, and infrastructure teams to evolve our database architecture and automation. You will be instrumental in scaling our PostgreSQL infrastructure and enhancing database reliability. If you are passionate about PostgreSQL, automation, reliability, and database tooling, let's connect! Role Responsibilities Build, deploy, and manage PostgreSQL database clusters in production environments. Develop and maintain database tooling for automation, monitoring and performance tuning. Implement high availability, backup and disaster recovery solutions. Work closely with Infrastructure and Applications teams to integrate database tools. Implement proactive solutions using observability tools to monitor database health. Optimize database performance, indexing strategies, and query tuning. Role Requirements (Must-Have Skills) Must-Have Skills Experience with building, scaling, and managing large-scale multi-tenant PostgreSQL database clusters, encompassing high availability failover, and disaster recovery strategies. Experience with infrastructure-as-code (Terraform, Ansible or Salt) . Experience in scripting languages (Python, Bash) for automation. Experience building and maintaining database tooling for automation and monitoring. Experience with containerization technologies like Docker and Kubernetes. Experience optimizing database performance and query tuning. Experience with alerting and monitoring tools such as Prometheus, Grafana, and Kibana. Nice-to-Have Skills Experience developing software in Go, Ruby or C/C++ Contributions to PostgreSQL or relevant open-source projects. Expertise in database schema migrations and automation using tools like Flyway, Liquibase or goose. Experience with connection pooling solutions such as PgBouncer, HAProxy. Experience with non-relational data stores such as Distributed & time-series databases (ex. Cassandra, Timescale) and key-value stores (eg., Redis) Compensation Compensation may be adjusted depending on work location. For Bay Area based hires: Estimated annual salary of $157,000 - $196,000 For New York based hires: Estimated annual salary of $150,000 - $187,000 Equity This role is eligible to participate in Cloudflare’s equity plan.
