Hadley Designs LLC: Amazon Brand Manager

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Flower Mound, TX, USA URL: http://www.hadleydesigns.com Own Your Catalog Like a Business, and Win on Amazon. Hadley Designs is seeking an Amazon Brand Manager to take full ownership of a multi-million dollar group of our products on Amazon, as the product manager, the merchandiser, and the CEO of that catalog. This is a high-impact role for an e-commerce brand builder who brings real judgment, reads a market quickly, and knows how to grow net profit, not just sales. You will report to the leader of our Amazon channel and work as part of a small, high-performing Amazon team. While this role operates deeply within Amazon, we welcome candidates who have built brands across e-commerce, DTC, or marketplace environments and are excited to bring that experience to a scaled Amazon catalog. How You'll Win at Hadley Designs You bring the judgment. We bring the brand, the catalog, and the team. Learn our brand. Get to know our brand, our expectations, your catalog group, and the standards and SOPs our team runs on. Run your catalog as its CEO. Make the calls on pricing, listings, imagery, promotions, and inventory, and own the net profit that results. Challenge the status quo. Bring better ideas, back them with a clear point of view, and help us raise the bar. We are looking for thinkers who are coachable and confident, not people waiting for a playbook. What You'll Own End-to-end ownership of your catalog group's performance, including revenue, net profit, pricing, advertising, and inventory Reading the competitive landscape, from merchandising and listings to imagery and pricing, and responding with strategy rather than reacting to every price drop Finding inspiration outside Amazon, on Etsy, TikTok, Pinterest, and in wider trends, and turning it into product and format ideas Product and format relaunches that lift your catalog How your products look and compete on the page, from main images to packaging and format Elevating our brand experience on Amazon so your catalog protects its margin and earns a higher price Advertising, listings, experiments, and inventory planning with our supply chain team, used as levers toward profit AI workflows that handle the routine work, so your time goes to the decisions that grow the catalog Clear weekly reporting on results, risks, and next moves What Success Looks Like Within your first 90
