Marketing Strategy & Operations Lead, Global Demand, Campaigns, Field Marketing

🏢 stripe 📍 San Francisco, New York, Seattle, Chicago, Atlanta, Remote in the US 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team The Marketing Strategy & Operations team is responsible for driving organizational effectiveness and leading transformational programs across Marketing, Self-serve, Startup and Sales Development. We create the processes and conditions that enable high performance across teams and help Stripe scale with focus and discipline. We partner closely with the CMO, Marketing and Sales Development leadership team to define strategy, lead operational planning, and steward execution across global programs. This role partners directly with the head of Global Demand, Campaigns, and Field Marketing. Our work spans cross-functional efforts that are critical to Stripe’s growth. From delivering strategic insights and frameworks to leading complex initiatives, we focus on what moves the needle most for the business. We care about results, not activity, and we have fun doing it. If you like hard problems, are analytical, and are an amazing teammate, we want to hear from you. What you’ll do Responsibilities Drive our annual strategic plan and operating plan, working in partnership with the Global Demand, Campaigns, and Field Marketing leadership team to define our must-wins, build plans to win, and allocate our resources to deliver on the plan. Define and manage our most critical OKRs that instill transparent, data-driven accountability for delivering our strategy to Stripe leadership. Run the core rhythms of the business and reporting to focus attention, inspection, and strategic steers in the right places at the right time. Drive critical leadership and team-wide communication forums (e.g. leadership onsites, all hands) to effectively cascade strategy and comms Leverage an AI-first approach to aggressively automate marketing reporting. Bring clarity to ambiguity; navigate complexity, distill insight from noise, resolve issues quickly, and empower teams to make smart, confident decisions. Work directly with senior leaders across the company and with cross-functional partners to accelerate progress, solve hard scaling initiatives, and incubate new programs. Own and improve the campaign operations process end-to-end across digital campaigns and events - from planning and briefing through asset production, regional activation, and performance measurement - with a focus on efficiency and collaboration. Build tooling and automation to integrate marketing workflows and automate global calendars of marketing activity and impact Define how we should effectively measure the success of our marketing efforts and align with Data, MarTech, and Eng teams to build the infrastructure to support our reporting goals Use data to tell a compelling story and drive alignment on where we’re winning and where we need to focus. Who you are We’re looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement. Minimum requirements 10 years of experience in consulting, strategy and operations, business operations, product strategy, product management, or similar roles with exposure to both business and technical skills Proven people management experience leading a team in strategy and operations, marketing operations, or a similar function, with a track record of hiring, developing, and retaining high-performing talent Background in B2B marketing, and core capabilities of Campaign and Field Marketing (e.g. digital campaigns, events) Experience working in highly cross-functional settings, and in a role that spans strategy, analysis, and operations in a fast-paced environment Critical and first-principles thinking, with the ability to zoom out to the most important questions Passion for digging deep into data to diagnose business issues and solve problems Excellent written and verbal communication, with the ability to speak to leaders across teams including product, engineering, analytics, sales, and partnerships An ability to execute with minimal guidance and with a high degree of ambiguity Preferred qualifications Prior experience in payments, finance, fintech, or high-growth technology companies Prior experience working directly with C-level executives and their direct reports Experience navigating large data sets and/or SQL
