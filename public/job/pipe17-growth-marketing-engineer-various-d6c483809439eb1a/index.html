Type: Individual Contributor · Location: Remote (US) · Reports to: VP Marketing Experience: 3–5 years · Target OTE: $105k–$115k This is a hands-on build role, and acts as the connective tissue to our cross-functional GTM team. You will work daily with Sales and Partnerships, because the programs you build feed their pipeline across mid-market brands, enterprise order management, and 3PL. About the role Pipe17’s Order Operations Platform is the connected OMS that unifies orders, inventory, products and fulfillment across selling channels (DTC, B2B, retail and agentic), ERPs and fulfillment locations. We sell a technical product to operators, their bosses and the rest of the enterprise buying group, and generic marketing does not work on them. We need someone who can build the systems that put the right message in front of the right account at the right moment, then run those systems without babysitting. You are part engineer, part marketer: comfortable in APIs, automation platforms, and LLM tooling, and equally comfortable judging whether a play will actually grow qualified pipeline. You will be the only person in this function, reporting directly to the VP of Marketing, with the latitude to build what does not exist yet. What you'll do Run account-based marketing execution. Build the named account lists for our enterprise and 3PL programs, and map the people inside each account. Keep account evidence current, built with agents rather than by hand: what each account runs, who runs it, what it costs them, and what changed. Run the mechanics: multi-touch sequences, executive outreach, dinner and event invitations, gifting, and follow-up. Own event and conference follow-up as a system. Target list and meetings booked before anyone travels, follow-up inside 72 hours. Give Sales and Partnerships what they need to work each account, when they need it. Build custom nurtures. Replace the single nurture track with custom paths, where treatment rises with intent and decays with time. High-intent visitors get a human fast, light engagement gets agent-drafted outreach keyed to what they did, and stalled or previously-too-small accounts get re-scored and re-contacted on a schedule. Own HubSpot. Own the roadmap and the admin. Fix the data model so it matches how we actually sell across three segments, clean up source attribution so we can say what produced pipeline, and build reporting the business trusts. Keep the stack small. We are not looking for someone who adds tools. Build the outbound engine. Build automated outbound end to end: signal detection, enrichment, sequencing, reply handling, and CRM sync, using the tools we have in house today (HubSpot, Clay, Propensity, Artisan, Gong). Detect the events that mean an account is in play: a replatform, an ERP migration, a 3PL change, a new channel, a second warehouse, a bad peak season, a new operations or technology leader. Use LLMs and agents to personalize at volume, grounded in real research about each account. Make it run without babysitting, and make it measurable. Must-have qualifications 3+ years in marketing operations, demand gen operations, GTM operations, or revenue operations at a B2B SaaS company. You have built an outbound engine from scratch, and can tell us what it produced in meetings or pipeline. You have run account-based programs, including building the target list when it did not exist, and are familiar with implementing and enabling use of ABM platforms. You have built custom nurtures with differentiated paths, and can show what they did to conversion. Deep HubSpot. You have designed the data model around how a business sells, and you are happy doing the admin yourself. You build. APIs, data manipulation, LLM tooling, and real depth in automation platforms such as Clay, n8n, Zapier, or Make. You can read API docs and debug when something breaks. You did it unprompted. You saw the gap and shipped the fix before anyone asked. Commercial instinct. You understand pipeline and conversion, and why reps ignore tools that technically work. A doer. Comfortable as the only person in the function, with direct exposure to the founders. Fluent in using AI to do the work, not just to talk about it. Nice to have Experience in ecommerce, retail technology, or logistics. Enterprise account-based marketing into retail or supply chain buyers. Agents or LLM workflows in production against a pipeline outcome. Built the function from nothing at a Series A to C company. Ran a long enterprise cycle and a fast mid-market cycle in the same system. Supported a partner or channel motion, including partner-sourced attribution. About Us Pipe17 is the AI-native Enterprise Order Operations Platform for brands and 3PLs. We replace the fragmented stack of iPaaS, middleware, and legacy OMS with one unified operational layer that manages orders, inventory, products, fulfillment, and exceptions across every channel and partner. With 300+ managed connectors, AI-powered workflows, and an industry-first MCP Server for agentic commerce, Pipe17 lets brands go live in weeks, scale into new channels in days, and run post-checkout operations without developer dependency or systems integrators. Our customers reduce operational costs by up to 85%, cut fulfillment errors by 99%, and launch new channels in days instead of months. Pipe17 is trusted by enterprise brands including Estée Lauder, Allbirds, e.l.f. Beauty, Made In Cookware, Wyze, Olly, Black Rifle Coffee Company, and Dude Wipes, as well as leading 3PLs including Ryder, FedEx, and Barrett Distribution. Pipe17 is a venture-funded software company headquartered in Seattle, WA with a significant presence in the San Francisco Bay Area.
