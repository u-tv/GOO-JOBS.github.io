Growth Manager - EMI

🏢 paytm 📍 Noida, Uttar Pradesh 💼 Full-time Employment 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Us: Paytm is India’s leading mobile payments and financial services distribution company. A pioneer of the mobile QR payments revolution, Paytm builds technology that enables small businesses and consumers to participate in the digital economy. Our mission is to serve half a billion Indians and bring them into the mainstream economy through technology. Primary Goal: Scale EMI transaction volumes across the EDC merchant portfolio,optimize cross-sell revenue, and automate field sales operations. Role Objective:- The primary objective of this role is to scale the EMI business by driving higher EMI transaction volumes on already installed EDC terminals and new merchant acquisition, improving merchant EMI activation rates, optimizing field sales productivity through data-backed lead allocation, and designing efficient channel payout structures. Key Result Areas:- 1. EMI Business Growth • Drive business strategy to scale EMI transaction volumes and brand subvention adoption across the existing EDC merchant base and opportunities with new merchant acquisition. 2. Field Enablement • Equip field teams with high-intent merchant leads derived from predictive data models to activate and promote EMI features on installed EDCs. • Streamline field operations and resolve operational troubleshooting bottlenecks to reduce drop-offs in EMI conversions. 3. Payouts & Incentives • Design and manage incentive frameworks for field sales teams and partner channels focused on driving EMI adoption and higher average ticket sizes. • Monitor payout efficiency against ROI and merchant lifetime value. 4. Operations & MIS • Build MIS tracking operational health, EMI conversion funnels, and portfolio performance. • Deliver executive dashboards, strategy decks, and leadership performance updates. • Sharing timely insights of business to drive sales. 5. Process Automation • Partner with Product, Engineering, and Risk teams to automate merchant verification and EMI enablement on existing terminals. • Build lightweight workflows using tools like SQL, AppSheet, and automated checks. Candidate Profile & Requirements: Core Competencies: Data-driven decision making, strong stakeholder management, cross-functional collaboration, and process optimization. Deep understanding of in-store consumer financing, brand subventions, and driving transaction velocity on an existing hardware fleet. Why join us : We aim at bringing half a billion Indians into the mainstream economy, and everyone working here is striving to achieve that goal. Our success is rooted in our people’s collective energy and unwavering focus on the customers, and that’s how it will always be. We are the largest merchant acquirer in India.
