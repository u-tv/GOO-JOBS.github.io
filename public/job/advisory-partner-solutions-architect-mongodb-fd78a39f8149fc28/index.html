We are looking for a passionate technologist to join our Pre-Sales organization and help ensure our growth is grounded in strong technical alignment between MongoDB, our partners, and the needs of our customers. You will work closely with sales, partner, and technical stakeholders to solve business problems with MongoDB, influence complex opportunities, and help build partner-led growth in India and across the region.
