About the Team: This team sits under our Growth Commerce team and is focused on the company’s pricing and product packaging strategies, which are vital drivers of revenue and business outcomes. This position requires a thorough understanding of how product packages are assembled, including determining optimal price points for various product offerings and developing a robust methodology for analyzing price elasticity to assess the impact of pricing changes on profit volume. This is a small but mighty team of 8+ people across Engineering, Data Science, and Design. Here’s what you’ll do day-to-day: Leading the pricing, packaging and promotion product team - with responsibility for driving experimentation to inform pricing, packaging & promotion strategies - as well as the optimal presentation of pricing, packaging & promotion within the customer experience Working with the Head of Pricing (marketing), this role will use experimentation to understand complex pricing/volume trade-offs for the purpose of informing pricing decisions. This role is also responsible for the presentation of prices and packages with the consumer experience to optimize revenue and consumer comprehension Collaborating closely with the Head of Pricing around pricing and packaging strategies Using strong analytical skills enabling the conversion of pricing strategies into experiments that quantify price elasticity as well as extrapolating individual experimental outcomes into broader observations around price/volume interaction Leading an Engineering, Data and Design team to execute experiments and build consumer experiences presenting pricing & packaging Innovating new promotions, understanding their consumer and business effectiveness via experimentation, and then integrating them into the consumer experience Here’s what we're looking for: 8+ years of growth product management experience, and ideally prior experience in an environment that builds skills related to economic analysis (consulting/banking) Exceptionally analytical with a strong quantitative background in economics, physics, or mathematics An individual with a background that has enabled them to develop expertise in pricing strategy, economic modeling, and experimentation frameworks. Experience in working with cross-functional teams in a technology or SaaS environment. Strong understanding of consumer psychology and behavioral economics in relation to pricing and promotions. A track record of using data-driven insights to influence business strategy and drive revenue growth. If you don't think you meet all of the criteria above but still are interested in the job, please apply. Nobody checks every box, and we're looking for someone excited to join the team.
