Associate Director - Commerce Platform

🏢 meesho 📍 Bangalore, Karnataka 💼 Full Time Employee 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About the Team The Commerce Platform team at Meesho drives end-to-end growth, strategy, and operational excellence across key verticals: - User Growth: Scales the full lifecycle funnel—from acquisition and activation to retention—ensuring sustainable user growth and engagement across the platform. - Marketplace: Focuses on strengthening the seller ecosystem and improving selection quality to create a robust and reliable marketplace experience. - Category: Identifies high-potential segments, launches and scales new categories, and drives assortment expansion to meet evolving customer demand. - Mall: Builds a trusted branded destination for quality-conscious users, enhancing the platform’s premium and branded shopping experience. - Pricing: Ensures competitiveness and affordability, enabling the platform to deliver strong value for customers while maintaining marketplace health. - Content Commerce: Powers content-led discovery through creator ecosystems and inspiration-driven shopping journeys that drive engagement, trust, and conversion. Together, these verticals identify whitespace opportunities, launch and scale new initiatives, and continuously optimize the user lifecycle through data-backed decisions. Operating in a fast-paced, high-ownership environment, the team combines structured problem-solving with rapid execution to deliver meaningful impact. If you’re excited about building for Bharat , this role offers both strategic depth and hands-on impact. About the Role As an Associate Director – Programs, you will be a senior leader responsible for one or more strategic charters that drive key business and customer outcomes. You will own end-to-end problem-solving for critical workstreams, navigating complex challenges and ensuring strong execution and operational excellence across the organization. This is a hybrid individual contributor and people-manager role. You will directly lead high-priority initiatives while managing and developing a team of Senior Managers and Managers. You will collaborate closely with cross-functional teams to translate ambiguous strategic priorities into structured, executable programs. This is a hands-on leadership role requiring strong analytical depth. You will work closely with data, conduct root-cause analyses, understand underlying customer and business dynamics, and use these insights to set direction and deliver measurable outcomes.
