Overview Every day, millions of people use Instacart to get the essentials they need, and along the way they discover new ways to save and earn rewards. Our payments, loyalty, offers and retailer storefront teams power those moments, with a mission to make affordability and financial ease accessible to all through experiences that feel seamless and rewarding. Instacart powers grocery shopping in two ways: the Instacart marketplace, where customers shop across many retailers in one app, and a white-label product that lets retailers run their own branded storefronts, loyalty programs and offers on our technology. This role sits on the team that designs the shared building blocks behind both, including offers, loyalty, payments and savings modules that retailers configure to create their own store experiences. Our customers face real financial pressure, and our retail partners need flexible ways to express their brands. As loyalty and white-label storefronts grow, we need stronger guardrails, configurations and governance so work done once can be reused with confidence across consumers, retailers and brands. This scope extends horizontally across both our primary marketplace and white-label solutions, flexing across workstreams while the work is still taking shape, then systemizing it: turning emerging patterns into well-crafted components, configurations and guidelines woven into product and tooling. The right candidate will work to ensure our retail partners and consumers have access to cutting-edge yet intuitive solutions for offers, discounts, loyalty programs. About the Job As a Senior Product Designer II on this platform, you’ll pair strong product judgment with deep craft in components and design language. Your focus is a kit of primitives and component foundations that deliver best-in-class experiences across loyalty, payments, savings and more, powering both the Instacart marketplace and the retailer storefronts built on our white-label offering. Through thoughtful design, you’ll bridge the gap between complex business goals and intuitive user experiences across offers to make the underlying system as thoughtful as the screens built on it. Day-to-day, you’ll work with a multidisciplinary team to identify the key moments across the funnel (for example, loyalty on Storefront home vs. the cart) and design components structured to assemble into cohesive, scalable experiences. You’ll balance the needs of customers (transparency and simplicity), retailers (business growth and customer loyalty), and brands (ad campaigns and conversions). Define the design language point of view for the retailer space: how storefronts, loyalty and offers express partner brands while staying consistent, accessible and trustworthy. Define configurable components that serve many surfaces and partners through set options rather than custom builds. Design the configuration layer that Professional Services, integrators and retailers use to set up components for their needs. Build first versions of experiences end-to-end, from discovery through delivery, including problem framing, exploration, prototyping, validation, and handoff. Flex across workstreams (loyalty, offers, retailer storefronts) while work is still taking shape, then systemize what you learn into patterns, components and guidelines. Use AI-enhanced tools to accelerate component exploration, prototyping, documentation and help the team adopt those practices. Partner with Product, Engineering, Research, Data Science and retail partners to shape requirements, and translate through clear storytelling and interactive prototypes.
