Salesloft: Regional Vice President, Enterprise Account Management

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: United States, Remote Job Title: Regional Vice President, Enterprise Account Management Location: United States, Remote This is a fully remote opportunity and can be worked from any location in the United States. ABOUT THE COMPANY: Revenue is complex, and clarity is rare. Our mission at Salesloft is to transform revenue complexity into clear, decisive action for every team driving growth. We’re building the industry’s first Predictive Revenue System, where humans and AI work together to make confident decisions based on real signals. Thousands of customers run on our platforms every day, which gives us Revenue Context: a view into what happens in a deal, when, and with what outcome. Our vision is a future where revenue growth is predictable and the teams driving it are unstoppable. If you want to help build it, alongside people who Move as One and let Evidence over Ego guide the work, we’d love to meet you. THE OPPORTUNITY Lead one of our highest-performing enterprise teams as it shows customers why a Predictive Revenue System is the way to run pipeline, forecast and growth. As our Regional Vice President, Enterprise Account Management, you’ll lead 6 to 8 seasoned enterprise sellers who own our largest customer relationships. At Salesloft, we connect pipeline generation, deal progression and forecasting in one closed loop through our Predictive Revenue System, and you’ll help your team bring that story to senior revenue and technology leaders. In your first six months you will: Keep a strong team on the field: assess the talent, decide where to upskill and where to add, and keep the team’s momentum through a rapidly changing market. Shift the conversations your team has: engage the executives who own the revenue problem and the leaders who own the AI and go-to-market strategy, and sharpen how we win against build-it-yourself and frontier-model alternatives. Grow the base: lift win rates and velocity on six- and seven-figure expansion opportunities, and open more cross-sell conversations with customers who don’t yet use us for their entire revenue lifecycle process. HOW YOU’LL WORK HERE Move as One: You’ll work daily with SEs, Customer Success, RevOps, Product and Marketing, and share credit for the wins. Our sellers own the renewal and the growth, so you’ll help them mobilize the whole company behind each customer. Evidence over Ego: You’ll inspect pipeline and forecast using real buyer signals, not gut feel, and say so when the data changes your mind. Lead from the Front: You’ll coach in the work, stay close to key deals without taking them over, and take ownership early, with or without a title that says you should. Make Your Mark: You’ll shape how we sell the Predictive Revenue System to enterprise buyers, not just run the existing playbook. Customer-Inspired: You’ll turn what customers tell you into what we build and say, including why they choose to build, buy or consolidate. WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR You’re a sales leader who makes experienced sellers better, builds trust with executives, and stays close to the business without taking it over. You’re as comfortable inspecting a forecast as you are coaching a hard conversation. If you want to learn more, do more, and become more, this is your next move. YOU’LL BRING A record of leading enterprise teams to results: Consistent quota attainment across 5+ years of leading enterprise sellers, with a team where several reps reach top-performer status. Hands-on leadership of complex deals: You’ve guided large, multi-stakeholder deals (six to seven figures) through renewal and expansion, and know how to add value in the room without taking over from the rep. Coaching that changes behavior and raises the bar: You coach to specific, observable selling behaviors (discovery, value articulation, deal strategy, executive conversations) and to MEDDPICC discipline in deal qualification and inspection. You do this through call reviews, role-play and regular 1:1s, and you hold sellers accountable for the change. You see results in win rates, deal velocity and pipeline quality, and you hire coachable sellers who raise the bar for the team. A forecast leaders can trust: You inspect pipeline using buyer evidence, stage discipline and conversion data, and you can explain what drove a miss. Executive and cross-functional presence: You can build a business case with a CRO or CFO and align SEs, Customer Success, RevOps and Product behind a customer. Comfort leading through change: You’ve kept a team focused and performing through shifts in product, comp, territory or market. IT’S A BONUS IF YOU HAVE Experience selling revenue technology (sales engagement, conversation intelligence, forecasting or revenue operations) to CROs, RevOps and Sales Enablement leaders Experience leading sellers through a combined renewal and growth motion, not new business alone Fluency with AI tools and workflows, and experience selling to the leaders who own AI strategy and the build-versus-buy decision Experience competing against internally built tools or displacing incumbents in enterprise accounts THE TEAM You’ll lead 6 to 8 seasoned enterprise sellers on one of the strongest teams in our enterprise segment, with several already at or pacing toward President’s Club. The team owns both retention and growth across our largest customers, at a time when Salesloft is changing how we sell and who we sell to. It’s a great place to grow: you’ll have a high-performing team, real influence on how we go to market, and the backing of leadership and enablement to do it well.
