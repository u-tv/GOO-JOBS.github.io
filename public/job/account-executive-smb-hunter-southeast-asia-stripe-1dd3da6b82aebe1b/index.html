Job Description

Account Executive, SMB Location: Singapore | Market: Southeast Asia Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world's largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team The SMB Account Executive team helps small and medium-sized businesses across Southeast Asia start and grow with Stripe. Based in Singapore, we work with prospective and existing customers to understand their business needs, identify opportunities across Stripe's product suite, and help them get more value from our platform. We pair a consultative approach with focused, efficient sales execution to acquire new users, drive product adoption, and grow and retain customer relationships. What you'll do As an SMB Account Executive based in Singapore, you'll manage the full sales cycle for new and existing SMB customers across Southeast Asia. You'll build pipeline, uncover customer needs, recommend relevant Stripe solutions, and close new business, expansion, and renewal opportunities. You'll work closely with cross-functional teams to help customers succeed while developing your sales and product expertise. Responsibilities Own the full sales cycle for SMB opportunities, from prospecting and qualification through commercial discussions and close. Build and manage a pipeline of new and existing customers through outbound prospecting, lead follow-up, research, and territory planning. Understand customers' business goals and pain points, and connect them with relevant solutions across Stripe's product suite. Drive growth within existing accounts by identifying opportunities for additional products, increasing adoption, and managing renewals. Deliver clear, compelling product walkthroughs and proposals that demonstrate how Stripe can support customers' goals. Manage multiple opportunities at high velocity, maintaining accurate pipeline records and forecasts. Partner with Solutions, Customer Success, Product, Risk, and Operations teams as needed to address customer needs and coordinate effective handoffs. Provide a thoughtful customer experience in every interaction, regardless of deal size, and share customer feedback to help improve our products and sales approach. Who you are We're looking for a curious, motivated seller who enjoys building relationships, solving customer problems, and working in a fast-paced environment. You're comfortable reaching out to prospective customers, learning technical concepts, and balancing new business opportunities with the growth of existing accounts. If you meet the minimum requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement. Minimum requirements 3+ years of relevant experience in sales or customer-facing roles. Ability to build pipeline through proactive outreach and manage sales opportunities from discovery through close. A consultative approach to understanding customer needs and communicating the value of relevant solutions. Excellent communication and relationship-building skills, with the ability to collaborate across internal and external teams. Strong organizational and problem-solving skills, with the ability to prioritize multiple opportunities in a fast-paced environment. Preferred qualifications Experience selling to or supporting customers in Southeast Asia. Experience selling SaaS, payments, or other technology products to SMB customers. Experience growing existing accounts through product adoption, upselling, or renewals. An interest in technology and familiarity with APIs, or a willingness to learn. Comfort using data and spreadsheets to evaluate pricing and communicate business value.
