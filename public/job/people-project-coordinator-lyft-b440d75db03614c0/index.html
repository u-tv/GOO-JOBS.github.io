People Project Coordinator

🏢 lyft 📍 Nashville, TN 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

At Lyft, our purpose is to serve and connect. We aim to achieve this by cultivating a work environment where all team members belong and have the opportunity to thrive. Lyft is changing fast. We’re integrating acquired businesses, growing in Europe and new global markets, launching new lines of business, and building a workforce for autonomous vehicles alongside our corporate teams. Half of our team members now live outside the US. Our People team has to keep pace, and that means designing programs that work for a bigger, more global and more varied Lyft. The People Strategy & Programs team leads complex, cross-functional work that comes to the People organization. We run the People team’s operating rhythms and make sure our biggest priorities get delivered across every People vertical: HR Operations, Total Rewards, Talent Acquisition, Team Member Experience and People Business Partners. As People Project Coordinator, you’ll own the operating rhythm and budget of the People team, including annual planning, OKRs and quarterly business reviews. You’ll also own workstreams within larger People programs, such as change management for a major cross-functional project or business initiative with large people impacts. You’ll report to the Chief of Staff to the Chief People Officer and work closely with People leaders and partners across the team. This role is for a problem solver who can run independently, build strong deliverables and keep work moving. You’ll coordinate across teams, develop materials leaders rely on, and track progress so nothing falls through the cracks. Over time, you’ll take on more facilitation and cross-functional work. Success looks like People team rhythms that run smoothly, workstreams that deliver on time, and leaders who trust your materials and updates.
