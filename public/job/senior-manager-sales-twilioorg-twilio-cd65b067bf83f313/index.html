About the job This position is needed to lead our growing Hybrid New Business / Strategic Sales team supporting our Social Impact customers to deliver life changing services with Twilio. The right person for this role is a go-getter who wants to develop and identify new revenue opportunities with existing enterprise logos in the not-for-profit, healthcare, and social impact space as well as land net new non-profit enterprise companies . As a sales leader, you will be managing and developing a global team of quota-carrying AEs. You will participate and lead in client meetings as well as work cross-functionally with stakeholders and corporate resources as required. You will be responsible for ongoing mentoring and development of the sales team which includes recruiting, hiring and training new team members on the sales process. In this role, you will report on sales activity and forecast to senior sales management. Responsibilities In this role, you’ll: Support the successful sale of multi-product solutions to existing customers and successfully onboard large enterprise customers with their first set of use cases Retain and grow existing revenue through strategic account management Generate new pipeline through cross-sell & up-sell motions and driving consistent pipeline generation through outbound SDR-led campaigns Collaborate with your AEs and cross functional business partners to land complex deals and advance relationships with the C-Suite. Run a daily/weekly/monthly operating cadence that drives accountability and supports achievement of quarterly revenue and sales targets.
