Senior Software Engineer, Android

🏢 duolingo 📍 New York, NY; Pittsburgh, PA 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Our mission at Duolingo is to develop the best education in the world and make it universally available. It’s a big mission, and that’s where you come in! At Duolingo, you’ll join a team that cares about finding innovative solutions to complex technical problems , running countless experiments (300+ at a time!) with our massive user base to make data-driven decisions, and educating our users and employees alike. You’ll have limitless learning opportunities, mentorship and collaboration with world-class minds, and a variety of projects with large scopes — while doing work that’s both fun and meaningful. Join our life-changing mission to develop education for our half a billion (and growing!) learners around the world. About the Role... We are hiring for Android Engineers at various levels, and below you can find more information about the teams we're hiring for: Math Progress: The Math Progress team builds the experiences that connect Duolingo's math lessons into a cohesive learning journey. We help learners find the right content by improving how they navigate topics, grades, and the course path, and we make their learning progress visible through mastery signals, scored reviews, and personalized practice. The team works cross-functionally and experiments rapidly to help learners build momentum and continually strengthen their math skills. Current User Retention: The Current User Retention team builds the features that keep millions of learners coming back to Duolingo every day. We own some of Duolingo’s best-known habit-building features: Streaks and Quests. We look for new ways to celebrate learners’ progress as their streaks grow, to give them flexibility when they need it, and to set goals that are motivating and achievable. The team works closely with cross functional collaborators to run constant A/B tests, and you’ll see your work reach Duolingo's highly engaged audience quickly. 🧠 You will... Collaborate on software projects with product design and backend aspects Develop, release, and maintain native Android application features in Kotlin Mentor and set technical direction for junior engineers on the team ✅ You have...
