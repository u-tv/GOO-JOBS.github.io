Job Description

Headquarters: Remote (US) At Current At Current, we partner with ambitious brands to create digital experiences that drive meaningful business results. By bringing together strategy, design, production, and technology, we help organizations solve complex challenges and build experiences that create lasting impact. We're a remote-first agency that thrives on collaboration, curiosity, and strong client partnerships. As we continue to grow, we're looking for a relationship-driven business development professional who can identify new opportunities, build trusted client relationships, and help expand Current's portfolio of work. delivery teams through organized project handoffs What You'll Bring 5–7 years of business development or sales experience, ideally within a digital agency, marketing services, consulting, or digital
