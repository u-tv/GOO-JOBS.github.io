Tax Senior Principal, New Business Initiatives

🏢 airbnb 📍 San Francisco, United States 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Airbnb was born in 2007 when two hosts welcomed three guests to their San Francisco home, and has since grown to over 5 million hosts who have welcomed over 2 billion guest arrivals in almost every country across the globe. Every day, hosts offer unique stays and experiences that make it possible for guests to connect with communities in a more authentic way. The Community You Will Join: The Tax Policy and New Business Initiatives team is responsible for identifying and setting Airbnb's global tax policy priorities and for ensuring that every new product and service, business initiative and material contract the company launches has its tax impact assessed, documented and, where required, implemented on the platform. The team collaborates across all areas of the Tax group, Legal, Public Policy, Product, Engineering, Payments and Finance, and works at the front end of Airbnb's product roadmap. This role allows a candidate to be immersed in a wide variety of tax issues across global direct and indirect tax, withholding and information reporting, and will work with the global Tax team, outside advisors, and product and engineering partners across Airbnb to ensure that any tax that needs to be collected, remitted, withheld or reported is handled correctly and on time. The Difference You Will Make: You will be responsible for the tax assessment of Airbnb's new products and services, business initiatives and contracts from concept through launch. This includes identifying tax consequences early, mastering the product and commercial facts, documenting positions in clear, defensible memoranda, and translating those positions into requirements that Product and Engineering can build. You will also be responsible for making sure the resulting obligations (collection, remittance, withholding, invoicing and reporting) are implemented on the platform accurately and on time, and for building the tools, processes and controls that allow the Tax team to review new products and services at scale as the company grows. A Typical Day: Review new product concepts, business initiatives, partnerships contracts, and identify potential tax consequences across direct taxes, indirect taxes (VAT, GST, sales and use taxes, digital services taxes, transient occupancy and other lodging taxes,), withholding taxes, and information reporting regimes (for example DAC7, US Form 1099 and 1042-S reporting, and comparable platform reporting rules in other jurisdictions). Prepare clear, well-reasoned tax memoranda and position papers that document the analysis, the conclusion and the operational requirements, and maintain a central record of positions taken. Analyze and make recommendations about the trade-offs and implications of alternative product designs, contract structures, tax positions and implementation approaches, balancing tax risk against business goals and launch timelines. Partner with the Indirect Tax Operations, Tax Platform and Tax Experience engineering teams to outline technical requirements to launch tax collection, remittance, withholding and reporting logic on the platform. Participate in product reviews, readiness meetings and contract negotiations, and communicate tax risk and mitigation options to non-tax audiences in plain language. Design and continuously improve the intake, triage and sign-off process for tax review of new products and contracts, including tooling, templates, controls and metrics. Collaborate with US and global Tax, Legal and Policy teams to ensure positions are consistent across regions and that downstream compliance, provision, controversy and audit needs are met. Monitor legislative and regulatory developments that affect platform tax obligations and assess their impact on existing and planned products. Manage external advisors where needed, and keep leadership informed of material tax risks and launch timelines. Your Expertise: A law degree or Bachelor's degree in Finance, Economics, Business with concentration in Tax. Advanced degree strongly preferred (JD, MBA, Masters in Taxation) and 10+ years of experience, with a focus on advising on the tax treatment of new products, business models and commercial arrangements. Experience at a law firm and/or Big 4 accounting firm is required; in-house experience at a technology or platform company is strongly preferred. Robust understanding of indirect tax (e.g. sales tax, VAT), withholding tax and information reporting rules as they apply to digital platforms and marketplaces, in the US and internationally. Strong foundation in tax with the ability to quickly learn new and complex tax issues, along with a demonstrated commitment to remain current in tax law developments. Demonstrated track record of designing processes, tools and controls that scale, and of documenting positions to an audit-ready standard. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with proven ability to explain complex tax issues to product, engineering and executive audiences and to influence decisions without direct authority. Demonstrated ability to work in a fast-paced environment and manage competing deadlines across numerous product launches and contracts at different stages of development. Strong organizational skills, sound judgment, and comfort operating with ambiguity. Your Location: This position needs to be based in the in the San Francisco as required by the business needs for the role.
