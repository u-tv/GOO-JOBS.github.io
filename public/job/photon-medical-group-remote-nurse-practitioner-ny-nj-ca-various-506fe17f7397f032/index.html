Photon Medical Group: Remote Nurse Practitioner | NY, NJ, CA

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: New York, New York, United States Remote Nurse Practitioner – Telemedicine Pre-Employment Clearances | NY,NJ,CA Location: Remote – New York State. Employment Type: Part-Time, W-2. Compensation: $25 per completed clearance review. Schedule: Flexible, self-managed availability. About the Opportunity Photon Medical Group is seeking New York State-licensed Nurse Practitioners to conduct remote pre-employment health clearances through our ClearToStart platform. As the program expands, we are particularly interested in multistate-licensed NPs, especially those licensed in New York, New Jersey, and California , who may qualify for additional opportunities as services expand. Providers manage their own availability through our EMR, allowing patients to schedule appointments within their selected time blocks. Position Highlights 100% remote telemedicine work $25 per completed clearance review Flexible scheduling with no on-call requirements Clearances typically take 10–15 minutes, including consultation, records review, and documentation Administrative and scheduling support provided Responsibilities Conduct brief telemedicine evaluations for pre-employment medical clearances. Review medical histories, immunization records, TB testing, laboratory results, and other relevant documentation. Determine clearance eligibility or identify additional testing and follow-up requirements. Complete and sign appropriate medical clearance and fitness-for-duty forms. Perform asynchronous records reviews and document clinical findings in the EMR. Qualifications Required Active, unrestricted New York State Nurse Practitioner license. Current national NP certification. A collaborating or supervising physician is required unless state requirements for independent/autonomous practice are met. Photon Medical Group does not provide a collaborating or supervising physician for this role. Candidates must have their own qualifying arrangement in place where required by state law. Strong clinical judgment, assessment, and documentation skills. Own reliable laptop or computer, reliable internet connection, and a private telemedicine workspace. Preferred Active NP licenses in multiple states, particularly NY, NJ, and CA. Experience in occupational health, employee health, primary care, urgent care, or telemedicine. Familiarity with pre-employment clearances, immunizations, and TB screening. Compensation & Scheduling $25 per completed clearance , including telemedicine consultation, asynchronous records review, and documentation. Providers enter their availability directly into the EMR, and patients schedule within those designated blocks. Appointment volume and earnings vary based on patient demand; no minimum number of appointments is guaranteed. How to Apply Submit your current resume or CV, including all states in which you hold an active NP license , your general weekday availability, and any relevant telemedicine or occupational health experience. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. About Photon Medical Group Photon Medical Group is a New Jersey physician-owned professional corporation and the clinical employer for this position. VitalCheck Wellness Inc. serves as Photon Medical Group’s administrative agent and provides non-clinical support only. Photon Medical Group is an equal opportunity employer. All qualified applicants are considered without regard to legally protected characteristics. To apply: https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/photon-medical-group-remote-nurse-practitioner-ny-nj-ca
