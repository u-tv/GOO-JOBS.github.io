Head of Content Design

🏢 asana 📍 Vancouver, BC 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Asana is looking for a Head of Content Design to lead and grow our content design practice across the company. You'll manage a team of content designers working across our portfolio of products: Agentic Work Management, Service Management, Client Management, and Command. You'll set the standard for how Asana speaks to customers on every surface, from a settings page to a conversation with an AI Teammate. In this player-coach role, you'll lead the practice, develop senior practitioners, and take on some of the highest-stakes writing projects yourself while collaborating with the Asana Design leadership team to shape both what we make and how we make it. We design the future of teamwork. Asana has a bold, ambitious mission: help humanity thrive by enabling the world's teams to work together effortlessly. Our design team is at the heart of that mission. Asana Design is an inclusive and collaborative community. We're a diverse group of designers from different backgrounds (product, brand and content design, design operations, producers, motion design, film, copywriting). We are a team that loves combining research, data insights, and peer feedback into a human-centred design process to produce designs that are core to the power of our applications. We partner with people across Asana to create our product, shape our brand, and drive greater impact through design. We use design to solve challenges and create experiences that customers love. We also take the time to get weird and have fun along the way. We believe every designer should have the opportunity to grow and learn by utilizing their strengths and nurturing their interests. This role is based in our Vancouver office with an office-centric hybrid schedule. The standard in-office days are Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday. Most Asanas have the option to work from home on Wednesdays. Working from home on Fridays depends on the type of work you do and the teams with which you partner. If you're interviewing for this role, your recruiter will share more about the in-office requirements. What you’ll achieve Lead, coach, and grow a team of content designers by setting clear expectations, developing their craft and careers, and building a team with a clear mission and measurable outcomes. Own the end-to-end quality of content across Asana's portfolio of products, including Agentic Work Management, Service Management, Client Management, and Command. Define and evolve Asana’s product voice, terminology, and content standards, building scalable systems—such as guidelines, patterns, design-system content, and tooling—that empower every product team. Establish Asana’s AI voice by defining the voice and tone for AI Teammates and agents, developing conversation design patterns, and distilling characteristics that make humans and agents feel like one team. Partner with Design, Product Management, Engineering, Research, and Product Marketing leaders to integrate content strategy into roadmap decisions and make content design a visible, measurable part of how Asana ships. Collaborate with Marketing and Brand Creative to ensure a cohesive voice and tone across the entire customer journey. Lead high-impact content design initiatives, including flagship launches, AI and conversational experiences, cross-product naming, and information architecture. Partner with Research and Data Science to validate content decisions through experiments and user studies, advocating for content improvements backed by evidence. Shape how the broader Asana Design organization operates as a member of its leadership team, including evolving design and content workflows alongside AI-assisted product development. About you Demonstrates curiosity about AI tools and emerging technologies, with a willingness to learn and leverage them to enhance productivity, collaboration, or decision-making 10+ years of experience designing content for digital products, including 4+ years managing content designers or UX writers and developing senior and lead-level practitioners. Proven track record of running a day-to-day content design practice—setting priorities across multiple teams, driving consistency, shipping products, and advocating for content design best practices cross-functionally. Demonstrated ability to build influence with Product, Engineering, and Marketing leaders so content design is embedded early in decision-making rather than treated as a final review step. Hands-on expertise in UX writing and content strategy for web and mobile products, covering microcopy, nomenclature, information architecture, accessibility, user education, and launch content. Experience designing content for conversational or AI-powered experiences, paired with a clear point of view on how products should communicate when systems generate language. Experience creating, governing, and scaling voice and tone guidelines and content systems—including AI content tooling—that cross-functional teams actively adopt. Proficiency in leveraging both quantitative and qualitative research to inform, make, and defend content decisions. Clear, persuasive written and verbal communication skills, with experience presenting to executives and cross-functional leadership. At Asana, we're committed to building teams that include a variety of backgrounds, perspectives, and skills, as this is critical to helping us achieve our mission.
