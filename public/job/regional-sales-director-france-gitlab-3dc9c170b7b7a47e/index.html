methodologies that bring structure, organization, and accountability to the team without losing agility.</li> <li value=&quot;6&quot;>Collaborate with regional sales leaders and cross-functional partners across account management, customer success, marketing, product, engineering, and operations to align territory coverage, share best practices, and ensure consistent execution across the region.</li> <li value=&quot;7&quot;>Develop and maintain strong relationships with key accounts at the executive level (CXO and program level), positioning GitLab as a strategic partner and identifying expansion opportunities.</li> <li value=&quot;8&quot;>Analyze pipeline, forecast, and performance data to identify gaps and opportunities, adjust plans so the team focuses on the highest-impact activities, and represent the Italian business internally by sharing insights on market trends, customer needs, and resource requirements to inform regional strategy.</li> </ul> <h2>What you’ll bring</h2> <ul> <li value=&quot;1&quot;>Experience building and leading high-performing sales teams, ideally within France, with a focus on coaching and developing talent.</li> <li value=&quot;2&quot;>Ability to design and execute a country go-to-market plan, including pipeline generation discipline, territory coverage, and disciplined deal execution.</li> <li value=&quot;3&quot;>Strength in balancing hands-on field work with strategic leadership, staying close to customers while managing day-to-day operations.</li> <li value=&quot;4&quot;>Familiarity with B2B software or technology sales and open source/DevSecOps concepts, with enthusiasm for learning GitLab’s platform and building long-term market presence.</li> <li value=&quot;5&quot;>Skills in creating structure, setting clear expectations, and driving organized, data-informed sales processes, including use of MEDDPICC or similar methodologies.</li> <li value=&quot;6&quot;>Strong relationship-building skills, with the ability to create trust and empathy with both customers and GitLab team members at all levels.</li> <li value=&quot;7&quot;>Comfort operating in a dynamic,
