We’re looking for a Finance Systems Engineer who is obsessed with end-to-end operational outcomes across systems. You’ll improve the reliability and efficiency of Go1’s finance operations, starting with quote-to-cash, working with Finance, RevOps and technical partners. Strong finance-domain expertise comes first, supported by practical familiarity with data, APIs and AI tools. Experience applying AI to finance workflows is a bonus. What you’ll do Own defined improvements to NetSuite order-to-cash workflows , from customer and sales-order data through invoicing, payments, credits, refunds, reconciliation and close support. Configure and troubleshoot NetSuite workflows, transaction forms, saved searches and invoice templates. Work with technical partners on changes that require deeper scripting or integration expertise . Follow data between systems, investigate mapping and integration errors, and help improve APIs, webhooks and automated workflows. Check that records remain complete, accurate , and reconciled. Investigate financial-system incidents and data-quality issues. Identify root causes, coordinate fixes , and reduce recurring manual work. Work with Accounting , Tax and RevOps to turn operational needs into clear requirements. Escalate accounting and tax-policy decisions to the accountable owner. Test changes in the appropriate environment , coordinate user acceptance and release readiness, and maintain clear documentation and support guidance. Preserve approvals, exception handling , and financial controls. Use AI tools where they help with analysis, documentation, troubleshooting , or test preparation. Check their outputs, protect financial data and retain human review before using results in a financial workflow.
