Title: Product Design About Us: Paytm is India's leading mobile payments and financial services distribution company. About the Role We are looking for a Product Designer 3 who combines strong problem-solving ability with solid design craft. In this role, you will own product areas end-to-end, create high-quality designs, and collaborate closely with product, engineering, and research to ship impactful solutions. What You’ll Do • Own Product Areas: Take end-to-end responsibility for medium-to-large product features, from concept to delivery. • Solve Problems with Clarity: Break down user and business challenges into clear, elegant design solutions. • Deliver with Craft: Create polished interaction and visual design that aligns with design systems and elevates user experience. • Collaborate Deeply: Partner with product managers, engineers, and researchers to ensure design is central to decision-making. • Contribute to Systems: Apply and extend existing design systems, ensuring consistency across product experiences. • Articulate Decisions: Present and defend design decisions with clarity and confidence to peers and stakeholders. • Mentor Juniors: Guide early-career designers through feedback and critique, contributing to their growth. What We’re Looking For • 3–5 years of experience in product design with shipped work that demonstrates craft and problem solving. • Strong portfolio showcasing interaction design, visual design, and product thinking. • Ability to translate requirements and research insights into actionable design solutions. • Working knowledge of design systems and scalable patterns. • Strong collaboration skills with cross-functional teams. • Proactive, detail-oriented, and committed to raising the quality bar. Why join us? • A collaborative output driven program that brings cohesiveness across businesses through technology • Improve the average revenue per use by increasing the cross-sell opportunities • A solid 360 feedback from your peer teams on your support of their goals • Respect, that is earned, not demanded from your peers and manager Compensation: If you are the right fit, we believe in creating wealth for you.
