Briefing Content Strategist

🏢 stripe 📍 Remote in the US 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies - from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups - use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team The Global Executive Content and Experiences team creates experiences for executive audiences that build advocacy and trust, strengthen relationships, and generate revenue. We develop content and programs that help Stripe attract, grow, and retain our most strategic users. What you’ll do As an Executive Briefing Content Strategist on the Executive Briefing team, you’ll turn account research, business insights, and Stripe’s product capabilities into tailored content for executive briefings. Your work will help executive audiences understand how Stripe can support their business objectives and create new opportunities for growth. You’ll partner with Executive Briefing Managers and colleagues across Product Marketing, Product, Sales, Customer Success, and Stripe’s executive leadership to develop materials that educate and inspire existing and prospective users. Through compelling narratives and visual storytelling, you’ll help build trust in Stripe as a financial infrastructure partner. Responsibilities Curate account-specific research and insights, translating them into tailored presentation content across industries, business models, and geographies Partner with Stripe leaders and cross-functional teams to create and repurpose narratives and visual content that advance account relationships and build trust in Stripe Develop bespoke, executive-facing content aligned with Stripe’s brand that connects users’ business objectives with Stripe’s solutions across our global briefing centers Gather feedback from the Executive Briefings team to refine the content strategy and improve how briefings communicate Stripe’s value Regularly share findings about content effectiveness and audience response with key stakeholders Measure and improve program approaches, applying industry best practices to increase effectiveness and efficiency Identify opportunities to reuse content and scale successful initiatives across executive engagement programs Who you are We're looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement. Minimum requirements Bachelor’s degree in communications, marketing, journalism, business, or a related field, or at least 5 years of experience in customer-facing business-to-business sales or marketing roles in lieu of a degree 7–10 years of experience in account planning or account strategy Storytelling, presentation development, and copywriting skills, with the ability to synthesize multiple sources into thoughtful narratives tailored to specific products, solutions, industries, or audiences and translate business objectives into solution-oriented recommendations Experience working with complex technical products and collaborating with Sales, Product, and Marketing teams to produce user-centric content Problem-solving, critical-thinking, analytical, and written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to manage projects and stakeholders and deliver work within tight deadlines Preferred qualifications Experience in executive briefing or sales enablement roles Experience with visual storytelling across presentations, videos, blogs, or webpages Graphic design skills Experience managing a creative agency
