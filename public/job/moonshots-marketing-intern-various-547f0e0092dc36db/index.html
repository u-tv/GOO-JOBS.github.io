Moonshots: Marketing Intern

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: [REMOTE] URL: http://mms.cx Job Brief: We are seeking a dynamic and motivated Marketing Intern to join our team and assist with our marketing efforts in the crypto industry. As a Marketing Intern, you will be responsible for finding and reaching out to Chief Marketing Officers (CMOs), Business Development Managers (BDs), and Growth Managers of various crypto projects. Your primary goal will be to establish collaborations between these projects and our crypto investors community. This is an excellent opportunity to gain hands-on experience in the crypto industry and contribute to the growth of our organization. Responsibilities: Research and identify potential leads, including CMOs, BDs, and Growth Managers of crypto projects. Utilize email marketing and cold mailing tools to approach and engage with the identified leads. Craft compelling email and social media messages to communicate the value proposition of collaborating with our crypto investors community. Conduct follow-ups with leads to nurture relationships and persuade them to consider collaboration opportunities. Collaborate with the marketing team to develop marketing strategies and initiatives to attract potential collaborators. Track and analyze the effectiveness of outreach efforts and report on key metrics and insights. Stay up to date with the latest trends and developments in the crypto industry to effectively communicate and engage with potential collaborators. Skills Required: Proficient in email marketing techniques and tools. Familiarity with cold mailing tools and strategies. Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English. Strong interpersonal and persuasive skills. Exceptional time management and organizational abilities. Patience and understanding in dealing with potential collaborators. Basic understanding of cryptocurrencies and the crypto industry. Ability to work independently and as part of a team. Results-oriented mindset with a focus on achieving targets and goals. Note: We will provide incentives based on your work performance to motivate and reward your efforts.On average, our marketing managers receive incentives of over $1,000 per month. Join our team as a Marketing Intern and gain practical experience while making a significant impact in the crypto industry. Apply now and be part of our exciting journey! To apply: https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/moonshots-marketing-intern
