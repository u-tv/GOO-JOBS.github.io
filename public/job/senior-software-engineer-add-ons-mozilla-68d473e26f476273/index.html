About this team and role: addons.mozilla.org (AMO) is the foundation of the Firefox add-ons ecosystem. It’s a showcase for developers to publish and grow their extensions, and a place of discovery for millions of users who customize Firefox to make it their own. Behind the scenes are the DevHub which enables developers to manage their extensions. To manage the ecosystem there are a series of Reviewer tools and automated moderation systems. As a Senior Software Engineer, you’ll bring a passion for crafting resilient systems that accelerate our review and moderation teams to provide a first-class experience for Add-on developers. You’ll work closely with engineers, reviewers, developer support, and Add-ons leadership to turn ideas into polished features, modernize the platform and its architecture, and see your work reach millions of people around the world. What you’ll do: Deliver major features and architectural improvements across the Add-ons stack, including automated moderation pipelines, Reviewer tools, and DevHub. Help establish and maintain engineering standards across areas such as code review, definition of done, production readiness, runbooks, incident response, on-call practices, coding conventions, and infrastructure as code. Create robust observability systems and data-backed insights. Provide peer feedback, share knowledge and help others grow. Build and improve the systems that enable Add-ons engineering to safely develop, deploy, observe, and operate production services. Improve our CI/CD pipelines using declarative, pipeline-as-code approaches that make changes reproducible, testable, and easy to review. Deliver work iteratively using a visible backlog, short feedback cycles, retrospectives, and demonstrations to stakeholders. Write clear technical documentation, including design notes, Architecture Decision Records, runbooks, and incident write-ups. Work effectively in unfamiliar codebases, tracing requests and asynchronous jobs end-to-end to understand failures and identify opportunities for improvement. Collaborate with designers, product managers, QA, and community contributors to deliver end-to-end improvements. Contribute in the open through pull requests, code reviews, and discussions. Work on: Feature development: submission flows, trust and safety signals, DevHub and Reviewer tools modernization. AMO application systems: Work within our Python/Django, MySQL, React/TypeScript, and asynchronous processing systems. Internal developer platforms : Create tools and paved paths that make it easier for teams to ship and operate software safely and consistently. Team Process: Contribute to team ceremonies, execute process improvement, and provide feedback on team effectiveness. Engineering standards : Help make production readiness, testing, recoverability, documentation, and safe deployment part of the normal development lifecycle. Platform reliability: Deployments, monitoring, and incident response. What you’ll bring: Strong hands-on experience building and maintaining production applications with Python/Django. Ability to read and understand unfamiliar codebases and trace application behavior across frontend, backend, asynchronous jobs, data stores, and infrastructure.
