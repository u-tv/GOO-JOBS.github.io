Job Description

Headquarters: United States URL: https://terac.com What We're Researching We're running a paid project focused on evaluating and training artificial intelligence models for complex financial compliance tasks. The goal is to translate real-world compliance workflows into structured data tasks that improve automated screening systems. Your expertise will directly shape how these models understand intricate regulatory frameworks. How It Works Selected participants will begin with two remote video interviews to discuss their background and approach to regulatory problem-solving. From there, you will complete a 10-hour trial period focused on creating sample compliance scenarios. Once onboarded, you will design and submit complex problems covering sanctions screening, transaction monitoring, and SAR narratives. This is a fully remote engagement where you manage your own time while contributing structured task data. Who This Is For We welcome senior compliance analysts, AML investigators, and sanctions screening specialists. Ideal candidates have extensive hands-on experience writing SAR narratives and conducting deep-dive transaction monitoring. Practitioners with a strong understanding of financial regulations and fraud prevention workflows will thrive in this role. What You'll Do Participate in two remote video interviews to discuss your compliance background Complete a 10-hour trial period creating sample regulatory scenarios Design complex test problems based on real-world transaction monitoring Write structured task data covering sanctions screening and SAR narratives Who Should Apply Professional experience in AML, transaction monitoring, or sanctions screening Hands-on background drafting SAR narratives for financial institutions Comfortable breaking down complex compliance workflows into structured tasks Available for a multi-stage vetting process including remote interviews Compensation $100 per hour Ready to participate?
