About Faire Faire is a technology wholesale platform built on the belief that the future is local. Independent retailers around the globe collectively represent a multi-hundred-billion-dollar wholesale market that has historically been fragmented and offline. At Faire, we're using the power of tech, data, and machine learning to connect this thriving community of entrepreneurs across the globe. Picture your favorite boutique in town — we help them discover the best products from around the world to sell in their stores. With the right tools and insights, we believe that we can level the playing field so businesses can grow and local communities can thrive. We’re looking for smart, resourceful and passionate people to join us as we power the shop local movement. If you believe in community, come join ours. About this role Capital is necessary in every business, but at Faire it serves as a core value proposition to our customers. The Finance & Strategy team ensures we have access to it and are spending it effectively, but also wears several other hats at Faire including strategic growth planning, company-wide performance management, and business analysis. As part of this team, you will help inform how Faire can optimally allocate capital in the form of customer benefits that translates to sustainable growth and customer loyalty. This is an ideal role for someone who wants to work in a dynamic, highly collaborative, and data-driven environment and have the opportunity to work closely with a variety of functions. What you’ll do Identify opportunities, develop hypotheses, and execute in-depth analyses to inform product strategy and execution for Faire’s proprietary financial products Own forecasting, goal-setting, and tracking of key inputs that drive ROI across Faire’s financial value props to inform company-level capital and resource allocation decisions Support company-wide capital markets strategies that ensure Faire remains front footed on the financing required to fuel program benefits Lead strategic planning across key growth initiatives and new product launches Enable the organization to remain highly data-driven at scale by synthesizing insights, building financial models, and developing KPIs for real-time performance monitoring Qualifications Bachelor’s degree and 5+ years in strategic finance, private equity / credit / venture capital, investment banking, and/or management consulting Investor mindset: nose for value with a broad toolkit to reason about risk/reward Strong modeling muscle: able to build and stress-test financial models that reflect a point of view, not just spreadsheet theory Analytical rigor: synthesize information, decompose problems, and use data and logic to diagnose root causes—comfortable going deep on unit economics without losing the strategic question Excellent communication: credible, structured storytelling for executives and cross-functional partners; able to zoom-in and zoom-out between detail and narrative Cross-functional: track record building trust and influencing results alongside a variety of different stakeholder groups Team-oriented: motivated by business outcomes and being great at your craft in service of enabling them Nice to have: Experience in fintech and/or SMB enablement technology Knowledge of SQL Salary Range San Francisco and New York City: the pay range for this role is $ 164,000 to $ 225,500 per year.
