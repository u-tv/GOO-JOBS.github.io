Product Manager, Cash Platform

🏢 stripe 📍 New York 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world's largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team This role is open to remote candidates located anywhere within the Eastern Time Zone. The Cash Platform team is part of Stripe's Money Movement Services Platform. We manage Stripe's FX and cash positions across the globe, owning two of the most commercially critical problems in our business: delivering the best possible FX trades for our users, and optimizing the cost and routing of cross-border payouts across countries. Stripe recently launched its own in-house bank, enabling near-instant movement of funds across accounts worldwide—and Cash Platform is at the center of bringing that infrastructure to Stripe's products and, increasingly, to our customers. What you'll do You'll work closely with engineering teams across Cash Platform and Money Movement Services, cross-functional partners spanning Legal, Treasury, Financial Crimes, and Sales, and senior stakeholders inside and outside Stripe. You'll define the strategy and roadmap for Stripe's FX and cross-border payout products, drive cost optimization and routing decisions at scale, and help shape the path toward monetizing Stripe's in-house banking infrastructure for users globally. Responsibilities You are expected to: Deeply understand the competitive landscape and define clear benchmarks for where Stripe's FX and cross-border payout products need to be Own the product strategy and roadmap for Cash Platform, balancing near-term cost and performance optimization with longer-term platform monetization opportunities Drive API design and technical decisions in close partnership with engineering leads, ensuring our infrastructure is both best-in-class and commercially oriented Lead cross-functional initiatives across Money Movement, Sales, and partner teams to accelerate delivery and adoption of FX and payout products Engage with senior customer and internal stakeholders to communicate roadmap direction, surface requirements, and build alignment Own decisions directly impacting the revenue and cost profile of Stripe's money movement business Who you are This role is open to remote candidates located anywhere within the Eastern Time Zone. We're looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement. Minimum requirements 5+ years of product management experience Technically deep: You have a strong technical foundation—ideally as a former engineer or data scientist—and are comfortable owning API design decisions and understanding systems end-to-end, including what is expensive from a cost and latency perspective Business-minded: You make decisions through the lens of revenue and commercial impact; this is infrastructure, not consumer product, and you understand the difference Cross-functional by default: You thrive in environments where success requires tight coordination across engineering, finance, legal, compliance, and sales—not working in silos Domain experienced: You've worked on FX, cross-border payments, treasury, or similar financial infrastructure at companies like Airwallex, Wise, Revolut, Payoneer, Ramp, or a major financial institution Strong executor: You move with speed and urgency, can manage complexity across multiple workstreams, and have a track record of shipping in ambiguous, high-stakes environments Strong communicator: You radiate intent—partners, stakeholders, and leaders know where you stand, what's blocked, and what's coming next
