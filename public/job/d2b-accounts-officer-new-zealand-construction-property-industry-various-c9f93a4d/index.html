D2B: Accounts Officer (New Zealand Construction & Property Industry)

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Manila, Metro Manila, Philippines URL: https://jobs.workable.com/company/dcaVtKQqXr9FwBNppNCBH9/jobs-at-d2b Position: ACCOUNTS OFFICER (NEW ZEALAND CONSTRUCTION & PROPERTY INDUSTRY) Salary range: Php 40,000-54,000/month (The final amount will be at the the client discretion basing on the candidate’s skills and experience.) Working Hours: Following New Zealand business hours Industry: Property Development & Construction Work set-up: 100% Remote work under Independent Contractor Agreement Holidays: New Zealand Holidays Recruitment process: possible multiple client interviews and an assessment About the Company & Role Our client is an established New Zealand-based business group with diverse operations across the property, construction, and related service sectors. The group manages multiple business entities and supports a range of commercial and operational activities, requiring efficient financial administration and accurate record-keeping. They are currently looking for a detail-oriented and organised Accounts Officer to support their finance and administrative functions. In this role, you will handle day-to-day accounts payable and receivable activities, process invoices, monitor payments, perform bank reconciliations, and assist with month-end accounting requirements across multiple business entities. You will also provide general administrative support while helping maintain efficient financial processes and accurate business records. This is a great opportunity for someone with solid accounting administration experience who enjoys working independently, managing multiple priorities, and being part of a collaborative team. Key Responsibilities Accounts Payable & Receivable Manage day-to-day accounts payable activities, including receiving, reviewing, verifying, and processing supplier invoices. Prepare and issue customer invoices using the company's accounting system. Ensure invoices and payments are processed accurately and within established timelines. Monitor failed or outstanding payments and coordinate with customers regarding payment concerns. Maintain accurate records of supplier and customer transactions. Bank Reconciliation & Financial Administration Perform regular bank account reconciliations and investigate any discrepancies. Verify purchase receipts against transactions and ensure supporting documents are available. Scan, organise, and electronically file invoices, receipts, and other financial records. Assist with month-end accounting activities across multiple business entities. Collate financial information and prepare reports as requested by management. General Administrative Support Provide administrative support to ensure smooth day-to-day business operations. Coordinate office-related requirements, including supplies, business cards, uniforms, mail, and other company purchases. Maintain organised financial and administrative documentation. Assist with other reasonable administrative and operational tasks as required. Process Improvement & Compliance Identify opportunities to improve accounting workflows and administrative processes. Proactively address discrepancies and resolve routine financial or administrative concerns. Maintain confidentiality and professionalism when handling sensitive financial information. Ensure assigned reports, records, and documentation are completed accurately and on time. Qualifications & Experience At least 1–2 years of relevant experience in Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, or a similar accounting administration role. Experience processing supplier invoices, customer billing, and financial transactions. Basic understanding of bank reconciliations and general accounting procedures. Proficient in using computers and familiar with accounting and administrative software. Strong numerical, analytical, and problem-solving skills. Excellent attention to detail and accuracy. A degree or qualification in Accounting, Commerce, Finance, or a related field is an advantage but not required. Essential Skills & Attributes Strong organisational and time-management skills. Excellent written and verbal communication skills. Ability to manage multiple tasks and meet deadlines. High level of accuracy when handling financial information. Proactive approach to identifying and resolving issues. Ability to work independently with minimal supervision. Flexible and willing to assist with various administrative responsibilities. Trustworthy and able to handle confidential information professionally. To apply: https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/d2b-accounts-officer-new-zealand-construction-property-industry
