P-1385 Sr. Staff Software Engineer (L7) Location: Bangalore, India At Databricks, we are passionate about enabling data teams to solve the world's toughest problems — from making the next mode of transportation a reality to accelerating the development of medical breakthroughs. We do this by building and running the world's best data and AI infrastructure platform so our customers can use deep data insights to improve their business. Founded by engineers — and customer obsessed — we leap at every opportunity to tackle technical challenges, from designing next-gen UI/UX for interfacing with data to scaling our services and infrastructure across millions of virtual machines. And we're only getting started. You will join Databricks' engineering organization, working with teams that develop products and features for thousands of enterprises worldwide. Our engineering teams build highly technical products at massive scale — operating one of the largest software platforms with millions of virtual machines, processing exabytes of data daily. At this scale, we regularly encounter cloud hardware, network, and operating system faults, and our software must gracefully shield customers from these challenges. The Impact You Will Have - Own the product or infrastructure direction of a major area and drive it to the next level with clear business impact and significantly increased adoption. - Lead complex, multi-team technical projects end-to-end that have direct impact on the long-term success of a product line. - Architect and deliver highly scalable, fault-tolerant systems that process massive volumes of data across thousands of customers. - Drive organizational roadmap and technical direction through strong influence and strategic vision. - Solve complex production issues wherever they occur, including low-level systems debugging and performance optimization on large-scale clusters. - Act as a force multiplier by initiating and developing tools, processes, and best practices that boost productivity, security, and quality across the organization. - Mentor senior engineers into effective technical leaders and contribute to recruiting top engineering talent. What We Look For - 10+ years of software engineering experience. - Deep expertise building and operating large-scale distributed systems in production environments. - Track record of driving major technical initiatives from vision through execution with measurable business impact. - Ability to break down complex, open-ended problems into solutions, knowns, and unknowns, and de-risk through prototyping and validation. - Strong technical judgment and ability to influence design decisions across organizations to deliver project outcomes. - Experience leading cross-organizational efforts with appropriate milestones and deliverables to ensure convergence and progress tracking. - Deep understanding of algorithms, data structures, and distributed computing fundamentals with proven application at scale. - Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with ability to articulate both strategy and tactics to achieve results. - B.S., M.S., or Ph.D. in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. About Databricks Databricks is the Data and AI company.
