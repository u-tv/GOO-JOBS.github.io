Staff Software Engineer, Affirm Bank

🏢 affirm 📍 Remote US 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

At Affirm, we exist for the moments that matter—giving people a clear, predictable way to pay over time, with no hidden fees, no surprises, and no tradeoffs on what matters most. Affirm is building its own bank, and this is one of the most senior individual-contributor seats on the engineering team that runs it. We’re looking for a Staff Software Engineer to be a technical anchor for a deliberately small, AI-native team that owns the systems behind Affirm’s bank: vendor-integrated banking infrastructure, the data platform, and the operational tooling that holds it together. You’ll set technical direction across that surface area, own the hardest problems on the team, and work directly with senior bank and Affirm leadership. This is a startup-like build inside an established company. You set the bar: the architecture, standards, and technical strategy the rest of the team builds against. Every system you ship serves real customers under a real bank charter, and the patterns you establish determine what the bank becomes. What You’ll Do Set technical direction. Own the medium-to-long-term strategy for the bank’s core surfaces: cloud infrastructure, data platform, core banking configuration, CI/CD, networking, and the Bank ↔︎ Affirm Inc integration. Make the architecture calls the team builds against. Breadth is expected; go deep where risk is highest. Own the hardest problems end-to-end. Design, build, integrate, and operate the most ambiguous, highest-stakes pieces of the stack, and own incident response for them. Turn problems no one else can fully scope into well-defined systems with clear failure domains, monitoring, and runbooks. Be the force multiplier. Set and hold the bar for code review, design review, and examiner-readable documentation. Establish patterns that let a small team punch above its size, and level up every engineer who joins after you. Ship AI-first. Treat AI tools and agentic workflows as load-bearing for code, design review, runbook generation, and operations. Push yourself and the team on what an AI-leveraged function can deliver in place of added headcount. Lead vendor selection and ownership . Drive evaluation, integration, and oversight of third-party platforms across core, data, identity, observability, and card processing. Carry the most critical vendor relationships end-to-end. Define the integration boundary with Affirm Inc. Set the domain edges, data flows, and integration patterns so the bank’s products work inside the Affirm ecosystem while preserving the legal and regulatory separation the charter requires. Drive this across Affirm Inc engineering teams whose systems integrate with the bank. Build for examiners. Partner with Legal, Compliance, Risk, BSA/AML, Finance, and Internal Audit so compliance is a property of the product from day one. FDIC and state examiners will inspect what you build; design and document it to hold up under examination. Own operational posture . Set reliability, observability, alerting, runbook, incident-response, and disaster-recovery practices that keep the bank standalone-operable and audit-ready, with an intentionally small footprint. Create and monitor metrics, escalate when needed, and support keep-the-lights-on and on-call work. I nfluence up and across . Communicate strategy, tradeoffs, and constraints clearly to bank executives, the Bank Board, Compliance, Risk, and engineers across Affirm. Grow the team and raise the bar . Mentor peers, give high-signal feedback, contribute to hiring, and lead by example.
