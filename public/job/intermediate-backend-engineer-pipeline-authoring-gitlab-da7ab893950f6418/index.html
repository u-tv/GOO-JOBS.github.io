An overview of this role The Pipeline Authoring group develops tools for creating and managing your pipelines. We balance maintaining core functionality of the product with developing newer areas. Our key areas of focus are CI/CD components and the CI/CD Catalog , which allows users to discover, reuse, and contribute CI/CD components to make software development more efficient and productive. As a Backend Engineer, you will work on maturing our Catalog and Component functionality and reliability, improve and maintain performance, scalability and security of Pipeline Creation, and always consider usability and user experience in these key areas of the product. Your contributions and collaboration with your fellow team members will ensure that GitLab CI/CD remains a best in class solution. Some of the things we have recently been working on include: Inputs for Pipelines Inputs for Jobs Inputs Enhancements CI/CD Catalog - UX improvements Learn more on the Pipeline Authoring direction pages: Pipeline Composition Component Catalog About the team The Pipeline Authoring team is made up of experienced Engineers, some having worked in this area for many years and some having joined more recently. The team shares a passion for finding solutions to complex challenges that scale and are maintainable. Located across five countries and several time zones, async communication is key to our success. The team lives by GitLab’s core values and is always eager to improve. Many things in GitLab begin with a pipeline, so our team regularly interacts with other GitLab teams, community contributors and customers as we strive to create the best possible Pipeline Authoring experience. Learn more on the Pipeline Authoring group handbook page and Pipeline Authoring Unfiltered playlist . What you’ll do Write high-quality, efficient, and maintainable code while adhering to best practices and contributing to code reviews. Ship small features and improvements with minimal guidance and support from other team members. Collaborate with the team on larger projects. Advocate for improvements to reliability, security, and performance. Recognize impediments to our efficiency as a team (“technical debt”), propose and implement solutions. Participate in agile development practices, including async planning sessions, stand-ups, and retrospectives, to drive iterative software development and continuous improvement for the team. Contribute to our development process by asking questions and providing feedback to make us better as a team. Embrace continuous learning and stay connected with industry best practices, tools, and technologies to drive improvements in our software. Participate in Tier 2 on-call rotations to assist troubleshooting product operations, security operations, and urgent engineering issues.
