Ping Identity: Director, Sales

Job Description

Headquarters: USA - Remote About Ping Identity: At Ping Identity, we believe in making digital experiences both secure and seamless for all users, without compromise. We call this digital freedom. And it's not just something we provide our customers. It's something that inspires our company. People don't come here to join a culture that's built on digital freedom. They come to cultivate it. Our intelligent, cloud identity platform lets people shop, work, bank, and interact wherever and however they want. Without friction. Without fear. While protecting digital identities is at the core of our technology, protecting individual identities is at the core of our culture. We champion every identity. One of our core values, Respect Individuality, reminds us to celebrate differences so you are empowered to bring your authentic self to work. We're headquartered in Denver, Colorado and we have offices and employees around the globe. We serve the largest, most demanding enterprises worldwide, including more than half of the Fortune 100. At Ping Identity, we're changing the way people and businesses think about cybersecurity, digital experiences, and identity and access management. Director, Sales - New Logo The Role The Director, Sales - New Logo leads a team of Strategic Account Executives responsible for creating and closing new enterprise customer relationships at Ping Identity. This leader owns new-logo strategy, territory design, pipeline creation, forecast discipline, deal execution, partner activation, customer relationships, and team development to deliver predictable, durable growth. The successful candidate is a customer-centered, outcomes-oriented sales leader who can translate identity security into measurable business value, create demand where no installed-base relationship exists, and guide sophisticated opportunities from first engagement through contract. Reports to: VP, Sales Location: Field-based Travel: Regular travel to customers, partners, and industry events; up to 50% as needed You Will Lead, coach, and develop a high-performing team of Strategic Account Executives; set clear expectations for new-logo growth, pipeline creation, forecast accuracy, customer engagement, partner leverage, and deal execution. Create and execute the new-logo go-to-market strategy, including ideal customer profile, territory design, account segmentation, whitespace analysis, coverage plans, investment levels, and quarterly operating rhythms. Build early-stage demand before a formal buying process through executive outreach, mission- and business-led account hypotheses, customer referrals, partner intelligence, events, communities, digital programs, and responsible use of AI and data signals. Establish a repeatable method for account entry and discovery that tests the problem, impact, urgency, decision process, funding, compelling events, and competitive position. Position Ping Identity’s platform against customer priorities and connect identity security to measurable outcomes such as modernization, Zero Trust, secure workforce and customer access, fraud reduction, compliance, privacy, and resilience. Lead disciplined opportunity execution from discovery through signature, including qualification, stakeholder mapping, technical validation, demonstrations, proofs of value, solution strategy, pricing, procurement, legal review, negotiation, and close plans. Maintain an accurate, evidence-based forecast that reflects funding, decision criteria, stakeholder support, procurement path, competitive position, partner role, implementation readiness, and timing risk. Build trusted relationships with security, identity, technology, risk, business, and executive stakeholders; coach the team to expand coverage beyond a single contact or department. Develop and activate a partner ecosystem of resellers, distributors, systems integrators, advisory firms, cloud and technology partners, and industry communities. Establish joint account maps, pursuit plans, executive engagement, enablement, and partner-sourced or partner-influenced pipeline goals. Orchestrate Sales Engineering, Solutions Specialists, Customer Success, Professional Services, Business Value, Marketing, Channel, Product, Finance, Legal, and executive sponsors around one customer narrative and clear ownership of next actions. Represent Ping Identity with credibility at customer briefings, executive meetings, partner events, and industry forums; bring field intelligence back to Product, Marketing, Channel, and Sales leadership. You Have 8+ years of enterprise software, SaaS, cybersecurity, identity, cloud, or infrastructure sales experience, including 3–5 years leading quota-carrying sales teams. A consistent record of meeting or exceeding revenue targets and developing net-new enterprise logos through complex, multi-stakeholder sales cycles. Demonstrated success building pipeline before a formal buying process, influencing requirements, creating executive sponsorship, and closing through customer value rather than product positioning alone. Strong expertise in territory planning, whitespace analysis, account-based selling, discovery, value selling, qualification, negotiation, forecasting, inspection, and close-plan discipline. Executive presence and the ability to create credibility without an existing relationship and guide senior stakeholders through change. Experience building partner-led and partner-influenced pipeline and coordinating internal specialists to win competitive pursuits. Strong leadership, communication, presentation, negotiation, change-management, and talent-development skills. Fluency with Salesforce and related sales, forecasting, account-planning, and productivity tools; analytical and data-driven decision-making. Experience selling or leading in identity security, IAM, CIAM, fraud prevention, identity verification, cybersecurity, or adjacent cloud-security markets preferred. What Success Looks Like The team consistently meets or exceeds quota, pipeline, and new-logo objectives. Territories have executable account plans linking priority accounts to business hypotheses, buying signals, executive relationships, partner coverage, and next actions. New-logo pipeline is created early, with measurable self-sourced, partner-sourced, and partner-influenced contributions. Forecasts are accurate, conservative, and explainable; material opportunities have verified decision criteria, stakeholder coverage, competitive strategy, close plan, and timing risk. Customers experience one coordinated Ping team and see identity security connected to measurable business, security, service, privacy, and resilience outcomes. Strategic Account Executives demonstrate disciplined account planning, discovery, qualification, value selling, stakeholder expansion, proposal leadership, negotiation, and post-award expansion planning. The team develops talent, shares field intelligence, uses data and AI responsibly, and delivers repeatable new-logo wins.
