About the Team At InMobi, we're reimagining how Inside Sales operates by bringing together AI agents, market intelligence, and human sales expertise. Our Agentic Inside Sales Organization (ISO) is being built as a dedicated revenue engine to drive new business across non-enterprise and mid-market advertisers, brands, agencies, and publishers globally, with a strong focus on North America. Unlike traditional Inside Sales, where sellers spend significant time researching accounts, prospecting, and managing outbound sequences, our AI agents handle much of this work. This allows our sellers to focus on what matters most: engaging customers, building relationships, closing deals, and accelerating revenue growth. We're looking for an ambitious, commercially driven sales leader who combines a strong hunter mindset with a genuine interest in AI, automation, and building the next generation of sales workflows. You will operate as an AI-enabled sales leader, managing intelligent agents that support account discovery, research, prospecting, and personalized outreach, while taking direct ownership of qualified opportunities, client engagement, negotiations, and closures.
