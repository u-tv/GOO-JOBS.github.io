Empower Tutoring: Educational Consultant (Full-Time)

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Toronto, Ontario, Canada URL: http://www.empowertutoring.ca Job Description Empower Tutoring is looking for an Educational Consultant located in Ontario for our online tutoring company. This is a full-time, remote position. Please see hours below. Hours: Regular hours: Mondays-Thursdays: 10:30 AM - 7:30 PM, Fridays 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM Subject to potential weekend shifts (3-4 hours on either Saturday OR Sunday) About Us Empower Tutoring is an Ontario-based tutoring company, offering online tutoring for high-school students in Math, Chemistry, Physics, English, and more. Our team of tutors provide personalized tutoring sessions, tailored to each student they work with. We are currently looking for a dedicated entry-level Educational Sales Consultant who is enthusiastic, high energy, and enjoys helping families with their tutoring needs. Responsibilities Respond to new family inquiries Run phone consultations with parents Understand each student's needs and goals Recommend the right tutoring plan Have pricing and enrolment conversations Follow up with families Hand off new families to Student Success Managers Write clear notes to support tutor matching Keep lead records accurate and up to date Only Apply If You... Genuinely enjoy talking to people and get energy from being on the phone most of the day Are comfortable calling parents and building rapport quickly Are a good listener and can understand family needs Can be quick on the spot during phone calls Learn new programs, systems, and processes quickly Are passionate about education Are a confident, extroverted and energetic person! Requirements University degree Knowledge of the Ontario curriculum Experience in customer service work or sales (preferably in an educational setting) Previous experience in education field or as a tutor is an asset Available full-time and willing to commit for the long-term Compensation $20.00/hour + purchases bonuses Please submit your cover letter and resume for consideration. Selected applicants will be contacted to schedule an initial interview. Please only apply via the job posting and do NOT call or email to apply. We look forward to hearing from you!
