Who we are About Stripe Stripe, LLC. is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies - from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups - use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career. What you’ll do Responsibilities Work with engineers, designers, and product managers to design and build highly scalable and secure consumer experiences that process billions of dollars per year Design and build APIs, services, systems and experiments that evaluate the effectiveness of new and existing product features against critical business metrics Coach and mentor junior engineers, assist in hiring, and partner to develop team culture/best practices Participate in all parts of the software development workflow to improve engineering standards, tooling, and processes Operate at the intersection of data science and software engineering - apply state of the art data analysis tools and ML to understand/improve product and user behaviors Build software including creation, modification, and testing of source code written in Ruby, JavaScript, Typescript, and JVM languages Who you are Minimum requirements 6 years of Software Engineering experience. Position also requires the following: 6 years of experience in building software with Typescript, Javascript, and Ruby; 4 years of experience in building web or mobile applications; 3 years of experience with SQL databases; 2 years of experience in writing technical design documents; and 1 year of experience in designing and implementing large scale consumer payment experiments. Salary: $190,400 – $285,600/yr. This salary range represents the base salary range for the role and any sales commissions / sales bonuses targets, if applicable, would be in addition to the base salary. 40 hrs/week 50% remote work permitted. Multiple Positions Available.
