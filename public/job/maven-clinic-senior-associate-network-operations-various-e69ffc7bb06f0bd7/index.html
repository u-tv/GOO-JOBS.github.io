Maven Clinic: Senior Associate, Network Operations

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: New York, NY; Remote, US (Hub cities) Maven Clinic is the world's largest virtual clinic for women and families on a mission to make healthcare work for all of us. Through Maven Enterprise, the company partners with more than 2,300 employers and health plans to provide end-to-end women's and family health programs spanning fertility and family building, maternity and newborn care, parenting and pediatrics, and menopause and midlife — improving clinical outcomes, reducing healthcare costs, and expanding equitable access to high-quality care at scale. Through its consumer platform, Maven provides direct access to virtual care across 30+ specialties, as well as dedicated hormone and GLP-1 care programs purpose-built for women. Founded in 2014 by CEO Kate Ryder, Maven Clinic has raised more than $425 million from leading healthcare and technology investors including General Catalyst, Sequoia, Dragoneer Investment Group, Oak HC/FT, StepStone Group, Icon Ventures, and Lux Capital. Recognized for innovation and industry leadership, Maven has been named to the TIME100 Most Influential Companies, CNBC Disruptor 50, Fast Company's Most Innovative Companies, and FORTUNE Best Places to Work. Learn more at mavenclinic.com An award-winning culture working towards an important mission – Maven Clinic is a recipient of over 30 workplace and innovation awards, including: TIME 100 Most Influential Companies (2023, 2026) Fortune Change the World (2024) CNBC Disruptor 50 List (2022, 2023, 2024) Fortune Best Workplaces for Millennials (2024) Fortune Best Workplaces in Health Care (2024) Fast Company Most Innovative Companies (2020, 2023) Fortune Best Workplaces NY (2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024) Maven is looking for a Network Operations Senior Associate to manage the day-to-day operations of the Maven Performance Network. As a key member of the Network Operations team, you will play a critical role in helping to manage and support Maven’s network of fertility clinics and partners. This role will require excellent organization, communication, and internal collaboration skills. You will assist with the onboarding and account management of partners, ongoing documentation of network operational processes, and managing of strategic initiatives related to network monitoring, clinic support, and more. We’re ideally looking for someone with previous experience in the fertility space, who is passionate about healthcare with A+ communication and strong organization and interpersonal skills. As a Senior Associate, Network Operations at Maven, you will: Support inbound emails and calls from fertility network partners related to Maven workflows and member benefits Manage end-to-end resolution of Maven member issues with fertility network partners Assist with implementation and transition of care within the network for new Maven Managed Benefit clients Serve as liaison between network stakeholders and internal teams to relay product bugs and feedback Lead onboarding sessions and refresher trainings with clinic partners Own fertility network directory maintenance and updates Create and maintain SOPs and assist with overall organization of Network Operations team resources Own network reporting initiatives and special projects Represent Maven at industry conferences Travel to clinic sites across the US to provide on-the-ground education and support We’re looking for you to bring: 3+ years of experience in provider operations, provider/customer success, and/or account management Fertility industry experience is a plus Fertility billing experience is a plus Stellar organizational and analytical skill sets with high Excel/Google Sheets fluency Strong, clear verbal and written communication Direct experience communicating with internal and external stakeholders Demonstrated ability to manage and document complex operational processes Resourceful, highly organized self-starter with a bias towards action and efficiency Trusted , team player looking to drive success in a fast-paced, start-up environment The base salary range for this role is $98,000 - $115,000 per year. You will also be entitled to receive equity and benefits. Individual pay decisions are based on a number of factors, including qualifications for the role, experience level, and skillset. Maven embraces a flexible hybrid work model. Our teams primarily operate from the New York Metropolitan area, NY, and remotely via San Francisco/Bay Area, CA, Seattle, WA. For those in our New York City office, we encourage in-person collaboration by requiring team members to work onsite three days a week (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday). For those based in Boston, DC, Chicago, Seattle, and San Francisco, we encourage in-person collaboration by requiring team members to attend monthly Work Together Days within these cities. This policy aims to balance remote work flexibility with the benefits of face-to-face interaction. At Maven we believe that a diverse set of backgrounds and experiences enrich our teams and allow us to achieve above and beyond our goals. If you do not have experience in all of the areas detailed above, we hope that you will share your unique background with us in your application and how it can be additive to our teams.
