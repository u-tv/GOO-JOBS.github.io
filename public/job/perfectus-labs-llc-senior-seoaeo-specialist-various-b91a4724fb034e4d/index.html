PERFECTUS LABS LLC: Senior SEO/AEO Specialist

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Vandalia, Ohio, United States URL: https://perfectuslabs.com Benefits: Bonus based on performance Health insurance Opportunity for advancement Paid time off Training & development Pay: $25.00 per hour + Benefits & Annual Bonus Schedule: Mon–Fri, 8 AM – 5 PM EST or 9 AM – 6 PM EST Role Summary Perfectus Labs is a fast-growing digital marketing powerhouse built on AI-powered innovation. We help service businesses scale by deploying the same sophisticated tools used by global enterprises—specifically through our flagship BookedSolid AI and Cognos AI SEO platforms. We are looking for a reliable, detail-oriented SEO/AEO Specialist to serve as the backbone of our Client Success team. This is a vital behind-the-scenes role focused on executing the daily and weekly tasks that keep our AI-driven campaigns running smoothly. If you are a process-driven individual who excels at managing repetitive tasks with surgical precision, you will thrive here. Key Responsibilities SEO/AEO Implementation: Master executing routine SEO optimizations and updates using our proprietary SEO AI platform, following specific directives from the Operations Team. Be able to own the product and become the SME. Report Building: Ensure the team is excelling with focus on Strategic Insights and Performance Analysis based on routine reporting and scorecards. Task Coordination: Act as the bridge between the client team and the vendor partners, managing internal requests are completed in a timely manner. Campaign Monitoring: Oversee ongoing SEO optimizations across many client accounts that you will manage and your team will manage. Process Adherence: Follow, create, and maintain checklists and SOPs without deviation to maintain a high standard of "error-free" output. Qualifications & Skills Experience : Proven background in a support, administrative, or operations role where extreme attention to detail was a requirement. Technical Proficiency : Comfortable with modern collaboration tools (Slack, Gmail, Google Sheets, and Docs). Expertise with GA4, Google Search Console, and Google Business Profile are required. Organizational Mastery : You are a "list person" who loves a clean workflow and can create and manage a consistent daily workload independently. Communication : Excellent written communication skills for internal and external coordination; with a professional, positive, and proactive attitude. Adaptability : A quick learner who can jump into new AI software and processes efficiently. Who This Role Is Perfect For You are a sharp, articulate individual who doesn't just "do tasks"—you understand that your execution is what allows our clients to drive 6–7 figures in additional revenue. You enjoy a high-growth environment, you are knowledgeable in different LLMs and their benefits to SEO/AEO, and you are Proven to Execute. About Perfectus Labs Our team has managed over $2 billion in ad spend, driven more than $20 billion in sales, and sent billions of marketing messages across channels. Our proprietary AI systems allow us to deliver results at a scale and speed that traditional teams simply can’t match. We operate on three core values: Positive : You stay focused on solutions and forward motion. Passionate : You care deeply about your work and the impact it has on small businesses.
