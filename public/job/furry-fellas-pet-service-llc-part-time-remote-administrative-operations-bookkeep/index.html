Headquarters: Warwick, Rhode Island, United States URL: http://www.furryfellaspetsitting.com/ Part-Time Remote Administrative Operations & Bookkeeping Specialist Furry Fellas Pet Service LLC | Rhode Island Pay: $19–$20+ per hour, depending on experience Hours: Approximately 5–10 hours per week initially, varying based on projects and business needs Workplace Type: Fully Remote Eligible States: Rhode Island, Massachusetts, and Florida Schedule: Flexible, with some weekday daytime availability required About the Position Furry Fellas Pet Service LLC, an established Rhode Island pet care company, is seeking an experienced, reliable, and highly detail-oriented professional to assist with administrative operations, payroll, bookkeeping, and special projects. This position is ideal for a resourceful self-starter who enjoys researching information, solving problems, staying organized, and completing projects independently. We are looking for someone who takes initiative, communicates effectively, pays close attention to detail, and consistently follows through on assigned responsibilities. Key Responsibilities Process payroll and assist with bookkeeping, financial records, and account reconciliations. Required Qualifications Minimum 3 years of relevant professional administrative experience. Hands-on payroll and bookkeeping experience. Proficiency with QuickBooks Online. Excellent organizational, research, problem-solving, and communication skills. Strong attention to detail and ability to identify and resolve discrepancies. Ability to work independently, manage multiple priorities, and consistently meet deadlines. Reliable computer, high-speed internet, and proficiency with Microsoft Word and Excel. Smartphone capable of using our company phone system. Quiet, professional remote workspace suitable for business calls. Preferred Qualifications Experience with any of the following is a plus, but not required: WordPress website updates and management Search Engine Optimization (SEO) Google Search Console Google Analytics (GA4) Google Business Profile Jotform creation, editing, and conditional logic Website maintenance and mobile-responsive design DaySmart Payroll, Time To Pet, or Microsoft 365/OneDrive Human resources and employee administration Experience working with a pet care business Schedule & Expectations This is a flexible, part-time remote position, averaging approximately 5–10 hours weekly initially. This is not an entry-level position. We are seeking an experienced professional who is comfortable taking ownership of assigned responsibilities and following projects through to completion.
