Technical Lead Norm Compliance

🏢 Norm 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Norm Norm builds AI-native professional services firms and the technology that powers them. Our mission is to help expert professionals deliver better work at greater scale. Developing the technology and the firms together lets us rethink how work gets done, how teams operate and what clients can expect. Professional judgment and experience guide what we build, while advances in the technology expand what our firms can deliver. Our shared agentic platform connects that progress across teams and services. Founded in 2023, Norm brings together experts across engineering, research, product, design and professional practice. Our legal and compliance platform serves institutions representing more than $35 trillion in combined assets under management. Norm also powers Norm Law, LLP, an affiliated AI-native law firm whose attorneys advise leading institutions on complex corporate matters. AI Fluency: Norm prefers all team members to be fluent in AI. Successful candidates actively use AI in their day-to-day work to support thinking, creation, and problem-solving. They use it to improve the quality and speed of their work and to continuously refine how work gets done end-to-end. Candidates should be prepared to demonstrate and discuss their AI usage throughout the interview process, including concrete examples of tools, workflows, and outcomes. We look for practical, hands-on experience, not theoretical familiarity. This Role We are hiring a hands-on Tech Lead for Norm Compliance, the team that owns Norm's compliance platform for asset managers: the product behind marketing and content review at some of the largest financial institutions in the US. You are an IC-style engineer who can also set an engineering roadmap and lead a team; you will lead up to seven engineers, own every technical decision inside the platform, and ship code yourself at least half of the week. The platform has been shipping for years. You will ramp with the engineers who know it best, then set its direction. How You'll Work The team is fully remote in the US and reports to the General Manager of Norm Compliance. You set the platform's technical direction, from architecture and standards to a roadmap you shape with the product manager and the General Manager. The team owns the platform end to end, including the infrastructure it runs on. Day to day you work with a product manager who owns the backlog and our commitments to clients, and with a client engagement/deployment strategy team that owns the relationships: you decide how the work gets done and how long it takes, they decide what we take on and when, and nothing reaches a client without both. The team runs its own on-call. You Will Own architecture, technical standards and code review for the platform, and contribute production code every week. Set the engineering roadmap for the platform with the product manager and the General Manager. Hire, onboard and grow a team of Staff and Senior engineers. Run on-call escalation. Answer clients' technical questions, in writing or through meetings, along the client engagement/deployment strategy team. Learn the platform with the engineers who run it today, ship with their review, then own it. Skills & Experience - Core Bachelor's or Master's in computer science or an equivalent field, or equivalent practical experience 8+ years building production software, including 3+ years leading a team of four to eight engineers while still shipping code Python and TypeScript in production; Postgres or similar; AWS Experience taking over a large codebase built by others and running it Experience setting and delivering a technical roadmap for a product with enterprise clients Experience on client calls, answering hard technical questions without over-promising Clear, brief writing Skills & Experience - Pluses Clients in financial services or another regulated industry Experience with LLM-backed products, Terraform, or a workflow engine such as Temporal Experience splitting a release pipeline or a shared repository, or hiring a team from zero What Success Looks Like - 90 Days You know the platform's architecture and its largest clients, and have shipped with code review from the team You own the platform's technical decisions and run on-call escalation The team has run an incident end to end, and you, the team and the product manager have a written way of working Comp and Benefits $220,000 - $290,000 a year plus equity.
