Alliants Limited: Customer Success Executive (APAC & Middle East)

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Singapore, Singapore URL: https://alliants.com Our hotel partners across Asia are mid-shift while our UK team sleeps — and we're looking for a Customer Success Associate to be the in-region contact who picks up their questions and issues in their own time zone. The role at a glance Title: Customer Success Associate Location: Remote, based in Singapore (nearby APAC time zones considered), working mostly APAC hours Compensation: £35,000 – £37,000 per year Contract: 2-year contract Team: Customer Success (APAC & Middle East pod), reporting to the Enterprise Customer Success Manager This is an associate-level customer success role on our APAC & Middle East team, and it's built around two things. You'll be our our in-region first response. When a property hits a problem overnight UK time, you pick it up, work with the hotel team and our in-region support, and hand the UK team a clear head start — rather than the hotel waiting for London to wake up. You'll own the day-to-day relationship with our standard properties across a region of more than 120 hotels and 13 brands. That means support follow-through, re-onboarding properties that need it, and making sure every hotel gets real value from our platform. Enterprise and Growth accounts sit with the Enterprise Customer Success Manager. You'll shadow and support that work as you learn, and take on more over time; this role is the in-region contact for standard properties. Role Focus & Operating Context You'll communicate with hoteliers the way great hoteliers talk to their guests. Every message is written by you, with care. You'll work mostly in APAC hours, with some overlap into UK afternoons — more at the start. You'll sit in a small regional pod alongside Product Solutions, Support and Sales, with property teams from front office to GM as your day-to-day contacts. What you'll work on and own The overnight queue — what happens when a property's guest messaging stops at 2am in London and you're the one awake Standard property relationships — day-to-day contact with front office, guest relations, operations and GMs Re-onboarding — showing property teams how the platform works, how to configure it and how to escalate Adoption — playbooks rolled out property by property, starting with the lowest-usage sites The regional read-out — a usage, adoption and support-trends dashboard reviewed monthly Clean records — accurate Salesforce data and product feedback routed to the right team What you'll bring Guest-facing experience in luxury hotel operations — front office, guest relations, guest experience or rooms division Written and spoken English to luxury-hotel standard — you know how to write to a GM A real understanding of how a property runs, shift to shift Ownership — you take a problem from first message to resolution without being chased Comfort with simple reporting and data (spreadsheets, PMS or CRM reports) Confidence explaining technology to non-technical hotel teams Based in Singapore or a nearby APAC time zone, and able to decide where to focus Works confidently on your own and knows when to escalate Adapts naturally across the cultures of APAC and the Middle East Wants to understand how the technology works underneath You'll be the named, same-time-zone contact for standard properties across APAC and the Middle East. You'll pick up issues raised outside UK hours, work them with the property and in-region support, and hand the UK a clear head start. You'll own tickets from standard properties alongside Support, escalating to the Enterprise Customer Success Manager when needed. You'll re-onboard properties where gaps remain after deployment, so teams use the platform with confidence. You'll roll out adoption playbooks property by property, starting with the lowest-usage sites, and build localised guides and training. You'll shadow enterprise calls and pick up location-level work on Enterprise accounts as you learn. You'll track usage, adoption and support trends in a regional dashboard, with a monthly read-out. You'll flag at-risk properties early, so we keep the hotels we have. You'll keep Salesforce accurate for every property you support. You'll route product feedback and requests through Product Solutions intake. Who is Alliants and what do we do? Alliants, established in 2009, is dedicated to producing customer engagement technologies and services that pave the way for a more human, sustainable, and promising future for hospitality. At Alliants, we are all in for our people and our industry. What's in it for you? We know we all work better in an autonomous, collaborative, diverse and equitable space. To support you in becoming the best version of yourself, we offer: Remote, in region: work remotely from Singapore, mostly in APAC hours Flexible hours: once you're settled in, a late day for the UK is balanced by a later start the next day Time to learn: around six months to learn the platform, including shadowing enterprise calls A regional team: work alongside our APAC & Middle East Customer Success and Product Solutions colleagues A warm welcome: to celebrate you joining Alliants, we'll plant a Great Oak tree in your honour Alliants celebrates diversity and is committed to creating an inclusive environment for all employees.
