Rubrik Job Board: Field Sales Operations Manager

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote - United States Rubrik has become the fastest growing enterprise software company in Silicon Valley. We are seeking a passionate and highly driven individual to join our world class, career enhancing Sales Operations team! This team partners with Field Sales Leaders on cutting edge GTM strategies, metric tracking, and infrastructure development. Sales Operations is integral to the overall success of the sales organization, providing data, process, and decision making that ultimately drives revenue, growth and sales productivity. Rubrik’s Americas Sales team is one of the fastest growing in the industry and is led by a dynamic team of sales executives and cross-functional leaders. The Field Sales Operations Manager will have the charter and opportunity to help shape the growth of the business through a combination of metric tracking and reporting, territory planning, and strategic projects focused on growth and optimization. What You’ll Do: Work directly with our Americas sales teams (AE to Sr. VP’s) to help grow and shape the business to drive growth, bringing a combination of analytical and operational experience Partner with the sales leadership to drive a strong operational cadence around forecasting, pipeline management, account management, and sales performance reporting Own a weekly reporting cadence that provides a view into the business segment health and potential blind spots in performance Drive accountability for key sales revenue KPI’s throughout the quarter Bring analytics and problem-solving acumen, with experience working on large datasets and initiatives to drive short, and long-term revenue goals Deliver Quarterly Business Reviews (QBR) to segment leaders and management, covering prior-quarter performance and progress Invest in identifying areas of improvement and managing/delivering ad-hoc projects pertaining to sales strategy/ops. and related areas to drive value Engage and be able to Influence cross-functional stakeholders in identifying and executing use-cases to drive top-line revenue Support sales teams with quoting and account management requests and navigate internal systems and processes (e.g. SFDC, CPQ, Clari, etc) Work with various internal partners (Order Mgmt., Deal Desk, Finance, Sales Management and Product Ops.) to drive efficient execution and root cause analysis for escalations Build a GTM plan, culminating in a the creation of equitable sales territories and work with sales and cross-functional teams in designing associated quotas and PG targets Support end-of-quarter and month-end closing procedures, including booking escalations, SFDC maintenance, system troubleshooting, etc. Experience You’ll Need: 5-7+ years in Sales Operations or similar role; Bachelor's degree or equivalent work experience Requires advanced communication skills and ability to interface directly with Sales VP’s Requires sales operations experience within Americas Tier1 Strategic accounts and build of GTM plans and associated territory carving Preferred tech experience: Salesforce CRM, CPQ, & Renewals modules. Clari Forecasting, Claude Code/CoWork Skill implementations, and Advanced Excel/ G-sheets Strong revenue reporting and sales/financial analytical skill and managing large datasets Prior experience with operations support for large customer segments or business units Experience in structuring problems, taking initiative and following through with strong execution Qualifications: Unwavering “customer first” mentality with passion for service level and execution quality Proactive, creative, and flexible, using good judgment to accomplish tasks on schedule with minimal direction and supervision High-energy with positive attitude and bias for productivity in all aspects of the job Passionate and motivated to make a massive impact and work hard in a fast-paced environment Strong people skills: Ability to develop relationships with Sales VP’s, drive consensus, and results from cross-functional teams Detail oriented, willing to roll up his/her sleeves and get things done, cross-functionally, on time, and with quality results #LI-MR2 The minimum and maximum base salaries for this role are posted below; additionally, the role is eligible for bonus potential, equity and benefits.
