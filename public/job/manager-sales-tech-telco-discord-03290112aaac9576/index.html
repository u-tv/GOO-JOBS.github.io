Manager, Sales - Tech & Telco

🏢 discord 📍 San Francisco Bay Area, Los Angeles, New York 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Discord has a highly engaged community of millions of daily active users who use the platform for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does: play video games. Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming, and we are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to hang out before, during, and after playing games. We are seeking a dynamic Manager, Sales for the Tech & Telco verticals to join our growing sales team at Discord. This role is a people leadership position, responsible for leading a team of Account Executives while also driving strategic initiatives and revenue growth within Tech & Telco clients. As a Manager, Sales you will play a critical role in coaching and developing your team, developing and executing sales strategies, managing senior-level client relationships, and working cross-functionally to advocate for Tech & Telo needs. You will also be active in the market, representing Discord at key industry events and working directly with clients to help them achieve their marketing goals using our ad solutions. This role is ideal for a highly motivated leader with a strong background in digital advertising, media sales, and account strategy who thrives in a fast-paced, zero-to-one environment. What you'll be doing: Leadership & Team Management Lead, mentor, and develop a team of Account Executives to achieve individual and team revenue goals. Team size may grow based on business needs. Establish a high-performance sales culture with a focus on coaching, collaboration, and professional growth. Partner with the Sr. Director, Sales to refine sales processes, set KPIs, and optimize team performance. Sales & Revenue Growth Own and drive revenue goals for the Tech & Telco sales team, ensuring the successful execution of sales strategies. Support team in identifying new business opportunities while deepening relationships with existing clients and agency partners. Guide and support the team in negotiating and closing high-value deals. Assist team in upfronts and Joint Business Planning with strategic accounts Industry & Market Engagement Serve as a thought leader within the Tech & Telco industry, actively participating in industry events, conferences, and networking opportunities. Represent Discord’s ad solutions and value proposition in external forums and discussions. Stay ahead of industry trends, emerging technologies, and competitive dynamics, sharing insights with the team and cross-functional partners. Cross-Functional Collaboration Work closely with Product, Marketing, RevOps, and other internal teams to align sales efforts with product offerings and market needs. Provide feedback on ad solutions and customer needs, helping shape the future of Discord’s advertising business. Partner with Marketing to develop go-to-market strategies, case studies, and client success stories. What you should have: 8+ years of experience in digital advertising, media sales, or brand marketing, with at least 2 years of leadership experience (team lead, manager, or similar). Strong track record of meeting or exceeding revenue targets and driving successful sales strategies. Experience managing and coaching sales teams, with a focus on performance, growth, and culture. Deep understanding of advertising and media buying dynamics, particularly within the Tech & Telco industry. Proven ability to build and nurture senior-level client and agency relationships. Strong strategic thinking and problem-solving skills, with the ability to balance team leadership and individual contributions. Excellent presentation, negotiation, and communication skills. Ability to work in a fast-paced, entrepreneurial environment, adapting quickly to new challenges and opportunities. Willingness to travel for industry events and key client meetings. This position is US-based only. We are seeking candidates located in the San Francisco Bay Area (Alameda, Contra Costa, Marin, Napa, San Francisco, San Mateo, Santa Clara, Solano, and Sonoma counties), Los Angeles Metropolitan Area, or the New York Metropolitan Area, or who are willing to relocate.
