About this role: You'll own the end-to-end CRM lifecycle for Udemy's Consumer business, creating relevant communications that convert and retain our registered learners. In this role, you'll drive strategy, execution, and analysis across email, WhatsApp, push notifications, in-app messages, and SMS. You'll manage everything from ideation and scoping through testing, QA, and reporting while partnering with business stakeholders to optimize campaigns and guide best practices. Your work will directly impact learner activation, conversion, and retention through data-driven insights and flawless campaign execution. About your skills: Data-Driven Marketing Essential for leveraging data to drive CRM campaign decisions, optimization, and actionable insights. Marketing Strategy and Techniques Core competency for developing lifecycle campaign strategy and execution across email, SMS, push, WhatsApp channels. Business Planning Enables setting campaign goals, devising tactics, and aligning actions to achieve KPIs. What you'll be doing: Own Udemy's subscription and behavior-based promotion lifecycle campaigns by driving strategy, execution, and analysis to achieve activation, conversion, and retention goals. Ensure flawless execution across multiple campaigns throughout the entire process, including ideation, planning, briefing, segmentation definition, testing plans, QA, and reporting. Leverage data to provide actionable insights that inform future strategy and campaign optimization. Collaborate with business requesters to drive innovation, optimize campaigns, and advise on best practices and overall strategy. Ideate, strategize, and execute on testing ideas and new opportunities to improve process and metrics. Partner with cross-functional teams to ensure priorities are clear, actioned on, and optimal learner experience is achieved. Qualifications: 5+ years of email marketing experience. Demonstrated planning and executional skills including planning, writing briefs, segmentation, campaign setup, QA, testing, and reporting, with experience managing and building lifecycle programs. Background working with business partners to create email process or tool solutions for problems. Demonstrated history managing multi-layered projects across various cross-functional teams, hitting milestones and achieving KPIs, with experience managing multiple projects with flawless execution. Demonstrated analytical and problem-solving skills with experience leveraging data to drive decisions and translating results into actionable learnings. Experience with Braze a plus.
