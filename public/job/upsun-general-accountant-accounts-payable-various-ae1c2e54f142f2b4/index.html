Impact of a General Accountant - Accounts Payable As a General Accountant - Accounts Payable, you will keep our vendor payments accurate, controlled and on time across multiple entities and currencies as Upsun grows globally. You will join our Finance and Accounting team of 9 people, which provides accurate reporting, strong controls and operational rigour across the business. You will work closely with accounting and consolidation on month-end close and reconciliations, support the external audit, partner with FP&A on spend visibility, and manage relationships with suppliers and our banking and treasury partners. What to expect Own the procure-to-pay cycle: Manage AP across our 8 entities in Europe, North America and APAC, from vendor master data and invoice processing to employee expense reimbursements, preparing payment runs for execution with our Treasury team. Process accurately and on time: Match invoices and code to the correct GL accounts, cost centers and VAT/tax codes. Work across entities and jurisdictions: Record AP transactions in line with local statutory and group reporting requirements, including VAT/tax treatment, intercompany recharges and multi-currency postings. Manage employee expenses: Review and process expense reports and reimbursements in Payhawk, checking them against the expense policy and supporting receipts. Make sure they’re coded correctly with VAT recovered, and that card transactions are reconciled. Protect every payment: Apply AP and payment controls, including approval workflows, segregation of duties, vendor bank detail verification and duplicate-payment checks, with documentation ready for audit. Partner across the business: Be the go-to contact for the CFO, FP&A and employees across the company on supplier spend, payments and vendor questions. Own your part of the close: Reconcile the AP subledger to the GL, book accruals and prepayments, reconcile employee expense and card accounts, and support the monthly close and external audit. Resolve exceptions: Investigate and resolve invoice discrepancies, payment rejections, vendor statement differences and employee reimbursement queries across currencies and time zones. Improve daily workflows: Maximize use of our ERP and AP tool (Payhawk) to reduce manual work. Share ideas to automate routine AP tasks, and help the team put them in place. What you bring Accounting standards: Hands-on experience applying IFRS, French GAAP (PCG) or US GAAP to AP transactions, accruals and reconciliations, plus working knowledge of VAT/GST on purchases. AP operations experience: You have run AP workstreams independently in a multi-entity, multi-currency environment. Covering invoice processing, employee expenses, payment proposals and vendor reconciliations. Controls knowledge: You understand payment and internal controls (approvals, segregation of duties, vendor bank changes), and you apply them consistently. You know when to escalate a high-risk item. Systems skills: You are proficient with a modern ERP such as NetSuite, banking platforms, and AP automation tools. You also have advanced Excel or Google Sheets skills. Precision and remote collaboration: You are detail-oriented and deadline-driven, especially at month-end. You work well with others remotely across time zones. Bonus points CPA or an equivalent qualification, such as ACCA. Working proficiency in French. Experience using AP automation tools to reduce manual work.
