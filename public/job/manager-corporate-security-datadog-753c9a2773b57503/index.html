Manager, Corporate Security

🏢 datadog 📍 New York, New York, USA 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Manager, Corporate Security – New York City (HQ) based The Global Safety & Security Team falls under the Operations team that runs many day-to-day operational functions that help keep our employees safe, happy and productive. We work closely with leadership and employees to ensure that Datadog continues to scale rapidly, yet smoothly and remains a fantastic place to work. We’re seeking an experienced team member to join our growing global team and play a critical role in keeping Datadogs and our assets safe and secure. You will work directly with the Global Safety & Security Sr Manager - AMER and cross-functional teams to support safety and security operations across the enterprise. Ideal candidates will be passionate about security operations, program development, leadership, project management, customer service and collaboration. This position is based in our New York City headquarters and may require occasional domestic and international travel. The role will focus particularly on three core pillars: Headquarters Security, Global Security Operations Center operations and occasional management of the executive protection program At Datadog, we place value in our office culture - the relationships and collaboration it builds and the creativity it brings to the table. This position requires being in-office 5 days a week. What You’ll Do: Headquarters Security: Manage daily safety and security operations at Datadog’s New York City headquarters, including contract security personnel, access control, visitor management, internal events and incident response. Oversee contract security personnel and vendors, including staffing, training, post orders, service levels, performance reviews and corrective actions. while maintaining a safe, professional and welcoming employee experience. Coordinate with building management, property security and internal teams to coordinate lobby operations, emergency response, life-safety matters and headquarters security incidents.. Identify and address risks associated with operating in a high-profile urban environment, including protests, demonstrations, workplace violence, suspicious activity, property crime and other incidents that may affect Datadogs, visitors or business operations. Develop Security risk mitigation strategies. Support the implementation and management of access control, video surveillance, intrusion detection, visitor management and other technical security systems. Global Security Operations Center: Advance Datadog’s GSOC as its central hub for global security monitoring, incident intake, triage, escalation, communication and response coordination. Develop and maintain GSOC standard operating procedures, escalation protocols, incident classifications, notification requirements, service levels and quality standards to ensure consistent global operations. Oversee resources supporting GSOC operations, including staffing, training, performance management, vendor relationships and operational readiness. Monitor global events, emerging threats and incidents that could affect Datadog’s employees, offices, executives, travelers, events or operations. Ensure timely and accurate communication during critical incidents, including security alerts, situation reports, leadership briefings and updates to impacted teams. Review incident trends, performance metrics and recurring risks to identify opportunities to improve processes, tools and response capabilities. Lead post-incident reviews and track corrective actions through completion. Executive Protection: Manage and advance Datadog’s executive protection program, including protective intelligence, executive threat monitoring, travel and event security and response to identified or emerging threats. Partner with executives, executive assistants and internal stakeholders to provide security support that is discreet, practical and aligned with Datadog’s culture and business needs when necessary. Manage vendors, ensuring that providers meet Datadog’s expectations for professionalism, confidentiality, service and operational performance. Who You Are: Experienced in managing corporate safety and security operations, ideally within a fast-paced technology environment, with a focus on headquarters security, GSOC operations and executive protection. Skilled in managing all aspects of a physical security program, including contract security personnel, access control, video surveillance, visitor management, event security, investigations and emergency response. Experienced in developing or managing a GSOC, security operations center, command center or similar 24/7 monitoring and incident response capability. Knowledgeable about GSOC operating standards, including incident intake, triage, escalation, communications, reporting, service levels, performance metrics and after-action reviews. Experienced in executive protection program management, including protective intelligence, threat monitoring, executive travel, advance planning, event security and third-party provider management. Able to assess complex or incomplete information, make sound decisions under pressure and communicate clear recommendations during sensitive or rapidly evolving situations. Experienced in conducting physical security and threat assessments, identifying vulnerabilities and developing proportionate mitigation strategies. Knowledgeable about workplace violence prevention, behavioral threat assessment, insider threat, crisis management and active assailant preparedness. Experienced with open-source intelligence, protective intelligence and threat-monitoring tools used to identify and evaluate potential risks involving employees, executives, facilities and business operations. Knowledgeable about technical security systems, with experience supporting physical security design and reviewing access control, surveillance, visitor management and intrusion detection solutions. An exceptional written and verbal communicator who can effectively convey security information across all levels of the organization, with a specific focus on executives and senior leadership. A strong cross-functional partner who can build trusted relationships with internal teams, building management, law enforcement, emergency services, peer organizations and external security providers. Experienced in building and growing safety and security programs that can scale with organizational needs while maintaining a strong employee and customer service focus. Able to exercise a high level of integrity, discretion and confidentiality when managing sensitive incidents, investigations and executive-related matters. Comfortable managing multiple priorities and operating with autonomy in a dynamic environment where risks, business needs and operational requirements may change quickly. 8+ years of progressively responsible experience in corporate security, physical security, GSOC operations, executive protection, law enforcement, intelligence, military security or a related field. 3+ years of experience managing contract security personnel or external security teams Able and willing to travel domestically and internationally and participate in an after-hours on-call rotation. Professional certifications or specialized training in corporate security, executive protection, threat assessment, emergency management or business continuity are preferred.
