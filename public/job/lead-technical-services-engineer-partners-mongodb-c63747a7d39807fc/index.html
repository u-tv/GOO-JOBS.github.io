Lead, Technical Services Engineer - Partners

🏢 mongodb 📍 Bengaluru 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

MongoDB's Technical Services Engineering team uses exceptional problem-solving and customer service skills, along with deep technical experience, to advise customers and solve their complex MongoDB problems: consulting on application and schema design, optimizing performance and scalability, resolving production outages, designing and reviewing operational procedures such as business continuity plans, and explaining MongoDB internals. As Lead, Technical Services Engineer, Partners, you will mentor, train, and coach a team of Partner TSEs while remaining hands-on with the most complex partner and mutual-customer issues. You will work with the team Manager and take on day-to-day operational responsibility for the team, ensuring caseload is addressed accurately, quickly, and professionally, with a constant focus on customer and partner success. You will also collaborate with Customer Success Management, Solutions Architects, Partner teams, and other internal stakeholders on high-profile partners and customers as we migrate and support business-critical applications. We are looking to speak to candidates who are based in Bengaluru for our hybrid working model.
