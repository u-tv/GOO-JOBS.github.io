Accounting Manager

🏢 affirm 📍 Remote US 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

At Affirm, we exist for the moments that matter—giving people a clear, predictable way to pay over time, with no hidden fees, no surprises, and no tradeoffs on what matters most. About the Finance team The Finance team ensures Affirm remains financially sound and strategically positioned for growth. Our team manages financial planning, accounting, pricing, vendor management, tax, investor relations, and corporate development. We deliver timely insights, accurate reporting, and careful analysis to support decision-making. From day-to-day financial operations to major investments, we enable sustainable, informed growth by maintaining strong fiscal discipline. About the role Affirm is looking for an experienced Accounting Manager to support and manage our international accounting operations, with a focus on statutory accounting, statutory audits, financial statement preparation, local compliance requirements, transfer pricing, and monthly close activities. In this role, you will help ensure Affirm’s international entities are supported by accurate books and records, timely close execution, audit-ready documentation, and compliant local reporting. You will coordinate closely with internal Accounting, Tax, Treasury, Legal, Payroll, People, Finance, Technical Accounting and Financial Reporting teams, as well as external auditors, accounting firms, tax advisors, and statutory service providers. You will bring strong US GAAP and local statutory accounting awareness, excellent project management skills, and the ability to manage multiple workstreams across jurisdictions. The ideal candidate is detail-oriented, highly organized, proactive, and comfortable driving work through cross-functional partners and external providers. You will be expected to deliver reliable, high-quality results, communicate clearly, and improve processes that make statutory accounting, audit support, and transfer pricing activities more scalable over time. You are a strong problem solver and communicator who is excited to support Affirm’s continued growth and mission to deliver honest financial products that improve lives. What you’ll do Manage statutory accounting and monthly close activities for international entities, including journal entries, accruals, reconciliations, variance analysis, and close checklist completion. Coordinate with external accounting, payroll, tax, statutory, and audit providers to ensure accurate local books, timely deliverables, and compliance with local requirements. Lead statutory audit planning and execution, including auditor onboarding, timelines, status tracking, request management, issue resolution, and stakeholder coordination. Prepare and review statutory financial statements, disclosures, footnotes, supporting schedules, and tie-outs to trial balances, reconciliations, and audit evidence. Own PBC coordination and audit testing support by gathering, validating, reviewing, and submitting accurate and complete audit documentation. Partner with Technical Accounting, Financial Reporting, Tax, Legal, Treasury, Payroll, People, Finance, and external advisors on local accounting requirements, statutory adjustments, and reporting matters. Manage audit completion and filing activities, including open item resolution, final approvals, signed documents, filing coordination, and completed audit file retention. Own or support transfer pricing calculations, intercompany invoicing, related journal entries, reconciliations, settlements, and cross-functional alignment. Review trial balances, general ledger activity, reconciliations, and financial statement variances to identify risks, unusual items, required adjustments, and recurring issues. Strengthen process improvements and controls across statutory accounting and audit workflows by standardizing templates, improving review checklists, clarifying ownership, documenting key judgments, enhancing audit trails, and building repeatable processes that reduce manual effort, improve accuracy, and support audit readiness. Identify control gaps, process inefficiencies, and documentation weaknesses; partner with stakeholders to implement scalable solutions, improve close governance, and ensure processes remain effective as entities, requirements, and reporting complexity evolve. Use systems and tools such as NetSuite, Blackline, Excel, Workday, and AI-enabled workflows to improve efficiency, accuracy, and scalability. What we look for CPA professional accounting designation; or 6+ years of relevant professional accounting experience with a bachelor’s degree in business or finance. Public accounting experience preferred, especially audit experience with multinational or public company clients. Strong knowledge of US GAAP, statutory accounting, financial statement preparation, account reconciliations, journal entries, and month-end close. Experience supporting or managing statutory audits, including PBC coordination, audit testing, financial statement review, audit completion, and filing. Experience working with international entities, local accounting requirements, statutory reporting, and external service providers. Experience with transfer pricing calculations, intercompany invoicing, intercompany reconciliations, and cross-border accounting activity. Strong project management skills, with the ability to manage multiple audits, deadlines, entities, and stakeholders at once. Excellent attention to detail and ability to produce accurate, complete, audit-ready work under tight timelines. Ability to partner effectively with Technical Accounting, Tax, Treasury, Legal, Payroll, People, Finance, external auditors, and local accounting providers. Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to explain audit status, accounting issues, risks, and recommendations clearly. Advanced Excel skills, including lookups, conditional formulas, pivot tables, large dataset analysis, and financial statement support. Experience with accounting systems and close management tools; NetSuite and Blackline experience preferred. Strong ownership mindset and desire to improve processes, reduce manual work, and make accounting workflows more scalable over time. Comfortable operating in a fast-paced, rapidly changing environment with competing priorities.
