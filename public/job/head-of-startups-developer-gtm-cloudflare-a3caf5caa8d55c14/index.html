If you’re the type of person who values curiosity over bureaucracy, and that AI is a partner in solving tough problems to keep the Internet moving forward, you’ll fit right in. About the Role Startups are where the next great companies, and the next great Cloudflare accounts, are born. The builders choosing their infrastructure stack today at seed and Series A will be running production workloads at massive scale in three to five years. We need a leader who has lived that journey firsthand. We are hiring a Head of Startups to own Cloudflare's Developer Platform GTM strategy for the startup segment. Unlike traditional programs-operations roles, which are managed by our dedicated Startup Programs team, this role acts as the strategic GTM leader who sets the vision for how Cloudflare wins with startups, builds deep relationships across the VC and accelerator ecosystem, and drives the commercial trajectory from first developer sign-up through expansion and committed spend. You will combine your ecosystem fluency with rigorous GTM execution to build repeatable motions, enable the field, and translate startup signals into actionable recommendations for Product and Pricing. We are looking for someone who has been in the founder's seat. A former CRO or co-founder who has raised venture capital, navigated board dynamics, and understands what startup CTOs and CEOs actually care about when choosing infrastructure. You bring instant credibility with founders and VCs because you have been one. You combine that ecosystem fluency with the GTM rigor to build repeatable motions, enable the field, and translate startup signals into actionable recommendations for Product and Pricing. What You Will Do Define and own the startups GTM strategy. Build the operating model for how Cloudflare acquires, activates, and grows startup accounts, from early-stage free-tier adoption through expansion into committed spend. Set goals, scorecards, and cadences for the segment. Build and leverage ecosystem relationships. Develop deep, trust-based relationships with venture capital firms, accelerators, and incubators. Make Cloudflare the default infrastructure recommendation when VCs advise portfolio companies on their stack. Lead executive engagement with founders and CTOs. Be the face of Cloudflare's Developer Platform to the startup community. Engage directly with startup founders, CTOs, and heads of engineering to understand their workloads and guide them onto Cloudflare. Drive activation, expansion, and commercial structure. Use usage telemetry to identify high-potential startups, find next-best actions and expansion paths, and work with the account team, Deal Desk, and Pricing to structure startup-appropriate commercial agreements as companies grow. Lead Cross-Functional Startup Initiatives. Partner closely with Startup Programs, Marketing, and BDR teams to ensure credits, onboarding, campaigns, and event presence are fully aligned with the broader GTM strategy and conversion funnel. Enable the field on startup motions. Ship playbooks, talk tracks, deal patterns, and objection handling tailored to startup accounts. Run enablement sessions with Sales leadership and measure adoption and lift. Close the loop with Product. Translate what you see in the startup ecosystem into clear, quantified recommendations on positioning, packaging, pricing, limits, and roadmap. Startups are often the earliest signal of where the market is heading. Make sure Product hears it. Partner with Marketing and BDR Teams. Drive startup-targeted campaigns, events, and community engagement in coordination with Marketing and BDR. Build Cloudflare's presence at the startup events and communities that matter. Who You Are Direct experience as a Venture-Backed Founder, Co-Founder, CEO, or C-Level GTM Executive (e.g. CRO). You have personally built, scaled, and raised venture capital for a startup. This firsthand experience is a strict requirement for the role. Deep fluency in the VC ecosystem: You understand the firms, broad dynamics, and exactly what drives infrastructure decisions in venture-backed companies. 10+ years in technology sales or GTM, with significant time in startup and developer-platform ecosystems. A track record of converting developer adoption into commercial outcomes. You understand the full journey from free sign-up to enterprise commit. Executive presence and credibility with founder and CTO audiences. You speak their language because you have been one. Fluent in usage-based and consumption pricing models, as well as startup-stage commercial mechanics: credits, usage ramps, free-tier conversion, and early commit structures. Data-driven and analytical. Comfortable with product usage telemetry and Sheets/SQL-level analysis to size opportunities, identify high-potential accounts, and measure impact. A builder's mindset: you lead through influence, get cross-functional work done without relying on hierarchy, and thrive in fast-moving environments with ambiguity. Bonus Points Experience with AI-native startups or AI infrastructure workloads such as inference, agents, sandboxed code execution, and globally distributed applications. Prior experience building or leading startup ecosystem programs at a developer-platform or cloud company. Hands-on familiarity with Cloudflare's Developer Platform.
