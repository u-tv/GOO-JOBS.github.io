Headquarters: Manila, National Capital District, Philippines URL: https://www.facebook.com/VectorOutsourcingSolutionsPhil Salary: 45,000 PHP / monthly Job Description: Oversee the overall recruitment operations and ensure timely fulfillment of manpower requirements. Coordinate with department heads and hiring managers regarding current and future staffing needs. Establish recruitment priorities and monitor the progress of all open vacancies. Ensure proper delegation of tasks and accountability within the recruitment team. Monitor recruitment performance, productivity, and achievement of hiring targets. Review recruitment reports, hiring metrics, and vacancy updates. Identify recruitment challenges and implement solutions to improve hiring efficiency. Report recruitment progress, hiring challenges, team performance, and recommendations to the Chief People Officer/ HR Manager. Ensure that action items and concerns raised during HR meetings are properly monitored and addressed. Follow team and/or client’s proper procedures, policies, and methodologies as instructed. At least 3–5 years of recruitment experience, with at least 2 years in a supervisory or leadership role and volume hiring experience. Proven experience managing end-to-end recruitment operations, manpower planning, and hiring targets. Strong knowledge of recruitment strategies, sourcing channels, and talent acquisition best practices. Experience handling recruitment reports, hiring metrics, and applicant tracking systems (ATS). Excellent leadership, team management, coaching, and decision-making skills. Strong communication, negotiation, and stakeholder management skills. Knowledge of Philippine labor laws and recruitment compliance. Ability to manage multiple priorities and meet hiring deadlines in a fast-paced environment.
