FEQ327R430 Forward Deployed Program Lead About the team The Office of the Field CCO works with the Chief Marketing and Chief Revenue Officers of Databricks’ largest customers to redesign how their commercial organizations operate. We build a peer community of CMOs and CROs, partner with them on go-to-market transformation programs, and ensure those programs deliver measurable business results. The work cuts across sales, marketing, field engineering, and partner teams, and it runs on the customer’s own data. About the role You will help to shape and lead Databricks’ strategy for driving AI transformations with our customers. You will report directly to the Field CCO. This is a role with broad scope that will require you to collaborate across the business: you will set how we deliver transformation engagements, select and run our first 10 pilot accounts, and shape the reusable solutions field engineering builds. You will get work done through account teams, field engineering, field marketing, and enablement. What you’ll do Shape the reusable solutions Decide, with field engineering, which pilot solutions become reusable skills and agents (for example, an account research agent or a pipeline risk review) so the next 10 accounts can deploy in weeks rather than months. Own how the pieces fit together into a coherent offering for a CMO or CRO: which components combine for which workflow, and what the customer needs in place to use them. Pressure-test field engineering’s designs against real customer workflows and set the acceptance criteria an asset must meet before it goes to the field. Collaborate with our AI FDE team, product team, and FE regional leadership on the solutionization of workflows/companions. Set the delivery model Define how a GTM transformation engagement runs from first executive conversation to production: stages, roles across account, field engineering, and professional services teams, entry and exit criteria, and governance. Set the standard for account plans on transformation opportunities and review each plan with the account team before it goes to the customer. A strong plan names the executive sponsor, the two or three workflows in scope (for example, pipeline forecasting or campaign attribution), the data required, and the business result being targeted. Select and run the pilot program Define the selection criteria for the first 10 pilot accounts (executive sponsorship, data readiness, commercial potential, willingness to go public) and finalize the list with sales leadership. Program-manage the pilots. Set the operating cadence: weekly working sessions with account teams, monthly customer steering committees, and a quarterly review with the Field CCO. Define and track the metrics for each pilot. Measure business outcomes such as forecast accuracy, sales cycle length, or cost per qualified lead, alongside workflow adoption and consumption. Identify stalled pilots early and escalate with a specific recovery plan. Build the CMO and CRO community Partner with field marketing to launch and run CMO and CRO councils across North America and EMEA: membership criteria, agenda, and a format that gives members a reason to return. Partner with the Office of the CMO on thought leadership for flagship events such as Data + AI Summit and executive dinners, including keynote narratives, customer case studies drawn from the pilots, and point-of-view papers on how commercial organizations are changing. Enable the field Partner with ops and the enablement team to build the mechanisms that teach field engineers and sellers how the solutions work, when to position them, and how to deploy them: playbooks, demo environments, certification paths, and deal reviews. Minimum Qualifications Education: Bachelor's degree in a technical or related field, or equivalent professional experience. Experience: 5-7+ years in product development of highly technical products, product operations, GTM strategy, business operations, technical program management, or deploying technical/AI solutions with customers. Cross-Organizational Execution: 4+ years collaborating on cross-functional programs that drive committed outcomes across Product, Field Engineering, and Marketing, including at the executive level. Communication: Ability to communicate technical concepts to both non-technical and technically proficient audiences, including tailoring messages for potential customers and executives. AI & Automation: Demonstrated and creative use of AI-driven tooling and automated workflows. Sales/Marketing Expertise: Experience building or working with MarTech/SalesTech, including an understanding of marketing workflows, MMM, MROI, and GTM ontologies. Preferred Qualifications Hands-On AI Agent Deployment: Direct experience scoping, deploying, or evaluating AI agents or predictive analytics workflows (experience with product development for AI agents a plus). Some coding experience with React, TypeScript, SQL, or Go. MBA or equivalent professional experience. Experience with customer-facing strategy , GTM strategy, or value engineering at a high-growth data or AI software company (experience interfacing with end customers to influence software development preferred).
