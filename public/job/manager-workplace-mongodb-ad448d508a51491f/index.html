Manager, Workplace

🏢 mongodb 📍 San Francisco 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

MongoDB is seeking a proactive, hands-on Workplace Manager to lead the day-to-day operations of our San Francisco office while overseeing the employee experience and operational performance of our North America portfolio of satellite (a.k.a. “spoke”) offices. This is an operations-first role for someone who takes pride in making workplaces safe, welcoming, functional, and consistently well run. You will be the visible, service-minded point of contact for employees, visitors, vendors, and local leaders; responding to requests, coordinating workplace services, maintaining accurate records, and following issues through to resolution. Alongside daily operations, you will help cultivate a connected hybrid workplace in our San Francisco office and our Spoke Offices through thoughtful events, local communications, and culture-building initiatives. The ideal candidate is organized, resourceful, comfortable working across multiple locations, and energized by the combination of hospitality, facilities coordination, vendor management, budget management, event execution, and employee engagement.
