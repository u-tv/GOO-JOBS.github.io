Job Location - Bengaluru, India Work Mode - Hybrid (3 Days WFO) Internship Duration - 6 Months starting Jan 2027 About the Role : We are looking for ambitious, tech-forward HR interns to join our People Operations team for a 6-month intensive apprenticeship. This is not a typical administrative internship—it is a fast-paced, high-impact, end-to-end People Operations role designed for high-potential talent. During your tenure, you will work directly on critical People Operations functions, HR business partnering initiatives, and high-impact cross-functional projects. If you are extremely data-fluent, comfortable with ambiguity, and eager to leverage modern HR technology and AI to solve business challenges, this role is built for you. Key Responsibilities : As a People Operations/HRBP Intern, you will gain hands-on ownership across the employee lifecycle, including but not limited to: Employee Experience & Engagement: Assist in designing and executing employee engagement programs, pulse surveys, feedback loops, and culture-building initiatives to drive retention and satisfaction. Compliance & Labor Code Audits: Participate in internal HR compliance audits, statutory labor code alignments, and document verification processes to ensure seamless operational governance. Payroll & Benefits Management: Support monthly payroll processing workflows, leave & attendance reconciliations, vendor coordination, and employee benefits administration. Onboarding & Exit Management: Own end-to-end joining logistics, orientation sessions, documentation, exit interviews, and offboarding workflows to deliver a memorable employee journey.
