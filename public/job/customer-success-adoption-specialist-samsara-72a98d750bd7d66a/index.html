Customer Success Adoption Specialist

🏢 samsara 📍 Remote - CA; Remote - Chicago; Remote - Seattle; Remote - TX 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are Samsara (NYSE: IOT) is the pioneer of the Connected Operations™ Cloud, which is a platform that enables organizations that depend on physical operations to harness Internet of Things (IoT) data to develop actionable insights and improve their operations. At Samsara, we are helping improve the safety, efficiency and sustainability of the physical operations that power our global economy. Representing more than 40% of global GDP, these industries are the infrastructure of our planet, including agriculture, construction, field services, transportation, and manufacturing — and we are excited to help digitally transform their operations at scale. Working at Samsara means you’ll help define the future of physical operations and be on a team that’s shaping an exciting array of product solutions, including Video-Based Safety, Vehicle Telematics, Apps and Driver Workflows, and Equipment Monitoring. As part of a recently public company, you’ll have the autonomy and support to make an impact as we build for the long term. About the role: As an Adoption Specialist on our Mid-Market team, you are the technical expert our customers and our Account Managers call when a high-value feature needs to go from &quot;purchased&quot; to &quot;running in the operation.&quot; You work on a project-scoped basis: each engagement has a defined goal, a timeline, and clear commitments from both Samsara and the customer. Think of the structure of a solutions architect team, delivered as part of the Samsara relationship rather than as a paid service. You pair deep product and technical knowledge with project management discipline. You dig into a customer's use case, understand how their industry and operating procedures shape the way they use Samsara, and design a practical path to adoption. You translate highly technical concepts into plain language for non-technical audiences, and you hold customers accountable to the milestones that make a project succeed. You are also a force multiplier for the broader Customer Success organization. Through research, documentation, and coaching, you help Account Managers learn new products and level up their product knowledge, and you work cross-functionally with Support, Product, and Engineering to solve problems and bring customer insight back to the teams building Samsara. Account Managers continue to own the customer relationship and the renewal; you own the project and the adoption outcome. This is a remote position open to candidates residing in the US except the San Francisco Bay Metro Area, NYC Metro Area, and Washington, D.C. Metro Area. You should apply if: You want to impact the industries that run our world : Your efforts will result in real-world impact, helping to keep the lights on, get food into stores, and most importantly, ensure workers return home safely. You have an innate curiosity about how businesses work : One day you'll be learning how a waste management company dispatches its routes and the next you may be digging into the inner workings of a food distribution center. You want to understand customer operating procedures well enough to shape our products around them. You thrive the most when solving technical problems : You enjoy diagnosing why something isn't working, researching workarounds, and building a solution that fits a customer's real-world workflow. You deliver to a deadline : You run projects with clear plans, owners, and dates, and you are comfortable having direct conversations when a customer or internal partner falls behind. You are a natural teacher : You can take a complex product concept and make it clear for a fleet manager, a safety lead, or an executive, and you enjoy helping teammates build their own expertise. You are the architect of your own career : This is a new role on a team that is still shaping its future. You will have plenty of autonomy and opportunity to define how the role works and master your craft in a hyper-growth environment. You want to be with the best : At Samsara, we win together, celebrate together, and support each other. You will be surrounded by the best and brightest professionals out there. In this role, you will: Be a deep Subject Matter Expert on the Samsara product, API, integrations and AI capabilities. Lead project-scoped adoption engagements with Mid-Market customers, from scoping and kickoff through delivery, to increase adoption of Samsara's technical features and drive measurable customer outcomes. Hold customers accountable to agreed-upon commitments, and escalate early and constructively when a project is at risk. Complete deep research into customer use cases, current workflows, and workarounds, and recommend practical, well-supported solutions. Develop a working understanding of the industries we serve and how Samsara's products are tailored to each industry and each company's operating procedures. Support the broader team in learning new products and upleveling product knowledge through training, documentation, and hands-on enablement. Work cross-functionally with Support, Product, and Engineering to troubleshoot complex issues, and share customer insights that inform the product roadmap. Capture what you learn in reusable playbooks, templates, and best practices so the team can scale the impact of each project. Champion, role model, and embed Samsara's cultural principles (Focus on Customer Success, Build for the Long Term, Adopt a Growth Mindset, Be Inclusive, Win as a Team) as we scale globally and across new offices. Minimum requirements for the role: 3 to 5+ years of experience in a customer-facing technical role such as implementation, solutions engineering, technical customer success, or professional services, ideally in SaaS, IoT, technology, or logistics. Bachelor's degree from a 4-year institution, or equivalent practical experience. Strong technical aptitude, with a track record of quickly learning complex products and troubleshooting issues independently. Demonstrated ability to manage multiple projects and deliver to deadlines, with clear planning and follow-through. Proven track record of communicating technical concepts clearly and empathetically to non-technical audiences, from day-to-day users to executives. Solutions-oriented with strong research and problem-solving skills. Strong communication, presentation, and organizational skills. Familiarity with Salesforce, Gainsight, Gong or similar GTM tools. Comfort with a rapidly changing, high-growth environment. An ideal candidate also has: Strong bias towards action , has the ability to think big, with insistence on high standards. AI First : Experience using AI tooling to drive efficiency and outcomes. API and Integration Experience : Experience building with APIs, and/or working with and building integrations. Project Management Experience : Experience running scoped, project-based customer engagements. Technical Proficiency : Experience with building deep knowledge of a suite of technical products (bonus points if familiar with integrating hardware with software solutions).
