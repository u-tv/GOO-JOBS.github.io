Learn more about our flexible approach to where we work. Overview We're hiring a Senior Software Engineer II to be the backend lead for Instacart's Pricing & Incentives team. The team is a mix of ML and backend engineers building the systems behind shopper pay and incentives. We use ML to make real-time, financially critical, compliance-sensitive decisions that keep shopper pay fair, protect contribution profit, and keep orders on time. We also own Shopper Incentives (getting shoppers online in the right place at the right time), Shopper Acquisition, and Tip Recommendations. The team drives roughly a third of the company's annual contribution profit goal, so your work will show up directly in business results. About the Job Serve as the backend lead for the pricing system, a financially critical platform where correctness and reliability matter Design, build, and operate scalable backend systems that power real-time pricing, shopper pay, and incentive decisions Build and improve ML infrastructure in close partnership with ML engineers, data scientists, and product managers Proactively identify gaps in system design, reliability, and correctness, and drive them to resolution Own systems end to end, from design and development through testing, deployment, and operations Grow into the go-to expert on the pricing stack, with a clear path toward Staff Engineer About You Minimum Qualifications 5+ years of professional software engineering experience, including experience at the senior level Experience building and operating scalable, production backend systems Experience owning financially critical or high-stakes systems where correctness matters A track record of driving business outcomes, not just shipping code Preferred Qualifications Experience working closely with ML engineers or on ML infrastructure Experience building real-time systems Experience in pricing, payments, marketplace, or other financially sensitive domains #LI-Remote Instacart provides highly market-competitive compensation and benefits in each location where our employees work.
