D2B: Marketing Coordinator (Dental)

Job Description

Headquarters: Manila, Metro Manila, Philippines URL: https://jobs.workable.com/company/dcaVtKQqXr9FwBNppNCBH9/jobs-at-d2b Position: Marketing Coordinator Salary: 2000 NZD Working Hours & Conditions: Part-Time , Hours To be confirmed Holidays : To follow ABOUT THE COMPANY: The Compnay is a premier, locally owned multi-disciplinary dental practice in Henderson, West Auckland. With over 20 dental professionals under one roof, we offer everything from family and general dentistry to high-value orthodontics, dental implants, oral surgery, and sedation dentistry for anxious patients. As our very first offshore team member, you will play a foundational role in driving patient acquisition and building our digital lead-generation engine. ROLE OVERVIEW: The Company is seeking a capable, hands-on Marketing Coordinator to support the Director of Marketing in executing marketing activity and driving new patient acquisition across our group of dental practices in New Zealand. The role combines digital marketing execution, lead generation support, content creation and marketing coordination. We are looking for someone who is proactive, commercially minded and comfortable working independently in a remote environment. RESPONSIBILITIES: Key Responsibilities Lead Generation and Digital Marketing Support marketing campaigns designed to generate new patient enquiries and bookings. Assist with lead generation initiatives across Google, social media, email and website channels. Monitor campaign performance and identify opportunities to improve lead generation and conversion. Coordinate with external digital agencies on campaign execution and reporting. Email Marketing and CRM Create, schedule and manage EDMs using Mailchimp. Develop email campaigns to generate enquiries, encourage bookings and re-engage patients. Support database segmentation and campaign targeting. Social Media and Content Manage and schedule organic social media content across Facebook and Instagram. Create engaging marketing content, captions and promotional posts. Edit basic reels and short-form video content using Canva or CapCut. Use AI tools such as ChatGPT to support content creation and marketing efficiency. Ensure content aligns with the company group’s brand guidelines. Website and Content Management Update website content, promotions and practice information. Write and edit website copy, blogs and patient communications. Support SEO and website conversion improvements. Marketing Coordination and Reporting Track marketing activity, budgets and campaign expenditure. Assist with monthly new patient reporting and marketing performance analysis. Create marketing collateral, including flyers, posters and promotional materials. Maintain marketing calendars, campaign schedules and project trackers. Liaise with practice managers and external agencies to coordinate marketing activity. 2+ years’ experience in marketing coordination, digital marketing or lead generation. Experience supporting campaigns focused on generating leads, enquiries or customer acquisition. Familiarity with digital marketing channels, including Google Ads and Meta Ads, without necessarily managing campaigns directly. Experience with Mailchimp or similar email marketing platforms. Strong Canva skills and basic video editing capability. Experience updating websites and creating digital content. Excellent written English and copywriting skills. Strong organisational, analytical and reporting skills. Comfortable using AI tools to support marketing execution. Proactive, self-sufficient and able to manage priorities independently. Healthcare, dental or professional services marketing experience would be advantageous. To apply: https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/d2b-marketing-coordinator-dental
