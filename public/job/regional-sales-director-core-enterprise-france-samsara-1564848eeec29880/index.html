At Samsara, we are helping improve the safety, efficiency and sustainability of the physical operations that power our global economy. This isn't a role where you sit above the deals</li> <li>Build the commercial muscles (cross-sell, multi-product attach, stakeholder mapping) that will drive durable growth as we expand our footprint across France's largest accounts</li> <li>Run a structured operating rhythm of pipeline reviews, deal inspection and forecasting</li> <li>Coordinate ADRs, Marketing, Solutions Engineering and partners around your team's priorities, influencing functions you do not directly own</li> <li>Hire, develop and lead an inclusive, engaged and high-performing team, developing AEs into strong Core Enterprise sellers</li> <li>Partner closely with your peer Regional Sales Directors, Samsara's wider leadership and Customer Success to ensure your team has the support it needs to win</li> <li>Champion, role model and embed Samsara's cultural principles (Focus on Customer Success, Build for the Long Term, Adopt a Growth Mindset, Be Inclusive, Win as a Team) as we scale globally and into new markets</li> </ul> <h3><strong>Minimum requirements for the role</strong></h3> <ul> <li>Three or more years of proven success leading new business sales at enterprise level, with a track record of revenue delivery and overachievement against target</li> <li>Has built something rather than inherited it, with experience building a market or a new sales function from scratch</li> <li>Full team lifecycle experience: has recruited, ramped and retained a team, and can show where each happened</li> <li>Direct experience selling into enterprise companies at comparable deal sizes and cycle lengths</li> <li>Experience establishing rigour in value-based selling, using methodologies such as MEDDIC or Command of the Message</li> <li>Demonstrated ability to coordinate supporting functions to drive pipeline and close business</li> <li>Fluent French and access to our Paris office</li> </ul> <h3><strong>An ideal candidate also has</strong></h3> <ul> <li>Experience at a high-growth technology company, helping to build or refine the enterprise go-to-market playbook rather than inheriting an established one</li> <li>A coaching-first leadership style, with a record of developing AEs from good to exceptional</li> <li>Intelligence and genuine coachability: you learn fast, welcome feedback and apply it</li> <li>A competitive, entrepreneurial streak: you create momentum without needing to own every function involved</li> <li>Comfort operating in a fast-moving, scaling organisation where not everything is perfectly defined yet, and where you are expected to build process while delivering results</li> <li>A blend of strategic thinking and tactical intensity: you can set the market strategy and also step into a deal review to show a rep where their qualification is weak</li> </ul><div
