Duel.com: Product Owner

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Malta Duel is a protest against everything wrong in gambling. The purest, fairest casino ever built. The Vatican of gamba. We're waging war on scammers, fake bets, and dark patterns — and we need a Product Owner who wants to burn the old playbook down. The role You own the product backlog across casino, sportsbook, and games. You translate vision into stories, stories into shipped features, and shipped features into better player experiences. You work directly with engineering and design — no layers, no bureaucracy, no nonsense. If you want to build the same casino everyone else has built, move on. What you'll do Own and prioritise the backlog across all platforms Turn product vision into clear user stories and tasks Monitor player engagement and performance — find what's broken, fix it, find what's missing, build it Gather and define requirements with stakeholders, functional and non-functional Drive growth through ideas that are actually different What you'll need 3+ years in a product ownership or management role Gambling or gaming industry experience preferred Comfortable working with both technical and commercial teams Experience with distributed, remote-first development teams Clear communicator, strong prioritisation instincts, ownership mindset Come build something pure.
