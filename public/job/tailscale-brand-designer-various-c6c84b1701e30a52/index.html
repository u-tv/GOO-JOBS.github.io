About Tailscale Tailscale is making safe connection effortless by delivering software that makes it easy to securely interconnect people and their devices, no matter where they are. From hobbyists to multinational corporations, teams of every size use Tailscale each day to protect their networks, share access to internal tools, and more. We're building a future for the Internet that's easy, sensible, and safe, like it used to be. Founded in 2019 and fully distributed, we're backed by Accel, CRV, Insight, Heavybit, and Uncork Capital. About the Role Tailscale is looking for an experienced Brand Designer to help bring our brand to life across every customer touchpoint. You'll create thoughtful, high-quality creative that helps people understand our products, builds trust with technical audiences, and supports some of our most important marketing initiatives. This role reports to the Team Lead and partners closely with Brand, Growth, Web, Content, Product Marketing, and Developer Relations. You'll own projects from concept through execution, contribute to the evolution of our design systems, and help us scale creative output without sacrificing quality. We're looking for someone who cares deeply about craft, thrives in collaborative environments but is able to work autonomously while requiring minimal direction, and enjoys solving complex communication problems through thoughtful design. Key Responsibilities Own brand design projects from creative brief through final delivery with limited guidance Design high-quality creative assets across web, campaigns, events, social media, presentations, product marketing, and internal communications Apply and thoughtfully extend Tailscale's visual identity across new formats and channels Create reusable templates, design systems, and creative processes that improve consistency and efficiency Partner closely with Growth, Web, Product Marketing, Content, and Developer Relations to deliver cohesive customer experiences Present design concepts and rationale to stakeholders and thoughtfully incorporate feedback Balance multiple projects while consistently delivering polished, production-ready work Contribute ideas that improve the quality, scalability, and effectiveness of our creative work What We Are Looking For 3+ years of professional experience in brand, marketing, or visual design Strong portfolio demonstrating exceptional design foundations: color theory, accessibility, typography, layout, and storytelling Fluency in Figma and Adobe Creative Cloud Experience designing for digital marketing, websites, campaigns, and brand systems Strong understanding of production-ready design across digital and print Excellent communication and presentation skills Ability to manage multiple projects and priorities with minimal oversight Experience collaborating cross-functionally with marketers, writers, developers, and other designers Strong attention to detail with a high bar for quality and consistency Comfortable giving and receiving constructive design feedback Actively seeks opportunities to refine processes, improve project organization, and make collaboration more effective through thoughtful recommendations. Curious by nature, with a desire to learn about the technical world of networking.
