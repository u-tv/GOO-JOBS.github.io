About the Role We’re looking for a Manager of GTM Engineering to help build the data and activation engine behind Tebra’s next generation of outbound growth. You’ll help create and evolve a comprehensive Total Addressable Market database of independent healthcare practices and related businesses, combining public, proprietary, and third-party data into actionable segments, signals, and outbound workflows. This role sits at the intersection of data engineering, Clay, GTM systems, and outbound strategy—and is ideal for someone who can both architect the system and get hands-on building the workflows that turn market intelligence into a pipeline. Your Area of Focus Build and evolve Tebra’s TAM database , creating a reliable and actionable view of independent healthcare practices, providers, billing companies, and other priority market segments. Own sophisticated Clay workflows and architecture , including enrichment waterfalls, provider and vendor integrations, scoring logic, custom signals, AI research, segmentation, and activation. Integrate data from multiple sources , including public datasets, APIs, enrichment vendors, web scraping, internal systems, and proprietary data, into a scalable GTM data model. Manage and develop two GTM Engineers, spanning database infrastructure and Clay/AI automation; translate business priorities into a clear technical roadmap, allocate work across the team, remove blockers, and ensure solutions are scalable, reliable, and tied to measurable GTM outcomes. Design and maintain the connection between Clay, Snowflake, Salesforce, Outreach, and other GTM systems , ensuring data can move reliably from source to enrichment to segmentation to activation. Build data quality and governance mechanisms , including deduplication, validation, suppression logic, enrichment freshness, field mapping, monitoring, and auditability. Develop and operationalize market signals that improve how we identify, prioritize, and engage prospective customers, including firmographic, technographic, behavioral, competitive, and other trigger-based signals. Partner with Marketing program owners and Sales leaders to co-develop outbound strategy , translating ICP, campaign, and market hypotheses into executable targeting, enrichment, and activation workflows. Design and test outbound motions that use improved data, AI, personalization, and trigger-based targeting to increase relevance and conversion while maintaining appropriate unit economics. Own the technical architecture behind outbound experimentation , enabling the organization to quickly test new audiences, signals, channels, personalization strategies, and campaign concepts. Monitor the economics of the GTM data stack , including enrichment usage, API costs, Clay credits, vendor overlap, cost per enriched record, and other infrastructure costs. Build monitoring and alerting for data pipeline failures, enrichment gaps, sync issues, activation problems, and other conditions that could impact outbound performance. Create scalable systems rather than one-off lists , designing architecture, documentation, and governance that allow Tebra’s TAM data and outbound capabilities to compound over time. Your Professional Qualifications 5+ years of relevant experience across GTM engineering, growth engineering, data engineering, revenue operations, outbound systems, technical growth, or a related discipline. Deep, hands-on experience with Clay , including building production-scale workflows, enrichment waterfalls, APIs, custom logic, scoring, AI enrichment, and downstream activation. Strong proficiency with SQL, APIs, webhooks, and scripting using Python, JavaScript, or similar languages . Experience working with cloud data warehouses such as Snowflake and understanding data modeling, ingestion, transformation, and data quality concepts. Experience integrating and operationalizing large or complex datasets from sources such as public registries, enrichment providers, scraping platforms, and third-party APIs. Experience with GTM systems such as Salesforce, Outreach, ZoomInfo, Marketo, Slack, and related enrichment or activation tools . Strong understanding of modern outbound strategy, including segmentation, ICP development, account prioritization, enrichment, trigger-based outbound, personalization, and campaign measurement. Experience building or supporting web scraping and external data collection workflows using tools such as Apify, SerpAPI, browser automation, or similar technologies. Strong understanding of data quality, deduplication, matching, governance, monitoring, and maintaining production-grade data systems. Ability to think in systems and unit economics, balancing technical sophistication with cost, reliability, speed, and commercial value. Strong analytical and commercial judgment, with the ability to connect data architecture and technical decisions to pipeline creation and revenue outcomes. Ability to work closely with Marketing, Sales, Analytics, and technical teams and translate between GTM strategy and implementation.
