Senior Energy Markets Analyst, PJM

🏢 Voltus 📍 US Only 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Voltus runs demand response programs across North American wholesale markets and utilities. As the Senior Energy Markets Analyst for PJM, you will own our PJM utility programs end to end. You will run one established utility program and help launch a second, newer one. You will also support Product and Partnerships in sizing and designing the next set of PJM utility programs, where much of our future revenue and margin sits. This is a high-autonomy role. You will manage your own work, with your manager setting priorities and reviewing results rather than directing day-to-day tasks. For each program you own, you will be the go-to expert inside Voltus and the main contact for the utility. What you will do Run our PJM utility programs end to end: registrations, nominations, dispatch, performance calculations and settlements. Help launch a new program and identify the next ones with Product and Partnerships. Advise Product and Partnerships on program design, opportunity sizing and utility proposals. You will not draft formal regulatory filings. Explain program rules in plain language to Sales, customers and Dispatch Ops, and write to utilities and PJM on registration and settlement issues. Support PJM - ELRP (Load Management) registrations starting in 2028. Guide and review the work of co-op and junior analysts. This role has no direct reports. What success looks like Within 60 days, you know the established program well enough to run it without the team's involvement. Registrations, nominations, tests, events and quarterly settlements are on time and accurate, and your manager reviews rather than does. New program registrations are submitted on time with no missed deadlines, and enrolled MW is at or near target. Baseline and performance methodology is agreed with Engineering and Product before the first dispatch. A written playbook for the new program exists that someone else could pick up. Product, Partnerships, Dispatch Ops and utility contacts come to you directly. Your analysis has informed a program design discussion with Product and Partnerships. What you bring Required 3 to 5 years of hands-on energy markets experience in demand response or a closely related area (registrations, performance calculations, settlements), in any ISO or utility program. Advanced Excel or Google Sheets: trackers, baseline-versus-metered-load calculations, and settlement reconciliations that others can audit. Basic SQL: read-only queries with joins, aggregations and filters to pull site, interval and dispatch data. Basic Python, with familiarity with Pandas, to process interval data across many sites and automate repetitive registration and settlement prep. Clear written communication, including plain-language explanations of program rules and professional correspondence with utilities and ISOs. Ownership: you make judgment calls on ambiguous program rules, know when to escalate, and build process where none exists. Availability during core Eastern hours and during test and event windows. PJM, the utilities and all program deadlines run on Eastern time. Preferred PJM experience, in this order of relevance: DR programs (ELRP, utility DR programs in PJM states); settlements and performance calculations; capacity market basics (PLCs, nominations, delivery year calendar); wholesale market operations.
