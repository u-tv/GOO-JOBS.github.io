Staff Offensive Security Engineer, Proactive threat

🏢 stripe 📍 Remote - US 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world's largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team The Proactive Threat team finds vulnerabilities and security weaknesses across Stripe's systems, applications, networks, and cloud infrastructure before adversaries do. We're a hybrid offensive function. We run penetration tests, emulate real-world threat actors in red team operations, and work closely with our defensive security teams to validate detection capabilities and improve Stripe's overall security posture. We are builders first. We build custom tooling, automation frameworks, and internal platforms that scale our offensive work and make our assessments repeatable and high-fidelity. Increasingly, that includes AI agents that find and validate vulnerabilities at a scale no human team could reach. We believe the best offensive security engineers are equal parts hacker and engineer. The team is distributed and works regularly with security, engineering, and product stakeholders across Stripe, including teams in Europe and Asia. What you'll do As a Staff Offensive Security Engineer on the Proactive Threat team, you'll set the technical direction for how Stripe tests itself the way a real adversary would. You'll be the team's most senior offensive practitioner. You'll stay deeply hands-on: you'll lead the hardest engagements yourself and write the tooling that makes everyone around you more effective. You'll own the strategy and the operating foundation of our red team program, including its tradecraft, infrastructure, rules of engagement, and coordination with detection and response. You'll shape how offensive security scales at Stripe through automation and agentic systems. Your work will span multiple teams: you'll partner with detection engineering, incident response, vulnerability management, and product engineering so that what we find makes Stripe measurably harder to attack.
