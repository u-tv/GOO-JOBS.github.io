About the Role Pinterest is becoming a performance-first ads platform, moving beyond its roots as a predominantly brand and social destination. Ads Quality sits at the center of that change. Personalization (Ranking) and Marketplace (Delivery) decide which ad each Pinner sees, where it shows, and what it earns. They balance three outcomes over the long term: Pinner satisfaction, advertiser return, and Pinterest revenue. Success requires solving some of the hardest problems in ads marketplaces. These include measuring and improving ad relevance at scale, delivering ROAS that wins performance budgets, and building a yield system that balances first-party demand with high-quality monetization of every ad slot. We’re looking for a Director of Product Management who has done this before: someone who has built and scaled ads ranking, delivery, or marketplace systems and turned them into measurable business results. Reporting to the VP of Product Management, Ads Quality, you’ll own the multi-year strategy and rebuild the Ads Quality product organization. You’ll lead a team of five PMs, scaling to 10–12 across two pods, and partner with an engineering organization of nearly 200. What You’ll Do Set the product strategy. Define the multi-year technical and business strategy for ads personalization and marketplace. Identify and size new opportunities, and turn strategy into sequenced roadmaps, investment cases, success metrics, and decision gates. Own the value equation. Improve Pinner Value through better ad relevance and personalization, Advertiser Value as measured by ROAS, and revenue per mille (RPM), all with a lifetime-value lens. Define how relevance and satisfaction are measured. Own delivery end to end. Drive discovery through launch and scale for new products and platform capabilities. Set architecture and quality standards, unblock critical technical and operating risks, and make sure the team has reliable measurement, experimentation, and operational readiness. Build the team. Assess and reshape the current team. Hire senior product leaders for the Personalization and Marketplace pods, and coach managers and senior ICs. Establish clear ownership and operating rhythms, and build a culture of urgency, rigor, and customer focus. Lead cross-functionally. Align Product, Engineering, Data Science, Design, Measurement, Ad Formats, Buying Platform, Ads Integrity, and Consumer teams around a shared plan. Make trade-offs explicit, drive decisions through ambiguity, and communicate progress, risks, and outcomes to executive leadership. Your Year One Priorities: Accelerate Pinterest’s shift to a performance-oriented ads platform. Prove ROAS delivery through new bidding types, much better personalization, and stronger conversion performance. Deliver a more sophisticated, efficient, and transparent yield system that balances first-party demand with high-quality monetization of every ad slot. What We’re Looking For 12+ years of product management experience, including senior leadership roles building and scaling ads products. Proven track record of improving ads performance and revenue outcomes through ranking, delivery, or marketplace systems. Deep expertise in at least one of the team’s core technical areas: Personalization and relevance: ML-driven recommendation and ads delivery systems, including relevance measurement, or Marketplace and ranking: auctions, bidding, pricing, and yield optimization at scale. Fluency in balancing multi-sided marketplace outcomes (user, advertiser, platform) and making long-term trade-offs. Excellent collaboration skills. You excel at finding common ground with partners who have different objectives to create shared goals. Strong commercial instincts. You’re comfortable driving advertiser ROAS and revenue outcomes, not just product metrics. Background in marketplaces, ecommerce, or social/ads platforms strongly preferred. Experience assessing, restructuring, and scaling product organizations, including hiring senior PM leaders. Executive presence, with experience communicating strategy, trade-offs, and risk directly to senior leadership. Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in a related field, or equivalent experience.
