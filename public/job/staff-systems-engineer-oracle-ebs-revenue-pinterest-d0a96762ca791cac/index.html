Are you passionate about building and scaling enterprise finance systems that power global business operations? Join Pinterest’s IT Enterprise Systems team, where you’ll play a key role in evolving our Oracle EBS and Finance technology landscape. This is an opportunity to drive meaningful impact by partnering with Revenue, Finance,, and cross-functional teams to deliver reliable, scalable, and business-critical ERP solutions that support Pinterest’s continued growth. What You’ll Do: Design end-to-end solutions across billing, receivables, collections, subledger, and related finance processes to improve business resilience and operational efficiency. Architect and enhance integrations across Oracle EBS Financials, SaaS applications, and cloud platforms that support revenue operations, reporting, and downstream financial processes. Use AI thoughtfully as a force multiplier for systems analysis, documentation, and operational efficiency, while applying sound judgment, governance, and risk awareness in business-critical Finance systems environments. Partner with Revenue and Finance business stakeholders to understand priorities, assess current-state challenges, and define scalable future-state solutions. Help define and execute the Oracle ERP roadmap in partnership with product owners, systems managers, and business leaders, aligning technology investments with strategic business priorities. Evaluate and improve existing finance and ERP processes, identifying opportunities to streamline workflows, strengthen controls, and increase operational efficiency. Ensure strong SDLC, change management, and SOX-compliant processes across the ERP ecosystem, with a focus on auditability, stability, and risk mitigation. Collaborate with Managed Service Partners and internal teams to promote operational excellence, support best practices, and continuously improve service delivery. Partner across Finance and IT to support broader ERP modernization and cloud transformation initiatives. What We’re Looking For: Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Information Systems, Engineering, Business Systems, Computer Science, or equivalent practical experience. 12+ years of progressive experience implementing, supporting, and enhancing Oracle EBS in large, complex enterprise environments. Deep expertise in Oracle EBS Financials, including modules such as Accounts Receivable (AR), Cash Management (CM), Subledger Accounting (SLA), EBTax, and Advanced Collections. Familiarity with Oracle Fusion Cloud Financials is a plus. Ability to use AI responsibly in enterprise environments, with attention to security, privacy, governance, and the validation of AI-assisted outputs before use in production or business-critical workflows. Strong functional understanding of end-to-end Finance processes, accounting operations, and ERP controls in a public-company or highly regulated environment. Experience leading Oracle EBS configuration, enhancements, issue resolution, release coordination, and cross-functional systems initiatives. Solid understanding of Oracle EBS integrations, enterprise application architecture, and cloud integration frameworks. Strong knowledge of SOX controls, audit readiness, compliance requirements, and change management practices within ERP and financial systems. Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with proven success working across Finance, IT, vendors, and global cross-functional teams. Strong problem-solving skills, systems thinking, and a track record of delivering reliable, scalable, and business-focused enterprise solutions. Join us to be part of a forward-thinking team at Pinterest, where your expertise will help lay the foundation for future financial innovation!
