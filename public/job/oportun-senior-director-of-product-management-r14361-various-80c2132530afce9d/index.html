Oportun: Senior Director of Product Management (R14361)

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote - US Who We Are Oportun (Nasdaq: OPRT) is a mission-driven financial services company that puts its members' financial goals within reach. With intelligent borrowing, savings, and budgeting capabilities, Oportun empowers members with the confidence to build a better financial future. Since inception, Oportun has provided more than $22.7 billion in responsible and affordable credit, saved its members more than $2.5 billion in interest and fees, and helped its members set aside an average of more than $1,800 annually. The Opportunity We’re looking for a Senior Director of Product Management to lead strategy, growth, and execution across key digital products and member experiences. You’ll help shape how Oportun serves members throughout their financial journey while building products that create meaningful customer value and support sustainable business growth. This is a hands-on leadership role. You’ll set product direction, develop roadmaps, lead discovery, guide cross-functional execution, and coach product managers while staying close to customers, data, and product performance. Success requires strong strategic judgment, deep customer empathy, and the ability to move comfortably between long-term vision and detailed execution. At Oportun, we move fast and we hold each other to a high bar. If you care about doing your best work while actually moving the needle for real people, you'll fit right in. What You'll Do Your day-to-day You’ll define product vision, strategy, and priorities that connect member needs with Oportun’s business objectives. You’ll own and evolve a portfolio of digital products and experiences, balancing growth, engagement, retention, customer value, and business performance. You’ll build outcome-focused roadmaps and make clear investment decisions across existing products, new capabilities, and emerging opportunities. You’ll develop a deep understanding of member behavior, friction points, and unmet needs through research, product analytics, funnel analysis, cohort performance, and experimentation. You’ll lead product initiatives from discovery and validation through launch, measurement, iteration, and scale. You’ll establish clear hypotheses, success measures, and performance metrics and use results to guide future product decisions. You’ll partner closely with Design, Engineering, Analytics, Marketing, Operations, Risk, Compliance, and other teams to resolve trade-offs and deliver strong member experiences. You’ll balance customer experience, growth, product economics, risk, regulatory requirements, and operational complexity when making product decisions. You’ll coach and develop product managers, set clear expectations, and strengthen product judgment, ownership, and accountability across the team. You’ll communicate product strategy, priorities, performance, risks, and trade-offs clearly to senior leaders and cross-functional partners. You’ll bring ideas for how we can improve our products, processes, and ways of working, proactively, not just when asked. You’ll look for practical ways to use AI to improve product discovery, analysis, experimentation, decision-making, and team productivity. You'll look for ways to use AI tools thoughtfully — to work smarter, reduce manual work, and make better decisions faster Who You Are The right fit looks like this You’re a strategic and hands-on product leader who combines strong customer judgment with data, business acumen, and disciplined execution. You know how to build products that customers value while making thoughtful trade-offs across growth, economics, risk, and operational complexity. You’re comfortable leading through ambiguity, making decisions with incomplete information, and creating clarity across teams. You communicate directly, build strong partnerships, and stay close enough to the work to understand what is working, what is not, and where the team should focus next. You’re also a strong people leader. You know how to coach product managers, raise the quality of product thinking, and create accountability for measurable outcomes. You're comfortable navigating complexity and don't wait for perfect information to move forward. You communicate clearly, give direct feedback, and expect the same in return. What You Bring Skills & experience Required 8+ years of product management experience, with increasing responsibility for product strategy, roadmap ownership, and measurable customer and business outcomes. Significant experience building and growing consumer digital products. Proven experience owning engagement, retention, growth, monetization, or other meaningful product-performance outcomes. 3+ years of experience directly managing and developing product managers. Strong track record leading cross-functional teams from discovery through launch, measurement, and iteration. Experience setting product strategy and translating it into focused, executable roadmaps. Strong product judgment and a high standard for simple, intuitive, and customer-centered experiences. Strong analytical skills, including experience with funnel analysis, cohort performance, product economics, experimentation, and performance measurement. Experience evolving established products while balancing current customer needs with new strategic priorities. Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to align senior stakeholders around priorities, risks, and trade-offs. Ability to operate effectively in fast-moving environments with competing priorities and significant ambiguity. Nice to Have Experience in fintech, financial services, lending, banking, payments, savings, budgeting, investing, or financial wellness. Experience building consumer mobile products or other high-engagement digital experiences. Experience with subscription, recurring-use, or habit-forming consumer products. Experience serving customers managing complex or variable financial needs. Experience working in a regulated environment and incorporating Risk, Legal, and Compliance requirements into product experiences. Experience leading products through periods of growth, transformation, or significant strategic change. Experience using AI tools to improve product research, analysis, experimentation, documentation, or team productivity. Our AI Mindset We build with AI. We lead with judgment. AI isn't a feature here. It's how we work. We're excited about what AI makes possible, and we're looking for people who share that curiosity. You don't need to be an expert. You need to be open to experimenting , willing to learn, and able to apply new tools in ways that actually move the needle. In this role, you'll be encouraged to explore AI tools, find practical use cases for your function, and bring ideas for working smarter. We believe the best outcomes come when human judgment and AI capability work together.
