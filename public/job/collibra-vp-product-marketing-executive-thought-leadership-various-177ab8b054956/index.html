Collibra: VP, Product Marketing & Executive Thought Leadership

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote, USA Joining Collibra's Marketing Team At Collibra, we believe the next decade of enterprise value will be decided by whether AI can be trusted to act. Agents are moving from demos to decisions, and the leaders deploying them need certainty at the speed of AI. It's why we built the Enterprise AI Control Plane, over eighteen years of ontology engineering, to govern context and ensure control across any data, any model, any agent. We are builders who come from a wide range of backgrounds to do what has never been done before. About this role We are looking for a VP, Product Marketing & Executive Thought Leadership to own the narrative for Collibra's business, and to lead the team that takes it to market. This is a category-defining role at a defining moment: the vocabulary of AI context is being written right now, and we intend to author it. You will make our CEO the recognized voice of the category, on every stage and in every byline that matters. You will build the joint story with the platforms the world runs AI on — Snowflake, Databricks, AWS, Google Cloud, SAP. And you will grow and coach a team of product marketers into a multi-layer org that ships market-moving launches without you in the room. You manage managers: directors own the day-to-day launches; you own the bar, the narrative, and the few things that cannot be delegated. This is a 0→1 category role inside an 18-year-old company — you'll define how a new market is positioned, messaged, and brought to the world. About you You are a builder of leaders, not a soloist. You've run a PMM org through managers and you know the difference between setting a standard and taking back the pen — you coach the launch from spec-sheet to stake, and you let your directors own it. You move fluently between a whiteboard with engineers and a green room with a CEO, translating architecture into a story technical and business leaders both lean toward. You've moved a category before, ideally from the challenger's seat, not the incumbent's. You have a passion for proof — every number you ship traces to a source — and an unapologetic focus on customer truth over internal belief. You thrive where the assets are good but the narrative and the discipline are the gap, and you'd rather open a new frontier than chase a competitor into theirs. About Collibra Collibra is building the Enterprise AI Control Plane — the layer that governs context and ensures control so enterprises can deploy AI with certainty. Eighteen years of ontology and semantic engineering sit underneath it. We are at the center of the most important shift in enterprise software: the move from data governance to governing the context and agents that act on it. We are an entrepreneurial company with a founder who wants to be the author of this category, and a marketing team rebuilding around the AI growth motion to make that real. VP, Product Marketing Managers at Collibra are responsible for Build and lead the team. Hire, develop, and coach director-level PMM leaders and their teams. Create an environment where strong people do their best work, raise the bar with every hire, and build a bench that ships launch-quality narrative without you in the room. Set the launch standard, your team's own execution. Your team runs day-to-day launches. You own the bar: the narrative spine, the proof discipline, and the go/no-go on whether a story is ready for the CEO and the market. You coach the story from spec-sheet to stake; you don't write every one. Own the category narrative. Enterprise AI Control Plane, “context and control,” and the Context Gravity thesis. This you hold yourself. Drive sales success through storytelling. Own the ICP journey (Hero journey) that turns narrative into pipeline: the demos, content, and sales collateral that make reps and partner sellers win. Set the storytelling standard across the funnel, first-call decks, demo scripts, solution maps, campaign briefs, customer proof, playbooks, so the field carries a story buyers move on, not a feature list. Measured by what closes, not what ships. Shape the CEO's narrative. Architect the keynote story arcs, anchor bylines, podcast and press narrative. Own strategic partner marketing. Set the joint narrative and co-marketing strategy with Snowflake, Databricks, AWS, Google Cloud, and SAP, and carry the executive partner relationships. Your team runs the joint launches, co-funded campaigns, and partner-event activations. Make Collibra the governance and context layer those platforms recommend by name. Own analyst relations strategy. Run AR as a growth lever and hold the principal analyst relationships yourself. Enable Collibra to position strongly in Gartner and Forrester briefings and quadrants. Own competitive read. Direct the day-to-day and longer-horizon competitive intelligence. Adjust the narrative to hold differentiation, without reacting into a challenger's frame. Run the thought-leadership program. Design the executive roundtable and content series for CAIOs, CIOs, and AI leaders; your team builds and runs it. Grounded in customer truth, not a vendor pitch. You have 15+ years in enterprise software, spanning product marketing, technology evangelism, and product management roles, ideally in databases, analytics, and AI. Proven ability to market to both technical and business leaders, up to C-level (CAIO, CIO, CDO), translating architecture into narrative for engineers and into business stakes for executives. 6+ years leading teams, with direct experience managing managers (director-level reports); built and developed a multi-layer PMM organization. Demonstrated ownership of a category narrative that moved a market, ideally from a challenger position, not the incumbent. Track record architecting executive keynotes or bylines that landed on a major industry stage. Strategic partner or alliance marketing experience across a hyperscaler / data-platform ecosystem (AWS, Google Cloud, Snowflake, Databricks, SAP, or equivalent). Hands-on analyst relations with Gartner and/or Forrester, including Wave/MQ engagement. Demonstrated proficiency in leveraging AI tools (e.g., Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT, Copilot) to solve real-world business challenges, drive measurable outcomes, or streamline workflows. A bachelor’s degree or equivalent related working experience is required. This position is not eligible for visa sponsorship. You are Experienced creating or shifting category vocabulary. Someone who has built a PMM function or messaging architecture from an early or rebuilding state. Able to operate without a large-company machine underneath you, scrappy, high-output, owns the outcome. Comfortable building the executive's voice rather than performing your own, and comfortable coaching launches rather than running them. Fluent with the agentic-AI buyer (CAIO / AI leader), not data alone. Experienced running partner co-marketing at scale inside or alongside a hyperscaler. Experienced with carrying a technology-evangelist or developer-facing platform mandate at scale.
