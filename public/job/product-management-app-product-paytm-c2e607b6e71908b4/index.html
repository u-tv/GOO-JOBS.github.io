Product Management - App Product

🏢 paytm 📍 Noida, Uttar Pradesh 💼 Full-time Employment 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Paytm: Paytm is India's leading mobile payments and financial services distribution company. Pioneer of the mobile QR payments revolution in India, Paytm builds technologies that help small businesses with payments and commerce. Paytm’s mission is to serve half a billion Indians and bring them to the mainstream economy with the help of technology. About the Role: UPI is the backbone of India's digital payments revolution — and Paytm is one of its largest participants, processing hundreds of millions of transactions every month. We are looking for a Product Manager to join our User Growth team and drive product strategy, user growth, and experience excellence across our UPI-powered flows.This role sits at the intersection of consumer behaviour, financial infrastructure, and regulatory nuance. You will own high-impact product surfaces and work closely with engineering, design, data, and business teams to ship products that millions of Indians use daily. Key Responsibilities 1. User Experience & Growth - Own end-to-end product vision, roadmap, and execution of products/features that drive engagement, frequency of use, and top-of-wallet positioning - Continuously improve funnel metrics — conversion, success rate, and drop-off — through A/B testing and experimentation 2. Platform Reliability & Scale - Define success metrics (SLAs, success rates, latency benchmarks) and hold engineering accountable to them - Build escalation frameworks and incident response playbooks for payment failures - Ensure platform readiness for high-traffic events such as festive seasons, IPL, and government disbursements 3. Insights & Experimentation - Build and maintain dashboards that track north-star and guardrail metrics across the UPI funnel - Drive a culture of rapid experimentation — hypothesis → test → learn → scale - Generate and socialize insights that influence upstream decisions in engineering, design, and business Required Qualifications: - 3–5 years of product management experience with a mandatory focus on consumer-facing products or fintech - Deep understanding of user psychology, engagement funnels, and conversion mechanics in high-frequency consumer apps - Proven ability to translate ambiguous problems into structured hypotheses and ship fast, iterative solutions - Comfort with SQL and product analytics tools (Mixpanel, Amplitude, Metabase, or equivalent)Strong written and verbal communication skills — ability to write crisp PRDs, present to leadership, and align cross-functional stakeholders - Demonstrated ownership mindset: you have shipped features end-to-end across product, engineering, and go-to-market Good to Have: - Prior experience working in a UPI-regulated environment or with NPCI product constructs - Experience with 0→1 product development alongside maintaining and scaling existing surfaces - Exposure to growth, lifecycle, or CRM-driven product work Why Join Us: - Work on one of India's most critical financial infrastructure products — UPI — at massive scale - Join a high-performance product org with strong engineering depth and a bias for shipping - Influence product decisions that affect hundreds of millions of transactions every day - Operate in a fast-moving, ownership-driven culture where PMs have real authority and real accountability
