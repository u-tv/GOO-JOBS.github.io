Integrated Hire: Account Manager – Client Services

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Houston, Texas URL: https://integratedhire.com Position: Account Manager – Client Services **Important**: This role requires at least five years of firsthand experience living and working in the United States, polished and natural spoken and written English, and a strong understanding of U.S. business culture and client expectations. Applicants who do not meet these requirements will not be considered. Location: Fully Remote / Work From Anywhere Schedule: US Central Time, Monday–Friday (Full-Time) Compensation: $2,500–$3,500/month Overview Integrated Hire (integratedhire.com) helps small and mid-sized U.S. businesses build high-performing remote teams by connecting them with experienced offshore professionals across administration, finance, customer service, marketing, operations, and more. We’re looking for a Client Services and Client Success Manager who is highly organized, responsive, and excellent at keeping clients happy. This is not primarily a sales role. The focus is on onboarding, client communication, follow-up, problem solving, and making sure the client experience stays smooth from the moment a client signs through the hiring process and beyond. A significant part of this role involves stepping in when things do not go according to plan. You may be handling an unhappy client, employee performance or attendance concerns, communication breakdowns, or other unexpected issues. You’ll take ownership, coordinate with the right people internally, and help drive each situation toward a resolution. This role is best suited for someone who is calm under pressure, strong with follow-through, comfortable speaking with U.S.-based clients, and able to manage many moving pieces without letting anything fall through the cracks. We’re open to experienced professionals based anywhere in the United States or abroad, provided they can consistently work U.S. business hours and communicate naturally and confidently with U.S. business owners.
