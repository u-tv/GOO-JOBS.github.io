Come and See: QA Automation Engineer

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Colorado Springs, Colorado, United States URL: https://comeandsee.bamboohr.com/careers JOIN THE COME AND SEE TEAM QA Automation Engineer Every person who presses play deserves a product that works, and quality is how we honor them. If you love building systems that catch problems early and see quality as a way to serve people well – we’d love to meet you. ABOUT US Come and See is the faith-based nonprofit media ministry behind The Chosen . Our mission is inviting everyone to encounter Jesus through exceptional storytelling that brings the Bible to life. Our Team Members work together as collaborators in the greatest mission on earth. ABOUT THE ROLE The QA Automation Engineer will build our automated testing strategy and coverage across web, mobile, connected TV, API, and backend platforms, integrate testing into our CI/CD pipelines, and support manual and exploratory testing where automation isn’t yet practical. This is a full-time, remote role open to candidates in the USA. WHAT YOU’LL DO Shape the testing strategy. Help define automation standards, choose the right testing layers for each product, and prioritize by risk, business impact, and effort. Champion testability. Advocate for quality throughout the development lifecycle and mentor teammates on where automation adds value and where manual validation still belongs. Build automated test suites. Design, write, and maintain tests for critical user journeys, APIs, and integrations, with reusable frameworks, fixtures, and patterns for test data and mocking. Keep tests reliable. Diagnose and fix flaky tests, and keep suites maintainable as products and architecture evolve. Choose the right tools. Evaluate, prototype, and recommend testing frameworks and services, including AI approaches to test creation and maintenance, before wider adoption. Wire testing into CI/CD. Work with Engineering on which tests run for pull requests, builds, deployments, and scheduled regression, and help set quality gates that don’t slow delivery. Make quality visible. Improve visibility into test results, failures, and trends, and trace failures to application code, test code, infrastructure, or environments. Test what automation can’t. Perform manual, exploratory, and regression testing across supported platforms and devices, and document and help diagnose defects. Improve with the team. Work with Product Managers, Engineers, and Product Operations to validate acceptance criteria, learn from recurring defects, and keep improving our practices. WHAT YOU’LL BRING 5+ years in software quality assurance, test automation, or software engineering, using modern automated testing frameworks Hands-on experience building and maintaining automated tests Experience testing web applications, APIs, mobile applications, or other digital platforms Strong grasp of testing principles, including regression, integration, end-to-end, exploratory, and risk-based testing Ability to write code or scripts for test automation, with working knowledge of CI/CD and Git workflows Experience diagnosing failures across applications, tests, environments, and integrations A personal faith in Jesus and alignment with our mission, values, and Statement of Faith BONUS POINTS: Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field, experience establishing or expanding an automated testing program, testing across web, iOS, Android, and connected TV, CI/CD integration, API and integration testing, cloud testing infrastructure or device farms, performance or accessibility testing, e xperience leveraging AI-assisted development or testing tools to improve engineering productivity and test automation , streaming, media, or high-scale consumer product experience, nonprofit or ministry/faith-based organizational experience BENEFITS: Medical, dental, vision, retirement match, HRA, disability and life insurance, generous and flexible time off, holidays, parental leave, and more.
