The Vice President of Enterprise Sales, East Opportunity The Vice President of Enterprise Sales, East and Canada is a senior leadership position reporting to the Senior Vice President of Enterprise Sales. We are seeking an entrepreneurial, growth-minded, and inspiring leader to build and manage a large, high-performing sales organization that drives a significant share of revenue for Okta.
