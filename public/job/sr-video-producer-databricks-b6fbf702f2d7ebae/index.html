Sr Video Producer GAQ127R43 As a Sr Video Producer on the Media Productions team at Databricks, you will work closely with the team and our clients to ensure proper intake and management of video production projects from ideation through final delivery. The Media Productions team is seeking a Video Producer with an accomplished background in all aspects of the video production process. This role will partner with many teams across the company to plan, design, and support our on-demand video and live production product experiences. In this role, you will report to the Sr Manager of Media Productions, based in the greater Bay Area. The team provides technology and media production support that delivers compelling and engaging content to all divisions across the enterprise with the goal of maximizing visibility, engagement and impact. Responsibilities : Collaborate closely with internal clients and external partners to help create various production projects and assets. Management of pre-production, production and post-production tasks for multimedia (audio/video) projects. Coordinate budgeting and logistics to each video project needs Staffing a production team using internal and external resources on a per project basis. Coordinate budgeting and logistics to each video project needs Travel to shoot locations for scouting and developing pre-production assets. Ability to build and manage a project budget and production schedule. Comfortable working with vendors to reserve and ship required equipment. Assisting with script writing and overall creative direction. Comfortable operating camera, lighting, and audio equipment to capture engaging and aesthetically pleasing footage in person and fully remote. Experience with Adobe Creative Cloud suite, including Premiere, After Effects, Illustrator, Photoshop, and Audition. Proper management of media assets. Capture and evaluate analytics to calculate a project's ROI based on predefined success metrics. Excellent project management and organization skills Manage project timelines, tracking budget, ensuring all deadlines are met. Direct on-camera talent and crew members during shoots Requirements : 8+ years of hands-on experience with developing web-based video content. 5+ years at a digital-first brand or agency Experience with Adobe Creative Cloud, Iconik, Kaltura, OpenReel, Databricks. B.A. in film production or similar field or equivalent experience. Quick decision-making ability under pressure. You can balance competing priorities and work alone or within a team equally well. You have excellent reliability, dependability, and integrity. You have solid attention to detail, accuracy and are highly organized.
