Customer.io: Accounting Manager

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Americas Remote About Customer.io Over 9,000 companies — from scrappy startups to global brands — use our platform to send billions of emails, push notifications, in-app messages, and SMS every day. Customer.io powers automated communication that people actually want to receive. We help teams send smarter, more relevant messages using real-time behavioral data. About the role Hi, my name is Diep Nguyen, VP Finance here at Customer.io , and I am looking for an Accounting Manager to partner with the Controller on our month-end close and serve as first-level reviewer for the accounting team. We're a global, remote-first, private company built on subscription and usage-based revenue, with a finance stack that includes NetSuite and FloQast, and we use AI throughout the business, including in Finance. Our accounting team covers revenue accounting, close, AP/AR, payroll, and treasury. You'll report to the Controller and work closely with partners in Sales, Legal, IT, People, and Engineering. What we value Ownership — You own a process from source data through the financial statements. Curiosity — You keep asking why until you find the system behind the break. A data-backed voice — You bring findings and recommendations to business owners with numbers behind them. Practical AI use — You apply AI to speed up accounting work, with documented human review. What you’ll do Partner with the Controller on the monthly and quarterly close in FloQast, tracking deadlines and sign-offs, and help shorten the close through process improvements and AI. Review and approve journal entries and reconciliations prepared by others on the accounting team, including cash, accounts receivable, prepaids, deferred commissions, fixed assets, accruals, deferred revenue, and payroll liabilities, and propose the bad debt reserve for Controller approval. Draft the monthly flux analysis with root-cause explanations for material variances, assemble the close review folder, and support the Controller in the review with the VP Finance and CFO. Assist the Senior Revenue Manager with revenue recognition (ASC 606), including deferred revenue, overage accruals, and usage true-ups. Review AP, card, and payroll accruals and entries to confirm liabilities are recorded completely. Prepare audit schedules for our external auditors and help document and test close controls with the Controller. Coach the accountants who prepare the close, and apply AI tools to flux analysis, accrual review, and reconciliation matching with documented human review. What we're looking for 6+ years of progressive accounting experience, including at least 2 years reviewing the work of others or managing accountants Bachelor's degree in accounting, finance, or a related field Hands-on month-end close experience at a SaaS company, including reviewing journal entries and reconciliations in an ERP such as NetSuite Working knowledge of US GAAP for the accounts you will review, including accruals and unrecorded liabilities, prepaids, fixed assets, and payroll and commission liabilities, with specific knowledge of the allowance for credit losses (ASC 326), capitalized sales commissions (ASC 340-40), and the evaluation of errors and their materiality (ASC 250) Experience supporting an external audit, including preparing schedules and responding to auditor requests A specific example of using AI to make an accounting process or the close more efficient, which you built or deployed, and what it changed Clear written and verbal communication with non-finance partners, and the judgment to know when to escalate to the Controller Nice to have: CPA license (active or inactive) or a Big 4 or public accounting background; experience with NetSuite, FloQast, and usage-based billing; SOX or internal controls exposure; and remote-first company experience Compensation & Benefits We believe in transparency.
