Cribl: Staff Software Engineer, Search Core

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote - United States URL: https://cribl.io Join the company that’s building the telemetry infrastructure for the AI era. At Cribl, we partner with IT and Security teams at many of the world’s biggest enterprises, including half of the Fortune 100, to bridge the gap between AI ambition and infrastructure reality. As the AI Platform for Telemetry, we give customers the choice, control, and flexibility to manage and analyze telemetry for both humans and agents, so they can build what’s next. We’re one of the fastest‑growing private companies and a leading player in a massive, fast‑moving market. With a global workforce, we’re remote‑first and grounded in a simple idea: software is a people business. Cribl is the place where curious, collaborative people can do their best work, grow fast, and bring their full selves to the herd. Why You'll Love This Role Cribl Search powers high-speed investigations for security and IT teams at some of the world's largest enterprises. Whether data lives in Lakehouse Engines or is spread across Federated sources, Search unifies ingest, storage, and analysis into a single experience — no tool-hopping, no complex orchestration. Teams collect once, search anywhere, and investigate with no blind spots. Now add agentic AI to the picture. AI-driven workloads are exploding, autonomous agents are firing queries alongside human analysts, and the volume and complexity of concurrent searches is growing fast. The query engine at the heart of Cribl Search needs to be faster, more concurrent, and more resilient than ever — and that's where you come in. Cribl is looking for a backend-focused Staff Software Engineer to join a remote-first, diverse, inclusive, cross-functional team of software developers focused on making search stupid fast. You will play a central role in evolving the query engine at the heart of Cribl Search – breaking through concurrency ceilings, optimizing query dispatching and execution, and tuning performance for workloads ranging from interactive analyst investigations to AI agents firing thousands of concurrent queries. This is an ideal role for anyone who is passionate about making Search blazing fast at a massive scale. On top of it all, you will have fun! Cribl strives to be a great place to work for everyone.
