R26,640–R28,860); Commission: £50for every eligible successful customer sign-up Minimum performance target: 10 successful sign-ups per month Working Hours & Conditions: Mon–Fri, 9am–5pm UK Time Holidays: TBC ABOUT THE COMPANY: TheClient is a UK-based full-service digital marketing agency located in Watford, Greater London. Operating as a recognized Google Partner, the agency specializes in managing online advertising campaigns, search engine optimization, and web development. ROLE OVERVIEW: We are seeking a driven, consultative Outbound SEO Sales Executive to connect with UK business owners and decision-makers. Working via an automatic dialler (no manual dialling required), you will contact a curated database of high-value UK service and product businesses. Your objective is to uncover search visibility gaps—particularly among companies already spending on Google Ads—educate prospects on the commercial value of organic search, and convert qualified opportunities into new SEO clients RESPONSIBILITIES: High-Volume Outbound Prospecting: Use our automatic dialler to contact allocated UK businesses (typically 5–20 employees across 643 targeted high-value service and product niches) and meet daily conversation quotas. Consultative Discovery: Engage with business owners, managing directors, and marketing/e-commerce leads. Ask targeted discovery questions to understand their high-value services/products, geographic catchment areas, and current Google Ads vs. organic performance. Value-Driven Pitching & Objection Handling: Clearly explain the difference between paid ads and organic search without jargon, positioning SEO as a complementary channel to capture buyers who scroll past sponsored listings. Confidently handle common objections (e.g., existing agencies, budget concerns, or long timelines). Pre-Call Verification & Research: Review prospect websites and search visibility across specific sectors and locations before pitching, ensuring accurate observations without making unverified claims. Pipeline & CRM Management: Follow the structured outbound call and sign-up process, accurately logging call outcomes, search observations, and follow-up actions in the CRM. Target Achievement: Consistently meet daily activity benchmarks and achieve a minimum target of 10 eligible customer sign-ups per month. Compliance & Integrity: Adhere strictly to UK direct-marketing regulations (TPS/CTPS, PECR, UK GDPR), suppression lists, and company compliance standards—never guaranteeing rankings or fixed ROI, and clearly communicating pricing and terms before sign-up. Sales Experience: Proven track record in B2B outbound telesales, cold calling, or consultative telephone sales. Digital Marketing Aptitude: Strong understanding of (or ability to quickly grasp) SEO fundamentals, PPC/Google Ads, and how search visibility translates into commercial leads and revenue. Communication Skills: Articulate, persuasive, and active listener capable of building rapport with UK business owners without sounding scripted. Resilience & Work Ethic: Comfortable working in a fast-paced, auto-dialler environment with daily conversation quotas and monthly sign-up targets. Attention to Detail: Disciplined with CRM administration, pre-call research, and regulatory compliance Base Salary: £1,200 – £1,300 per month (approx. R26,640 – R28,860, subject to exchange rates). Uncapped Earning Potential: £50 commission for every eligible customer sign-up. Achieving the minimum target of 10 sign-ups adds £500/month (approx. R11,100), bringing total monthly earnings to £1,700 – £1,800 (approx. R37,740 – R39,960). Work-Life Balance: Fixed Monday to Friday schedule, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM UK time. Structured Onboarding & Tools: Full training programme covering SEO product knowledge, objection handling, and a pre-qualified 643-niche UK prospect database with automatic dialling.
