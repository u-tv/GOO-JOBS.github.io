Technical Enablement Business Partner

🏢 okta 📍 Singapore Office 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Secure Every Identity, from AI to Human Identity is the key to unlocking the potential of AI. Okta secures AI by building the trusted, neutral infrastructure that enables organizations to safely embrace this new era. This work requires a relentless drive to solve complex challenges with real-world stakes. We are looking for builders and owners who operate with speed and urgency and execute with excellence. This is an opportunity to do career-defining work. We're all in on this mission. If you are too, let's talk. Are you passionate about translating deep technical architecture into high-impact presales execution? Do you thrive at the intersection of complex technology, adult learning, and go-to-market strategy? As a Technical Enablement Business Partner (Sales Engineering) , you will serve as the strategic bridge connecting regional Sales Engineering (SE) leadership, Product/Engineering teams, and front-line presales execution. You are not just an instructor; you are an embedded advisor who diagnoses regional technical capability gaps, builds leveled curriculum, coaches SEs through complex architecture pitches and proof-of-concept (POC) hurdles, and uses conversation intelligence telemetry to measurably accelerate deal velocity. In this role, you will combine subject matter depth across modern identity, cloud architectures, and developer integration patterns with rigorous enablement methodologies to drive the technical readiness of our global Presales organization. What You’ll Do 1. Strategic Business Partnering & Skill Gap Diagnostics Act as the dedicated enablement trusted advisor to regional Presales Directors and front-line SE Managers, directly aligning enablement programs with revenue goals and regional GTM priorities. Conduct regular diagnostic gap analyses using conversation intelligence (e.g., Gong), manager syncs, win/loss data, and technical evaluation outcomes. Design and execute tailored enablement sprints addressing specific territory dynamics, competitive pressures, and segment nuances (Enterprise, Commercial, and Developer-led motions). Identify and eliminate friction points across the technical evaluation lifecycle—including POCs, RFPs, and architectural reviews—to accelerate time-to-technical-win. 2. Technical Onboarding, Hands-On Coaching & Certifications Own the operational execution of the regional SE onboarding pathway, accelerating time-to-first-autonomous-demo and autonomous field readiness across Day 30, 60, and 90 milestones. Facilitate high-impact, interactive workshops including whiteboarding clinics, discovery labs, live demo simulations, and technical objection-handling sessions. Lead and coordinate regional technical summits, hackathons, and immersion clinics. Direct stage-gated certification programs (e.g., foundational platform demos, deep-dive architectural teardowns, advanced POC defense) to guarantee technical excellence before customer deployment. Empower front-line SE managers with coaching rubrics, milestone checklists, and 1:1 audit frameworks to reinforce learning in the field. 3. Product Line Specialization & Leveled Curriculum Architecture Serve as the dedicated Technical Enablement Subject Matter Expert (SME) for flagship identity solutions (examples: Workforce Identity Cloud and Customer Identity Cloud / Auth0). Maintain deep architectural and practical fluency in identity protocols (SAML, OIDC, OAuth), developer integration patterns, APIs, SDKs, and emerging identity security domains (ISPM, PAM, ITDR). Design and maintain tiered learning pathways (L100 Foundational, L200 Core Architecture, L300 Advanced Edge Cases & Custom Extensibility) that systematically advance SEs into technical domain specialists. Build hands-on lab environments, sandbox exercises, and realistic architecture challenge scenarios. Partner closely with Product Management, Product Marketing, Technical Marketing, and Field CTOs to run the New Product Introduction (NPI) engine—translating roadmaps into field-ready delta training, battlecards, and presales execution packages ahead of General Availability (GA). 4. Analytics, Telemetry & Business Impact Leverage conversation intelligence to ols (Gong) to evaluate real-world messaging adoption, track customer response patterns, and pinpoint emerging competitor counter-tactics. Define and monitor quantitative metrics tracking the transition from curriculum completion to active field execution. Deliver monthly and quarterly enablement business reviews (QBRs) to senior GTM leadership showcasing ramp velocity, certification pass rates, POC acceleration, and measurable ROI. What You Bring to the Role 5+ years of total professional experience in Sales Engineering (Presales), Technical Enablement, Solutions Architecture, or Developer Relations within enterprise SaaS. Hands-on technical background in identity and access management (IAM), modern application architecture, cloud security, or developer tooling. Working knowledge of standard protocols and frameworks: OIDC, OAuth 2.0, SAML, REST APIs, JSON Web Tokens (JWT), and modern web/mobile architectures. Proven track record of designing and delivering technical training curricula, hands-on labs, and certification programs for technical audiences. Strong business acumen and consultative skills, with demonstrated experience partnering directly with front-line technical managers and senior leadership.
