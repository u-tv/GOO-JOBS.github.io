Keeper Security: Communications Intern

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote, US Description Keeper is hiring a bright and talented Communications Intern to assist Keeper’s Global Communications team this Fall/Winter. This is a 100% remote internship from select locations with an opportunity to work a hybrid schedule for candidates based in the Chicago, IL metro area. Keeper’s cybersecurity software is trusted by millions of people and thousands of organizations, globally. Keeper is published in 23 languages and is sold in over 150 countries. Join one of the fastest-growing cybersecurity companies and gain valuable skills assisting our communications team in our public relations and social media efforts. About Keeper Keeper is a pioneer of zero-knowledge and zero-trust security built for any IT environment. Its core offering, KeeperPAM®, is an AI-enabled, cloud-native platform that protects all users, devices and infrastructure from cyber attacks. Recognized for its innovation in the Gartner Magic Quadrant for Privileged Access Management (PAM), Keeper secures passwords and passkeys, infrastructure secrets, remote connections and endpoints with role-based enforcement policies, least privilege and just-in-time access. Learn why Keeper is trusted by leading organizations to defend against modern adversaries at KeeperSecurity.com . About the Role The Communications Intern will be mentored by and report to Keeper’s Communications Director and will be responsible for supporting the Communications and Social Media teams on daily tasks and projects. This is a fall and winter internship with a fixed 6 month duration. This is a paid internship designed to offer professional experience. Please note that we do not offer school credit for this internship. Responsibilities Support the Communications and Social Media teams with projects and daily tasks, including updating and maintaining the company’s media page, content creation for social media, announcement planning and execution, and writing for PR and internal communications Exhibit a high level of professionalism and creative thinking Show a strong desire to learn along with a drive to succeed in assigned tasks Requirements Must have a strong desire to learn and professional drive to succeed Interest in learning about Keeper’s products, cybersecurity and industry trends Excellent verbal and written communication skills, attention to detail and be personable and dependable Familiarity with drafting internal and/or external communications content including employee memos, press releases and media advisories Experience copywriting and creating engaging social media content Strong organizational and planning skills Ability to assist in administrative duties including reporting performance data, scheduling social media content and updating media page on website Current enrollment in a related BS or master’s degree is preferred Basic working knowledge of Google and Microsoft Office Products Keeper Security, Inc. is an equal opportunity employer and participant in the U.S. Federal E-Verify program. We celebrate diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive environment for all employees.
