Revenue Operations Senior Associate

🏢 faire 📍 New York City, NY; San Francisco, CA 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Faire Faire is a technology wholesale platform built on the belief that the future is local. Independent retailers around the globe collectively represent a multi-hundred-billion-dollar wholesale market that has historically been fragmented and offline. At Faire, we're using the power of tech, data, and machine learning to connect this thriving community of entrepreneurs across the globe. Picture your favorite boutique in town — we help them discover the best products from around the world to sell in their stores. With the right tools and insights, we believe that we can level the playing field so businesses can grow and local communities can thrive. We’re looking for smart, resourceful and passionate people to join us as we power the shop local movement. If you believe in community, come join ours. About this role Revenue Operations sits at the center of how our Sales and Success teams operate. We build the systems, processes, insights, and tooling that help hundreds of customer-facing teammates spend more time selling, coaching, and helping independent businesses grow. We're looking for a highly analytical, AI-forward operator to join our Revenue Operations team to help shape how our go-to-market organization makes decisions. You'll partner closely with Sales leaders to improve forecasting, pipeline diagnostics, sales performance, compensation operations, and operational infrastructure while building scalable analytical frameworks that make our business smarter over time. You'll also play an important role in helping our Revenue organization adopt AI and automation to eliminate manual work and improve decision making. If you love solving ambiguous business problems, building scalable systems, and turning data into action, we'd love to meet you. What you’ll do Partner on core Revenue Operations foundations including quota setting, book builds, compensation administration, and monthly business reporting Build scalable reporting and analytical frameworks that improve visibility into funnel health, pipeline quality, and sales performance Develop diagnostics that help Sales leaders identify opportunities, understand performance drivers, and make better decisions Improve forecasting infrastructure and manager tooling to increase confidence in revenue projections Perform ad hoc analytical deep dives that uncover actionable insights and influence go-to-market strategy Identify opportunities to automate operational workflows using AI and modern tooling, improving team productivity and reducing manual work Collaborate closely with Sales, Success, Finance, Data, and cross-functional partners to continuously improve how our revenue organization operates We are looking for someone Is exceptionally analytical You enjoy breaking down complex business problems into structured analyses. You don't stop at identifying what happened—you figure out why it happened and what should happen next. Is AI-forward You naturally incorporate AI into your daily work. You're excited by new tools, constantly looking for ways to automate repetitive work, improve workflows, and increase the quality and speed of decision making. Takes ownership You don't wait to be told what to do. You proactively identify opportunities, build plans, and drive projects from idea through execution. Executes with urgency You move quickly, prioritize effectively, and consistently deliver high-quality work. You enjoy building scalable processes that make teams more effective. Builds strong partnerships You empathize with customer-facing teams and know how to influence through data, communication, and collaboration. You're just as comfortable working with Sales leaders as you are digging into a spreadsheet. Loves learning You're curious by nature. You enjoy tackling new problems, seek feedback, and continuously improve your craft. Qualifications 3-5+ years of experience in Revenue Operations, Sales Operations, Business Operations, Strategy & Operations, Consulting, Finance, or a similarly analytical role Advanced Excel and/or Google Sheets skills Experience building dashboards and analyzing large datasets Experience translating data into clear business recommendations Experience managing cross-functional projects from beginning to end Experience using AI tools to improve productivity, analysis, or operational workflows Experience with sales compensation tools such as CaptivateIQ is a plus Strong SQL proficiency Why you'll love this role You'll work on some of the highest-leverage problems in our sales organization - from improving how we forecast revenue to designing analytical frameworks that shape sales strategy. You'll also have the opportunity to help redefine how Revenue Operations works in an AI-first world. We believe AI should eliminate repetitive work and enable teams to spend more time solving meaningful business problems. If you're excited about building smarter systems and having a direct impact on business performance, you'll fit right in.
