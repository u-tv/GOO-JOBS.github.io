Enterprise New Business Account Executive

🏢 asana 📍 Tokyo 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Enterprise New Business Account Executive Our sales team is committed to customer centricity by focusing on understanding and meeting the unique needs and aspirations of each customer, finding efficient paths to profitable, long-term partnerships. We employ a bottom-up enterprise sales model by helping smaller, successful teams evolve into enterprise-wide deployments while acting as a key voice of the customer to our product team. We are looking for a driven, strategic, and adaptable Enterprise Account Executive to join our Tokyo team and power the Asana revenue engine. In this role, you will partner closely with cross-functional teams to help Japanese enterprise customers navigate complex work challenges, maximize the value of Asana, and scale our business across the region. This role is based in our Tokyo office with an office-centric hybrid schedule. The standard in-office days are Tuesday, Thursday and one additional day of choice, which may be determined by your team. Most Asanas have the option to work from home on the two other weekdays they are not in office. If you're interviewing for this role, your recruiter will share more about the in-office requirements.
