Solutions Architect

🏢 databricks 📍 Munich, Germany 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

The Role At Databricks, our core principles are at the heart of everything we do; creating a culture of proactiveness and a customer-centric mindset guides us to create a unified platform that makes data science and analytics accessible to everyone. We aim to inspire our customers to make informed decisions that push their business forward. You will be an essential part of this mission, using your technical expertise to demonstrate how our Data Intelligence Platform can help customers solve their complex data challenges. As a Solutions Architect, you will lead the technical strategy for your customers — owning architecture discussions, driving platform adoption, and serving as a trusted advisor to customer technical leads and architects. You combine deep technical expertise with strategic thinking to position Databricks as the foundation of your customers’ data and AI strategy. This is a hands-on, technically demanding role where you will code, build, and demo—not just present slides. The Impact You Will Have Own and execute the end-to-end technical strategy for your accounts: from initial technical discovery and proving value by helping your customers to evaluate against their success metrics to production deployment and consumption growth. You will develop into a ‘champion’ and trusted advisor for your customer to guide on addressing their challenges. Lead complex architecture discussions: designing scalable, production-grade solutions spanning data engineering, ML/AI, and real-time analytics Build successful relationships in the stakeholder network with your clients (up to C-Level), connecting and translating technical solutions with business value Drive technical wins in competitive scenarios by demonstrating Databricks’ differentiation through custom-built solutions Orchestrate cross-functional resources (post-sales implementation resources, technical experts, product engineering, partners) to deliver comprehensive solutions for complex customer needs Scale best practices in your field and support customers by authoring reference architectures, how-tos, and demo applications, and help build the Databricks community in your region by leading workshops, seminars, and meet-ups. Influence product direction by providing structured feedback on customer requirements and competitive gaps What We Look For Proven experience in engaging customers in technical sales, challenging their questions, guiding clear outcomes, and communicating technical and value propositions. 4+ years in technical pre-sales, design solutions architecture, data engineering/warehousing/science, or a senior hands-on technical role Track record of driving platform user adoption, use case expansion and consumption growth within accounts Proven ability to lead technical discovery from data engineer to C-Level, architecture discussions with senior technical decision-makers — whiteboarding, design reviews, and trade-off/POV analysis Strong coding proficiency in Python and SQL — you must understand and demonstrate live coding, debugging, and solution-building skills (via vibe-coding) Proficient with Big Data Analytics technologies, including hands-on expertise with complex proofs-of-concept (ideally with Databricks platform or demonstrated ability to achieve proficiency rapidly) with a technical specialization in one area (e.g. data engineering,, ML/genAI, DWH migrations, streaming/real-time data) Excellent communication skills — able to translate complex architectures into business value for both technical and executive audiences Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a quantitative discipline (or equivalent experience) Interview Process: Recruiter Screen → Hiring Manager Screen → Design and Architecture Interview → Live Coding Assessment → Build, Demo, Pitch!
