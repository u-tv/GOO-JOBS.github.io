Senior Manager, Revenue Operations

🏢 faire 📍 San Francisco, CA 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Faire Faire is a technology wholesale platform built on the belief that the future is local. Independent retailers around the globe collectively represent a multi-hundred-billion-dollar wholesale market that has historically been fragmented and offline. At Faire, we're using the power of tech, data, and machine learning to connect this thriving community of entrepreneurs across the globe. Picture your favorite boutique in town — we help them discover the best products from around the world to sell in their stores. With the right tools and insights, we believe that we can level the playing field so businesses can grow and local communities can thrive. We’re looking for smart, resourceful and passionate people to join us as we power the shop local movement. If you believe in community, come join ours. About this role We're looking for a Revenue Operations Senior Manager to own how our revenue org runs. You'll oversee all Revenue Operations for our three sales and success motions (Brand Partnerships, Brand Success and Retailer Sales) across North America and Europe. This is an ownership role, not a support role. You'll own the operating system that powers the business: the dashboards, the weekly cadences, the forecast, the goals and the books. You'll partner closely with the Head of RevOps and GTM AI as their right hand on RevOps, and you'll lead and uplevel a growing team of RevOps professionals. If you love building clean systems, sweating the details, and asking whether the way things are done is actually the right way, you'll fit right in. What you’ll do Own the revenue operating system Design and run the weekly, monthly and quarterly operating cadences for Brand Partnerships, Brand Success and Retailer Sales, including business reviews and QBRs Own the core dashboards and reporting the revenue org runs on, so leaders get a fast, accurate read on the business and act on it Build and own our centralized GTM metrics repository, so stakeholders at every level can move through GTM data in a logical flow, from top-line results down to the drivers, and get a holistic picture of the business. Set the metric definitions and data standards behind it Own forecasting and pipeline health Build and run the forecast process across all three motions, from methodology to the weekly forecast call Diagnose why forecasts miss and fix the root causes, from stage definitions to pipeline hygiene Surface risks and opportunities early enough for leaders to change the outcome Own goaling and book building strategy Set the right goals for each team, both what they're goaled on and how ambitious the targets are Design how accounts are segmented, prioritized, assigned and rebalanced across reps and markets Take a critical eye to our current approach and change what isn't driving the right behaviors or outcomes Scale the GTM org Audit and simplify processes and workflows so reps spend less time on admin and more time with customers Partner with GTM Engineering and Analytics to keep Salesforce and our data foundations clean and reliable, including for AI-powered tools Lead and uplevel the team Hire, coach and develop a high-performing RevOps team with clear ownership, accountability and career paths Set a high bar for quality and pace, and build a culture where people call out what isn't working We're Looking For Someone Who... Owns it. You don't wait to be asked. When something in the business is broken or unclear, you take it on, make the call and see it through. Thinks from first principles. You don't just run the playbook. You ask whether the goals, books and processes are right, form a hypothesis and test it. Is maniacal about the details. You know the numbers cold, your definitions are precise, and nothing goes to a leader that you haven't checked. Is obsessed with operational excellence. You can't leave a clunky process alone. You simplify, automate and measure whether it got better. Builds strong teams. You raise the performance of the people around you, coach directly and aren't afraid of hard conversations. Cares about the reps. You get real satisfaction from making reps' jobs easier and more impactful, and you sit with them to understand how they work. Is low ego, high motor. You roll up your sleeves, give the team the credit and bring relentless energy.
