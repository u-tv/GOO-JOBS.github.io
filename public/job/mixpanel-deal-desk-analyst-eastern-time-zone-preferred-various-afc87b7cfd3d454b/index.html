Mixpanel: Deal Desk Analyst (Eastern Time Zone Preferred)

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote, US (Remote) About Mixpanel Mixpanel is the leading product intelligence and analytics platform, trusted by more than 29,000 companies to help understand how people use the products they build. By combining powerful analytics with AI that knows your business, Mixpanel helps teams see what’s working, diagnose what’s not, and decide what to build next. Learn more at mixpanel.com . About the Deal Desk Team The Deal Desk team keeps quote-to-cash fast, accurate, and in line with company policy. We help Sales structure deals that grow revenue and limit financial risk. The team is growing, covers multiple time zones, and works every day with Finance, Sales, Legal, Partnerships, and executive leadership. About the Role As a Deal Desk Analyst, you’ll own quote review and approval for your book of deals, work through non-standard terms, and run Salesforce CPQ improvements from idea to rollout. You’ll handle unusual contract scenarios on your own and escalate only when risk calls for it. You’ll also start shaping how Sales, Finance, and Legal work together on deals. You’ll report to the Deal Desk Manager in San Francisco. Responsibilities Advise Global Sales on deal structures that keep quote-to-cash efficient, and be the first call on non-standard terms Review and approve CPQ quotes against company policy, scrutinizing SLAs and customer-specific requests Draft commercial language on order forms to reflect negotiated terms, and route DocuSigns through signature Spot deal risks early, recommend ways to reduce them, and escalate the highest-risk deals with a clear proposal Review and approve post-sales changes, including amendments, restructures, and re-bills, on time and accurately Own CPQ day to day: troubleshoot bugs, document fixes, and run improvements from scoping through rollout Build and run CPQ and deal-basics training for AEs, including the enablement materials that go with it Own Google and Amazon Marketplace transaction processing, and keep the process accurate and on schedule Lead cross-functional projects with Sales, Finance, and Legal that make deals simpler to close We’re Looking For Someone Who Has Reviewed consumption-based SaaS contracts and can interpret non-standard terms, including SLAs Worked in Salesforce and CPQ and fixed issues without hand-holding Worked in order management, finance operations, deal desk, or sales operations, or built equivalent experience elsewhere A working understanding of SaaS business models, consumption and AI pricing, and revenue recognition principles Owned a project from start to finish and can describe what changed because of it Made data-driven recommendations on deal structure and defended them to Sales or Finance partners Stayed steady when priorities shifted, and worked comfortably through ambiguity Worked independently with little supervision and partnered well across teams, cultures, and time zones Bonus Points For DealHub experience Using AI tools to speed up analysis or workflows Hands-on marketplace transaction processing Building training or enablement content for sales teams #LI-Remote
