Job Description

Airbnb was born in 2007 when two hosts welcomed three guests to their San Francisco home, and has since grown to over 5 million hosts who have welcomed over 2 billion guest arrivals in almost every country across the globe. Every day, hosts offer unique stays and experiences that make it possible for guests to connect with communities in a more authentic way. The Community You Will Join: The Airbnb ACC Enterprise Ops team is a dynamic business unit dedicated to delivering safe, quality stays and exceptional experiences for travelers worldwide. It helps increase supply effectiveness, utilization, and impact using people-powered operations. The Difference You Will Make: As a Supervisor, you will lead a high-performing team that sources, qualifies, and enriches leads to accelerate supply growth in priority geographies and markets. Blending inspirational people leadership with program-management rigor, you will: Maintain a healthy, predictable pipeline of qualified leads Optimize tooling, processes, and KPIs to improve velocity and conversion Surface actionable insights that shape go-to-market and product strategies Foster a culture of inclusion, learning, and operational excellence Your leadership will directly expand Airbnb’s supply and unlock new revenue streams for the business. A Typical Day: A typical day for a Supervisor in a Lead Generation team at Airbnb blends hands-on operational management with program-level thinking to drive supply growth across markets. The role is not limited to tracking daily output. It involves continuously diagnosing performance, improving lead quality, optimizing processes, and aligning with cross-functional stakeholders to ensure that generated leads translate into high-quality hosts on the platform. You are expected to operate both at the execution layer (managing operational metrics) and at a strategic level (building scalable systems, improving conversion funnels, and supporting broader supply growth goals).
