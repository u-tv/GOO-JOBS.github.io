About the Role sa.global is looking for a driven and talented Business Development Manager with proven sales expertise to join our dynamic team. Backed by a strong technical team, your primary responsibilities will include identifying business opportunities and generating new sales. This role requires building and maintaining successful relationships with both prospects and existing clients. You will be capable of building strategic relationships at the CIO, VP, and Director levels while engaging tactically within divisional or departmental groups to develop new business opportunities and successfully close them. This exciting opportunity is ideal for a true hunter who is motivated by pursuing new sales opportunities and is eager to play a key role in the growth of our business. Main Responsibilities Develop opportunities by identifying potential clients within the target market and conducting appropriate research on prospective clients’ business and equipment needs. Negotiate and close agreements with clients while establishing long-term, high-value relationships. Manage the entire sales cycle, including prospecting, discovery, solution proposal development, presentations, negotiations, and closing. Partner with sales teams to create winning proposals for current and prospective clients. Collaborate with delivery teams to ensure contracted product specifications are executed on time and as agreed. Become a subject matter expert in our business products, processes, and operations, while staying up to date with industry news and trends. Build and maintain a strong pipeline of qualified sales opportunities and proactively follow up on leads. Sell business solutions and services to new clients. Leverage global marketing campaigns and drive local demand generation activities that attract prospective and existing clients to build a robust sales pipeline. Conduct regular account planning and business development sessions with Microsoft contacts to jointly target opportunities, develop offerings, and secure funding.
