Design Program Manager, AI Evals (London, United Kingdom)

🏢 figma 📍 London, England 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Figma is growing our team of passionate creatives and builders on a mission to make design accessible to all. Figma’s platform helps teams bring ideas to life—whether you're brainstorming, creating a prototype, translating designs into code, or iterating with AI. From idea to product, Figma empowers teams to streamline workflows, move faster, and work together in real time from anywhere in the world. If you're excited to shape the future of design and collaboration, join us! As Figma's AI capabilities expand, so does the work required to evaluate them. We're looking for a Design Program Manager, Evals to own the operating engine behind our design eval programs - the contractor and vendor operations, program rhythms, and quality standards that determine how fast and how well we can assess AI-generated design output. You'll help set the quality and velocity bar across the entire eval pipeline, and shape where our contractor and vendor investment goes. You'll be a close operating partner to our Design Director for AI Research, working alongside design program management and technical program management counterparts across the organization. You'll own the day-to-day - hiring and enabling contractors, managing vendor logistics and budgets, keeping eval workstreams moving - while building a point of view on how the program should evolve. You should be able to look at design output, speak credibly to craft, and translate that judgment into standards others can apply consistently. What you'll do at Figma: Drive quality and velocity across the design eval pipeline - running annotation rhythms, raising calibration and judgment standards, and closing the loop between eval output and product decisions Set the standard for high-quality reference designs so the eval pipeline has a reliable benchmark for what good looks like Own contractor hiring end to end: sourcing, screening, and closing - including the judgment call on when to scale headcount up or pull back based on eval demand Manage vendor logistics - budgets, SOWs, contracts, and purchasing - partnering with Legal, Security, and Procurement Run day-to-day program management for contractors and eval workstreams: status, blockers, timelines, and dependencies Partner with model designers to review contractor eval output and hold the quality bar Identify opportunities to automate or remove operational overhead across eval workstreams, including through internal tooling We’d love to hear from you if you have: 3+ years of experience in design program management, DesignOps, or an adjacent design role Deep craft expertise - you can speak to UI judgment and coach others on what good design looks like Experience procuring, onboarding, and managing design contractors or third-party vendors Experience driving operational programs that improve how teams work: rituals, processes, and documentation Strong project management and written communication skills, including coordinating contractors and vendors across multiple time zones While it’s not required, it’s an added plus if you also have: Hands-on depth with Figma's product offering — auto layout, variables, components, Slides Experience orchestrating AI tools, APIs, or workflow automations to simplify program management work Hands-on experience running or participating in design eval or quality review programs At Figma, one of our values is Grow as you go.
