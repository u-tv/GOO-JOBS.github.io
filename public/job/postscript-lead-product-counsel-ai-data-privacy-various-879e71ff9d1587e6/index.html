Postscript: Lead Product Counsel (AI, Data & Privacy)

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote Postscript is the AI-powered, all-in-one retention platform trusted by 20,000+ Shopify brands, including Brooklinen, Ruggable, True Classic, and Dr. Squatch. Our mission is to maximize the lifetime value of every shopper. We built the best-in-class SMS platform, expanded into email, and launched revenue-driving AI products you won’t find anywhere else. Now we’re building the most AI-forward team in ecommerce, and we’re just getting started. Backed by Greylock and Y Combinator. Fully remote since 2018. Postscript is hiring a Lead Product Counsel to own AI, privacy and data law for our platform. You will be the legal partner to the teams building AI-enabled conversations between brands and shoppers across 20,000 merchants and millions of consumers. The ideal candidate is a curious, effective and practical attorney who will work cross-functionally to provide counsel and compliance strategies to teams across Postscript on various AI, privacy and data protection matters. They are also interested in how AI is built and deployed in marketing and advertising, and in using AI in their own legal work. This is a great opportunity for someone who wants to be on the cutting edge of AI deployment and policy in marketing technology. What you’ll do Own and keep improving Postscript’s privacy, data and AI programs, including the policies, consumer notices, consent and opt-out processes, data retention practices and records behind them. Partner closely with product, engineering, security and marketing teams on AI, data and privacy projects, from early design through launch, and advise on day-to-day questions. Build privacy-by-design and responsible AI practices into how products get made, including privacy and AI impact assessments and guardrails for our AI agents, such as appropriate testing and human oversight . Help commercial and procurement teams draft, review and negotiate privacy, AI and data terms with customers and vendors. Conduct periodic privacy and AI training. Monitor and advise on new and proposed privacy, AI, data protection and consumer protection laws that could affect the business. Work closely with our in-house policy team to advocate for Postscript and our customers on new and proposed legislation. Advise on privacy and AI requirements in new markets as Postscript expands. What you bring A JD and active membership in at least one US state bar. 8+ years of legal experience, preferably including in-house at a technology company. Experience counseling product teams on privacy and data protection, and running or materially building a privacy program. Strong knowledge of US privacy law (CCPA/CPRA and other state privacy laws that followed), including service provider, processor, and controller roles and "sale" and "share." Working familiarity with GDPR. Experience advising on AI or machine learning products, ideally generative or agentic AI. Experience advising a marketing technology, ad tech, customer data platform, identity or commerce business on how it collects, combines and uses consumer data. Working knowledge of consumer protection law as it applies to marketing claims, AI disclosures, pricing and promotions. Experience drafting product terms and negotiating data processing, AI and data-use terms. Business sense and product intuition. You form a view quickly under ambiguity, explain trade-offs in plain language, and get to "yes, if" more often than "no." Curiosity and creativity. Unsettled questions interest you, and you help teams find the structure that lets a product ship rather than stopping at the risk. Hands-on use of AI in your own legal work, with the rigor to check what it produces. Nice to have In-house experience at an ecommerce, martech or ad tech company. Experience with data clean rooms, measurement, retail media or third-party data licensing. Experience engaging with regulators (FTC, state attorneys general) or working on privacy class actions. Policy or legislative experience on AI, privacy or pricing. Exposure to the EU AI Act. A CIPP/US, CIPP/E or AIGP certification. What You’ll Love About Us Salary range of USD $218,000-$300,000 base plus significant equity (we do not have geo based salaries) High growth startup - plenty of room for you to directly impact the company and grow your career! Work from home (or wherever) Fun - We’re passionate and enjoy what we do Competitive compensation and opportunity for equity Flexible paid time off Health, dental, vision insurance About the legal team Postscript Legal covers messaging compliance, privacy and product law, commercial work, litigation and policy. We treat compliance as a product advantage: our approach to consent and AI eligibility is something merchants choose us for. We also help shape the rules as founders of the Ecommerce Innovation Alliance , a leading industry policy advocacy group. The team is small and senior, and we work AI-first. We have built our own AI tools for drafting, research and review, we try new tools as they ship, and we teach each other what works. We also have dedicated internal AI operations support for design, development, integrations and larger projects. The company sets aside time for you to develop AI skills, not just licenses, because we think this is the future of how in-house legal teams will work. The tools make us faster. They do not make the call: every piece of advice still carries the judgment of the lawyer who gave it.
