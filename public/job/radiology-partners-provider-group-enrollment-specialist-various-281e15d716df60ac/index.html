Radiology Partners: Provider Group Enrollment Specialist

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: URL: http://www.radpartners.com WHO WE ARE AND WHAT WE DO: Radiology Partners, through its affiliated practices, is a leading radiology practice in the U.S., serving hospitals and other healthcare facilities across the nation. As a physician-led and physician-owned practice, our mission is to transform radiology by innovating across clinical value, technology, service, and economics, while elevating the role of radiology and radiologists in healthcare. Using a proven healthcare services model, Radiology Partners provides consistent, high-quality care to patients, while delivering enhanced value to the hospitals, clinics, imaging centers and referring physicians we serve. Position Summary: Prepare and submit Medicare and state Medicaid enrollment applications, including initial enrollments, revalidations, and updates. Gather and validate required provider and group practice documentation to ensure application accuracy and completeness. Manage and track enrollment application status, follow up with payers, and resolve delays or submission issues. Process and maintain Changes of Ownership (CHOWs), Changes of Information (COIs), and accurate provider enrollment records in accordance with CMS and state requirements. Coordinate and ensure compliance by collaborating with internal teams, responding to payer inquiries, monitoring regulatory updates, and supporting internal and external audits. DESIRED PROFESSIONAL SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE: High school diploma or equivalent required; Associate’s or Bachelor’s degree in Healthcare Administration, Business, or a related field preferred. 1–3 years of experience in healthcare provider enrollment, credentialing, or a related administrative function preferred Working knowledge of Medicare and Medicaid enrollment processes, CMS 855 forms, PECOS, and at least one state Medicaid portal. Proficiency in preparing, submitting, and tracking enrollment applications, managing deadlines, and maintaining accurate provider records. Strong attention to detail, organization, time management, written and verbal communication, analytical problem-solving, and the ability to work independently and collaboratively.
