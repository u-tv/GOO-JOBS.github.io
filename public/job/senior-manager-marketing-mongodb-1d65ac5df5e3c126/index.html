About the Role This role blends two disciplines into one seat: the ecosystem-building, community-first instincts of a marketer who lives inside the AI-native world, and the pipeline rigor of a regional field marketer. You'll be MongoDB's face across Seattle's AI-native accounts — AI infrastructure providers, AI-native software companies, and frontier labs, alongside the founders, operators, and VCs around them — while owning an integrated marketing plan spanning digital, events, webinars, and account-based programs that accelerates deal velocity across the region. This is a hands-on, builder role, and it requires someone equally comfortable shaping strategy and executing programs on the ground, fluent in the technical language of AI/ML products, agentic platforms, and developer tools, and able to earn the technical respect of a builder audience rather than &quot;enable&quot; it in the traditional sense. We are looking to speak to candidates who are based in Seattle for our hybrid working model.
