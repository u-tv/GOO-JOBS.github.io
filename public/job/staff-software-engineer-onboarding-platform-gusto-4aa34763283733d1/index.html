Staff Software Engineer, Onboarding Platform

🏢 gusto 📍 San Francisco, CA - Hybrid 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Gusto At Gusto, we're on a mission to grow the small business economy. We handle the hard stuff — payroll, health insurance, 401(k)s, and HR — so owners can focus on their craft and their customers. With teams in Denver, San Francisco, and New York, we support more than 500,000 small businesses nationwide and are building a workplace that reflects the people we serve. All full-time employees receive competitive base pay, benefits, and equity (RSUs) — because everyone who helps build Gusto should share in its success. Offer amounts are determined by role, level, and location. Learn more about our Total Rewards philosophy . AI is a fundamental part of how work gets done at Gusto. We expect all team members to actively engage with AI tools relevant to their role and grow their fluency as the technology evolves. AI experience requirements vary by role and will be assessed during the interview process. About the Role Gusto is opening more ways for a small business to get started with us: new front doors beyond payroll, on the web and inside LLM-based assistants. As a Staff Engineer on the Onboarding Platform team, you'll build the shared platform that makes those front doors possible. That means the onboarding models, orchestration, reusable components and tracking that let product teams launch a new onboarding experience in weeks instead of quarters. This is a full-stack, high-leverage role with two kinds of customers: the small businesses getting started on Gusto, and the product teams building on your platform. You'll shape technical direction, work across team boundaries to drive platform adoption, and raise the engineering bar around you. About the Team Onboarding Platform is part of Growth Onboarding. Our charter is to enable consistent, seamless onboarding across every channel a business uses to come to Gusto. We measure success by how quickly teams can stand up a new front door. We're a pod of engineers, a product manager and a designer with a bias toward building and shipping. We partner closely with the product teams across Gusto who build on what we create. If you want your work to multiply across teams and shape the first impression for a company serving 500,000+ small businesses, this is the team. Here's what you'll do day-to-day: Design and build the shared onboarding models, APIs and orchestration behind new front doors, including entry points and data prefill and sync across products. Partner with the product teams launching new front doors to drive platform adoption, and anticipate what they'll need before they ask. Build reusable frontend components and widgets, so teams assemble onboarding flows instead of rebuilding them. Improve the LLM onboarding experience, including enabling cross-sells through MCP. Set up consistent tracking across front doors, so every team measures onboarding the same way. Raise the engineering bar through pragmatic technical direction, strong reviews and mentoring, and make sharp scoping calls that ship the simplest version first. Here's what we're looking for: 8+ years of professional software engineering experience, including meaningful time owning customer-facing product surfaces or shared platforms in a large production codebase. Strong full-stack range. We use Ruby on Rails and React/TypeScript, and experience with them is desirable, but we care more about depth than the specific stack. Experience building platform capabilities that other teams build on, such as APIs, data models and reusable UI components, and driving their adoption. Demonstrated AI fluency: you use AI tooling in your day-to-day engineering, can speak concretely about where it helps and where it doesn't, and keep up as the tools change. High ownership in ambiguity: you shape a fuzzy need into something buildable and ship it without waiting for a perfect spec. A strong collaborator and influencer across team boundaries, with a track record of leveling up the engineers around you. [Bonus] Experience working on a platform team, building shared capabilities, tooling, or infrastructure that other engineering teams depend on. [Bonus] Experience in growth or onboarding (funnels, conversion, activation), or building on LLM and MCP surfaces. A Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. Our cash compensation amount for this role is targeted at $163,000–$204,000 in Denver & most remote locations, and $197,000–$247,000 for San Francisco & New York. Stock equity is additional.
