Should have understanding of data protection technologies & laws, including DLP, DSPM, DPDPA, IT Act and international regulations like GDPR 12. Exposure to Cloud Environment & Application Security are plus points. Qualifications and Experience: 1. 8-12 years of experience in security management. 2.
