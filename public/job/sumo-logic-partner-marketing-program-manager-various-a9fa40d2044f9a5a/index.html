Sumo Logic: Partner Marketing Program Manager

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote USA Sumo Logic is seeking a highly organized, analytical, and process-driven Partner Marketing Program Manager to support the growth and operational effectiveness of our global partner marketing organization. This role will be responsible for managing the systems, processes, and governance that power our partner marketing programs, with a primary focus on our Partner Relationship Management (PRM) platform, Market Development Funds (MDF), partner program operations, and performance reporting. The ideal candidate brings experience in B2B technology channel marketing, understands the complexities of working with MSSPs, VARs, and distributors, and thrives on building scalable processes that improve efficiency, accountability, and business impact. This is a hands-on role for someone who enjoys bringing structure to complex programs, improving operational workflows, and enabling regional teams and partners to execute successfully. What Success Looks Like In this role, success means: Establishing a reliable, well-managed PRM platform that supports global partner marketing needs. Improving partner and internal team adoption of Impartner. Implementing consistent MDF governance, approval processes, and financial tracking. Providing accurate, timely visibility into MDF spending, utilization, and program performance. Reducing manual processes and administrative delays across partner marketing operations. Improving partner accountability and reporting of marketing-generated opportunities and pipeline. Enabling regional marketing teams to execute programs efficiently through standardized tools and processes. Building scalable operational foundations that support Sumo Logic’s growing global partner ecosystem. Required Qualifications 5+ years of experience in B2B technology marketing, partner marketing, channel operations, or marketing program management. Hands-on experience working with Partner Relationship Management (PRM) platforms, preferably Impartner. Demonstrated experience managing MDF programs, including funding requests, approvals, budget tracking, governance, and reporting. Understanding of MSSP, VAR, and distributor (DISTI) partner ecosystems. Experience managing program budgets, financial tracking, and reconciliation processes. Strong understanding of channel marketing operations and partner program workflows. Experience with Salesforce and familiarity with marketing automation platforms. Strong analytical skills and experience developing dashboards, reports, and performance metrics. Excellent organizational, project management, and process improvement skills. Ability to manage multiple priorities and coordinate stakeholders across global teams. Strong attention to detail, financial accuracy, and operational accountability. Excellent written and verbal communication skills. Ability to work independently and proactively identify opportunities for improvement. Preferred Qualifications Direct experience administering Impartner, including MDF workflows and partner portal management. Experience developing or implementing MDF governance frameworks and standardized approval processes. Experience supporting global partner marketing programs across North America, EMEA, and APJ. Familiarity with two-tier distribution models and partner marketing programs involving MSPs, MSSPs, VARs, and distributors. Experience with Salesforce reporting, partner attribution, and pipeline tracking. Experience supporting PRM integrations, workflow automation, and data quality initiatives. Background in cybersecurity, SaaS, cloud, observability, or enterprise technology. Experience developing partner enablement materials and training programs.
