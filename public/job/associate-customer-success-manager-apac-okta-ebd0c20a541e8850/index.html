🏢 okta 📍 Bengaluru, India 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Secure Every Identity, from AI to Human Identity is the key to unlocking the potential of AI. Okta secures AI by building the trusted, neutral infrastructure that enables organizations to safely embrace this new era. This work requires a relentless drive to solve complex challenges with real-world stakes. We are looking for builders and owners who operate with speed and urgency and execute with excellence. This is an opportunity to do career-defining work. We're all in on this mission. If you are too, let's talk. JOB PURPOSE Okta's Silver customers across ASEAN, India, Korea and Hong Kong carry real renewal ARR and our best Silver-to-Gold pipeline, but there are too many of them for 1:1 coverage. As a Digital CSM on the Asia Silver team, you will turn Gainsight, Salesforce and usage signals into prioritised, repeatable motions that protect renewals, get customers to value faster, and surface qualified expansion. You won't hold a fixed book of relationships. You will own the system that decides which Silver customers get which touch, and you'll measure whether it works. If you like building scalable motions that change customer outcomes, and you care about results more than send volume, this is your role. WHAT YOU'LL DO Run the signal-to-action loop. Monitor renewal forecasts, Gainsight risk and CTAs, activation data and account context. Segment the Silver book into worst-case renewals, flat with churn risk, and flat with growth potential, and route each to the right motion. Protect the base. Prioritise the worst-case renewals [window to validate, e.g. 3 quarters out]. Trigger and track risk playbooks with named owners, and run targeted saves and Path-to-Green plans, escalating to AEs, Renewal Managers, Support or CSM leadership when needed. Improve adoption. Close post-sale activation gaps by driving the core value features (FastPass, Workflows, LCM, OIG, Device Assurance) through activation CTAs, onboarding programs and digital nudges. Help customers reach first value inside [90] days, and connect each feature to a business outcome the customer can point to. Identify expansion. Qualify mature, growth-ready accounts through 1:many outreach and partner cohorts. Support AEs in identifying growth opportunities with smaller tactical activities such as Lead Pass or adoption assessments. Build and run programs. Design multi-channel outreach (email sequences, webinars and cohort sessions, community and partner-enabled campaigns). Test and iterate on messaging, and build and maintain self-service assets. Flag gaps where assets don't exist. Use AI as part of the job. Use tools like Claude, Gemini and NotebookLM to synthesise account context, generate customer-ready content and automate your own workflows. We expect practical fluency, not familiarity. Measure and report. Report on revenue protected, churn risk reduced, activation speed, qualified expansion and Silver-to-Gold conversion, so leadership can see which programs move outcomes and which don't. Be the voice of the Silver customer. Surface patterns from the book to product, marketing and CS leadership. Partner across the field. Work with AEs, SEs, Renewal Managers, Support and partners to get the best outcome for each account without friction. WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR 3+ years in customer success, digital or scale CS, lifecycle marketing or customer marketing, ideally in SaaS or security A record of running 1:many programs with measured outcomes (activation, renewal, pipeline), not just campaign activity Proficiency with Gainsight (or similar) and Salesforce, including building segments, triggers and dashboards. Demonstrated AI fluency, shown through real deliverables, workflows or scalable motions The ability to read risk and adoption data and turn it into a clear next action Clear, confident writing for customers across diverse APJ markets. Comfort holding a renewal-risk conversation and escalating to the right owner, even when you don't hold the commercial relationship Working knowledge of identity and access concepts, or the curiosity to build it quickly. Okta Professional and Administrator certifications are expected within your first 90 days.
