AccuLynx: Senior Software Engineer

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Beloit, WI URL: http://acculynx.com Senior Software Engineer AccuLynx is a fast-growing SaaS provider of business management software for roofing contractors. With more than 10 years in the business and impressive year-over-year revenue growth, we have quickly established ourselves as the leading software product in this multi-billion dollar industry. AccuLynx is actively looking for smart and passionate Senior Software Engineers to join our growing Engineering team! We are headquartered out of Beloit, WI and would love to have you join us for major planning sessions and meetings, but this role has the option to be remote. We do not hire based on buzzwords or popular acronyms. We expect you to have mastered at least one language and be eager to master any technology or language in the future. If you want to be part of a collaborative team committed to providing a best-in-class software user experience, please read on! What You Will Do: Support Engineering team in delivering well-written, well-tested code to complete new features or address issues in the product Collaborate on the design, implementation, testing and review of new features and fixes Review code and contribute feedback to improve the quality, readability, and testability of the code base across Engineering Architect, design, develop, deploy and operate services that serve AccuLynx’s thousands of users Use Agile process to experiment and improve on software development practices at AccuLynx What might you work on? Implement real time communication tools to connect our customers. Create a fast and responsive work-scheduling application for use in the field. Build an automation platform so our customers can customize their workflow to reduce wasted movement. Solve advanced mapping and geolocation problems. Build out our public RESTful API. Review junior Engineering team members’ work for accuracy and provide coaching and feedback Perform other related duties as assigned Your Qualifications: Top notch coding skills 7-10 years of software development experience 7+ years of full-stack web development: frontend, backend and database Smart and gets things done Excellent written, verbal and interpersonal communication skills Belief that performance is a feature Experience with our stack: Vue.js, C#, Sql Server, Redis, ElasticSearch, Git Confidence you will make a difference in our product Leverages AI-powered development tools (e.g., GitHub Copilot, Claude Code) to accelerate coding, testing, and debugging workflows while maintaining high code quality standards Actively evaluates and integrates emerging AI coding assistants into the team's development process, sharing best practices to improve overall engineering velocity Bonus Points If You Have: Familiarity with Agile methodologies. Understanding of HTML5 and mobile responsive development. Contributions to OSS projects or Stackoverflow. Availability for after hours support. We work at a sustainable 40h/week pace but sometimes things go bump in the night. Why We Love AccuLynx: AccuLynx’s success as the #1 business management software for roofing contractors over the past 11 years is thanks to us investing in our employees, maintaining company values and focusing on a strong company culture. Our positive work environment has allowed us to retain employees that have been with us since our company’s inception and has provided the solid groundwork for us to develop an industry-leading product while exceeding our customer’s expectations. Because of our commitment to our company values and culture, we were recently officially recognized as a Great Place to Work Certified™ organization, with 90% of our employees naming AccuLynx as a great place to work. We’re proud to regularly be recognized for software, product and company culture achievements. Our team’s shared belief in AccuLynx's mission promotes a culture of collaboration, innovation and fun. We have built a benefits program to match the strength of our team. This program includes: Attractive compensation packages Flexible paid time off - 3 weeks off in your first year! Competitive health coverage (medical, dental, vision) 401K matching and safe harbor contributions Our Interview Process: We do not hire based on buzzwords or popular acronyms. We expect you to be a master of your domain and be eager to learn any technology or language in the future. We aim to reply to your application within a week of receiving it. Our interview process consists of several stages, each one allowing us get to know you better, professionally and technically. It is also an opportunity for you to gain a better understanding of our culture and the work we do at AccuLynx. The stages include: CV, open source code, or public portfolio review Phone interview with HR team Face to Face conversation with a few members of your team. Applicants must be able to work legally within the United States. Please, no 3rd party inquiries. AccuLynx is an Equal Opportunity Employer committed to inclusion and employing a diverse workforce. All applicants will receive consideration without regard to race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, sexual orientation, gender identity, gender expression, veteran status, disability, or other legally protected characteristics.
