About the Role: Gusto is looking for a Corporate Paralegal to own the operational backbone of our Corporate Legal function. You’ll run the recurring work that keeps a fast-growing, multi-entity, regulated company in good standing. That includes entity compliance, corporate licensing across our money transmission, PEO, and health insurance businesses, insurance renewals and claims, audit coordination, board and governance support, and real estate and lease tracking. You’ll also support M&A and other corporate transactions as our deal volume grows. You’ll work with outside counsel and service providers across this work, including our outside licensing partner. You’ll keep their work on schedule and check it for accuracy, so every filing, license, and deadline is right and examination-ready. You’ll also be a core contributor on regulatory examinations, managing request lists, evidence, and commitments. At Gusto, Legal & Compliance operates as an AI-native team. Much of this work already runs on AI-assisted drafting and tracking, and you’ll own taking that automation further, with human-in-the-loop controls for accuracy and accountability. If you’re not satisfied doing a repeatable task by hand twice, and you take pride in getting every filing and deadline right, this is the role. About the Team: Gusto’s Legal & Compliance team partners closely with leadership and teams across the business to enable innovation, facilitate responsible company growth, accelerate Gusto’s strategic priorities, and keep the company in good standing as it scales. We are passionate about applying AI-native ways of working, thoughtful risk management, and a daily service mindset to improve the experience of small business owners and their employees. Each team member is an owner, a trusted advisor, and a meaningful contributor to Gusto’s mission. Within L&C, Corporate Legal is a small, high-impact team that keeps Gusto’s foundation strong as the company scales. That means you’ll see your work power the company’s biggest moves and help shape how a modern, AI-forward corporate legal function runs. Here’s what you’ll do day-to-day: Entity Management: Own recurring entity administration across 20+ US and foreign entities: annual reports, franchise tax filings, registered agent management, good standing certificates, officer consents, and entity records and structure charts. Support formations, dissolutions, and post-acquisition integration. Licensing: Run the day-to-day management of Gusto’s licensing portfolio across money transmission, PEO, and health insurance, including applications, expansion filings, change notices, license conditions, surety bonds, and the regulatory filing calendar. You’ll manage our outside licensing partner, serve as the final check before anything reaches a regulator, and turn licensing requirements into clear procedures and guidance for teams across Gusto. Examinations & Regulatory Requests: Serve as a core contributor on regulatory examinations, driving request lists, evidence collection, document production, quality control, and findings and commitments through closure. You’ll coordinate high-risk data requests, manage regulator correspondence, escalate substantive matters, and keep documented evidence ready for every filing and exam. Board & Governance: Support Gusto’s board and governance cycle with minutes, consents, signature collection, and corporate recordkeeping. You’ll get a front-row seat to how a high-growth company is governed. Insurance Program Management: Partner with Finance to run Gusto’s corporate insurance renewal cycles, including renewal applications, underwriting questionnaires, and certificate requests, and support claims tracking with our broker. Audit Support: Coordinate the legal side of the annual audit by managing requests, tracking open items, drafting first-pass responses, and maintaining the litigation and liability tracker. Real Estate: Manage lease administration across our offices, including key dates, renewal and notice windows, amendments, subleases, and routine landlord and broker requests. Automation: Help reinvent how Corporate Legal works. You’ll start by automating our highest-volume work, then carry that further across the whole function, from governance and transactions to research and drafting. You’ll have real latitude to rethink workflows, build new AI-enabled tools, and set the standard for a modern corporate legal team, always with thoughtful human review. Here’s what we're looking for: 3+ years of corporate paralegal experience in house at a high growth or regulated company, or at a law firm with a strong corporate practice, ideally in fintech, payments, or money services Hands on experience with entity management and corporate governance (annual reports, registered agents, good standing certificates, consents, minutes, and corporate records) Experience with regulatory filings, licensing documentation, and change of control notices, including familiarity with state money transmission requirements (net worth, permissible investments, surety bonds, authorized delegates, periodic reporting) and state PEO licensing, and comfort working with regulators and outside counsel Excellent written and verbal communication, with credibility in front of Directors and senior executives on routine board and governance matters Sharp drafting and attention to detail across consents, minutes, certificates, and other corporate documents. You catch the mismatched entity name, outdated effective date, and missing signature page before anyone else does Strong organization and project management, with the drive to run multiple time sensitive workstreams independently, and high discretion with confidential corporate information Experience supporting M&A or secondary transactions, and with equity administration and cap table platforms (e.g., Shareworks) A Notary Public commission (NY or CA), or willingness to obtain one An obsession with AI. You use tools like Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Glean daily for legal research, drafting, and redesigning real workflows, and you review AI output before relying on it Our cash compensation amount for this role is $93,615/yr to $115,459/yr in Denver & most major metro locations, and $110,640/yr to $136,456/yr for San Francisco & New York.
