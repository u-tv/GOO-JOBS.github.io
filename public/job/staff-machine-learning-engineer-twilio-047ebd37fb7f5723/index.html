About the job We are actively recruiting for this role to fill an existing vacancy. Responsibilities In this role, you’ll: Conduct deep-dive research into and implement state-of-the-art LLM inference and orchestration. Identify opportunities to leverage small language via fine-tuning/distillation. Build ML systems that hold up at Twilio scale. Set standards and tooling for building evals across a range of AI/ML capabilities. Rapidly acquire new technical skills and knowledge in a fast-paced, high-delivery environment. We are always looking for people who will bring something new to the table! *Required: 8+ years applied ML/AI experience with a track record of owning technically ambitious systems end to end rather than contributing to someone else's design. Comfort operating in a 0-to-1 setting with ambiguous requirements and a strong bias for action. Proficiency in Python programming language. Experience building cloud-based services on AWS, GCP, or Azure, with high-volume data, streaming or real-time inference paths, and a range of data stores. Strong fundamentals of statistical machine learning algorithms, transformer models, LLMs. Extensive experience with ML Ops and LLM Ops patterns, including designing and implementing rigorous evaluation metrics for non-deterministic AI features at scale. Strong communication skills and highly effective collaborator who works well with teammates. Familiarity with AI-assisted development tools (e.g., Claude, Codex, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, etc.). Desired: Prior experience building evals for autonomous agents and large language models. Evidence of technical leadership/impact, such as contributions to open-source data/ML projects and/or published technical presentations, blog posts, papers, or research.
