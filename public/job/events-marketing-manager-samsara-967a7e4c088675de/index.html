Events Marketing Manager

🏢 samsara 📍 CDMX3 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are Samsara (NYSE: IOT) is the pioneer of the Connected Operations™ Cloud, which is a platform that enables organizations that depend on physical operations to harness Internet of Things (IoT) data to develop actionable insights and improve their operations. At Samsara, we are helping improve the safety, efficiency and sustainability of the physical operations that power our global economy. Representing more than 40% of global GDP, these industries are the infrastructure of our planet, including agriculture, construction, field services, transportation, and manufacturing — and we are excited to help digitally transform their operations at scale. Working at Samsara means you’ll help define the future of physical operations and be on a team that’s shaping an exciting array of product solutions, including Video-Based Safety, Vehicle Telematics, Apps and Driver Workflows, and Equipment Monitoring. As part of a recently public company, you’ll have the autonomy and support to make an impact as we build for the long term. About the role: Interested in joining a top-notch marketing team? We are looking for an energetic, highly organized, team player who loves a fast-paced environment and thrives on results. In this role, you will meticulously plan, execute & measure new events and programs for Samsara. These events can include industry trade shows, hosted events, customer and executive experiences, regional summits, educational programs and workshops, strategic sponsorships, partner activations, and other high-impact field marketing programs. This is a highly cross-functional role that requires close partnership across Sales, Account Development, Product Marketing, Marketing Operations & Product Management. You should be results-driven, detail-oriented, and passionate about driving business impact. This is a fast-growing area of the business that plays a strategic role in our future success. This is a remote role open to candidates based in Mexico City and will require travel up to 50% in Mexico. You should apply if: You want to impact the industries that run our world: Your efforts will result in real-world impact—helping to keep the lights on, get food into grocery stores, reduce emissions, and most importantly, ensure workers return home safely. You are the architect of your own career: If you put in the work, this role won’t be your last at Samsara. We set up our employees for success and have built a culture that encourages rapid career development, countless opportunities to experiment and master your craft in a hyper growth environment. You’re energized by our opportunity: The vision we have to digitize large sectors of the global economy requires your full focus and best efforts to bring forth creative, ambitious ideas for our customers. You want to be with the best: At Samsara, we win together, celebrate together and support each other. You will be surrounded by a high-calibre team that will encourage you to do your best. In this role, you will: Own trade shows and hosted events strategy, planning, execution, and success for a strategic company industry vertical, territory or segment Execute end-to-end event program management and logistics from initial planning to post-event program follow up, including vendor management, budget management, event promotions, sales enablement, data cleansing/tracking, KPI measurement, and maintenance of all event assets Manage event budget forecast and reporting, evaluate and communicate results against goals, and generate learnings for improvement Facilitate timely event lead processing within Salesforce, measure event impact, and develop post-event reports and communicate insights Own project management tools, documentation, event calendar, and processes used for managing event marketing programs & tradeshows Bring energy, creativity, and new ideas to build a successful event program Champion, role model, and embed Samsara’s cultural principles (Focus on Customer Success, Build for the Long Term, Adopt a Growth Mindset, Be Inclusive, Win as a Team) as we scale globally and across new offices Minimum requirements for the role: 3+ years of professional experience in field marketing, event marketing, or integrated marketing, with demonstrated ownership of customer-facing programs from strategy and planning through execution and post-event measurement. Proven ability to manage multiple programs simultaneously in a fast-paced environment, navigate shifting priorities, and consistently deliver against deadlines without compromising execution quality. Strong cross-functional collaboration skills, with demonstrated experience partnering closely with Sales, Marketing, Operations, Product Marketing, agencies, vendors, and other internal and external stakeholders. Excellent project management and organizational skills, with exceptional attention to detail and the ability to build scalable processes, documentation, timelines, and operational frameworks. Proficient using a CRM (e.g., Salesforce) and/or Marketing Automation platforms. Must be fluent in Spanish and English. Based in/near Mexico City and comfortable going into the office when needed. Ability to travel up to 50% for events. An ideal candidate also has: A positive attitude, a team player mentality, and an unmatched love and enthusiasm for all things events. A demand generation mindset for event assessment, planning, and execution. Experience communicating with and presenting to senior executives, VIPs, customers, and partners. Experience managing strategic partnerships, sponsorships, and third-party event relationships to maximize brand presence and business impact. Outstanding skills in problem-solving, project management, creative thinking, written/verbal communication, and presenting.
