Institutional Sales Manager, Hedge Funds

🏢 coinbase 📍 Remote - USA 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Ready to do the most impactful work of your career? Coinbase is building the future of finance for people, institutions and their agents, with one place to trade crypto, equities, derivatives and prediction markets, use stablecoins, and connect AI agents with clear controls. The bar is high and the pace is intense, and we like it that way. This isn’t a place for complacency. It’s a place to be pushed past your perceived limits and do the best work of your career. If you’re ready to build the future of finance with a championship team who refuse to settle for “good enough,” you belong here. Coinbase is a remote-first, but not remote-only company. Expect to get together quarterly for in-person working sessions As an Institutional Sales Manager on the US Hedge Fund sales team within Coinbase Institutional, you'll own a book of US hedge funds and grow it. Coinbase Institutional serves leading hedge funds with full-service prime brokerage: qualified custody, agency execution, portfolio margin and cross-margin, US futures through our FCM, and international derivatives. You'll bring in new funds, activate them on new products, and expand what existing clients do with us, representing Coinbase's counterparty strength, track record of trust, and comprehensive product suite across digital assets and related asset classes. What you'll do: Own and grow a named book of US hedge funds: multi-manager platforms, macro, quant, relative value, and crypto-native funds. Originate new relationships by building target lists and getting in front of decision-makers, including PMs, COOs, CFOs, and heads of trading and treasury. Lead the full prime sales motion for each named client, from pitch to live trading, across custody, execution, portfolio margin and cross-margin, US futures, international perpetuals and options, staking, and financing, partnering with Legal, Credit, Risk, and Onboarding on commercial terms, documentation, and operational due diligence. Grow wallet share in existing accounts and run go-to-market campaigns for new product and feature launches. Keep pipeline and forecasts accurate in Salesforce and bring client feedback back to Product, helping shape how Coinbase competes for hedge fund business. Required Skills and Experience: 5+ years selling prime brokerage, financing, clearing, or execution services to hedge funds at a bank, prime broker, FCM, or crypto prime, with a track record of growing a book (top candidates will typically have 8+ years). Fluency in prime brokerage economics (margin, financing, securities lending, cross-margining, clearing) and in how funds choose, split, and move prime brokers. Relationships with decision-makers at US hedge funds and a track record of turning them into revenue, including experience closing multi-product mandates and growing revenue inside existing accounts. Comfort with the legal, credit, and operational work of onboarding institutional clients, with a working understanding of crypto market structure (spot vs. futures, basis trades, custody models) or clear evidence you learn complex markets fast. Discipline with Salesforce and pipeline management. Utilizes generative AI responsibly, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality.
