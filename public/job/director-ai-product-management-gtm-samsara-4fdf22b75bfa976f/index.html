Director, AI Product Management (GTM)

🏢 samsara 📍 Remote - US 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are Samsara (NYSE: IOT) is the pioneer of the Connected Operations™ Cloud, which is a platform that enables organizations that depend on physical operations to harness Internet of Things (IoT) data to develop actionable insights and improve their operations. At Samsara, we are helping improve the safety, efficiency and sustainability of the physical operations that power our global economy. Representing more than 40% of global GDP, these industries are the infrastructure of our planet, including agriculture, construction, field services, transportation, and manufacturing — and we are excited to help digitally transform their operations at scale. Working at Samsara means you’ll help define the future of physical operations and be on a team that’s shaping an exciting array of product solutions, including Video-Based Safety, Vehicle Telematics, Apps and Driver Workflows, and Equipment Monitoring. As part of a recently public company, you’ll have the autonomy and support to make an impact as we build for the long term. About the role: Samsara's Go-To-Market AI product team builds the internal products our sellers, customer success managers, and support reps use every day. As Director, AI Product Management (GTM), you will own an AI-powered GTM platform that creates a step change in impact, brings in new ways of working, centralizes data and insights, and acts as the foundational system for our GTM organization—from problem identification to development, adoption, and measurable business impact. Functioning much like a general manager with product at the core, this broad leadership role requires you to build, coach, and grow a high-performing team of product managers while driving enablement, cross-functional alignment, and strategic execution. This is a remote position open to candidates residing in the US. In this role, you will: Own the strategy, roadmap, and business outcomes for our internal AI-native GTM platform, and be accountable for whether the business adopts it. Manage the platform as one portfolio, so every part of it shares the same data, intelligence and building blocks, and users get one consistent experience. Build an AI-native product practice. Take the best of traditional product management, such as PRDs, prioritization, launch readiness and measurement, and reinvent it with AI so the team moves faster and builds better. Understand internal users' problems and analyze data to identify what to build, sometimes finding the need before users can articulate it. Write clear requirements and partner closely with engineering, which leads technical implementation, to deliver a high-quality product. Make sure you and your team test the product firsthand. Keep pace with an engineering team that moves fast and ships at high velocity. Gather input from the field quickly, turn it into a clear direction for engineering, and use AI to shorten the loop from feedback to shipped improvement. Define what quality means for AI products, including accuracy, trust, evaluation and human review, and hold every release to that bar. Drive adoption by coaching sellers and partnering with Sales Enablement and Product Marketing, and measure the business impact of what you ship. Set clear, measurable goals for adoption, productivity and business impact, instrument the product to track them, and use the results to decide what to build next. Resolve cross-functional dependencies, such as aligning teams on shared datasets with Data Engineering or getting Product Marketing content into the AI knowledge base, and take on the analytics and operational work needed to make the product succeed. Use AI tools in your own work to build prototypes and mini-apps that engineers can take forward into production, and apply AI to improve how Samsara's go-to-market teams work. Hands-on AI experience is a requirement for this role. Champion, role model, and embed Samsara’s cultural principles (Focus on Customer Success, Build for the Long Term, Adopt a Growth Mindset, Be Inclusive, Win as a Team) as we scale globally and across new offices Hire, develop and lead an inclusive, engaged, and high performing team of product managers. Minimum requirements for the role: 10+ years of experience in product management, or in strategy and operations roles where you owned a product built with engineering. 5+ years of experience leading, developing and mentoring product managers or product-oriented teams, while staying close to execution. Demonstrated ownership of an internal or business-facing product, including defining the problem, working with engineering to deliver it, driving adoption and showing measurable business impact. Hands-on experience with AI, such as building or shipping AI-enabled products, using AI tools in your own work, or prototyping with them. Strong analytical skills, with examples of using data to identify problems, shape product decisions and measure results. Technical fluency and ability to establish credibility with engineers. Strong problem-solving and structured thinking, with a record of driving work to completion across teams and stakeholders. An ideal candidate also has: Experience owning internal tools or AI products used by sales, customer success or other go-to-market teams. Familiarity with the systems and data that run a go-to-market organization, such as CRM, forecasting and sales enablement tools. Experience defining quality, evaluation and trust standards for AI products. Experience coaching end users and working with enablement teams to get a new tool used. Experience leading product teams that use AI throughout their own work, from research and requirements to prototyping and launch.
