Job Description About this job We are seeking an ambitious and results-oriented Director of Sales to join our Retail Merchandising Analytics team. In this individual contributor role, you will drive new revenue growth, expand existing client relationships, and position NIQ's Retail Merchandising Analytics solutions while expanding opportunities across the broader NIQ portfolio, including collaboration programs, measurement, retail media intelligence, and emerging commerce solutions. You will combine the drive of a hunter with the discipline of a farmer, acquiring new logos, growing established accounts, and closing complex deals with strategic precision. Working in close partnership with Account owners, the Implementation team, Product Area Experts, and the Customer Success team, you will ensure that prospects and clients realize maximum value from our offerings. You will also serve as the voice of the customer, bringing market feedback and emerging needs back to our product management and operational teams to fuel future innovation. Responsibilities Pipeline Management Build and manage a robust sales pipeline through disciplined opportunity management, rigorous pipeline hygiene, and consistent rhythm and pace. Deliver accurate and timely sales forecasts, ensuring a strong pipeline-to-quota coverage at all times. Client Acquisition Proactively identify and acquire new clients to expand the book of business through targeted prospecting, lead generation, and consultative engagement. Execute land-and-expand strategies, starting with pilot engagements and growing into broader solution deployments. Account Management and Expansion Cultivate deep relationships with existing clients, identifying upsell and cross-sell opportunities that align with client business goals. Act as a trusted advisor to client stakeholders, understanding their challenges and positioning our Retail Analytics solutions as strategic assets. Consultative Selling and Solution Development Use a consultative sales approach to uncover client needs, define problem statements, and partner with Product Area Experts to elevate the business. Scope, position, and close deals that deliver measurable business impact for clients. Platform and Solution Selling Position NIQ's Retail Merchandising Analytics solutions, including pricing, assortment, merchandising, space planning, and related analytics offerings, as strategic drivers of client growth and profitability. Identify opportunities to expand client relationships through complementary solutions across the NIQ portfolio, including collaborative programs, retail media intelligence, and emerging commerce innovations when aligned to business needs. Lead value-based discussions that connect analytics, collaboration, and media investments to measurable business outcomes. Partner with Product Area Experts to develop proposals, pilots, and business cases that demonstrate both immediate client value and long-term platform expansion opportunities. Bring market feedback and emerging client needs back to Product, Marketing, and Leadership teams to help inform future solution development and go-to-market strategies. Sales Presentations and Demonstrations Prepare and deliver compelling, customized sales presentations that clearly articulate the business value and differentiation of our solutions. Carry out effective product demonstrations in partnership with Product Area Experts and Sales Engineers. Negotiation and Deal Closure Lead high-stakes negotiations, structuring complex deals that satisfy client needs while driving profitable growth. Close business with a disciplined, strategic approach to contract and commercial terms. Market Research and Competitive Intelligence Conduct continuous research on industry trends, competitor activities, and emerging technologies. Leverage insights to refine sales strategies and position our Retail Analytics solutions competitively. Cross-Functional Collaboration Partner closely with account owners, Product Area Experts, and Customer Success managers throughout the sales cycle to deliver tailored solutions and ensure long-term client success. Provide structured, actionable feedback to Product, Marketing, and Delivery Customer Success teams to inform roadmaps and go-to-market strategies. Training and Mentoring Provide guidance, mentorship, and sales best practices to junior team members, contributing to the development of a high-performing sales culture. Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Business, Marketing, Analytics, or a related field; Master's degree preferred. 10-12 years of consultative sales experience selling data, with a proven record of exceeding sales targets and closing complex, multi-stakeholder deals. Expertise selling merchandising, pricing, assortment, space planning, category management, or other analytically driven retail solutions is strongly preferred. Experience selling data, analytics, or measurement platforms is a plus along with familiarity with retail media, commerce media, or AI-enabled retail solutions. Exceptional strategic negotiation skills with a demonstrated ability to influence C-level executives and senior stakeholders. Outstanding communication and presentation skills; ability to translate complex technical concepts into clear business value. Highly organized and disciplined in pipeline management, deal qualification, and sales forecasting. Comfortable operating independently while collaborating effectively across a matrixed organization. Willingness to travel for client meetings, conferences, and industry events as needed. Success Profile The ideal candidate will achieve the following within the first 12-18 months: Consistently achieve or surpass quarterly and annual revenue goals. Maintain 3x pipeline coverage and high win rates on qualified opportunities. Successfully land new strategic accounts, building strong foundations for long-term partnerships. Grow revenue within existing accounts by expanding solution footprint and increasing client dependency on delivered Retail Merchandising Analytics insights. Build multi-solution or platform deals, with a target of one new booking that includes two or more solutions. Demonstrate mastery of complex deal cycles, from prospecting and qualification through to contract negotiation and deal closure. Develop opportunities across emerging solution areas, including retail media and commerce innovation offerings, while maintaining a primary focus on Retail Merchandising Analytics revenue growth. Stay ahead of industry trends, contributing thought leadership that positions the company as a market leader. Work seamlessly with account owners, Product Area Experts, and implementation and Customer Success teams to deliver exceptional client experiences and long-term value.
