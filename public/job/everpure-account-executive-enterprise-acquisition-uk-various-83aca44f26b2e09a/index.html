Everpure: Account Executive, Enterprise (Acquisition) UK

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote, United Kingdom Everpure (NYSE: P) has evolved from storage pioneer to data platform, closing fiscal 2026 with $3.7 billion in revenue, its first billion-dollar quarter, and accelerating growth into FY27. Our strategic agenda spans the companies defining the next era of technology - hyperscalers, AI labs, the AI hardware supply chain, data platform providers, and the broader AI ecosystem. This type of work—work that changes the world—is what the tech industry was founded on. So, if you're ready to seize the endless opportunities and leave your mark, come join us. The Role We are seeking a highly driven, strategic, and consultative Account Executive to lead the full sales cycle and drive new business growth across our strategic Enterprise accounts in the UK. This patch is a net-new logo hunter's playground. The territory spans some of the most demanding, deep-pocketed accounts in the UK: global quant trading houses and hedge funds where milliseconds and data integrity define competitive edge, systemically important financial institutions and asset managers with balance sheets in the hundreds of billions, a Big Four professional services giant, a global advertising and marketing holding company, and a mix of consumer and industrial brands with complex, multi-region infrastructure needs. These are organisations where data is the business - where how it's managed, moved, protected and put to work determines whether they win or lose. None of them are customers today. That's the opportunity: a blank sheet, a strategic account list built for someone who wants to build relationships from zero, navigate sophisticated buying committees, and win logos by solving real data management problems - not inherit a book and defend it. What You'll Do Drive business growth by leading the full enterprise sales cycle, from strategic prospecting to consistently exceeding sales quotas. Apply solution and consultative selling to uncover business challenges using data and co-create tailored solutions focused on measurable customer outcomes. Develop multi-level, trusted relationships to secure and sustain executive access and engagement for maximum enterprise impact on key accounts. Execute strategic account management and account expansion (cross-sell/upsell/new logos) by developing and maintaining multi-year plans for high-value customers. Ensure operational excellence and consistent execution by managing sales systems, leveraging tools, and proactively collaborating with internal teams and external partners. What You'll Bring Demonstrated success in direct and indirect enterprise accounts, with a consistent record of meeting or exceeding quotas. A strong 'sales hunter' mentality, with a relentless focus on proactive new logo acquisition and identifying high-potential opportunities. Expertise in strategic account management disciplines, including deep account planning and relationship building across all organizational levels. A robust background in consultative selling, leading discussions with unique insights and building business cases tied to measurable outcomes (ROI, TCO). Proven ability to acquire new customers and drive account expansion by identifying white space and effectively leveraging channel partner relationships. A deep understanding of the storage industry, cloud computing, and modern data center architecture. High level of motivation and intrinsic drive, including emotional intelligence, strategic mindset, and accountability for delivering results. Business proficiency in English.
