Job Description

Fin is the AI Customer Agent company on a mission to help businesses provide perfect customer experiences. Our AI Agent Fin is the highest-performing AI Customer Agent on the market today, enabling businesses to deliver impeccable, always-on customer support across the customer journey – from service, to sales, to ecommerce. Powered by our own AI models, Fin resolves complex customer issues end-to-end across every channel, with minimal set-up and integration. Fin can also be combined with our natively integrated Intercom help desk for one single system that is designed to meet the needs of modern day support teams. Founded in 2011, Fin became one of the fastest growing companies and remains one of the largest private software companies in the world with nearly 30,000 global businesses using our products to transform their customer support. Driven by our core values, we push boundaries, build with speed and intensity, and relentlessly deliver incredible value to our customers. What's the opportunity? Solutions PMM at Fin owns the narratives that shape how Fin wins in market: the stories the field uses in customer conversations, the narratives that power our campaigns, and the competitive strategy that helps us take and defend ground. This is a senior IC role. You drive impact through the quality of your thinking and your ability to move sales, enablement, and campaigns to execute with confidence. This role is competitive-first. Your first mission is to establish our compete function from the ground up - the intelligence, the messaging, and the system that keeps the field ahead of a fast-moving market. It's roughly 70% competitive today, expanding over time to support sales plays and enablement as the function matures. In a moment where AI makes it easy to copy features and stand up new competitors overnight, this is one of the highest-leverage roles in PMM. The Role You'll build and own how Fin positions itself against the competition - differentiation against our tier 1 competitors, a sustainable competitive intelligence program, and the plays and content that let the field act on it in real deals. You'll decide what &quot;great compete&quot; looks like at Fin and stand it up. You'll work directly with sales leadership, research, SEs, GTM strategy, and sales enablement. You'll be hands-on with the product, the market, and every competitor's moves. What will I be doing? Establish Fin's competitive function from the ground up - the intelligence, the messaging, and the operating system that keeps the field current without creating noise Define our competitive differentiation messaging against tier 1 competitors, and make sure it lands in compete campaigns, content, and field conversations Stand up a sustainable, AI-driven competitive intelligence program with Research, Solutions Engineering and GTM - tracking moves, updating positioning, and keeping the field ahead Operationalise competitive strategy so the field can act on it: battle cards, competitive plays, and objection handling that reps actually use Synthesise win/loss data, customer interviews, and market signals into strategic recommendations that feed back into product, marketing and ICP decisions Co-build the proactive compete motion - the sales plays and campaigns that drive win rates, not just defend against losses Over time, extend into sales plays and field enablement as the compete function matures - raising how the field sells against every alternative Create high-quality core PMM assets yourself, and maintain the competitive 'brain' of context that feeds our AI-first approach What we're looking for This role is for someone who is a hybrid: part strategist, part storyteller, part technologist. You move between customer insight, product detail, competitive analysis, and creative expression without friction.
