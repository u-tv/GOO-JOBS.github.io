Headquarters: Home, West Virginia, United States URL: https://davies-group.com Business Operations Manager Department: Forensic Accounting Employment Type: Permanent - Full Time Location: Home United States Compensation: $92,800 - $139,200 / year Description MDD, a Davies Company, is a community of outstanding people. We welcome different perspectives, support each other’s ambitions and grow together. In a fast-changing business environment, we adapt and look ahead. We succeed because we are multi-talented: in the skills of our teams, specialisms, and sector expertise. Working together, we are greater than the sum of our parts. The Business Operations Manager, FAE combines administrative support with a broader focus on practitioner productivity and operational effectiveness. The role removes day-to-day operational barriers and streamlines access to internal resources, allowing practice leaders and team members to focus on client service, business development, and delivery. Based in North America and reporting to the Chief of Staff, FAE, the role is the regional operational contact for the forensic accounting and forensic engineering practices. The Business Operations Manager will help team members navigate internal processes, resolve day-to-day business issues, and coordinate with corporate functions. The successful candidate will build strong relationships across the organization and partner with IT, Finance, Legal, Risk, Procurement, Marketing, Facilities, and other shared services teams to help the FAE division operate efficiently and effectively. Key Responsibilities Practice Operations & Service Delivery Serve as the primary operational contact for FAE in North America, connecting team members with internal processes, resources, and expertise. Manage operational requests from intake through resolution, maintaining ownership, monitoring progress, and escalating as needed. Monitor regional issues and recurring challenges, anticipating emerging support needs in line with practice priorities. Support practice reporting, metrics, and business tracking as requested by the Chief of Staff. Support operational initiatives led by the Chief of Staff or FAE leadership, that may require global support from time to time. Cross-Functional Partnership & Integration Partner with IT, Finance, Legal, Procurement, Marketing, Risk Management, Facilities, and other shared services to address practice needs. Coordinate multi-stakeholder requests, establishing clear responsibilities, dependencies, and next steps. Represent FAE’s operational needs within corporate processes, approval workflows, and service channels. Build effective partner relationships to improve responsiveness and team member experience. Coordinate operational requirements for integrating new teams, offices, and business units into FAE. Client & Contract Administration Coordinate client due diligence, vendor questionnaires and registrations, insurance certificates, NDAs, and related compliance documentation. Obtain accurate, complete, and timely information from internal experts to support client and commercial requests. Manage client contract administration, routing agreements to Legal and communicating billing, reporting, rate, and other operational obligations to relevant teams. Support contract and rate renewal tracking, giving commercial owners timely notice for review and negotiation. Respond to routine practitioner questions on existing contract terms, processes, and documentation; refer interpretation and legal questions to the Chief of Staff. Maintain records and reusable response content to support consistent, efficient handling of recurring requests. Process Improvement & Knowledge Management Identify recurring operational issues, recommend workflow, guidance, and service improvements, and develop practical solutions to reduce practitioners’ administrative burden. Maintain FAE procedure, contact information, and practical operating guidance. Support the rollout and adoption of operational tools, processes, and technology. Communicate operational processes, changes, and resources clearly across the practice. Skills, knowledge & expertise Bachelor's degree in business administration, operations management, or a related field, or equivalent relevant experience. Five or more years of experience in business operations, professional services administration, practice management, or a similar cross-functional role. Experience working in professional services, consulting, engineering, accounting, insurance, legal services, or another knowledge-based organization, preferred. Demonstrated ability to manage multiple requests, priorities, and stakeholders simultaneously. Strong organizational, written and verbal communication, relationship management, and problem-solving skills. Experience coordinating client due diligence, vendor registration, compliance documentation, contract administration, or related requests, improving cross-functional business processes, and developing practical operating guidance. Ability to work independently, exercise sound judgment, and navigate a matrixed organization. To apply: https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/davies-business-operations-manager
