Recruiting Operations Manager

🏢 mongodb 📍 Austin; New York City; Palo Alto; San Francisco 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

MongoDB is seeking a technical, execution-obsessed individual to join the Recruiting Operations team as a RecOps Partner. In this role, you will oversee RecTech and Process Optimization, serving as the primary strategic partner and operational liaison to HR Tech, translating Talent Acquisition strategy into an actionable systems roadmap. You will architect complex operational workflows, drive complex, multi-quarter roadmaps and bring the technical fluency and business acumen needed to evaluate the viability and impact of technical solutions. Reporting directly to the Sr. Manager, CoreOps, you will operate with high autonomy to drive cross-functional execution and change management. You will provide strategic guidance, technical domain oversight, and peer mentorship to other individuals within the team, supporting them as individual product owners of their deliverables while elevating operational rigor across the team. We are looking to speak to candidates who are based in New York City, Austin, Palo Alto, or San Francisco for our hybrid working model. What You'll Do Drive RecTech Modernization & Tooling Strategy: Act as the primary liaison to HR Tech to distill TA vision into a cohesive technology roadmap. Oversee high-complexity initiatives, such as the deployment of our Offer-to-Hire workflow using AI and automation and pilots for enterprise-grade interview and debrief intelligence suites Architect Complex Workflows & Systems: Develop end-to-end process maps for all key recruiting workflows to identify friction points and structural opportunities. Architect major workflow redesigns, such as overhauling the end-to-end offer process to minimize manual interventions Optimize Greenhouse as a Core System of Work: Consolidate high-priority issue areas across the hiring lifecycle to eliminate fragmented, manual workflows. Leverage native Greenhouse features and strategic integrations to standardize processes, lower compliance risks, eliminate administrative overhead, and elevate data integrity Drive Cross-Functional Execution & Autonomy: Independently own and drive goals, steering change management and adoption across global teams for all systems and process updates. Resolve complex escalations, operating with autonomy while proactively flagging for support when necessary Mentor Junior Members of the Team: Act as a dedicated mentor and sounding board for the RecTech and Process Optimization sub-domain owners, supporting their development into strong Product Owners without having direct administrative or people management duties Leverage Data to Drive Strategic Outcomes: Proactively identify internal TA needs (recruiters, coordinators, hiring teams), act as a compelling communicator across cross-functional groups, and regularly leverage data diagnostics to drive operational outcomes What You'll Bring Advanced Expertise: 8+ years of experience architecting complex operational workflows, recruiting technology evaluations and implementations, and multi-quarter systems roadmaps Scope & Complexity: Proven track record of independently owning multi-faceted goals, driving cross-functional execution, and resolving complex operational escalations. Operates with high autonomy and demonstrates sound judgment on when to flag for escalation Commitment to Mentorship & Influence: Proactively identifies TA needs, acts as a compelling communicator across technical and non-technical stakeholders, and demonstrates a proven ability to mentor and uplevel junior team members Technical Aptitude & Tooling Expertise: Deep technical knowledge of core recruiting platforms (e.g., Greenhouse, Gem, Guide).
