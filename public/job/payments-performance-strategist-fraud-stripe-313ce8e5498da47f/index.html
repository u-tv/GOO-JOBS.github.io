Payments Performance Strategist, Fraud

🏢 stripe 📍 San Francisco, CA 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies - from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups - use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team The Global Payments Performance Team identifies payment optimization opportunities for Stripe’s merchants. We serve a critical role both in demonstrating thought leadership on challenging payment topics, and also partnering with our high-potential merchants to set them up for long term success. We want a partnership with Stripe to be a competitive advantage for our customers, large and small, by providing them with best-in-class technology in concert with insightful and specific advice on how to harness this technology to achieve their business goals. What you’ll do The Global Payments Performance Team leads data-driven discussions with existing and prospective merchants on payment optimization strategies across acceptance, fraud, card network costs, and cross-border flows. In this role, you will bring deep fraud expertise to that work, helping merchants balance fraud prevention with payment conversion, customer experience, and cost. You will be instrumental in building our optimization narratives and tools and delivering on our merchant engagement model. You will also collaborate with Sales, deploying your expertise to differentiate Stripe in competitive opportunities, and partner with our Customer Success organization to help merchants succeed with Stripe. Responsibilities Lead discussions with existing and prospective merchants, and provide the Sales team with tools and training to lead discussions with merchants and prospects, with a focus on fraud prevention and its impact on overall payment performance. Serve as both an internal and merchant-facing expert on fraud optimization strategies, helping merchants balance fraud losses, legitimate payment conversion and customer experience. Gather and build on Stripe’s wide-ranging payments expertise to create optimization narratives/tools supported by rigorous analysis. Use existing tools to deploy sophisticated optimization playbooks and analyses across Stripe’s customer portfolio, identifying drivers of fraud and recommending improvements to fraud controls and strategies. Synthesize and relay customer feedback and our findings to inform Stripe’s product roadmap, including unmet fraud prevention needs and insights from competitive evaluations. Provide feedback on our tools and playbooks to help continuously improve our self-serve offerings, and translate fraud engagement learnings into repeatable analyses, guidance, and training. Support competitive fraud solution evaluations by helping merchants assess performance, operational requirements, and total economic value. Who you are We're looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement. Minimum requirements 7+ years of relevant experience in payments, e-commerce fraud strategy, risk analytics, or fraud prevention advisory, including improving merchant payment-fraud outcomes. Deep understanding of e-commerce fraud prevention and the tradeoffs between fraud losses, false positives, authentication friction, payment acceptance, and customer experience. Proficiency in SQL and experience translating large-scale transaction analysis into actionable recommendations and measurable business outcomes. Experience providing subject matter expertise and strategic advisory to merchants or internal business stakeholders on payments and fraud optimization. Experience leveraging AI tooling for complex analysis, and building AI based playbooks for scaling expertise. Preferred qualifications Experience owning fraud strategy, using third-party fraud solutions, or working directly with enterprise merchants in a fraud strategy, advisory, or solutions role. Understanding of card network rules and regulations, local payment methods, and the regulatory environment (PSD2/PSD3), particularly where they intersect with fraud prevention, 3DS/SCA, liability shift, and fraud monitoring programs. Experience running competitive evaluations and measuring fraud reduction alongside conversion, operational costs, and overall business impact. Experience with dashboard building, modeling, or reporting.
