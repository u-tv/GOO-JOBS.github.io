Senior Security Engineer, Offensive Security

🏢 dropbox 📍 Remote - US: Select locations 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Role Description As a Senior Offensive Security Engineer within Dropbox Security, you will investigate how attackers could compromise our products and infrastructure, including AI-enabled applications and agentic systems. You will help identify meaningful attack paths and improve the controls intended to prevent, detect or contain them. This is a hands-on offensive security and threat research role with broad scope and meaningful impact. You will independently define and lead complex investigations and multi-phase red team exercises, prioritize work based on risk, develop new attack techniques, and build tooling and automation that expand the reach of our security testing. You will combine practical AI security expertise with broader offensive security skills across applications, cloud infrastructure, and identity systems. Working closely with Product Security, Detection & Response, Threat Intelligence, Privacy, and engineering teams, you will translate technical findings into clear business risk, recommend practical improvements, and validate their effectiveness through retesting. Sound judgment, clear communication, responsible execution, and ownership through verified outcomes are essential to the role. You will help shape the Red Team’s technical direction while strengthening its tools, research methods, and testing practices. Alongside experienced researchers, you will bring your own areas of expertise, investigate emerging technologies, and advance Dropbox’s offensive security capabilities, with opportunities to deepen your expertise, publish research, and see your work directly influence security decisions across Dropbox. Our Engineering Career Framework is viewable by anyone outside the company and describes what’s expected for our engineers at each of our career levels. Check out our blog post on this topic and more here . Responsibilities Plan and execute offensive security assessments by defining objectives, scope, rules of engagement, and safeguards, adapting your approach as new evidence emerges. Investigate unfamiliar applications, infrastructure, APIs, and trust boundaries to identify and trace meaningful attack paths. Develop and safely demonstrate exploits and attack chains that clearly establish potential impact to Dropbox. Build and improve offensive security tools, agents, automation, and testing harnesses, ensuring results are reliable, repeatable, and appropriately validated. Assess security risks across AI-enabled applications, agents, tool integrations, and supporting infrastructure, including the responsible use of AI-assisted research techniques. Partner with security, privacy, legal, and engineering teams to communicate risk, strengthen prevention and detection capabilities, address root causes, and verify improvements through retesting. Share research, mentor colleagues and improve the team’s methods through lessons from completed work. Many teams at Dropbox run Services with on-call rotations, which entails being available for calls during both core and non-core business hours. If a team has an on-call rotation, all engineers on the team are expected to participate in the rotation as part of their employment. Applicants are encouraged to ask for more details of the rotations to which the applicant is applying. Requirements 9 + years in offensive security, vulnerability research, red teaming or closely related security engineering, or equivalent demonstrated capability. A track record of independently leading complex investigations or engagements and discovering and exploiting non-trivial vulnerabilities. Understanding of application security, authentication and authorisation, cloud infrastructure, APIs and software supply chains, with deep practical expertise in one or more areas and the ability to trace attack paths across systems and trust boundaries. Hands-on experience attacking or evaluating LLMs, AI agents, ML systems, AI-enabled applications, or agentic architectures . Familiarity with security risks in AI-enabled applications, agents and tool integrations, and the ability to investigate unfamiliar technologies. Ability to write code to build or adapt offensive tools and automation, and to read and investigate unfamiliar production codebases. Preferred Qualifications Experience developing novel attacks, repeatable security evaluations or testing frameworks for AI-enabled systems, or building and validating AI-assisted offensive research capabilities. Expertise across multiple attack surfaces, or advanced specialist capability in areas such as exploit development, reverse engineering, endpoint security, cloud exploitation or software supply-chain attacks. Experience developing automated vulnerability discovery, fuzzing, program analysis or evaluation systems across large codebases or complex environments. Experience conducting coordinated exercises with defenders to measure detection and response effectiveness and validate improvements against realistic attack techniques. Contributions through original research, vulnerability disclosures, open-source tools, technical publications, internal training or mentoring.
