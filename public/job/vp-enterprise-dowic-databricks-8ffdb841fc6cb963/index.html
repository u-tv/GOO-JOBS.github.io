Metro Area) - Req SLSQ128R37 About the Team The Public Sector team is responsible for aggressively growing top-line revenues and driving new business through the implementation of scalable, repeatable, structured systems and processes while embracing the operational challenges of building and scaling a high-growth business through its next stage of expansion. As Databricks establishes and scales its dedicated National Security practice, this team sits at the forefront of enabling mission-critical data, analytics, and AI capabilities across the defense and intelligence landscape. You will report to Mike Daniels – Vice President and General Manager, Public Sector. Mission : Databricks is seeking a seasoned, entrepreneurial, and transformational VP, Department of War (DoW) & Intelligence Community (IC) to build, lead, and scale our dedicated National Security practice. This senior executive will serve as a key business leader within the Public Sector organization, responsible for scaling our DoW and IC sales organization, aggressively growing top-line revenues, and driving net-new mission adoption through scalable, repeatable, structured systems and processes. The impact you will have: Build, lead, and scale Databricks’ dedicated National Security practice across the Department of War (DoW) and Intelligence Community (IC), driving exceptional sales and operational execution to achieve ambitious revenue goals. Create and execute new strategic go-to-market plans tailored to defense and intelligence mission requirements, positioning Databricks to win and displace legacy incumbents across the National Security software and AI landscape. Design and drive the sales methodologies, team structure, and coverage models needed to build a high-performing National Security sales organization. Act as a strategic thought leader and advisor to the broader Executive Team; serve as a true business leader and general manager, partnering closely with Field Engineering, Product, Security/Compliance, Deal Desk, and Public Sector leadership to support the needs of the DoW and IC environments.. Work closely with our FDE team to ensure customer value is realized and our promised value is delivered Develop short- and long-term DoW and IC market strategies and forecasts through in-depth mission and budget analysis, and communicate execution progress and sales productivity to senior management. Serve as an exceptional leader and change agent, collaborating across the organization to bring disparate teams together into a cohesive, mission-obsessed unit. Function in a high-level strategic capacity while maintaining hands-on accountability for tactical team execution and pipeline progression. Recruit, hire, develop, and manage a world-class team of DoW and IC Enterprise Account Executives and front-line sales leaders, leveraging your deep network across the National Security community to ensure the team achieves and exceeds sales goals. Deliver regular and accurate forecasts and ensure monthly, quarterly, and annual targets are achieved. Cultivate executive relationships at the SES, Flag/General Officer, Agency Leadership, and Defense Industrial Base CxO levels, and personally sponsor complex, multi-year sales cycles across DoW and IC programs. Leverage your network and experience to drive strategic alignment with key defense ecosystem partners, Global Systems Integrators (GSIs), and prime contractors to accelerate mission adoption and procurement through vehicles such as OTAs and Tradewinds. National Security, DoW & IC Domain Expertise Comprehensive understanding of the Department of War (DoW) and Intelligence Community (IC) environments, including mission priorities, federal acquisition cycles, and classified cloud architectures. Demonstrable success selling enterprise software, data, or AI platforms across Defense and Intelligence agencies.
