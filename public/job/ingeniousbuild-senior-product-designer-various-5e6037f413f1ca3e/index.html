Ingenious.build: Senior Product Designer

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Poland - Remote At INGENIOUS.BUILD, we have developed a top-notch platform for companies in the real estate and construction industries worldwide. Our platform enables companies to easily manage all aspects of their projects from start to finish, both internally and with other project participants. Joining INGENIOUS.BUILD means embarking on an incredible journey to create the first cloud application for managing the entire lifecycle of a real estate construction project. You'll be building what some consider the holy grail of construction project management. It's a big, bold, and audacious endeavor, and if you have what it takes, you'll play a direct role in its development. And if you’ve got what it takes, you’ll be directly involved in its development. The rest, as they say, will be history... About the role : We are actively seeking a skilled Senior Product Designer to join our dynamic team. As a Product Designer at INGENIOUS.BUILD, you’ll play a key role in crafting and enhancing user interfaces for our platform in close collaboration with product management and engineering teams. You’ll have the opportunity to build prototypes, gather feedback from stakeholders, and ensure the seamless integration of design elements across our platform. Your work will directly contribute to delivering exceptional user experiences and the overall success of our product. If you’re passionate about creating intuitive, user-centered designs and thrive in a collaborative environment, we’d love to hear from you. In this position, you wil l : Collaborate closely with product management and engineering teams to create impactful user interfaces Develop clickable prototypes to demonstrate design concepts and gather feedback Work with stakeholders to understand their needs and incorporate feedback into design iterations Lead rapid design exploration and prototyping to validate concepts early in the product development cycle. Plan and execute user research and testing efforts, including A/B tests, usability studies, and experiments. Synthesize research, behavioral data, and test outcomes into clear design recommendations. Drive continuous design iteration based on user feedback, product metrics, and business impact. Conduct competition research to inform design decisions. Assist developers during the implementation phase by providing support and clarifications Utilize INGENIOUS.BUILD Design System to maintain consistency across the product Take ownership of features and drive them from conception to delivery Create user-friendly interfaces for the complex B2B web application Skills and Experience needed : Minimum of 5 years experience as a UX/UI/Product Designer or in a similar role Demonstrated strong teamwork skills coupled with a problem-solving attitude Proven experience in rapid design iteration, prototyping, and concept validation. Hands-on experience with user research and testing methods, including A/B testing, usability testing, and experiment analysis. Strong ability to synthesize user insights, behavioral data, and business needs into clear design solutions. Deep understanding of product design processes, interaction design, and user-centered design best practices. Familiarity with WCAG accessibility rules Proficiency in Figma Ability to create and maintain Design System Demonstrated self-motivation, a proactive mindset, and an ownership mentality to drive projects independently and deliver high-quality results Benefits of Working with Us: We provide high-performance company laptops for optimal productivity Unlimited PTO lets you take the time you need to recharge One-time $500 office budget to set up your workspace $50 monthly for educational resources, rolling over until year-end Stock options so you can share in the company’s growth We're fired up to learn more about you to see how you can help us on our mission! We genuinely look forward to receiving your application after submitting your information a member from the Recruitment Team will reach out accordingly.
