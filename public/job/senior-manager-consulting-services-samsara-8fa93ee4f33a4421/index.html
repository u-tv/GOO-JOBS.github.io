Senior Manager, Consulting Services

🏢 samsara 📍 Remote - US 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are Samsara (NYSE: IOT) is the pioneer of the Connected Operations™ Cloud, which is a platform that enables organizations that depend on physical operations to harness Internet of Things (IoT) data to develop actionable insights and improve their operations. At Samsara, we are helping improve the safety, efficiency and sustainability of the physical operations that power our global economy. Representing more than 40% of global GDP, these industries are the infrastructure of our planet, including agriculture, construction, field services, transportation, and manufacturing — and we are excited to help digitally transform their operations at scale. Working at Samsara means you’ll help define the future of physical operations and be on a team that’s shaping an exciting array of product solutions, including Video-Based Safety, Vehicle Telematics, Apps and Driver Workflows, and Equipment Monitoring. As part of a recently public company, you’ll have the autonomy and support to make an impact as we build for the long term. About the role: Samsara’s Consulting Services organization helps customers realize lasting value from our platform, from our largest enterprise accounts to thousands of customers in our Scale segment. Two teams do this work in different ways. Resident Consultants (RCs) are embedded long-term in enterprise customers, where they administer the platform, run adoption and change programs, and act as trusted advisors to customer executives. Training Specialists design and deliver one-to-many learning programs, including cohort-based sessions, live webinars, and digital assets, that help customers adopt the platform at scale. As the Senior Manager, Consulting Services, you will lead the Resident Consultant team directly and the Training Specialist team through a frontline manager, and build the team needed to execute our strategy and roadmap for embedded and scaled services. You will play a key role in implementing strategic initiatives, improving operational efficiency, and raising the quality bar across both teams, while influencing the broader Customer Success function. This role requires a blend of strategic thinking, strong leadership, and a passion for customer outcomes. You will be a visible partner to Sales, Customer Success, Product, Finance, and Samsara executives. This is a remote position open to candidates residing in the US. You will be able to travel within the United States for customer, team, and company needs (up to 25%). You should apply if: You want to impact the industries that run our world: The programs your teams deliver can affect whether truck drivers deliver goods on time and without accidents, whether students get dropped off safely from school, or whether power gets restored quickly after a natural disaster. You thrive the most when solving problems: Our constantly expanding technology and the complexities faced by our customers provide an exciting range of challenges. With a growth mindset and a desire to learn, you will strategically partner with customers and internal teams to find unique solutions that keep operations safe, efficient, and sustainable. You are a natural relationship builder: Whether the relationship is with customer executives or with cross-functional teams at Samsara, you are in constant communication and collaboration with key stakeholders to win as a team. You are the architect of your own career: If you put in the work, this role won’t be your last at Samsara. This Customer Success organization is still shaping its future, and you will have plenty of autonomy and opportunities to master your craft in a hyper-growth environment. You want to be with the best: At Samsara, we win together, celebrate together, and support each other. You will be surrounded by the best and brightest professionals out there. In this role, you will: Lead the Resident Consultant team directly and the Training Specialist team through a frontline manager, build the team needed to execute the strategy and roadmap, and make sure the two teams work together across the customer lifecycle. Play a key role in implementing strategic initiatives for both service offerings, including packaging, pricing guardrails, and capacity planning, and in improving operational efficiency. Ensure ongoing success and value realization for enterprise customers who use Resident Consultant services, and for the many customers who learn through our training programs. Proactively identify opportunities to improve customer satisfaction, and develop and refine mechanisms to monitor customer experience, such as CSAT, value tracking, and learning outcomes. Identify, assess, and mitigate operational and strategic risks across both teams, and develop risk management strategies, such as coverage plans, bench capacity, and escalation paths. Set high standards for execution and champion operational excellence across both teams, including clear definitions of what &quot;good&quot; looks like for executive stakeholder communication, program delivery, and instructional quality. Partner with Sales, Deal Desk, and Finance to scope, price, and staff new engagements, and to forecast headcount against demand. Promote cross-team collaboration with Customer Success and Product to identify knowledge gaps and adoption barriers, and streamline the processes and workflows that connect these teams. Keep executives informed of progress, report on team and customer outcomes, and advocate for change when needed. Minimum requirements for the role: 6+ years managing teams in Customer Success, consulting, professional services, or customer education, including recruiting, developing, and retaining top talent. Experience building or overseeing customer training or education programs delivered one-to-many, such as live virtual sessions, cohort-based programs, or digital learning. Working knowledge of instructional design, and the ability to use engagement, adoption, and customer satisfaction data to improve programs and decisions. Experience scaling a service offering, planning capacity, and partnering with Sales and Finance on scoping and pricing. Exceptional executive stakeholder management and communication skills, emotional intelligence, and prioritization. Proven track record of building trust and collaborating across teams, from customer executives to day-to-day users of our solutions and internal partners in Sales, Product, Engineering, and Finance. Self-starter who takes initiative and thrives in a fast-paced, change-heavy environment. Demonstrated ability to foster an inclusive team environment that values diverse perspectives. Ability to travel within the United States for customer meetings, team events, and company events (up to 25%). Bachelor’s degree from a 4-year institution. An ideal candidate also has: Led a team of customer-facing consultants delivering long-term engagements with Fortune 500 clients. Coached or managed a frontline manager, led more than one team with distinct functions, or built a team from the ground up as demand grew.
