Global Community Programs Lead

🏢 airbnb 📍 United States 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Airbnb was born in 2007 when two hosts welcomed three guests to their San Francisco home, and has since grown to over 5 million hosts who have welcomed over 2 billion guest arrivals in almost every country across the globe. Every day, hosts offer unique stays and experiences that make it possible for guests to connect with communities in a more authentic way. The Community You Will Join: Airbnb empowers millions of hosts to build businesses, forge connections with guests, and positively impact their local communities. What sets Airbnb apart is the strength of its global host community. Hosts are at the core of everything we do. The Airbnb Community team partners with Hosts by integrating their feedback into our products and policies, fostering connection, and supporting their growth and success. The Difference You Will Make: As a Programs Lead you'll support programs that connect our Community Team to key business and brand initiatives. Your key responsibilities, including program planning and project management, will help to ensure our team stays aligned and on track to deliver high quality work and impact for our hosts.
