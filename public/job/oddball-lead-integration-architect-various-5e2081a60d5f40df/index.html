Oddball: Lead Integration Architect

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: URL: https://www.oddball.io Oddball believes that the best products are built when companies understand and value the things they are working on. We value learning and growth and the ability to make a big impact at a small company. We believe that we can make big changes happen and improve the daily lives of millions of people by bringing quality software to the federal space. This position is contingent upon contract award. We’re looking for a Lead Integration Architect to join our CMS team, designing how a modern claims platform connects with the legacy systems behind Medicare claims processing. What you'll be doing: Define the integration architecture between a commercial claims platform and legacy systems, including mainframe Set technical direction, patterns, and standards for the integration engineers Design APIs and data flows that move claims and beneficiary data reliably and securely Review designs and code, and resolve the hardest integration problems Align on interfaces and dependencies with CMS, the prime, and Oddball teams Make sure designs meet CMS security and compliance requirements Mentor senior engineers and grow the integration team What you’ll bring: Deep experience architecting integrations between modern platforms and legacy systems Experience with Medicare or healthcare claims systems, or commercial claims platforms Strong API and integration design skills across REST , messaging, and batch Experience with AWS cloud environments Familiarity with COBOL or mainframe environments is a plus Experience with Java is a plus Thrives in a remote, collaborative Agile environment and genuinely enjoys working across teams and vendors Communicates clearly and openly, whether whiteboarding with engineers or briefing CMS leadership Perform other related duties as assigned Requirements: Applicants must be authorized to work in the United States.
