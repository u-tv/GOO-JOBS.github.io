PPSL - Growth Management – Acquisition & Retention (Offline Payments)

🏢 paytm 📍 Noida, Uttar Pradesh 💼 Full-time Employment 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Us: Paytm is India’s leading mobile payments and financial services distribution company. A pioneer of the mobile QR payments revolution, Paytm builds technology that enables small businesses and consumers to participate in the digital economy. Our mission is to serve half a billion Indians and bring them into the mainstream economy through technology. About the Role: We are looking for a sharp, hands-on, and data-driven Growth Manager (Acquisition & Retention) to join our offline payments growth team. This role owns end-to-end growth across two connected pillars: scaling high-quality merchant acquisition and driving sustained activation, engagement, retention, and win back across our offline payments ecosystem (including QR, Soundbox, and EDC journeys). The ideal candidate combines first-principles business and funnel thinking with strong analytical rigor, rapid experimentation, and practical execution. You will work closely with Product, Engineering, Analytics/Data Science, Field Sales/GTM, Risk, operations, and Marketing to turn funnel leaks and lifecycle cohorts into measurable, sustainable growth. ​ Key Responsibilities: ​Acquisition & Onboarding Funnel: ​ Scale Acquisition & Funnel Conversion : Identify high-potential merchant segments and geographic pockets, optimize acquisition funnels across assisted and DIY/digital touchpoints, and improve lead-to-onboarded and onboarded-to-active conversion rates. ​ GTM & Channel Growth Partnerships: Partner with field sales, GTM, and lifecycle teams to sharpen targeting, onboarding nudges, and early-stage activation playbooks so acquired merchants reach first meaningful transaction faster. ​ Retention, Engagement & Winback: Own Lifecycle & CLM: Drive activation, repeat usage, retention, and winback across merchant cohorts using structured Customer Lifecycle Management (CLM), behavioral triggers, and targeted interventions. ​ Prevent Churn & Recover Dormancy: Diagnose early drop-off signals, usage decay, and churn drivers across service and product journeys; design and test retention and reactivation campaigns that protect active base health and unit economics. ​Analytics, Experimentation & AI Workflows: Build a Data-Led Growth Engine: Track funnel metrics, cohort retention curves, activation velocity, and unit economics; uncover growth bottlenecks and size high-impact opportunities. Run Rapid Experimentation: Formulate clear hypotheses, design and execute A/B and cohort experiments, and rigorously measure incremental business impact before scaling winners. Leverage AI & Automation: Use AI, automation, and agentic workflows to streamline segmentation, personalization, lifecycle nudges, reporting, and day-to-day growth execution. ​ Cross-Functional Execution: ​ Partner Across Teams: Collaborate seamlessly with Product, Engineering, Data, Operations, Risk, and Business teams to ship growth improvements quickly and maintain strong execution hygiene. Track Benchmarks & Competition: Monitor market trends, merchant behavior, and competitive moves to spot fresh acquisition and retention levers early. ​ What We’re Looking For: Experience: 6+ years of experience in growth, lifecycle marketing/CLM, product growth, or commercial analytics within high-velocity consumer tech, fintech, payments, or marketplace environments. Dual Acquisition + Retention Mindset: Proven ability to look at the full funnel—balancing top-of-funnel/acquisition efficiency with downstream activation, habit formation, and retention Analytical & Experimentation Fluency: Strong command of SQL/Excel/BI tools, cohort and funnel analysis, experimentation design, and metric-driven decision-making. Execution Bias & Ownership: High bias for action, comfort navigating ambiguity and cross-functional dependencies, and a structured approach to solving messy operational and product problems. Modern Tooling Comfort: Practical familiarity with AI-assisted workflows, automation tools, or data scripting to move faster with insights and interventions. Why join us: Work with a high-performing and passionate product, design, and engineering team. Shape the future of credit for millions of users. Build at scale in one of India’s most dynamic and regulated spaces. Flexible and inclusive work environment with fast decision-making and ownership
