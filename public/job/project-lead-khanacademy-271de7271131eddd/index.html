ABOUT KHAN ACADEMY Khan Academy is a fast-paced, nonprofit startup on a mission to provide a free, world-class education for anyone, anywhere. We already reach millions of students every month and are growing rapidly. We’re building a library of world-class instructional and practice resources that empower learners. Whether they’re studying matrices, mitosis, or multivariable calculus, we want to offer students the resources to realise that they can learn anything. ABOUT KHAN ACADEMY INDIA Khan Academy India aims to deliver a world-class user experience to learners in India that is locally relevant and is enabled by a strong on-the-ground team and operations. Our learning system is mastery-based, allowing students to master key concepts at a pace that suits them before moving on to more challenging content. From serving under 500,000 learners in 2016, we are now serving almost 4 million learners a month across our websites, apps and YouTube channels. These learners include both independent learners accessing us at home and teacher-directed learners in schools. Our focus is to reach the underserved by making our content accessible in local languages. Khan Academy is already available in English, Hindi, Odia, Marathi, Kannada, Telugu, Assamese, and Punjabi. ABOUT THE INDIA SCHOOLS TEAM In line with our focus on reaching the underserved, we partner with government school systems to improve student learning outcomes by enabling teachers and students to optimally utilise the tools and resources on the Khan Academy platform. From our government partnerships, we reached 2.1 million learners last year, partnering with 70,000+ schools across various states. Our goal is to expand our scale to reach over five million learners in three years. The Schools Team works in a lean and collaborative way with our government partners, developing strong implementation plans and providing support every step of the way. We learn and constantly iterate by gathering feedback and through data-backed insights and efficacy studies. With a small team, we have managed to reach 1 Lakh+ teachers and 2.1 million learners across various states. ABOUT THE ROLE Khan Academy India is looking for an experienced and energetic Project Manager. You will play a vital role in helping government higher secondary schools in Andhra Pradesh adopt and use the Khan Academy platform. This position is a unique opportunity to be part of a three-way project with UNICEF, the AP education department, and Khan Academy India. It is an exciting chance to see how Khan Academy and Khanmigo can directly build teacher skills on a large scale. You will lead a team of training leads, manage project goals, and work closely with senior government officials. The ideal candidate will balance government relations with a deep knowledge of education. You will be directly responsible for designing and conducting capacity-building initiatives, ensuring training quality, and utilising field data to continuously improve program delivery. The lessons you learn here will help guide Khan Academy’s future work in AP and other states. Fixed-Term Contract (Immediate Start – December 2027) In this role, you would: Provide field level insights and inputs to shape the design of the program and various aspects of it. Play a key role in the pre-survey and then the ongoing monthly Teacher Professional Development session design Support programme implementation: Work with the Samagra Shiksha, Andhra Pradesh and the state nodal officer to help support the implementation of the Khan Academy learning platform across government higher secondary schools in Andhra Pradesh.
