Senior Manager, Revenue

🏢 mongodb 📍 Gurugram 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

The Revenue team is a highly visible, global team responsible for accurate and timely revenue reporting. The team manages the monthly and quarterly revenue close, supports contract review and accounting conclusions, evaluates revenue treatment for new and existing product offerings, maintains controls over revenue reporting, and supports revenue forecasting and analytics. This role will lead the Revenue Operations team in Gurugram, India, with responsibility for managing and developing Revenue Managers and Revenue Analysts. The Senior Manager will be the primary point of leadership for the India Revenue Operations team and will be accountable for the quality and timely execution of the revenue processes owned by the team. The role will partner closely with global Revenue leadership and cross-functional teams to manage close execution, resolve complex revenue and operational issues, strengthen processes and controls, and continue building the capabilities of the India team. We are looking to speak to candidates who are based in Gurugram for our hybrid working model.
