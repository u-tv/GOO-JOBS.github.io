Risk Team Lead, User Policy Operations

🏢 stripe 📍 Mexico City 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies - from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups - use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team This role sits within User Policy Operations. User Policy Operations assesses the risk that users pose to Stripe against our Terms of Service and related policies. Its work helps ensure that the businesses we support are aligned with those policies, and it protects Stripe, our partners, and the broader user community. We strive to deliver frictionless experiences for all of our users, whether they are an Independent Business, Startup, SMB, or Enterprise, and our mission is to provide every Stripe user with the best support experience possible. What you’ll do In this role, you will be a player-coach leading a team of Risk Operations associates who assess complex and unique user situations against Stripe's Terms of Service and related policies. Their assessments determine whether Stripe can continue to support these users. Many of them are among Stripe's most strategic, so the work calls for urgency, sound judgment, and a clear view of the risk each user poses to Stripe. Your team will often work beyond the written workflow and policy. They will dig into the facts of each case, assess the risk to Stripe, and turn that into clear, well-reasoned recommendations. As their leader, you will help them move quickly through ambiguity, get up to speed fast on the situations they're handling, and work through friction when engaging across the risk ecosystem, including cross-functional partners and account teams. Through clear goals, guidance, and mentorship, you will set both individuals and the team up for success. To be a fit, you will have a coaching mindset, proven ability to communicate at different levels, a track record of driving operational performance, and a passion for inspiring a team to deliver outstanding results.In this role, you will act in a player-coach capacity and are accountable for supporting Risk/Partner Risk Operations associates.
