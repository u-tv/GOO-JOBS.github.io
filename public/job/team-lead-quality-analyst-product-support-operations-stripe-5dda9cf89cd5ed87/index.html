Team Lead, Quality Analyst, Product Support Operations

🏢 stripe 📍 Mexico City 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies - from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups - use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team As Stripe’s user base and global footprint grows dramatically, we have distinctly unique support problems resulting from both our type of scale and the type of businesses we partner with. The Product Support Operations (PSO) team plays a key role in efficiently solving our users’ issues. As part of our PSO team, you will be a critical driver in delivering exceptional user experiences. This role goes beyond basic troubleshooting—it's about using your technical expertise, analytical mindset, and project management skills to strategically improve our support systems, processes, and product quality. We strive to deliver frictionless experiences for all of our users, whether they are an Independent Business, Startup, SMB, or Enterprise and our mission is to provide all Stripe Users with the best support experience possible. Our Quality team sits at the heart of that mission. We are responsible for defining and upholding the standards that shape every user interaction across our support verticals. We believe that quality is not just a score — it is a reflection of the care and expertise we bring to every user, every time. What you’ll do In this role, you will own quality outcomes at your site, leading a team of approximately 6 Quality Analysts spanning multiple support verticals. Your team plays a critical role in upholding the quality standards that underpin the experience we deliver to Stripe's users and you will be the person who drives that standard forward at your site. Beyond your team, you will serve as a key voice in the evolution of our quality program and a strategic partner to vertical leads. To be a great fit, you will have a quality first mindset, the ability to translate data into action, a track record of coaching others to grow, and the cross functional credibility to influence without authority across a multi vertical environment. Responsibilities Own UIQ quality scores across all verticals at your site, driving teams to meet and sustain a minimum 90% quality score Serve as the final escalation layer for Disputes at your site, ensuring fair, consistent, and well reasoned outcomes Review quality trends for each vertical at your site, identifying patterns and surfacing risks before they become issues Complete case work to stay close to the day to day realities of the verticals’ subject matter and maintain subject matter familiarity Participate in calibration sessions across verticals to drive alignment and consistency in scoring Help lead rubric updates and contribute to the continuous evolution of our quality framework Drive projects at the quality program level, contributing to broader initiatives that improve how quality is measured and managed across PSO Deliver high-level quality reporting for all verticals at your site, providing leadership with a clear and actionable view of performance Provide insights and recommendations back to vertical leads, translating quality data into concrete operational guidance Manage and develop a team of ~6 Quality Analysts representing verticals including Payments, Revenue Suite, Account & Connect, Money Management, Enterprise, and Community Ops Set clear goals and expectations for individual and team performance Coach and mentor individuals through structured career development conversations Provide continuous performance feedback and facilitate formal performance reviews Foster a culture of continuous improvement and psychological safety within the team Drive and own initiatives that make the team an engaging and motivating place to work Support recruitment and hiring initiatives as the team evolves Who you are We're looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement. Minimum requirements Experience coaching or leading teams, with a demonstrated ability to manage and develop individual performance Prior experience in a QA or quality assurance capacity within a product support or customer service organisation Experience mentoring others and delivering constructive, growth-oriented feedback Proven subject matter expertise in product support or customer service operations Ability to work cross-functionally across multiple teams or verticals to drive results and foster partnership Strong analytical skills — able to identify trends in quality data and translate them into clear, actionable insights Excellent interpersonal and written communication skills Exemplary planning and time management skills, balancing people leadership, reporting, and program-level responsibilities Preferred qualifications Experience delivering quality or business performance reporting to leadership Experience participating in or facilitating calibration sessions Experience contributing to rubric development or quality framework design Experience with new process launches and continuous improvement initiatives Stripe is an equal opportunity employer. We celebrate diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive environment for all employees.
