Headquarters: Switzerland URL: https://jobs.workable.com/company/1kK2WRMtDjeCSG59BKEaSi/jobs-at-pricelabs About the Role: We're looking for a Mid-Market Solutions Consultant based in Europe to help expand our presence in the German markets. In the role, you'll work with our growing Mid-Market team to engage with small to mid-sized property managers and support them through their initial discovery of PriceLabs to becoming active users. The role operates during CET hours (9:30 AM to 6:30 PM CET / 1 PM to 10:00 PM Indian Standard Time), focusing primarily on converting smaller to mid-sized inbound prospects. Compensation: €30,000 – €40,000 per year (total compensation, including base salary and variable pay), depending on experience and location. Key responsibilities: Serving as the primary point of contact for new small to mid-sized property managers throughout their onboarding process Driving conversions of new prospects by conducting compelling and detailed virtual product demonstrations Assist new prospects in setting up their PriceLabs accounts and optimize their PriceLabs usage as per their unique revenue management strategies and preferences.
