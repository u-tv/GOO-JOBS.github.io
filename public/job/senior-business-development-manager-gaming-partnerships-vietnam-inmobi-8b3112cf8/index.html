About the Role InMobi is seeking for a dynamic and results-oriented Senior Business Development Manager to join our team in Vietnam. As a Senior BD Manager, your key focus will be on building and expanding our partnerships within the gaming industry. The ideal candidate will possess a strong understanding of the mobile gaming landscape in VIetnam, excellent relationship-building skills, and a proven track record of driving revenue growth through strategic partnerships. You will be responsible for identifying, negotiating, and managing partnerships with game developers, publishers, and other key players in the Vietnamese gaming market. You will also spend considerable time interacting with internal product, sales, and marketing stakeholders to explore new avenues of supply and revenue growth, thereby effectively creating an action plan that syncs perfectly with our broader business objectives. In this role, you will report to the Head of Gaming Partnerships—Asia Pacific (APAC), who is based at our headquarters in Bangalore, India. Procure any required collateral from customer teams for any ad-sales pitches (case studies, media-kits, demographic split of users, daily active user count etc. What we require of you Experience: 5-8 years of experience in the mobile gaming industry, mobile advertising, or digital marketing.
