The Role Auth0 is a developer product at its core — and increasingly, the developers building with us aren't just humans. AI agents are now a first-class consumer of our SDKs, APIs, and documentation. This role owns the architectural vision for how Auth0 is experienced across three dimensions: DX (Developer Experience — how human developers build with Auth0), UX (User Experience — how users experience Auth0 products), and AX (Agentic Experience — how AI agents interact with and build on Auth0). You'll work across our SDK ecosystem, public APIs, documentation, and product surfaces to ensure that every touchpoint is consistent, delightful, and easy to build on — whether the builder is a human or an agent. You'll also contribute directly to how we build internally: creating the skills, guardrails, and patterns that our own in-house agents use, ensuring that agent-assisted development at Auth0 produces the same high-quality, consistent output as human-led development. What you'll be doing Define and drive the architectural vision for DX, UX, and AX across Auth0's public products, SDKs, and APIs Identify and address inconsistencies across Auth0's product surfaces, establishing patterns and standards that make the experience feel unified and intentional Architect and contribute to the SDK and API design layer, ensuring our developer-facing interfaces are ergonomic, consistent, and built for both human and agent consumers Design and build agent skills, guardrails, and contextual tooling (MCP servers, SKILL.md, llms.txt, structured documentation) that enable agents to build correctly with Auth0 Embed within Engineering teams for large initiatives, providing architectural guidance and lighter-touch direction for smaller efforts Partner closely with the Design team as a key stakeholder, ensuring engineering implementation reflects and reinforces the product's UX direction Contribute to Auth0's Developer Experience Principles in practice — not just as guidance, but through hands-on implementation that raises the bar Lead by doing: reviewing, prototyping, and contributing code across SDKs, tooling, and documentation systems Participate in our Engineering Leadership Team with other architects, directors, and executives Mentor and uplevel engineers across the organization on experience-focused architecture What you'll bring to the role Experience : 15 to 20 year .
