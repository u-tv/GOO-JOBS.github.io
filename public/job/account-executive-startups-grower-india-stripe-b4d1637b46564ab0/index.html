Account Executive - Startups, Grower (India)

🏢 stripe 📍 Bangalore 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the India Startups team India is producing a new generation of SaaS companies, digital-native businesses, platforms, marketplaces, and AI-first startups with global ambitions. These businesses need more than a payments provider. They need financial infrastructure that can support rapid growth, international expansion, new products, and increasingly complex business models. The Startups Grower team helps Stripe’s largest and fastest-growing startup users realize more value from Stripe. We combine commercial ownership with deep curiosity about our users’ businesses. We work with founders, CFOs, product leaders, and engineering teams to understand where they are going next, then bring together the right people and products across Stripe to help them get there. This is an opportunity to shape how Stripe serves India’s most ambitious technology companies. You will join a focused, entrepreneurial team in Bengaluru while working closely with colleagues across Stripe’s global Product, Solutions Architecture, Customer Success, Risk, Legal, Finance, and Operations organizations. What you’ll do You will own and grow a portfolio of high-potential startup customers. Your goal will be to build trusted, long-term relationships, retain and expand existing revenue, and help users adopt the Stripe products that best support their next phase of growth. Responsibilities Develop a deep understanding of each user’s business model, growth plans, technical architecture, and financial infrastructure Build trusted relationships with founders and senior stakeholders across finance, product, engineering, and operations Create account plans that identify retention risks, expansion opportunities, decision-makers, and clear paths to measurable user value Lead renewals and commercial negotiations with a focus on durable partnerships, strong outcomes for users, and responsible revenue growth Identify and close opportunities for additional product adoption, new use cases, deeper integrations, and international expansion Run thoughtful discovery and product conversations that connect Stripe’s capabilities to outcomes such as revenue growth, improved conversion, fraud reduction, faster launches, and greater operational efficiency Manage a portfolio with rigor, using data to prioritize accounts, forecast accurately, and focus time on the opportunities with the greatest potential Design and execute scaled campaigns for groups of users with similar needs while maintaining a high-quality, consultative experience Coordinate across Solutions Architecture, Customer Success, Product, Risk, Legal, Finance, and Operations to resolve complex issues and set users up for long-term success Bring structured feedback from Indian startups to Stripe’s Product and leadership teams, helping improve our products, positioning, and market strategy Build relationships across India’s startup ecosystem, including founders, investors, accelerators, and technology communities What success looks like In this role, success means: Retaining and growing a portfolio of strategic startup users Increasing adoption of relevant Stripe products and use cases Building senior, trusted relationships that extend beyond a single transaction or renewal Identifying opportunities early and leading them to a clear commercial outcome Creating a predictable, high-quality operating rhythm across account planning, pipeline management, forecasting, and execution Helping Stripe better understand and serve high-growth companies building from India for the world Who you are We are looking for a commercially strong, intellectually curious account executive who enjoys helping high-growth companies solve important problems. You can move comfortably between a founder conversation, a technical discovery session, a pricing discussion, and an internal strategy review. You are equally comfortable going deep on a small number of strategic opportunities and executing at pace across a broader portfolio. If you meet the minimum requirements, we encourage you to apply. Preferred qualifications are helpful, but they are not required. Minimum requirements 8+ years of experience in account management, consultative sales, customer growth, or another client-facing commercial role, including meaningful experience in the Indian market A demonstrated track record of retaining and expanding relationships with technology companies or other complex B2B customers Experience leading renewals, upsells, cross-sells, or multi-product commercial negotiations Strong discovery skills and the ability to translate business and technical priorities into relevant solutions Ability to build credibility with founders and senior stakeholders across finance, product, and engineering Strong commercial judgment, including comfort with pricing, business cases, contracts, and competing customer priorities Analytical discipline and the ability to use data to prioritize a portfolio, build account strategies, and forecast accurately Experience coordinating complex work across multiple internal teams Ability to operate with ownership and sound judgment in an ambiguous, fast-moving environment Strong written and verbal communication skills in English Preferred qualifications Experience in payments, fintech, SaaS, cloud, developer tools, or financial infrastructure Experience working with venture-backed startups, digital-native companies, SaaS businesses, platforms, marketplaces, or businesses expanding internationally Existing relationships within India’s founder, venture capital, accelerator, or technology ecosystem Familiarity with APIs, online payments, subscription business models, fraud prevention, platforms, or cross-border commerce Experience working in a global organization with geographically distributed teams Experience with spreadsheets, CRM analytics, SQL, or a demonstrated willingness to learn Why this role matters India’s most ambitious startups are building products for customers across the world. In this role, you will help those companies develop the financial infrastructure they need to scale, while shaping Stripe’s relationships and long-term growth in India. The work combines commercial impact, technical problem-solving, ecosystem building, and the opportunity to partner with some of India’s most exciting founders and technology teams.
