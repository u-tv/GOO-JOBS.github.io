Bitwarden: Sr. Director Revenue Accounting

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: US Remote Senior Director, Revenue Accounting Bitwarden is looking for a Sr. Director of Revenue Accounting to own how we recognize, record, and report revenue across both self-service and sales-assisted channels. This leader is accountable for the full revenue cycle, from contract and invoice through cash and recognized revenue. You will own our revenue stack (Stripe, HubSpot, and Rillet) end to end and partner closely with Sales, Legal, FP&A, and Engineering. What You'll Own: Own end-to-end order to cash operations for both self-service (PLG, credit card, via Stripe) and sales-assisted (enterprise, multi-year, custom contract) revenue streams. Own revenue recognition under ASC 606 for self-service and sales-assisted contracts, including performance obligations, standalone selling price, contract modifications, multi-year and ramped deals, usage-based components, and channel or reseller arrangements. Own deferred revenue, revenue schedules, and the monthly revenue close in Rillet, and deliver accurate revenue results to Corporate Accounting for month-end close. Design billing and revenue processes for large, complex transactions: multi-entity contracts, custom pricing, mid-term upgrades and downgrades, true-ups, and co-terming, from HubSpot deal creation through Stripe invoicing to Rillet recognition. Own the data flow and reconciliation across HubSpot, Stripe, and Rillet, including the bookings to billings to revenue reconciliation, the deferred revenue rollforward, and AR subledger to general ledger tie-outs. Own sales tax compliance across all channels, including nexus monitoring, tax calculation and collection in Stripe, exemption certificate management, return filing and remittance, and audit readiness, in partnership with external tax advisors. Write technical accounting memos for non-standard enterprise contracts and review them with external auditors. Own SOX controls for the revenue cycle and serve as the main contact for audit fieldwork on revenue. Define and monitor KPIs: billing accuracy, days sales outstanding, dunning/failed payment recovery rate, invoice cycle time, and dispute rates. Partner with Sales to structure non-standard enterprise deals so they translate cleanly into accurate, compliant billing in Stripe. Own sales commission accounting and administration, including commission calculations and payouts, ASC 340-40 capitalization and amortization of commission costs, and accrual accuracy, and partner with Sales leadership, FP&A, and HR on commission plan design, quota and attainment rules, and the cost and controls impact of plan changes. Drive Stripe configuration and automation (subscription schedules, proration rules, dunning/failed payment recovery, invoicing rules) to reduce manual touch on high-volume self-service billing. Partner with SalesOps to own the HubSpot-to-Stripe-to-Rillet data pipeline — ensuring deal terms, billing events, and revenue entries stay in sync, and closing gaps with native integrations, middleware, or custom tooling as needed. Collaborate with Product/Engineering on Stripe/product OTC enhancements, new pricing models, and packaging changes. Lead, mentor, and scale a billing, collections and commission teams (analysts, specialists, managers) as transaction volume and deal complexity grow. What We're Looking For: 10+ years of accounting experience, including 5+ years in SaaS revenue accounting and 3+ years leading teams. CPA or equivalent public accounting experience Deep, hands-on ASC 606 experience for SaaS, including large and non-standard enterprise transactions: custom terms, multi-year deals, usage-based or hybrid pricing, amendments, and co-terming. Experience accounting for both self-service (high-volume, automated, credit-card-based) and sales-assisted (contract-based, high-value) revenue. Experience with SOX, external audit management, and building controls in a growing company. Experience managing billing, collections, or order-to-cash teams, and commission Track record of building scalable processes and automating manual workflows, including integrations between CRM, billing, and accounting systems. Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills; comfortable in ambiguity and fast-changing environments. In the United States, the starting base compensation range for this role is $200,000 - $225,000. Actual compensation may vary based on level, relevant experience, and skill set as assessed in the interview process, as well as market data by location. See our careers page for a list of benefits.
