Key Responsibilities Manage delivery for multiple concurrent customer engagements, from contract signature through go-live and customer acceptance. Act as the customer's day-to-day delivery contact, working alongside the account manager who owns the overall relationship. Scope each engagement with the customer and internal teams, turn it into clear requests for the teams doing the work, and follow each one through to completion. Track what was promised to each customer and confirm it has been delivered before the engagement closes. Manage each customer's transition to ongoing account management and support, making sure the receiving teams have what they need. Own defined workstreams on larger strategic programs, under the direction of a Senior Service Delivery Manager, who remains the customer's primary contact. Advocate for the customer inside Vultr: raise their priorities, flag risks early and escalate delays that can be avoided. Report clear, accurate status to customers and internal stakeholders, drawing on what teams already track. Plan and run cross-functional internal initiatives, including coordination with external partners and vendors where needed. Apply and improve the team's delivery methods, templates and standards.
