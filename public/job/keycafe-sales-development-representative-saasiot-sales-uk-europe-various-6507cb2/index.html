Keycafe: Sales Development Representative - SaaS/IoT Sales, UK & Europe

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Bulgaria URL: http://keycafe.com Description Are you motivated by turning inbound interest into real sales opportunities — and getting rewarded for it? At Keycafe, we’re looking for a Sales Development Representative (SDR) to identify, qualify, and convert inbound leads into high-quality meetings for our Account Executive team. You’ll be the first point of contact for prospective customers across UK & Europe playing a critical role in driving revenue growth for our global SaaS/IoT platform. This role is ideal for someone who thrives in a fast-paced sales environment, enjoys engaging with decision-makers, and consistently meets or exceeds quotas. You’ll respond to inbound inquiries, qualify business fit, and set up meetings that move opportunities through the sales pipeline. You don’t need a technical background — but you do need curiosity and the ability to confidently explain how Keycafe’s SmartBox and cloud platform solve operational challenges for businesses. Fluency in English (written and spoken) is required, as you'll communicate with customers across multiple European markets. If you’re results-driven, organized, and energized by hitting targets in a collaborative remote team environment, we’d love to meet you. Requirements What You’ll Do Respond promptly to inbound leads via phone and email Make follow-up calls to warm prospects across UK & Europe Communicate professionally with customers in English Qualify inbound inquiries and assess business fit Navigate organizations to identify decision-makers Present Keycafe’s value proposition clearly and confidently Craft persuasive follow-up emails that drive engagement Schedule qualified meetings for Account Executives Achieve or exceed monthly quotas for qualified opportunities Why Join Keycafe Be part of a fast-growing global SaaS/IoT company Direct impact on revenue growth and company expansion Collaborative, high-performance team culture About Keycafe Keycafe, headquartered in Vancouver, powers secure and seamless key management for businesses worldwide. Our SmartBox system combines IoT hardware with cloud software to give organizations total control over their keys — anytime, anywhere. From hospitality and property management to logistics, fleets, and government, companies rely on Keycafe to keep operations secure, efficient, and scalable. Keycafe serves customers across 40+ industries, including hospitality, real estate, automotive, fleet management, and logistics. We have active customers throughout North America, Europe, Asia, and Australia.
