Vice President, Revenue Operations

🏢 dropbox 📍 Remote - Canada: Select locations 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Role Description Dropbox is seeking a Vice President of Revenue Operations to define and lead the operating strategy for the company’s global B2B go-to-market organization. This executive will be responsible for building a high-performing, scalable revenue engine that enables predictable and efficient growth across SMB, Mid-Market, and Enterprise segments while supporting Dropbox’s evolution across product-led, sales-assisted, and enterprise motions. This leader will serve as a key strategic partner to the Chief Business Officer and broader executive leadership team, translating company strategy into the operating model, planning frameworks, systems, insights, and execution mechanisms required to deliver durable growth. The role will own Revenue Operations and Marketing Operations across the customer lifecycle, ensuring tight alignment between demand generation, pipeline creation, sales execution, retention, expansion, and overall revenue performance. The VP of Revenue Operations will have enterprise-wide accountability for revenue planning, forecasting, segmentation, capacity and coverage strategy, incentives, GTM systems, analytics, operating cadence, and revenue productivity. This leader will play a central role in shaping how Dropbox allocates resources, evaluates performance, scales new products and motions, and modernizes its GTM organization through data, automation, and AI. Responsibilities Define and lead Dropbox’s global revenue operating strategy, ensuring the company’s GTM model is designed to support long-term growth, efficiency, and increasing enterprise complexity
