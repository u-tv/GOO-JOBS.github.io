Two Chairs: Mental Health Therapist (Part-Time, W-2)

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Massachusetts, United States URL: http://www.twochairs.com Why Choose Two Chairs? Compensation: Boston, MA residents earn a clinical session rate of $76/hour (which covers attended sessions, mandatory trainings, and monthly all hands) and an administrative rate of $23/hour (which covers documentation, no shows/late cancels, meetings, onboarding). For For residents outside of Boston, MA, clinical rates range from $58.50 to $85.00/hour based on location. AI-Assisted Documentation : Spend less time on administrative notes and more time on client care with our optional built-in AI documentation tools. Support for Wellness & Professional Growth: Enjoy a wellness/professional development stipend (up to $300 annually), case consultation, free CE credits through monthly APA-accredited courses, Psych Hub membership, and wellness events. Additional W-2 advantages you won’t find with 1099 contract work: No tax headaches – We handle withholdings and filings, so you don’t have to Earn paid sick time – Accrue up to 80 hours per year Merit-based raises – Be recognized and rewarded for your contributions Retirement benefits – Access an employer-sponsored 401(k) plan Malpractice insurance provided – Full coverage at no cost to you Access to state-mandated benefits – Like unemployment, disability, and paid leave where available About You Independently Licensed in Massachusetts as a Licensed Independent Clinical Social Worker (LICSW), Licensed Marriage & Family Therapist (LMFT), Licensed Mental Health Counselor (LMHC), or Licensed Psychologist (PhD or PsyD). Willing and motivated to become licensed in other states. We'll cover the entire cost. 2+ years of clinical experience with adults (18+), 6+ months of teletherapy experience, proficiency with clinical assessments (e.g., PHQ-9), and comfort with EHR systems. About the Role Deliver remote, evidence-based therapy to adult clients (18+), practicing modalities like CBT, DBT, ACT, MBCT, TLDP, IPT, EFT, and MI. Schedule & Caseload: Maintain a client caseload of 7-20 sessions per week, with a commitment of 8-24 total hours of weekly availability Monday - Friday, 8:00 a.m. - 9:00 p.m. EST. Approximately 85% of total time will be dedicated to direct client care, including ongoing therapy sessions and new client assessments. Available for manager check-ins (1 hour/week during your first 6 weeks, followed by 30 minutes/month. Use data to drive effective treatment and adjust your approach based on client progress. Collaborate with other mental health providers as needed and refer clients when appropriate. All documentation to be completed, signed, and locked in EMR within 48 hours of client contact.
