Manager - User Experience : F&E

🏢 meesho 📍 Bangalore, Karnataka 💼 Full Time Employee 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About the Team You will join the Fulfilment and Experience (F&E) team at Meesho. F&E is the operational and technological backbone powering India’s most cost-efficient, reliable, and scalable e-commerce network, serving 100M+ monthly active users . From disintermediating traditional logistics through Valmo (Meesho’s unbundled micro-entrepreneur supply platform) to driving zero-commission seller enablement and real-time speed platforms, F&E operates at the intersection of deep systems thinking, network engineering, and structural innovation. We solve industry-defining supply chain problems at a scale that few companies globally can match. About the Role This role offers a direct mandate to reshape a function that touches every single Meesho order, with full autonomy over team structure, roadmap, and how you solve the problem. It also gives you a seat at the table with senior leadership on one of Meesho's most visible 2-year bets, while allowing you to work at the intersection of scale (India's largest value e-commerce platform) and zero-to-one building.
