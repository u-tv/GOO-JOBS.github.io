Sr. Manager, GTM Strategy & Sales Packaging

🏢 lyft 📍 New York, NY 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

At Lyft, our purpose is to serve and connect. We aim to achieve this by cultivating a work environment where all team members belong and have the opportunity to thrive. Lyft Ads gives brands a unique way to reach consumers during high-intent moments: on their way to concerts, games, nights out, and everyday destinations. In this role, you'll set the strategy behind how we bring those moments to market, arm our sellers to win, and connect advertisers with millions of riders across the country. Lyft Ads is looking for a Senior Manager of Sales Packaging & Go-to-Market Strategy to build and lead the function that turns ridership data, cultural moments, and product capabilities into the packages our sales team sells every day. This is a strategic leadership role: you'll set the packaging and pricing agenda for the full Lyft Ads portfolio, build the go-to-market motions behind our biggest revenue moments, and give sellers the stories, structure, and pricing tools they need to win. You are a builder. You operate comfortably at the intersection of strategy and execution, and you have a track record of driving commercial outcomes in ambiguous, fast-moving environments. You bring a POV to partnership and packaging conversations, and you know how to align internal and external stakeholders around a shared vision. You are energized by the challenge of taking a scaled, high-growth advertising business and making it even better: more differentiated, more efficient, and more impactful for the brands and agencies that invest with Lyft Ads. You'll operate at the intersection of data, product, pricing, creative, and sales, and you'll be expected to bring an independent point of view on where Lyft Ads should show up, what we should charge, and how we package multi-product solutions with measurement built in. This role owns and manages the Lyft Ads Studio team, sits at the table with Sales leadership, and works in close partnership with Data Science, Finance, Product Marketing, Product, and cross-functional leadership across the business.
