Great brands help make Pinners’ inspirations a reality, and we’re looking for a Client Account Manager II to help lead the way. In this role, you will support one of Pinterest’s most strategic businesses in LATAM, partnering closely on the Mercado Libre business across Hispanic markets . You will help advertisers get the most out of Pinterest by driving strong campaign execution, performance optimization, measurement rigor, and clear client communication. You’ll collaborate closely with sales and cross-functional teams while helping bring structure, insights, and momentum to a complex regional account. What you’ll do: Build and nurture strong relationships with partners by proactively communicating on campaign performance, optimizations, updates, and growth opportunities Partner closely with the
