Aveanna Healthcare: Patient Support Representative (REMOTE)

Headquarters: URL: http://aveanna.com Overview Inbound Call Center Representative Starting Pay: $19/HR As an Inbound Call Center Representative, you’ll play a vital role for our Patient Support Department, which is our first point of contact for our patients to call in and seek information and ask questions regarding their services and orders with us here at Aveanna. We are looking for someone who can confidently manage 40-50 inbound calls per day with compassion and empathy! Whether you have experience in this industry or are brand new, our tailored training will provide you with the tools and skillset to navigate the ever-changing healthcare landscape. This role is extremely important, as you will be the first person they speak with to properly represent Aveanna and our values. Our Mission: To revolutionize the way homecare is delivered, one patient at a time.
