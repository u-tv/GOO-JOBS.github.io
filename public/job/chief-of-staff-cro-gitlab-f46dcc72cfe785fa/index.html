An overview of this role Join us at a pivotal moment as we scale our revenue operations globally. As Chief of Staff to the CRO, you'll serve as a strategic partner and force multiplier to our revenue leadership team, driving operational excellence and orchestrating high-impact initiatives. As part of the Office of the CRO, you'll also manage our Communications lead and CRO Initiatives Program Manager, giving you direct ownership over how the CRO's key initiatives get built and run. This highly visible role offers a unique opportunity to shape strategy at the executive level while developing your path to senior leadership. You'll work at the intersection of strategy, operations, and execution translating vision into action while maintaining the organizational agility needed in a high-growth environment. What you'll do The responsibilities encompass direct assignments driven by the CRO as well as autonomous leadership efforts informed by the feedback and key priorities of the CRO leadership team. This role will help enable communication, collaboration, strategy execution, and special projects across the CRO organization. Serve as a trusted advisor and thought partner to the CRO and revenue leadership on strategy, initiatives, and organizational priorities Drive leadership operations , including weekly executive staff meetings, QBRs, and strategic planning cycles with insights that inform decision-making Lead the CRO team's goal-setting and tracking across the global revenue organization Effectively triage escalations and drive cross-functional resolution of complex business blockers and time-sensitive customer issues Serve as CRO proxy in reviewing account plans, preparing briefing materials, and managing key customer and partner meetings with clear agendas and follow-ups Ensure timely follow-up on critical customer and partner interactions to drive business outcomes Serve as CRO Board Liaison, prepare board materials, investor updates, and collaborate on executive presentations and organizational announcements Maintain strategic partnerships with CEO's Chief of Staff, Sales Strategy & Analytics, Field Operations, Finance, and People teams to drive cross-functional alignment Develop and maintain strong relationships with executives and senior leaders to achieve project objectives and deliverables Identify delegation opportunities and optimize the CRO's time allocation to ensure focus on the highest-value strategic activities What you'll bring 8-12 years of progressive experience in strategic roles such as chief of staff, top-tier management consulting, corporate strategy, or revenue operations leadership at high-growth technology companies. You combine strategic thinking with exceptional execution skills and thrive in fast-paced, ambiguous environments. Proven track record leading cross-functional projects, influencing senior stakeholders, and delivering results in matrixed organizations Executive presence and confidence in managing conversations with Board members, C-Suite, and senior leaders across the organization Strong analytics and reporting capabilities with experience translating data into actionable insights that drive business decisions Advanced proficiency in CRM systems (Salesforce), business intelligence tools, and presentation software Demonstrated success in building scalable processes and operating systems that improve organizational efficiency Exceptional organizational and time management skills with the ability to shift priorities quickly while maintaining control across multiple projects Collaborative mindset with strong emotional intelligence and ability to work effectively across all organizational levels and functions Detail-oriented and results-driven with strong initiative, self-motivation, and a bias toward action Exceptional written communication, comfort documenting in public, and experience operating in an async environment Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Finance, or related field (or equivalent work experience) Ability to travel as needed to support the CRO About the Team The GitLab Revenue Team builds and scales the go-to-market capabilities that power our global growth and customer success. Acting as strategic partners to customers and internal stakeholders, the team aligns revenue solutions with company objectives to acquire, expand, and retain world-class customers. The Revenue Team spans multiple functions working in coordination: Sales teams drive new business and expansion across territories and segments; Customer Success ensures adoption and value realization; Sales Strategy & Analytics provides insights and enablement; Field Operations optimizes processes and systems; and Partner teams extend our market reach through channel and alliance relationships. As a fully distributed, async-first team, the Revenue Team serves as the growth engine of the company, provides data-driven insights on pipeline and forecast, and champions initiatives that strengthen market execution while modeling the collaboration practices that enable GitLab's remote culture to thrive at scale.
