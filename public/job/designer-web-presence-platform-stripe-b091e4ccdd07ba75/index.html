Designer, Web Presence & Platform

🏢 stripe 📍 Canada 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies - from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups - use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team Designers on the Presence team work at the intersection of craft and impact—balancing clarity, polish, and systems thinking with strategic storytelling and experimentation. We collaborate closely with engineering, marketing, content, and product to deliver work that is not only beautiful, but measurably moves the business. What you’ll do As a Designer on the Presence team, you’ll lead complex design projects across stripe.com, helping people understand Stripe’s products, see what’s possible for their businesses, and take the next step. Your work will span new product pages, stories tailored to specific industries and audiences, experiments, and reusable patterns that make exceptional web experiences possible at scale. You’ll drive projects from early problem framing through launch and iteration, operating independently within your focus area. You’ll turn loosely defined briefs into clear design directions, bring cross-functional partners along, and balance distinctive storytelling with intuitive, effective experiences. This is a hands-on role for a designer who brings a strong point of view, cares deeply about the details, and takes responsibility for how the work performs once it ships. Responsibilities Lead the end-to-end design of complex projects across stripe.com—from defining the problem and exploring concepts to detailed design, implementation, and iteration. Create clarity from ambiguous briefs by synthesizing user needs, business goals, content, and technical constraints into a coherent design direction and feasible plan. Translate complex products and capabilities into compelling web experiences that balance storytelling, usability, and clear paths to action. Build productive partnerships with engineering, product marketing, content design, brand, and product—aligning on goals, integrating feedback, and communicating decisions and tradeoffs clearly. Partner closely with engineers throughout the process to explore technical possibilities, develop interactions, co-create solutions, and ensure design intent carries through to the shipped experience. Apply and evolve shared components, patterns, and systems, exercising judgment about when to use existing approaches and when a new solution is needed. Use research, user testing, and performance data to inform decisions, evaluate shipped work, and identify opportunities for improvement. Raise the quality of the team’s work through thoughtful critique, sharing expertise, and leading by example in visual and interaction craft. Participate in hiring and help new teammates get up to speed. Who you are We're looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement. Minimum requirements 5+ years of relevant design experience, ideally in web, product marketing, or brand contexts. A strong portfolio demonstrating exceptional visual craft, systems thinking, and the ability to make complex information clear and engaging. Experience independently leading complex design projects from problem framing through launch in a cross-functional environment. Deep fluency in layout, typography, color, hierarchy, and interaction, with meticulous attention to how these elements work together. The ability to turn a loose brief into a clear approach, make thoughtful tradeoffs, and maintain momentum as requirements evolve. Strong communication and collaboration skills—you can explain your thinking, facilitate alignment, and manage partner expectations proactively. Proficiency in Figma and experience applying and evolving component-based design systems. Experience designing for CMS-driven websites using modular, scalable patterns. Fluency with AI design tools and workflows for ideation, prototyping, and creative exploration, paired with strong design judgment. Preferred qualifications Experience translating complex technical or financial products into accessible, compelling web experiences. Experience collaborating closely with front-end engineers, including using prototypes to explore interactions and technical possibilities. Understanding of accessibility, web performance, and SEO best practices. Experience designing for international audiences or multilingual websites. Experience using experimentation and performance insights to improve user journeys and conversion. A track record of improving shared design patterns, systems, or ways of working—not just delivering individual pages. How we work Web Presence & Platform (WPP) is organized into two pillars: Platform and Presence. The Platform pillar builds internal systems, tooling, and infrastructure to support the web. The Presence pillar owns Stripe’s public-facing storytelling across stripe.com—including our homepage, product landing pages, industry and solutions pages, and other high-impact go-to-market surfaces. Together, we shape how millions of people understand what Stripe is and what it can do for them.
