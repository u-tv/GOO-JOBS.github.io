Assistant Manager- Copy Writer

🏢 paytm 📍 Noida, Uttar Pradesh 💼 On-roll 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About the role We’re looking for a copywriter to shape how Paytm communicates across brand campaigns, marketing creatives, videos and social media. You’ll work with designers, video editors and marketers to turn briefs into creative concepts and clear, engaging copy. You’ll adapt your writing to suit different audiences, formats and channels. What you’ll do • Develop campaign ideas, taglines, headlines and messaging for Paytm’s design and marketing initiatives. • Write copy for digital and print creatives, brand campaigns, social posts and video scripts. • Adapt campaign messaging across formats while keeping the core idea and Paytm’s brand voice consistent. • Bring insights from Indian pop culture, internet trends and everyday language into creative discussions. • Collaborate with designers, video editors and marketing teams from brief to launch. Review copy in the final creative for clarity, accuracy and tone. What we’re looking for • A degree from a reputed college or university, preferably in English, Journalism, Mass Communication, Advertising or a related field. • A portfolio that shows a range of writing, such as campaign concepts, marketing copy, scripts or social content. • Strong writing and editing skills in English, with a natural ear for conversational Hinglish. • An understanding of what resonates with Gen Z audiences in India and the judgment to use cultural references where they fit. • The ability to turn a brief into a strong creative idea and carry it across multiple formats. • Comfort handling multiple briefs, meeting deadlines and incorporating feedback.
