Senior Manager, Support Engineering (U.S. Government) An overview of this role As a Senior Manager, Support Engineering on our U.S. Government support team, you’ll co-lead a team of engineers supporting complex enterprise deployments in highly regulated environments. You’ll combine people-focused leadership, technical judgment, and business acumen to develop engineers, strengthen our support program, and help customers realize value from GitLab. You’ll also connect the team’s technical contributions to outcomes that matter across the business. This includes telling a compelling, evidence-based story about how Support Engineering helps protect revenue, enable growth, remove barriers to adoption, and accelerate customer time to value. You’ll help executives and sales teams understand the significance of the work engineers perform and how that work supports customer and organizational success. Beyond individual cases, you’ll recognize Support Engineering as a valuable source of business intelligence. By bringing together engineers’ firsthand observations, product trends, and recurring customer pain points, you’ll turn field signal into actionable insights for Product, Engineering, Sales, and Customer Success. What you’ll do Co-lead, coach, and develop a team of Support Engineers, building a culture of trust, accountability, technical excellence, and customer-focused decision-making. Drive hiring, onboarding, performance management, and career development, helping engineers build technical expertise, communication skills, and an understanding of their broader business impact. Partner with other Support Engineering Managers to shape program strategy, establish priorities, plan capacity, and evolve processes and tooling as customer and business needs change. Translate complex technical work into clear, compelling narratives for executives and sales teams, connecting engineers’ contributions to revenue protection, growth opportunities, customer adoption, and faster time to value. Use operational data, customer context, and qualitative feedback to evaluate support effectiveness, communicate impact, and guide investment and improvement decisions. Establish a repeatable approach to capturing engineers’ insights and identifying product trends, recurring customer pain points, adoption barriers, and emerging government requirements. Partner with Product, Engineering, Sales, and Customer Success to turn support intelligence into actionable recommendations for product improvements, enablement, and customer engagement. Maintain technical engagement by joining customer calls, reviewing tickets, and helping engineers navigate complex or sensitive issues, while developing the team’s ability to resolve challenges independently. Coordinate responses to escalated and critical customer situations during working hours, providing clear communication and sound judgment. Champion improvements that address recurring problems, reduce customer friction, and strengthen the quality and scalability of our U.S. Government support offering. What you’ll bring Demonstrated success leading and developing technical support or engineering teams, including coaching, performance management, career development, and leadership through growth and change. A strong technical background supporting complex enterprise software or infrastructure, with the ability to assess technical challenges, recognize the value engineers deliver, and understand the implications for customer outcomes. Experience translating technical accomplishments into business value for executive and sales audiences, using clear storytelling supported by data and customer context. Business acumen to connect support outcomes with revenue protection, customer retention, growth, product adoption, and time to value. The ability to treat engineers’ knowledge and customer interactions as sources of business intelligence, synthesizing individual observations into meaningful trends and actionable recommendations. Experience setting priorities, planning resources, and improving support programs while balancing immediate customer needs with longer-term organizational goals. A people-focused leadership approach that builds trust, encourages candid feedback, recognizes contributions, and creates opportunities for engineers to grow. Experience managing high-severity incidents and sensitive escalations, including effective communication with customers, executives, and cross-functional stakeholders. The ability to influence across Product, Engineering, Sales, and Customer Success, building alignment around customer needs and shared business outcomes. Familiarity with U.S. Government customers and the operational, security, and regulatory constraints that shape their environments. We also welcome candidates with relevant experience in other complex or regulated industries. About the team Our U.S. Government Support team formed in 2019 and has grown alongside the adoption of GitLab within government agencies. We help customers troubleshoot complex environments and use GitLab’s DevSecOps platform securely and effectively. Distributed across the United States, we combine synchronous and asynchronous collaboration to support customers and build strong working relationships in a remote-first environment. Our team includes both tenured and newer engineers, bringing diverse technical experience and perspectives. We’re focused on developing our people, scaling our operations, strengthening crisis management for sensitive systems, and deepening our partnerships across GitLab. As a senior leader, you’ll help make the team’s technical expertise and customer knowledge more valuable to the broader organization, while ensuring engineers have the support, direction, and development opportunities they need to succeed.
