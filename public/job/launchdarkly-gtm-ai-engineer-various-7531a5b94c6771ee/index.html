LaunchDarkly: GTM AI Engineer

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote - US About the Job: LaunchDarkly helps engineering teams ship software faster and more safely through feature management, experimentation, and AI delivery tooling. As a GTM AI Engineer, you will design, build, and operate the AI-powered systems that make our sales, marketing, customer success, and partnerships teams more effective. You will sit at the intersection of engineering and revenue. You’ll turn messy go-to-market workflows into reliable, measurable automation, such as agents that research accounts, tools that surface buying signals, and pipelines that keep our data trustworthy. You’ll work directly with GTM stakeholders to find high-leverage problems, ship quickly, and prove impact with data. Responsibilities: Design and build LLM-powered agents and workflows for use cases like account research, lead and account prioritization, outbound personalization, call summarization, deal inspection, renewal risk detection, and customer health insights. Focus on end user experience to optimize delivery of content, process adherence and tool utilization Integrate AI systems with the GTM stack (Salesforce, Outreach, Gong, Marketo, Slack, Zoom, data warehouse, and enrichment providers). Build and maintain data pipelines that unify product usage, CRM, marketing, and support data into reliable inputs for models and agents. Partner with Sales, Marketing, CS, and RevOps leaders to translate business problems into technical solutions, and prioritize by revenue impact and effort. Establish evaluation frameworks, guardrails, and monitoring for AI outputs, covering accuracy, hallucination rates, cost, latency, and adoption. Use LaunchDarkly’s own products, such as feature flags, AI Configs, and experimentation, to roll out, test, and iterate on GTM AI systems safely. Prototype quickly, then productionize the winners with solid engineering practices (testing, observability, documentation, access controls). Enable GTM teams through training, documentation, and playbooks so AI tools are adopted and trusted. Stay current on the AI tooling landscape and recommend build vs. buy decisions. Qualifications: 4+ years of software engineering, data engineering, or ML/AI engineering experience (or equivalent hands-on experience) Strong proficiency in Python and/or TypeScript, plus SQL Hands-on experience building applications with LLMs (prompt design, tool use/function calling, RAG, agent frameworks, structured outputs) Experience integrating with REST/GraphQL APIs and SaaS platforms, ideally CRM and sales engagement tools Familiarity with data warehouses and transformation tools (Snowflake, BigQuery, dbt, or similar) Ability to evaluate and monitor AI system quality using test sets, human review, and automated evals Strong communication skills, with the ability to explain technical tradeoffs to non-technical stakeholders A bias toward shipping, iterating, and measuring outcomes Pay: Target pay ranges based on Geographic Zones* for Level 4: Zone 1: San Francisco/Bay Area or NYC Metropolitan Area, Boston, Seattle - $196,600 - $270,270 ** Zone 2: Irvine, LA, Monterey, Santa Barbara, Santa Rosa, Austin, Portland, Philadelphia, Chicago - $176,900 - $243,210 ** LaunchDarkly operates from a place of high trust and transparency; we are happy to state the pay range for our open roles to best align with your needs. Exact compensation may vary based on skills, experience, and location. *Within the United States, our geographic pay zones are defined by counties surrounding major metropolitan areas. **Restricted Stock Units (RSUs), health, vision, and dental insurance, and mental health benefits in addition to salary. Developers can target product experiences to any customer segment and maximize the business impact of every feature. And by gradually rolling out new application components, they escape nightmare "big-bang" technology migrations.
