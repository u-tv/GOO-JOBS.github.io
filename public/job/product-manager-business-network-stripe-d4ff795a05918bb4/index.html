Product Manager, Business Network

🏢 stripe 📍 AMER 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies - from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups - use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team The Business Network team is integral to Stripe’s Global Payments and Treasury Network, designed to allow businesses to discover and transact with one another seamlessly. Our goal is to create powerful network effects that supercharge the products and services that Stripe provides to millions of businesses. We are a dynamic team looking for innovative and passionate individuals to help shape the future of our offerings. What you’ll do Responsibilities Develop a deep understanding of the needs of businesses interacting on the network and identify critical pain points to address. Drive the product development lifecycle by setting strategic priorities, outlining product requirements, and leading cross-functional teams in delivering impactful solutions for our users. Collaborate with design, engineering, and other stakeholders to create user-centric product experiences that are simple yet powerful. Represent the Business Network externally with partners, clients, and stakeholders to gather feedback and promote our initiatives. Monitor key performance indicators and use data analytics to drive product decisions and optimizations. Communicate product updates and vision effectively with Stripe’s leadership and broader organization to align efforts and increase visibility. Who you are We're looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement. Minimum requirements 10+ years of Product Management experience Computer Science background or equivalent technical experience Demonstrated experience of leading initiatives across large engineering organizations, spanning multiple teams to influence tech roadmap planning and execution Strong written and verbal communication skills, structuring ambiguous concepts clearly and from first principles Thrive in a collaborative environment involving different functions, stakeholders, and subject matter experts Deep empathy for users balancing autonomy and guidance in digital commerce experiences Preferred requirements Background in launching new products or features, particularly in a startup or early-stage environment. Familiarity with complex technical systems and an ability to balance tactical and strategic considerations in a product roadmap. Experience building APIs or new integration experiences for users Experience in building or operating systems that involve user identity, payments, or financial transactions.
