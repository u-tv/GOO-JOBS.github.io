Risk & Monitoring Analyst III

🏢 coinbase 📍 Remote - USA 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Ready to do the most impactful work of your career? Coinbase is building the future of finance for people, institutions and their agents, with one place to trade crypto, equities, derivatives and prediction markets, use stablecoins, and connect AI agents with clear controls. The bar is high and the pace is intense, and we like it that way. This isn’t a place for complacency. It’s a place to be pushed past your perceived limits and do the best work of your career. If you’re ready to build the future of finance with a championship team who refuse to settle for “good enough,” you belong here. Coinbase is a remote-first, but not remote-only company. Expect to get together quarterly for in-person working sessions Risk & Monitoring Analyst III As a Risk & Monitoring Analyst III, you'll join the Risk & Controls team, which is responsible for conducting QA audits on Transaction Monitoring alerts across Coinbase's CX and Compliance production teams. You'll audit TMS and KYC-related activity to identify opportunities that improve programmatic execution, from procedural updates and analyst training to metrics creation and check-and-challenge reviews. Your insights will flow directly into feedback loops that strengthen how Coinbase detects and responds to financial crime. What you'll do: Execute weekly QA audit allocations on complex TMS and KYC cases, performing detailed error analysis and documenting findings with clear, actionable recommendations for production teams. Conduct root cause analysis on audit findings, identifying procedural gaps, recurring themes, and knowledge gaps across CX and Financial Crime Compliance teams to drive targeted upskilling and process improvements. Build and deliver QA metrics, insights, and data-driven recommendations to Client Service Management, using trend analysis and impact assessment to prioritize the highest-value improvements. Partner with Data Analytics, Operations, Financial Crime Compliance, and L&D teams to launch or enhance processes, workflows, and training programs based on QA findings. Design and refine QA rubrics, frameworks, and scoring methodologies to ensure consistent, scalable audit standards across compliance operations. Lead improvement projects end-to-end, from impact analysis and stakeholder alignment through execution and measurement, ensuring outcomes are delivered on time and to quality standards. Required Skills and Experience: 4+ years of hands-on experience executing QA, audit, or compliance testing programs across financial services, with working knowledge of BSA/AML laws, regulations, and rules. Experience working across multiple AML systems and programs (e.g., KYC, AML, EDD, TMS, sanctions), with the ability to audit complex cases and identify systemic control gaps. Demonstrated track record developing compliance metrics and reporting, with intermediate-to-advanced proficiency in Excel/Google Sheets and PowerPoint/Google Slides. Proven ability to conduct root cause analysis, translate findings into procedural improvements, and collaborate cross-functionally with global partners across CX, compliance, analytics, and L&D. Experience managing projects from scoping through delivery, with a track record of meeting quality standards and deadlines in a fast-paced compliance environment. Utilizes generative AI responsibly, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality.
