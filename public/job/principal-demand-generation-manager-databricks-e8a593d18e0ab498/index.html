Principal Demand Generation Manager

🏢 databricks 📍 United States 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

MKTQ227R22 Reporting to the Senior Director of Demand Generation, this role is responsible for uniting cross-functional stakeholders to develop and execute the biannual, strategic, and integrated Demand Generation and Field Marketing plan. You’ll act as a central connecting point between go-to-market teams—ensuring we align with the company's overarching priorities and deliver measurable growth. The impact you will have: Strategic Alignment: Lead collaboration across Demand Generation, Partner Marketing, Field Marketing, and other key leaders to articulate how integrated half-year plans roll up to company goals, securing executive alignment and approval. Business Driver/SME: Informs and champions transformational operational excellence: A strong, scalable, operating rhythm that drives accountability, transparency, and measurability throughout the campaign development process. Data-Driven Insights: Synthesize complex data and insights to guide executive decision-making and strategic adjustments. Forward Thinking: Advocates and prioritizes a roadmap, asks about the future of integrated campaign development, and identifies and prioritizes high-impact projects to help scale the organization effectively. What we look for: 7+ years of professional experience, of which 5+ years of experience are in demand generation in a high-growth SaaS startup. Strong communication and stakeholder management skills; skilled at influencing across senior levels. Proven ability to build operational frameworks, manage budgets, and drive cross-functional alignment. Comfort working in dynamic, fast-paced environments and managing competing priorities. Analytical mindset with proficiency in data-driven decision-making and campaign performance analysis. Past experience working on integrated campaigns with expertise in end-to-end campaign development, execution, and measurement. Prioritize and achieve in an environment that requires fresh thinking and new ideas. Strong growth mindset, having an open mind to new ideas and growth opportunities.
