Senior Director, Global B2B Marketing

🏢 lyft 📍 San Francisco, CA 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

At Lyft, our purpose is to serve and connect. We aim to achieve this by cultivating a work environment where all team members belong and have the opportunity to thrive. Lyft's Global B2B Platform is expanding beyond North America into a global portfolio spanning multiple owned and partner rideshare, taxi and luxury brands. This role leads marketing for the global B2B platform, and is accountable for both the strategy (audience-first, vertical go-to-market, GTM) and the operating backbone (the tooling, data, and systems) that let one marketing organization run campaigns, measure pipeline, and enforce brand and compliance standards across several distinct markets and brands. This is a senior, build the function role leading a global, matrixed marketing team, with both direct and dotted line reports. You will lead a team that plans, executes and measures marketing efforts across brands with distinct market dynamics, languages, and go-to-market maturity. A core part of the role is establishing a single source of truth for the acquisition pipeline, spend, and results to measure LTV that rolls up cleanly across the business. You will report into Lyft marketing leadership and sit on the marketing leadership team, representing global B2B marketing in cross-functional and executive forums. This is a Global role. Responsibilities: Global B2B marketing strategy and operating model Set the marketing strategy for Lyft's Global B2B Platform globally, spanning Lyft's Global B2B Platform in North America and international rideshare and taxi brand partners Orchestrate the marketing to the administrators across government, healthcare, B2B, Automotive, Transit, Business Travel (many verticals) as the single invoicing and billing platform is developed Build the operating rhythm (planning cycles, budget allocation, reporting cadence) that lets a central team support multiple brands without becoming a bottleneck Decide what should be centralized (brand standards, martech, measurement) versus what should stay local (language, market-specific offers, local media buys), and codify that split so new brands can be onboarded predictably Marketing tooling and systems Own the marketing technology stack for global B2B: customer relationship management (CRM) and marketing automation integration, campaign and content management tooling, and reporting infrastructure that unifies pipeline and performance data across brands Build the systems and dashboards needed to see one accurate view of pipeline, marketing qualified leads (MQLs) and sales qualified leads (SQLs), spend, and return on investment (ROI) across every brand in the portfolio, not just Lyft's Global B2B Platform North America Partner with data, analytics, and engineering teams to close gaps where brand-level systems do not talk to each other, and prioritize the fixes that unblock global reporting Evaluate and select vendors and platforms that can scale across multiple brands and markets, with an eye toward cost and integration complexity, not just feature fit for a single market Vertical go-to-market strategy Lead the audience-first go-to-market model built around the buyer's problem rather than the product, starting with Healthcare (Non-Emergency Medical Transportation, or NEMT) as the highest-revenue priority, and scaling the playbook to additional verticals such as Automotive, Universities, and Hospitality and Events. Direct a small number of orchestrated tentpole programs per year per priority vertical, spanning digital, live events, paid media, social, and press Maintain a thought leadership agenda, including original research and points of view, that establishes Lyft as the transportation authority in each priority vertical Cross-functional and executive partnership Build trust-based partnerships with sales, marketing, operations, finance and product leadership across every brand in the global portfolio Partner with sales on account-based marketing (ABM) for priority accounts, and own full-funnel marketing from demand generation through lead qualification and sales handoff Ensure every program complies with the relevant regulatory and marketing requirements of its industry and market, including healthcare marketing compliance where applicable Team leadership and scale through partners Lead through influence as there is hard line and dotted line reporting Build and lead (sometimes hard line, sometimes dotted line) a global team, hiring and developing people who can operate a shared playbook across markets rather than reinventing it per brand and market Manage relationships with marketing and creative agencies to scale execution beyond what the internal team can deliver Build co-marketing programs with industry and technology partners Measurement and prioritization Define success metrics for every program and report business impact against them: pipeline influenced, conversion, and program ROI, rolled up consistently across brands and regions Bring evidence-based prioritization to a growing portfolio of global initiatives, using data to decide where the next dollar of investment should go.
