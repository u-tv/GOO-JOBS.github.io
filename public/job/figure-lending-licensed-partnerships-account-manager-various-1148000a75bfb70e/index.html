Figure Lending: Licensed Partnerships Account Manager

Headquarters: URL: https://figure.com About Figure Figure Technology Solutions, Inc. (Nasdaq: FIGR; OPEN: FGRS) is the leading blockchain-native capital marketplace for the origination, funding, sale and trading of tokenized assets. More than 480 partners use its loan origination system and capital marketplace. Collectively, Figure and its partners have originated over $30 billion of loans to date, among other products. The fastest growing components are Figure Connect, its credit marketplace, and Democratized Prime, Figure’s on-chain lend-borrow marketplace. Figure’s ecosystem also includes DART (Digital Asset Registry Technology) for asset custody and lien perfection, and $YLDS, a yield-bearing stablecoin registered with the SEC as a security under the Securities Act of 1933. Figure is the market leader in real-world asset (RWA) tokenization. The company has received triple-A ratings from S&P and Moody’s on multiple loan securitizations, the first of its kind for blockchain finance. We’re proud to be recognized as one of Forbes’ Top 50 Fintech Companies and CNBC’s World’s Top Fintech Companies . About the Role As the Licensed Partnerships Account Manager, you will be the primary point of contact for key private-label lending partners across the mortgage, banking, and fintech sectors. In this role, you will manage strategic partner relationships, track performance, and identify opportunities to expand adoption, engagement, and volume across Figure platforms. You will translate data and insights into meaningful recommendations, guide partners through initiatives and integrations, and collaborate cross-functionally to support execution, resolve issues, and drive growth. You will lead partner touchpoints, including QBRs, escalations, and roadmap discussions, while maintaining strong operational rigor and helping partners achieve measurable results. What You’ll Do Manage relationships with key private-label lending partners across mortgage, banking, and fintech Track and interpret performance metrics to identify trends, opportunities, and risks Collaborate with finance, legal, product, marketing, credit, and technical teams to support partner needs Guide partners through integration, implementation, and ongoing adoption initiatives Lead QBRs and partner discussions that clearly tie goals to measurable outcomes Serve as a trusted advisor for partner leadership, providing insights and strategic recommendations Support escalations and coordinate with internal teams to drive resolutions Identify opportunities to increase engagement, expand use cases, and grow revenue Maintain strong CRM hygiene, operational consistency, and clear partner documentation Represent partnerships at events and leverage industry touchpoints to strengthen relationships What We Look For 3+ years of experience in enterprise customer success, relationship management, or account management High technical aptitude with the ability to learn Figure’s applications and understand mortgage-related workflows Strong written and verbal communication, presentation skills, and relationship-building ability Solutions-oriented mindset with the ability to translate data into actionable insights Proven success achieving and exceeding performance targets Strong organizational skills with excellent attention to detail Comfort working in a fast-paced, evolving environment with multiple priorities Operational rigor with strong CRM discipline and dependable follow-through Ability to travel up to 15% Plus: experience in mortgage, blockchain, and/or fintech environments Adhere to all company security policies and data handling procedures. Complete mandatory security awareness training within required timeframes. Promptly report any suspected security incidents or suspicious activity to the Security team.
