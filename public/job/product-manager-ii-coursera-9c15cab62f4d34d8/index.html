About Coursera + Udemy Coursera and Udemy are now one company, bringing together two mission-driven brands to create the world’s most powerful platform for turning learning into progress. Together, we help more than 300 million learners and 12,000+ enterprise customers build the skills they need for a world being reshaped by AI. Read more about the combined company by visiting our blog . Why join us now? AI is transforming how people learn, work, and grow, and the need for new skills has never been greater. Coursera brings trusted content and credentials from leading university and industry partners, while Udemy brings a dynamic skills marketplace and global network of real-world experts. By combining these strengths, we can connect more people and organizations to the skills they need, when they need them. Shape what comes next By joining our team, you’ll have the opportunity to reshape how the world learns and applies skills—and help millions of people participate in the new economy. Bring your ideas, expertise, and perspective to meaningful work that can make a difference at global scale. Team Overview: Coursera’s Product Management team drives our mission of providing universal access to world-class learning. Product Managers lead high-impact product initiatives, balancing strategic vision with hands-on problem solving to create integrated learner, partner, and customer experiences. They partner closely with Engineering, Design, Data, and Business stakeholders to deliver innovative solutions in an evolving, AI-driven education landscape. Job Overview: As a Consumer Growth Product Manager, you will play a pivotal role in optimizing Coursera’s growth engine, scaling acquisition channels, piloting new products, and driving monetization conversion across our consumer learning platform. You will operate at the intersection of data-driven experimentation, conversion rate optimization (CRO), and growth strategy, removing friction across the learner funnel and scaling business metrics. Customer-Centric Curiosity: Exhibit deep curiosity to understand customers and all elements influencing their experience, offering comprehensive, integrated solutions that address real needs. Thriving in Ambiguity: Operate effectively in uncertain, fast-changing environments, as often found in startup-like situations. Balanced Skills vs. Domain Experience: While domain expertise is beneficial, strong intellectual capacity, curiosity, proactiveness, and solid foundational product skills enable quick adaptation even without prior domain exposure. IC Excellence: For individual contributors, comfortably navigate between strategy and execution to deliver impact. Integrated Experience Design: A track record of designing end-to-end integrated experiences is a strong plus. Tool Familiarity: Familiarity with tools in the PM space, such as Figma, generative AI tools, Amplitude etc. Speed of Innovation: Demonstrates the ability to rapidly prototype, test, and iterate solutions to keep pace with evolving customer needs and emerging technologies. Balances speed with rigor to deliver impactful outcomes quickly. Responsibilities: 4+ years of experience in Product Management. Define and drive product roadmap for your area, aligning customer value with business goals and technical feasibility. Translate customer insights and market trends into integrated, scalable solutions. Lead execution from ideation through launch, delivering measurable outcomes. Partner with Engineering, Design, and Data teams to deliver product features and experiences. Leverage data and customer feedback to inform decisions and continuously improve. Design and run high-velocity A/B testing and experimentation programs across different surfaces Added advantage if: You have experience building monetization products You have taken products from idea to launch, with proven results post-launch. You have a high bar across the board - from your own contributions to the people you work with, to the products you work on.
