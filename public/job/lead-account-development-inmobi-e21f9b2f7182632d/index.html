Brief Description: As a Lead in Account development, you will act as a bridge between Supply and Demand Operations, ensuring smooth execution of publisher growth, deal health management, and campaign performance optimization. You will be responsible for managing operational workflows, analyzing performance data, and ensuring scalability through process automation and collaboration. Key Responsibilities: Manage the end-to-end publisher lifecycle , from onboarding and activation to performance optimization and retention. Ensure clean account setup, ads.txt and sellers.json compliance , and uphold traffic hygiene standards. Analyze performance indicators such as VADR, IVT, fill rate, and yield trends to identify growth opportunities. Drive cross-sell and upsell initiatives across formats, regions, and demand channels to maximize publisher revenue. Collaborate with Supply Ops, Business Development, and Product teams to streamline setup processes, improve scalability, and strengthen monetization efficiency. Contribute to publisher-side reporting automation, funnel health tracking, and anomaly surfacing through dashboards and analytical tools. Maintain adherence to content policies, brand safety, and data transparency across all supply partners. Requirements: 4-5 years of experience in adtech operations, programmatic, or publisher/demand account development. Hands-on exposure to ads.txt/sellers.json, SSP-DSP integrations, PMP execution, and traffic hygiene. Proficiency in Excel, SQL, or data visualization tools. Strong attention to detail, analytical problem-solving, and process orientation. Ability to work cross-functionally and manage multiple operational priorities simultaneously.
