The Opportunity: Okta is seeking a Manager, Order to Cash (OTC) Finance Technology to join our Business Technology Organization. Reporting to the Director of Finance Technology, you will play a pivotal role in the Finance Technology Delivery team, ensuring a seamless and efficient end-user experience. In this role, you will collaborate across multiple departments and global locations, working closely with teams in Finance, IT, Engineering, GTM, and International Operations. Your responsibilities will include optimizing internal processes, enhancing best practices, and developing innovative solutions to address both business processes and technical challenges. If you have a passion for problem-solving, automation, and transformation, we’d love to hear from you! What you’ll be doing: As a manager, you will lead a globally distributed team of business analysts and engineers, delivering E2E solutions, including Enhancements, Integrations, customizations, and others. You will evaluate potential new applications and solutions against business requirements, ensuring they are aligned with broader architectural and security standards and meet tactical and strategic objectives. You will collaborate with a broad set of Businesses, BT partners, and vendors. Use keen business process understanding, appreciation for simplicity and quality, out-of-the-box thinking, and a bit of elbow grease to build holistic solutions that meet business and technology objectives while furthering Okta’s long-range goals. Leverage your hands-on experience on ERPs such as Netsuite/Oracle Fusion Cloud and your functional and technical knowledge in Order Management, Billing, Payment, collection, and Cash & Treasury Management GL reconciliation. Able to guide the team on designing, building, testing, and deploying solutions within Order to Cash(OTC) business processes and applications. Provide technical support and troubleshoot day-to-day production issues across the ERP and peripheral systems. Collaborate with BT and Business teams to identify system enhancements, integrations, and process automation opportunities. Contribute to our overall SDLC processes across business and BT while improving the health of the existing NetSuite application. Proactively understand business pain points and propose solutions that are scalable and efficient. What you’ll bring to the role: 12+ years of professional experience architecting, leading, and delivering enterprise business solutions in OTC areas within NetSuite or Oracle Fusion Cloud and Revenue SaaS Application like Revpro. 5+ years of experience as a people manager leading high-performing teams of business analysts and software engineers in agile environments. Deep functional expertise in Order-to-Cash (OTC) processes, including Sales Orders, Revenue Recognition, Billing (Subscription and Consumption models), Pricing, Returns, Credit/Debit Memos, and Collections in the High-Tech/Software industry. Hands-on experience leveraging AI tools and frameworks—such as NetSuite Prompt Studio, SuiteScript Generative AI APIs, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architectures—to customize model behaviors, automate text generation, and enhance system intelligence. Proven track record implementing agentic AI solutions for proactive exception management, alongside intelligent conversational agents to automate complex OTC workflows, streamline dispute resolution, and optimize cash collection cycles. Proven experience integrating ERP systems with SaaS and enterprise solutions, including Coupa, Anaplan, Xactly, Stripe, Avalara/Vertex, RevPro, Snowflake, and EDI platforms. Demonstrated ability to apply advanced analytics and AI-driven insights across end-to-end OTC processes to eliminate fulfillment and billing bottlenecks and scale operational performance. Exceptional communication skills with a strong capacity to bridge technical and executive domains, influencing cross-functional stakeholders while upholding Scrum/agile methodologies. Cross-functional knowledge of adjacent financial domains, including Procure-to-Pay (P2P), Record-to-Report (RTR), Tax, and Treasury, is preferred. And extra credit if you have experience in any of the following! A Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or Management Information Systems or equivalent years of industry experience NetSuite Administration or ERP consultant certification is an advantage. Certification in ERPs such as Oracle Fusion or is a plus.
