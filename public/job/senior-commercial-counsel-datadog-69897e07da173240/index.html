Senior Commercial Counsel

🏢 datadog 📍 Melbourne, Australia 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

The Legal team supports Datadog’s rapid growth by providing a wide range of legal services across a highly dynamic technology company, from driving new business via contracts and compliance efforts, to protecting Datadog’s offerings. Datadog’s Legal team collaborates with virtually every team across the organization, from engineering to product to marketing. Within Legal, the Commercial, Alliances & Procurement team focuses on all commercial engagements, channel and alliances agreements, procurement matters, and related legal topics across Datadog’s global footprint. We are a collaborative, people-first team that values acting as trusted advisors, communicating clearly, embedding inclusion, and holding ourselves accountable. This Melbourne-based role offers the chance to play a critical part in the success of a rapidly growing and innovative SaaS company across APJ, with a particular focus on supporting our growing public sector business in Australia and New Zealand. You will lead complex negotiations with private and public sector customers and partners across APJ, and serve as a trusted advisor to internal stakeholders as our business expands in the region. You will help shape Datadog’s contracting and compliance approach for government customers and other fast-evolving markets in APJ. You will collaborate closely with Sales, Deal Desk, Procurement, and Privacy, providing practical solutions that balance legal risk with business objectives. The role also gives you exposure to cutting-edge SaaS areas such as privacy, security, and AI. At Datadog, we place value in our office culture: the relationships and collaboration it builds, and the creativity it brings to the table. We operate as a hybrid workplace so that Datadogs can create the work-life harmony that best fits them. What You’ll Do Negotiate and close commercial agreements, NDAs and ancillary documents supporting customer sales and partner engagements across APJ. Lead negotiations with public sector customers in Australia and New Zealand, navigating government procurement frameworks and contracting requirements. Give practical, decisive legal guidance to business teams, exercising sound judgment on risk trade-offs and escalation, and translating business needs into contractual commitments. Coach sales team members on negotiation strategies, including regular internal training sessions and office hours. Improve contract templates, playbooks, and processes, including public sector terms, in line with emerging laws and regulations across APJ. Support knowledge-sharing and mentorship across the Commercial Legal team and contribute to broader team initiatives and special projects. Build strong relationships with stakeholders across APJ, including Deal Desk, Procurement, and regional sales leadership. Who You Are A seasoned lawyer (qualified in Australia or another relevant jurisdiction) with at least 8 years’ law firm and/or in-house commercial transactional experience. Experience negotiating with public sector or government customers, with a strong understanding of government procurement requirements in Australia and New Zealand. Strong experience negotiating SaaS contracts and related areas such as privacy, security, intellectual property, and AI. Fluent in English with excellent written and verbal communication. Proficiency in other APJ languages is valuable but not required. Thrives in negotiating, communicating, and educating, and is comfortable leading sensitive conversations with legal and business counterparts. Has integrity, excellent judgment, and high emotional intelligence, and builds strong collaboration with peers and stakeholders. Comfortable working independently and across time zones within a distributed APJ team, adhering to established processes and recognizing when to seek guidance. Driven to close contracts, with the ability to prioritize multiple deals under tight deadlines. Able to work from Melbourne, Australia. Datadog values people from all walks of life. We understand not everyone will meet all the above qualifications on day one. That’s okay. If you’re passionate about technology and want to grow your skills, we encourage you to apply.
