Smartsheet: Software Engineer II, AI Experience (Remote Eligible)

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: -REMOTE, USA- For over 20 years, Smartsheet has empowered teams to manage work seamlessly and scale solutions smarter. Now, in our most ambitious chapter yet, we are uniting human teams with AI agents. By orchestrating the work agents do best, automating manual tasks and uncovering insights at scale, we create the space for people to focus on what truly matters: judgment, creativity, and big thinking. That is magic at work, and it’s what we show up for every day. Smart Assist is Smartsheet's AI agent for work management, used by enterprise customers to analyze, create, and act on their work. AI Experiences builds the orchestration for Smart Assist and the user-facing implementations that expose Smartsheet's Intelligent Work capabilities to any AI surface, so users can reach the right intelligence at the right time through a coherent, reliable experience. AI Experiences is a pillar within Applied AI, and this is a full-stack team. The work spans these areas: the Smart Assist frontend (UI components, interaction patterns, and rendering for AI experiences across Smartsheet); the Smart Assist backend and orchestrator; UX for Smart Assist tools and agents, from response formatting and onboarding to error states. On the backend, the orchestrator routes each request to the right sub-agent based on user intent, keeps conversation state across multi-turn interactions, handles fallbacks when sub-agents are unavailable, and controls feature-flagged rollout of new capabilities. We partner closely with Agent Engineering on much of this logic. The role. You will own features and components of Smart Assist from prototype to production, across the front end, the orchestrator and back-end services. You will turn ambiguous product asks into reliable, well-measured behavior customers can trust, and work with product, design, and AI Experience to ship. You Will: Own a core piece of the Smartsheet AI experience, or a significant part of one (for example conversational analytics, chart-to-dashboard creation, or a new entry point into the assistant) from design through production. Build full-stack: user-facing components, the services behind them, and the contracts between the two, with typed APIs, clear error states, and tests. Design and ship agent tools, skills, MCP tools, and MCP Apps with contracts the model can use reliably, and decide when an action belongs in a tool, a service, or the model. Shape how agent output appears to users: streaming responses, loading and failure states, and handling of long or partial results, working with design and product. Build and evolve the Smart Assist orchestrator: intent-based routing to sub-agents, multi-turn conversation state, fallback and degradation handling, and feature-flag rollout of new capabilities, partnering closely with Agent Engineering. Populate the context the agent receives, such as user locale, timezone, and workspace state. Define how quality is measured for your area: build golden cases and scorers, set regression gates, and use traces and production signals to decide whether a failure needs a prompt, tool, context, or UI fix. Instrument features with tracing, structured logs, and latency, cost, and error dashboards, and act on what they show. Take part in on-call, security, and cost work for Smart Assist, shared with Agent Engineering. Reproduce production failures and review code for your team. Partner with PMs, designers, Agent Engineering, AI Platform, MCP, and data teams. Learn how customers use what you build and flag gaps in requirements and tests. Use AI coding tools to ship faster without lowering the quality bar. What we're building next: Our goal for the coming year is to make Smart Assist safe to trust and hard to leave. You will help shape work across four themes: Be everywhere: an assistant that understands what you are looking at, such as the rows you have selected, and meets you in the flow of work, including smoother import and file upload. Build the habit: an assistant that remembers how you like to work, with personal, team, and organization profiles, quick commands, and a growing UI component catalog. Earn the trust: previews before changes apply, linked sources and explanations for results, AI edits visible in history and activity logs, and a view into the agent's reasoning. Work together: a directory of agents, @mentions, and the ability to save and share results as pages. Further out, the vision is a Smart Assist that finds you before you ask: a proactive, insight-first surface that learns how your workspace works, suggests prompts personalized to you, brings in context from tools like Slack, Zoom, and documents, supports multiplayer conversations, and offers help inside the chat itself. You would help define the trust model for that experience from real customer learnings and feedback. You Have: 2–4 years of professional software engineering shipping production systems. Strong in Python and/or TypeScript/React, with good habits in testing, code review, and debugging. Experience building and shipping customer-facing features end to end, including APIs, data modeling, and user-facing behavior. 1+ years building LLM-powered features in production: tool or function calling, agent loops, prompt and context design, or multi-step workflows. Working knowledge of how agents fail (bad tool choice, context overflow, hallucinated arguments, runaway cost) and how to design around it. Experience designing eval scorers and golden datasets, and using results to make and defend a decision. Experience with MCP tools and MCP Apps, structured tool calling, or reusable agent skills. Comfort with production observability and with data-heavy environments (Databricks or equivalent). Evidence of using AI coding agents to ship faster without lowering the quality bar. Clear communication of quality, cost, and reliability findings to technical and non-technical audiences. Legally eligible to work in the U.S. on an ongoing basis. Strong plus: Experience with conversational or chat UI at scale; LLM tracing tools (LangSmith, Phoenix, Braintrust or similar); prompt versioning or CI-integrated evals; dashboard, charting, or data-visualization products; enterprise SaaS permissions and entitlements; prompt-injection mitigation and output validation. What success looks like: First 90 days: ship a feature or improvement to production with eval coverage, and take your first on-call rotation. First 6 months: own a feature or significant part of a project with measured gains in task success, latency, or user adoption, and contribute at least one reusable component or pattern. Why this role: You build the Smart Assist experience used by enterprise customers, with real users and a measurable quality bar. You work across the stack, from the interface to the agent tools behind it, so you see your work reach customers end to end. The problems are distinctive: enterprise-grade permissions and security, and one assistant that works across a broad work-management product. You work on the hard parts of production agents, on a team that values engineering rigor and diverse perspectives.
