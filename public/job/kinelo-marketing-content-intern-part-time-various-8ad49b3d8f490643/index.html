Kinelo: Marketing & Content Intern (Part-Time)

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: San Francisco, California, United States Marketing & Content Intern (Part-Time) Remote (US) About Kinelo Kinelo is an early-stage AI startup building technology that helps companies work faster by keeping company knowledge current and making it available to both people and AI. Our proactive AI Coworkers use that knowledge to take on work, coordinate across teams, and keep people informed. We're looking for a marketing intern to help us build and grow Kinelo's presence in the market. The role covers a wide range of marketing activities, from content, social media, and creative production to market research, partnerships, and campaigns. You'll work directly with our founding GTM lead and have plenty of opportunities to come up with ideas and execute them. On a given day or week, you might Come up with content ideas for X, LinkedIn, and other channels, then help turn those ideas into posts, articles, videos, or visual content. Develop creative video concepts, plan and edit short-form videos, and experiment with different styles and formats. Design graphics, social assets, and other visuals for our website, content, and marketing campaigns. Research AI trends, competitors, and relevant industry conversations to identify interesting topics, market opportunities, and new ideas worth exploring. Find relevant creators, influencers, newsletters, and communities, research their audiences, and help coordinate potential collaborations. Support marketing campaigns, from researching target audiences and developing content to coordinating distribution and tracking results. Help create website content, customer stories, presentations, and other marketing materials. Research journalists, podcasts, events, and other opportunities to get Kinelo in front of relevant audiences. Experiment with different marketing ideas, see what works, and bring recommendations for what we should try next. We're looking for someone who Is currently studying marketing, communications, journalism, media, or a related field. Has previous marketing/content internship experience OR has built and managed their own social media accounts. Writes well and has a good instinct for what makes content interesting, useful, or worth sharing. Has experience creating and editing videos, especially short-form content, and is comfortable developing creative concepts from scratch. Has a good eye for visual design and experience with tools like Canva, Figma, CapCut, Adobe, or similar. Understands social media platforms, follows relevant creators and trends, and has opinions about what makes good content. Is a strong researcher who can dig into unfamiliar topics, find useful information, and turn it into ideas or recommendations. Is actively using AI tools in their own work, studies, or creative projects, and enjoys experimenting with new tools and finding ways to get better results. You don't need a technical background, but you should be excited about what AI makes possible. Is resourceful, organized, and comfortable figuring things out independently. Logistics Part-time, approximately 10-20 hours per week $25/hr Remote (US) To apply: https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/kinelo-marketing-content-intern-part-time
