Smartsheet: Director, Finance Systems & ERP

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: -REMOTE, USA- For over 20 years, Smartsheet has empowered teams to manage work seamlessly and scale solutions smarter. Now, in our most ambitious chapter yet, we are uniting human teams with AI agents. By orchestrating the work agents do best, automating manual tasks and uncovering insights at scale, we create the space for people to focus on what truly matters: judgment, creativity, and big thinking. That is magic at work, and it’s what we show up for every day. Corporate Systems Engineering builds and operates the software platforms, integrations, and automations that power Smartsheet’s core business functions across Finance, Sales/GTM, and People & Culture. Our team owns mission-critical systems and workflows that enable how the company hires, sells, bills, pays, reports, and scales. We operate at the intersection of software engineering, enterprise platforms, and business-critical data, treating internal systems with the same rigor, reliability, and product mindset as customer-facing software. Financial Systems Engineering owns and evolves the technology ecosystem that powers Smartsheet’s financial operations and monetization capabilities. The team is responsible for platforms including NetSuite, Coupa, Planful, Stripe, ZoneBilling, Metronome, and supporting technologies such as Avalara, along with the integrations, data, automation, and engineering capabilities that connect these platforms across the enterprise. As Director, Finance Systems & ERP, you will lead the engineering organization responsible for Smartsheet’s financial technology ecosystem and define the long-term technical strategy for how these platforms evolve. You will own the architecture, engineering maturity, technical health, and delivery of systems supporting core financial operations, procurement, planning, payments, billing, revenue processes, and emerging monetization models. This is a strategic and highly visible technology leadership role. You will lead major transformation initiatives including ERP modernization, billing transformation, and the evolution toward usage-based and consumption-based monetization. You will represent Corporate Systems in executive steering committees and major transformation programs, partnering closely with Finance, Accounting, Procurement, Product Engineering, Sales/GTM, Data, and other technology leaders. You will partner closely with Product Managers across Financial Systems and Billing & Monetization to combine business and product strategy with a forward-looking technical strategy. You will be expected to challenge legacy architectures and operating models, identify opportunities to simplify and automate financial processes, and determine how modern software engineering, enterprise data, automation, and AI can improve how Finance technology operates. You will report to the Senior Director of Corporate Systems and collaborate closely with Product and Engineering leaders across the organization.
