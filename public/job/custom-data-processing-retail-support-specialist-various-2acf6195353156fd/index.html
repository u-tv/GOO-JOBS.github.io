Custom Data Processing: Retail Support Specialist

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Romeoville, Illinois, United States URL: https://workforcenow.adp.com/mascsr/default/mdf/recruitment/recruitment.html?cid=e4d85aa2-30b6-4ecf-853d-99b7e8a6653c&ccId=9201073424092_2 Retail Support Specialist Employment Full-time, Exempt Location Remote Department Portfolio Management & Delivery Reports To Director, Training About CDP Custom Data Processing, Inc. (CDP) is a premier provider of data management systems and services for the public health community. Since 1979, we’ve delivered technology that makes a difference, from WIC services and electronic health records to environmental health and data analytics. Built on family values, we operate with integrity. Our diversified mix of solutions gives us a deeper perspective; one we use to understand the whole picture and serve public health professionals across the country. The Role As a Retail Support Specialist, you’ll help grocery stores and other WIC retailers accept WIC benefits through our WIC Direct EBT system. You’ll work support tickets from retailers, state agencies and third party processors, help test and certify point-of-sale (POS) systems, and support special projects like WIC online shopping. What You’ll Do Support tickets and retailer support Work support tickets from retailers, state agencies and WIC online shopping customers, and troubleshoot POS issues until they’re resolved Train retailers, answer their questions, provide APL support, and activate or deactivate WIC Direct accounts Ensure tickets are resolved based on CDP standards Review data and run audits to keep the master retailer list in WIC Direct accurate POS testing and certification/ validation Help test and certify integrated POS systems for new and existing CDP customers Load benefits onto test cards so POS providers can run their certification transactions Work with store corporate contacts, POS providers, payment processors and state vendor units to keep systems updated, certified and ready for WIC EBT transactions Special projects Support special projects such as WIC online shopping, helping new and existing online retailers get set up and transacting Stay with customers as they move from implementation into day-to-day operations, so nothing falls through the cracks The Ideal Candidate You are a natural troubleshooter. When a POS system rejects a transaction, you dig in until you find the root cause, not just a workaround. You are organized with a busy ticket queue. You track every open issue, follow up without being asked, and close the loop with retailers and state agencies. You are a clear communicator. You can walk a store manager through a fix and explain the same issue to a payment processor’s technical team. You are curious about new technology. Projects like WIC online shopping excite you, and you pick up new systems quickly. You care about CDP’s mission and the families who rely on WIC to put healthy food on the table. Qualifications Minimum Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in information technology, computer science or a related field, or equivalent work experience. 3 years of experience troubleshooting and resolving issues with POS devices. Knowledge of POS systems, payment processors and testing methods Clear, patient communicator who can adjust their style to the person in front of them, moving between technical and non-technical communication. WIC transaction process knowledge, including Federal rules and regulations Ability to travel frequently, including overnight stays. Nice to Have Experience working a ticket queue in a help desk or service management tool Experience supporting e-commerce or online ordering platforms Compensation & Benefits The salary range for this position is $60,000 – $72,000. Actual compensation within that range is based on factors unique to each candidate during the hiring process. CDP also offers a comprehensive benefits package and meaningful opportunities for growth and advancement within the company. This is a remote position open and open immediately. Our Culture At our core, we value our relationships, both internally and externally. We respect everyone’s contributions: our staff and customers all have a seat at the table. We listen, we comment, and we decide our way forward based on what is best for all. Our relationships are paramount because they determine our long-term success. When everyone feels empowered, everyone succeeds. Ready to make an impact?
