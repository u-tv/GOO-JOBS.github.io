Social Media Manager, Brand & Influencers

🏢 robinhood 📍 Menlo Park, CA; New York, NY 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Join us in building the future of finance. Our mission is to democratize finance for all. An estimated $124 trillion of assets will be inherited by younger generations in the next two decades. The largest transfer of wealth in human history. If you’re ready to be at the epicenter of this historic cultural and financial shift, keep reading. About the team + role We are building an elite team, applying frontier technologies to the world’s biggest financial problems. We’re looking for bold thinkers. Sharp problem-solvers. Builders who are wired to make an impact. Robinhood isn’t a place for complacency, it’s where ambitious people do the best work of their careers. We’re a high-performing, fast-moving team with ethics at the center of everything we do. Expectations are high, and so are the rewards. We are looking for a Social Media Manager, Brand & Influencers to help define how Robinhood shows up for the next generation of investors. This is a hands-on creative role for someone who lives and breathes internet culture, understands what resonates with Gen Z, and knows how to turn that understanding into original, engaging social content. This role is based in our Menlo Park, CA or New York, NY offices, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week. At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams. What you’ll do We're looking for a maker, not just a strategist. Someone who can spot an emerging trend, develop a creative concept, and bring it to life through content that feels native to the platform. You'll help build a distinct, culturally relevant voice for Robinhood through always-on content, social-first storytelling, and unexpected brand moments that earn attention and spark conversation. You'll combine strong creative instincts with an understanding of brand strategy, platform behavior, and audience insights. You'll move quickly, experiment often, and know when to jump on a cultural moment versus when to create something entirely new. This role owns social-first brand content, creative development, and activations, with a particular focus on engaging Gen Z audiences. It does not include day-to-day community moderation or customer support, which are owned by dedicated teams. Create social-first content for Gen Z: Concept, develop, and produce original, culturally relevant content for Robinhood's social channels, with an emphasis on TikTok, Instagram, X, and emerging platforms. Bring ideas from trend or insight to execution, creating content that feels native to the internet, not traditional brand advertising. Bring social ideas to life: Don't just brief ideas — make them happen. Write scripts, develop concepts, capture content, collaborate with creators, and work with creative partners to bring social ideas to life quickly. Build Robinhood's voice with the next generation: Help shape a distinct, recognizable brand personality that resonates with Gen Z through storytelling, cultural relevance, and a deep understanding of how younger audiences engage with brands. Turn trends into content: Stay immersed in internet culture, emerging creators, platform formats, and conversations shaping Gen Z. Identify opportunities for Robinhood to participate in culture in ways that feel timely, authentic, and ownable. Develop always-on social programming: Build repeatable content formats, original series, and creative franchises that give younger audiences a reason to follow, engage with, and share Robinhood's content. Drive social-first campaigns and activations: Partner with Brand, Creative, and Product to develop campaigns, creator collaborations, and unexpected cultural moments that build awareness and affinity with younger audiences. Experiment, learn, and iterate: Test new creative approaches, formats, and ideas at a high cadence. Use performance data, social listening, and audience behavior to understand what's working and continuously evolve our content. Balance creativity with strong brand judgment: Push creative boundaries while ensuring content aligns with Robinhood's brand standards and the regulatory requirements of financial services. What you bring 5+ years of experience in social media, content creation, or social-first creative roles, ideally at a consumer brand, creative agency, or as a creator with experience building content for Gen Z audiences. A strong understanding of finance and investing. You stay informed on financial markets, investing trends, and the conversations shaping the industry. A portfolio of social content you've actually made. You can point to original videos, posts, series, or creative concepts you've personally developed and brought to life, with a clear understanding of what made them successful. Deep fluency in Gen Z and internet culture. You understand the humor, trends, creators, communities, and cultural conversations shaping younger audiences, and can translate those insights into content without making a brand feel forced or out of touch. Strong hands-on creative skills. You're comfortable writing, concepting, scripting, capturing, and developing content, and can move from idea to execution without relying on a large production team. A track record of making content that breaks through. You've created social-first work that earns organic engagement, sparks conversation, and gives audiences a reason to care about a brand. A platform-native mindset. You understand how content should look, feel, and behave differently across TikTok, Instagram, X, YouTube, and Reddit, and know how to adapt creative ideas accordingly. A bias toward action and experimentation. You thrive in a fast-moving environment, can turn ideas around quickly, and aren't afraid to test unconventional creative approaches. Strong creative and brand judgment. You know how to balance cultural relevance with brand credibility and can navigate the creative constraints of a highly regulated industry. An analytical mindset. You use audience insights, social listening, and performance data to inform creative decisions and continuously improve your work. What we offer Challenging, high-impact work to grow your career. Performance driven compensation with multipliers for outsized impact, bonus programs, equity ownership, and 401(k) matching. Top Tier benefits to fuel your work, including 100% paid health insurance for employees with 90% coverage for dependents. Access to the Robinhood Employee Fund that gives eligible US employees the opportunity to invest in a private employee fund that provides exposure to Robinhood Ventures funds. Access to the best AI tools on the market and continuous AI skill-building for every employee, technical or not. Lifestyle wallet - a highly flexible benefits spending account for wellness, learning, and more. Employer-paid life & disability insurance, fertility benefits, and mental health benefits. Time off to recharge including company holidays, paid time off, sick time, parental leave, and more! Exceptional office experience with catered meals, events, and comfortable workspaces.
