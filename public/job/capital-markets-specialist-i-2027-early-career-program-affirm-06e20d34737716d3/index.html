At Affirm, we exist for the moments that matter—giving people a clear, predictable way to pay over time, with no hidden fees, no surprises, and no tradeoffs on what matters most. About Affirm’s Early Career Program Affirm’s Early Career Program (ECP) brings together interns and new graduates across Engineering, Business, and Risk & Analytics. You’ll do meaningful work alongside your team while becoming part of a community of peers who are learning and growing together. Through our structured one-year program, you’ll get dedicated onboarding, mentorship, and professional development to help support your growth and confidence. And with our hybrid-office approach, you’ll have opportunities to connect in person, collaborate with your team, and build relationships across Affirm. About the Capital team The Capital team manages the financial foundation that supports Affirm’s lending and innovation. We lead the funding, liquidity, and risk management strategy across capital markets, treasury, deal structuring, analytics, and bank partnerships. The team secures efficient funding while supporting sustainable business growth. We balance cost, flexibility, and risk to ensure Affirm’s financial resilience through all economic cycles. By optimizing capital structure and managing liquidity, we help build a scalable and resilient financial platform. About the role We're looking for an outstanding team member to join Affirm’s Capital Markets team! Affirm Capital is responsible for raising the capital that funds our originations, a critical part of Affirm’s revenue strategy. As a Capital Markets Specialist I, you will own a significant portion of the platform’s day-to-day capital activities, which drive Affirm’s ability to scale its consumer point-of-sale lending business globally. This is a complex role that is essential to the functioning of a consumer finance and payments platform. The ideal team member will have a strong desire to grow their knowledge in structured debt finance or debt capital markets; strong technical and interpersonal skills; and the desire to work closely with both internal and external stakeholders. What you’ll do Manage and execute the Capital Markets team’s work streams, with an initial focus on operations, which requires frequent collaboration with numerous internal and external counterparts Actively work on Affirm’s existing and prospective capital transactions including; asset-backed securities (‘ABS’), whole loan sales (‘forward flow’), warehouse / debt financing structures and international funding facilities (Canada, UK and beyond)! You will assist in structuring, negotiating, and executing our transactions with the goal of growing into a deal leadership role over time Take charge of cross-functional communication with Engineering, Product, Commercial, and Credit/Risk teams to drive scale for Affirm’s capital platform and funding strategy Build and manage relationships with existing and potential investors Develop a strong understanding of Affirm’s business from a unique perspective and learn how Capital Markets fits into broader corporate initiatives What we look for On track to graduate in Spring 2027 with a degree in Finance, Economics, or a related field Interest in structured finance, private credit, debt capital markets, investment banking, fixed income sales & trading, or another credit-oriented role Financial modeling and transaction management experience, along with an understanding of fixed income principles Exceptional communication skills and ability to manage relationships with internal and external stakeholders Experience with Excel; SQL and Python a plus High level of humility, accountability and a strong desire to learn and grow Excellent analytical skills and attention to detail Clear alignment with Affirm’s core values Base Pay Grade - H Equity Grade - 2 Affirm focuses on providing a simple and transparent pay structure which is based on a variety of factors, including location, experience and job-related skills.
