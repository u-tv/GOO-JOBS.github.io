People Project Manager

🏢 lyft 📍 Nashville, TN 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

At Lyft, our purpose is to serve and connect. We aim to achieve this by cultivating a work environment where all team members belong and have the opportunity to thrive. Lyft is changing fast. We’re integrating acquired businesses, growing in Europe and new global markets, launching new lines of business, and building a workforce for autonomous vehicles alongside our corporate teams. Half of our team members now live outside the US. Our People team has to keep pace, and that means designing programs that work for a bigger, more global and more varied Lyft. The People Strategy & Programs team leads complex, cross-functional work that comes to the People organization. We run the People team’s operating rhythms and make sure our biggest priorities get delivered across every People vertical: HR Operations, Total Rewards, Talent Acquisition, Team Member Experience and People Business Partners. As People Project Manager, you’ll own high-complexity programs end to end as the People team transforms alongside the business. You’ll report to the Chief of Staff to the Chief People Officer and partner closely with People leaders, Finance, Legal and business leaders across Lyft. This isn’t a role where you hand work off once the plan is set. You’ll facilitate the strategy, validate it with subject matter experts, lead when the work needs a leader, and get into the details to keep it moving. This role is for a systems thinker who’s comfortable with ambiguity and enjoys every part of the People function. Success looks like complex programs that land well, People leaders who see you as a trusted partner, and repeatable approaches that make the next big project easier than the last. What you’ll work on: Your portfolio will shift with the business, but example programs include: Standing up People programs for new and growing workforces, such as hourly and field teams Partnering with the business on workforce and location strategy: the right roles in the right places at the right cost Driving AI and automation adoption across the People team, from prioritization through measuring impact Designing org structures and ways of working built for speed and scale, from spans and layers to decision rights and our matrix model What success looks like: By month 3: Owning 1 - 3 active programs with clear plans, owners, metrics and status reporting in place By month 6: Leading at least one major cross-functional program, such as an integration or org design effort, with minimal oversight By month 12: Recognized by People leaders as the go-to partner for complex, cross-functional work, with at least one repeatable playbook or framework in use across the team recommendations with subject matter experts across the People team Building strong relationships with senior leaders and assembling the right cross-functional teams to deliver Developing
