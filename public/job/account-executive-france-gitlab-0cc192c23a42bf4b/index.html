travel to meet customers and attend events as needed.</li> </ul> <h2>What you'll bring</h2> <ul> <li value=&quot;1&quot;>Experience selling software as a service (SaaS) solutions.</li> <li value=&quot;2&quot;>Ability to manage a broad mid-market territory as the primary point of contact, owning a book of business from prospecting through close, adoption, and expansion.</li> <li value=&quot;3&quot;>Skill in building and maintaining strong customer relationships, including effective discovery, negotiation, and closing to support long-term customer outcomes.</li> <li value=&quot;4&quot;>Experience conducting consistent outbound prospecting, creating and maintaining a healthy pipeline, and maintaining accurate territory and forecast documentation.</li> <li value=&quot;5&quot;>Ability to collaborate closely with cross-functional partners (sales development, customer success, renewals, marketing, partners, and technical teams) to coordinate account strategies.</li> <li value=&quot;6&quot;>Ability to connect customer business challenges to GitLab's DevSecOps platform and communicate its value to different audiences, documenting customer insights and lessons learned; relevant, transferable sales experience from a variety of backgrounds is welcome.</li> <li value=&quot;7&quot;>Fluent French language skills (required).</li> <li value=&quot;8&quot;>Experience selling to development or DevSecOps teams is helpful, but not required.</li> </ul> <h2>About the team</h2> <p>You'll join the Europe, Middle East, and Africa Sales team, a distributed group of Account Executives, sales managers, and business development team members.
