Senior Manager, Workplace Operations, Safety & Security

🏢 mongodb 📍 Palo Alto 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About the Role MongoDB is hiring a Senior Manager within our Workplace function, part of the Americas Employee Experience department. You will run day-to-day facilities operations for our West Coast offices, create the events strategy that supports our hybrid work model, and lead workplace health, safety, physical security, and business continuity for the region. The goal is safe, secure, welcoming workplaces and an engaging employee experience. You will also manage a team of Workplace Coordinators across the West Coast office. You will act as the senior regional/site operator balancing everyday workplace vitality with enterprise resilience. By partnering with global Workplace counterparts, Real Estate, IT, People, Legal, and executive site sponsors, you will ensure our spaces are safe, legally compliant, operationally sound, and supportive of a dynamic hybrid workforce. We are looking for a proactive, collaborative communicator who can drive change and combine sound technical knowledge with practical judgement. Key Responsibilities People Leadership & Operational Excellence Lead, mentor, and develop a distributed team of Workplace Managers and Coordinators across regional sites, fostering operational rigor, customer-first service, and proactive risk management. Set regional operational objectives, SLAs, and performance KPIs; coach the team on complex vendor escalations, budget governance, and cross-functional partner management. Build and maintain physical safety, security awareness, and inclusive hospitality best practices across all regional sites. Facilities and Workplace Operations Oversee daily facilities operations across offices, including space planning, janitorial, HVAC, food services, sustainability practices and partnerships and prompt resolution of maintenance issues. Own key local vendor relationships and escalations; review of vendor contracts and renewals, manage vendor RFPs, approve invoices and manage service requests and operating costs within budget. Use data and workplace technology to improve space planning, seating, and operational efficiency. Measure service delivery effectiveness through SLA, Pulse, Utilization and other metric driven analysis. Own the regional operational budget (OPEX); review and approve vendor invoices, forecast site operating costs, and identify efficiency opportunities. Events and Employee Experience Support West Coast executive events roadmap in partnership with executive support, marketing, CTD and AV/IT teams; including town halls, QBRs, offsites, and ERG sponsored events. Partner with the Community and Events team and the West Coast Site Leadership Committee to maintain the site events calendar, sponsor, promote and lead key community building events. Operationalize hybrid work at scale, using data to drive compliant, cost-effective site operations that connect to our real estate and geo strategies. Deliver a consistent, high-quality hybrid employee experience in the office services we provide and events we create across our West Coast offices. Health, Safety, and Physical Security Own the regional health and safety policy, framework and standards; coordinate risk assessments, inspections, corrective actions, and compliance with applicable regulations. Lead the physical security program: site standards for access control, visitor management, alarms, surveillance, and badging, based on a risk-led strategy and site threat assessments. Manage security and safety vendors and tools (including Alert Media and Verkada), with clear accountability and performance measures. Establish processes for reporting and investigating incidents and near misses, and train site leaders, first aiders, fire wardens, and other responders. Stay up to date with industry practice and legislative changes. Lead and/or participate in key operating mechanisms and establish a cadence to educate and instill safe/secure practices across sites. Business Continuity and Incident Response Maintain emergency response plans, escalation paths, and communications for scenarios such as fire, medical emergencies, earthquakes and other severe weather, security threats, and utility disruption. Lead or support incident response and crisis cases, including tracking and supporting employees and keeping stakeholders informed in collaboration with key regional/site leaders.. Run drills, exercises, and lessons-learned reviews, and track remediation. Maintain dashboards, risk registers, and incident and compliance records, and report on key risks, trends, and priorities to leadership. Create consistent regional practices, contribute to global standards, and embed safety and security in everyday workplace decisions. Requirements 10+ years in facilities, office management, workplace safety, physical security, business continuity, workplace/employee experience, or a related workplace risk portfolio, including vendor management and events support. Experience leading and guiding teams. Knowledge of health and safety principles, risk assessment, incident investigation, emergency preparedness, and physical security fundamentals. Space management and programming experience. Strong organizational, analytical, and project management skills; able to prioritize competing demands and deliver through influence. Excellent written and verbal communication and sound judgement in sensitive, time-critical situations. Proficiency with Google Suite, MS Office, Slack, and Mac. Ability to travel regularly across sites. Most Desirable Experience with multiple sites or regulatory environments, ideally in a multinational or tech company. Professional certification in safety, security, business continuity, or risk management; familiarity with ISO 45001, 22301, or 31000. Experience running drills, tabletop exercises, or post-incident reviews, and with access control or incident management platforms. How Success Will Be Measured Offices run smoothly and within budget, and the events roadmap is delivered well. Sites have current, consistently applied safety and security standards, with risks owned and reduced. Assessments, drills, training, and corrective actions are completed on schedule, and incidents are handled with urgency and follow-through.
