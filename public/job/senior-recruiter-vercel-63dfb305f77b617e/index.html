Senior Recruiter

🏢 vercel 📍 Hybrid - San Francisco, New York City 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Vercel: Vercel is the agentic infrastructure company, freeing people and agents to ship what's next. For more than a decade we've helped builders move from idea to production with speed, security, and exceptional developer experience. Now we're scaling our products for both agents and people to ship and run software, built in the open and trusted by OpenAI, PayPal, Ramp, Supreme, and millions of developers worldwide. About the Role: Vercel is looking for a Senior Recruiter to help us scale our Sales, Field Engineering, Partnerships, and other GTM and/or G&A functions as needed. You'll partner directly with business leaders and hiring managers on everything from steady, high-volume pipelines to senior, hard-to-fill searches. You'll drive the full recruiting process for your functions: developing sourcing strategy, building pipelines of qualified passive candidates, running a rigorous and efficient interview process, and closing offers. You'll act as a consultative partner to your hiring managers, bringing market insight, calibration feedback, and clear recommendations. We're looking for someone who is comfortable managing a full req load across multiple stakeholders, uses data to prioritize and to report on progress, and uses AI and recruiting technology to work more efficiently without compromising candidate experience. This role is hybrid and based in one of our San Francisco or New York City, with three in-office days per week (Monday, Tuesday, and Friday). What You Will Do: Own full-cycle recruiting for your assigned GTM functions, from search kickoff through offer acceptance, and take on new functions as hiring priorities change Partner with hiring managers to define role requirements, align on the hiring bar, and develop targeted sourcing strategies Build and maintain consistent, high-quality pipelines, including a focus on passive candidates, using direct outreach, referrals, networks, and communities Screen candidates against clearly defined competencies and present hiring managers with well-qualified, calibrated slates Manage the interview process end to end, keeping candidates, interviewers, and hiring managers informed and moving Structure and negotiate offers in partnership with Compensation and other People teams, guiding candidates through to close Track pipeline metrics, report on progress and risk to hiring managers and recruiting leadership, and use the data to improve your process About You: You have 6+ years of full-cycle recruiting experience supporting go-to-market and/or technical roles You've recruited in fast-paced, high-growth environments where hiring priorities shift quickly and speed matters without compromising quality You've supported multiple functions or role types and can ramp on a new hiring profile efficiently You have a track record of closing competitive candidates and managing offer processes from expectation-setting through acceptance You've built trusted relationships with hiring managers and can influence decisions on profile, process, and candidates You source proactively and can point to hires generated through your own outreach You use recruiting data and metrics to identify issues early, prioritize your work, and communicate clearly with stakeholders You use or even build AI tools in your recruiting workflow and can describe where they've improved your speed or quality Bonus If You: Have recruited for developer tools, infrastructure, or frontend technology companies with strong GTM functions, or are comfortable discussing frontend development, web performance, and cloud technologies Are familiar with Vercel's technology stack, competitive landscape, and market positioning Have built recruiting processes and systems for GTM teams from the ground up Compensation & Benefits: Competitive compensation package, including equity.
