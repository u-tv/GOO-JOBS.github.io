An Overview about the Role We're looking for a Senior Product Manager to own the Lead-to-Opportunity (L2O) domain within our Salesforce and GTM technology ecosystem. This role sits at the intersection of marketing operations, sales process, and technology owning the roadmap for how leads are captured, scored, routed, and converted into pipeline, and how our broader GTM tech stack supports that motion. This is a foundational hire one of the first product managers on a team actively transforming from a reactive BSA model into a proactive, roadmap-driven product organization. You'll be both an individual contributor driving your own domain and a mentor helping BSAs grow into product manager roles. You'll have direct influence over how we shape the team's future operating model. routing, conversion, opportunity and the broader GTM technology stack integrated with Salesforce Translate business strategy from Marketing, Sales Development, RevOps, and GTM leadership into sequenced, prioritized system capabilities with clear outcomes and success metrics Define the domain's strategic priorities each quarter and half, balancing net-new capability delivery, stabilization, and technical debt reduction with transparent trade-off frameworks Drive quarterly planning ceremonies, partnering with Engineering Managers and BSAs to align capacity with roadmap commitments Own domain-level documentation: process maps, current-vs-future state lead lifecycle flows, and decision logs that keep stakeholders and the team aligned on scope and ownership Stakeholder Partnership & Requirements Translation Serve as the primary product voice for all L2O and GTM Systems initiatives, partnering closely with Marketing, Marketing Operations, SDR/BDR leadership, Sales, and RevOps Lead technical constraints to business stakeholders and business context to engineers Team Mentorship & Capability Building Act as a product craft mentor and senior thought partner for the BSA team, Program Strategy & Delivery Oversight Lead initiatives end-to-end: discovery, requirements, prioritization, solution design review, UAT, launch, and post-launch measurement Write and maintain clear What You'll Bring 7+ years in Salesforce product management, business systems analysis, or Marketing/Revenue Operations technology roles, with demonstrated growth into product ownership Strong working knowledge of Lead-to-Opportunity processes; lead capture, scoring, routing, and conversion and how they connect marketing execution to sales pipeline Hands-on familiarity with the GTM technology stack: Salesforce Sales Cloud plus marketing automation platforms (Marketo, HubSpot, or Pardot), lead scoring and routing tools, and data enrichment or intent providers Good Understanding of quoting process Demonstrated ability to own and communicate multi-quarter internal product roadmaps to senior business and technology stakeholders Proven experience leading initiatives end-to-end with multiple stakeholders and competing priorities, especially across Marketing and Sales Sufficient technical fluency to engage meaningfully with engineering on API design, data model decisions, and integration trade-offs — without needing to write code Experience mentoring or upskilling analysts and BSAs, you enjoy developing people, not just managing tickets Excellent written and verbal communication; able to represent technical trade-offs to non-technical executives and translate business asks into clear technical requirements Comfortable operating in ambiguity and driving structure where none exists Experience with AI-assisted development workflows, data governance and compliance for lead data, or prior people-management experience About the Team You'll be one of the first product managers on a team actively transforming from a reactive BSA function into a proactive, roadmap-driven product organization. The Lead-to-Opportunity domain sits at the top of GitLab's revenue funnel; the quality of data, routing, and conversion here affects every downstream system and every seller. Getting this right is not an IT problem; it's a revenue problem. You'll have direct influence over how we shape the team's future operating model and how the next generation of BSAs grows into product managers.
