Copywriting Lead

🏢 affirm 📍 Remote Canada 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Affirm is reinventing credit to make it more honest and friendly, giving consumers the flexibility to buy now and pay later without any hidden fees or compounding interest. This role is based on the Creative Content Team, which is part of the larger Content Design Team at Affirm. We’re the heart of Affirm’s communications, UX experiences, and products shaping how we engage with our users across both web and the Affirm app. You’ll join a lively group of 10+ Content Designers who work independently and collaboratively to create clear, user-friendly, and effective product experiences. As part of the overall Product organization at Affirm, we ensure that every user interaction is meaningful and aligned with Affirm’s broader goals, seamlessly integrating messaging into product touchpoints. We’re looking for a senior-level Product Copywriter to join our team, playing an essential role in shaping and delivering clear, impactful messaging across our product and marketing communications—specifically email, push notifications, and web. If you’re looking to contribute meaningfully to high-visibility projects and grow within a forward-thinking, creative team, this is an exciting opportunity. What You’ll Do Write and refine content across product touchpoints, including email, push notifications, in-app messaging, and web, ensuring a consistent voice and tone. Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including Product Managers, Product Marketing, Design, and Legal & Compliance to ensure that product messaging aligns with both user needs and business objectives. Develop and maintain messaging frameworks, content templates, and writing standards to create scalable, consistent content that serves multiple product and marketing functions. Drive content strategies for campaigns, from ideation through A/B testing, ensuring that messaging enhances the overall user experience and helps drive key business goals. Contribute to the evolution of Affirm’s voice and tone, refining how we communicate with both consumers and merchants across digital touchpoints. Support content optimization by analyzing performance metrics and iterating on content based on testing insights, user feedback, and performance reviews. Collaborate with legal and compliance teams to ensure that all content complies with regulatory standards and internal guidelines. Work closely with the Product Design team to ensure that messaging is integrated into product design and user experiences seamlessly. Champion a content-first approach to design, advocating for the inclusion of content in product decisions to elevate user experiences and engagement. What We Look For 4+ years of experience in copywriting and/or content design, ideally within a product or tech-focused environment. Hands-on experience writing for email, push notifications, web, and in-product messaging, with a solid understanding of best practices for each medium. Proven ability to collaborate effectively with cross-functional partners, including product marketing, designers, product managers, and engineers, to create customer-centric solutions. A self-starter mentality, capable of managing multiple projects simultaneously, meeting deadlines, and adapting to shifting priorities in a fast-paced environment. Attention to detail with a strong commitment to clarity, scalability, and consistency across all written content. Experience with legal and compliance review processes, ensuring that all content meets necessary regulatory standards. A passion for content innovation, bringing fresh ideas and new approaches to how we communicate with customers.
