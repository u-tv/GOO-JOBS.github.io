You'll make an impact by: Developing and executing product marketing and branding strategies for business-to-consumer and business-to-business audiences Translating customer insights, market research, competitive intelligence, and industry trends into differentiated product positioning and go-to-market plans Partnering with product management, marketing communications, design, and market-launch advertising to increase awareness, engagement, lead generation, and sales Providing creative direction for marketing content and promotional assets while maintaining consistency with brand standards across customer touchpoints Establishing key performance indicators, evaluating campaign and marketing performance, and using data-driven insights to improve results and marketing effectiveness You’ll
