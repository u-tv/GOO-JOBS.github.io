UMAEZ LLC: Customer Support - Resolution & Outbound Retention Specialist (Voice)

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Indonesia URL: https://jobs.workable.com/company/hS1Fg6yA2YGb3vBeEZy9fP/jobs-at-umaez-llc About US We are a 7-8 figure direct-to-consumer (DTC) brand portfolio operating across the US and international markets, led by our flagship brand GODA. We build and scale high-demand beauty, fragrance, and personal care products shipped directly to hundreds of thousands of customers. At our scale, customer trust, fast problem resolution, and post-purchase loyalty are everything. When a customer experiences friction, whether a delayed delivery, a subscription billing question, or a public escalation, this role turns a frustrated buyer back into a loyal brand advocate. The Role Standard customer support reacts to incoming tickets. As Executive Resolution & Outbound Retention Specialist, you prevent churn and protect brand reputation. Sitting inside our Outbound, Retention & Reputation team, you are the senior voice customers speak with when high-priority issues arise, and the proactive specialist who reaches out by phone and email to recover failed payments, retain subscribers, and grow verified 5-star reviews. What You'll Be Doing BBB & High-Priority Escalation Ownership 24-Hour SLA Resolution: Monitor, respond to, and resolve incoming BBB complaints and public review escalations within a strict under-24-hour response window. Phone-First De-Escalation: Conduct calm, empathetic outbound calls to frustrated US customers, diagnose what went wrong in Shopify/Commslayer, and deliver fair, tailored solutions (express reshipments, Loop exchanges, store credit, or approved refunds). Official Portal Closure: Draft clear, non-defensive written responses inside the BBB Business Portal and follow up directly with customers until they confirm satisfaction and close the case. Cross-Functional Fulfillment Coordination: Partner directly with our Order Fulfillment team to expedite stuck, delayed, or partially fulfilled orders tied to escalated cases. Outbound Retention, Payment Recovery & Reputation Growth Failed Payment & Win-Back Calls: Execute daily outbound phone calls and tailored email follow-ups to help subscribers update failed payment methods and keep active deliveries running smoothly. Subscription Save Outreach: Connect with at-risk or canceling subscribers to understand friction points and offer flexible alternatives (skips, cadence adjustments, or product swaps). Trustpilot Review Generation: Reach out to verified happy GODA customers via phone and email to encourage authentic 5-star reviews on Trustpilot and public storefronts. Escalated Email Queue: Handle sensitive email threads escalated from Inbound Customer Care involving dispute/chargeback threats, billing misunderstandings, or repeat delivery issues. Tracking, Compliance & Root-Cause Feedback 100% Case Hygiene: Maintain accurate, real-time logs across Commslayer, Shopify, and the BBB resolution tracker so zero open cases fall through the cracks. Upstream Prevention: Tag recurring escalation drivers (carrier delays, renewal confusion, SKU defects) and share weekly insights with the CS Manager and Process Experts to fix root causes. Must-Haves Proven Voice + Email Experience: 2+ years in DTC e-commerce customer support, escalations, retention, or account resolution, with strong comfort making daily outbound phone calls and writing tailored escalation emails. US-Overlap Availability: Able to work a full-time shift overlapping with US daytime/afternoon hours to reach US customers live by phone. Composed, Persuasive Communication: Clear, warm, natural English (spoken and written) with the emotional intelligence to de-escalate upset buyers without sounding scripted or defensive. High Follow-Through Discipline: You never mark a case finished until the resolution is confirmed by the customer and properly logged in external portals. Tech-Stack Fluency: Hands-on comfort working daily in e-commerce order dashboards, helpdesks, cloud VoIP dialers, and Google Sheets trackers. Strong Advantages Reputation & Dispute Experience: Prior hands-on management of Better Business Bureau (BBB) complaints, Trustpilot outreach, or chargeback/dispute prevention. DTC Core Tools: Direct experience with Shopify, Commslayer (or Zendesk/Gorgias), Loop Returns, Aircall/RingCentral, and subscription platforms (Recharge, Skio, or Stay AI). Subscription Brand Background: Previous experience supporting a high-volume DTC beauty, fragrance, wellness, or personal care subscription brand. Instant No's Phone Hesitation: You prefer hiding behind email macros rather than picking up the phone to speak directly with an upset customer. Copy-Paste Mindset: You rely on rigid policy scripts instead of listening to the customer's actual issue and tailoring the fix. Loose Documentation: Forgetting to update trackers or close portal responses. Accurate records protect both the customer and our brand rating. How We Measure Your Performance BBB SLA & Closure Rate: 100% first outreach within 24 hours and measurable month-over-month improvement in GODA's BBB resolution rate and profile rating. De-Escalation & Retention Save Rate: Percentage of escalations, failed payments, and at-risk subscriptions resolved without chargebacks or churn. Outbound Activity Consistency: Daily completion of outbound call and email targets across BBB, payment recovery, and review queues. QA & Compliance Score: Consistent adherence to brand voice, pre-approved resolution authority limits, and zero billing/compliance errors. Hiring Process & Compensation Competitive base salary based on experience + QA-gated monthly performance bonus tied to retention saves, payment recovery, and escalation resolution. 100% remote role inside a structured 7-8 figure DTC Customer Experience organization with clear growth into Senior Anchor, QA, or Team Lead roles. Selection Gate: Shortlisted candidates complete a brief 5-minute live phone de-escalation roleplay and a short written BBB response test.
