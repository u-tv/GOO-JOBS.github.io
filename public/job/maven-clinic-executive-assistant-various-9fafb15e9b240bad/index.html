Maven Clinic: Executive Assistant

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: New York, New York, United States; New York, NY; Remote, US (Hub cities) Maven Clinic is the world's largest virtual clinic for women and families on a mission to make healthcare work for all of us. Through Maven Enterprise, the company partners with more than 2,300 employers and health plans to provide end-to-end women's and family health programs spanning fertility and family building, maternity and newborn care, parenting and pediatrics, and menopause and midlife — improving clinical outcomes, reducing healthcare costs, and expanding equitable access to high-quality care at scale. Through its consumer platform, Maven provides direct access to virtual care across 30+ specialties, as well as dedicated hormone and GLP-1 care programs purpose-built for women. Founded in 2014 by CEO Kate Ryder, Maven Clinic has raised more than $425 million from leading healthcare and technology investors including General Catalyst, Sequoia, Dragoneer Investment Group, Oak HC/FT, StepStone Group, Icon Ventures, and Lux Capital. Recognized for innovation and industry leadership, Maven has been named to the TIME100 Most Influential Companies, CNBC Disruptor 50, Fast Company's Most Innovative Companies, and FORTUNE Best Places to Work. Learn more at mavenclinic.com An award-winning culture working towards an important mission – Maven Clinic is a recipient of over 30 workplace and innovation awards, including: TIME 100 Most Influential Companies (2023, 2026) Fortune Change the World (2024) CNBC Disruptor 50 List (2022, 2023, 2024) Fortune Best Workplaces for Millennials (2024) Fortune Best Workplaces in Health Care (2024) Fast Company Most Innovative Companies (2020, 2023) Fortune Best Workplaces NY (2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024) About the Role: As a Senior Executive Assistant, you will support four to five members of Maven’s C-suite and play an important role in helping them use their time effectively. You’ll manage complex calendars, coordinate travel and meetings, anticipate needs, and keep priorities and follow-ups moving. This role calls for strong judgment, discretion, and a calm, proactive approach. You’ll work closely with the executives you support, the broader Executive Assistant team, and partners across Maven to make sure the details are handled and leaders are prepared to focus on the work that matters most. What you'll do: Manage complex calendars and scheduling. Coordinate internal and external meetings across competing priorities, maintain clean calendars, and proactively resolve conflicts. Build in appropriate preparation, travel, and follow-up time. Help executives make the best use of their time. Learn their priorities and working styles, flag calendar tradeoffs, identify meetings that may need to be delegated or reconsidered, and surface conflicts or risks early. Coordinate meetings from start to finish. Prepare agendas and materials as needed, confirm attendees and logistics, and make sure meeting links, rooms, and technology are ready. Help executives prepare for high-stakes conversations and track resulting follow-ups. Manage travel and expenses. Arrange door-to-door travel and accommodations, share clear itineraries and relevant materials in advance, monitor travel changes, and submit expenses promptly in line with company policy. Communicate on executives’ behalf. Serve as a thoughtful point of contact for colleagues and external stakeholders. Draft and coordinate clear, concise communications that reflect each executive’s tone and priorities. Support board and investor logistics. Coordinate scheduling, materials, and logistics for board meetings, investor interactions, and other external stakeholder engagements, with discretion and care. Place after “Own logistics of events and gatherings.” Track work and close loops. Maintain an organized view of open tasks, deadlines, and commitments; keep executives and stakeholders informed; and make sure handoffs have clear owners and next steps. Be comfortable working cross-functionally with teams that your execs depend on. Support special projects. Take ownership of selected projects that improve executive operations, team coordination, or the EA team’s shared resources. Identify and implement process improvements for your executives and the EA team. Own logistics of events and gatherings. Coordinate executive and team meetings, offsites, dinners, celebrations, and other events, managing logistics and working with internal partners and vendors often on the ground. Project manage cross-functional strategic initiatives. Be a key player in project management of key projects within your executive’s teams and function, enabling your exec to ensure effective ways of working with their teams and achieving key strategic goals. Use AI thoughtfully to improve how the work gets done. Apply approved AI tools to streamline tasks such as meeting preparation, travel briefs, communications, follow-up tracking, and repeatable workflows. Review AI-generated work for accuracy, use good judgment about what information belongs in which tools, and maintain confidentiality. What you'll bring: 4-6 years of experience as an Executive Assistant, including experience supporting senior or C-suite executives; experience supporting multiple executives is a plus. Strong organization and attention to detail, with the ability to manage several priorities and adapt as plans change. Sound judgment and the confidence to ask clarifying questions, make recommendations, and escalate risks or blockers early. Comfort working in a fast-paced, high-growth environment, with the ability to shift priorities quickly. A proactive and hands-on approach: you anticipate needs, prepare ahead, and follow through without needing repeated reminders. Clear, polished communication across email, Slack, meetings, and in-person interactions. The discretion and reliability to handle confidential company, personnel, and executive information. Responsiveness and reliability. Reachable for urgent matters, with the ability to support executives consistently. Experience coordinating complex meetings, travel, and events. Comfort using Google Workspace, Slack, project-management tools, and virtual meeting platforms. Curiosity about AI and experience using AI tools in your work, or the initiative to learn and apply them responsibly. A team player with a collaborative working style, strong accountability, and extreme ownership, who shares knowledge, supports coverage, and helps the EA team work well together. Flexibility to adjust your schedule occasionally, including for travel or time-zone differences, to meet business needs or time-sensitive changes. The base salary range for this role is $128,000 - $150,000 per year. You will also be entitled to receive equity and benefits. Individual pay decisions are based on a number of factors, including qualifications for the role, experience level, and skillset. This role requires active work authorization in the US. Maven embraces a flexible hybrid work model. Our teams primarily operate from the New York Metropolitan area, NY, and remotely via San Francisco/Bay Area, CA, Seattle, WA. For those in our New York City office, we encourage in-person collaboration by requiring team members to work onsite three days a week (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday). For those based in Boston, DC, Chicago, Seattle, and San Francisco, we encourage in-person collaboration by requiring team members to attend monthly Work Together Days within these cities. This policy aims to balance remote work flexibility with the benefits of face-to-face interaction. At Maven we believe that a diverse set of backgrounds and experiences enrich our teams and allow us to achieve above and beyond our goals. If you do not have experience in all of the areas detailed above, we hope that you will share your unique background with us in your application and how it can be additive to our teams.
