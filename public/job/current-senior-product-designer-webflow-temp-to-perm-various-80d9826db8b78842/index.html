Job Description

Headquarters: Remote (US) Senior Product Designer (Webflow) At Current At Current, Senior Product Designers (Webflow) lead the design and delivery of engaging digital experiences across multiple client engagements. You'll partner closely with cross-functional teams across strategy, production, engineering, and client services to create thoughtful, user-centered websites that are both beautifully designed and expertly built in Webflow. Senior Product Designers are strategic, hands-on creators. You're equally comfortable conducting UX discovery, designing polished experiences in Figma, and building production-ready websites in Webflow. You own projects from concept through launch, partnering with engineering only when advanced custom development or integrations are required. What You'll Do Lead UX and visual design across websites and digital experiences for multiple client engagements Partner with strategy, production, engineering, and client stakeholders to define user experience and creative direction Create user flows, wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity designs in Figma Design and build responsive, production-ready websites directly in Webflow Develop scalable design systems, reusable components, and CMS structures in Webflow Translate business goals into intuitive, accessible, and conversion-focused digital experiences Collaborate with engineers on advanced functionality, integrations, and custom development when needed Optimize websites for usability, accessibility, responsiveness, and SEO Conduct UX reviews and recommend improvements based on best practices and user insights Stay current on UX trends, accessibility standards, and evolving Webflow capabilities What You'll Bring 5–7 years of UX/UI, Product, or Digital Design experience, ideally within an agency or consulting environment 2+ years building production-ready websites in Webflow Advanced proficiency with Figma and Webflow Strong portfolio demonstrating end-to-end ownership from UX strategy through final Webflow implementation Experience building scalable Webflow websites using CMS Collections, Components, and design systems Experience with Client-First methodology, Relume, and Finsweet Attributes Strong understanding of UX, responsive design, accessibility (WCAG), and information architecture Working knowledge of HTML and CSS with the ability to partner effectively with engineers Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills Nice to Have Experience with Lottie, GSAP, or interactive animations Experience integrating Webflow with HubSpot, Memberstack, Airtable, Zapier, Make, or similar platforms Experience using analytics or A/B testing to inform design decisions Familiarity with Jira, Workfront, or similar project management tools Experience working within Agile delivery environments Why Current At Current, we believe the best digital work happens when strategy, design, production, and technology operate as one team. We partner with ambitious brands to create digital experiences that are thoughtful, scalable, and built to drive real impact. We're a remote-first agency with teams across the U.S., working collaboratively across websites, platforms, campaigns, CRM ecosystems, and digital product experiences. Our environment is fast-paced, highly collaborative, and built for people who enjoy solving complex technical challenges alongside smart, supportive teammates. This is a temporary-to-permanent opportunity . The role will begin as a hourly position, with the opportunity to transition into a full-time employee based on business needs and individual performance. The target hourly compensation range for the temporary portion of this role is $45.00 - $65.00 per hour . If converted to full-time, the target range of base compensation for this role is $100,000 - $125,000 annually . Actual compensation is influenced by a wide array of factors, including but not limited to skill set, level of experience, and location.
