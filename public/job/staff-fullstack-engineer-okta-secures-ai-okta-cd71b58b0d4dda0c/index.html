🏢 okta 📍 Toronto, ON, Canada 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Secure Every Identity, from AI to Human Identity is the key to unlocking the potential of AI. Okta secures AI by building the trusted, neutral infrastructure that enables organizations to safely embrace this new era. This work requires a relentless drive to solve complex challenges with real-world stakes. We are looking for builders and owners who operate with speed and urgency and execute with excellence. This is an opportunity to do career-defining work. We're all in on this mission. If you are too, let's talk. Get to know Okta Okta is The World's Identity Company. We free everyone to safely use any technology, anywhere, on any device or app. Our flexible and neutral products, Okta Platform and Auth0 Platform, provide secure access, authentication, and automation, placing identity at the core of business security and growth. At Okta, we celebrate a variety of perspectives and experiences. We are not looking for someone who checks every single box. We're looking for lifelong learners and people who can make us better with their unique experiences. The AI Acceleration Team AI agents are now acting on behalf of employees, calling tools, reading data, and making changes inside the systems companies care most about. Every one of those actions raises an identity question: who is this agent, who is it acting for, and what is it allowed to do? Okta is building the products that answer it. The AI Acceleration team exists to get those products to customers faster. We're part of the Okta Secures AI Pillar, and our job is to accelerate its roadmap wherever we can have the biggest impact. In practice, that means picking up high-priority work alongside the teams that own it, ramping quickly, and shipping. The Staff Full Stack Engineer Opportunity As a Staff Full Stack Engineer on the AI Acceleration team, your mission is to help build the industry's first Identity Security Fabric for the agentic era. You'll be a technical leader across the Okta Secures AI Pillar's highest-priority work, helping us deliver on our North Star: &quot;To get AI right, you have to get identity right.&quot; You will help deliver our four-pillar maturity model (Discover, Onboard, Protect, and Govern), providing enterprise-grade visibility and governance for both human and non-human identities. By building solutions that move beyond reactive perimeter security, you will help organizations prevent &quot;Shadow AI&quot; and turn AI risk into business value. This role requires deep expertise in distributed systems and a commitment to AI-native engineering as our standard. You will rethink backend design, validation, and delivery through AI-augmented workflows, ensuring our platform provides secure, interoperable, and scalable access, and enforcing the principle of least privilege for every agent action. What you'll be doing Turn ambiguous, high-priority roadmap goals into clear specs, technical designs, and scoped plans, and make the trade-offs explicit Design and build full stack features, from backend services and APIs to the admin console experience Build secure, identity-aware systems where every agent action is authenticated, authorized, and audited Make and defend architectural decisions, and capture them so the owning teams can build on them after we move on Partner with Product, Design, and engineering leads across the business unit and company to agree on scope, unblock shared decisions, and keep work moving Drive AI-native development practices, helping the team rethink how we design, review, and ship software with AI as a core part of the workflow Leave things better than you found them: CI/CD, observability, documentation, reliability, and support processes Invest in your own growth and the growth of the engineers on your team, and raise the technical bar of the team What you'll bring to the role Experience applying AI across the full software development lifecycle, beyond basic tool usage. You can evaluate new AI tooling with good judgment, spot when AI output is wrong, and bring a team along Proven experience designing scalable backend architectures and integrating complex APIs with modern web technologies, including making and defending architectural decisions with lasting impact A solid understanding of web authentication and authorization: how auth flows work, and best practices for securing sensitive data Deep expertise building reliable, secure, enterprise-grade software in Java or another type-safe language Proficiency in JavaScript/TypeScript, with the ability to build and ship frontend features, not just unblock them Strong understanding of cloud architecture, with experience designing and operating large-scale services on AWS Comfort with ambiguity and change: you can join work mid-flight, write down what &quot;done&quot; looks like, and adjust as priorities shift Extra credit if you have experience in any of the following Identity and Access Management (IAM), OAuth/OIDC, or authorization systems AI agents, MCP, or API gateways (Kong, Envoy, or similar) Building software that customers install and run in their own environments, such as plugins, SDKs, or sidecars Leading a team through a meaningful shift in ways of working, such as AI adoption, developer tooling, or engineering culture Education and Training B.S. or M.S. in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
