The Role We seek a technical, hands-on Security Engineering Manager to build and lead a security engineering team within Auth0 Customer Identity that secures how we build and use AI. The team's work spans two areas. The first is building security tooling and controls for AI, including gateways and guardrails that secure the use of MCP servers, LLMs, agents, and the systems around them. The second is architecting and reviewing the security of our AI products, working shift-left with engineering and product teams so that security is built in from the start of design instead of added afterward. This role leads a single team embedded within the Customer Identity product unit and works across Product, Engineering, and the broader Okta Security organization to turn AI security guidance into shipped controls, supporting the growth of our global Customer Identity business. We are an engineering-focused organization that seeks to stay on the cutting edge of security technology and the AI threat landscape. In this role you will set direction for a team that doesn't just advise on AI risk — it builds the gateway, guardrails, and tooling that enforce secure AI usage, and reviews AI systems early enough to shape their design. You will lead a globally distributed group of talented engineers who share a passion for security and enjoy solving complex problems at scale. This role works closely with and alongside the broader Okta Security team in support of Okta's company-wide security strategy and Okta’s Secure Identity Commitment . This is a player-coach role. You will stay hands-on: designing and prototyping, writing and reviewing code, challenging architectures, and earning the respect of strong engineers, while also hiring, growing, and retaining your team and shaping its roadmap.
