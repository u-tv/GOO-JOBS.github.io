LaunchDarkly: Executive Assistant

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote - US About the Job: LaunchDarkly is seeking an Executive Assistant to partner closely with our Chief Revenue Officer and support the broader global Revenue organization as it scales. This role will help the CRO stay focused on the highest-priority work by managing their calendar, travel, meetings, and competing priorities. The right person will anticipate needs, resolve scheduling conflicts, and make thoughtful decisions about what requires the CRO’s attention. The Revenue organization spans Sales, Customer Success, pre- and post-sales, Partnerships, and international markets. As the organization grows, the EA may also provide administrative support to other senior Revenue leaders. The right person will be comfortable working across time zones, navigating senior stakeholders, handling confidential information, and learning the CRO’s priorities and working style. LaunchDarkly is a rapidly growing software company with a strong mission and vision carried out by a talented and diverse team of employees. Our goal is to help teams build better software, faster. Software powers the world and LaunchDarkly empowers all teams to deliver and control their software. Responsibilities: Manage the CRO’s calendar, priorities, meeting flow, and time allocation across a globally distributed organization. Triage scheduling requests and prepare the CRO for key meetings by confirming logistics, participants, and materials; recommend what should be delegated, deferred, or declined. Manage domestic and international travel, bookings, logistics, expenses, approvals, and last-minute changes. Plan and coordinate Revenue leadership offsites, including quarterly gatherings, and other executive events; attend select events in person. Coordinate scheduling and logistics for the CRO’s participation in Revenue leadership meetings and other recurring forums in partnership with the Chief of Staff and Revenue Operations. Provide administrative support to the global Partnerships leader and, as the organization grows, other senior Revenue leaders. Coordinate meetings with customers, partners, executives, and other strategic stakeholders. Build strong relationships across Revenue, Finance, People, Legal, Product, Engineering, Marketing, and other cross-functional teams while maintaining systems that improve visibility and follow-through. Handle sensitive information with discretion and confidentiality and support administrative follow-through for select executive priorities and events. Qualifications: Experience as an Executive Assistant, Executive Business Partner, or comparable role supporting senior or C-level executives in a globally distributed organization. Experience working in a software or technology company; high-growth or B2B SaaS experience is a plus. Experience managing executive calendars, travel, expenses, complex scheduling, and meeting or event logistics. Demonstrated ability to anticipate problems, protect executive focus, and make sound decisions about competing priorities. Strong organization, attention to detail, follow-through, written and verbal communication, and interpersonal skills. Ability to learn an executive’s working style and business priorities, manage up, and support multiple senior leaders as needs evolve. Sound judgment, discretion, emotional intelligence, and comfort handling confidential information. Proficiency with Google Workspace, Slack, Zoom, and related collaboration tools. AI fluency and curiosity, with the ability to use emerging tools thoughtfully to improve productivity, organization, and administrative workflows while protecting confidential information. Experience supporting a CRO, President, CEO, or other externally facing executive is a plus.
