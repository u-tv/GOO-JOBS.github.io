Job Description

About the Role: We are looking for a highly analytical and data-driven Business Analyst who can derive actionable insights to support business growth, product strategy, and operational efficiency. The ideal candidate should have strong problem-solving abilities, hands-on experience with SQL and Power BI, and the ability to work closely with cross-functional teams to drive data-backed decision making. Key Responsibilities: ● Analyze large datasets to identify trends, business opportunities, and performance gaps. ● Build and maintain dashboards, MIS reports, and business performance trackers using Power BI. ● Write complex SQL queries for data extraction, validation, and analysis. ● Partner with business, product, marketing, and operations teams to support strategic initiatives. ● Monitor key business metrics and provide actionable recommendations to stakeholders. ● Automate recurring reports and improve reporting efficiency. ● Present insights and findings to leadership teams in a clear and structured manner. Requirements: ● 2–5 years of experience in Business Analytics / Data Analytics. ● Strong proficiency in SQL and Power BI is mandatory. ● Good understanding of data visualization, reporting, and dashboarding. ● Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving skills. ● Experience working with large datasets and deriving business insights. ● Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills. ● Prior experience in fintech, consumer internet, or digital businesses will be preferred. Preferred Skills: ● Knowledge of Excel, Python, or other analytics tools is a plus. ● Exposure to product analytics, customer lifecycle analysis, or performance marketing analytics would be an advantage.
