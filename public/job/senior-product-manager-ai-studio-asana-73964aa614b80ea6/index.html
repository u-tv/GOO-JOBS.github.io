Senior Product Manager, AI Studio

🏢 asana 📍 San Francisco 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Asana is building the platform for human and agent collaboration in the enterprise. With the launch of Agentic Work Management (AWM), AI is now a foundational part of the platform, and this shift brings a new class of problems. Customers need to trust agentic work, govern it, and see a return on it before they will scale it. Many of the opportunities that matter most for customers sit between our suite of AI products, rather than inside any particular one, which is where you come in. This role sits in our AI Platform Group on a small, fast-moving team with a deliberately broad mandate: work across the boundaries other product teams operate within, identify the cross-cutting problems and opportunities that no single team owns, and build leveraged solutions that create scalable, differentiated value for our customers. This role is based in our New York City office with an office-centric hybrid schedule. The standard in-office days are Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday. Most Asanas have the option to work from home on Wednesdays. Working from home on Fridays depends on the type of work you do and the teams with which you partner. If you're interviewing for this role, your recruiter will share more about the in-office requirements. What you’ll achieve Work directly with enterprise customers and prospects to discover the challenges they face when managing agentic work in Asana, and bring back what you learn to inform your roadmap. Discover and quickly validate new, high-leverage opportunities, then work cross-functionally to deliver end-to-end solutions, fast. Consistently choose leverage over local wins by selecting problems whose solutions compound across Asana's AI products rather than improving a single surface in isolation. Help set the standard for what Asana invests in versus not as the AI capability landscape shifts, piloting new capabilities and accelerating their adoption and integration into our AI products. Evaluate and sequence key strategic capabilities, including AI ROI measurement and reporting, AI governance, monitoring, administration, and managing complex cross-functional agentic workflows. About you Demonstrates curiosity about AI tools and emerging technologies, with a willingness to learn and leverage them to enhance productivity, collaboration, or decision-making. Senior IC product management experience in enterprise SaaS, with a proven track record of shipping complex products end-to-end. Hands-on experience building or shipping an AI-first product, with working fluency in model deployment and hosting, inference hosting choices, evals and quality measurement, fine-tuning, and data privacy trade-offs. A platform instinct to identify capabilities built once that serve several products over point solutions. Comfortable navigating ambiguity, greenfield problems, and leading open-ended outcomes. Strong prioritization and sequencing instincts to evaluate multiple directions and decisively invest in high-impact initiatives. Proven track record of working directly with enterprise customers to unblock deals, drive revenue, and influence cross-functional teams. Thrives in a fast-paced environment partnering shoulder-to-shoulder with engineering and executing hands-on discovery and prototyping. At Asana, we're committed to building teams that include a variety of backgrounds, perspectives, and skills, as this is critical to helping us achieve our mission.
