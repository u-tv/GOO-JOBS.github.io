Senior Communications Lead, Product and Consumer Communications Pinterest is looking for a Senior Communications Lead to join our Product and Consumer Communications team. In this role, you’ll help shape how people understand and experience Pinterest through compelling consumer narratives, and build Pinterest’s reputation as a visual shopping platform in tech circles. You’ll lead communications workstreams for cross-functional campaigns, trends, product launches and cultural moments, partnering across the company to bring Pinterest’s value to life for consumers, and cement its leadership for ML and AI advances. This is an opportunity to build trusted media relationships, specifically with emerging new channel publications, and deliver high-impact storytelling to help more people see Pinterest as a place to find inspiration through visual search and take action. This role reports directly to the Senior Director of Product, Consumer, and Creator Communications. What you’ll do Develop and execute communications plans, connecting press, social, digital, storytelling and cultural opportunities to Pinterest’s product and consumer priorities. Lead product launch and consumer campaign communications workstreams independently, with full ownership over outcomes. Build and maintain trusted relationships with reporters, editors, creators and other influential voices; identify timely opportunities to tell stories that highlight Pinterest. Shape proactive and reactive pitching strategies, and draft compelling pitches, newsroom posts, social copy, executive materials and other consumer-facing content. Advise cross-functional partners on communications strategy and messaging, working closely with teams and executives across Consumer, Product, Marketing, Research, Creator and Communications. Lead cross-functional projects from planning through delivery, align stakeholders, manage changing timelines and escalate sensitive issues appropriately. Use audience insights, platform trends and media performance to inform storytelling and evaluate communications impact. Manage crisis communications, bringing sound judgment and care to fast-moving situations. What we’re looking for Bachelor’s degree in a relevant field, such as communications, public relations or journalism, or equivalent practical experience. Minimum of 10 years of experience in communications, public relations or media relations, either in-house or at an agency; experience in technology, consumer brands or culture-focused communications is a plus. A proven track record of developing product and consumer communications strategies and delivering stories, campaigns or launches that earn attention and resonate with audiences. Established media relationships and experience pitching reporters, shaping coverage opportunities and working with creators or other influential voices. Excellent writing and editing skills, with the ability to adapt voice and message across press, social, digital and executive-facing formats across a variety of audiences. Experience leading complex, cross-functional communications projects with minimal supervision and making thoughtful decisions in a fast-moving environment. Strong judgment, discretion and composure when handling sensitive issues, ambiguity or competing priorities. A collaborative approach and the ability to build trust with partners across functions and levels.
