Senior Infrastructure Software Engineer, Build & Test Toolchains

🏢 dropbox 📍 Remote - Poland 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Role Description As a Senior Infrastructure Software Engineer on Dropbox’s CI Automation team, you will build the platform that helps engineers and coding agents turn code changes into trustworthy software. Our CI systems run hundreds of thousands of builds and hundreds of millions of tests each day across Dropbox products and services. You will own a core part of the platform from strategy and design through implementation, rollout, measurement, and operation. The work spans distributed scheduling and execution, caching, test intelligence, workload isolation, observability, reliability, and cost optimization. You will collaborate with teams across Dropbox to make feedback faster and more actionable while treating quality, security, provenance, and safety as core design requirements. This role is well suited to an engineer who enjoys deep systems work and close partnership with developer customers. Your work will directly influence how quickly and confidently Dropbox can build, validate, and ship software in an agentic SDLC. Join us in redefining the future, where your passion for building scalable, reliable systems will drive meaningful change on a global scale. Our Engineering Career Framework is viewable by anyone outside the company and describes what’s expected for our engineers at each of our career levels. Check out our blog post on this topic and more here . Responsibilities Design, build, and operate distributed CI infrastructure supporting large-scale build and test workloads. Own a core platform capability from an ambiguous developer need through measurable production impact. Improve feedback latency, reliability, debuggability, and infrastructure efficiency across developer and agent-driven workflows. Make data-informed architecture and prioritization decisions that balance performance, scalability, security, quality, and cost. Drive technical alignment and adoption across developer productivity, product, platform, and infrastructure teams. Raise the engineering bar through design reviews, operational learning, documentation, and mentorship. On-call work may be necessary occasionally to help address bugs, outages, or other operational issues, with the goal of maintaining a stable and high-quality experience for our customers. Requirements BS, MS, or PhD in Computer Science or related technical field involving coding (e.g., physics or mathematics), or equivalent technical experience. 9+ years of professional software development experience building and operating production backend or infrastructure systems. Proven track record constructing, managing, and owning complex, large-scale distributed backend systems with cross-team dependencies and measurable outcomes. Proficient in programming and debugging across a range of languages such as Python, Go, C/C++, Java, or Rust. Strong understanding of distributed systems concepts, including concurrency, failure handling, availability, performance, and safe rollouts. Extensive experience working with operating system internals, filesystems, databases, networks, and compilers considered advantageous. Experience leveraging metrics and production signals to improve system reliability, latency, throughput, and cost. Ability to navigate and thrive in ambiguous situations, create clarity, communicate technical decisions, and influence across teams. Ability to set medium-to-long-term strategy for business-impacting projects, take ownership of long-term projects and see them through to completion. Preferred Qualifications Experience with CI/CD, build systems, test infrastructure, developer platforms, or internal tooling at scale. Experience with Bazel, remote execution, caching, build graphs, monorepos, or test selection. Experience with multi-tenant compute, workflow orchestration, scheduling, isolation, or capacity management. Experience designing safety, provenance, or policy mechanisms for automated software workflows. Experience measuring and improving developer feedback loops, platform adoption, or cost per validated change.
