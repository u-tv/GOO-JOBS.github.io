Manager - Revenue Assurance Accounting

🏢 paytm 📍 Noida, Uttar Pradesh 💼 Full-time Employment 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Paytm Paytm is India's leading mobile payments and financial services distribution company. Pioneer of the mobile QR payments revolution in India, Paytm builds technologies that help small businesses with payments and commerce.Paytm's mission is to serve half a billion Indians and bring them to the mainstream economy with the help of technology. Senior Manager – Revenue Assurance & Accounting Experience: 7–10 years Location: Noida About the Role As a Senior Manager in Revenue Assurance & Accounting, you will own end-to-end revenue accounting, intercompany reporting, audit readiness, and financial controls across core business verticals and subsidiaries. In a high-volume, multi-entity fintech ecosystem, precision and speed in revenue recognition are critical to financial integrity and regulatory compliance. You will partner closely with business, product, tax, and audit teams to ensure accurate monthly closings, seamless onboarding of new revenue streams, and continuous automation of financial workflows. Key Responsibilities Lead Monthly Revenue Closing & Reconciliations: Drive timely monthly revenue closings across core operations and subsidiaries, managing unbilled revenue accounting, SAP vs. MIS reconciliations, debtor reviews, and resolution of complex accounting queries. Manage Intercompany & Related Party Transactions (RPT): Oversee intercompany approvals for unbilled bookings and invoicing, prepare intercompany revenue-sharing workings per agreements, and report RPT transactions and limits for audit and HOD compliance certifications. Drive Statutory & Internal Audit Closure: Prepare, review, and deliver accurate audit schedules for One97 Communications Limited (OCL) and subsidiaries, partnering with statutory and internal auditors to resolve queries and close audit observations. Strengthen Revenue Assurance & Leakage Controls: Identify revenue leakage or uncharged scenarios by vetting operational workflows against commercial agreements, ensuring complete and accurate billing. Enable New Vertical Onboarding & Regulatory Compliance: Define accounting treatments and revenue workflows for newly launched business verticals, manage merchant TDS payout accounting, and draft responses to AD banks for legacy FEMA and RBI queries. Champion Process Automation & Governance: Continuously upgrade Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and lead the automation of revenue assurance and reconciliation processes to scale with transaction growth. Required Qualifications Qualification: Qualified Chartered Accountant (CA) or equivalent master's degree in Finance/Accounting. Domain Expertise: Proven experience in Revenue Accounting, Revenue Assurance, Month-End Closing, and Statutory/Internal Audit management within high-volume transaction environments. Technical & ERP Proficiency: Hands-on expertise in SAP (FICO/SD modules), advanced Excel/data reconciliation tools, and handling large-scale MIS datasets. Regulatory & Accounting Knowledge: Strong command of Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS 115), Related Party Transaction (RPT) compliance, TDS regulations, and FEMA/RBI reporting frameworks. Stakeholder Management: Ability to collaborate effectively with cross-functional business teams, internal auditors, statutory auditors, and senior finance leadership. Preferred Qualifications Experience in the fintech, digital payments, e-commerce, or high-growth consumer internet sectors. Prior exposure to multi-entity subsidiary accounting and intercompany billing structures. Track record of implementing financial process automation or ERP system enhancements. AI Expectations Paytm is an AI-first organization, and we look for finance leaders who leverage modern tools to elevate accuracy and speed: Utilize AI-assisted tools for large-scale data reconciliation, anomaly detection in MIS vs. ERP datasets, and variance analysis. Leverage AI capabilities to automate repetitive reporting workflows, draft SOPs, and synthesize regulatory or audit documentation. Continuously explore AI-driven financial analytics to improve close timelines and decision support. Ideal Candidate High Ownership & Precision: Takes end-to-end accountability for numbers, controls, and audit outcomes with zero compromise on accuracy. Bias for Action & Speed: Thrives in a fast-paced environment, driving quick resolution of reconciliation bottlenecks and audit queries. Learning Agility & Adaptability: Comfortable navigating evolving business models, new product launches, and complex regulatory requirements. Career Stability & Progression: We value candidates who demonstrate consistent career progression, sustained ownership, and meaningful impact across their career.
