Job Description

FEQ327R144 As a Partner Solutions Architect, you will focus on cultivating technical relationships with one of our largest Global C&SI partners. You will strengthen our relationships with key data and application leaders to accelerate indirect revenue, enable their data and application development practices, and execute on long-term GTM strategy. The impact you will have: Strategic Technical Partnership: Be a technical partner and thought leader to the Databricks GTM partnerships team, providing technical expertise to better understand the partner landscape, driving key strategic programs, and identifying opportunities to deepen partner technical capabilities. You will embed with the partner technical teams to enable their practices, support troubleshooting, evangelize Databricks in their developer communities, and serve as an escalation point for complex technical issues. Joint Solution Development: Collaborate with partners to identify high-value industry-specific GenAI applications, develop joint solutions, and codify reference architectures / best practices to accelerate time to deployment. Customer Deal Support: Intervene directly to unblock strategic customer deals where the partner is the primary delivery vehicle, providing deep technical expertise and solution architecture guidance. Partner Ecosystem & Events: Represent Databricks at partner events (PKO, DAIS, EBC, etc), customer workshops, AWS/Azure summits, and industry conferences. Lead or support partner-specific developer events, hackathons, and technical enablement sessions, especially for technically native communities. Product Feedback: Validate and gather feedback on Databricks products and offerings, especially as they relate to partner use cases and deployment patterns, and deliver this feedback to relevant product teams to inform product roadmap and partner strategy. What we look for: 8+ years of experience in technical customer- and partner-facing roles, including Solutions Architect, Sales Engineer, Partner Sales Engineer, and Technical Account Manager 5+ years of experience designing and architecting distributed data systems Programming experience in Python, SQL, or Scala Track record of partnering with Global C&SIs and/or cloud providers to solve complex technical challenges, from initial solution design through customer delivery Exceptional ability to build relationships and communicate technical concepts to diverse stakeholders, including C-suite executives, engineering and IT teams, and more Strong presentation and technical communication skills with technical and business stakeholders Familiarity with common Agentic frameworks, application development, or a background in machine learning or data science Excitement to engage in cross-organizational collaboration, work through trade-offs, and balance competing priorities A love of teaching, mentoring, and helping others succeed Passion for thinking creatively about how to use technology in a way that is safe and beneficial, ultimately advancing AI systems Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, or equivalent experience through work experience Can travel up to 30% when needed Pay Range Transparency Databricks is committed to fair and equitable compensation practices.
