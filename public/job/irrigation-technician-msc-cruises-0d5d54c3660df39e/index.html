Irrigation Technician

🏢 MSC Cruises 📍 Rum Cay, 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Where passion meets opportunity The best of your adventures is the one you have yet to sail! Your Purpose Responsible for the operation and maintenance of all irrigation and drainage systems on the island and grounds. This role is based on MSC's island reserve, Ocean Cay. Staff accommodation provided. Your Impact Watering lawns, palms and flower beds and other areas as needed or scheduled. Monitors daily water usage and informs Maintenance Engineer of any needed adjustments to the irrigation water usage and computer settings. Operates irrigation heads in dry areas needing additional water. Designs, implements and maintains mechanical and electrical components of island irrigation system. Repairs equipment associated with irrigation (i.e., irrigation pipes and irrigation heads valves etc.). Performs preventive maintenance on all equipment associated with irrigation. Maintains complete records on all equipment maintenance associated with irrigation. Controls expenses associated with irrigation equipment and activities. Maintains inventory and ordering of parts needed for irrigation equipment. Performs all record-keeping relative to irrigation system including weather and rainfall data; assists in recording fertilizer and pesticide applications. Performs daily tank and pump water usage reports. Helps apply or water-in pesticides and fertilizers under the supervision of the Pesticides Application Specialist through fumigation. Operates dump trucks and other light equipment in hauling materials and removing debris. May occasionally supervise crews for special projects. Performs other appropriate tasks assigned by the Supervisor. Your Journey so far Fluent English, verbal and written. Knowledge of any other core language is an advantage. High School diploma and GED required. Some experience working for a landscaping service or golf course grounds crew, specifically working with the irrigation system. Proven ability to work within a harmonious and diverse culture team environment. Working knowledge of basic electricity and hydraulics related to an irrigation system, including automatic valves and controllers and various types of pumps and pumping systems. Knowledge of Rainbird system preferred. Knowledge of principles and practices of turf grass management and general horticulture. Knowledge of pump station maintenance, operation, and repair. Knowledge of tools, methods and materials used in grounds maintenance work. Ability to operate irrigation pumps, sprayers and drip irrigation systems. Knowledge of and ability to perform required role in emergency situations.
