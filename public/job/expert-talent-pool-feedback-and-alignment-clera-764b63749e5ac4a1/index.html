About the Role Join a remote expert network as a senior rater and reviewer, evaluating complex AI model outputs and helping calibrate other graders. Your work supports the development of high-quality, trustworthy AI systems across specialized fields. What You'll Do Rank and rate expert-level model answers using detailed rubrics and write clear rationales. Evaluate steps in model reasoning across fields such as mathematics, coding, law, medicine, and finance. Fact-check complex claims against primary sources and verify accuracy. Assess answers against content policies, style guides, and edge cases. Review other raters' work, calibrate standards, and develop or refine grading guidelines. Assess search relevance and provide feedback in live sessions. What We're Looking For At least 5 years of professional experience in rating, review, editing, fact-checking, trust and safety, or a relevant professional domain. Native or near-native written English proficiency. Experience grading or reviewing expert-level answers against rubrics, policies, or style guides. Experience leading or reviewing other raters, such as as a QA lead, rubric owner, or reviewer of reviewers. An advanced degree, professional license, or deep working expertise in the domain you grade. Hands-on AI data annotation experience, search-quality rating, or experience writing and maintaining grader guidelines is a plus. Compensation & Benefits Compensation is $50 to $120 per hour, depending on the project, field, and expertise level. Payment is based on hours worked. Location Fully remote. Projects typically run from one week to several months.
