Staff Product Designer — AX & Growth (DLG) About the Role Auth0, part of Okta, is redefining identity for a world where users aren't only human. AI agents are beginning to sign up, authenticate, and act autonomously — and Auth0 is building the infrastructure and experience layer for that shift. This role is a foundational design hire on Auth0's India Design Team, part of the global design team. Working at the frontier of Agent Experience (AX) : designing for a largely headless, invisible-UI world where the &quot;user&quot; is often code acting with intent, not a person. You'll join a small pod of PMs and engineers actively defining Auth0's agentic vision from scratch — there's no existing pattern library, so you'll help create it: how agents sign up, authenticate, execute tasks, and how humans stay in control without micromanaging them. Alongside this, you'll own signup and onboarding design for a new customer: the AI-powered developer, whose workflows involve agents and automated tooling from day one. The same instincts that build trust for a human overseeing an agent also build trust for a developer signing up for a self-serve product. We're looking for a self-starter who generates their own hypotheses and builds the roadmap as they go. What You'll Do Agent Experience (AX) - Shape Auth0's agentic vision alongside a small, fast-moving team of PMs and engineers. - Define the &quot;what&quot; for agents — interaction models for signup, execution, and backend operations, largely invisible to a screen. - Define the &quot;how&quot; for humans — trust, transparency, and control (errors, edge cases, audit trails) for orchestrating agents. - Identify the problems worth solving yourself; establish patterns and protocols others adopt. - Own the full lifecycle: definition, prototyping, validation, iteration. Growth & Onboarding (DLG) - Define signup and onboarding for the AI-powered developer — rethinking self-service as workflows become increasingly automated. - Own subscription, pricing & packaging strategy end to end; shape the Growth roadmap with Product and Engineering. - Define how design impact is measured (e.g., via Pendo, Heap); lead research and turn insight into action. - Simplify high-leverage developer workflows into elegant, self-service experiences. Craft & Influence - Be a thought partner to the design manager; raise the craft bar across the Global Design Team. - Mentor designers; translate AI/technical concepts into practical guidance for PMs and engineers. - Represent design in roadmap discussions for both AX and Growth. Who You Are - 10+ years of human-centered product design experience with a portfolio showing strategic ownership and strong craft. - A true self-starter — you've built experiences or practices from scratch, without a roadmap handed to you. - Comfortable designing for headless, low-UI, API-first surfaces. - Experience designing for AI agents or agentic systems is a huge plus — you've tackled invisible-UI, trust, or automation problems before, not just discussed them. - Track record owning onboarding/self-service experiences at scale, backed by PLG and conversion metrics fluency (Pendo, Heap, or similar). - Comfortable in ambiguity, technical conversations, and fast-moving, undefined team settings. - A multiplier — you elevate others and build alignment without formal authority. - Bonus: engineering/front-end background. Why Join Us - Help write the vision, not just implement it — a founding voice on AX and Growth - Shape two frontiers at once: invisible AX for agents, and growth for AI-powered developers. - Operate at staff scope, with real influence over strategy and craft. - We believe in augmentation, not replacement — and in design as problem-solving, not artifacts. - Be a founder of a new discipline, architecting how growth and agentic systems work together.
