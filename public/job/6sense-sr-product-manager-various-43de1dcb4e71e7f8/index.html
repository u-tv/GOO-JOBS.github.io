Senior Product Manager, Audience & Discovery The Role As the Senior Product Manager, Audience & Discovery, you will own the strategy, roadmap, and execution for the capabilities that allow marketing and revenue teams to build and manage audiences, explore account and contact details, and take action across Activation channels. These capabilities support some of the core functionality of the 6sense Agentic GTM platform. This role covers the Audience & Discovery product area on the Activation team. You will build intuitive, scalable experiences for segment and list management, account and contact exploration, and handoff to Activation channels, in close partnership with Engineering, Design, Data Science, Customer Success, and go-to-market teams. This is an individual contributor role reporting to the Sr. Director - Product Management. Key Responsibilities Define the product vision, strategy, roadmap, and success metrics for Audience & Discovery product lines. Build workflows that help users create, organize, and act on audience segments and lists at scale; Design experiences that let users search, filter, and explore accounts and contacts, and understand the details and context behind each one. Enable action from every audience and record, with seamless, reliable handoff to Activation channels such as Advertising, Intelligent Workflows, AI Email, Sales Intelligence and Agents at scale. Define how agents, RevvyAI and APIs can create, retrieve, update, and act on audiences and account and contact data across the Agentic GTM platform. Translate customer needs into clear requirements, user journeys, acceptance criteria, and measurable outcomes. Partner cross-functionally to launch high-quality features, make tradeoffs using data and customer feedback, and improve usability, reliability, and scale. Define and track success metrics for adoption, audience creation and reuse, discovery engagement, activation rate, and business impact. Establish feedback loops with customers and internal teams to continuously improve audience quality and discovery effectiveness. Partner with internal teams to monitor product health, identify friction points, and improve performance, reliability, and scalability over time. Performance Metrics Adoption and active usage of audience management and discovery workflows. Growth in audience creation, reuse, and activation across channels. Discovery engagement, including time to find and act on the right accounts and contacts. Reliability and performance of audience segments, lists, and export experiences at scale. Customer impact, including satisfaction, retention, pipeline influence, and product differentiation. Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Data Science, or a related field; MBA or advanced degree preferred. 6–8 years in product management, with experience in B2B SaaS, AI products, or sales and marketing technology. Strong technical fluency across data products, APIs, integrations, analytics, and AI-enabled and agentic experiences. Experience partnering with Engineering, Design, Data Science, Customer Success, Sales, and Product Marketing to deliver customer-facing products. Strong customer orientation, analytical judgment, and communication skills for technical and non-technical audiences. Key Competencies Strategic, customer-centered product thinker with strong ownership and judgment. Data-driven decision maker who can connect product usage, customer value, and business outcomes. Systems thinker who can operate in ambiguity and simplify complex customer workflows. Strong collaborator who can align cross-functional teams and drive execution from discovery to launch.
