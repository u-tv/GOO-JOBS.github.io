About Databricks: At Databricks, we are passionate about enabling data teams to solve the world's toughest problems, from making the next mode of transportation a reality to accelerating the development of medical breakthroughs. We do this by building and running the world's best data and AI infrastructure platform so our customers can use deep data insights to improve their business. Founded by engineers and deeply customer-obsessed, we leap at every opportunity to solve hard technical challenges, from designing modern application interfaces to architecting highly available, distributed systems at massive scale. And we're only getting started. The Role: As a Senior Staff Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE), you will be the principal technical authority working with our largest and most complex federal government customers to solve their most critical software, data, and AI challenges using the Databricks platform. You will define the technical vision, pioneer novel system architectures, and lead the implementation of enterprise-scale applications spanning backend infrastructure, API integration, and AI-powered product development. At the Senior Staff level, you are a force multiplier. We work cross-functionally to shape long-term strategic priorities and initiatives alongside engineering leadership, product management, and developer relations. You will act as a trusted advisor to technical executives at customer organizations, integrating deeply with client codebases and systems while mentoring other FDEs and engineering teams. This is a hands-on, customer-facing role for elite builders and technical strategists who thrive at the intersection of software engineering, distributed systems, and massive business impact. Please note: this role is hybrid and has a requirement to travel to customer locations 20-25% of the time. The impact you will have: Strategic Solution Leadership: Lead the most complex and high-stakes customer technical projects. Pioneer and build robust, first-of-their-kind reference architectures, massive-scale custom enterprise applications, and seamless integrations with cutting-edge AI/ML models. Enterprise Transformation: Drive executive-level technical strategy as strategic customers implement transformational software projects. Oversee the end-to-end design, build, and deployment of industry-leading, data-intensive AI applications across highly matrixed federal organizations. Visionary Guidance: Act as a trusted technical advisor to C-suite and VP-level customer stakeholders. Guide technical leadership on software architecture, system design, and platform adoption, successfully steering their long-term Databricks development strategy. Architectural Pioneer: Own and define software architecture decisions for systems with unprecedented scale or security requirements. Ensure applications are secure, highly available, scalable, and set the standard for modern software engineering and Databricks best practices globally. Deliver Technical Excellence: Orchestrate multi-team, multi-quarter technical engagements. Align Databricks technical teams, Architects, and Customer engineering leadership to ensure flawless delivery of mission-critical software components. Product Engineering Influence: Partner heavily with Databricks Core Engineering and Product leadership. Translate complex, edge-case developer challenges from the field into actionable product roadmap initiatives, APIs, and platform enhancements. Customer Immersion & Navigation: Embed with customer engineering teams, navigating highly complex organizational structures to deeply understand entrenched software challenges and deliver code-level, systemic impact. Ecosystem Multiplication: Act as a technical multiplier. Pioneer and open-source massive-scale code accelerators, global development frameworks, and engineering best practices that scale impact across all federal accounts and elevate the entire FDE organization. What We're Looking For: Minimum Qualifications: 10+ years of experience in software engineering, backend development, or distributed systems engineering, with a proven track record of architectural leadership. Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Information Systems, Applied Science, Mathematics, Engineering, or equivalent relevant years of experience in an Engineering function. Expert-level proficiency writing scalable production code in Python, Scala, Java, Go, or JavaScript/TypeScript, and architecting solutions using modern application frameworks. Deep, authoritative multi-cloud architectural expertise (AWS, Azure, GCP) with the ability to design cross-cloud or hybrid distributed systems. Advanced experience with distributed computing with Apache Spark™ and deep internal knowledge of the Spark runtime engine, optimization, and scaling. Mastery of CI/CD pipelines, containerization (Docker, Kubernetes), and infrastructure as code (Terraform) for complex, high-security production deployments. Expert knowledge of MLOps, deploying custom ML/AI models, and integrating LLMs and AI APIs into massive-scale production environments. Proven ability to design and deploy performant, end-to-end software architectures that combine backend services, ML/AI models, and user-facing interfaces with zero-downtime requirements. Extensive experience steering complex technical project delivery—managing massive scope, driving agile engineering teams, and translating ambiguous business requirements into actionable, enterprise-grade software solutions. Exceptional technical documentation, system white-boarding, and presentation skills, with the ability to explain complex distributed systems to both elite engineers and non-technical executives. Deep experience working with massive enterprise or federal clients, managing conflicts, and driving consensus across a highly diverse and senior stakeholder range. Demonstrated ability to mentor senior engineers, continuously explore new development tools, and push the boundaries of what is possible with Databricks-based solutions. Travel to customers 20-25% of the time. Preferred Qualifications: 12+ years of experience in software engineering, backend development, and building mission-critical, enterprise-level distributed systems. Recognized industry thought leadership (e.g., published papers, tech blog posts, open-source maintainer status, or conference speaking experience).
