Headquarters: Remote (US) At Current At Current, we partner with ambitious brands to create digital experiences that drive meaningful business results. activities Improve proposal templates, business development workflows, CRM processes, and operational documentation What You'll Bring 3–5 years of experience as a Producer, Project Manager, Account Manager, Business Development Coordinator, or similar role within a digital agency or consulting environment Experience coordinating proposals, pitches, RFP responses, or client-facing deliverables Exceptional project management and organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities and deadlines Strong written and verbal communication skills with confidence working across
