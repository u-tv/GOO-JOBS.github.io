About this role: We are seeking a collaborative and intellectually curious Sales Operations Analyst to join Coursera’s Revenue Operations team. You enjoy exploring data, solving business problems, and developing practical ideas that help teams work more effectively. Working closely with regional Sales Operations Managers and enterprise sales teams, you will help turn insights into action and improve the processes and tools that support revenue growth. This role offers broad exposure to sales strategy, analytics, and operations across regions. You will work alongside experienced Revenue Operations professionals and sales leaders, learn how a global SaaS business operates, and build your analytical, technical, and business skills through meaningful projects. It is an opportunity to take initiative, contribute fresh ideas, and help shape how the team uses data, AI, and automation to scale. About your skills: Sales Operations: Core discipline for managing and optimizing sales processes, supporting teams, and aligning strategies with business objectives in this regional role. Business Analytics: Essential for analyzing pipeline, forecasting, and performance data to develop insights and drive business planning decisions. Data Integration: Important for combining data from Salesforce, Databricks, and other sources to create unified reporting and analysis for leadership. What you’ll be doing: Partner with regional Sales Operations Managers to understand business challenges and develop analyses and solutions that support revenue growth and operational goals. Build and enhance Salesforce reports and dashboards that give sales teams and business leaders clear visibility into performance and the metrics that matter. Develop analytical models and conduct ad-hoc analysis to uncover trends across pipeline, bookings, and sales productivity, translating findings into actionable recommendations. Improve the quality and consistency of CRM data and reporting, helping establish trusted information and common metrics across regions. Contribute to territory and account management initiatives that strengthen sales coverage and support the execution of go-to-market strategy. Work with sales leaders, representatives, and cross-functional teams to solve operational challenges, remove bottlenecks, and improve sales productivity. Support forecasting, planning, and business reviews through analysis of regional and segment performance, highlighting opportunities to improve business results. Help design and improve scalable processes, tools, and reporting approaches, using AI and automation to make sales operations more effective. Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in business, finance, economics, analytics, information systems, or a related field. 2+ years of relevant experience in Sales Operations, Revenue Operations, business analytics, or a related operational or analytical role. Hands-on Salesforce experience, including building and maintaining reports and dashboards, managing CRM data, and supporting sales users. Advanced proficiency in Excel or Google Sheets, with experience organizing complex datasets and building models to support business analysis. Strong analytical and creative problem-solving skills, with the ability to break down complex questions and develop practical, data-driven recommendations. A proactive approach to learning and taking ownership, with strong attention to detail and a commitment to delivering well-vetted analysis. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to simplify complex findings and present them in a way that encourages action. Ability to prioritize competing needs, meet deadlines, and collaborate effectively in a dynamic environment across regions and time zones.
