The Sales Planning team sits at the center of InMobi's Brand Business, translating product capabilities, market insights, customer needs, and data into narratives that help our sales teams win. As the Manager - Sales Planning, you will lead the strategic presales and GTM function for the Brand Business. This is a highly strategic, B2B-focused role that combines Product Marketing, sales planning, client solutioning, and sales enablement. You will own the development of differentiated GTM narratives, proactive market-facing collateral, and scalable frameworks that strengthen seller effectiveness and accelerate revenue growth. You will partner closely with Product Marketing, Product, Sales Leadership, BI, Trading, Supply, Deal Desk, and centralized Marketing teams across North America, APAC, and EMEA. You will also remain close to execution - setting the quality bar for RFPs, client proposals, narrative decks, and strategic customer opportunities while building repeatable processes for the broader team. What You'll Be Doing GTM Strategy and Product Marketing Own the end-to-end GTM narrative and sales planning strategy for the Brand Business across North America, APAC, and EMEA. Develop differentiated positioning, messaging, value propositions, audience narratives, and customer-centric solution frameworks. Partner closely with Product Marketing and Product teams to translate platform capabilities and launches into clear market-facing stories and sales readiness programs. Gather and synthesize customer feedback, competitive intelligence, market trends, and advertiser needs to identify market gaps and evolving opportunities. Create and maintain messaging frameworks, persona-based narratives, use-case playbooks, and proof points that support the full sales cycle. Champion the Brand Business narrative internally and externally, ensuring consistency across regions, channels, and customer touchpoints. Sales Planning and Strategic Solutioning Lead the development of complex RFP responses, proposals, media strategies, and executive presentations for strategic opportunities. Translate advertiser objectives and campaign briefs into differentiated media recommendations and sophisticated client solutioning frameworks. Develop proactive GTM materials including vertical narratives, industry playbooks, tentpole collateral, one-pagers, case studies, competitive battlecards, and solution briefs. Connect insights from BI, Trading, Supply, Product, and Deal Desk into a unified and persuasive customer story. Design visually compelling, story-driven presentations for customers, agencies, Sales teams, and leadership audiences. Establish best practices and quality standards for all customer-facing deliverables across the Sales Planning team. Sales Enablement Build scalable sales enablement programs that improve seller productivity, solution confidence, and win rates. Equip Sales teams with best-in-class market insights, competitive intelligence, messaging frameworks, customer stories, and objection-handling tools. Partner with regional Sales leaders to identify enablement gaps and proactively develop solutions aligned to revenue priorities. Work with centralized Marketing execution teams to bring GTM strategies to life through vertical narratives, tentpole collateral, case studies, demand and supply collateral, and industry events. AI Transformation Lead the adoption of AI across the Sales Planning function and define an AI-first operating model for the team. Build scalable AI workflows that automate research, proposal generation, content development, presentation creation, and knowledge reuse. Develop prompt libraries, AI playbooks, and reusable frameworks that improve quality, consistency, and productivity. Evaluate and deploy tools such as Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT, Gamma, Canva AI, and other emerging platforms. Coach the broader Sales Planning and Sales organization on prompt engineering, responsible AI use, and high-impact business applications. Cross-Functional Leadership and Team Development Act as the central hub connecting Sales, Product Marketing, Product, BI, Trading, Supply, Deal Desk, and centralized Marketing teams. Influence product positioning, roadmap conversations, and GTM priorities by bringing customer feedback and market intelligence into cross-functional discussions. Lead strategic initiatives from concept through execution, ensuring high-quality and timely delivery with measurable business outcomes. Mentor and coach Sales Planning professionals in storytelling, PMM fundamentals, client solutioning, AI adoption, and executive communication. Establish scalable processes, templates, operating rhythms, and measurement frameworks across the function. Balance long-term strategic planning with hands-on involvement in high-priority customer opportunities.
