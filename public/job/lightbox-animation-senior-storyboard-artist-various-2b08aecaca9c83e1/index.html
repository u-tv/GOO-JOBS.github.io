We are looking for a talented ​ Senior Storyboard Artist with enthusiasm to join our great team on the exciting new project, Animated Feature TAD 4!! ​You will be responsible for drawing a scene-by-scene visual story based on script. First of all, you're probably interested in knowing that...we offer 100% remote work! WE NEED YOU TO BE ABLE TO DO: Illustrate the narrative, plan shots, and draw panels to demonstrate action and maintain continuity between scenes. Have a great sense of visual storytelling. Meet with creative supervisors to discuss objectives of storyboard; what is desired or to be achieved, receiving written and verbal description of a sequence. Create quick thumbnail sketches through to finished detailed storyboard panels. Create storyboards by implementing storytelling objectives. Illustrate script pages and come up with story ideas. Pitch Storyboards when required. Work with Director and Supervisor to define staging and camera action. Develop character personality and dramatic/comedic action. Ensure quality and style of show is consistently achieved in storyboard work. Meet all deadlines as determined by the Production Team and Supervisor. Think about what the writer is actually trying to say and how best to say it, every choice you make has an impact! Replace words with visuals coherently and clearly, thinking about editing or animation limitations. Ability to work autonomously and independently in a fully remote environment, with that comes the supreme individual responsibility to prioritize and manage multiple tasks and maintain your own initiative. Excellent communications skills. Great team player, create a collaborative and welcoming environment. Proven ability to meet deadlines, prioritize workload and within budgets, adhere to the production schedule and deliver completed assignments on time. Align with the style, sensibility of the project and the Director vision. WE WILL MAKE THE PERFECT MATCH IF YOU HAVE: 5+ years of professional experience from either animation TV series or feature animation movie. Practical knowledge of cinematic language, acting, staging and composition. Ability to align with style and sensibility of feature, able to express story and character emotions through drawings. Strong storytelling abilities to feature character, emotion and movement. Ability to invent action and express it through drawing. Strong knowledge in narrative and filmmaking. Knowledge of Blender is a plus. Ability to take feedback constructively and implement it effectively, to reach the quality desired. Anticipation and quick reaction for problem solving. Demonstrate great autonomy and proactivity in your work. Great attention to detail. Outstanding interpersonal and collaborative skills. Do you think we fit? Well, keep reading.
