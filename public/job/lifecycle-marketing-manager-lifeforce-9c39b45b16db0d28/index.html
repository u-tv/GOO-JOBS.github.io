Lifecycle Marketing Manager

🏢 Lifeforce 📍 US Only 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Employment type: Full-time Experience: 4+ years Reports to: Chris Stella, VP Growth About Lifeforce We are in the early stages of a generational shift in how people approach their health. No longer satisfied with the reactive, fragmented care offered by the traditional healthcare system, millions of people are taking a more proactive approach. They want to understand how their bodies work, take control of how they feel and perform, and invest in living longer, healthier lives. Lifeforce is leading this shift as the world's largest longevity medicine program. Founded alongside clinical and scientific leaders from Cleveland Clinic, Massachusetts General Hospital, and Harvard Medical School, Lifeforce brings together the diagnostics, expertise, and treatments people need to understand and optimize their health, both today and for decades to come. Our comprehensive program combines advanced diagnostics, expert clinicians, dedicated health coaches, and personalized supplement, pharmaceutical, and lifestyle protocols. All delivered from home, at the price of a gym membership. Overview Lifeforce is hiring a Lifecycle Marketing Manager to own every message a lead or member gets from us after the first click. You'll run email, SMS, push, and in-app messaging across the full journey: turning leads into members, getting new members through their first diagnostics and clinician visit, keeping members engaged with their protocols, and bringing back the ones who drift. This is a hands-on role. You will build the journeys, write and test the messages, set up the segments, and read the results yourself. You'll use AI tools to move faster on copy, personalization, and analysis, and you'll build a lifecycle program that scales as our membership, clinician network, and Ultimate Longevity Center (ULC) locations grow. You'll work closely with growth, product, clinical, coaching, and member experience teams. Paid acquisition brings people to the door. You make sure they walk in, stay, and get results. Key Responsibilities Lead Nurture & Conversion Build and run nurture journeys that turn leads from paid, organic, and referral channels into members. Work with the growth team to match messaging to the ads, landing pages, and offers that brought each lead in. Test offers, timing, and content to raise lead-to-member conversion. Onboarding & Activation Own the new-member journey from sign-up through first lab draw, results review, and first clinician and coach visits. Cut drop-off at each step by finding where members get stuck and fixing it with clear, timely messages. Partner with member experience and operations so messages match what members actually see and do. Retention, Engagement & Win-Back Build programs that keep members engaged with their protocols, follow-up diagnostics, and coaching. Drive adoption of supplements, prescriptions, and other offerings where they fit a member's plan. Run churn-prevention, win-back, and referral programs. Segmentation, Testing & Analytics Build segments and triggers from member behavior, lifecycle stage, and program data. Run a steady testing roadmap across subject lines, content, channels, timing, and offers. Own lifecycle reporting: conversion, activation, retention, churn, and revenue by journey. Use AI tools like Claude to draft and personalize content, analyze results, and speed up production. Platform & Compliance Own our lifecycle platform setup, data flows, templates, and deliverability. Work with clinical and compliance teams to keep every message accurate and compliant with HIPAA, TCPA, and CAN-SPAM. Document journeys and processes so the program can scale without adding headcount. Cross-Functional Partnership Partner with growth on full-funnel strategy and shared conversion goals. Work with product on in-app messaging, onboarding flows, and member data. Bring member insights back to marketing, product, and clinical teams. What Success Looks Like In your first 90 days: you've audited our current journeys and data, launched a rebuilt lead nurture and new-member onboarding journey, and set up clear reporting on conversion and activation by stage. In your first year: you're running a full lifecycle program from lead to long-term member, with a steady testing cadence, measurable gains in lead-to-member conversion, activation, and retention, and journeys that scale to new markets and ULC locations. Qualifications Required 4+ years of lifecycle, CRM, or retention marketing experience in a consumer subscription, DTC, or membership business. Hands-on experience building journeys in a platform like Braze, Customer.io, Iterable, or Klaviyo. Strong copywriting skills across email, SMS, and push. Comfort with data: segmentation, A/B testing, cohort analysis, and reporting. SQL is a plus. Regular use of AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT, or similar) to speed up content and analysis. Clear written and verbal communication. Self-directed and effective in a fully remote environment. Preferred Experience in digital health, telehealth, wellness, or healthcare. Working knowledge of HIPAA, TCPA, and CAN-SPAM requirements. Experience with Segment, Amplitude, Mixpanel, or a similar data stack. Experience at a startup or high-growth company. Experience building a lifecycle program from scratch. Traits for Success Passionate about health, wellness, longevity, or human performance. Writes clearly and cares about the member on the other end of every message. Tests everything and lets the data decide. Comfortable doing the detailed work, not just planning it. Raises problems early and comes with a proposed fix. Comfortable in a fast-moving, high-growth environment.
