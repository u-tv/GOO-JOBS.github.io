Client Growth Manger Team Overview: InMobi’s Client Services (CS) Org oversees the end-to-end relationship and business management for InMobi’s advertising clients across the globe. This function is structured regionally and focuses on nurturing and growth of client relationships in the specific region (APAC), serving their needs across InMobi’s different ad platforms (InMobi DSP, CTV and Glance). The team runs the region's overall revenue engine from the demand side while working closely with internal and external stakeholders. The team combines exceptional client servicing skills with an excellent understanding of the technological, operational, and commercial aspects of the mobile advertising ecosystem. It is focused on driving sales strategy, scaling existing accounts, growing InMobi’s market share in the regions, and developing our reputation of being an industry leader in mobile advertising. What will you be doing? InMobi is looking for an outstanding Lead - Client Growth Manager to join our APAC Client Services team in Bangalore. This role is a client facing one where the person will be the primary POC for driving any kind of client conversation associated with his/her set of key accounts. This person will own stakeholder management both internally (Sales, Product, Adops, Engg, Design, Supply, Legal, Finance, Marketing) and externally (Advertisers and Agencies) to drive cross-functional support to maximize media flow and net revenue, as well as strengthen the customer relationship with InMobi. We are looking for sharp, analytical, quick learners with strong communication skills, client facing experience and a positive attitude, who can solve complex problems in a structured manner, have experience of working in cross-functional roles and develop close strategic relationships with key clients in the mobile programmatic ecosystem, while proactively and clearly communicating data and insights to sales, supply, product teams and to global leadership.
