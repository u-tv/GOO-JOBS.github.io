Headquarters: Remote Eastern Canada | Remote Eastern United States BeyondTrust is a place where you can bring your purpose to life through the work that you do, creating a safer world through our cybersecurity SaaS portfolio. Our culture of flexibility, trust, and continual learning means you will be recognized for your growth, and for the impact you make on our success. You will be surrounded by people who challenge, support, and inspire you to be the best version of yourself. The Role BeyondTrust is looking for a Senior Director of Engineering to lead one of our core enterprise security product portfolios. You will own engineering for the portfolio end to end: technical direction, delivery, quality, and the people and leaders who make it happen. Our products protect privileged access for enterprises around the world and are deployed on-premises, as appliances, and as SaaS. We are looking for a leader who can serve a demanding enterprise customer base today while moving the portfolio forward on modern cloud architecture and AI-driven engineering. This is a senior, high-autonomy role. You will be expected to set direction, make and own significant technical and business decisions, and represent engineering with executives, product leadership, and customers.
