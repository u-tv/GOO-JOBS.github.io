GSI Specialty Seller, Identity Resilience (Global)

🏢 Rubrik Job Board 📍 US Only 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

This role will own the Identity Resilience go-to-market motion across Rubrik's strategic GSI and advisory partners in the Americas.Â You will turn GSI relationships into sourced pipeline, co-developed joint solutions, and closed revenue for identity protection and recovery across Active Directory ,Okta and Entra ID and make Rubrik part of each GSI's standard identity security, cyber recovery, and Zero Trust offerings. Key Responsibilities: Pipeline and Revenue Own a quarterly pipeline and bookings target for GSI-sourced and influenced Identity Resilience opportunities. Identify opportunities in GSI installed bases: AD and Entra ID modernization, M&A and divestiture identity work, ransomware recovery readiness, and cyber insurance and regulatory programs. Drive deals from first conversation to close alongside Rubrik AEs, SEs, and Regional Directors, using partner-sourced expansion (PSX) and new-logo (PSNL) plays. Attach Identity Resilience to Cloud Resilience and data protection deals to expand deal size and strategic relevance. Run a disciplined cadence: weekly forecast, deal reviews, and clean Salesforce hygiene (account categorization, partner attribution, deal registration). GSI Engagement and Enablement Build relationships across GSI identity and security practice leaders, cyber recovery and incident response teams, managed security services, and sellers. Create a joint account plan per priority GSI: target accounts, executive sponsors, solution focus, and quarterly milestones. Train and certify GSI sellers, architects, and delivery teams on Rubrik's identity value proposition, competitive positioning, and services attach. Lead recurring pipeline reviews, win/loss reviews, and joint account mapping sessions. Joint Solutions and Offerings Co-create packaged offerings that bundle Rubrik Identity Resilience with GSI identity assessment, recovery planning, managed detection and response, and incident response services. Work with Product, Product Marketing, and GSI practices on repeatable assets: solution briefs, reference architectures, recovery runbooks, pricing guidance, and SOWs. Align identity resilience with Rubrik's cyber recovery and Cloud Resilience motions, including clean-room and orchestrated recovery of AD ,Entra ID and Okta Help GSIs build managed-service and recurring-revenue models around Rubrik. Ecosystem and Go-to-Market Coordinate with Rubrik's Microsoft, security, and identity ecosystem partners on co-sell and field engagement, including Azure Marketplace and MACC-eligible purchasing where applicable. Stay current on identity attack techniques, AD and Entra ID architecture, and compliance drivers; position Rubrik against native Microsoft capabilities and specialist identity recovery vendors. Partner with Marketing and Partner Programs on joint campaigns, workshops (e.g., ransomware tabletop exercises), and executive briefings; support customer meetings and RFP/RFI responses. Publish win stories and references, and relay field and partner feedback to Product and Sales Leadership. Required Qualifications: 10+ years in enterprise software, cybersecurity, or identity and data protection sales, including 5+ years in a partner, alliance, or GSI-facing role. Proven record of sourcing and closing six- and seven-figure deals through GSIs or large advisory partners. Strong knowledge of Active Directory, Entra ID ,Okta and the identity threat landscape, including credential-based attacks and identity recovery after a breach. Established relationships, or proven ability to build them, with major GSIs (e.g., Accenture, Deloitte, HCLTech, Cognizant, Infosys, Wipro, Kyndryl, PwC). Experience building joint offerings and enabling partner sellers and delivery teams. Strong forecasting, deal management, and Salesforce discipline; excellent executive communication and negotiation skills. Travel of approximately 30-40%. Preferred Qualifications: Background selling identity security, cyber recovery, or data protection solutions. Experience selling to CISO, identity, and infrastructure leaders, and with the security services buying centers inside GSIs. Familiarity with MEDDPICC and partner-led deal registration; experience with Microsoft co-sell and Azure Marketplace. Relevant credentials or equivalent hands-on identity architecture credibility (e.g., Microsoft identity, CISSP). Success Metrics (First 12 Months): Pipeline coverage against target (e.g., 3-4x) and closed-won bookings from GSI-led Identity Resilience deals. Number of GSIs with an active joint identity plan and at least one packaged joint offering. Number of GSI sellers and architects trained and certified.
