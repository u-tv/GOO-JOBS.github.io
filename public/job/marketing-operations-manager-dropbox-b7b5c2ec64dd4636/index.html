Marketing Operations Manager

🏢 dropbox 📍 Remote - US: Select locations 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Role Description As the Marketing Operations Manager, you will serve as a deeply embedded, data-oriented partner to the Marketing team and help the business understand and driv e performance across the Marketing-to-Revenue funnel. You will analyze the funnel from marketing engagement through qualification, pipeline, and revenue. You’re focused on identifying growth opportunities, conversion gaps, leakage, handoff issues and translating those findings into clear recommendations for Marketing, SDR, Sales, and RevOps leaders. This is not a traditional reporting or martech administration role. Your primary value will be your ability to frame ambiguous business questions, diagnose the underlying drivers of performance, distinguish signal from noise, and help cross-functional teams make better operating and investment decisions. You will also be expected to use AI and automation extensively to reduce repetitive analytical work, accelerate insight generation, and spend more time on interpretation, problem solving, and decision support. Responsibilities Own analysis of the Marketing-to-Revenue funnel from engagement and qualification through pipeline progression and revenue. Identify opportunities, conversion gaps, funnel leakage, performance anomalies, and handoff issues across Marketing, SDR, and Sales. Connect marketing activity to downstream pipeline and revenue outcomes to help inform investment and prioritization decisions. Build clear analyses and reporting that surface meaningful trends, risks, opportunities, and recommended actions. Collaborate with cross-functional partners to i mprove lifecycle definitions, lead scoring, routing, SLAs, and measurement frameworks where funnel analysis identifies opportunities. Partner with Marketing SLG, RevOps, SDR, Sales, Analytics, and Systems teams to resolve cross-functional funnel and data-quality issues. Use AI and automation to accelerate query development, recurring reporting, anomaly detection, synthesis, and first-pass insight generation while remaining accountable for validation and final recommendations. Requirements Experience analyzing B2B marketing and sales funnels from marketing engagement through pipeline and revenue. Strong analytical judgment and demonstrated ability to diagnose ambiguous business or funnel-performance problems. Experience working with Salesforce data and familiarity with Marketo or another B2B marketing automation platform. Experience using BI or analytics tools to explore, measure, and communicate funnel performance. Working knowledge of SQL or sufficient fluency to independently inspect and analyze structured data. Strong written and verbal communication skills with demonstrated ability to influence cross-functional stakeholders without direct authority. Experience translating analysis into clear recommendations and business actions rather than stopping at reporting. Preferred Qualifications Experience working within Revenue Operations or in a highly integrated Marketing and RevOps environment. Experience partnering directly with Demand Generation, Marketing Operations, Data Science, SDR, and Sales teams. Experience improving lead scoring, lifecycle stages, routing, SLAs, attribution, or funnel measurement. Experience building reusable analytical frameworks or standardized operating metrics. Experience using AI tools to improve analytical workflows while maintaining strong validation and quality controls. Durable Skills AI fluency means using these tools to amplify human judgment, not replace it.
