Sumo Logic: Principal Product Marketing Manager

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote US Principal Product Marketing Manager — AI Systems & Content Architecture The Mission As a Principal Product Marketing Manager — AI Systems & Content Architecture, you will determine the architecture of our narrative and collateral engine. Rather than operating as an artisanal, bespoke content creator, your mandate is to establish our foundational positioning architecture and engineer high-leverage, AI-driven workflows that generate enterprise-grade sales collateral at scale. You will own the strategic narrative for Sumo Logic’s Log Analytics Platform across Observability and Security personas, establishing automated knowledge loops, scalable asset generation engines, and self-serve frameworks that allow a focused team to deliver the output and velocity typically associated with a much larger company. Strategic Transformation to AI-Led Architecture AI-Augmented Asset Production: Replace manual drafting with prompt frameworks and retrieval pipelines to rapidly generate high-impact technical collateral from raw inputs. Self-Serve Content Engines: Provide modular asset systems and AI repositories for field teams to dynamically generate persona-aligned collateral on demand. Tiered Distribution: Focus full-funnel GTM on high-impact Tier-1 shifts while empowering PMs with self-serve playbooks for Tier-2/3 releases. Strategic Systems Architect: Shift from tactical execution to designing the future operating model of Sumo Logic's product marketing engine. Core Responsibilities 1. Strategic Positioning Architecture & Master Messaging Establish and govern master narrative, value matrices, and core messaging across Observability and Security audiences. Maintain a single source of truth for product capabilities and proof points driving downstream generative tools and campaigns. Synthesize technical inputs into buyer-centric value propositions for practitioners and C-suite leaders. 2. High-Leverage, AI-Augmented Collateral Production Modernize creation of sales assets, including solution briefs, pitch decks, white papers, and video scripts. Build AI-assisted content pipelines (prompt libraries, RAG, structured templates) converting technical specs into field assets. Partner with Sales Enablement to integrate generated collateral into field delivery tools and LMS platforms. 3. Self-Serve Field Content Systems & Modular Asset Kits Design modular presentation kits allowing AEs and SEs to independently assemble stage-specific decks. Maintain a searchable knowledge repository of approved copy blocks, proof points, and architectural diagrams. Replace ad-hoc collateral requests with structured intake, template updates, and self-serve workflows. 4. Tiered GTM Launch Frameworks Enforce a disciplined GTM release tiering system to optimize team bandwidth and commercial impact. Drive cross-functional GTM strategies and core collateral packages for Tier-1 flagship innovations. Provide self-serve launch toolkits for Product Managers to document routine Tier-2/3 feature updates. Own end-to-end launch readiness and coordination across a portfolio of programs, from planning through post-launch measurement. Build and apply AI-powered workflows, such as automated launch tracking, content drafting, messaging QA, and stakeholder updates, to scale your impact and free up time for strategic work. Qualifications & Experience Domain Credibility: 10+ years of experience in Product Marketing within complex B2B SaaS, log analytics, cloud infrastructure, observability, or cybersecurity market segments. PMM Craftsmanship: Deep, hands-on experience crafting high-converting sales presentations, technical white papers, solution briefs, and persona-aligned value propositions. AI & Workflow Fluency: Practical enthusiasm and hands-on capability with modern generative AI tools to structure prompt chains, synthesize technical input, and scale content generation workflows. Systems Mindset: A builder who naturally defaults to creating reusable templates, standardized blocks, and self-serve frameworks rather than writing one-off documents from scratch. Cross-Functional Collaboration: Strong interpersonal skills to partner effectively with Product Management on technical inputs, and with dedicated Sales Enablement partners on field distribution. About Us Sumo Logic, Inc. helps make the digital world secure, fast, and reliable by unifying critical security and operational data through its Intelligent Operations Platform. Built to address the increasing complexity of modern cybersecurity and cloud operations challenges, we empower digital teams to move from reaction to readiness—combining agentic AI-powered SIEM and log analytics into a single platform to detect, investigate, and resolve modern challenges. Customers around the world rely on Sumo Logic for trusted insights to protect against security threats, ensure reliability, and gain powerful insights into their digital environments.
