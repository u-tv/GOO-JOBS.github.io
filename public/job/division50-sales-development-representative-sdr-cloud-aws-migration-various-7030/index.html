division50: Sales Development Representative (SDR) - Cloud & AWS Migration

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Lebanon, Pennsylvania, United States URL: https://jobs.workable.com/company/gRudG3AGTciXHWmUBsNe3T/jobs-at-division50 Division50 is hiring a bilingual Sales Development Representative to support an AWS cloud migration campaign for a technology distribution and cloud services partner. You’ll connect with founders, CEOs, and CTOs at funded technology startups across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, Kuwait, and Oman. You’ll identify startups with a real opportunity to move workloads from Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud Platform, or another cloud provider to AWS. Through outbound calls and follow-ups, you’ll understand their current cloud setup, business needs, and migration plans, then book qualified meetings for the client’s sales team. What you’ll do Research funded technology product and platform startups across the five target markets and verify their fit before outreach. Cold call founders, CEOs, and CTOs and follow up through email and LinkedIn. Run discovery conversations to establish the prospect’s current cloud provider, approximate monthly cloud spend, workloads under consideration, decision-making authority, and migration timeline. Identify hosting or data residency requirements and flag them for the client’s cloud team. Book meetings with qualified decision-makers, confirm attendance, and provide clear handoff notes to the client’s sales team. Keep accurate CRM records and contribute to weekly reporting on outreach, follow-ups, and qualified meetings held. B2B outbound sales experience in cloud services, IT infrastructure, managed IT services, or a technical SaaS product. Hands-on cold-calling experience and confidence speaking with founders, CTOs, or other senior technical decision-makers. Working knowledge of AWS, Microsoft Azure, or Google Cloud, with the ability to discuss cloud workloads and migration at a discovery level. Strong account research and lead qualification skills, including the ability to verify startup funding and ask direct questions about spend and timing. Fluent Arabic and advanced English (C1/C2), with clear, professional spoken and written communication. Immediate availability. Experience prospecting GCC startups, working with a cloud services partner, or supporting cloud migration campaigns is preferred. A foundational cloud certification is a plus. To apply: https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/division50-sales-development-representative-sdr-cloud-aws-migration
