You will work primarily with JavaScript and TypeScript across browser and Node.js environments. Job Description: You understand developers and care deeply about their experience. You know how to design intuitive APIs, libraries, CLIs, SDKs, documentation, and examples that help developers integrate a product quickly, correctly, and securely. In this role, you will build and maintain developer-facing software for Auth0, including SDKs, libraries, CLI tooling, Quickstarts, code samples, and implementation guidance. You will work closely with engineering, product, documentation, support, developer relations, and community teams to identify and remove friction from the developer journey. This is primarily a software engineering role, with meaningful opportunities to engage directly with developers, understand their feedback, and represent their needs across Auth0. You will be: Develop and maintain libraries that enable users to integrate seamlessly with Auth0. Directly shape and develop our open-source CLIs and SDKs. Write, curate, and edit developer resources, including tutorials, examples, guides, and documentation. Own Auth0 code samples and implementation guidance, making them clear, structured, discoverable, and useful for both developers and AI agents. Improve our Quickstart guides and onboarding experiences. Troubleshoot and fix issues with our libraries, SDKs, CLI, and integrations. Gather and channel user feedback within the company to improve the Auth0 developer experience. Serve as an internal and external subject-matter expert, providing domain knowledge and technical reviews. Engage directly with the developer community and share technical expertise at conferences and other technology events. You'd be a great fit if you: Have 5+ years of professional experience with JavaScript and TypeScript, including Node.js development, backend systems, and API specifications.
