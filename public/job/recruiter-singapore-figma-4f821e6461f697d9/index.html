Recruiter (Singapore)

🏢 figma 📍 Singapore 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Figma is growing our team of passionate creatives and builders on a mission to make design accessible to all. Figma’s platform helps teams bring ideas to life—whether you're brainstorming, creating a prototype, translating designs into code, or iterating with AI. From idea to product, Figma empowers teams to streamline workflows, move faster, and work together in real time from anywhere in the world. If you're excited to shape the future of design and collaboration, join us! We are looking to hire an experienced full life-cycle Recruiter to support the organization through its growth in Singapore and across APAC. You'll be a driving force in building and executing the talent strategy, partnering with key stakeholders, and hiring top talent across key business functions. This role will allow you to build strong relationships, champion our values, and contribute to our org-wide recruiting best practices and processes. This is a full-time role based in our Singapore office within a hybrid working environment. What you'll do at Figma: Manage full-cycle recruiting processes for our business organizations, in particular, go to market functions like sales & marketing Partner with leaders to develop top of funnel strategies for key roles Source and build healthy, representative pipelines to engage passive talent including: cold outreach, events, and content production while also effectively handling inbound applications and referrals Leverage data to inform your hiring strategy and articulate these insights to executives in order to influence and drive positive change Identify, conceptualize and drive business improvement initiatives We’d love to hear from you if you have: 5+ years of full-cycle in house recruiting experience in a high growth business Experience partnering with senior executives on high volume searches in go to market organizations Expert knowledge of the SaaS / scaling tech landscape in APAC, particularly Singapore Advanced sourcing and passive engagement skills Ability to build a talent strategy alongside your hiring managers, while delivering an exceptional candidate experience While it’s not required, it’s an added plus if you also have: Experience hiring across multiple countries in APAC Proficiency in an additional Asian language (e.g Japanese, Korean) At Figma, one of our values is Grow as you go. We believe in hiring smart, curious people who are excited to learn and develop their skills. If you’re excited about this role but your past experience doesn’t align perfectly with the points outlined in the job description, we encourage you to apply anyways.
