SLSQ427R582 Global Genie Sales Leader We are looking for a highly strategic and execution-oriented Genie Global Leader to lead a team of specialists responsible for growing the Databricks Genie business globally. Databricks Genie extends the Data Intelligence Platform into conversational AI through Genie One , delivering a true AI co-worker for non-technical business teams and leaders. By grounding query accuracy in business context through the Genie One Ontology , Genie One enables enterprise decision-makers to effortlessly query, analyze, and extract trusted insights directly from governed enterprise data using natural language. This role sits at the center of a major shift in enterprise architecture as organizations move away from fragmented stacks consisting of legacy BI tools, static dashboards, and separate AI interfaces toward a unified, conversational data platform powered by enterprise context. You will lead a team of specialists partnering with Account Executives, Solution Architects, and Sales Leadership to help customers modernize their business intelligence, democratize data access across non-technical users, and operationalize conversational AI in production analytics workflows. This role requires both strong sales leadership and the ability to establish and scale a new sales motion across a global field organization. This is a highly visible global role across Sales, Product, and Engineering, and will help define how Databricks engages business intelligence leaders, data leaders, and non-technical business stakeholders at scale. The impact you will have: Drive Revenue Growth Build and exceed quarterly and annual consumption and booking targets for Genie Develop pipeline across legacy BI migration, AI-driven business intelligence, and enterprise-wide data access opportunities. Help customers replace or modernize legacy BI tools and point solutions by establishing Databricks as their primary conversational analytics platform Establish repeatable sales plays that drive land and expand adoption Lead and Develop a Specialist Team Recruit, hire, and develop a high-performing team of Genie Sales Specialists Coach the team on complex deal strategy, technical qualification, and executive engagement Create a culture of accountability, customer focus, and operational excellence Partner with regional sales leadership to integrate specialists into account plans and territory strategies Strategic Customer and Partner Engagement Establish executive relationships with corporate executives, CIOs, CTOs, Chief Architects, platform leaders, and technical decision makers Position Databricks as an application data platform in addition to a data and AI platform Support strategic customers pursuing application modernization or real-time digital transformation initiatives Collaborate with cloud providers, SIs, and ISVs to accelerate adoption Influence Product and GTM Strategy Act as the voice of the field to Product and Engineering teams Provide structured feedback on customer requirements, competitive dynamics, and market adoption patterns Partner with Product Marketing to refine messaging, positioning, and competitive differentiation Help shape Databricks’ long-term AI/BI and enterprise data intelligence platform strategy What we look for: 15+ years of experience in enterprise software, SaaS, cloud platform, or developer platform sales 5+ years of experience leading high-performing enterprise sales or specialist teams Demonstrated success building new categories or scaling new product businesses Previous Sales experience in fast paced innovative start up companies in domains such as cowork, search and ai/bi and conversational ai.
