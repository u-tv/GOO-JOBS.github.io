Resolv.Global: Seasonal Customer Care Representative

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Ciudad de México, Mexico URL: https://resolv.global We are seeking customer-focused and dependable Seasonal Customer Care Representatives to join our growing remote team. In this role, you will support customers across phone, email, webchat, and social channels , helping resolve inquiries related to orders, billing, product information, returns, and general account support. We’re looking for strong communicators who can deliver professional, empathetic service while thriving in a fast-paced remote environment. If you enjoy helping people, solving problems, and creating positive customer experiences, we’d love to hear from you. Key Responsibilities Provide excellent customer service through phone, email, chat, and social channels. Assist customers with order inquiries, returns, billing questions, and product information. Accurately enter customer and order information into internal systems. Resolve customer concerns professionally and escalate issues when necessary. Maintain records of customer interactions and actions taken. Identify opportunities to improve customer satisfaction and support sales initiatives. Collaborate with team members and supervisors to ensure smooth operations. Qualifications Strong English verbal and written communication skills Customer service or call center experience preferred Ability to multitask in a fast-paced environment Basic computer proficiency and ability to navigate multiple systems Typing speed of 30+ WPM preferred Flexible and team-oriented attitude Work Environment Fully remote position Must be comfortable working with your camera on during the entire shift Must be comfortable working weekends and flexible schedules as needed Independent contractor agreement Weekly payments in USD $5.50 USD per hour Equipment We will provide a laptop; however, to be considered for this role, you must have an additional monitor, a USB headset, and a stable, wired (Ethernet) internet connection. Hiring Process To ensure fairness and consistency, all applicants must complete every stage of the hiring process. Our process includes: Resume Review Pre-Screen Questionnaire One-Way Video Interview Pre-Screen interview Live Interviews Background Check Please Note This is an English-speaking role that requires strong written and verbal English communication skills. All resumes must be submitted in English to be considered for this position. A background check might be required for candidates who are shortlisted as part of the final hiring process. Employment will be subject to successful completion of the applicable background check. Requirements Requirements Advanced English proficiency Minimum of 1-2 years of Customer Experience experience Strong communication and organizational skills Professional attitude and ability to multitask Strong computer literacy To apply: https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/resolv-global-seasonal-customer-care-representative
