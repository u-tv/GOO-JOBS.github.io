About Paytm Paytm is India's leading mobile payments and financial services distribution company. Pioneer of the mobile QR payments revolution in India, Paytm builds technologies that help small businesses with payments and commerce. Paytm’s mission is to serve half a billion Indians and bring them to the mainstream economy with the help of technology. Location : Noida Duration : 5 months | January - May 2027 Work Mode : In-office, 5 days a week About the Role We’re looking for a Visual Design Intern who can bring craft, taste, and a strong sense of direction to Paytm’s visual language — someone who thinks beyond a single polished screen and can translate everyday, utility-first product moments into visuals that feel considered and unmistakably Paytm. You’ll work on illustration, iconography, and visual systems for product surfaces used at scale — sourcing references, building a point of view, and executing that direction with craft, alongside designers and stakeholders across the team. Throughout the internship, you’ll have a dedicated design mentor, participate in design critiques, and learn how a considered visual direction gets built, defended, and scaled across a product. About the Role We’re looking for a Product Design Intern who thinks beyond pretty screens and cares about solving real user problems. You’ll work on a product alongside designers, product managers, and engineers—understanding users, exploring solutions, building prototypes, and taking experiences from early ideas to polished designs. Throughout the internship, you’ll have a dedicated design mentor, participate in design critiques, and learn how thoughtful design decisions are made in a fast-moving product environment. What You’ll Do ● Work on product flows, user journeys, wireframes, and high-fidelity designs. ● Understand user needs and translate them into clear design solutions. ● Collaborate with product managers, developers, and senior designers. ● Create interactive prototypes for testing and stakeholder reviews. ● Contribute to UX research insights, competitor analysis, and usability improvements. ● Design experiences that are intuitive, accessible, and scalable. ● Use design systems to maintain consistency across products. ● Take feedback, iterate fast, and improve design quality with every round. What We’re Looking For ● Final-year student pursuing a degree in Design, HCI, Interaction Design, Visual Communication, or a related field. ● Strong UX fundamentals and product thinking. ● Good visual design skills with attention to detail. ● Ability to understand problems, explore alternatives, and clearly explain design decisions. ● Understanding of user flows, interaction patterns, wireframing, and prototyping. ● Hands-on experience with Figma or similar design tools with an understanding of design systems. ● Familiarity with AI tools as part of the design workflow. ● Curiosity, ownership, and openness to feedback. ● A portfolio with strong case studies or UX explorations that demonstrate your process and thinking Why Join Us? ● Design for real impact Your visuals will live inside a product used at scale. ● Learn from experienced designers Get dedicated mentorship, regular critiques, and feedback. ● Grow through collaboration Work closely with cross-functional teams and learn through continuous 360° feedback. ● Be part of the team Build strong relationships with peers and mentors through ownership, craft and contribution. How to Apply Share your resume and portfolio through the application form. We want to understand how you think.
