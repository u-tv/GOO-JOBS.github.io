Developer Experiences Manager

🏢 vercel 📍 Hybrid - London 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Vercel: Vercel is the agentic infrastructure company, freeing people and agents to ship what's next. For more than a decade we've helped builders move from idea to production with speed, security, and exceptional developer experience. Now we're scaling our products for both agents and people to ship and run software, built in the open and trusted by OpenAI, PayPal, Ramp, Supreme, and millions of developers worldwide. About the Role: As a Developer Experiences Manager, you'll build and grow Vercel's developer community across Europe, the Middle East, and Africa. You'll turn global community priorities into a regional plan, then deliver the meetups, workshops, and hackathons that help developers learn, build, and ship with Vercel. Within your first year, you'll have an EMEA plan in motion, a network of local community hosts, and programs that earn repeat attendance. You'll bring regional insights back into our global programs. This is a hybrid role with 3 in-office days per week in London. What You Will Do: Own the EMEA developer community plan: priority cities, audiences, partners, calendar, and budget Run meetups, workshops, hackathons, and builder gatherings, plus activations around conferences and product moments, from concept through follow-up Recruit, onboard, and support local community hosts with playbooks, programming guidance, and feedback Evaluate sponsorships and partnerships against audience relevance, developer value, and expected outcomes Partner with Developer Relations on technical sessions and demos covering Vercel, Next.js, v0, and the AI SDK, and track results to decide where time and budget go About You: 3+ years building developer communities, developer programs, or technical events Owned complex events or programs end to end, including budgets, vendors, and partners, with measurable outcomes Built developer communities with sustained participation across multiple EMEA markets Technical fluency in modern web development and AI tooling, enough to shape sessions with technical speakers Available for regional travel and occasional evening or weekend events Bonus If You: Have supported a community host or ambassador network across multiple countries Have worked with Next.js or the Vercel platform Have run hackathons or builder events with startups or universities Compensation & Benefits: Competitive compensation package, including equity. Inclusive Healthcare Package. Learn and Grow - we provide mentorship and send you to events that help you build your network and skills. Flexible Time Off. We will provide you the gear you need to do your role, and a WFH budget for you to outfit your space as needed. The United Kingdom base pay range for this role is £70,000 - £97,000. Actual salary will be based on job-related skills, experience, and location. Compensation outside of the United Kingdom may be adjusted based on employee location. The total compensation package may include benefits, equity-based compensation, and eligibility for a company bonus or variable pay program depending on the role.
