Solutions Architect

🏢 mongodb 📍 Bengaluru 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

MongoDB is seeking a Partner Solutions Architect to focus on our Large Deals exclusively on strategic, high-value pursuits with Global System Integrators (GSIs). This role will lead the technical strategy and execution of complex GSI-led bids, helping MongoDB and its GSI partners develop differentiated solutions and secure large, strategic opportunities. The successful candidate will operate across MongoDB, GSI partners, customers, sales teams, delivery organizations, and executive stakeholders to develop compelling joint solutions, strengthen technical win strategies, and drive successful outcomes for large transformation programs. We are looking for candidates who are based in Bengaluru/Gurugram/Delhi/Hyderabad/Chennai for our hybrid working model. migration, cloud, application development, data, analytics, and AI solutions Identify technical risks, competitive threats, delivery constraints, and commercial considerations early in the bid
