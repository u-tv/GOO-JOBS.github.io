Sr. Project/Program Manager II

🏢 samsara 📍 Remote - FL; Remote - GA; Remote - PA 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Who we are Samsara (NYSE: IOT) is the pioneer of the Connected Operations™ Cloud, which is a platform that enables organizations that depend on physical operations to harness Internet of Things (IoT) data to develop actionable insights and improve their operations. At Samsara, we are helping improve the safety, efficiency and sustainability of the physical operations that power our global economy. Representing more than 40% of global GDP, these industries are the infrastructure of our planet, including agriculture, construction, field services, transportation, and manufacturing — and we are excited to help digitally transform their operations at scale. Working at Samsara means you’ll help define the future of physical operations and be on a team that’s shaping an exciting array of product solutions, including Video-Based Safety, Vehicle Telematics, Apps and Driver Workflows, and Equipment Monitoring. As part of a recently public company, you’ll have the autonomy and support to make an impact as we build for the long term. About the role: We are looking for a Senior Project/Program Manager II to be a hands-on partner to our accounting, tax, and O2C leaders. You are an AI wizard, up to date on the newest technology, and ready to support teams through the self supported transition to automated workflows. You will turn business ideas into well-defined, well-tested system and process improvements, serving as the translator between finance and technical teams from first intake through go-live and hypercare. Over time, you will own a portfolio of projects aligned to these teams, while staying flexible across all three areas because they are closely connected. This is a remote position open to candidates residing in the US except Alaska, Austin Metro, Boulder Metro, California, Chicago Metro, Connecticut, Dallas Metro, Denver Metro, Houston Metro, Maryland, Massachusetts, New Jersey, New York, Rhode Island, Seattle Metro, and Washington, D.C. Relocation assistance will not be provided for this role. You should apply if: You want to impact the industries that run our world: Your efforts will result in real-world impact – helping to keep the lights on, get food into grocery stores, reduce emissions, and most importantly, ensure workers return home safely. You like solving puzzles: You enjoy untangling a messy process or system problem, determining root cause and impact, and landing on a solution that works. You enjoy making other people’s jobs easier: You get energized by partnering with business stakeholders to make their work more efficient, and you measure success by the problems you take off their plate. You like hands-on work across teams: You would rather be in the details with people from finance, accounting, tax, and IT than managing from a distance, and you build trust by being a reliable partner to each of them. You are a self-starter who works well remotely: You think critically, take ownership of your projects, and keep work moving day to day from your own workspace, with a collaborative team and leaders you can lean on when you need them. You want to be with the best: At Samsara, we win together, celebrate together and support each other. You will be surrounded by a high-calibre team that will encourage you to do your best. In this role, you will: Partner with business stakeholders across accounting to intake and discuss their challenges and project ideas, assess scope and initial level of effort, identify the right technical resources, and help leaders prioritize work using our project intake framework, including sizing the business backlog (for example, with t-shirt sizes) so you can talk with IT about capacity. Turn those ideas into clear business requirements documents (BRDs) and detailed requirements that state the problem to solve, not the solution design, with defined in-scope and out-of-scope items, and maintain project artifacts such as timelines and RAID logs. Put AI to work, and know it well. You understand how models work under the hood (neural networks, training and backpropagation, tokens and context windows), the difference between a model and the agents and applications built on it, and the difference between LLMs and agentic AI. You consult with business stakeholders on AI solutions that don't need IT to build or support: you know skills, workflows, and MCPs, can spot where AI is the right tool for a problem and where it isn't, and know its limits (hallucinations, security, data privacy). You use it to speed up drafting of requirements, test scenarios, and project reporting, keep a human in the loop, and check every output. Act as the key contact between business stakeholders and IT teams (such as FinSys, GTMS, and Integrations) through design and development. Translate between finance and technical language, and between IT teams (such as Salesforce, NetSuite, and integrations) to help pinpoint root causes, document key design decisions and trade-offs, and use good judgment on which decisions you can guide and which to bring back to stakeholders for confirmation. Coordinate user acceptance testing (UAT) end to end: draft unit test scenarios and UAT and end-to-end test cases with stakeholder input, maintain the requirements traceability matrix (RTM), manage timelines and tester availability across concurrent projects, triage defects hands-on with business and technical teams (digging into the fields, logic, and formulas to understand how the system should work and why) through resolution and retesting, and provide UAT summaries against entry and exit criteria. Lead go-live readiness and support hypercare, including go/no-go decisions and sign-offs, steering committee updates, and joint business and IT status reporting, escalating risks early. Connect business stakeholders with our enablement teams on SOPs and training materials, and clearly explain what is changing so enablement can work with the business audience to update guides, SOPs, and training to match what is being deployed. Grow into owning a portfolio of projects while remaining flexible across corporate accounting, tax, and O2C, offering guidance to teammates as the team grows. Work with IT in their language: understand agile concepts such as epics, stories, bugs, story points, and sprints, and protect IT's capacity by offering MVPs and phases when a request that looks simple to the business is complex underneath. Build SOX controls into projects: determine which systems are in or out of scope for IT general controls (ITGCs), plan control design and evidence, run user access reviews with IT, and understand how SOC 1 and SOC 2 reports apply to finance systems. Champion, role model, and embed Samsara’s cultural principles (Focus on Customer Success, Build for the Long Term, Adopt a Growth Mindset, Be Inclusive, Win as a Team) as we scale globally and across
