The GTM (Go-To-Market) Engineering team at InMobi builds and scales the systems that power revenue operations and customer engagement. The team owns a comprehensive Salesforce ecosystem—including Sales Cloud, Data Cloud, and Agentforce —along with Experience Cloud (Customer Community), enabling seamless workflows for Sales, Account Management, and Business teams. A key focus area is building intelligent agents and automations using Agentforce , integrated across Salesforce, Slack, and community platforms. These agents drive productivity, automate workflows, and enable real-time, context-aware actions for internal teams and customer-facing use cases. The team also leverages Slack as a collaboration and execution layer, tightly integrated with Salesforce to trigger workflows, surface insights, and enable faster decision-making. On the backend, the team develops and maintains a scalable middleware platform using Apache Camel and Java, powering event-driven integrations and reliable data exchange across systems. The architecture blends CRM, data platforms, agent-based automation, and distributed systems—focusing on extensibility, real-time processing, and high impact business outcomes. This is a high-impact engineering team operating at the intersection of CRM, AI-driven agents, integrations, and modern enterprise platforms.
