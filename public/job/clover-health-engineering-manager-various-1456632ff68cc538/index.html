Clover Health: Engineering Manager

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote - USA At Counterpart Health, we are transforming healthcare and improving patient care with our innovative primary care tool, Counterpart Assistant. By supporting Primary Care Physicians (PCPs), we are able to deliver improved outcomes to our patients at a lower cost through early diagnosis and longitudinal care management of chronic conditions. We're looking for an Engineering Manager to join our team. Our managers take on a player/coach role, balancing technical leadership, execution, and people management. The primary responsibility of Engineering Managers is to build and develop strong teams while ensuring high-impact results. We empower engineers to take ownership of problems and support them with mentorship, guidance, and business context. As an Engineering Manager, you will: Drive team impact by fostering high technical productivity and reliability. Cultivate a healthy, results-driven, and collaborative team culture. Represent the team in engineering-wide planning, initiatives, and processes. Partner with Product Managers and Designers to shape our technical roadmap and solve problems that improve the patient and doctor experience. Set and communicate clear team objectives that align with Counterpart's company goals and roadmap. Lead hiring, development, and retention efforts to build a high-performing team. You should get in touch if: You have technical leadership experience managing teams of 3+ engineers. You have a strong background in system design and proficiency in general-purpose programming languages such as Python, Ruby, Go, or Java. You have a track record of leading development projects while creating opportunities for others to grow into leadership roles. You have experience in fast-paced environments, making quick, strategic decisions under competing constraints. You have a proven ability to successfully deliver complex web applications and services. You are adaptable and able to work across various technical systems and concerns. Benefits Overview : Financial Well-Being : Our commitment to attracting and retaining top talent begins with a competitive base salary and equity opportunities. Additionally, we offer a performance-based bonus program and regular compensation reviews to recognize and reward exceptional contributions. Physical Well-Being : We prioritize the health and well-being of our employees and their families by offering comprehensive group medical coverage that include coverage for hospitalization, outpatient care, optical services, and dental benefits. Mental Well-Being : We understand the importance of mental health in fostering productivity and maintaining work-life balance. To support this, we offer initiatives such as No-Meeting Fridays, company holidays, access to mental health resources, and a generous annual leave policy. Additionally, we embrace a remote-first culture that supports collaboration and flexibility, allowing our team members to thrive from any location. Professional Development : We are committed to developing our talent professionally. We offer learning programs, mentorship, professional development funding, and regular performance feedback and reviews. Additional Perks : Reimbursement for office setup expenses Monthly cell phone & internet stipend Flexibility to work from home, enabling collaboration with global teams Paid parental leave for all new parents And much more! About Counterpart Health: In 2018, Clover Health set out to do something unprecedented: build a clinically intuitive, AI-enabled solution that fits within physicians' workflows to help support the earlier diagnosis and management of chronic conditions. Years later, that vision is a reality, with thousands of practitioners using Counterpart Assistant during patient visits to improve disease management, reduce medical expenses, and drive success in value-based care. With an exceptional team of value-based care and technology experts, Counterpart Health is driving value-based care at the speed of software. Counterpart Health is a subsidiary of Clover Health.
