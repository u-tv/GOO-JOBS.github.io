About this role: Coursera’s Enterprise organization helps companies, governments, educational institutions, and nonprofits upskill and retrain their workforces. As Account Director (Public Sector), you’ll serve as Coursera’s Global Government Industry Leader on the Public Sector HQ team, reporting to the Global Public Sector VP. You’ll own the global government strategy and carry a global government sales quota measured by annual contract value (ACV). You’ll partner with Public Sector Regional Directors across the Americas, EMEA, and Asia to turn local market insights into a global plan for revenue growth, market share, and customer satisfaction. You’ll lead market prioritization, government messaging, field enablement, and revenue performance reporting. You’ll shape pricing and packaging, support strategic deals, and represent Coursera with senior government leaders and at industry events. Success will be measured by global government ACV attainment, new ARR, renewals, expansion, customer satisfaction, regional field feedback, and the strength of executive and partner relationships. About your skills: Sales Strategy: Use market trends, competitive intelligence, and regional feedback to prioritize government markets and build plans that drive revenue and market share. Business Development & Account Management: Develop new government opportunities and grow existing relationships through strategic deal support, renewals, expansion, and partner-led selling. Executive Influence: Engage senior government and C-suite leaders on workforce transformation, skills, and AI adoption, and use customer insights to influence internal decisions. Sales Process: Bring rigor to pipeline, forecasting, revenue reporting, and complex government deals, including stakeholder alignment and procurement. What you’ll be doing: Own the global government sales quota and revenue lifecycle, driving new ARR, renewals, and expansion. Track performance and use regional field feedback to guide execution across the Americas, EMEA, and Asia. Set the global government strategy by analyzing market trends and competitive threats, prioritizing markets, and shaping pricing, packaging, and field enablement. Build Coursera’s government value proposition around workforce transformation, skills, and AI adoption. Partner with Marketing on campaigns and with Partnerships to identify and develop government ecosystem partners. Support regional sales teams in closing large, strategic government opportunities. Build relationships with senior decision-makers, position Coursera as a trusted advisor, and represent Coursera as a speaker or keynote at government events. Bring government customer and field insights into product roadmap discussions. Define service-level agreements with HQ teams and partners across Marketing, Partnerships, SDR, Customer Success, Services, Product, Finance, Content, Legal, and RevOps to support implementation, retention, and growth. Qualifications: Deep government or public-sector experience, with a track record of selling to or supporting government entities and applying market and competitive insights to sales strategy. 5+ years of enterprise SaaS sales experience across new logo acquisition, account management, and renewals, with demonstrated success helping close complex, multi-stakeholder deals with six-figure ARR. Strong communication and presentation skills, with experience selling to C-suite executives, articulating a differentiated value proposition, and providing industry direction to sales teams. Experience working with partner networks to scale field engagement and aligning cross-functional HQ and regional stakeholders around commercial priorities. Fluency in English and ability to travel up to 25%. Preferred qualifications Enterprise SaaS experience in EdTech or HR Tech, experience selling to HR or L&D leaders, or thought leadership in workforce transformation, skills, or learning strategy. A record of growing existing accounts through upsell and cross-sell, securing multiyear enterprise agreements, or supporting global rollouts. Salesforce proficiency, familiarity with sales methodologies, and strong pipeline, forecasting, and deal-review practices. Proficiency in additional languages and an interest in improving public outcomes through education and learning.
