Abnormal: Director, Professional Services

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote - USA About the Role Abnormal AI is hiring a Global Director of Professional Services to set the vision and growth strategy for our global services organization. You’ll lead the teams that help enterprise customers implement Abnormal and migrate from legacy Secure Email Gateways (SEGs). You’ll own delivery performance across regions and build a services model that helps customers go live faster and with less disruption. You’ll help to build our new product implementation strategy as Abnormal AI becomes a multi-product behavioral security platform. Who You Are You’re a services leader who can turn a long-term strategy into clear priorities for managers and delivery teams. You use customer outcomes and financial data to make decisions about where to invest, and you’re comfortable leading complex customer negotiations and communicating trade-offs to executives. What You Will Do Set the global Professional Services strategy and growth plan. Define service offerings, delivery models, and investment priorities in line with Abnormal’s business goals as we further expand into a multi-product platform. Lead and develop managers across regions. Set clear performance expectations, build succession plans, and align hiring and staffing with forecasted customer demand. Own delivery performance across the portfolio. Use time to go live, on-time delivery, utilization, customer satisfaction, and services financial metrics to guide decisions and measure return on investment. Set implementation and migration standards across regions and serve as the executive escalation point for complex or at-risk engagements. Lead major customer negotiations and build strategic partnerships. Work with Sales and Customer Success on engagement scope and handoffs, and bring recurring deployment issues to Product and Engineering. Represent Professional Services in executive leadership and board discussions, communicating performance, risks, and investment priorities. Help to build out automations and agentic solutions to further improve the team’s effectiveness and efficiency. Must Haves 7+ years leading a Professional Services or technical consulting organization, including experience managing managers and leading teams across regions. Experience setting a multi-year services strategy and scaling delivery for complex enterprise SaaS or cybersecurity implementations or migrations. Experience using delivery and financial metrics to forecast demand, allocate capacity, and evaluate services investments. Track record of leading complex customer negotiations, building strategic partnerships, and resolving escalations with senior customer stakeholders. Nice to Haves Experience with enterprise email security, Secure Email Gateways, or cloud email migrations. Experience building a partner-led delivery program or introducing new services offerings. #LI-LM3 Actual compensation will be determined based on several non-discriminatory factors including skills, experience, qualifications, and geographic location. In addition to base salary, this role may be eligible for bonus or incentive compensation, equity, and a comprehensive benefits package.
