By combining these strengths, we can connect more people and organizations to the skills they need, when they need them. Shape what comes next By joining our team, you’ll have the opportunity to reshape how the world learns and applies skills—and help millions of people participate in the new economy. Bring your ideas, expertise, and perspective to meaningful work that can make a difference at global scale. If you’re ready to make a global impact, help scale unique products across Coursera + Udemy, and grow your career, apply below. About this role: We are seeking a highly motivated and experienced Enterprise Strategic Account Executive to join our sales team. You’ll identify and close new business opportunities with large enterprise-level clients (25k+ employees), build long-term value in accounts, and establish key business relationships. To succeed in this role, you must know how to navigate and manage complex sales cycles, build champions, and develop C-suite relationships. You sell on value and are excited about the mission of Udemy. About your skills: Problem Solving: Skilled in creative problem-solving techniques to address client needs, overcome obstacles, and drive sales outcomes, ensuring customer satisfaction and business success. Long-term Thinker: Able to visualize a long-term path for customer growth and build toward joint success by multi-threading and deeply understanding the unique value Udemy creates, connecting it to what is most meaningful to your customers. Curiosity: A natural curiosity and hunger for knowledge, constantly seeking to understand market trends, customer preferences, and competitive analysis to inform strategy and drive business success. Outbound Mindset: A proactive outbound mindset, driven to identify new business opportunities, cultivate relationships with prospects, and effectively communicate value, supported by research and rigor throughout the process. Hustle is in your DNA. AI Proficiency: Strong AI literacy, adopting and mastering new sales technologies such as GenAI, conversational intelligence, and AI-powered tooling to drive efficiency. You use prompt engineering to create personalized messaging and gain insight for customer meetings, and you leverage data-driven insights from AI tools to craft high-impact pitches for C-level executives.
