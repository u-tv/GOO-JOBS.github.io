About the Job Responsible for identifying, assessing, and monitoring key accounting risks and controls to develop solutions that align with the company’s long-term objectives. Duties include: Conducting comprehensive evaluations of risks and internal controls under financial compliance and other regulatory frameworks to ensure accuracy and efficiency; Advising on improvement areas related to engineering and technology systems and practices to support business growth, including assessing risk and controls related to ERP systems; Developing and implementing risk management strategies by leveraging data analytics, automation tools, and artificial intelligence to streamline audit processes; Overseeing the design and execution of internal audit team strategies to evaluate team metrics and support performance improvements; Collaborating with senior management and audit committees to share audit findings with external auditors; Developing comprehensive reports and dashboards to present high-impact findings and actionable recommendations to leadership teams; Other duties as assigned. About You Minimum Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, Information Technology, or related discipline (or foreign equivalent). 4 years of experience in the job offered or related. 4 years of experience working with financial reporting systems and related reporting tools; 4 years of experience with accounting and audit standards including GAAP and GAAS; 4 years of experience evaluating risks and designing or assessing controls over internally developed (homegrown) technology platforms and environments; 4 years of experience assessing the design and operating effectiveness of information technology general controls (ITGCs) and automated financial controls; 4 years of experience executing internal audit engagements, including pre-IPO readiness and SOX compliance assessments; 4 years of experience leveraging advanced data analytics and automation tools (such as Alteryx, SQL, Python, or comparable technologies) to support audit and risk activities.
