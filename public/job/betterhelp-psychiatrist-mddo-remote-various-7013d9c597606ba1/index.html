BetterHelp: Psychiatrist (MD/DO) - Remote

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Texas, United States URL: http://betterhelp.com/linkedin BetterHelp is a technology-enabled mental health marketplace that connects patients with therapists and psychiatric providers covered by their insurance plans. By bringing patients, providers, and insurers together, we aim to remove barriers to care and help more people find the right support for their needs. Why Join BetterHelp Competitive earning potential: Estimated effective hourly earnings of $119–$242 per hour *, based on modeled assumptions for a 60-minute period that may include patient visits and applicable CPT codes. Expand access to mental healthcare: Provide psychiatric care to individuals who may otherwise face barriers to finding an available provider who accepts their insurance. Deliver collaborative care: Work alongside BetterHelp’s network of licensed therapists to provide thoughtful, evidence-based treatment. Spend more time on patient care: BetterHelp supports credentialing, payer enrollment, and platform operations so you can focus on your patients. Work from anywhere: Deliver care 100% remotely through BetterHelp’s telemedicine platform. Build a schedule that works for you: This is a flexible, part-time 1099 contract opportunity. What You’ll Do You’ll provide compassionate, evidence-based psychiatric care in a convenient virtual setting including: Conduct video consultations and communicate with patients through telemedicine. Assess, screen, diagnose, and prescribe medication when clinically appropriate. Develop individualized treatment plans based on each patient’s needs. Coordinate treatment planning and care delivery with BetterHelp’s network of licensed therapists. Deliver empathetic, high-quality care while upholding BetterHelp’s clinical and service standards. Share feedback with BetterHelp’s product, engineering, and operations teams to help improve the provider and patient experience. Who We’re Looking For We’re looking for psychiatrists who are passionate about expanding access to mental healthcare and comfortable delivering high-quality care virtually. You’ll be a strong fit if you have: At least one year of experience as a licensed Psychiatrist. An active medical license (MD/DO) in good standing. Experience prescribing medication when clinically appropriate and permitted by applicable state law. Confidence using technology and online tools to deliver virtual care. Strong communication skills and a patient-centered approach. Even better if you have: Previous telemedicine experience. Active medical licenses in multiple states. Fluency in additional languages. Important to Know This is a part-time, independent contractor (1099) position , not a salaried employee role. BetterHelp will support credentialing and enrollment with multiple health plans to help expand access to insured patients. All patient care is delivered remotely through BetterHelp’s telehealth platform. Licensed providers make all clinical decisions independently and in accordance with applicable state telehealth laws, regulations, and professional standards. License/Certification Medical License ( Required ) Location United States ( Required ) Work location: Remote *Estimated effective hourly earnings of $119–$242 are illustrative estimates for licensed Psychiatrists and are not a guaranteed hourly rate, wage, or salary. Estimates are derived from modeled assumptions regarding the number of completed visits per hour, CPT code mix, and utilization of add-on codes (such as 90833) when clinically appropriate and properly documented. Actual earnings will vary based on individual clinical decisions, case mix, scheduling availability, payer reimbursement, licensure, geographic market factors, and other applicable considerations.
