Director of Customer Marketing & Advocacy

🏢 mongodb 📍 United States 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

MongoDB is hiring a Director of Customer Marketing & Advocacy to showcase our customers and support one of our strongest growth levers. This is a build-and-influence role: you will scale customer marketing into a strategic growth function impacting market share, mindshare and awareness with a focus on enterprise segments and AI-native startups. We're not looking for someone to run customer marketing as usual. We want a leader who will innovate, drive change, and take this program to the next level: an AI-enabled engine that finds, matches, and activates customer voice at speed and scale, and a program other companies point to as the bar they aspire to. You will replace manual, ad hoc processes with automation and AI, so customer stories reach every channel faster and with less friction and innovate the customer marketing function and model. You will build a world where our customers show up everywhere: on stage, in analyst and press conversations, across our brand campaigns and content, and throughout the buying journey. You will also create a channel for customer insight where the insight we gather from customers will sharpen how Product and Product Marketing position, message, and launch. You will make sure every one of those moments is planned, measured, and tied to corporate goals. Customer voice will be the foundation of MongoDB's brand, building awareness and mindshare with the buyers and builders who matter most. You will lead the customer marketing team across AMER and EMEA and partner closely with Brand, Communications, Strategic Events, Social Media, Product Marketing, Executive Programs, Sales, Customer Success, Partners, Product, and Investor Relations. This role can be based remotely in the United States. What You'll Do Put customer voice at the center of MongoDB's story, across every channel Make the customer voice the heart of our brand. Partner with Brand Marketing & Creative to make customer outcomes a foundational part of MongoDB's brand narrative and campaigns, building awareness and mindshare and shaping how the market sees us Lead customer storytelling and content. Set the editorial bar for case studies, video, and executive point-of-view content, and make sure those stories feed brand, social media, communications, events, and demand programs Bring customers to every stage. Work with Communications to connect customers with the press, analysts, and industry influencers who shape enterprise and developer opinion, so credible third-party voices carry our story further than we can alone Reimagine the program and build the AI-enabled engine Redefine what customer marketing can be, and set the bar. Set the charter, operating model, and multi-year roadmap for a function that unifies storytelling, advocacy, executive engagement, and influencer relationships into one system, and build a program that peers benchmark themselves against Build an AI-enabled engine that scales. Use automation and AI to find, match, and activate customer voice across channels, from story intake and customer matching to content tagging and performance reporting. Replace ad hoc, one-off requests with clear standards, shared ownership across teams, defined dependencies, and accountability for results Grow a deep, diverse customer community. Lead the global strategy for recruiting and nurturing customers across enterprise segments and AI-native startups, so we always have the right voice for the moment and the channel Embed customer proof across the business. Bring customer evidence into brand campaigns, product launches, demand programs, and sales conversations, so it becomes a dependable part of how we market, launch, and win Turn customer voice into insight. Make customer insight a two-way exchange, not just a channel for outbound proof. Capture what customers tell us through stories and bring it back to Product and Product Marketing to inform positioning, messaging, and launches Drive impact Prove the business case. Define KPIs and build the attribution model that connects customer proof to brand awareness and mindshare, revenue growth, retention, and expansion Influence up and across. Build executive sponsorship across Brand, Communications, Social, Events, Sales, Customer Success, Product, Partners, Product Marketing, and Investor Relations Build and lead the team. Set vision, priorities, and metrics; hire, coach, and develop a high-performing global team, including people managers.
