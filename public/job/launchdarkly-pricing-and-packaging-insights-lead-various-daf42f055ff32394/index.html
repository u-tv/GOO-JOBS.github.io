LaunchDarkly: Pricing and Packaging Insights Lead

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote - US About the Job: LaunchDarkly is hiring a Pricing and Packaging Manager to own how we package, price and sell our products. You will work with Product, Sales, Customer Success, Finance and Marketing to make our commercial model easy to buy, easy to expand and fair to customers at every stage of adoption. The role sits in Revenue Operations and reports to the head of that team. Responsibilities: Own the pricing and packaging strategy across our products, including list prices, tiers, usage metrics, add-ons and enterprise terms. Make the final recommendation on list pricing and packaging. Build and maintain pricing models that link packaging choices to land and expand bookings, average contract value, gross dollar retention and margin. Independently set evaluation criteria and recommend strategic changes based on results. Design packaging for new offerings, including AI-related products, with Product and Product Marketing from concept through launch. Run pricing research and experiments: win/loss analysis, customer interviews, competitive benchmarking and willingness-to-pay studies. Translate findings into clear recommendations. Set discounting and deal-desk guardrails, and review non-standard deals with Sales, Finance and Legal. Approve guardrail changes in partnership with the CRO and CFO. Analyze funnel, pipeline, deal-size and renewal data to find where pricing helps or hurts conversion and expansion. Partner with Sales and Customer Success on adoption-led renewal, upsell and cross-sell strategies. Align pricing with compensation and quota design so incentives reward the deals the packaging is built to produce. Lead through influence across senior stakeholders to align decisions and drive adoption. Keep pricing documentation, quoting rules and billing configuration accurate with operational owners; this work is secondary to strategy and business-outcome ownership. Qualifications: 12 or more years in pricing, packaging, product strategy, revenue operations, strategy and operations, or a related analytical role, ideally at a B2B SaaS or developer-tools company. Hands-on experience designing pricing for usage-based, seat-based or hybrid models, and for both product-led and sales-led motions. Strong quantitative skills: you build models in spreadsheets or SQL, read funnel and cohort data, and explain trade-offs in plain language. Working knowledge of CRM and quoting data (for example Salesforce) and of how bookings, ARR and retention are measured. Clear writing and presentation skills, and comfort taking a recommendation to executives and the field. Experience influencing executive decisions and leading cross-functional work without direct authority. Curiosity about software delivery and how engineering teams adopt tools, so pricing fits how customers use the product. Nice to have : Experience pricing developer platforms, infrastructure or observability products. Experience pricing AI products, including usage-based or consumption models. Background in sales compensation, deal desk or CPQ and billing systems. Experience running pricing or packaging changes through a customer migration. Familiarity with an experimentation or analytics tool such as LaunchDarkly itself.
