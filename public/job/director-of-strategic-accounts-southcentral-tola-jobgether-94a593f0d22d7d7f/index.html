Director of Strategic Accounts, South/Central (TOLA)

🏢 Jobgether 📍 US Only 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, which manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Director of Strategic Accounts, South/Central (TOLA) based in the United States. As Director of Strategic Accounts, you will lead enterprise sales across the South/Central (TOLA) region, driving business growth and establishing strategic relationships with some of the world's largest organizations. This is a hunter-focused role, offering the opportunity to build a sales pipeline from the ground up, develop new business opportunities, and close high-value enterprise deals. You will engage directly with senior security, IT, and technology leaders, introducing innovative AI-powered cybersecurity solutions to complex enterprise environments. Working closely with cross-functional teams, you will help shape sales strategies, refine market positioning, and influence product development through customer insights. This is an exciting opportunity to join a high-growth technology environment where entrepreneurial thinking, commercial ownership, and individual impact are highly valued. Accountabilities: Own the full enterprise sales cycle, from prospecting and lead generation to negotiation and closing, across strategic accounts in the South/Central (TOLA) region. Build, develop, and manage a strong sales pipeline, proactively identifying new business opportunities and securing net-new enterprise customers. Establish and nurture relationships with C-level executives and key decision-makers, including CISOs, CIOs, security architects, and leaders across IT, compliance, and engineering. Lead structured, consultative sales processes and navigate complex, multi-stakeholder enterprise purchasing cycles. Partner with Solutions Engineering teams to deliver tailored product demonstrations and manage proof-of-value (POV) engagements aligned with customer objectives and measurable business outcomes. Collaborate with Product and Engineering teams to communicate customer feedback, market intelligence, and emerging business needs, helping inform product development and roadmap priorities. Contribute to the continuous improvement of go-to-market strategies by providing insights on messaging, pricing, competitive positioning, and market opportunities. Take ownership of regional growth objectives while helping establish and scale an effective enterprise sales motion. Requirements: At least 8 years of experience in B2B enterprise software sales, with a demonstrated track record of closing six- and seven-figure deals with Global 2000 or Fortune 500 organizations. Proven hunter mentality, with the ability to independently generate pipeline, secure new enterprise customers, and consistently deliver against ambitious sales targets without relying heavily on inbound opportunities. Previous experience in a startup or high-growth technology environment, demonstrating the ability to build relationships, create opportunities, and close deals in markets where brand recognition alone does not drive sales. Background in cybersecurity or related technical security solutions, with the ability to engage confidently and credibly with CISOs, security architects, and Security Operations Center (SOC) leaders. Strong experience managing complex, consultative enterprise sales cycles involving multiple stakeholders and senior-level security and IT executives. Proficiency in established enterprise sales methodologies such as MEDDICC, MEDDPICC, Force Management, Challenger, or equivalent frameworks. Based in the South/Central United States, with strong knowledge of the regional enterprise market and an established network of relevant industry relationships. Excellent communication, negotiation, relationship-building, and presentation skills, combined with strong commercial judgment and strategic thinking. Ability to work autonomously, manage competing priorities, and thrive in a fast-paced, ambitious, and evolving environment. Preferred: Experience selling into organizations undergoing cybersecurity modernization or AI-driven transformation initiatives. Preferred: Familiarity with AI-native platforms, emerging technologies, or innovative cybersecurity solutions. Benefits: Competitive compensation package, including an attractive base salary and performance-based earning potential. Equity options, providing the opportunity to participate in the company's long-term growth and success. Opportunity to join a well-funded, high-growth technology business backed by $125 million in funding. Direct exposure to experienced founders and a world-class leadership team with extensive cybersecurity and technology expertise. Opportunity to collaborate with a dedicated AI research team working at the forefront of agentic AI innovation. High-impact position with significant ownership over regional sales strategy, enterprise relationships, and business development. Opportunity to influence go-to-market strategies, product positioning, and the evolution of an emerging technology category. Remote, field-based working arrangement within the South/Central United States. Dynamic and collaborative environment offering professional development, mentorship, and opportunities for career growth.
