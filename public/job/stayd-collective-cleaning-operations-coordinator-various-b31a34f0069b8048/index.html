Stayd Collective: Cleaning Operations Coordinator

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Argentina URL: https://stayd.co Cleaning Operations Coordinator Haven Full-time | Remote — LATAM Department : Housekeeping & Field Ops Schedule : Monday–Friday, 8:00am–5:00pm ET | Occasional weekend coverage as needed Reports to : COO, Cleaning Compensation : USD $1,200–$1,500 / month depending on experience. About the brand Haven manages cabins and mountain homes in the Great Smoky Mountains and around Knoxville, Tennessee. Steep driveways, hot tubs, wood stoves, and a season that swings from quiet weeks to a full house every weekend. It is a market where property care is guest experience: a cold hot tub is a ruined anniversary, and a turnover that runs late is a family waiting in the driveway. The role You are the person who makes sure every cabin is guest-ready before the next family arrives. You are the main communication point between Haven operations and our Tendwell cleaning teams. Your day starts with the departures, arrivals and cleaning schedule, and from there you follow up with cleaners on progress and ETAs, clear whatever is blocking a turn, coordinate re-cleans and supply fixes, and confirm each home is ready before a same-day check-in. When a guest checks out late, you work out what it does to the cleaning schedule and tell operations what timing is realistic. It is a role for someone who is highly responsive and calm under same-day pressure. Asking for an update is not the same as getting the home ready. If a turn is yours, you own it until it is confirmed complete. Where you sit You sit in Housekeeping & Field Ops and work every day with Tendwell cleaners and Haven’s operations team. This is not a role where you are handed a queue and left alone. This role is remote and based in LATAM, covering U.S. Eastern Time working hours. Because Stayd runs shared systems across every brand, a process you improve here tends to end up running in six other markets, which is the closest thing we have to a promotion path. What you’ll own Follow up with cleaners on turn status, ETAs and anything blocking completion. Coordinate re-cleans, missed items and supply fixes. Confirm guest-ready status for every same-day arrival. Assess how late checkouts affect the cleaning schedule and communicate realistic timelines. Relay delays, updates and completion details to Haven operations, escalating urgent issues promptly. Collect and post completion evidence, including photos where required. Keep communication with cleaners, internal teams and stakeholders clear and organized. Spot recurring issues and help improve cleaning workflows and SOPs. What you bring Experience in short-term rentals, hospitality, housekeeping coordination or field operations is strongly preferred. High responsiveness and the ability to manage urgent, same-day issues without losing track of the rest. Strong attention to detail. Comfort following up with field teams, again and again, until the job is done. Clear written English for communication with cleaners and the operations team. Availability to work U.S. Eastern Time hours, plus occasional weekend coverage. Reliable internet and a quiet place to work. Compensation & Benefits $1,200–$1,500/month, depending on experience. Fully remote, with flexible scheduling within U.S. time-zone overlap. A path to grow into a broader operations leadership role across the portfolio.
