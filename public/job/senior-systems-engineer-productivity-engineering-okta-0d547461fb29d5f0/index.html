The Senior Systems Engineer Opportunity As a Senior Systems Engineer at Okta, you will be at the heart of how our global workforce communicates, collaborates, and creates. You won’t just be &quot;keeping the lights on&quot;; you will be responsible for architecting workflows, securing our ecosystem, and ensuring that our stack - from Google Workspace to Slack and Gemini - works seamlessly for every employee, everywhere. What You’ll Be Doing Infrastructure Management: Administer and optimize our core productivity suite, including Google Workspace, Gemini, Slack, Asana, Smartsheet, and other tools. AI Integration & Strategy: Lead the deployment, management, and troubleshooting of enterprise AI tools (specifically Gemini), ensuring they are integrated safely and effectively into daily workflows. Enablement: Create technical documentation and partner with cross-functional teams to drive adoption and best practices for productivity tools through effective training and support. Global Support: Act as a Tier 3 escalation point for complex technical issues, supporting a fast-paced, 24/7 global environment from one of our US hubs. Lifecycle Management: Responsible for the complete lifecycle management of multiple enterprise SaaS applications. Governance & Standards: Define and enforce global standards for tool usage, security permissions, and data governance. Problem Solving & Innovation: Consistently look for root-cause trends while solving platform problems, devising solutions based on limited information and adapting existing approaches to resolve unusual SaaS circumstances. Process Automation: Identify manual bottlenecks and use APIs or scripting to automate repetitive administrative tasks. Data Insights & Goal Delivery: Independently draw out meaningful insights from system data to own and deliver quarterly goals on time with a high standard of quality. What You’ll Bring to the Role Technical Expertise: Proven deep experience managing enterprise-level Google Workspace (DNS, mail routing, Vault, DLP) and administrative experience with tools such as Slack, Asana, Jira, ServiceNow, and Smartsheet. AI Proficiency: Practical experience managing and deploying Gemini and other AI-driven productivity interfaces within a corporate framework. SaaS Architecture & Security: Possess complete knowledge of SaaS principles, API integrations, and RBAC security best practices, acting as the fully qualified professional to resolve complex issues creatively. Operational Agility: Experience working in a fast-paced environment supporting a distributed, global company. Governance Mindset: A track record of implementing &quot;least privilege&quot; access models and driving standards in usage and best practices. Automation and Scripting Skills: Proficiency in Apps Script, Python, or Bash to manage large-scale migrations or system integrations. Communication: Excellent verbal and written skills to lead training and influence standard usage across the org. Stakeholder Alignment: Enhance networks with senior internal partners who may be unfamiliar with SaaS technicalities and use persuasion to align stakeholders on new processes. Leadership & Peer Support: Assist leadership with forming teams that have a diverse mix of perspectives, provide specific, actionable, and timely feedback to peers, and demonstrate resilience under pressure. Education & Experience: Requires 5+ years of related experience in a professional role. What you can look forward to as an Okta employee!
