Overview The Developer Experience team is the force multiplier for Instacart's engineers, owning the platforms, tools, and practices that take a change from an engineer's laptop to production. As R&D shifts to an increasingly agentic way of working, the systems around shipping code, including code review, CI, and build infrastructure, must handle a far greater volume of changes without cost and time scaling linearly. As a Staff Software Engineer, you will lead the technical direction for that transformation, supporting 1,400 engineers today and all of R&D as it grows to roughly 1,800 people in 2027. This is a hands-on technical leadership role: you will write code, set architectural direction, and partner with engineering leaders across the company. About the Job Lead the technical direction for local and remote development environments, including ephemeral environments, image-based provisioning, health checks, and the inner development loop, with a focus on cutting the time it takes to get a working remote environment. Lead the technical direction for CI pipeline infrastructure and the commit-to-feedback loop, including CI reliability, failure diagnostics, flaky-test reduction, and shift-left validation that gives engineers faster feedback before they push upstream. Keep CI cost and failure rates flat or falling while the volume of changes grows 2x or more, and reduce our dependence on external providers for build and CI processes. Own the architecture and evolution of our pre-production environments, including staging stability, service-owner accountability, and environments that let engineers and AI agents validate changes end to end in parallel. Drive the reliability and resilience of our git infrastructure at monorepo scale, including disaster recovery plans. Advance AI-powered developer tooling across the workflow, including AI-assisted code review, agentic development workflows, and platforms that let engineers run many AI sessions in parallel. Design and drive multi-quarter initiatives that measurably improve developer velocity, CI reliability, pre-production health, and the local development loop, and lead a small team of engineers on a one-year effort to revamp our developer experience systems. Own the developer experience metrics for the systems you lead: define what good looks like, instrument it, and close the loop by confirming that the developer pain that prompted the work actually went away. Partner with infrastructure, platform, and product engineering teams, and with vendors such as Buildkite, GitHub, Docker, and AWS, to roll out platform changes with minimal disruption. Set the technical bar for reliability, performance, upgrade cadence, and developer ergonomics, and mentor engineers across the broader engineering organization. About You Minimum Qualifications 10+ years of software development experience. Deep, hands-on experience operating CI/CD systems at scale (e.g., Buildkite, GitHub Actions, Jenkins, CircleCI), including pipeline design, reliability, and performance tuning for large engineering organizations. Experience building or operating local/remote development environments or developer platforms (e.g., ephemeral environments, dev containers, internal PaaS), with a point of view on the inner development loop. Track record of leading cross-team technical initiatives with measurable impact on developer velocity, CI reliability, pre-production stability, or feedback-loop time. Experience with cloud infrastructure (AWS preferred), containerization (Docker), and orchestration (ECS/Kubernetes or equivalent). Experience with large monorepos and the git infrastructure challenges that come with them. Strong technical leadership: driving direction, mentoring engineers, and influencing without direct authority. Preferred Qualifications Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or equivalent practical experience. Experience owning or evolving a developer environment platform used by a large engineering org. Experience stabilizing pre-production / staging environments at scale, including establishing service-owner accountability and SLAs. Experience with AI-powered developer tooling, such as AI code review, agentic coding workflows, or platforms that run async agentic work for engineers. Experience defining and instrumenting developer productivity metrics (CI failure rate, feedback-loop time, pre-production pass rate, local dev loop).
