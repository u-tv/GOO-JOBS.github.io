Affirm: Affirm Bank Accounting Lead

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote US At Affirm, we exist for the moments that matter—giving people a clear, predictable way to pay over time, with no hidden fees, no surprises, and no tradeoffs on what matters most. About the Finance team The Finance team ensures Affirm remains financially sound and strategically positioned for growth. Our team manages financial planning, accounting, pricing, vendor management, tax, investor relations, and corporate development. We deliver timely insights, accurate reporting, and careful analysis to support decision-making. From day-to-day financial operations to major investments, we enable sustainable, informed growth by maintaining strong fiscal discipline. About the role W e are looking for a self-motivated, hardworking, and analytically minded professional to join the Bank Record to Report (Controllership) team during a pivotal moment of growth! The Record to Report team is a growing function within the Bank CFO department, responsible for core day-to-day accounting. As Affirm Bank is currently in its formation phase, we are actively building out our foundational team. Reporting to the Bank Controller, the Accounting Lead will play a key role in the formation and operational build-out of the Bank, including the G/L and daily operational flows, ensuring successful integration of our accounting systems and processes into Affirm’s systems and processes, and developing the regulatory function. Working alongside the Affirm, Inc. Accounting function, we ensure all business transactions are recorded accurately and timely, for ultimate reporting to management, regulators, and the Bank board. What you’ll do You will prepare workpapers, journal entries, and reconciliations for key accounts in the areas of deposits and other Bank activities You will work closely with cross-functional teams to document and incorporate new transactions or processes into the GL You will project-manage cross-functional close improvement efforts as an individual contributor You will conduct flux analyses on your assigned accounts and assist in building a controlled regulatory reporting function to ensure the timely and accurate preparation of required filings (e.g., Call Reports and Federal Reserve reporting) You will help author procedural documentation and work to improve daily, monthly, and quarterly processes You will review work prepared by Staff Accountants and assist in mentoring and coordinating members of the GL team on projects You will serve as a liaison with cross-functional teams while maintaining the independent oversight necessary for a regulated banking entity What we look for You will serve as a liaison with cross-functional teams while maintaining the independent oversight necessary for a regulated banking entity You have recent experience in general ledger accounting within banking or financial services, with extensive knowledge of GAAP and regulatory reporting requirements You are adaptable and will enjoy working on a rapidly growing team You are curious and love to find new ways of understanding problems or improving processes You demonstrate strong analytical and problem-solving skills and can communicate your thinking clearly You hold a Bachelor’s Degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field, and an accounting designation such as CPA is preferred Base Pay Grade - I Equity Grade - USA 5 USA base pay range (CA, WA, NY, NJ, CT) per year: $115,000 - $165,000 USA base pay range (all other U.S. states) per year: $101,000 - $151,000 Employees new to Affirm typically come in at the start of the pay range.
