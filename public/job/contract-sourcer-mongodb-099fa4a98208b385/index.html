Job Description

As a Contract Sourcer on our Talent Discovery team, you will be the pipeline generation engine supporting MongoDB's most critical hiring needs across the business. You will have the unique opportunity to become a sourcing subject matter expert, recruiting for technical and non-technical roles alike. In this role, you will identify, engage, and qualify top talent. This opportunity is ideal for individuals who embrace change, thrive in a fast-paced, ever-evolving environment, and are excited to grow their careers in recruiting while expanding their expertise across a broad range of functions as business priorities evolve. We are looking to speak to candidates who are based in Gurugram for our hybrid working model. In this role, you will Support critical MongoDB roles globally through strategic passive talent sourcing. Individuals will be expected to quickly pivot to support new areas of MongoDB’s business as hiring needs arise, and show flexibility to take on various positions across Go-To-Market and Technology teams as needed Work closely with recruiters to understand business needs and deliver qualified talent accordingly, becoming an expert in the external market and a consultative talent advisor Screen candidates for nuanced roles, ensuring strong alignment to role requirements and maintaining exceptional documentation and data integrity as candidates are progressed within the hiring process Partner with coordinators, hiring managers and interviewers to deliver an exceptional candidate experience, maintaining a high-touch and personalized process for all candidates The right person for this role will Possess a proven track-record within technical recruiting, maintaining deep expertise in passive candidate sourcing specifically Maintain creative and out-of-the-box sourcing techniques to tap into new pools of talent, while uncovering new tactics through research and knowledge-sharing with peers Have an analytical mindset, with the ability to conduct autonomous research and utilize data to inform strategy and influence stakeholders Exhibit exceptional organizational and time-management skills, with previous experience in high-volume business environments.
