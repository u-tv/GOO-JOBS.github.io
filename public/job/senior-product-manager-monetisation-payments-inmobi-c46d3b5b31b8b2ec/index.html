To learn more, visit glance.com. About Accelerate: Accelerate is InMobi’s AI-native, self-serve campaign management platform for SMBs, D2C brands, agencies and enterprise advertisers. An advertiser describes their brand and goals; Accelerate’s agents plan, create, launch and optimize campaigns across ad platforms such as TikTok and OpenAI, with more supply integrations on the way. We are building toward a fully autonomous marketing platform. The product team is organized by capability, not segment. This role sits on the demand side, reporting to the Group Product Manager, alongside Customer Experience Core (campaign creation, onboarding and activation). About the role: You will build Accelerate’s payments and money infrastructure from zero. Every dollar or rupee an advertiser puts into Accelerate flows through what you build: how they fund their account, how spend is drawn down across ad platforms, how they are invoiced, refunded and taxed, and how we settle with suppliers. There is no legacy system to inherit. You will define the wallet, the ledger behind it, and the integration with our third-party payments partner (Vyre), then own the revenue metrics that depend on them. Getting this right is what lets an SMB go from signup to live campaign in minutes, and what lets finance trust every number on the dashboard. You will work with a Senior Product Associate on the pod, engineering, Central Design, and InMobi finance, tax and legal teams. What you will own: Advertiser wallet. A prepaid balance advertisers top up and Accelerate draws down as campaigns spend. Includes funding methods, auto-recharge, low-balance alerts, credit lines for larger accounts, and the rules for how a balance can and cannot be used. Ledger and reconciliation. The source of truth for every credit, debit, hold, refund and adjustment. Spend reported by each ad platform must reconcile to the ledger, with clear handling for mismatches. Payments integration. End-to-end integration with Vyre, our third-party payments partner: collections, payment methods, failures and retries, webhooks, and the fallback plan if a provider is down. Billing, invoicing and tax. Invoices, receipts, GST, TDS on ad spend, and multi-currency handling as we add international advertisers. Supplier settlement. How we pay out and reconcile with ad platforms, working with the business team that handles supplier contracts. Segment needs. SMBs prepaying by card or UPI, agencies funding many client accounts from one place, and enterprise clients on invoiced terms. Revenue metrics. Top-up conversion, payment success rate, time to first funded campaign, wallet balance retention, and reconciliation accuracy. Every revenue number in Accelerate’s success framework depends on your system. What we’re looking for: 6+ years of product management, with at least 3 years owning payments, wallets, billing or ledger systems. You have built a payments or wallet system from scratch, or led its first major version. Scaling a mature system alone is not enough. Deep understanding of double-entry ledgers, idempotency, reconciliation, refunds and chargebacks. You can sit with engineers and discuss failure modes, not just flows. Hands-on experience integrating a third-party payment provider or aggregator end to end. Working knowledge of Indian payments regulation and tax: RBI rules on prepaid instruments, GST, TDS. You know when to bring in legal and what to ask them. You work AI-native (see “How we work” below). You prototype, write and reason with LLMs as part of your daily work. Clear written communication. You can explain a ledger design to an engineer, a finance controller and an SMB owner, each in their own terms. Strong pluses You built a wallet or stored-balance feature inside a non-fintech product, such as a marketplace, mobility, food delivery or SaaS platform. Experience with ad-platform billing (prepaid, postpaid or credit-line models) or marketplace payouts. Experience with cross-border payments, multi-currency balances or FX. Experience with B2B billing for agencies or multi-entity accounts. Some time as an engineer, or comfort reading API docs and querying data directly. How we work: Accelerate is an AI-native product, and we build it the AI-native way. Every PM on the demand side is expected to: Prototype before you spec. Turn an idea into a clickable prototype with AI tools in hours, and test it with users and the team before writing requirements. Write and think with LLMs. Draft PRDs, edge cases, test cases and analyses with an LLM as a working partner. Then apply your own judgment to what it gets wrong. Hand off with less back-and-forth. Give engineering and design specs precise enough to build from, with prototypes and acceptance criteria attached, so fewer decisions get made in meetings. Stay close to the data. Pull and analyze data yourself rather than waiting on a dashboard request. Bring design sense. Care about the details of the customer experience and partner with design as a peer, not a requester.
