Headquarters: Within the client's Global Information Services (GIS) organization is a team of dedicated resources to build a digital center of excellence. These digital offerings connect our customers and partners to our products, services, solutions, and talent, making it simple and easy for the customer to choose OC – driving an increase in engagement, loyalty, retention, and sales. The Stibo Platform Architect will work closely with our digital center of excellence, integration architects, enterprise architects, IT product owners, business digital product managers and partners to develop a deep understanding of our business strategies and our user journeys Leveraging this understanding, the Platform Owner – Stibo is responsible for establishing a common framework to build and run the product data, asset management, syndication of data, and product visualization tools supporting the company’s initiatives across our market facing, digital worker and manufacturing digital portfolios. This role will set the vision and strategy needed to ensure rapid, iterative development and global deployment of industry best features for each of these critical areas. Specifically ensuring we have a comprehensive, nimble, modern, secure, modular, and scalable platform that we can leverage across businesses and global regions. This role will also promote education and drive consensus in setting our platform standards and direction across the GIS organization and with our key partners.
