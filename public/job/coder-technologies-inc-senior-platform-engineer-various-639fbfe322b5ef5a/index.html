Coder Technologies Inc.: Senior Platform Engineer

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: United States As a Senior Platform Engineer at Coder, you will own the infrastructure Coder's own engineers build on every day. Our internal environments run the same deployment path we publish for customers, so when something does not hold up at our scale, we find it before a customer does. We avoid building anything so specific to ourselves that a customer could not run the same thing. Platform Engineering owns three areas. We run the internal environments our engineers develop in, the self-service environments our field teams use to demo Coder, and the release pipeline that publishes every Coder artifact. You will take ownership of components across these and stay accountable for how they behave in production after they ship. Our users are Coder’s engineers and field teams, and you’ll work with them to decide what to build and how we know it worked. What you'll do here Own one or more components of our Kubernetes platform end to end, from the Terraform and Flux configuration to how they run in production. Improve the platform's observability, so that metrics, logs and alerts land in one stack with a runbook behind every alert. Keep our Kubernetes clusters current, including version and add-on upgrades. Apply cost and performance efficiency to our infrastructure. Work on workspace isolation and container runtimes. Contribute to the release and publishing pipeline for coder/coder, including its workflows, signing and publish targets. Design for least privilege, with short-lived credentials and no shared static keys. Write an RFC when a change warrants one, and a pull request a reviewer can follow without decoding it. Mentor one or two engineers on the components you know best. Run pilots with named users, gather feedback, and measure adoption and time to a useful result (e.g. from request to an environment someone can actually demo on) Own docs, onboarding and support for what you ship. What we're looking for 4+ years in infrastructure or platform engineering, with direct ownership of production systems other people depend on. Strong technical depth across Linux, Kubernetes, containers and Infrastructure as Code, Terraform in particular. Production experience with at least one major public cloud. Most of our infrastructure runs on AWS. Hands-on experience operating Kubernetes clusters, including upgrades, add-ons, autoscaling and the failure modes that come with them. Experience building and owning CI/CD pipelines, and treating them as production software. A working understanding of IP networking and HTTP fundamentals. Has changed or retired a platform feature based on how people used it, or can describe how they would decide to. Good instincts for where cloud spend goes and what a change will cost. Clear written communication. Most of this team's work is agreed in a document before it is built. Bonus tacos if you have (Tacos? If you need an ice-breaker, ask how we say thanks by giving tacos!) GitOps at scale with Flux or Argo CD. Prometheus, Grafana or OpenTelemetry, including alerts you defined and owned. Sandboxed container runtimes such as Kata, gVisor or sysbox. Release engineering, artifact signing or supply chain security. Prior work on developer tooling or internal developer platforms. Public open source contributions. You still carve out time to code, either at work or on passion projects. Our tech stack Backend: Go, Postgres, Kubernetes, gRPC Infrastructure: AWS, Terraform, Flux, Helm Observability: Prometheus, Grafana CI/CD: GitHub Actions General: GitHub, Slack, Discord, Notion About Coder Coder is the leading platform for AI development Infrastructure, enabling enterprises to securely run human and AI-driven development workflows in consistent, governed environments. Coder provides self-hosted, agent-ready workspaces that unify developer productivity and platform governance. With Coder, enterprises can confidently evolve from human-only development to AI-assisted and autonomous workflows, without sacrificing security, compliance, or performance. Learn more at coder.com .
