Senior Software Engineer, Agentic AI

🏢 Clover Health 📍 US Only 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Clover is reinventing health insurance by working to keep people healthier. We value diversity in backgrounds and experiences. Healthcare is a universal concern, and we need people from all backgrounds and walks of life to help build the future of healthcare. Clover's engineering team is empathetic, caring, and supportive. We are deliberate about the team and culture we are building. We seek engineers who are strong in their own work and care deeply about supporting each other's growth. We are looking for a senior software engineer to make Counterpart Health's existing customer-facing platform agentic. You will build applied AI systems that retrieve relevant information, call tools, and help healthcare users complete meaningful work. You will work with engineers, data scientists, and healthcare professionals, with ownership across data pipelines, backend services, model behavior, and the user experience. As a Senior Software Engineer, you will: Simplify the complexities of healthcare by building systems that multiply human efforts. Build and maintain customer-facing agentic AI software, with ownership from data ingestion through to the user interface. Develop retrieval-augmented generation, or RAG, pipelines for ingestion, ETL, storage, indexing, retrieval, and context assembly. Build agent workflows that call tools and use application services, with clear permissions, execution limits, and human review where needed. Choose models, storage, retrieval, and orchestration patterns based on product needs and measured results. Protect PHI and tenant boundaries across data, prompts, retrieval, tools, and outputs in a HIPAA-regulated environment. Use customer feedback and failure analysis to improve answers and tool execution. Measure and improve quality, reliability, latency, and cost. Master new tools and technologies to solve concrete user problems while keeping the system simple enough for the team to maintain and operate. Help the team define development practices that support fast iteration and quality, including tests and documentation of key implementations. Work with Product Managers and operational staff to choose useful workflows, define success, and deliver features to customers. You should get in touch if: You have personally owned a production agentic system used by external customers. You can explain your contribution across ingestion, ETL, storage, retrieval, the tool-calling loop, inference, and UI integration. You have used evaluations to improve a live system. Can explain the test data, failure categories, release criteria, and limits of automated or LLM-as-judge scoring. You bring senior software engineering experience and can write, test, debug, and operate the application code behind an AI feature. Python and TypeScript or JavaScript experience are useful, as is comparable experience in another production language. You have experience with SQL, APIs, data pipelines, and cloud services such as GCP or AWS. You have handled practical LLM limitations, such as context-window limits, unsupported answers, retrieval failures, and incorrect tool calls. Experience with healthcare or another regulated industry is a plus. Experience with tools such as LangGraph, Langfuse, pgvector, or OpenSearch is useful, but we care more about your engineering decisions and production results than a particular framework. Using AI coding assistants, building demos, or shipping internal tools alone does not meet the production AI experience required for this role. You will love this job if: You are curious about how systems work and use pragmatic engineering to solve complicated problems. You test whether an AI approach improves the workflow and use simpler software when it is the better choice. You enjoy a collaborative environment where you help define priorities and own delivery. You bring clarity to ambiguity and change your view when new evidence calls for it. You take responsibility for what reaches customers. You investigate broken behavior, fix the cause, and improve the tests and evaluations that should catch it. You care about what good technology can do for people and want to work with healthcare professionals on tools they can trust.
