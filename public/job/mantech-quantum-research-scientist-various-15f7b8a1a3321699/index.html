MANTECH: Quantum Research Scientist

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: United States URL: https://www.MANTECH.com Description & Requirements Elevate your career with MANTECH International Corporation! Join a dynamic team dedicated to national security through cutting-edge technology. Since 1968, MANTECH has led in delivering advanced solutions to government intelligence, the Department of Defense, and Federal Civilian sectors. Dive into innovation in Digital Transformation, Cybersecurity, IT, Data Analytics and Software Development. Your journey to impactful work and rapid growth starts now—be extraordinary at MANTECH! ***This is for a future opportunity*** MANTECH seeks a motivated, career and customer-oriented Quantum Research Scientist to join our team in a remote role. The Quantum Research Scientist will advance quantum information science and optical quantum systems, leveraging expertise in continuous-variable photonics and theoretical algorithm discovery to translate complex physical phenomena into experimental prototypes and publishable research. Responsibilities include but are not limited to: Conducting foundational and applied research to advance quantum information science, with a specialized focus on continuous-variable (CV) photonic quantum computing. Leading theoretical algorithm discovery, establishing novel qubit modalities, and engineering advanced error-correction schemes tailored for optical and photonic quantum systems. Prioritizing experimental physics integration within photonics modalities over alternative architectures. Translating complex mathematical models and quantum physical phenomena into experimental prototypes, technical specifications, and publishable findings. Collaborating closely with academic partners and cross-functional engineering teams to map cross-platform control policies to alternative modalities. Documenting research progress and disseminating findings through technical papers, presentations, and peer-reviewed publications. Minimum Qualifications: Master’s degree in Physics, Applied Physics, Mathematics, or a related technical discipline. 4+ years of professional experience working in quantum computing or quantum information science. Demonstrated foundational research experience in theoretical algorithm discovery, novel qubit modalities, quantum error-correction schemes, or experimental quantum physics. Demonstrated experience translating complex mathematical models and physical phenomena into experimental prototypes or technical specifications. Ability to travel up to 25% within the Continental U.S. Preferred Qualifications: PhD in Quantum Physics, Atomic Physics, Condensed Matter, or Computer Science with a Quantum Information Science focus. A robust peer-review record, industry recognition, patents, or publications in top-tier journals (e.g., Nature, PRX, IEEE). Professional certifications such as IBM Certified Quantum Computation using Qiskit Developer, MIT xPro Quantum Computing Fundamentals, or Advanced HPC/GPU Acceleration (e.g., NVIDIA CUDA). Experience applying machine learning and AI algorithms to quantum control or complex physical simulations. Clearance Requirements: Must be a US Citizen. Physical Requirements: Must be able to remain in a stationary position 50% of the time. Must be able to communicate, converse, and exchange information with peers and senior personnel. Constantly operates a computer and other office productivity machinery. The projected compensation range for this position is $94,800.00-$157,200.00.
