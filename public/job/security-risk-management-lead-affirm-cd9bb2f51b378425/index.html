At Affirm, we exist for the moments that matter—giving people a clear, predictable way to pay over time, with no hidden fees, no surprises, and no tradeoffs on what matters most. BEGIN JOB DESCRIPTION HERE Security Risk Management Lead Affirm is reinventing credit to make it more honest and friendly, giving consumers the flexibility to buy now and pay later without any hidden fees or compounding interest. Affirm values security as being critical to the company’s continued success. Our mission is to cultivate a culture of security at Affirm, enabling the company to succeed in building honest financial products. The Security Risk Management team builds and deploys common governance, risk, and compliance processes and controls, conducts audits, and ensures that technologies and business processes are built with data protection and compliance in mind. The ideal candidate will design, develop, configure, and implement solutions to complex technical and business issues related to the Security Third Party Program and broader Security Risk Management program. They will work across highly complex functions, interface professionally with business stakeholders, and identify and recommend scalable functional, technological, and control solutions. They should be able to navigate ambiguity, drive multiple initiatives simultaneously, and act as a subject matter expert (SME) within the Security Risk Management team. This person will play a key role in evolving and automating the Security Third Party Program through thoughtful process design, workflow automation, and cross-functional partnership. What You'll Do Lead and mature Affirm’s Security Third Party Program, including the design, implementation, and continuous improvement of processes, controls, and operational workflows Partner closely
