Commercial Counsel, Enterprise

🏢 datadog 📍 New York, New York, USA 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

This opportunity provides a platform for professional growth and the chance to play a critical role in the success of a rapidly growing and innovative SaaS company. In this role, you will continue developing your negotiation and communication skills in support of sophisticated enterprise deals, while also having the opportunity to influence and refine Datadog's business and operational policies. The role involves close collaboration with cross-functional teams such as sales, deal desk, privacy, customer trust, and product legal, serving as a trusted advisor who provides strong solutions and practical advice. This position offers exposure to cutting-edge SaaS technology and the evolving legal issues around it, including in areas such as privacy, security, and AI/ML, allowing you to stay at the forefront of the industry. At Datadog, we place value in our office culture - the relationships and collaboration it builds and the creativity it brings to the table. We operate as a hybrid workplace to ensure our Datadogs can create a work-life harmony that best fits them. What You’ll Do: Independently review and negotiate customer subscription agreements and ancillary documents such as DPAs and BAAs. Partner with a seasoned legal team to provide practical and decisive guidance to internal business teams. Coach sales team members on strategies for negotiating with customers. Support the ongoing improvement of our contract templates, playbooks, and processes. Collaborate with other teams within the legal department and across the company to support Datadog’s revenue growth and reduction of risk by being a trusted advisor, translating business needs into contractual commitments and legal advice. Who You Are: Have at least 4 years of law firm and/or in-house commercial transactional experience, including substantive experience negotiating SaaS contracts. Someone who is looking to expand their experience and skills in negotiating, communicating, and educating. Someone with integrity, excellent judgment, and a high degree of emotional intelligence; you are prepared to build strong collaboration channels with peers and stakeholders across the business and ready to identify pathways to success with our customers. Comfortable working independently, adhering to established processes, and able to recognize when you need guidance. Excited to drive contracts to closure and comfortable partnering with clients in sales, where you will prioritize multiple deals simultaneously while supporting tight deadlines. Familiar with SaaS and related technology areas, including privacy, information security, and intellectual property. Licensed to practice law and have earned a JD and bar admission in the U.S. Able to work out of our New York office. Datadog values people from all walks of life. We understand not everyone will meet all the above qualifications on day one. That's okay. If you’re passionate about technology and want to grow your skills, we encourage you to apply.
