About role We are looking for a high-ownership Central Growth manager to drive measurable monetization outcomes across the Paytm consumer app. This role will own the “growth & monetization levers to increase ARPU, engagement, and category adoption, while maintaining tight ROI discipline. The mandate is to build a stronger, more profitable consumer growth business.
