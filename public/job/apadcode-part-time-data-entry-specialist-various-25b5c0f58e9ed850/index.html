APADCode: Part-Time Data Entry Specialist

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Bangalore, Karnataka, India URL: https://apadcode.com About APADCode APADCode is a technology and business solutions company providing software development, data management, and operational support services to businesses. We are looking for reliable, detail-oriented individuals to join our team as Part-Time Data Entry Specialists. This is an excellent opportunity for individuals seeking flexible remote work. No technical background or previous professional experience is required. Basic computer literacy, good English communication skills, and a willingness to learn are all you need to get started. Key Responsibilities Enter, update, and maintain information in spreadsheets, databases, and internal systems. Review and verify data for accuracy and completeness. Organize digital documents, records, and files. Perform basic online research and information collection. Follow established procedures and complete assigned tasks within deadlines. Communicate with team members regarding task progress and updates. Maintain confidentiality and handle business information responsibly. Requirements English Proficiency: Upper-intermediate (B2) level or higher, with good written and verbal communication skills. Computer Skills: Basic proficiency in using computers, web browsers, email, and spreadsheets. Internet Connection: Reliable, stable internet connection suitable for remote work. Equipment: Access to a personal laptop or desktop computer. Availability: Ability to commit 10–20 hours per week. Attention to Detail: Ability to enter and review information accurately. Time Management: Capable of working independently and meeting deadlines. Reliability: Responsible, organized, and responsive to communication. No programming skills, technical qualifications, or previous data entry experience are required. What We Offer Competitive hourly compensation of $10–$25 USD, depending on qualifications and responsibilities. Flexible part-time schedule. Fully remote working environment. Training and guidance for assigned tasks. Opportunities to gain experience in business operations and data management. Potential for ongoing collaboration for various interesting projects based on performance and business needs. How to Apply Interested candidates should submit a brief introduction, an updated resume (if available), their English proficiency level, and their weekly availability. We welcome applications from students, recent graduates, career changers, and individuals looking for additional part-time income. APADCode, Inc — Equal Opportunity Employer We evaluate applicants based on their qualifications, communication skills, reliability, and ability to perform the responsibilities of the role. To apply: https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/apadcode-part-time-data-entry-specialist
