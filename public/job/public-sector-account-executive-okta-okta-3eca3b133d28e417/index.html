Okta is The World’s Identity Company Okta frees everyone to safely use any technology, anywhere, on any device or app. Our flexible and neutral platform places identity at the core of security, productivity, and growth. As organisations modernise their security architecture and accelerate AI adoption, identity has become the critical control plane. Okta Workforce Identity helps organisations secure access for employees, contractors, partners, privileged users, applications, devices, and non-human identities such as service accounts, automation tools, API keys, and AI agents. Join our team. We’re building a world where Identity belongs to you. The Okta Workforce Identity Sales Team Okta Workforce Identity helps organisations securely connect their people to the technology they need to do their best work. We help customers strengthen authentication, govern access, automate identity processes, reduce risk, and improve user experience at scale. As AI adoption grows, organisations must secure not only human users, but also the non-human identities powering automation and AI. Okta helps customers build a trusted identity foundation for secure AI adoption through visibility, governance, least-privileged access, and control across human and non-human identities. As a Public Sector Account Executive, you will drive territory growth through net-new logo acquisition and expansion across government and highly regulated customers. You will work closely with the broader Okta ecosystem, including xDRs, Solutions Engineering, Partners, Customer First, and Professional Services, to help customers modernise identity and strengthen cyber resilience.
