Engineering Manager, Query Integration

🏢 mongodb 📍 Austin; New York City; Palo Alto; San Francisco; United States 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Join the MongoDB Server Query team, and help us build a world-class distributed open source query engine. Our team plays a crucial role in the experience and performance of data processing. We are responsible for the MongoDB Query Language and the lifecycle of each query, from parsing to optimization to plan selection and execution. Also included are user-facing features that integrate into the query engine, such as full-text search, vector search, and workload observability. Our global team is growing fast, and we are looking for people leaders as part of this expansion. We support office-based as well as remote work, and align projects with convenient work hours for each time-zone. We have tons of interesting problems to solve with direct impact on users for transactional, time-series, and analytical workloads. As we grow, we need engineering managers to help us structure the organization, improve technical processes, provide career mentorship, guide the product roadmap, and manage projects. This role can be based out of our New York City office, Austin office, Palo Alto office, or San Francisco Office or remotely in the US.
