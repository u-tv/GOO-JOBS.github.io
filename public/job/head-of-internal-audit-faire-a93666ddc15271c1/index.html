Head of Internal Audit

🏢 faire 📍 San Francisco, CA 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Faire Faire is a technology wholesale platform built on the belief that the future is local. Independent retailers around the globe collectively represent a multi-hundred-billion-dollar wholesale market that has historically been fragmented and offline. At Faire, we're using the power of tech, data, and machine learning to connect this thriving community of entrepreneurs across the globe. Picture your favorite boutique in town — we help them discover the best products from around the world to sell in their stores. With the right tools and insights, we believe that we can level the playing field so businesses can grow and local communities can thrive. We’re looking for smart, resourceful and passionate people to join us as we power the shop local movement. If you believe in community, come join ours. About this role We're looking for a Head of Internal Audit to build Faire's Internal Audit function and lead our SOX (financial controls) program as we prepare for public company requirements. You’ll partner with process owners across the company to make sure our key controls are well designed, operating effectively, and supported by complete and accurate data. This role owns the audit plan, is an independent voice on risk and controls, and partners closely with the Audit Committee. You'll inherit a small SOX team and shape how it's structured as Internal Audit grows, with scope that may expand into areas like enterprise risk management. You'll also help govern how Faire builds and uses AI, and use data and AI to make audit faster and more continuous. As Finance and the business rebuild workflows around AI, you’ll make sure those workflows are SOX compliant by design: advising on controls throughout the build process, setting the standard for evidence and review, and giving the Audit Committee and our external auditor confidence in the results. You understand our technology environment, have built or scaled an audit function at a fast-growing tech company, and are ready to roll up your sleeves. You see the Internal Audit function as a partner to the business, not a checkpoint. This role reports to the CAO, with a functional reporting line to the CFO and the Audit Committee of the Board. What you’ll do Build and lead Faire's Internal Audit function, including the audit plan, team, and outside partners Lead our SOX team and own the SOX program, including readiness for our first external audit of internal controls Review controls over our technology: cloud, software changes, system access, data, and cybersecurity Provide independent assurance over how Faire builds and uses AI, including model errors, bias, and privacy Modernize audit with data analytics, automation, and responsible AI to test more and monitor continuously Give the Audit Committee and executives a clear, candid view of risk and control health Partner with Finance, Legal, Security, Privacy, and Engineering to advise on controls early for new products, systems, and expansion Track issues to closure and push for root-cause fixes Hire, coach, and develop the team and manage outside audit partners Take on special projects, analysis, and reporting as needed Qualifications Bachelor's degree in accounting, finance, information systems, or a related field, plus at least one of CPA, CIA, CISA, or CISSP 12+ years in audit and risk, including 6+ reviewing IT and financial controls and 3+ leading teams Deep SOX experience, including IPO readiness or first-year external auditor review of internal controls Strong understanding of the technology environment, including IT controls, cloud, data, and cybersecurity Working knowledge of AI risk and governance, including generative and agentic AI, and experience using AI or analytics in audit Knowledge of US GAAP, COSO, PCAOB, and IIA standards Experience presenting to Audit Committees, boards, and senior executives Experience selecting audit and risk technology Experience building an internal audit function at a tech company; hyper-growth or marketplace experience is a plus Strong communicator with sound judgment in ambiguous situations Resourceful and not afraid to roll up your sleeves Salary range: San Francisco: The salary range for this role is $200,000 - $275,000 per year.
