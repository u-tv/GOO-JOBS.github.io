Assistant Program Manager – Fulfilment & Experience (Strategic Initiatives) ( Kirana Club)

🏢 meesho 📍 Bangalore, Karnataka 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

About Kirana Club India has over 12 million kirana stores, and most still depend on traditional wholesale markets to source products for their shops. Kirana Club is changing how these retailers discover, source, and replenish inventory. With 4 million+ kiranas on the platform, we're building a commerce marketplace that connects retailers directly with brands and sellers. Now part of the Meesho family, we're entering our next phase of growth, with an opportunity to rethink how B2B commerce and fulfilment work at scale. About the role We're looking for someone who enjoys solving complex business problems, is exceptionally comfortable with numbers, and can turn ideas into real-world outcomes. You'll work on some of the most important medium-term initiatives in our fulfilment and supply chain business — projects that can fundamentally improve our cost structure, delivery experience, and ability to serve retailers across India. This is a high-ownership individual contributor role, not a traditional operations management position.
