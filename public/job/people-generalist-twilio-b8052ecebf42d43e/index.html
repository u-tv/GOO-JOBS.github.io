See yourself at Twilio Join the team as Twilio’s next People Generalist. About the job This position is needed to support delivery of People priorities. This role supports a highly senior organization consisting of approximately 300 Product Management employees. You will be an integral part of the People Generalists team, reporting to the Global People Generalists Manager, and partnering closely with and supporting the work of international and functional HRBPs and COEs across the People team. Twilio is looking for a People Generalist who lives the Twilio Magic and has a demonstrated track record of providing advice and assistance with HR queries in a wide variety of areas including benefits & compensation, offboarding, global mobility and employee relations. Responsibilities In this role, you’ll: Partner with the HRBP team supporting our GTM and Marketing functions to support program roll-outs and on-going process execution Analyse HR data and trends across the organisation they support (e.g. headcount, attrition, performance) to generate insights, trends and prepare clear and customised reports, metrics and presentation materials for HRBPs and senior leadership to support decision-making Own and manage performance management cases end-to-end, partnering with managers to ensure fair, consistent, and timely outcomes in line with company policy Coach and guide managers through the performance management process, providing advice, documentation support, and best practice to build manager capability and ensure a positive employee experience Support HR Leader on ad hoc projects Help to execute and where required lead business led initiatives (re-orgs, job role and level assessments, etc) Partner cross functionally with key stakeholders: Talent Management, IT, ER, HR operations, Compliance, Recruiting, Compensation, etc. to ensure communications, program, and process alignment Program Management- annual cyclical events such as calibration, pulse surveys etc Maintain confidentiality of core HR data and projects Qualifications Twilio values diverse experiences from all kinds of industries, and we encourage everyone who meets the required qualifications to apply.
