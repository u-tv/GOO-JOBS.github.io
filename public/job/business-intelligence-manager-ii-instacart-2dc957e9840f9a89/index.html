Business Intelligence Manager II

🏢 instacart 📍 Canada - Remote (ON, AB, BC, or NS Only) 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

We're transforming the grocery industry At Instacart, we invite the world to share love through food because we believe everyone should have access to the food they love and more time to enjoy it together. Where others see a simple need for grocery delivery, we see exciting complexity and endless opportunity to serve the varied needs of our community. We work to deliver an essential service that customers rely on to get their groceries and household goods, while also offering safe and flexible earnings opportunities to Instacart Personal Shoppers. Instacart has become a lifeline for millions of people, and we’re building the team to help push our shopping cart forward. If you’re ready to do the best work of your life, come join our table. Instacart is a Flex First team There’s no one-size fits all approach to how we do our best work. Our employees have the flexibility to choose where they do their best work—whether it’s from home, an office, or your favorite coffee shop—while staying connected and building community through regular in-person events. Learn more about our flexible approach to where we work. WHY THIS ROLE IS ON THE MENU Instacart's Commercial Analytics team sits at the intersection of the industry's richest grocery data, our most strategic retail partnerships, and the CPG category intelligence that powers growth across the platform. As our retailer relationships scale and the complexity of our business deepens, we need someone who can support the data and analytics layer for the entire Commercial organization. We're hiring a Business Intelligence Manager II to build the data and AI foundation the Commercial org runs on: the models, semantic layer, dashboards, and AI tools that let analysts go deeper and let partner managers get trusted answers. This individual contributor role is the first hire in a new Data & AI function for Commercial Partnerships, with room to grow as it scales. The role will partner closely with our Commercial Insights and Partnerships teams, and the work will shape how Instacart grows its most important partnerships. WHAT YOU'LL COOK UP IN YOUR FIRST YEAR Analysts move faster because of your foundation. Core 1P and 3P data is modeled, tested, and documented. You work with Data Engineering to maintain and extend our retail data layer, so analysts spend their time on insight, not on reconciling numbers. Third-party data is a trusted asset. You manage our 3P data partnerships and maintain source-of-truth documentation and examples, so technical teams across the company use it consistently. Non-technical stakeholders self-serve. Partner Managers and Strategy & Planning stakeholders get accurate answers through dashboards and AI tools built on the semantic layer, with the guardrails and evals that make them trustworthy. You know your users, built what they actually use, and can show adoption. Repeat requests become products. You've turned recurring asks into self-serve tools, and you can show the hours saved or the requests that stopped coming. Cross-functional teams are better equipped to answer &quot;why&quot; and &quot;what's next.&quot; You've built tools and frameworks that help stakeholders move from raw data to clear direction. You're the go-to expert on commercial data. Teams rely on you for where data comes from, what it means, and its caveats, including first- and third-party sources. THE SECRET INGREDIENTS WE'RE LOOKING FOR You sit between data and the business. You're as comfortable modeling data as you are sitting with an account manager to understand what they actually need, and you measure success by how many people can answer their own questions because of what you built. Must-have pantry staples (Minimum Qualifications) 5+ years of experience in business intelligence, data analytics, or analytics engineering, building data products (models, dashboards, and self-serve tools) for business stakeholders. Strong SQL and solid data modeling fundamentals: choosing the right grain, designing fact and dimension tables, and handling data that changes over time. Hands-on experience with a modern data stack (e.g. dbt, Snowflake, Airflow). Experience with modern BI platforms and a track record of building reliable, effective tools and dashboards. Hands-on use of LLMs or AI tools in data workflows. Strong communication and stakeholder management. You turn ambiguous business questions into data requirements, explain data caveats to non-technical audiences, and reshape requests when needed. A self-starter orientation — you don't wait to be handed a problem; you identify it, frame it, and drive toward an answer. Optional garnishes (Preferred Qualifications) Experience in retail, e-commerce, or grocery — familiarity with the dynamics of online marketplaces is a meaningful accelerant. Experience partnering with sales, account management, or partnerships teams. Experience managing data from external vendors or third-party providers. Experience with semantic data layers or metric frameworks.
