Finer print You will be designing the user experience for Okta Privileged Access , one of the fastest-moving products at Okta. We provide unified access and governance for privileged resources — servers, service accounts, and secrets, whether on-prem or cloud — without compromising user experience. That last part is your job. Our users are IT and security admins, infrastructure engineers, and developers who need access to production. What you will do Drive and execute an ambitious product design strategy for your team ensuring a meaningful impact on both user experience and business outcomes. Bring a systems-thinking lens to the conversation and connect the dots between user insights and business goals to create experiences that keep millions of customers safe every day. Drive impact by framing problems and exploring solutions in collaboration with user research, UX writing, product management, engineering, marketing, and data science to deliver best in class experiences. You will have the opportunity to lead through influence. Design end-to-end flows that involve multiple personas – IT Admin, Security admins, End-user – and consider complex use cases that span the Okta portfolio ensuring that every user of Okta can do their work efficiently while staying secure. Perfect the details to design scalable and usable design patterns that drive a lasting impact, ensuring they not only meet a high bar for design quality and efficiency but also enhance efficiency and consistency across the product. Regularly present work, concepts, and new ideas to cross-functional team members, adjacent teams, and senior leadership to drive alignment, showcase design impact and influence key product decisions. Advocate and encourage thoughtful use of AI for brainstorming and iterating on potential solutions, prototyping, and collaborating with team members What we are looking for You easily move between big picture thinking and being detail-oriented.
