The Team: The Enterprise Architect - Finance will design, govern, and lead the strategic architecture for all critical financial, billing ,revenue and legal systems across the enterprise including core ERP platforms (Oracle NetSuite and Oracle Fusion Cloud) , Financial Planning & Analysis , and Procurement (Coupa) . This role is instrumental in driving the company’s financial transformation, operational scalability, and modernization initiatives , ensuring seamless system integration, data integrity, compliance, and long-term sustainability. Additionally, the position leads the integration of agentic AI capabilities and advanced AI frameworks into financial workflows to automate processes and unlock operational efficiencies. The ideal candidate will combine deep technical and architectural expertise with strong business acumen to shape a cohesive, cloud-first ecosystem that empowers Finance, Legal, and Business stakeholders to achieve strategic goals efficiently and effectively. The Opportunity: Strategic Architecture & Roadmapping Define the target-state architecture for Finance and Legal applications to ensure alignment with enterprise strategy and growth objectives. Develop and maintain a multi-year roadmap for modernization across ERP, FP&A, and other finance systems. Lead the architecture design and governance for major financial transformation initiatives, global system implementations, and M&A integrations. Partner with Finance, IT, and business stakeholders to ensure technology investments drive measurable business outcomes and ROI. Promote architectural standards and principles that enhance interoperability, security, and system resilience. Conduct build versus buy evaluations to guide key technology selection and architecture decisions across the finance ecosystem. Strategic Vision: Ability to translate complex business needs into scalable, sustainable technical architectures. Leadership & Influence: Skilled communicator who can bridge the gap between business strategy and technology execution. Analytical Mindset: Expert in diagnosing integration challenges and resolving architectural conflicts across multi-system environments. System & Data Integration Architect and standardize data flows, integrations, and interfaces between NetSuite, Fusion Cloud, Anaplan, Coupa, and other finance applications. Define and govern financial data models and master data hierarchies (Chart of Accounts, Legal Entities, Departments, Cost Centers) to maintain a single source of truth across platforms. Ensure accurate and timely data movement between systems — e.g., ERP actuals to Anaplan for forecasting, or commission data from Xactly to Fusion GL. Evaluate and implement integration technologies such as REST/SOAP APIs, iPaaS platforms, and native connectors to achieve secure, high-performance, and scalable data exchanges. Champion data governance and quality frameworks , enabling reliable financial reporting and analytics. Technical Expertise & Governance Serve as the enterprise architecture authority and subject matter expert across Finance and Legal platforms: Oracle NetSuite & Fusion Cloud: Lead SuiteSuccess design, advanced module integration, SuiteScript coding standards, multi-subsidiary enterprise architecture, EPM (Planning & FCCS), data migration initiatives, and OCI security frameworks. Financial Planning: Drive Connected Planning model design, architectural performance optimization, and governance best practices. Coupa: Oversee Procure-to-Pay (P2P) integrations, supplier master data governance, and spend management system architecture. AWS Architecture: Leverage multi-layer AWS cloud infrastructure to architect, deploy, and scale AI-driven enterprise solutions. AI & MCP Integration: Implement Model Context Protocol (MCP) and agentic AI frameworks within existing SaaS ecosystems to seamlessly connect and automate cross-platform workflows. Audit / SOD: Ensure governance frameworks support Segregation of Duties (SOD), compliance auditing, and robust internal controls across financial systems. Establish, govern, and enforce enterprise-wide architecture frameworks, design principles, and technical best practices . Ensure all solution designs strictly comply with SOX controls, security policies, and global regulatory standards to safeguard data integrity and audit readiness. Direct technical evaluations, architecture reviews, and proof-of-concept (POC) initiatives for next-generation platforms to continuously enhance Finance and Legal capabilities. Drive continuous innovation through intelligent automation, advanced analytics, and workflow optimization across the enterprise technology ecosystem. What you’ll bring to the role 12+ years of experience in Enterprise or Solutions Architecture , with a focus on Finance, ERP, and SaaS ecosystems . Demonstrated success in architecting and integrating complex enterprise solutions across NetSuite, Coupa, Fusion Cloud, Anaplan, and others . Proven leadership in at least three full-cycle, global system implementations or financial transformation programs . Deep understanding of finance and legal processes , including Record-to-Report, Procure-to-Pay, Order-to-Cash, Plan-to-Report, and Contract-to-Cash. Expert knowledge of enterprise integration frameworks and technologies: SuiteScript/JavaScript, Python, API orchestration, AI MCP, and Agent event-driven architectures Strong foundation in data modeling, master data management (MDM) , and metadata governance. Familiarity with data warehousing, BI tools, and analytics platforms supporting financial and operational reporting. Exposure to Enterprise Architecture frameworks (TOGAF, Zachman) and architecture tooling (LeanIX, Lucidchart, etc.). Education Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Information Systems, Computer Science , or a related field.
