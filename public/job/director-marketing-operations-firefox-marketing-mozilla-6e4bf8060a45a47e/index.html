Why Mozilla? Mozilla Corporation is the non-profit-backed technology company that has shaped the internet for the better over the last 25 years. We make pioneering brands like Firefox, the privacy-minded web browser. Now, with more than 225 million people around the world using our products each month, we’re shaping the next 25 years of technology and helping to reclaim an internet built for people, not companies. Our work focuses on diverse areas including AI, social media, security and more. And we’re doing this while never losing our focus on our core mission – to make the internet better for people. The Mozilla Corporation is wholly owned by the non-profit 501(c) Mozilla Foundation. This means we aren’t beholden to any shareholders — only to our mission. Along with thousands of volunteer contributors and collaborators all over the world, Mozillians design, build and distribute open-source software that enables people to enjoy the internet on their terms About this team and role: Mozilla Communications, Marketing and Policy (CMP) Team is looking for an operator to make sure the entire team runs smoothly. That covers budget, people, process, offsites, templates, tools and the systems underneath all of them. The team has grown quickly across brand, product marketing, media, creative, communications and policy, and its operations have not kept pace with the work. This is a hands-on, technically fluent operations role. The largest part of the job is the tools and automations the team uses to run itself, so that budget tracking, onboarding, planning, partner management and everyday workflows take less manual effort and hold up as the team changes. You will choose and set up those tools, build the automations, and get the team using them. The wider marketing technology and data stack stays with our MarTech team, and you will work closely with them. What you’ll do: Own the tools the team uses to run itself (project and workflow management, budget and spend tracking, partner and contract records, shared knowledge, AI assistants), and decide what gets added or retired. Keep the team's operational data and shared sources of truth (budget, headcount, partners, projects, the contract library, planning and process folders) accurate, current, and in one place, so no one is reconciling spreadsheets by hand. Lead the team's automation program: design and build AI agents and automated workflows that take manual effort out of how the team operates, and set the guardrails, ownership, and maintenance for the automations staff build themselves. Run the marketing budget cycle in partnership with our Finance lead: set up annual envelopes by function, keep spend tracking current, support monthly reconciliation and forecasting, and run the spend approval process so every dollar has an owner and a line. Own people operations for the team with our People partners: new hire onboarding, the org chart, headcount and open roles, and the basics every new team member needs on day one. Own the marketing operations process, playbooks and templates, keep them current, and train the team on them. Run the partner contract process for marketing: draft SOWs and change orders with the team that owns each agency, technology or creator partner, move them through review to signature, track renewals, and run partner onboarding and regular reviews against scope and cost. Run the team's learning program with function leads, who keep ownership of craft: bring in new ideas, tools and ways of working through training, outside speakers and shared examples, so the team keeps building its skills. Plan and run team offsites and working sessions, and support all hands meetings and other core team communication moments, from budget and logistics through the materials behind them. Make sure the CMO is reporting on the team's key metrics properly: set the standard view, collect each function's numbers on time, and have it ready for monthly, QBR and board reporting. The analysis behind the numbers stays with the functions and data science. Own and drive CMP’s business rhythms including annual planning, QBRs, board presentations, and monthly ET business reviews. Run the mechanics of annual and quarterly planning: the calendar, the templates and the inputs each function owes, in partnership with the marketing leaders who set the plan itself. Bring the team's tool and automation needs to the MarTech and web teams, who own the wider marketing technology and data stack. Lead and develop a small operations team – the CMO Office. Report directly to the Chief Marketing Officer. What you’ll bring: 10+ years in marketing operations or business operations, including at least 3 years leading operations for a marketing organization of 50 or more people A track record of building team systems with AI and automation tools, with examples of what you built and what it replaced.
