CircleCI: Senior Support Engineer

🏢 Various 📍 Remote 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

Headquarters: Remote; Tokyo Senior Support Engineer Team: Customer Engineering and Success, CircleCI About the team Support Engineering is part of Customer Engineering and Success at CircleCI. We give technical support to customers after they buy. Our team works around the clock across North America, Asia Pacific, and Europe. We build and manage AI agents that answer customer questions and solve technical problems. People step in when a problem needs deeper work. We also share what we learn with Product and Engineering to make CircleCI better. About the role Senior Support Engineers take on the hardest customer problems and incidents. You help set how our AI support agents handle those problems, and you step in when agents cannot. You also mentor teammates and help the team grow its skills. You use AI tools every day. You check all AI output, and you help others on the team do the same. What you will do Design, build, and improve AI support agents. Measure how well they work. Own the hardest customer problems from start to finish, including those agents cannot solve. Lead the team during incidents. Keep customers informed with clear and timely updates. Review AI-assisted work from teammates and coach them. Mentor teammates. Share what you know so that no single person is the only expert. Check new product features for how easy they will be to support. Work with Product and Engineering to fix problems early. Set team goals. Improve team processes and help content. Advocate for customer experience with Product and Engineering. Meet response targets on the cases you own. You describe what the product can and cannot do. We do not oversell. Join the on-call rotation. What you need More than 3 years in technical product support or software engineering. You have solved complex problems. Hands-on experience building or running AI tools, workflows, or agents. You review results for accuracy. Skill at tracing failures across services in cloud or distributed systems. You read logs and test ideas to find the cause. Hands-on experience with Docker and Kubernetes. You have used tools like kubectl to find why an application or cluster is failing. Experience supporting self-hosted or on-premises software. You have used tools like Helm and support bundles to find why an installation is failing. Hands-on experience with build, test, and deploy automation. You have written automated tests and set up a CI/CD pipeline for a project. Experience running self-hosted build runners on Linux, Windows, macOS, or Arm, including in networks with limited access. Experience with SDLC practices and related tooling; infrastructure as code (such as Terraform), CI/CD, Git, and Linux. Experience working with API programmatically, scripting against them with a CLI or SDK. You can write scripts to reproduce problems. Knowledge of networking and security. Experience troubleshooting single sign-on (SSO), OAuth, and access controls. You have worked with identity providers and code-hosting sign-in. Experience mentoring other engineers. Clear writing and speaking. You can handle sensitive customer situations calmly. Experience working across time zones, with clear handoffs. Nice to have Experience with AWS, Google Cloud, or Azure, including OIDC trust policies. Advanced experience with observability, tracing and monitoring tools. Experience connecting AI tools to company data and systems. Knowledge of iOS application testing and code signing. Experience with security response, such as vulnerability (CVE) triage, certificate problems, or abuse reports. Experience querying data with SQL and building dashboards. About CircleCI CircleCI is the world’s largest continuous integration/continuous delivery (CI/CD) platform, and the hub where code moves from idea to delivery. As one of the most-used DevOps tools - processing more than 3 million jobs a day - CircleCI has unique access to data on how the most effective engineering teams work, and the tools to help software companies successfully leverage the power of AI into their commercial applications. Companies like Hinge, HuggingFace, and Samsung use us to improve engineering team productivity, release better products, and get to market faster.
