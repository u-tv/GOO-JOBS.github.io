Manager Compliance

🏢 mongodb 📍 Gurugram 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

MongoDB is looking for a Manager, International Compliance to own statutory, regulatory, and corporate compliance for MongoDB's entities outside the US. This role sits at the intersection of Controllership, Legal, and Tax, ensuring MongoDB operates in full compliance with local corporate, tax, labor, and exchange control regulations across its international footprint, while supporting the broader global controllership agenda. This is a strong fit for someone who thrives on owning a compliance function across multiple jurisdictions, has strong experience navigating varied regulatory frameworks, and can lead a small team while partnering closely with global Finance, Legal, and Tax stakeholders. operations Maintain a compliance calendar and tracker spanning all applicable jurisdictions and statutes, with clear ownership and escalation paths Corporate Governance & Secretarial Coordination
