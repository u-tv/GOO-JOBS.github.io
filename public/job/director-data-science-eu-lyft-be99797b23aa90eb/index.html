Director, Data Science, EU

🏢 lyft 📍 San Francisco, CA 💼 Full-time 💰 Not disclosed

Job Description

At Lyft, our purpose is to serve and connect. To do this, we start with our own community by creating an open, inclusive, and diverse organization. Data Science is at the heart of Lyft’s products and decision-making. As the Director, Data Science for Lyft EU, you will work in a dynamic environment, where we embrace moving quickly to build the world’s best transportation. Data Scientists take on a variety of problems, ranging from shaping critical business decisions to building algorithms that power our business insights. We’re looking for a passionate, driven Director, Data Science with strong experience in two-sided marketplaces and production machine learning to take on some of the most interesting and impactful problems and lead some of the best scientists in the industry. This role will be overseeing the Product Science teams, which drive experimentation, metrics, and product insights across rider and driver experiences, and the ML Engineering teams, which build and operate the production models behind pricing, matching, ETAs, and marketplace efficiency. You will be working on one of the most profitable and fastest growing revenue centers in our company. We are the team that solves some of mobility’s hardest problems: balancing supply and demand in real time, setting prices that work for riders and drivers, and measuring the true impact of product changes in a two-sided marketplace. As a trusted leader in the broader Data Science org, you will define and drive the Data Science strategy, vision, and roadmap for Lyft EU, while ensuring that the team has all the necessary tools and infrastructure to perform their work. You will also be responsible for building and executing a strategy for self-service data science and analytics experiences across the organization, overseeing the Science, Product, and ML Engineering teams for Lyft Europe. In this role, you will be a key partner to Engineering, Operations, Regional leaders, and cross-functional stakeholders to ensure consistent delivery of what the organization and our partners need. Responsibilities: Build and lead a high-performing team of data scientists Experience leading and managing teams of managers is preferred Work with cross-functional partners in product, engineering, design, finance, and operations to achieve business goals Prioritize and lead deep dives into our data to uncover new product and business opportunities Prioritize and lead development of productized models to automate operational decision-making and drive operational productivity Develop analytical frameworks, roadmaps, and metrics for the team and broader organization Collaborate with other leaders and executives to build data-informed business strategy and product roadmaps Serve as a champion of sound decision-making at all levels of the company Experience: Advanced Degree in a quantitative field like statistics, economics, applied math, operations research or engineering 4+ years demonstrated experience leading work in the two-sided marketplace space 3+ years demonstrated experience building scaled products and services from early stages 10+ years general hands-on technical experience in a data science or equivalent ML role 5+ years people management experience building and leading data science teams Track record of guiding teams through unstructured technical problems to deliver business impact Skilled at managing cross-functional relationships and communicating with leadership across multiple teams Willingness to collaborate and communicate with others to drive cross-team and cross-organization initiatives forward Benefits: Great medical, dental, and vision insurance options with additional programs available when enrolled Mental health benefits Family building benefits Child care and pet benefits 401(k) plan to help save for your future In addition to 12 observed holidays, salaried team members have discretionary paid time off, hourly team members have 15 days paid time off 18 weeks of paid parental leave.
